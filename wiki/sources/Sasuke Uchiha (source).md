# Sasuke Uchiha (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Sasuke Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Uchiha)
Raw file: `raw/Sasuke Uchiha.md`

## Summary

Companion biography to [[Naruto Uzumaki (source)]], same exhaustive format, covering
Sasuke's full arc: the Uchiha massacre and his relationship with Itachi, his defection and
descent into an "avenger" ideology, the truth about Itachi's sacrifice, his reconciliation
with Naruto, and his post-war life as the "Supporting Kage."

## Key takeaways

- Fills in the Uchiha massacre in detail: [[Itachi Uchiha]] killed their clan on Konoha
  leadership's orders (to prevent a coup) but deliberately spared and psychologically
  protected Sasuke, staging himself as a hateable villain so Sasuke would have a reason to
  grow strong rather than despair.
- Sasuke's ideology curdles over time from simple revenge into a warped "True Hokage"
  philosophy — one person should shoulder all of a village's hard, hated decisions alone —
  which is what drives the [[Team 7]] Valley of the End rematch already documented on
  Naruto's page.
- Confirms the post-war reconciliation arc: pardoned on Naruto and Kakashi's word, becomes
  a wandering vigilante investigating the Ōtsutsuki clan, eventually marries
  [[Sakura Haruno]] and fathers [[Sarada Uchiha]], whom Sakura raises largely alone during
  his long absences.
- **New open thread**: in *New Era: Part II*, Sasuke is labeled a criminal again for helping
  Boruto, goes into hiding as Boruto's mentor, and is critically incapacitated in a fight
  with Code — bitten by a "Claw Grime" that drains his chakra and grows into a separate
  being (Hidari), leaving Sasuke's own status in the current storyline unresolved, in
  parallel with [[Naruto Uzumaki]]'s. See [[Team 7]] for the combined picture.
- Introduces [[Itachi Uchiha]] as a full entity (previously only mentioned in passing) and
  corroborates/extends [[Sarada Uchiha]]'s introduction from the aniSearch/Wikipedia sources.
