# Sai (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Sai](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sai)
Raw file: `raw/Sai.md`

## Summary

Exhaustive fan-wiki biography of Sai, the Root-trained replacement for [[Sasuke Uchiha]] on
Team Kakashi: his emotionless Root conditioning and the loss of his "brother" Shin, his
gradual rediscovery of genuine emotion and friendship through Team 7, his marriage to Ino
Yamanaka, and his active role as Anbu Chief straight through the *New Era: Part II* crisis
chapters — making this one of the most current sources ingested so far.

## Key takeaways

- Explains Sai's Root background: raised by [[Orochimaru]]'s former handler Danzō Shimura to
  have no name, personality, or attachments; his one exception was art, encouraged by a Root
  "brother," Shin, whose death (and Sai's later encounter with Shin's reincarnated corpse in
  the war) is the emotional hinge of his character arc.
- Details his rocky introduction to [[Naruto Uzumaki]] and [[Sakura Haruno]] (nicknaming
  Sakura "Ugly," secretly tasked with assassinating Sasuke on Danzō's orders) and his genuine
  change of heart, choosing to protect the bond between Naruto and Sasuke instead.
- Confirms he marries Ino Yamanaka (taking the Yamanaka name), becomes Anbu Director/Chief,
  and has a son, Inojin — directly corroborating [[Team 10 (Asuma) (source)]]'s account
  of Ino's marriage.
- **Most significant find**: this source's "New Era: Part II" section (Boruto's Return Arc,
  Matsuri and Ryū Arc, Mamushi Arc) confirms **Shikamaru Nara is acting/interim Hokage**
  during the period [[Naruto Uzumaki]] is missing — a detail not previously in the wiki. Sai
  serves as his security chief through this period, and the arc introduces unresolved
  political tension: the Fire Daimyō's advisor Kobu suspects Shikamaru of helping the
  criminalized Boruto escape custody. See [[Team 7]] "Current crisis" section, now updated
  with this.
- Also confirms Yamato and Sai served together under Kakashi/Yamato-led Team 7 configurations
  during Part II (Tenchi Bridge, Power arc), corroborating [[Yamato (source)]].
