# Hashirama Senju (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Hashirama Senju](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hashirama_Senju)
Raw file: `raw/Hashirama Senju.md`

## Summary

Exhaustive biography of Hashirama Senju, First Hokage and co-founder of Konohagakure alongside
his childhood friend-turned-rival [[Madara Uchiha]]. Covers his Warring States Period
upbringing, the founding of Konoha, his death, and his extensive posthumous legacy (his DNA
and cells directly explain [[Yamato]]'s Wood Release, Danzō Shimura's arm, and Madara's final
revival).

## Key takeaways

- **Founding Konoha**: Hashirama and Madara ended the Senju–Uchiha war by uniting their clans;
  Hashirama was elected the village's first Hokage after Tobirama insisted on a democratic
  process rather than simply appointing Madara.
- **The Madara relationship**: a lifelong friendship-rivalry that never fully broke even as
  enemies — Hashirama refused to kill Madara for years, wanted him as his successor, and
  ultimately killed him only reluctantly at the Valley of the End. They reconcile fully in
  their final moments during the Fourth Shinobi World War (corroborates and expands on the
  account already on [[Madara Uchiha]]).
- **Family and successors**: brother of [[Tobirama Senju]] (who often acted as his more
  realistic counterweight); grandfather of [[Tsunade]]; helped train a young
  [[Hiruzen Sarutobi]].
- **Posthumous legacy**: his exhumed cells are the direct origin of [[Yamato]]'s Wood Release
  (via [[Orochimaru]]'s experiments), Danzō Shimura's Hashirama-cell arm, and the substance
  used to heal Obito Uchiha and later Naruto/Sasuke's lost arms — ties together several
  threads already in the wiki under one root cause.
- **Reincarnation arc**: alongside Tobirama, Minato, and Hiruzen, reincarnated via Impure
  World Reincarnation during the Fourth Shinobi World War; helps [[Sasuke Uchiha]] understand
  Konoha's founding and the Uchiha's history before dying a second, final death.
