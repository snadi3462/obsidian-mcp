# Deidara (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Deidara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Deidara)
Raw file: `raw/Deidara.md`

## Summary

Biography of Deidara, Akatsuki's explosive-clay artist: an Iwagakure defector forcibly
recruited after losing to [[Itachi Uchiha]]'s genjutsu, partnered first with [[Sasori]] and
later with Tobi (Obito), whose obsessive belief that "art is a fleeting explosion" defines
both his fighting style and his death — a self-detonation against [[Sasuke Uchiha]].

## Key takeaways

- Explains his lifelong grudge against [[Itachi Uchiha]] (the humiliating genjutsu defeat
  that forced him into Akatsuki) and how it transfers to [[Sasuke Uchiha]] as Itachi's proxy
  during their climactic battle — corroborating and expanding [[Sasuke Uchiha
  (source)]]'s brief account of the same fight.
- Details his artistic philosophy clash with [[Sasori]] (fleeting explosions vs. eternal
  puppets) as the defining friction — and mutual respect — of their partnership.
- Confirms his death: after losing both arms in escalating fights (Gaara's sand, Kakashi's
  Kamui), Deidara detonates himself in a final suicide bombing against Sasuke, a sacrifice
  ultimately rendered pointless when Sasuke escapes via summon.
