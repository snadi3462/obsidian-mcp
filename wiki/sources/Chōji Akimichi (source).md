# Chōji Akimichi (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Chōji Akimichi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%8Dji_Akimichi)
Raw file: `raw/Chōji Akimichi.md`

## Summary

Biography of Chōji Akimichi, [[Team 10]]'s kind-hearted heavy hitter: his lifelong best-friendship with Shikamaru Nara, his clan's calorie-to-chakra techniques, his near-fatal willingness to sacrifice himself for his friends, and his adult life marrying Karui and raising their daughter Chōchō.

## Key takeaways

- Explains the origin of Chōji's defining friendship: as a bullied child, Shikamaru Nara was the first to treat him with genuine respect, beginning a best-friendship built on watching clouds and sharing chips that anchors both of their characters for the rest of the series.
- Details his willingness to risk death for his friends: using the fatal Three Coloured Pills to kill Jirōbō rather than let Shikamaru be mocked, and struggling (but ultimately succeeding) to fight his own reincarnated sensei [[Asuma Sarutobi]] during the war.
- Confirms he marries Karui and has a daughter, Chōchō, in the *New Era* — corroborated by [[Asuma Sarutobi (source)]]'s "legacy" section and [[Ino Yamanaka (source)]].
- Reaches the Kawaki Arc (fighting alongside Konoha's jōnin against Isshiki) but does not continue into the *New Era: Part II* crisis chapters — his status during Naruto's disappearance is unconfirmed.
