# Akatsuki (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Akatsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki)
Raw file: `raw/Akatsuki.md`

## Summary

Full organizational history of Akatsuki, the criminal organization at the center of Part
II's antagonist structure — across its four successive "eras" under different leaders
(Yahiko, Nagato/Pain, Tobi/Obito, and a minor post-war revival under Shin Uchiha), its
membership roster, internal partnerships, and its true origin as a tool of Black Zetsu and
Kaguya Ōtsutsuki.

## Key takeaways

- Reveals Akatsuki's true original purpose was benign: founded by war orphans Yahiko, Nagato,
  and Konan to bring peace to Amegakure, before Yahiko's forced suicide (at Danzō's and
  Hanzō's hands) radicalized Nagato into "Pain," whose philosophy of shocking the world into
  peace via mass destruction defines the organization's Part I/II activity.
- Confirms [[Orochimaru]] was an early member who defected before the story's start (taking
  his ring, which is why he's never replaced) — corroborating [[Orochimaru (source)]] —
  and details [[Itachi Uchiha]]'s membership and his partnership with Kisame Hoshigaki, useful
  cross-reference for [[Itachi Uchiha (source)]].
- Establishes the full manipulation chain behind the entire war: Black Zetsu tricked Madara
  Uchiha into founding the *idea* of Akatsuki, then used Obito (as "Tobi," posing as Madara)
  to steer Nagato's real Akatsuki toward capturing all nine tailed beasts — all in service of
  reviving Kaguya Ōtsutsuki, unbeknownst to virtually everyone involved until the very end.
- Details team pairings, the ring system, and the organization's brief, failed revival by Shin
  Uchiha fifteen years after the war — relevant background for any future Shin Uchiha source.
