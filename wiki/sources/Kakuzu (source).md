# Kakuzu (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Kakuzu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakuzu)
Raw file: `raw/Kakuzu.md`

## Summary

Biography of Kakuzu, [[Hidan]]'s Akatsuki partner and the organization's money-obsessed
"treasurer": a Takigakure defector who extends his life by harvesting hearts from his
victims (and former partners), eventually killed by [[Kakashi Hatake]] and [[Naruto
Uzumaki]] during the hunt for Asuma's killers.

## Key takeaways

- Explains his defection: after failing to assassinate Hashirama Senju (the First Hokage) on
  a mission, Takigakure punished rather than honored him, leading Kakuzu to kill the village
  elders and flee with their forbidden techniques — the origin of his five-heart immortality
  technique.
- Details the mutual-loathing-but-flawless-teamwork partnership with [[Hidan]] (Kakuzu's
  greed vs. Hidan's religious zealotry) that makes them, per Shikamaru Nara, "unstoppable so
  long as they are together" — corroborating [[Asuma Sarutobi (source)]]'s account of
  their fight against Team 10.
- Confirms his death: after losing all five hearts to [[Kakashi Hatake]], Team 10, and
  [[Naruto Uzumaki]]'s newly-perfected Rasenshuriken during the Akatsuki Suppression Mission,
  Kakuzu is finished off by Kakashi's Lightning Cutter — directly corroborating [[Asuma
  Sarutobi (source)]] and [[Chōji Akimichi (source)]]'s accounts of the same fight.
