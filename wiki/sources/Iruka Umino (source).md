# Iruka Umino (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Iruka Umino](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Iruka_Umino)
Raw file: `raw/Iruka Umino.md`

## Summary

Biography of Iruka Umino, [[Naruto Uzumaki]]'s Academy instructor and the first adult in the
village to treat him with genuine care rather than fear or pity. Orphaned by the same
Nine-Tails attack that made Naruto a jinchūriki, giving the two a parallel loneliness that
becomes the foundation of their relationship.

## Key takeaways

- **Mentor to Naruto**: initially uneasy teaching the container of the fox that killed his own
  parents, but becomes the first adult Naruto genuinely respects — protects him from Mizuki's
  attack after his jinchūriki status is revealed, and lets him graduate the Academy for the
  effort behind his self-invented technique rather than by the letter of the rules.
- **Naruto's father figure**: stands in as Naruto's father at his wedding to [[Hinata Hyuga]]
  at Naruto's own request — corroborates and adds texture to the wedding already described on
  [[Naruto Uzumaki]].
- **Mentored by Hiruzen**: the Third Hokage ([[Hiruzen Sarutobi]]) comforted Iruka after his
  parents' deaths and became his own model for what teaching should look like, which is why
  Iruka pursued a teaching career himself.
- **New Era**: continues teaching at the Academy for decades, is eventually promoted to
  Headmaster, and remains a recurring, grounding presence across Naruto's *Boruto*-era
  storyline (including watching over Naruto and Hinata's daughter [[Himawari Uzumaki]] during
  an Academy trial-lesson arc).
