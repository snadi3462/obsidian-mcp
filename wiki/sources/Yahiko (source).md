# Yahiko (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Yahiko](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yahiko)
Raw file: `raw/Yahiko.md`

## Summary

Biography of Yahiko, [[Akatsuki]]'s original founder and leader: an Amegakure war orphan who
dreamed of ending war by becoming powerful enough to force peace, and whose death — a
self-sacrifice to save Konan — sets in motion Nagato's transformation into "Pain" and
everything that follows in Part I/II.

## Key takeaways

- The shortest and most focused of the three founder biographies: establishes Yahiko as the
  group's natural leader and public face, always deferring privately to Nagato as the "true"
  vessel for their shared dream, per [[Nagato (source)]] and [[Konan (source)]].
- Confirms his death — forced by Hanzō and Danzō Shimura into a choice between Konan's death
  or his own, Yahiko impales himself on Nagato's kunai — and its lasting legacy: his corpse
  becomes the Deva Path, the body Nagato/Pain most commonly speaks through for the rest of
  the story, treated as the "true" leader even after death.
- Corroborates [[Madara Uchiha (source)]]'s account of an early meeting with "Madara"
  (secretly Obito) during the Third Shinobi World War — Yahiko's sharp-eyed distrust of the
  masked man is the detail that keeps Nagato and Konan wary of him for years afterward.
