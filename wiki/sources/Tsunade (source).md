# Tsunade (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Tsunade](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tsunade)
Raw file: `raw/Tsunade.md`

## Summary

Exhaustive fan-wiki biography of Tsunade, the Fifth Hokage and world's strongest kunoichi:
granddaughter of the First Hokage, third of the Sannin alongside [[Orochimaru]] and
[[Jiraiya]], driven from the shinobi life by grief and hemophobia before Naruto's example
brings her back to lead Konoha, train [[Sakura Haruno]], and later predecess [[Kakashi Hatake]]
as Hokage.

## Key takeaways

- Explains her hemophobia's origin: both her younger brother Nawaki and her lover Dan Katō
  died pursuing the dream of becoming Hokage, and she failed to save Dan from fatal blood
  loss — leading her to conclude "Hokage is a fool's job" and abandon shinobi life for years.
- Details how [[Naruto Uzumaki]] restores her faith in dreams (mastering the Rasengan despite
  no natural talent, echoing Nawaki and Dan), directly leading to her accepting the Fifth
  Hokage title and, later, her devoted training of [[Sakura Haruno]] in medical ninjutsu —
  corroborating and expanding [[Sakura Haruno (source)]]'s account of that mentorship.
- Confirms she assigns [[Yamato]] and [[Sai]] to the reformed Team 7/Team Kakashi
  specifically to manage Naruto's Nine-Tails chakra and Danzō's Root oversight, respectively
  — corroborating both [[Yamato (source)]] and [[Sai (source)]].
- Details her complex, permanently strained relationship with [[Orochimaru]] (attempted to
  kill him rather than heal his arms; remains wary of him even after his reform) contrasted
  with her platonic, deeply affectionate bond with [[Jiraiya]], whose death she privately
  mourns.
- Her Pain's Assault heroics (transferring all her chakra through Katsuyu to shield the
  village, lapsing into a coma) and Fourth Shinobi World War role (war council command)
  precede her succession by [[Kakashi Hatake]] as Sixth Hokage, already noted on his page.
  This source's own coverage ends in the *New Era* (Kawaki Arc, aiding Shikamaru's
  coordination against Isshiki) and does not reach the *New Era: Part II* crisis chapters.
