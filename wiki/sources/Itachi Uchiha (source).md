# Itachi Uchiha (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Itachi Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Itachi_Uchiha)
Raw file: `raw/Itachi Uchiha.md`

## Summary

Exhaustive fan-wiki biography of Itachi Uchiha — Konoha's child prodigy turned Anbu double
agent, mass murderer of his own clan, and secret protector of both [[Sasuke Uchiha]] and the
village until his death. The first dedicated source for a character previously only known
through [[Sasuke Uchiha (source)]]'s summary of him.

## Key takeaways

- Substantially deepens the massacre backstory already on [[Itachi Uchiha]]'s page: Itachi
  was a genuine pacifist traumatized by war as a child, became a double agent reporting the
  Uchiha coup plot to Konoha leadership, and was given an impossible choice by [[Danzō
  Shimura]] — support the coup and lose the whole clan including Sasuke, or wipe out the clan
  himself and spare Sasuke. He chose the latter, and recruited "Madara" (actually Obito) to
  help, on the condition Konoha itself be spared.
- Details his infiltration mission inside [[Akatsuki]] (to keep it from moving against
  Konoha) and his partnership/friendship with Kisame Hoshigaki — corroborating and expanding
  [[Akatsuki (source)]]'s account of the same team.
- Confirms the mechanics of his death: dying already of a terminal illness, Itachi lets
  Sasuke defeat him, using his last act to plant a failsafe (a crow containing Shisui's
  Mangekyō Sharingan) that will later protect Sasuke from Tobi's manipulation.
- Details his post-death reincarnation during the Fourth Shinobi World War: fighting
  alongside Naruto and Killer B against a reincarnated Nagato, then reconciling fully and
  honestly with a revived Sasuke before dispelling the Impure World Reincarnation and
  returning to the afterlife — this is a fuller account of a scene already summarized on
  [[Sasuke Uchiha (source)]].
- **Legacy section** directly ties Itachi's influence to Sasuke's adult habits (forehead-
  poking his wife and daughter) and to Sasuke's post-war "world's darkness" resolve — useful
  cross-reference for [[Sasuke Uchiha]] and [[Sarada Uchiha]].
