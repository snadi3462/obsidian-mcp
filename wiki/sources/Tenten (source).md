# Tenten (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Tenten](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tenten)
Raw file: `raw/Tenten.md`

## Summary

Biography of Tenten, Team Guy's weapons and fūinjutsu specialist: her failed attempt to emulate [[Tsunade]] as a medical-nin, her development of a unique sealing-scroll weapons arsenal, and her adult career running a ninja tool shop and proctoring the Chūnin Exams.

## Key takeaways

- Explains her dream of becoming a legendary kunoichi like [[Tsunade]], and how repeated failure at medical ninjutsu (poor chakra control) redirected her toward fūinjutsu and weaponry instead — a formative arc of finding her own strength rather than imitating someone else's.
- Confirms her strong loyalty to teammates [[Rock Lee]] and Neji Hyuga despite finding their antics exhausting, and her role saving Neji during the Kazekage Rescue Mission and defeating her own duplicate during the raid on the Akatsuki lair.
- Confirms her adult career: opens a weapon shop, becomes a Chūnin Exams proctor (alongside Shino Aburame), and excels at space–time ninjutsu/puppetry.
- Reaches the Kawaki Arc (fighting and being quickly defeated by Isshiki) but does not continue into the *New Era: Part II* crisis chapters.
