# Team 10 (Asuma) (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Team 10 (Asuma)](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_10_(Asuma))
Raw file: `raw/Team 10 (Asuma).md`

## Summary

Short team-overview page for Team Asuma / Team 10 — the "Ino–Shika–Chō" generation:
Shikamaru Nara, Chōji Akimichi, Ino Yamanaka, led by Asuma Sarutobi until his death in Part II
(Kakashi temporarily leads them afterward for revenge against his killer, Hidan). Thin on
individual detail, but the marriage/family facts here corroborate and are corroborated by
[[Sai (source)]].

## Key takeaways

- Confirms Team 10 is the "Ino–Shika–Chō" formation, inherited from their parents'
  generation — the naming pattern already noted (without a page) on [[Team 7]]'s concept
  page; this source is the first dedicated ingest of that team.
- Details Asuma Sarutobi's death fighting Akatsuki's Hidan, and the team's unauthorized
  revenge mission — [[Tsunade]] initially forbids it (their sub-four-man squad doesn't meet
  requirements) until [[Kakashi Hatake]] volunteers to lead them, corroborating
  [[Tsunade (source)]]'s account of the same event from her side.
- Confirms the team's adult marriages/families: Shikamaru becomes [[Naruto Uzumaki]]'s
  advisor as Hokage, marries Temari, and has a son Shikadai; Ino marries [[Sai]] (taking the
  Yamanaka name reversed — Sai becomes "Sai Yamanaka") and has a son Inojin, corroborating
  [[Sai (source)]]; Chōji marries Karui and has a daughter Chōchō.
- **Shikamaru's later prominence matters beyond this source**: [[Sai (source)]]'s
  *New Era: Part II* chapters reveal Shikamaru is acting/interim Hokage during
  [[Naruto Uzumaki]]'s disappearance — this team-overview page predates and doesn't cover
  that, but is the source of Shikamaru's baseline advisor role that makes it plausible. A
  stub [[Shikamaru Nara]] entity page has been created; a dedicated source is still needed
  for a full biography.
