# Ino Yamanaka (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Ino Yamanaka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ino_Yamanaka)
Raw file: `raw/Ino Yamanaka.md`

## Summary

Biography of Ino Yamanaka, [[Team 10]]'s mind-technique specialist: her childhood friendship-turned-rivalry with [[Sakura Haruno]], her clan's sensory and mind-affecting techniques, her marriage to [[Sai]], and — critically — the most current-era account yet ingested, running deep into the *New Era: Part II* crisis as Shikamaru Nara's telepathic communications officer.

## Key takeaways

- Confirms and expands the Sakura friendship/rivalry arc already on [[Sakura Haruno]]'s page, and confirms Ino's marriage to [[Sai]] and their son Inojin, corroborating [[Sai (source)]] and [[Team 10 (Asuma) (source)]].
- Details her role as head of the Konoha Barrier Team and a telepathic communications specialist (Mind Body Transmission Technique), making her the operational hub for village-wide alerts throughout the Kawaki Arc.
- **Major new finding**: Ino's "New Era: Part II" section (Boruto's Return Arc) is the most detailed account yet of the current crisis. It confirms, via Shikamaru relaying what Boruto told him: **[[Naruto Uzumaki]] and [[Hinata Hyuga]] are alive** (not confirmed dead, contrary to ambiguity in earlier sources), and **[[Himawari Uzumaki]] is confirmed as the new Nine-Tails jinchūriki**, having used that power to heal an injured Boruto — resolving (rather than just flagging) the open question on [[Kurama]]'s page about how Himawari could be a jinchūriki after Kurama's death.
- Also reveals a major new plot thread: Boruto is **captured and interrogated (escalating to physical torture) by Konoha as a suspected traitor** after his return, with [[Shikamaru Nara]] — confirmed here as acting **Hokage** — privately convinced of his innocence but unable to act openly, and Ino refusing his request to secretly reconnect him with Boruto out of loyalty to protocol and suspicion of Boruto herself. This is now the sharpest picture of the "Current crisis" available and has been used to update [[Team 7]], [[Kurama]], and [[Himawari Uzumaki]].
