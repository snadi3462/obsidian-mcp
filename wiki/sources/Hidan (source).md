# Hidan (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Hidan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hidan)
Raw file: `raw/Hidan.md`

## Summary

Biography of Hidan, [[Kakuzu]]'s Akatsuki partner: an immortal, sadistic zealot of the Jashin
faith who kills [[Asuma Sarutobi]] but is left — uniquely among Akatsuki members — not dead
but trapped underground and slowly starving, since his immortality makes him nearly
impossible to actually kill.

## Key takeaways

- Explains his immortality (granted through Jashinist ritual experimentation) and his curse
  technique, which links his body to an opponent's so that any damage Hidan inflicts on
  himself is mirrored on them — the mechanism behind Asuma Sarutobi's death.
- Confirms in detail Shikamaru Nara's revenge plan and Asuma's death, corroborating and
  significantly expanding [[Asuma Sarutobi (source)]] and [[Kakuzu (source)]]'s
  accounts of the same battle from Hidan's own side.
- **Notably, Hidan does not die** — unlike every other Akatsuki member covered this ingest,
  he survives Shikamaru's explosive trap (dismembered but conscious) and is buried alive,
  confirmed still alive but starving to death from lack of nutrition as of the manga's final
  word on him. A later, unrelated Jashinist copycat ("Ryūki") fails to replicate his legacy.
