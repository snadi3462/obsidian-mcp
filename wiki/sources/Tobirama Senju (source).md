# Tobirama Senju (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Tobirama Senju](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tobirama_Senju)
Raw file: `raw/Tobirama Senju.md`

## Summary

Biography of Tobirama Senju, Second Hokage and [[Hashirama Senju]]'s younger brother — the
pragmatic institution-builder whose inventions (the Academy, the Anbu, the Chūnin Exams, the
Shadow Clone Technique, the Impure World Reincarnation, the Flying Thunder God Technique) shape
the entire rest of the series, for better and worse.

## Key takeaways

- **Counterweight to Hashirama**: consistently the realist to his brother's idealism —
  forced Hashirama's Hokage selection to be democratic, insisted other villages pay for
  tailed beasts, and wanted to kill [[Madara Uchiha]] outright rather than negotiate.
- **Origin of Uchiha marginalization**: created the Konoha Military Police Force explicitly
  for the Uchiha clan to run, intending it as a mark of trust — but the clan experienced it as
  a way of sidelining them, seeding the resentment that eventually leads to the Uchiha Clan
  Downfall. Directly relevant to [[Sasuke Uchiha]] and [[Itachi Uchiha]]'s backstory.
- **Teacher of Hiruzen**: trained [[Hiruzen Sarutobi]], Homura Mitokado, and Koharu Utatane as
  Team Tobirama; died in the First Shinobi World War acting as a decoy for Hiruzen's team,
  passing him the Hokage title with his last act.
- **Reincarnation arc**: alongside Hashirama, Minato, and Hiruzen, reincarnated via his own
  invented Impure World Reincarnation technique during the Fourth Shinobi World War to help
  fight Madara and Obito Uchiha; ironically only made possible by the jutsu he outlawed and
  regretted creating.
