# Orochimaru (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Orochimaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Orochimaru)
Raw file: `raw/Orochimaru.md`

## Summary

Exhaustive fan-wiki biography of Orochimaru, one of the three legendary Sannin alongside
[[Tsunade]] and [[Jiraiya]]: his descent from prodigy to human-experimenting missing-nin, his
long antagonism toward [[Sasuke Uchiha]] and Konoha, his eventual reform, and his role in the
*New Era* as a semi-reformed researcher and father to the synthetic human Mitsuki.

## Key takeaways

- Establishes the Sannin's shared origin: Orochimaru, [[Tsunade]], and [[Jiraiya]] trained
  together under Hiruzen Sarutobi (the Third Hokage) and earned the "Sannin" title together
  after surviving Hanzō — the same team that later fractures completely, with Orochimaru as
  the one who defects.
- Explains why he defected: caught performing human experimentation (including the
  Hashirama-DNA experiments that produced [[Yamato]]) by Hiruzen, who couldn't bring himself
  to kill his own student and let him escape.
- Details his long campaign against Konoha: orchestrating the Chūnin Exams invasion (Konoha
  Crush) that kills Hiruzen, mentoring [[Sasuke Uchiha]] as a body-vessel candidate, and being
  absorbed into Sasuke's own body when Sasuke turns the tables on him during the Itachi
  pursuit — a plot beat [[Sasuke Uchiha (source)]] should be cross-checked against.
  Orochimaru is later revived by Sasuke during the war and fights alongside Konoha.
- Confirms his post-war pardon (under 24-hour surveillance) and reform into a legitimate
  researcher: reestablishes Otogakure, creates synthetic humans (Log, Mitsuki) via cloning
  rather than kidnapping, and sends his "son" Mitsuki to live in Konoha under Naruto's
  protection — a striking contrast to his Part I/II characterization.
- This source's coverage ends in the *New Era* (Kara Actuation Arc) and does **not** reach
  the *New Era: Part II* crisis chapters — unlike [[Sai (source)]], it gives no
  indication of Orochimaru's status during Naruto's disappearance.
