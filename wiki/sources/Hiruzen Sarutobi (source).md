# Hiruzen Sarutobi (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Hiruzen Sarutobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hiruzen_Sarutobi)
Raw file: `raw/Hiruzen Sarutobi.md`

## Summary

Biography of Hiruzen Sarutobi, Third Hokage, "The Professor," and teacher of the Sannin
([[Orochimaru]], [[Tsunade]], and Jiraiya). Ties directly into several already-ingested
sources: he's the shared teacher figure in [[Orochimaru (source)]] and [[Tsunade (source)]],
the Hokage who assigned [[Kakashi Hatake]] to Team 7, and the mentor figure behind
[[Iruka Umino]]'s own devotion to teaching.

## Key takeaways

- **Teacher of the Sannin**: trained [[Orochimaru]], [[Tsunade]], and Jiraiya; favored
  Orochimaru as a fellow prodigy, which made Orochimaru's eventual betrayal and defection from
  Konoha (after Hiruzen caught him mid-experiment but couldn't bring himself to strike him
  down) his deepest personal regret.
- **The Uchiha massacre's chain of command**: confirms from his own side the account already
  on [[Sasuke Uchiha]] and [[Itachi Uchiha]] — Danzō Shimura ordered Itachi to slaughter the
  Uchiha clan without Hiruzen's knowledge; Hiruzen only learned afterward, removed Danzō from
  the Konoha Council in response, and personally promised Itachi he'd protect Sasuke.
- **Naruto's early years**: assigned Kakashi to lead Team 7 specifically to mentor Naruto and
  watch over Sasuke; withheld Naruto's parentage to protect him from Minato's enemies; outlawed
  mention of the Nine-Tails to shield Naruto from the village's hatred.
- **Death**: dies during the Konoha Crush/Invasion of Konoha, fighting Orochimaru (his former
  student) and reincarnated versions of [[Hashirama Senju]] and [[Tobirama Senju]]
  simultaneously — seals away all three using variants of the Dead Demon Consuming Seal,
  managing to at least permanently disable Orochimaru's arms before dying.
- **Family**: father of [[Asuma Sarutobi]]; grandfather of Konohamaru Sarutobi (named but not
  yet a dedicated wiki page).
