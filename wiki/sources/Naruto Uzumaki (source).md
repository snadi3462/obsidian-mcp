# Naruto Uzumaki (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki)
Raw file: `raw/Naruto Uzumaki.md`

## Summary

An exhaustive fan-wiki biography of Naruto Uzumaki: background/birth, personality, abilities
(chakra, ninjutsu, senjutsu, jinchūriki transformations), and a chapter-by-chapter plot
history from the Academy through the current *Boruto* era, plus creation notes and trivia.
Far more granular than the Wikipedia character-list entry already in the wiki — this source
is the basis for the expanded [[Naruto Uzumaki]] entity page.

## Key takeaways

- Fills in Naruto's origin in detail: born to [[Minato Namikaze]] (Fourth Hokage) and
  [[Kushina Uzumaki]], both of whom die protecting him and sealing the Nine-Tails
  ([[Kurama]]) inside him as an infant. Raised an orphan, ostracized by the village.
- Confirms and details the [[Team 7]] arc already in the wiki (bell test, Land of Waves,
  Chūnin Exams, Sasuke's defection, Kaguya battle, Valley of the End rematch) with much more
  granularity than the Wikipedia source.
- Documents the courtship and marriage to [[Hinata Hyuga]] in detail (Pain's Assault
  confession, *The Last: Naruto the Movie*, *Konoha Hiden* wedding) — corroborates the
  existing wiki claim that they marry and have two children.
- **Status update / open contradiction**: the existing [[Naruto Uzumaki]] page (built from
  the Wikipedia source) stated simply that Naruto "becomes Seventh Hokage" with an implied
  settled ending. This source's later sections (Kawaki Arc, New Era: Part II, Flashforward)
  reveal a more complicated current status — Naruto loses Kurama permanently after using
  Baryon Mode against Isshiki Ōtsutsuki, and later he and Hinata are trapped unconscious for
  three years in a dimension created by Kawaki/Ada, with Boruto wrongly blamed for his death
  and Konoha itself later destroyed. This is flagged directly on the updated entity page
  rather than silently overwritten, since it's a "current state as of the ongoing story"
  fact rather than a resolved ending.
- Introduces several figures central to Naruto's story not yet in the wiki: [[Kurama]] (the
  Nine-Tails), [[Jiraiya]] (mentor, dies in Pain's Assault), [[Minato Namikaze]],
  [[Kushina Uzumaki]], and [[Kawaki]] (adopted-into-Konoha Kara defector, later central to
  Naruto's disappearance).
- This ingest covers the major arcs and biography in depth; the exhaustive chapter-by-chapter
  Boruto-era plot (Chūnin Re-Examination Arc onward) and the "In Other Media" / full Trivia
  sections were skimmed rather than filed page-by-page — see index.md open threads.
