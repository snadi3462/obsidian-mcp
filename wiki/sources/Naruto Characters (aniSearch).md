# Naruto ➜ Characters (aniSearch)

Source: [aniSearch — Naruto characters](https://www.anisearch.com/anime/2788,naruto/characters)
Raw file: `raw/Naruto ➜ Characters.md`

## Summary

A community-database character roster (secondary + extra characters) with cover images and
profile links. Sparse on prose — mostly a name/image index rather than narrative detail —
but useful as a checklist of characters the Wikipedia article's prose doesn't fully cover
(e.g. Zabuza, Haku, the Sound Four, Hashirama/Tobirama Senju, various minor Konoha ninja).

## Key takeaways

- Confirms roster overlap with the Wikipedia source for Team 8/Team 10/Team Guy members
  (Kurenai, Kiba, Shikamaru, Ino, Rock Lee, Neji, Tenten) and adds names not yet detailed
  in this wiki: Temari, Kankuro, Gaara's siblings' sensei Baki, the toad summons
  (Gamabunta, Gamakichi, Gamatatsu), and [[Kurama]] (Naruto's tailed beast).
- "Extra" section lists antagonists/side characters not covered in the Team 7 ingest pass:
  Zabuza Momochi, Haku, the Sound Four (Kimimaro, Jiroubou, Kidoumaru, Tayuya), and the
  First/Second Hokage (Hashirama and Tobirama Senju).
- This source is a good candidate for cross-checking completeness once entity pages exist —
  it's essentially an index of who else needs a page.
