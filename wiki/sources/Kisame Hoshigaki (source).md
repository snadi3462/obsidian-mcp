# Kisame Hoshigaki (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Kisame Hoshigaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kisame_Hoshigaki)
Raw file: `raw/Kisame Hoshigaki.md`

## Summary

Biography of Kisame Hoshigaki, [[Itachi Uchiha]]'s Akatsuki partner and former Seven Ninja
Swordsmen of the Mist member: a disillusioned killer who finds real, if guarded, friendship
and purpose alongside Itachi, and dies by suicide to protect Akatsuki's secrets from
interrogation.

## Key takeaways

- Deepens [[Itachi Uchiha (source)]]'s brief account of the Itachi/Kisame partnership —
  Kisame's disillusionment after killing his own trusted superior Fuguki (who was secretly a
  traitor) parallels Itachi's own comrade-killing history, the basis of their bond.
- Confirms Kisame's loyalty was ultimately to Itachi personally, not to Akatsuki's goals —
  after Itachi's death, only Obito's manipulation (revealing his identity) keeps Kisame
  engaged with the organization's plans.
- Details his death: cornered during the Fourth Shinobi World War, Kisame chooses assisted
  suicide via a shark-filled water prison rather than risk revealing Akatsuki's secrets under
  interrogation — corroborating his general nihilistic outlook.
