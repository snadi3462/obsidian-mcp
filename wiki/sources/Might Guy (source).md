# Might Guy (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Might Guy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Might_Guy)
Raw file: `raw/Might Guy.md`

## Summary

Exhaustive fan-wiki biography of Might Guy, leader of Team Guy and Kakashi's self-declared
lifelong rival: his father Might Duy's death opening the Eight Gates to save him, his taijutsu
mastery and the Eight Gates, his role backing up [[Team 7]] through Part I and II, and his
near-fatal duel with Madara Uchiha in the Fourth Shinobi World War that ends his ninja career.

## Key takeaways

- Explains the origin of Guy's "Power of Youth" philosophy: his father Might Duy, the
  "Eternal Genin," taught him that hard work matters more than talent, then died opening all
  Eight Gates to save Guy and his teammates from the Seven Ninja Swordsmen of the Mist —
  establishing the same self-sacrificial logic Guy repeats decades later against Madara.
- Details his and [[Kakashi Hatake]]'s decades-long rivalry (eating contests, Rock-Paper-
  Scissors, a running "50 wins to 49" score) which coexists with genuine deep friendship and
  battlefield trust — Guy is one of the people who tries hardest to pull Kakashi out of his
  post-Obito depression.
- Confirms Guy leads [[Team Guy]] (Rock Lee, Neji Hyuga, Tenten) and repeatedly backs up
  [[Team 7]]/Team Kakashi on missions (Kazekage Rescue, Sasuke Recovery).
- Climax: during the Fourth Shinobi World War, Guy opens all Eight Gates (Night Guy) to
  nearly kill Madara Uchiha as the Ten-Tails' jinchūriki — Madara compares his performance to
  the First Hokage. Naruto's newly-acquired Yin–Yang Release saves Guy's life afterward, but
  the attack permanently destroys his right leg, ending his active ninja career; he becomes
  a wheelchair user and an Academy taijutsu teacher in the *New Era*.
- Unlike [[Sai]], [[Yamato]], [[Orochimaru]], and [[Tsunade]], this source does not follow
  Guy into the *New Era: Part II* crisis chapters — his story as covered here ends with him
  attending memorial services and teaching at the Academy, with no confirmed later status.
