# Sakura Haruno (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Sakura Haruno](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sakura_Haruno)
Raw file: `raw/Sakura Haruno.md`

## Summary

Companion biography to [[Naruto Uzumaki (source)]] covering Sakura's full arc: her
ordinary background relative to her teammates, her friendship-then-rivalry with Ino, her
medical-ninjutsu training under Tsunade, her enduring love for Sasuke through his years as a
criminal, and her life post-marriage raising [[Sarada Uchiha]] largely alone while running
Konoha's medical department.

## Key takeaways

- Confirms she marries Sasuke and takes the name **Sakura Uchiha** — the wiki's existing
  pages should be read as referring to the same person under either surname; her page title
  is kept as "Sakura Haruno" for continuity with earlier sources but this should be noted
  wherever her married status matters.
- Details her medical ninjutsu training under Tsunade as the turning point that resolves her
  Part I inferiority complex relative to Naruto and Sasuke; she becomes Head of Konoha's
  Medical Department and a teacher of medical ninjutsu.
- Fills out [[Sarada Uchiha]]'s introduction significantly: raised mostly by Sakura alone
  due to Sasuke's long absences, briefly doubts her own parentage as a child (resolved in
  the *Sarada Uchiha Arc* against Shin Uchiha), later becomes a genin partnered with Boruto.
- **Notable new fact, cross-referenced to [[Kurama]] / [[Naruto Uzumaki]]**: by the
  *New Era: Part II* chapters, Sakura treats an injured Inojin who is fully healed by
  **Himawari Uzumaki**, described as "the new Nine-Tails' jinchūriki." This is the first
  source to state that the Nine-Tails now has a jinchūriki other than Naruto — worth
  reconciling against [[Naruto Uzumaki (source)]]'s account of Kurama's death during
  the Kawaki Arc. See [[Himawari Uzumaki]] and the updated [[Kurama]] page.
