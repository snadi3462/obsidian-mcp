# Neji Hyūga (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Neji Hyūga](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Neji_Hy%C5%ABga)
Raw file: `raw/Neji Hyūga.md`

## Summary

Biography of Neji Hyūga, Team Guy's Byakugan prodigy: his fatalistic belief in an inescapable destiny (rooted in his father's death as a sacrifice for the Hyūga main house), his transformation after losing to [[Naruto Uzumaki]], and his death during the Fourth Shinobi World War protecting [[Hinata Hyuga]] and Naruto.

## Key takeaways

- Explains the source of Neji's fatalism: his father Hizashi died as a sacrificial body-double to protect his twin brother Hiashi (Hinata's father) during a diplomatic crisis — a sacrifice Neji long misunderstood as coerced servitude rather than a choice, souring his view of the Hyūga main house and of "destiny" generally.
- Details his defeat by Naruto in the Chūnin Exams finals as the turning point that reframes his fatalism into self-determination — the same beat already covered from Naruto's side on [[Naruto Uzumaki]]'s page.
- **Confirms Neji's death**: during the Fourth Shinobi World War's climax, Neji dies shielding both Hinata and Naruto from the Ten-Tails, telling Naruto with his last words that he understands now why his father chose to die protecting someone. His death directly motivates the Allied Forces' following counterattack.
- His *New Era* appearance is limited to a Time Slip Arc cameo (already deceased in the "present," this is a past-timeline appearance) and a Legacy section — Hinata visits his grave with [[Himawari Uzumaki]], who calls him "uncle" despite never meeting him. Confirms his death as permanent and settled, unlike the ambiguous statuses on other current-era pages.
