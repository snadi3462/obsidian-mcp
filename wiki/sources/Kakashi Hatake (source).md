# Kakashi Hatake (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Kakashi Hatake](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi_Hatake)
Raw file: `raw/Kakashi Hatake.md`

## Summary

Companion biography to [[Naruto Uzumaki (source)]] covering Kakashi's full arc: his
childhood under his father Sakumo, Team Minato and the Third Shinobi World War (the mission
where he receives Obito's Sharingan), his eventual leadership of Team 7, and his post-war
tenure as Sixth Hokage through his retirement into an advisory role in the current era.

## Key takeaways

- Explains the origin of Kakashi's rigid rule-following: his father Sakumo, a celebrated
  shinobi, was disgraced and driven to suicide after breaking Shinobi Rules to save his
  teammates on a mission — Kakashi resolved never to repeat that "failure," which is why he
  initially prioritizes missions over teammates as a child, in direct tension with his later
  teaching that teamwork matters most.
- Details the Kannabi Bridge mission that defines Kakashi's adolescence: [[Obito Uchiha]]
  sacrifices his life (or so it seems at the time) to save Kakashi, giving him his Sharingan
  eye as a parting gift after Kakashi loses his own. [[Rin Nohara]], the third member of
  Team Minato, is rescued in the same mission.
- Confirms and extends what [[Naruto Uzumaki (source)]] already established: Kakashi
  becomes Sixth Hokage after the war, later passes the mantle to Naruto, and — even in
  retirement — continues investigating threats to the village (e.g. tracking Koji Kashin
  during the Kawaki Arc), keeping him active and relevant through the current storyline
  rather than fully stepping back.
- Introduces [[Obito Uchiha]] and [[Rin Nohara]] as full entities, both central to Kakashi's
  backstory and previously only mentioned in passing on his page.
