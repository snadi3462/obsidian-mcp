# Konan

Amegakure kunoichi, [[Akatsuki]] co-founder alongside [[Yahiko]] and [[Nagato]]; Nagato's
steadfast partner and, after his death, de facto leader of Amegakure. **Deceased** — killed
by Obito Uchiha.

## Background

Orphaned in the Second Shinobi World War, Konan found and bonded with fellow orphan [[Yahiko]]
(with whom she shared reciprocated romantic feelings), and the two later found and rescued a
dying [[Nagato]]. Trained by [[Jiraiya]] alongside them, she became [[Akatsuki]]'s only
consistently loyal, level-headed member — the "angel" to Nagato/Pain's "god" in Amegakure's
public mythology.

## Personality

Smart, stoic, and calm — a contrast to Yahiko's brashness and Nagato's early sensitivity —
Konan places her teammates' safety above her own without hesitation and remains the one
person capable of reading Nagato's true emotions beneath his god persona. After Nagato's
death, she becomes fiercely protective of both his legacy and [[Naruto Uzumaki]], the person
he entrusted the mission of world peace to, declaring Amegakure's loyalty to him.

## Abilities

A paper-ninjutsu master (Dance of the Shikigami): transforms her entire body into controllable
sheets of paper, usable as weapons, clones, explosives, or wings for flight — durable enough
to survive direct hits from [[Jiraiya]]'s fire techniques.

## Key arcs and death

Fights [[Jiraiya]] alongside Nagato during his infiltration of Amegakure (a battle that ends
in his death). After Nagato's own death, formally defects from Akatsuki, taking his and
Yahiko's remains back to Amegakure for a shrine. During the Fourth Shinobi World War, Konan
nearly kills Obito Uchiha (Tobi) in a devastating paper-and-explosives trap, but he survives
via the forbidden Izanagi technique and kills her in return — her last act is affirming that
Naruto embodies the "flower of hope" she, Yahiko, and Nagato had all believed in.
