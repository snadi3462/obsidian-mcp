# Itachi Uchiha

Elder brother of [[Sasuke Uchiha]]; Uchiha clan prodigy, former Anbu Captain, and secret
Konoha double agent inside [[Akatsuki]]. **Deceased** — died of terminal illness during his
duel with Sasuke, having allowed it.

## Background and personality

A war-traumatized pacifist from early childhood, Itachi trained obsessively not out of
bloodlust but to "erase fighting from the world." A genuine prodigy — youngest post-war
Academy graduate, chūnin at 10, Anbu at 11, the youngest-ever Anbu Captain at 12 — his talent
was recognized even by Orochimaru and by Shisui, the "strongest Uchiha of his generation,"
both of whom rated Itachi above themselves. Socially awkward and inclined to observe rather
than engage, Itachi carried real, deep love for his family and for Konoha, and bore no grudge
against [[Naruto Uzumaki]], recognizing him as the true victim of the Nine-Tails' attack.

Became a double agent inside the Uchiha clan's coup conspiracy, reporting to the Third Hokage
and the Konoha Council in hopes of a peaceful resolution. When his best friend Shisui died
attempting (and failing) to use his Kotoamatsukami to force a bloodless resolution, and the
coup proved unstoppable, Danzō Shimura gave Itachi an impossible choice: let the coup proceed
and have the entire clan — including Sasuke — killed in the resulting war with Konoha, or
personally exterminate the clan and be allowed to spare Sasuke. Itachi chose his brother.

## The Uchiha Clan Massacre

With help from "Madara" (secretly Obito Uchiha, whom Itachi recruited on the condition that
Konoha itself be spared), Itachi killed the entire Uchiha clan in a single night, personally
executing his own parents — who, despite his betrayal, told him with their dying breath that
they were proud of him. To give Sasuke both motive to live and a controlled path toward
strength, Itachi staged himself as a hateable villain: using Tsukuyomi to torment Sasuke with
visions of the massacre, denying he ever loved him, and explicitly directing Sasuke to hate
him and grow strong enough for revenge, even suggesting he acquire his own Mangekyō Sharingan
to do it. He then joined Akatsuki as a secret double agent — his true mission was to keep the
organization from moving against Konoha, not to serve its goals.

## Life in Akatsuki

Partnered first with Jūzō Biwa (killed in the line of duty), then with Kisame Hoshigaki, with
whom Itachi formed a genuine, if guarded, friendship — Kisame always deferred to Itachi's
judgment despite his own greater taste for violence. Itachi contracted a terminal illness at
some point during this period and kept himself alive through medicine and sheer will,
specifically so he could survive long enough to die by Sasuke's hand as planned. See
[[Akatsuki (source)]] for the organization's full structure and Itachi's place in it.

## Death

Having confirmed through their reunion that Sasuke had grown strong enough, Itachi let him
win their final battle at an Akatsuki hideout — even sealing away a briefly-summoned
[[Orochimaru]] with his Susanoo's Sword of Totsuka along the way, removing Sasuke's cursed
seal as a side effect. Dying of his illness rather than any wound Sasuke dealt him, Itachi's
last act was to gently poke Sasuke's forehead (an affectionate childhood gesture, not the
threatened eye-taking) — secretly sealing Amaterasu into Sasuke's eyes as a failsafe against
Tobi. He died smiling, having genuinely apologized to Sasuke at the very end.

## Reincarnation and reconciliation

Revived against his will via Kabuto Yakushi's Impure World Reincarnation during the Fourth
Shinobi World War, Itachi fights alongside [[Naruto Uzumaki]] and Killer B against a
reincarnated Nagato — freeing himself from Kabuto's control using the very crow/Kotoamatsukami
failsafe he'd planted in Naruto years earlier. Reunited with a now-revived Sasuke, Itachi is
finally fully honest with him: admitting his manipulation, sharing his true memories, and
telling Sasuke he wants no forgiveness and will love him regardless of what he chooses to do.
His reincarnated body dissolves and his soul returns to the afterlife.

## Legacy

Itachi's plan to make Sasuke a hero backfires in the short term (Tobi redirects Sasuke's
manufactured hatred toward Konoha itself), but the underlying protection — his eyes, his
final honesty, and his crow failsafe — ultimately helps guide Sasuke back from the brink.
Sasuke later adopts Itachi's forehead-poke as his own affectionate habit with [[Sakura
Haruno]] and [[Sarada Uchiha]] — see [[Sasuke Uchiha]] for how this legacy plays out in his
adult life. [[Naruto Uzumaki]] posthumously restores some of Itachi's public honor by
crediting him (without revealing the full truth) as someone who supported him.
