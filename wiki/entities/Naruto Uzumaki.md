# Naruto Uzumaki

Titular protagonist of the *Naruto* franchise. Member of [[Team 7]]. Son of
[[Minato Namikaze]] and [[Kushina Uzumaki]]; jinchūriki of [[Kurama]]; husband of
[[Hinata Hyuga]]; father of Boruto and Himawari Uzumaki.

## Background

Born the night his parents sealed half of Kurama's chakra into him and gave their lives
doing it — Minato split the fox's chakra and sacrificed his soul to seal it, while Kushina
died from the extraction/resealing ordeal. Orphaned and raised without knowing who his
parents were ([[Hiruzen Sarutobi]] withheld it to protect him from Minato's enemies), Naruto
grew up shunned by most of Konoha, who resented him as the container of the beast that
devastated the village. This isolation drove both his early prankster behavior and his
lifelong craving for acknowledgement, which crystallized into his defining goal: becoming
Hokage. [[Iruka Umino]], his Academy instructor, becomes the first adult to treat him with
real, unconditional care — a bond significant enough that Naruto later asks Iruka to stand in
as his father at his own wedding (see Marriage and family, below).

## Personality

Boisterous, naive, and slow with abstract explanations but observant in ways others miss.
His nindō ("never go back on his word") is the throughline of his character — a
self-confidence built on hard work and perseverance rather than natural talent. Carries a
literal inner darkness ("Dark Naruto," the personification of his suppressed grudge against
the village) which he resolves not by rejecting but by accepting as part of what shaped him.
After Jiraiya's death and Nagato's story, his motivation shifts from personal acknowledgement
to breaking the world's cycle of hatred — a goal that drives his conduct through the Fourth
Shinobi World War and into his time as Hokage.

## Abilities (summary)

- **Shadow Clone Technique** — his first trademark technique; unusually large chakra
  reserves let him field hundreds of clones, which he also uses to accelerate training (each
  clone's experience transfers back to the original on dispersal).
- **Rasengan** — his second trademark technique, learned from [[Jiraiya]]; later combined
  with wind-nature chakra (something even Minato and Kakashi couldn't do) to create the
  Rasenshuriken, and developed into many larger/combined variants.
- **Sage Mode / Senjutsu** — trained at Mount Myōboku; unlike Jiraiya, achieves a complete,
  balanced Sage Mode.
- **Jinchūriki transformations** — progresses from involuntary, anger-triggered access to
  [[Kurama]]'s chakra (Version 1/2) to a full cooperative partnership with Kurama (Nine-Tails
  Chakra Mode / Kurama Mode) after learning the fox's true name, then Tailed Beast Mode,
  Six Paths Sage Mode (granted by Hagoromo Ōtsutsuki), and ultimately Baryon Mode — a
  last-resort fusion of his and Kurama's chakra that is fatal to Kurama with repeated use.

## Part I & II — key arcs

- **Team 7 formation**: assigned to [[Team 7]] with Sasuke and Sakura under Kakashi; passes
  Kakashi's bell test through teamwork.
- **Land of Waves**: first involuntary access to Kurama's chakra, defending Sasuke against
  Haku.
- **Chūnin Exams**: defeats Kiba, loses to Neji in the preliminaries' aftermath but wins
  their finals match; refuses to cheat on the written exam even when Hinata offers to help.
- **Sasuke Recovery arc**: fails to bring Sasuke back after his defection to Orochimaru;
  this failure defines much of Part I's ending and Part II's throughline.
- **Pain's Assault**: after Jiraiya's death, masters Sage Mode and defeats all Six Paths of
  Pain; Hinata confesses her love for him and is nearly killed protecting him, triggering his
  six-tailed transformation. Chooses not to kill Nagato, is acknowledged as a hero by the
  village afterward.
- **Fourth Shinobi World War**: reunites with the tailed beasts' collective chakra, fights
  alongside Sasuke against Madara, Obito, and Kaguya Ōtsutsuki; Obito's dying wish is for
  Naruto to become Hokage.
- **Valley of the End rematch**: after Kaguya's defeat, Naruto and Sasuke fight one final,
  near-fatal battle over their conflicting visions for the village; both lose an arm. Sasuke
  finally acknowledges Naruto as necessary to him, ending their rivalry.

## Marriage and family

Naruto's feelings for [[Hinata Hyuga]] develop gradually — from finding her "weird" as a
child to recognizing his love for her during *The Last: Naruto the Movie*, when he rescues
her from Toneri Ōtsutsuki. They marry in *Konoha Hiden: The Perfect Day for a Wedding*
(Naruto, orphaned, asks [[Iruka Umino]] to stand in as his father). Their children are Boruto and
Himawari Uzumaki — named in tribute to Neji Hyuga, who died protecting Naruto and Hinata
during the war (Boruto = "bolt," echoing Neji's name meaning "screw"; Himawari = sunflower,
Neji's favorite flower).

## Becoming Hokage

Naruto achieves his lifelong goal, becoming the Seventh Hokage, succeeding Kakashi (who
had succeeded Tsunade). As Hokage he is noted to struggle balancing village duties with
family time, eventually finding better balance after a confrontation with Momoshiki
Ōtsutsuki makes him recognize the cost to his family.

## Later status — open thread / unresolved

**This updates the earlier version of this page**, which described Naruto's story as
settling into "becomes Hokage, marries Hinata" — accurate but incomplete. Per this source's
later (*Boruto*-era) chapters:

- During the Kawaki Arc, Naruto permanently loses [[Kurama]] after using Baryon Mode to
  defeat Isshiki Ōtsutsuki — Baryon Mode is fatal to the tailed beast fused into it, and
  Kurama's death measurably reduces Naruto's fighting ability going forward.
- In *New Era: Part II*, Naruto and Hinata are trapped, unconscious, for three years inside
  a dimension created through Kawaki and Ada's actions. Boruto is wrongly blamed for
  Naruto's apparent death, with the truth not made public outside Konoha.
- The **Flashforward** section describes Konoha's destruction four years after an Ōtsutsuki
  attack, with Kawaki telling Boruto he will send him to "where he sent the Seventh Hokage" —
  implying Naruto's ultimate fate is still open at the point this source was written.

This is a live contradiction with any wiki content that treats "Naruto becomes Hokage" as a
final, stable resting point — later sources on the *Boruto* storyline should be checked
against this before treating his status as settled.

**Update from [[Ino Yamanaka (source)]]** (Boruto's Return Arc, later in *New Era: Part
II* than the sources above): Boruto tells Shikamaru Nara directly that **Naruto and Hinata
are alive**, not confirmed dead — the clearest statement yet on their status, though still
short of describing their actual whereabouts or condition. Shikamaru (now confirmed acting
Hokage) believes this account privately but cannot act on it publicly; see [[Team 7]]
"Current crisis" and [[Shikamaru Nara]] for the surrounding political situation.

Two further data points from [[Sasuke Uchiha (source)]] and [[Sakura Haruno (source)]]
sharpen this picture rather than resolve it:

- [[Sasuke Uchiha]] is independently incapacitated around the same period, drained by a
  Code-created parasite while protecting Boruto — the entire surviving core of [[Team 7]]
  is in crisis simultaneously by *New Era: Part II*. See [[Team 7]] for the combined view.
- [[Himawari Uzumaki]] (Naruto's own daughter) is later described as "the new Nine-Tails'
  jinchūriki," which doesn't obviously square with Kurama's death here — see [[Kurama]].

## Trivia

- His name is a pun on *narutomaki*, the spiral fish-cake topping used in ramen (his
  favorite food); "Uzumaki" means whirlpool/maelstrom.
- Modeled on Dragon Ball's Son Goku; Kishimoto deliberately kept him "simple and stupid"
  since he dislikes overly clever protagonists.
- Naruto is the fourth known jinchūriki to survive a tailed beast's extraction (alongside
  Hagoromo Ōtsutsuki, Kushina Uzumaki, and Obito Uchiha).
