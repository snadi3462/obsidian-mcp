# Chōji Akimichi

Akimichi clan heir and [[Team 10]]'s heavy hitter — kind-hearted, self-doubting, and fiercely loyal to Shikamaru Nara.

## Background and personality

Bullied as a child for his weight until Shikamaru Nara defended him and the two became inseparable best friends over cloud-watching and shared snacks — the foundation of Chōji's entire character. Sensitive about his weight (insults trigger him into action faster than almost anything else) but otherwise kind, polite, and chronically low on self-confidence, a trait both his father Chōza and sensei [[Asuma Sarutobi]] identify as his real weakness, believing his kindness is actually his greatest strength.

## Abilities

Converts consumed calories into chakra via his clan's Multi-Size Technique, enabling the body-expanding Human Bullet Tank and, at greatest need, the fatal Three Coloured Pills that grant temporary chakra butterfly wings — a technique he later masters safely without the pills by the Fourth Shinobi World War.

## Key arcs

- **Sasuke Recovery Mission**: takes all three Three Coloured Pills to kill Jirōbō after he mocks Shikamaru, nearly dying from the toll; saved by Nara clan medical research and [[Tsunade]]'s expertise.
- **Fourth Shinobi World War**: initially can't bring himself to fight his reincarnated sensei [[Asuma Sarutobi]], but finds the resolve to defeat him after his father shields him from an attack — Asuma's dying words years earlier (calling Chōji's kindness his true strength) come full circle here.
- **New Era**: marries Karui and has a daughter, Chōchō, whom he trains in the clan's techniques — corroborated by [[Asuma Sarutobi (source)]]'s legacy section and [[Ino Yamanaka (source)]].

## Status

Reaches the Kawaki Arc (fighting alongside Konoha's jōnin against Isshiki) but the ingested source does not continue into the *New Era: Part II* crisis chapters.
