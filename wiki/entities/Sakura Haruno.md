# Sakura Haruno

Member of [[Team 7]], [[Naruto Uzumaki]]'s classmate, and [[Sasuke Uchiha]]'s wife (married
name **Sakura Uchiha** — this page keeps the "Haruno" title for continuity with earlier
sources). Mother of [[Sarada Uchiha]].

## Background and personality

Unlike her teammates, Sakura had an ordinary upbringing with no great tragedy or legacy. As
a child she was bullied for her large forehead until classmate Ino Yamanaka defended her and
became her first friend; the two later became rivals over Sasuke, ending their friendship
so they could "compete" for him. Internally, Sakura projected emotions she didn't want to
show outwardly onto a separate persona nicknamed "Inner Sakura" — a coping mechanism that
faded once she grew genuinely more self-assured during her training under Tsunade.

Her Part I arc is defined by inadequacy: unlike Naruto and Sasuke she couldn't contribute
much in battle and repeatedly needed rescuing, which drove her to dedicate years to closing
the gap. Kishimoto has said he had little fixed conception of an "ideal heroine" when
designing her and leaned on her energy and infatuation with Sasuke as her defining early
traits — but the character grows well past that over the series.

## Medical ninjutsu

Training under Tsunade is the turning point: Sakura becomes one of the world's greatest
medical-nin, inheriting Tsunade's unbending will and self-sacrificial streak (she'll place
herself at risk so others don't have to). This training resolves her earlier insecurity and
is the basis of her adult career — she later becomes Head of Konoha's Medical Department and
a teacher of medical ninjutsu.

## Love for Sasuke

Sakura's feelings for Sasuke are constant from childhood through his years as an
international criminal — she confesses to try to stop his defection, is rejected but
genuinely thanked, and afterward dedicates herself to getting strong enough to help bring
him back. Her love survives his darkest period (including a moment she considers killing
him herself to avert a war) and is validated after his reconciliation with Naruto, when
Sasuke shows her a rare softer side.

## Marriage and family

Marries Sasuke and becomes deeply loyal to him despite his frequent long absences on
Ōtsutsuki-related missions — she raises their daughter [[Sarada Uchiha]] largely alone.
In the *Sarada Uchiha Arc*, Sarada's doubts about her parentage (fueled by Sakura's
reluctance to discuss Sasuke's dark past) come to a head; Sakura resolves it by rescuing her
family from Shin Uchiha and confirming their biological bond. The family remains fond but
unconventional — Sasuke periodically returns for brief visits (a family photo, a shared meal
on the village's "Parent and Child Day") before leaving again.

## Current status

Still active as a senior medical figure in the *New Era: Part II* chapters — treating
Inojin and Moegi after Human God Tree attacks, and supporting Sarada through the strain of
Mangekyō Sharingan overuse (deteriorating eyesight) as Sarada throws herself into helping
Boruto, echoing Sakura's own younger devotion to Sasuke.
