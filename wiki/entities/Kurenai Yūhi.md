# Kurenai Yūhi

[[Team 8]]'s jōnin sensei, later Kurenai Sarutobi — Konoha's foremost genjutsu master and [[Asuma Sarutobi]]'s partner.

## Background and personality

Childhood friends with [[Asuma Sarutobi]] since their Academy entrance ceremony, the two grew into a long, quietly acknowledged romantic partnership that the narrative treats as an open secret among their peers. Caring and protective of her students — especially Hinata, whom she takes on knowing full well how the Hyūga main house undervalued her — Kurenai is honest and unyielding, but capable of deception when protecting someone: she lies about her reasons for sealing student Yakumo Kurama's dangerous, uncontrollable kekkei genkai (Shino sees through the lie, though the true motive is left ambiguous).

## Key facts

- Becomes pregnant with Asuma's child shortly before his death fighting Hidan; gives birth to their daughter, Mirai Sarutobi, and raises her alone.
- Retires from active shinobi duty after the Fourth Shinobi World War to be a full-time mother — the only one of the four principal Part-I sensei (with [[Might Guy]], [[Kakashi Hatake]], and herself) not defined by a Hokage-adjacent role in later life.
- Remains a recurring, trusted advisor to her former students as adults — Shino turns to her for teaching advice in the *New Era*.

## Abilities

A genjutsu prodigy rivaling even Itachi Uchiha, specializing in flora-based illusions (Demonic Illusion: Tree Binding Death) capable of affecting multiple targets simultaneously.

## Status

Reaches the *New Era* (Chūnin Re-Examination Arc, watching over an injured Mirai) but the ingested source does not continue into the *New Era: Part II* crisis chapters.
