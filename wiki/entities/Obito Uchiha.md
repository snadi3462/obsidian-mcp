# Obito Uchiha

Childhood teammate of [[Kakashi Hatake]] and [[Rin Nohara]] on Team Minato, under
[[Minato Namikaze]].

## Overview

Believed teamwork and loyalty to comrades matter more than rigidly following mission
orders — a philosophy at odds with young Kakashi's, and one that Kakashi eventually adopts
as his own defining belief after Obito's sacrifice. During the Third Shinobi World War's
Kannabi Bridge mission, Obito is fatally crushed by a cave-in while saving Kakashi, and
gives Kakashi his own Sharingan eye as a final gift before apparently dying.

## Rescue and manipulation by Madara

Per [[Madara Uchiha (source)]], Obito's "death" is not the end of the story: [[Madara
Uchiha]] finds him badly injured, rebuilds his body with Hashirama Senju's cells, and
deliberately deepens Obito's despair by orchestrating [[Rin Nohara]]'s death at Kakashi's own
hands. A broken Obito offers his services to Madara, who spends his final years grooming him
as an heir — teaching him of the Sage of Six Paths, the Ten-Tails, and the Eye of the Moon
Plan, and, with his dying breath, handing Obito his own name and identity as "Madara Uchiha."
[[Zetsu (source)]] adds that White Zetsu personally cared for and befriended Obito during
this rehabilitation period, becoming a genuine (if strange) friendship.

## Later revelation

Obito is later revealed to have survived, becoming the masked man ("Tobi") behind much of
[[Akatsuki]]'s scheming and the mastermind who orchestrated Kurama's attack on Konoha the
night [[Naruto Uzumaki]] was born — carrying out the plan Madara entrusted to him, unaware
(as Madara himself was) that Black Zetsu had been manipulating the entire chain toward
reviving Kaguya Ōtsutsuki. He eventually returns to his original values, fighting alongside
[[Team 7]] against Kaguya Ōtsutsuki in the Fourth Shinobi World War, where he sacrifices
himself again — this time permanently — shielding Kakashi. His dying wish is for Naruto to
become Hokage.
