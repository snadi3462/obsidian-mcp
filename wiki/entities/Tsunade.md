# Tsunade

Fifth Hokage, world's strongest kunoichi and greatest medical-nin, and one of the legendary
Sannin alongside [[Orochimaru]] and [[Jiraiya]]. Granddaughter of the First Hokage,
[[Hashirama Senju]]. Mentor of [[Sakura Haruno]]. Predecessor of [[Kakashi Hatake]] as Hokage.

## Background

Trained under [[Hiruzen Sarutobi]] alongside [[Orochimaru]] and [[Jiraiya]]; the three became the
legendary Sannin after surviving Hanzō. Tsunade's life is defined by loss: her younger
brother Nawaki and her lover Dan Katō both died pursuing their shared dream of becoming
Hokage, and her failure to save Dan from fatal blood loss left her with lasting hemophobia.
Convinced that dreaming — and the Hokage title specifically — only leads to pointless death
(citing her own grandfather, great-uncle, and teacher, all of whom died as Hokage), Tsunade
abandoned shinobi life for years, taking Dan's niece Shizune with her as a disciple.

## Personality

Blunt, short-tempered, and openly fond of drinking and (notoriously unlucky) gambling — she's
nicknamed "The Legendary Sucker" for how badly and often she loses. Beneath the vices is a
fierce, protective faith in others' potential: she becomes furious at anyone (including the
Konoha Council) who doubts a shinobi's ability to succeed, believing it's better to let people
try and fail than to prevent them from trying at all. [[Naruto Uzumaki]] is the pivotal
figure who restores her belief in dreams — his effort mirrors Nawaki and Dan so closely that
she eventually overcomes her hemophobia entirely to protect him.

## Medical mastery

Widely regarded as the world's greatest medical-nin: her Creation Rebirth technique offers
near-total self-healing at the cost of her own lifespan, and she personally set up Konoha's
modern medical-nin system. Trains [[Sakura Haruno]] (recognizing in her the same protective
drive that defined Nawaki and Dan) into one of the era's top medical-nin — see
[[Sakura Haruno (source)]] for that relationship from Sakura's side.

## Key arcs

- **Search for Tsunade**: [[Orochimaru]] offers to heal his damaged arms and revive Nawaki
  and Dan in exchange for her medical help; she nearly kills him instead, then accepts the
  Fifth Hokage title after her resolve to protect Naruto overcomes her fear of blood.
- **Tenchi Bridge Reconnaissance Mission**: assigns [[Yamato]] to lead the reformed Team 7
  (specifically to suppress Naruto's Nine-Tails chakra) and accepts Danzō's demand to place
  [[Sai]] on the team as his informant — corroborated by both [[Yamato (source)]] and
  [[Sai (source)]].
- **Pain's Assault**: transfers all her chakra through Katsuyu to shield the entire village
  remotely, then falls into a coma after confronting the Six Paths of Pain directly.
- **Fourth Shinobi World War**: coordinates Allied Shinobi Forces strategy from headquarters
  and the front lines; succeeded as Hokage after the war by [[Kakashi Hatake]].

## Status

The ingested source's coverage ends in the *New Era* (Kawaki Arc, aiding Shikamaru Nara's
coordination against Isshiki Ōtsutsuki) and does not reach the *New Era: Part II* crisis
chapters covered in [[Sai (source)]] — her status during [[Naruto Uzumaki]]'s
disappearance is unconfirmed, flagged as an open thread.
