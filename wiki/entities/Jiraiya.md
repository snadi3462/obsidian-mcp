# Jiraiya

One of the three Sannin; [[Naruto Uzumaki]]'s godfather and mentor.

## Overview

Godfather to Naruto by virtue of being the author of the book Naruto was named after
(*The Tale of the Utterly Gutsy Shinobi*, whose protagonist Naruto Musasabi lends Naruto his
name). Trains Naruto directly after the Chūnin Exams, teaching him to harness [[Kurama]]'s
chakra via summoning and eventually the Rasengan. Unlike Naruto, Jiraiya was never able to
achieve a fully balanced Sage Mode.

## Death

Killed by Pain (Nagato), leader of Akatsuki, prior to Pain's Assault on Konoha. His death
is the emotional catalyst for Naruto's Sage Mode training at Mount Myōboku and his eventual
confrontation with Pain — Naruto explicitly frames his fight with Pain as finishing what
Jiraiya started. Naruto later builds a memorial for him.
