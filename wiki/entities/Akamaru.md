# Akamaru

[[Kiba Inuzuka]]'s ninken partner and a member of [[Team 8]] in his own right.

## Background and personality

Given to Kiba as a puppy by Kiba's mother Tsume, Akamaru and Kiba became inseparable almost immediately. Active and devoted, with a wry, faintly scientific way of thinking about humans (per his one dedicated point-of-view story, *Rai no Sho*) — he finds it odd that his friends keep "settling down" instead of focusing on being shinobi, and is overprotective of Kiba's relationship with Tamaki as a result.

## Abilities

Heightened senses (especially smell) that let him judge an enemy's strength by scent alone. In combat, transforms into a feral duplicate of Kiba via the Beast Human Clone technique to perform the collaboration techniques that define the pair's fighting style — see [[Kiba Inuzuka]] for the shared technique list.

## Key facts

- Grows from a small puppy in Part I to a full-sized adult dog Kiba rides in Part II.
- Carries an exhausted Neji Hyuga during the Fourth Shinobi World War and receives a direct chakra transfer from [[Naruto Uzumaki]] in the war's climax.
- By the *New Era* he's elderly, mostly naps, and has offspring of his own.

## Status

Like [[Kiba Inuzuka]], the ingested source's coverage ends at the Kawaki Arc — his status during the *New Era: Part II* crisis is unconfirmed.
