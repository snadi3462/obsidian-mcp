# Minato Namikaze

The Fourth Hokage. Father of [[Naruto Uzumaki]], husband of [[Kushina Uzumaki]].

## Overview

Known as the "Yellow Flash." When Tobi attacked Konoha the night of Naruto's birth to seize
the newborn jinchūriki, Minato rescued Naruto, then fought and freed Kurama from Tobi's
control. Facing a fox too powerful to seal into an infant safely, Minato split its chakra in
half — sealing the Yin half in himself and sacrificing his soul to seal the Yang half in
Naruto — before dying alongside Kushina from their wounds. He left a fragment of his chakra
within the sealing to intervene once more, later reappearing to Naruto during Pain's Assault
to repair the seal and offer encouragement. Naruto strongly resembles him physically and
inherited his habit of relying on jutsu over hand seals.
