# Rin Nohara

Third member of Team Minato alongside [[Kakashi Hatake]] and [[Obito Uchiha]], under
[[Minato Namikaze]].

## Overview

Captured by Iwagakure ninja during the Kannabi Bridge mission in the Third Shinobi World
War; her rescue by Kakashi and Obito is the mission during which Obito is fatally crushed
saving Kakashi and gives him his Sharingan eye. Kakashi is later, elsewhere in his history,
described as having accidentally killed Rin — a loss that compounds his personal history of
failing to protect teammates (alongside his father Sakumo's death and Obito's apparent
death). The circumstances of that later death aren't detailed in sources ingested so far.
