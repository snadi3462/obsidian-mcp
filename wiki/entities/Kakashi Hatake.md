# Kakashi Hatake

Easygoing, perpetually-late leader of [[Team 7]] ([[Naruto Uzumaki]], [[Sasuke Uchiha]],
[[Sakura Haruno]]).

## Background

Raised by his father Sakumo after his mother's early death. Sakumo was a celebrated shinobi
who, on a mission, chose to save his teammates' lives over completing the objective; Konoha
(including the very teammates he saved) vilified him for it, and he later died by suicide.
Kakashi resolved never to repeat what he saw as his father's mistake, becoming a rigid
follower of the Shinobi Rules — a trait that put him at odds with his own teammate
[[Obito Uchiha]], who believed abandoning comrades was worse than abandoning a mission.

A prodigy who graduated the Academy at 5 and made jōnin at 12, Kakashi was placed on
Team Minato with [[Obito Uchiha]] and [[Rin Nohara]] under [[Minato Namikaze]]. During the
Third Shinobi World War's Kannabi Bridge mission, Kakashi initially chose to abandon a
captured Rin to complete the mission — until Obito's rebuke ("it's worse to abandon your
teammates") changed his mind. Obito is fatally crushed saving Kakashi from a cave-in shortly
after, giving Kakashi his own Sharingan eye as a final gift (Obito is later revealed to have
survived). This mission is the origin of both Kakashi's Sharingan and his defining belief —
passed on to Team 7 — that teamwork matters more than any individual rule.

Kakashi's later personal losses compound this history: his mentor Minato Namikaze sacrificed
himself, he accidentally killed teammate Rin Nohara, and Obito was believed dead for years
before being revealed alive as the masked antagonist behind Akatsuki's plans.

## Overview

This past shapes his role as a mentor figure — he is described as pivotal to Naruto's
development as a ninja, deliberately structuring Team 7's bell test around the same
teamwork lesson Minato once taught him.

## Becoming and stepping back from Hokage

Confirmed by [[Naruto Uzumaki (source)]] and detailed further here: Kakashi accepts the
Hokage position roughly a year after the war ends (Naruto needled him about it beforehand,
unaware Tsunade was listening). He later pardons Sasuke alongside Naruto and officiates as a
senior figure through the Blank Period, attending Naruto and Hinata's wedding. He eventually
passes the mantle of Hokage to Naruto and retires from active duty — but continues assisting
village matters and advising his successor, remaining an active investigator well into the
current *Boruto*-era storyline (e.g. tracking the Kara operative Koji Kashin during the
Kawaki Arc).

## Key facts

- Voiced by Kazuhiko Inoue (Japanese), Dave Wittenberg (English).
- Succeeds [[Tsunade]] as Sixth Hokage after the Fourth Great Ninja War; Naruto succeeds him
  as Seventh Hokage years later — Kakashi is a direct link in the Hokage succession line.
