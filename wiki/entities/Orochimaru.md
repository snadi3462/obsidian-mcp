# Orochimaru

One of Konoha's legendary Sannin, alongside [[Tsunade]] and [[Jiraiya]]. Former missing-nin
and human experimenter; [[Sasuke Uchiha]]'s onetime mentor; later a semi-reformed researcher
and father (via cloning) to Mitsuki.

## Background

Orphaned young, Orochimaru trained under [[Hiruzen Sarutobi]] (the Third Hokage) alongside
[[Tsunade]] and [[Jiraiya]] — his talent, insight, and determination marked him as a
once-in-a-generation prodigy, more so than either teammate; Hiruzen favored him above his other
students, seeing a genius comparable to himself. The three earned the "Sannin" title together
after surviving an encounter with Hanzō during the Second Shinobi World War. Driven by a
life-ambition to learn every secret of the shinobi world and achieve immortality (a fixation
Jiraiya attributes to his parents' early deaths), Orochimaru was eventually caught by Hiruzen
performing unethical human experimentation — including the Hashirama-DNA experiments on
kidnapped children that produced [[Yamato]] — and defected rather than face justice, aided by
Hiruzen's inability to kill his own student. Per [[Hiruzen Sarutobi (source)]], Hiruzen
considered failing to stop Orochimaru his deepest personal regret as Hokage.

## Personality

Widely described as "twisted": charismatic enough to gather devoted followers he'll
nonetheless send on suicide missions without real attachment, treacherous by nature
(assassinating and impersonating the Fourth Kazekage), and possessed of a near-messianic god
complex about his own worth. A perfectionist who values genuine development in his subjects
and experiments over blind loyalty — genuinely impressed rather than threatened by
[[Sasuke Uchiha]] and [[Naruto Uzumaki]]'s growth even when it works against him. His
post-war characterization softens considerably: he comes to value the "natural development"
of people (particularly his synthetic son, Mitsuki) over control and manipulation, though he
still uses deception when it serves that goal.

## Key arcs

- **Chūnin Exams / Konoha Crush**: brands [[Sasuke Uchiha]] with the Cursed Seal of Heaven
  as part of a long-term plan to take his body; murders the Fourth Kazekage and impersonates
  him to orchestrate an invasion of Konoha, killing Hiruzen Sarutobi in the process.
- **Search for Tsunade**: attempts to force [[Tsunade]] to heal his arms (damaged fighting
  Hiruzen) in exchange for reviving her dead brother and lover; she instead nearly kills him,
  precipitating her acceptance of the Fifth Hokage title.
- Was an early member of [[Akatsuki]] before the main story begins, defecting (and taking his
  ring, why he's never officially replaced) before [[Itachi Uchiha]] joined — corroborated by
  [[Akatsuki (source)]].
- **Sasuke Recovery Mission onward**: continues training [[Sasuke Uchiha]] as his intended
  next body, until Sasuke turns the body-transfer technique against him and absorbs him
  during the Itachi pursuit — see [[Sasuke Uchiha (source)]] for the same events from
  Sasuke's side. Later revived by Sasuke during the Fourth Shinobi World War and fights
  alongside Konoha, citing curiosity about Sasuke's "different path."
- **New Era**: pardoned under 24-hour Konoha surveillance; reestablishes Otogakure as a
  legitimate village and creates synthetic humans (Log, Mitsuki) through cloning rather than
  kidnapping. Sends Mitsuki to live in Konoha, forming a genuine (if unconventional)
  parental bond with him.

## Status

during [[Naruto Uzumaki]]'s disappearance is unconfirmed, flagged as an open thread.
