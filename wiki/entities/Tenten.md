# Tenten

Team Guy's weapons and fūinjutsu specialist.

## Background and personality

Dreamed as a child of becoming a legendary kunoichi like [[Tsunade]], and tried to follow her path into medical ninjutsu before her poor chakra control forced her to abandon it — she found her own strength instead in fūinjutsu-based weaponry, a formative lesson in not imitating someone else's path. Strongly opinionated about women's capability in combat (pushing back hard on Neji Hyuga's early skepticism), inquisitive and observant, and often exasperated by teammates [[Rock Lee]] and [[Might Guy]]'s antics — but fiercely loyal underneath, risking herself to rescue Lee during the Chūnin Exams and defending Neji when needed.

## Abilities

Stores a vast arsenal of weapons in sealing scrolls, unsealing them with explosive tags and wire-guided precision (Twin Rising Dragons, and later a large-scroll rain of weapons); by adulthood is regarded as Konoha's top weapon user and skilled in space–time ninjutsu/puppetry.

## Key arcs

- **Kazekage Rescue Mission**: pressures Kisame Hoshigaki alongside her team; later defeats a duplicate of herself created by the Akatsuki lair's barrier defense.
- **New Era**: opens her own ninja tool shop and becomes a Chūnin Exams proctor alongside Shino Aburame.

## Status

Reaches the Kawaki Arc (fighting and quickly losing to Isshiki alongside Kiba and Lee) but the ingested source does not continue into the *New Era: Part II* crisis chapters.
