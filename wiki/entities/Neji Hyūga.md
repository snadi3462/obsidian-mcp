# Neji Hyūga

Team Guy's Byakugan prodigy, cousin of [[Hinata Hyuga]]. **Deceased** — killed in the Fourth Shinobi World War.

## Background and personality

Born into the Hyūga clan's branch house, Neji grew up believing his father Hizashi had been coerced into dying as a sacrificial body-double to protect his twin brother Hiashi (Hinata's father) during a diplomatic crisis with Kumogakure. This misunderstanding calcified into a fatalistic belief that everyone's life is predetermined and inescapable — Neji saw himself as a genius unjustly bound to serve the main house, and grew cold, resentful, and even violent toward Hinata as a result. Naruto's refusal to accept a similarly limiting narrative about himself, and his subsequent defeat of Neji in the Chūnin Exams finals, breaks this fatalism: Neji learns his father chose his death rather than having it forced on him, and reorients his life's philosophy toward self-determined destiny — protecting the people he cares about, especially Hinata, whom he comes to shield rather than resent.

## Abilities

Wielded an especially powerful Byakugan and was widely considered the strongest Hyūga of his generation, specializing in the clan's Gentle Fist style (Eight Trigrams Sixty-Four Palms, Eight Trigrams Palms Revolving Heaven) — precise strikes that disrupt an opponent's chakra pathway system at minimal physical contact.

## Death

During the Fourth Shinobi World War's climax, fighting the Ten-Tails, Neji shields both [[Naruto Uzumaki]] and Hinata from a fatal volley of attacks, taking the blow meant for Hinata after she had already taken one meant for Naruto. Dying in Naruto's arms, Neji tells him he finally understands why his father chose to die protecting someone he loved. His death — and Hinata's insistence that his sacrifice not be wasted — directly triggers the Allied Forces' turning-point counterattack against the Ten-Tails.

## Legacy

Buried in Konoha; [[Naruto Uzumaki]] and [[Hinata Hyuga]] name their children partly in his honor (Boruto echoing "screw" for Neji's name meaning, Himawari for his favorite flower — see [[Team 7]] for the full account). [[Himawari Uzumaki]] regards him as an uncle despite never having met him, and regularly visits his grave with Hinata.
