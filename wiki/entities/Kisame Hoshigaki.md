# Kisame Hoshigaki

Former Seven Ninja Swordsmen of the Mist member, [[Itachi Uchiha]]'s [[Akatsuki]] partner —
"the Monster of the Hidden Mist." **Deceased** — died by assisted suicide to protect
organizational secrets.

## Background and personality

Killed his own trusted superior, Fuguki, upon learning Fuguki had betrayed Kirigakure by
selling secrets — an act that left Kisame disillusioned, believing his entire life and sense
of duty had been a lie. This disillusionment made him receptive to Obito Uchiha's "world of
truth" pitch (the Eye of the Moon Plan) when first approached. Loves fighting and is quite
sadistic, but genuinely deferential to and protective of [[Itachi Uchiha]] once partnered
with him — the only Akatsuki pairing built on real, if understated, friendship. Believes he
and Itachi are both "comrade-killers" destined for meaningless deaths, a nihilism Itachi
gently pushes back against near the end of his own life.

## Abilities

Wields Samehada, a sentient, chakra-absorbing sword that shaves flesh rather than cutting it;
possesses massive chakra reserves (earning him the nickname "Tailless Tailed Beast") and
mastery of Water Release, capable of reshaping entire battlefields.

## Death

Cornered by Killer B and the Fourth Raikage during the Fourth Shinobi World War's setup
(Fourth Shinobi World War: Countdown arc), Kisame chooses to trigger his own death via a
water-prison technique rather than risk revealing Akatsuki's secrets under interrogation —
consistent with the principle he lived by since killing Fuguki. His body becomes a Zetsu
decoy so the real Kisame can infiltrate Kumogakure hidden inside Samehada; the sword is
eventually claimed by Killer B.
