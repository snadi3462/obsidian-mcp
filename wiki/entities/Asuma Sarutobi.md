# Asuma Sarutobi

Son of the Third Hokage, [[Hiruzen Sarutobi]], former member of the Twelve Guardian Ninja, and sensei of [[Team 10]] — Shikamaru Nara, [[Ino Yamanaka]], and [[Chōji Akimichi]].

## Background and personality

Left Konoha for years over a disagreement with his father before returning to resume active duty; only came to fully understand and respect [[Hiruzen Sarutobi|Hiruzen]]'s sacrifice for the village after his father's death during the Konoha Crush. Blunt to the point of calling his students exactly what they are ("glutton," "loud mouth," "slacker") while genuinely caring for and believing in each of them — he's the one who first recognizes Shikamaru's genius-level intellect (IQ over 200) beneath the laziness. Long, quietly acknowledged romantic partner of [[Kurenai Yūhi]] since childhood.

## Abilities

A close-combat specialist using wind-chakra-infused trench knives (Chakra Blades); helped train [[Naruto Uzumaki]] in wind-nature chakra.

## Death

Dies during the Akatsuki Suppression Mission fighting [[Akatsuki]] members Hidan and Kakuzu: fatally wounded by Hidan's blood-ritual technique, he gives each student individually tailored parting words before entrusting Shikamaru with protecting his and Kurenai's then-unborn daughter, Mirai. His death is the defining trauma that reshapes all three of his students — see [[Chōji Akimichi]] and [[Ino Yamanaka]] for how it plays out for each of them, and [[Kurenai Yūhi]] for her side of the relationship. Reincarnated against his will during the Fourth Shinobi World War via Impure World Reincarnation, forced to fight (and coach) his own students, before his soul is finally released and returns to the afterlife.

## Legacy

Left behind daughter Mirai Sarutobi (with Kurenai) and trained the current-generation "Ino–Shika–Chō" trio who continued his tradition of celebratory barbecue dinners with their own children.
