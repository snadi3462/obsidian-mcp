# Might Guy

Leader of Team Guy (Rock Lee, Neji Hyuga, Tenten) and [[Kakashi Hatake]]'s self-declared
lifelong rival. Konoha's taijutsu master.

## Background

Son of Might Duy, Konoha's "Eternal Genin," who never resented the mocking nickname and
taught Guy that hard work and youth-driven perseverance matter more than natural talent. Guy
failed the Academy entrance exam and had little early aptitude for ninjutsu or genjutsu,
but embraced taijutsu as his path — a decision cemented when Duy died opening all Eight Gates
to save Guy and his team from the Seven Ninja Swordsmen of the Mist, satisfying Duy's one
rule for using the technique: giving his life to protect something precious.

Guy declared Kakashi his lifelong rival in childhood, driven by a desire to prove his
perseverance could match Kakashi's natural genius. Their rivalry (eating contests, Rock-
Paper-Scissors, a running "50 wins to 49" score that never actually changes because the
contests are so varied) coexists with genuine deep friendship — Guy is one of the few people
who tries hardest to help Kakashi through his depression after Obito and Rin's losses, and
later petitions to have Kakashi removed from Anbu when he sees the "darkness" the role
requires of him.

## Personality

Embodied by his "nice guy" pose (thumbs-up, wink, and a proverbial *ping*) and his self-
declared "Power of Youth" (*seishun*) philosophy. Genuinely selfless and willing to sacrifice
himself without hesitation for anyone he considers precious, but capable of real seriousness
when the situation demands it. Has an almost father-son bond with his student Rock Lee, whom
he dedicates his life to training after recognizing Lee's dream of becoming a splendid ninja
through taijutsu alone, just as Guy himself did.

## Abilities

A late-blooming taijutsu master specializing in the Strong Fist style, eventually recognized
(by Itachi Uchiha and Kakashi both) as one of Konoha's strongest ninja. Trained by his father
in the Eight Inner Gates from a young age, and uniquely able to open the first seven
instantly without sequential unlocking. Each gate trades physical/chakra strain for immense
power boosts; opening all eight is near-certainly fatal, so Guy reserves it as an absolute
last resort. Signature techniques include Front Lotus, Reverse Lotus, Morning Peacock,
Daytime Tiger, Evening Elephant, and the ultimate Night Guy.

## Key arcs

- **Chūnin Exams**: mentors [[Team Guy]] through the exams; his student Rock Lee is nearly
  crippled fighting Gaara and is saved by [[Tsunade]]'s risky surgery.
- **Kazekage Rescue Mission**: Team Guy backs up [[Team 7]], fighting Kisame Hoshigaki en
  route.
- **Fourth Shinobi World War**: opens the Eight Gates (Night Guy) against Madara Uchiha as
  the Ten-Tails' jinchūriki, dealing damage that even Madara compares to the First Hokage.
  The attack shatters Guy's right leg; [[Naruto Uzumaki]]'s newly-learned Yin–Yang Release
  saves his life afterward, but the injury ends his active ninja career.
- **New Era**: unable to continue as an active shinobi, becomes an Academy taijutsu teacher
  and Kakashi's advisor, using his own disability as a teaching example for students like
  Metal Lee. Attends [[Naruto Uzumaki]] and [[Hinata Hyuga]]'s wedding.

## Status

Confined to a wheelchair for the rest of his life since the war. The ingested source does not
follow him into the *New Era: Part II* crisis chapters (see [[Team 7]]) — his last confirmed
appearance is attending a Fourth Shinobi World War memorial service.
