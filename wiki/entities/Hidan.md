# Hidan

Yugakure defector, [[Akatsuki]]'s Jashinist zealot — [[Kakuzu]]'s partner, one half of the
"Zombie Combo." **Status: unique among Akatsuki — not dead**, but buried alive and slowly
starving.

## Background and personality

A devout missionary of Jashin, a faith centered on ritualized slaughter, Hidan massacred his
own Yugakure neighbors when the village abandoned its shinobi identity for tourism, then
joined the cult before being granted near-total immortality through its secret techniques.
Foul-mouthed, disrespectful, and openly contemptuous of [[Akatsuki]]'s leader Pain (the only
member to be), Hidan is sadistic and masochistic in equal measure — he relishes inflicting and
sharing pain through his curse ritual, and his arrogance about his own immortality repeatedly
gets him into trouble his partner [[Kakuzu]] has to bail him out of.

## Abilities

Effectively unkillable by conventional means (only malnutrition can end him) and heals almost
instantly from any physical damage. His signature technique links his body to an opponent's
after consuming their blood — any injury Hidan inflicts on himself while linked is mirrored
onto them, letting him kill by stabbing his own heart.

## Key arc: killing Asuma Sarutobi

During the Akatsuki Suppression Mission, Hidan links himself to [[Asuma Sarutobi]] and kills
him via his curse ritual, despite Shikamaru Nara's efforts — corroborated in full by [[Asuma
Sarutobi (source)]] and [[Kakuzu (source)]]. Shikamaru later exacts revenge,
trapping Hidan in an explosion within Nara clan forest land and burying the remains.

## Status

**Unlike every other Akatsuki member covered this ingest, Hidan does not die.** Dismembered
and buried by Shikamaru's trap, he survives — Akatsuki believes him dead, but he is confirmed
still alive underground, slowly starving from lack of nutrition rather than actually dying. A
later, unrelated copycat ("Ryūki") fails to replicate his legacy and is arrested by Konoha.
