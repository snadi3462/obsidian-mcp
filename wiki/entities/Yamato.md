# Yamato

Wood Release-wielding Anbu (codename "Tenzō") who leads [[Team 7]] / Team Kakashi as a
temporary replacement for [[Kakashi Hatake]].

## Background

As an infant, Yamato was abducted by [[Orochimaru]] and made one of sixty children injected
with First Hokage Hashirama Senju's DNA in an attempt to replicate his Wood Release. Every
other subject died; Yamato alone survived, emerging with an unquantified Wood Release and the
First's ability to suppress tailed beasts' chakra. Having lost all memory of his identity, he
was found and raised by Danzō Shimura, recruited into Root under the codename Kinoe. Sent to
assassinate the Third Hokage, he was defeated but spared by a young [[Kakashi Hatake]] — the
beginning of a mentor-student relationship that eventually pulls Yamato away from Root
entirely. He takes the name "Tenzō," later "Yamato," and serves in Kakashi's Anbu unit
alongside a young [[Itachi Uchiha]].

## Personality

Discreet, cautious, and stoic even under pressure — a contrast to Kakashi's more relaxed
leadership style; Yamato admits he's willing to "rule by fear" if his team misbehaves,
usually via a deliberately terrifying "ghoul-eyes" expression that [[Naruto Uzumaki]] finds
especially unsettling. Genuinely playful underneath the severity (running gags of pranking
Naruto), and has a real hobby/interest in architecture that dovetails with his Wood Release —
he builds instant wooden houses for the team to camp in and later helps rebuild Konoha after
Pain's attack.

## Abilities

Possesses Hashirama Senju's Wood Release kekkei genkai (unrefined compared to the original),
letting him grow trees and structures for offense, defense, tracking, and shelter, plus a
regeneration ability from the same genetic source. Most strategically important: using the
First Hokage's Necklace as an amplifier, Yamato can suppress a jinchūriki's tailed-beast
chakra — the specific reason [[Tsunade]] assigns him to lead Team 7 in Kakashi's absence.
This suppression ability is lost once the necklace is destroyed partway through Part II.

## Key arcs

- **Tenchi Bridge Reconnaissance Mission**: leads the reformed Team 7 (Naruto, [[Sakura
  Haruno]], [[Sai]]) to meet a spy in [[Orochimaru]]'s ranks; restrains and later earns the
  trust of Sai after uncovering his true assassination mission against Sasuke.
- **Fourth Shinobi World War**: captured and drained by Kabuto/Obito to strengthen the White
  Zetsu Army using his Hashirama-derived cells; freed when Madara's Infinite Tsukuyomi is
  broken.
- **New Era**: assigned to covertly surveil the pardoned [[Orochimaru]], eventually becoming
  famous as the only Konoha shinobi who can approach him without fear.

## Status

The ingested source's coverage ends in the *New Era* (Mitsuki's Disappearance Arc); unlike
[[Sai]], it does not follow Yamato into the *New Era: Part II* crisis chapters, so his status
during [[Naruto Uzumaki]]'s disappearance is unconfirmed — flagged as an open thread.
