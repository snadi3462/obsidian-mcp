# Sasuke Uchiha

[[Naruto Uzumaki]]'s main rival and best friend. Member of [[Team 7]]. Husband of
[[Sakura Haruno]]; father of [[Sarada Uchiha]]. Younger brother of [[Itachi Uchiha]].

## Background

Grew up adoring his older brother Itachi, a clan prodigy whose accomplishments overshadowed
him and drew their father's attention away from him. Itachi tried to compensate, but their
relationship grew strained as Itachi became increasingly withdrawn in the lead-up to the
Uchiha Clan Massacre. Sasuke returned home one night to find the entire clan slaughtered and
Itachi standing over their parents' bodies. Itachi used Tsukuyomi to torment him with visions
of the massacre, told him he'd never loved him, and — before leaving — deliberately
encouraged Sasuke to hate him and grow strong enough for revenge. Sasuke spent the following
years single-mindedly training for that purpose, forming no other attachments.

## The truth about Itachi

Sasuke kills Itachi in single combat (Itachi allows it, using his last act to gently poke
Sasuke's forehead rather than take his eyes as threatened — secretly sealing a failsafe
technique into them instead). Sasuke then learns from Tobi (Obito/Madara) that Itachi killed
the clan on Konoha leadership's orders — specifically, an impossible choice forced on him by
Danzō Shimura to prevent the Uchiha coup from starting a new World War — but spared and
shielded Sasuke throughout, secretly loving him the entire time. This revelation devastates
Sasuke and redirects his hatred toward Konoha itself. See [[Itachi Uchiha]] and [[Itachi
Uchiha (source)]] for the full account from Itachi's own side, including his
reincarnation-era reconciliation with Sasuke during the Fourth Shinobi World War.
[[Hiruzen Sarutobi (source)]] independently corroborates this chain of command from the
Third Hokage's own side — Danzō acted without Hiruzen's knowledge, and Hiruzen personally
promised Itachi he would protect Sasuke afterward, a promise reflected in his later choice to
assign [[Kakashi Hatake]] to Sasuke's genin team.

## Descent and warped ideology

Sasuke's fall from anti-hero to full antagonist is gradual: he abandons his new team (Taka)
without concern for their fates when convenient, sacrifices Karin to strike at Danzō, and
allies with [[Akatsuki]] when useful. Tobi convinces him he's carrying the entire Uchiha clan's
inherited hatred, feeding an increasingly unstable, purist pride in his clan. This eventually
crystallizes into a "True Hokage" ideology: Sasuke concludes that if one person (himself)
shoulders all of a village's hardest, most hated decisions alone, everyone else can be
unified — in their hatred of him — without further internal conflict. He plans to sever his
remaining bonds to commit to this fully.

## Reconciliation with Naruto

Naruto's insistence that Sasuke's isolationist plan is neither possible nor wise — and his
admission that he suffers whenever he and Sasuke are apart — is what finally moves Sasuke to
let go of his hatred. After fighting alongside Naruto against Madara and Kaguya Ōtsutsuki in
the Fourth Shinobi World War, the two have one final, near-fatal duel at the Valley of the
End. Both lose an arm; Sasuke is defeated for the first time in his life and accepts that
Naruto is as necessary to him as he is to Naruto, ending the rivalry. He is pardoned for his
crimes on Naruto and Kakashi's word and leaves to wander the world as a vigilante — keeping
his returned forehead protector as a symbol of their friendship — later returning to
investigate the Ōtsutsuki clan alongside Konoha and becoming known as the "Supporting Kage."

## Marriage and family

Marries Sakura Haruno, who becomes **Sakura Uchiha**; their daughter is [[Sarada Uchiha]].
Sasuke is largely absent for years on his Ōtsutsuki investigation, leaving Sakura to raise
Sarada alone — a gap that causes real strain (Sarada briefly doubts her parentage) before
the family reconciles. Per [[Itachi Uchiha (source)]], Sasuke adopts his late brother's
habit of poking foreheads as an affectionate gesture toward both Sakura and Sarada.

## Reincarnated Hokage arc

At Sasuke's own request, [[Orochimaru]] reincarnates the first four Hokage — [[Hashirama
Senju]], [[Tobirama Senju]], [[Hiruzen Sarutobi]], and Minato Namikaze — during the Fourth
Shinobi World War so Sasuke can question them directly about Konoha's founding and the
Uchiha's history. Hearing their accounts (and Hiruzen's confirmation of the massacre's true
chain of command) is what finally convinces Sasuke he doesn't want to see Konoha destroyed,
setting up his shift to fighting alongside the Allied Shinobi Forces against Madara and Obito.

## Later status — open thread, parallels Naruto's

In the Kawaki Arc, Sasuke fights alongside Naruto against Jigen and Isshiki Ōtsutsuki,
losing use of his Rinnegan eye to Boruto (possessed by Momoshiki Ōtsutsuki) at the climax —
see [[Naruto Uzumaki]] for the full account.

**This continues past where Naruto's own page leaves off.** In *New Era: Part II*, Sasuke is
again branded a criminal — this time for helping Boruto — and goes into hiding as Boruto's
mentor. About a year later, he and Boruto are attacked by Code; Sasuke sacrifices himself to
fend off Code's forces, takes Code's left eye, but is bitten by a "Claw Grime" that drains
his chakra and grows into a separate being (Hidari), leaving Sasuke incapacitated. Boruto
vows to save him. **As of the sources ingested so far, Sasuke's fate here is unresolved**,
running in parallel with [[Naruto Uzumaki]]'s own unresolved disappearance — see the
"Current crisis" note on [[Team 7]] for the combined picture of the whole original team's
open threads.
