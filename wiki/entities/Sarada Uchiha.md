# Sarada Uchiha

Daughter of [[Sasuke Uchiha]] and [[Sakura Haruno]].

## Overview

Raised almost entirely by Sakura, since Sasuke is away for years at a time on Ōtsutsuki
investigation missions. As her Academy graduation approaches, Sarada presses Sakura for
details about Sasuke's past; Sakura's reluctance to discuss his dark history leads Sarada to
doubt whether her parents are even really married, or whether Sasuke is really her father.
She leaves the village to find him herself. The doubt is resolved in the *Sarada Uchiha Arc*
(also adapted as *Naruto Gaiden: The Seventh Hokage and the Scarlet Spring*): Sakura rescues
the family from the antagonist Shin Uchiha, and the confrontation confirms Sarada and
Sasuke's biological bond, after which the family reconciles.

She later becomes a genin partnered with Boruto Uzumaki and inherits the Uchiha Sharingan,
progressing to a Mangekyō Sharingan. By the *New Era: Part II* chapters, overuse of it has
begun visibly damaging her eyesight, but she pushes through it out of devotion to helping
Boruto — a parallel the wiki notes to Sakura's own younger devotion to Sasuke.
