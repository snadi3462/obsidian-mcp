# Kawaki

A Kara defector taken in by [[Naruto Uzumaki]] and central to the unresolved arc that closes
out the current wiki coverage of Naruto's story.

## Overview

Introduced as a tattooed, heavily-modified youth (Shinobi-Ware implants granting
Orochimaru/Jugo-like physiology alteration) brought into Kara by Jigen and bestowed a Kāma
mark to be turned into a weapon. After fleeing Kara, he's brought to Konoha by Boruto and
taken in by the Uzumaki family under Naruto's protection. Naruto mentors him — training him
in ninjutsu, giving him a prosthetic arm, defending him from Kara operatives (Delta, Jigen,
Isshiki) — and the two develop a father-figure bond, in tension with Kawaki's rivalry/found
sibling relationship with Boruto.

## Later significance — central to Naruto's disappearance

Per [[List of Naruto characters]], the franchise's prologue foreshadows Kawaki and Boruto
becoming mortal enemies, with an older Kawaki implicated in Konoha's eventual destruction.
The [[Naruto Uzumaki (source)]] source confirms and extends this: through Kawaki (and
Ada)'s actions, Naruto and [[Hinata Hyuga]] end up trapped unconscious in another dimension
for three years, with Boruto wrongly blamed for Naruto's apparent death. In the Flashforward
section, Kawaki tells Boruto he will send him to "where he sent the Seventh Hokage" —
confirming Kawaki, not Boruto, is responsible for whatever ultimately happened to Naruto,
though the full resolution isn't covered by sources ingested so far.
