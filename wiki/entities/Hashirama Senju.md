# Hashirama Senju

First Hokage of Konohagakure, co-founder of the village alongside his lifelong friend and
rival [[Madara Uchiha]]. **Deceased.** Older brother of [[Tobirama Senju]]; grandfather of
[[Tsunade]].

## Background

Born into the Senju clan during the Warring States Period, Hashirama grew up in constant war
against the rival Uchiha clan and lost three younger brothers to the fighting before his
remaining brother [[Tobirama Senju]] pushed for a new system to end the cycle. As a boy,
Hashirama secretly befriended a young Uchiha named Madara across enemy lines; the two later
united their clans to found Konohagakure, a village where children wouldn't need to die in
battle. Hashirama was elected the village's first Hokage after Tobirama insisted on a
democratic process rather than simply handing Madara the position.

## The Madara relationship

Hashirama and Madara's friendship-rivalry defines both their lives: Hashirama could never
bring himself to kill Madara even as declared enemies, offered to unite their clans and later
wanted Madara to be his successor, and only killed him — reluctantly, at the Valley of the
End — after Madara abandoned any hope of peaceful coexistence. Reincarnated together during
the Fourth Shinobi World War, the two reconcile fully in Madara's final moments; see
[[Madara Uchiha]] for the same relationship from his side.

## Personality

Excitable, boisterous, and prone to rapid mood swings between high spirits and sulking, but
capable of real seriousness and self-sacrifice for causes he cared about — he was willing to
kill himself to secure peace between the Senju and Uchiha before Konoha even existed. His
optimism about human nature (the "Will of Fire," treating the whole village as one family)
became Konoha's foundational philosophy, and regularly needed Tobirama's more pragmatic
pushback to stay grounded.

## Abilities

Hailed as the strongest shinobi of his era, the "God of Shinobi." Sole practitioner of Wood
Release (a combination of earth and water chakra), able to single-handedly subdue eight of the
nine tailed beasts, enter Sage Mode instantaneously, and match the fully-powered Madara in
direct combat.

## Death and reincarnation

Died of natural causes after failing to achieve lasting peace in his lifetime, passing the
Hokage title to Tobirama. Reincarnated via Impure World Reincarnation during the Fourth
Shinobi World War (summoned by [[Orochimaru]] at [[Sasuke Uchiha]]'s request), Hashirama helps
Sasuke understand Konoha's founding and the Uchiha's history before fighting alongside the
Allied Shinobi Forces against Obito Uchiha and the reincarnated Madara, then dying a second,
final death after Madara's defeat.

## Legacy

His exhumed cells and DNA are directly responsible for several other threads in the wiki:
[[Orochimaru]]'s Wood Release experiments on sixty children produced only one survivor,
[[Yamato]]; Danzō Shimura's arm was infused with Hashirama cells; and the same genetic
material was later used to heal Obito Uchiha and replace Naruto and Sasuke's lost arms. He also
personally helped train a young [[Hiruzen Sarutobi]].
