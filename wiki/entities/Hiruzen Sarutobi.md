# Hiruzen Sarutobi

Third Hokage of Konohagakure, "The Professor," and teacher of the Sannin — [[Orochimaru]],
[[Tsunade]], and Jiraiya. **Deceased.** Father of [[Asuma Sarutobi]].

## Background

Trained under [[Tobirama Senju]] alongside Homura Mitokado and Koharu Utatane, with additional
personal tutelage from [[Hashirama Senju]]. Succeeded Tobirama as Third Hokage after
Tobirama's death, then went on to train his own team of prodigies — [[Orochimaru]],
[[Tsunade]], and Jiraiya — becoming the mentor figure behind two already-ingested sources on
this wiki.

## The Sannin and Orochimaru's defection

Hiruzen favored [[Orochimaru]] above his other students, seeing in him a genius comparable to
himself. When he discovered Orochimaru's unethical human experimentation, Hiruzen couldn't
bring himself to kill his own student and instead let him escape — a decision he considers his
deepest personal failure, one that indirectly produces [[Yamato]] (Orochimaru's sole surviving
Wood Release test subject) and everything that follows from Orochimaru's defection.

## The Uchiha massacre's chain of command

Confirms, from his own side, the account already covered on [[Sasuke Uchiha]] and
[[Itachi Uchiha]]: Danzō Shimura ordered Itachi to slaughter the Uchiha clan without Hiruzen's
knowledge or approval. Hiruzen learned of it only after the fact, removed Danzō from the
Konoha Council in response, and personally promised Itachi he would protect Sasuke — a promise
he keeps, including assigning [[Kakashi Hatake]] to lead Naruto's genin team partly to watch
over Sasuke.

## Naruto's early years

Withheld Naruto's parentage from the village to protect him from Minato's surviving enemies,
and outlawed any mention of the Nine-Tails to shield Naruto from being treated as the monster
itself. Also mentored a young [[Iruka Umino]] through his own grief after losing his parents —
the model Iruka later credits for his own decision to become a teacher.

## Death

Dies during the Konoha Crush/Invasion of Konoha, simultaneously fighting his former student
[[Orochimaru]] and reincarnated versions of [[Hashirama Senju]] and [[Tobirama Senju]]. Uses
variants of the Dead Demon Consuming Seal to release the two Hokage's souls and permanently
seal Orochimaru's arms, dying from a fatal wound in the process — a death regarded as
foundational grief for the entire village, including a young [[Naruto Uzumaki]].
