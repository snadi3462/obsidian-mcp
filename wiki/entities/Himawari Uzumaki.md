# Himawari Uzumaki

Daughter of [[Naruto Uzumaki]] and [[Hinata Hyuga]]; younger sibling of Boruto Uzumaki.
Named after Neji Hyuga's favorite flower (the sunflower) in tribute to his death protecting
Naruto and Hinata.

## Later significance — corroborated, mechanism still unresolved

[[Sakura Haruno (source)]] describes Himawari, in the *New Era: Part II* chapters, as
"the new Nine-Tails' jinchūriki," able to fully heal a gravely injured Inojin.
[[Ino Yamanaka (source)]] independently corroborates this — during the Boruto's Return
Arc, an injured Boruto is healed by "Himawari, the new Nine-Tails jinchūriki." Two separate
sources now treat this as an established fact of the current era, not a one-off claim. **The
mechanism connecting Kurama's death (see [[Kurama]]) to Himawari's status is still not
explained** — whether this is a reborn/successor Nine-Tails or something else entirely
remains the open question, worth checking against any future source on the *Boruto* endgame.
