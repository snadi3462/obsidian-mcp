# Kurama

The Nine-Tailed Fox (Nine-Tails), a tailed beast sealed inside [[Naruto Uzumaki]] on the
night of his birth by his parents [[Minato Namikaze]] and [[Kushina Uzumaki]].

## Overview

Attacked Konohagakure the night Naruto was born after being torn from Kushina (its previous
jinchūriki); Minato split its chakra in half, sealing the Yin half in himself and the Yang
half in infant Naruto. For most of Naruto's childhood, Kurama is an antagonistic presence —
its negative influence amplifies Naruto's darker impulses ("Dark Naruto"), and early access
to its chakra is only possible in moments of great anger, at the cost of Naruto's control.

The relationship shifts fundamentally once Naruto learns Kurama's true name and the two
develop a genuine friendship/partnership, granting Naruto full cooperative access to its
power (Kurama Mode) with none of the earlier drawbacks. Kurama's chakra underlies most of
Naruto's signature late-series abilities: Tailed Beast Mode, the Tailed Beast Ball, and
ultimately Baryon Mode.

## Death

Kurama dies as a direct consequence of Baryon Mode — a fusion technique against Isshiki
Ōtsutsuki that consumes both Naruto's and Kurama's life-force like nuclear fusion. Kurama
hid this cost from Naruto deliberately, knowing Naruto wouldn't risk it otherwise. Its death
permanently strips Naruto of Kurama's chakra and the abilities built on it — a significant,
lasting downgrade to his fighting capability going into the later *Boruto*-era arcs.

## Possible successor — corroborated, mechanism still unresolved

[[Sakura Haruno (source)]] describes [[Himawari Uzumaki]] (Naruto's daughter), in the
*New Era: Part II* chapters, as "the new Nine-Tails' jinchūriki." This is now independently
corroborated by [[Ino Yamanaka (source)]], which describes Himawari using that power to
heal an injured Boruto during the Boruto's Return Arc — two separate sources now treat
Himawari's jinchūriki status as an established fact of the current era, not a one-off claim.
**What sources ingested so far still don't explain is the mechanism**: how this squares with
Kurama's death during the Kawaki Arc — whether a new Nine-Tails exists, whether Kurama itself
was somehow revived, or something else. Flagged here and on [[Himawari Uzumaki]] as the
remaining open question, pending a future source on the *Boruto* endgame.
