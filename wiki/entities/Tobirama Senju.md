# Tobirama Senju

Second Hokage of Konohagakure, younger brother of [[Hashirama Senju]], and the pragmatic
institution-builder whose creations shape the rest of the series. **Deceased.**

## Background and personality

Where Hashirama was idealistic and impulsive, Tobirama was blunt, calculating, and obsessed
with well-defined rules — the realist counterweight who forced Hashirama's Hokage selection to
be democratic and wanted to kill [[Madara Uchiha]] outright rather than negotiate with him.
Deeply loyal to his brother despite frequently having to rein him in, and a firm believer in
the same "Will of Fire" philosophy Hashirama championed, just enforced with far less patience.

## Institutions and inventions

As Hokage, Tobirama built the systems that define Konoha for the rest of the series: the
Academy, the Anbu, and the Chūnin Exams (all later adopted by other villages too). He also
invented the Shadow Clone Technique, the Flying Thunder God Technique, and the Impure World
Reincarnation — the last of which he personally outlawed as a kinjutsu, regretting its
existence even as it goes on to play a major role in the Fourth Shinobi World War.

## Origin of Uchiha marginalization

Created the Konoha Military Police Force explicitly for the Uchiha clan to run, intending it
as a sign of trust and a way to channel their energy productively — but the clan experienced it
as being sidelined from real influence, seeding decades of resentment that eventually culminate
in the Uchiha Clan Downfall. This makes Tobirama an indirect root cause of the backstory
already covered on [[Sasuke Uchiha]] and [[Itachi Uchiha]].

## Teacher of Hiruzen

Trained [[Hiruzen Sarutobi]], Homura Mitokado, and Koharu Utatane as Team Tobirama. During the
First Shinobi World War, Tobirama volunteered as a decoy against Kumogakure's Kinkaku Force so
the rest of his team could escape, passing the Hokage title to Hiruzen with his final act
before being killed.

## Reincarnation arc

Alongside Hashirama, Minato, and Hiruzen, reincarnated via his own Impure World Reincarnation
technique during the Fourth Shinobi World War to help fight Obito Uchiha and the revived
Madara — an outcome only possible because of the jutsu he'd invented and outlawed.
