# Kakuzu

Former Takigakure elite shinobi, [[Akatsuki]]'s money-obsessed "treasurer" — [[Hidan]]'s
partner, one half of the "Zombie Combo." **Deceased** — killed by [[Kakashi Hatake]].

## Background and personality

Defected after Takigakure punished rather than honored him for failing to assassinate
Hashirama Senju (the First Hokage) — Kakuzu killed the village elders in retaliation and fled
with their forbidden techniques, becoming a bounty hunter who extends his own life by
harvesting hearts from powerful victims. Greedy and miserly to the point of arranging all his
goals around profit, but genuinely loyal and cautious as a fighter — more disciplined than his
reckless partner [[Hidan]], whom he tolerates specifically because Hidan's immortality means
Kakuzu can never accidentally kill him the way he killed his previous four partners.

## Abilities

His body is held together by hundreds of thread-like tendrils (Earth Grudge Fear) that let
him detach limbs, repair wounds instantly, and — his signature ability — store up to five
harvested hearts, each granting a different elemental affinity via an independent, semi-
autonomous mask creature. Requires being killed five times (once per heart) to actually die.

## Death

During the Akatsuki Suppression Mission (hunting Asuma Sarutobi's killers), Kakuzu loses his
hearts one by one to [[Kakashi Hatake]], Team 10, and finally [[Naruto Uzumaki]]'s newly
perfected Rasenshuriken — corroborated in detail by [[Asuma Sarutobi (source)]] and
[[Hidan (source)]]. Left with no hearts remaining, Kakuzu is finished off by Kakashi's
Lightning Cutter, who tells him that being surpassed by the next generation is simply the
natural cycle of life.
