## Open threads / unresolved

- **The entire surviving original Team 7 is mid-crisis as of the most recent ingested
  sources.** [[Naruto Uzumaki]] and [[Sasuke Uchiha]] are both incapacitated/missing in the
  *New Era: Part II* chapters; see the "Current crisis" section on [[Team 7]] for the
  combined picture. Per [[Ino Yamanaka (source)]], Boruto has told Shikamaru that Naruto
  and Hinata are alive, but this remains privately held rather than publicly confirmed — and
  Boruto himself is now imprisoned in Konoha as a suspected traitor. Any future source
  touching the *Boruto* endgame should be checked against this before treating any of these
  fates as settled.
- **[[Shikamaru Nara]] is acting/interim Hokage** during Naruto's disappearance, confirmed by
  both [[Sai (source)]] and [[Ino Yamanaka (source)]], with political tension
  building around whether he'll be removed over his handling of Boruto. Of all sources
  ingested so far, only these two reach the *New Era: Part II* chapters directly — every
  other current-era character's status (Might Guy, Yamato, Orochimaru, Tsunade, and every
  Team 8/Team 10/Team Guy member) is unconfirmed rather than "unaffected" during this period.
- **[[Himawari Uzumaki]] is the new Nine-Tails jinchūriki** despite [[Kurama]]'s death —
  independently corroborated by two sources this ingest, but the mechanism is still
  unexplained.
- **Hidan is confirmed alive but starving, buried underground** — the sole Akatsuki member
  from this ingest who doesn't die. No source has followed up on whether he's ever found;
  worth checking against any future source touching post-war Konoha cleanup.
- Named in the raw sources but without wiki pages yet: Boruto Uzumaki, Mitsuki; Jigen,
  Isshiki Ōtsutsuki, Delta, Momoshiki Ōtsutsuki, Kaguya Ōtsutsuki, Code, Ada, Amado (Kara/
  antagonist figures); Temari, Shikadai, Inojin, Chōchō, Metal Lee, Sarada's genin-era peers
  (next generation, named repeatedly but not yet ingested as sources); Hanzō, Danzō Shimura,
  Kabuto Yakushi, Konohamaru Sarutobi (repeatedly pivotal but still without their own
  dedicated sources); plus the exhaustive chapter-by-chapter Boruto-era arcs (Chūnin
  Re-Examination onward) that were skimmed rather than filed page-by-page across all ingests
  so far.
