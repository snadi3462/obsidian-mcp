# Team 7

The genin team at the center of the *Naruto* series: [[Naruto Uzumaki]], [[Sasuke Uchiha]],
[[Sakura Haruno]], led by [[Kakashi Hatake]].

## Timeline

- **Part I**: forms as Team 7 (第7班, *Dainanahan*) under Kakashi, passing his bell test
  through teamwork rather than individual skill (details per [[Naruto Uzumaki (source)]]).
- **End of Part I**: disbands after Sasuke's defection to Orochimaru and Naruto's departure
  from Konohagakure to train.
- **Part II**: reforms as "Team Kakashi" (カカシ班) with two replacements — [[Sai]] (taking
  Sasuke's position, initially on a secret mission for Danzō) and [[Yamato]] (acting
  captain, standing in for Kakashi at times — specifically chosen by [[Tsunade]] for his
  ability to suppress Naruto's Nine-Tails chakra).
- **Fourth Great Ninja War**: the original four reunite to fight Madara Uchiha and then
  Kaguya Ōtsutsuki — [[Kakashi Hatake]] and [[Obito Uchiha]] shield Naruto and Sasuke from
  a fatal attack, and Obito uses his last strength to protect Kakashi in turn, dying in the
  process; his dying wish is for Naruto to become Hokage.
- **Post-war**: Naruto and Sasuke's final, near-fatal battle at the Valley of the End
  resolves their rivalry over conflicting visions for the village; the group is fully
  reunited afterward, and Sasuke is pardoned on Naruto and Kakashi's word.
- **Marriages**: Naruto marries [[Hinata Hyuga]]; Sasuke marries [[Sakura Haruno]] (becoming
  Sakura Uchiha), and their daughter [[Sarada Uchiha]] later becomes a genin herself,
  partnered with Naruto's son Boruto.
- **Kakashi's Hokage tenure**: succeeds [[Tsunade]] as Sixth Hokage after the war, later
  passes the mantle to Naruto and retires from active duty — but stays involved as an
  advisor and investigator well into the current *Boruto*-era storyline.

## Current crisis (New Era: Part II) — open thread

By the most recent chapters covered in the ingested sources, every surviving original member
of Team 7 is in an unresolved crisis simultaneously:

- **[[Naruto Uzumaki]]**: permanently loses [[Kurama]] to Baryon Mode against Isshiki
  Ōtsutsuki, then is trapped unconscious with [[Hinata Hyuga]] for three years in a
  dimension tied to [[Kawaki]]; his ultimate fate is unresolved as of the Flashforward
  section of [[Naruto Uzumaki (source)]]. **Update**: [[Ino Yamanaka (source)]]
  has Boruto tell Shikamaru directly that Naruto and Hinata are alive — the clearest
  statement yet, though their whereabouts/condition remain undescribed.
- **[[Sasuke Uchiha]]**: branded a criminal again for helping Boruto, goes into hiding as
  his mentor, and is critically incapacitated by a Code-created parasite (Hidari) while
  protecting him — also unresolved as of [[Sasuke Uchiha (source)]].
- **[[Sakura Haruno]]** and **[[Kakashi Hatake]]** are the two members still stably active,
  holding the village together (Sakura at the hospital, Kakashi investigating threats and
  advising) while the next generation (Boruto, [[Sarada Uchiha]], [[Kawaki]]) takes on the
  active fighting role.
- Complicating the picture further: [[Himawari Uzumaki]] is described as a new jinchūriki
  of the Nine-Tails despite Kurama's death — now corroborated by two independent sources
  ([[Sakura Haruno (source)]] and [[Ino Yamanaka (source)]], the latter showing her
  healing an injured Boruto with it) — see [[Kurama]] for the still-unresolved question of
  the mechanism.
- **Who is running the village in Naruto's absence**: [[Sai (source)]] and
  [[Ino Yamanaka (source)]] fill in a gap the earlier sources left open —
  [[Shikamaru Nara]] (Team 10, Naruto's long-time advisor) is serving as **acting/interim
  Hokage** during this crisis, with [[Sai]] as his security chief and [[Ino Yamanaka]]
  running his private telepathic communications. This isn't a quiet caretaking role: the
  Fire Daimyō's advisor Kobu suspects Shikamaru of covering for the criminalized Boruto
  Uzumaki and is building a case to have him removed, and per [[Ino Yamanaka
  (source)]]'s Boruto's Return Arc, **Boruto himself has been captured and interrogated
  (escalating to physical force) by Konoha as a suspected traitor** after briefly returning
  to fight off Code — even though he's the source of Shikamaru's information that Naruto and
  Hinata are alive. Shikamaru privately believes Boruto but can't act on it openly; Ino,
  asked to help him secretly reconnect with Boruto, refuses out of suspicion and loyalty to
  protocol. See [[Shikamaru Nara]], [[Sai]], and [[Ino Yamanaka]] for detail.

This should be treated as the wiki's current frontier on the Naruto storyline — any new
source touching the *Boruto* endgame should be checked against all of the above before
anything here is treated as settled. Of the sources ingested so far, [[Sai (source)]]
and [[Ino Yamanaka (source)]] are the only ones to reach these *New Era: Part II*
chapters directly — [[Might Guy (source)]], [[Yamato (source)]],
[[Orochimaru (source)]], [[Tsunade (source)]], and the *Team 8*/*Team 10*/*Team
Guy* member sources ingested alongside this batch all stop earlier in the *New Era*, so
those characters' status during the crisis is unconfirmed rather than "unaffected."

## Backup and parallel teams

[[Team 7]] operates within a wider cast of Konoha 11 teams that repeatedly cross paths with
it on missions: [[Team 8]] (tracking specialists, led by Kurenai Yūhi) and [[Team 10]] (the
"Ino-Shika-Cho" formation, led by Asuma Sarutobi) both provide backup on Akatsuki- and
Orochimaru-related missions, and [[Team Guy]] backs up the Kazekage Rescue and Sasuke
Recovery missions specifically. [[Tsunade]] (as Fifth Hokage) and [[Orochimaru]] (as
antagonist, then reformed ally) both shape Team 7's story from outside its own roster — see
their pages for detail.

## Design note

Kishimoto has said the original trio (Naruto, Sasuke, Sakura) was built as the template for
every subsequent three-person genin team in the series — the "Ino-Shika-Cho" naming pattern
used for [[Team 10]] (Shikamaru, Choji, Ino) is described as an echo of this same structural
idea, though Team 10 was itself inherited from their parents' generation.
