# Team Guy

Konoha genin team built around taijutsu and physical combat, in deliberate contrast to
[[Team 7]]'s and [[Team 8]]'s ninjutsu/genjutsu-heavy rosters. Members: [[Rock Lee]] (pure
taijutsu, no ninjutsu/genjutsu ability), Neji Hyuga (Byakugan/Gentle Fist prodigy), and
[[Tenten]] (weapons and fūinjutsu), led by [[Might Guy]].

## Roster

- **[[Might Guy]]** (sensei) — Kakashi Hatake's lifelong rival, taijutsu master; wheelchair-
  bound Academy teacher since the Fourth Shinobi World War.
- **[[Rock Lee]]** — Guy's protégé, taijutsu-only prodigy; jōnin, husband, and father to
  Metal Lee by the *New Era*.
- **Neji Hyuga** — Byakugan prodigy; **dies** in the Fourth Shinobi World War shielding
  Naruto Uzumaki and Hinata Hyuga — see [[Neji Hyūga]].
- **[[Tenten]]** — weapons/fūinjutsu specialist; opens a ninja tool shop and becomes a
  Chūnin Exams proctor by the *New Era*.

## Notable facts

- Along with [[Team 8]] and [[Team 7]]'s Part II lineup, Team Guy is the only Konoha 11 team
  to lose a member during Part II — Neji Hyuga, during the Fourth Shinobi World War (Team 8
  and Team Kakashi lose none; [[Team 10]] loses its sensei, Asuma Sarutobi).
- Recurring operational overlap with [[Team 7]]: backs it up on the Kazekage Rescue Mission
  and the Sasuke Recovery Mission specifically.
- The three surviving members (Guy, Lee, Tenten) all reach the *New Era*'s Kawaki Arc,
  fighting and quickly losing to Isshiki's invasion, but none of their sources continue into
  the *New Era: Part II* crisis chapters covered on [[Team 7]] — their status during Naruto's
  disappearance is unconfirmed.
