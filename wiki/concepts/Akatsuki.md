# Akatsuki

Criminal organization operating outside the hidden villages' system, responsible for most of
Part II's antagonist pressure. Went through four distinct "eras" under different leadership,
each pursuing "peace" through radically different — and progressively more destructive —
means. Secretly a tool of Black Zetsu's centuries-long plan to revive Kaguya Ōtsutsuki, a
scheme running through [[Madara Uchiha]] and [[Zetsu]] since before the organization's
founding.

## Eras

- **Yahiko's Akatsuki**: founded by war orphans [[Yahiko]], [[Nagato]], and [[Konan]] of
  Amegakure — trained by [[Jiraiya]] — to end the Five Great Nations' proxy wars in their home
  country. Ends when Danzō Shimura and Hanzō ambush the founders, forcing Yahiko's suicide.
- **Nagato's Akatsuki**: [[Nagato]], radicalized by Yahiko's death into the "Pain" persona
  (using Yahiko's own corpse as the Deva Path), redirects the group toward a philosophy of
  shock-and-awe destruction to force world peace. This is the Akatsuki most of the *Naruto*
  story is fought against — recruiting S-rank missing-nin ([[Orochimaru]], [[Itachi Uchiha]],
  [[Kisame Hoshigaki]], [[Deidara]], [[Sasori]], [[Kakuzu]], [[Hidan]]) to hunt down and seal
  the nine tailed beasts. Ends with Nagato's death and redemption during Pain's Assault on
  Konoha, and [[Konan]]'s defection.
- **Tobi's (Obito's) Akatsuki**: [[Obito Uchiha]], posing as [[Madara Uchiha]], had been
  secretly steering Nagato's Akatsuki the entire time toward his real goal — reviving the
  Ten-Tails to cast the Infinite Tsukuyomi. Openly takes over after Nagato's death,
  reincarnates past members via Kabuto Yakushi's Impure World Reincarnation for the Fourth
  Shinobi World War, and is ultimately defeated alongside Kaguya Ōtsutsuki's revival and
  re-sealing — the war that kills nearly every member covered in this ingest.
- **Shin's Akatsuki**: a minor, failed revival roughly fifteen years after the war by Shin
  Uchiha, a former Orochimaru test subject fixated on [[Itachi Uchiha]]'s legacy; ends when
  Shin's own clones betray and kill him.

## The true architect: Madara Uchiha and Zetsu

[[Madara Uchiha]]'s biography is the master key connecting every era above: he secretly
implanted his own Rinnegan into infant [[Nagato]], recruited and manipulated [[Obito Uchiha]]
into carrying his name and plan forward, and unknowingly harbored [[Zetsu]] (specifically
Black Zetsu) inside his own body for years, believing it a manifestation of his own will. In
truth, Black Zetsu had been manipulating Madara — and by extension the entire organization —
for centuries in service of reviving Kaguya Ōtsutsuki. Every Akatsuki leader, including
Madara himself, was ultimately a pawn in this longer scheme.

## Notable cross-cutting facts

- Killed several [[Team 10]]/[[Team 7]]-adjacent characters directly: [[Hidan]] and [[Kakuzu]]
  kill [[Asuma Sarutobi]] (see his page); Akatsuki as a whole is the reason [[Team 7]] and its
  backup teams spend most of Part II on manhunts and rescue missions.
- Nearly every member of Nagato's-era Akatsuki dies during the Fourth Shinobi World War or
  its immediate lead-up — [[Hidan]] is the sole exception, left alive but unable to escape.
