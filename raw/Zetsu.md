**White Zetsu** (白ゼツ, *Shiro Zetsu*) was half of the [Akatsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki "Akatsuki") member **Zetsu** (ゼツ, *Zetsu*), the other being [Black Zetsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Zetsu "Black Zetsu"). He was one of the first victims of [Kaguya Ōtsutsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kaguya_%C5%8Ctsutsuki "Kaguya Ōtsutsuki") 's [Infinite Tsukuyomi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Infinite_Tsukuyomi "Infinite Tsukuyomi"), and was eventually pulled from the [Demonic Statue of the Outer Path](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Statue_of_the_Outer_Path "Demonic Statue of the Outer Path") by Black Zetsu,[^4] and infused with the [DNA](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hashirama%27s_Artificial_Body "Hashirama's Artificial Body") of [Hashirama Senju](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hashirama_Senju "Hashirama Senju") by [Madara Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Madara_Uchiha "Madara Uchiha").[^5]

## Background

![White Zetsu and Tobi overseeing Obito's rehabilitation.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/bd/White_%26_Guruguru.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140116202944)

White Zetsu and Tobi overseeing Obito's rehabilitation.

White Zetsu was originally one of the victims of Kaguya Ōtsutsuki's Infinite Tsukuyomi, who was transformed after being [tethered](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/God:_Nativity_of_a_World_of_Trees "God: Nativity of a World of Trees") to the [God Tree](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/God_Tree "God Tree") over the course of years and ultimately stored within the Demonic Statue of the Outer Path. After Madara Uchiha [summoned](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning_Technique "Summoning Technique") the statue and cultivated Hashirama Senju's DNA into a [large flowering tree](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hashirama%27s_Artificial_Body "Hashirama's Artificial Body"), Black Zetsu removed White Zetsu and the other victims from inside the husk, leading Madara to believe they were artificial humans created by the statue's [chakra](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra "Chakra") and Hashirama's DNA.[^6] [^4] White Zetsu and other mutated humans were then tasked with intelligence gathering before he and a [spiral-patterned Zetsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tobi_\(Zetsu\) "Tobi (Zetsu)") were entrusted with looking after the rehabilitating [Obito Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Obito_Uchiha "Obito Uchiha"). Over time, the duo became friends with the young Uchiha.[^7]

When Obito's rehabilitation was near completion, White Zetsu returned to the [base](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mountains%27_Graveyard "Mountains' Graveyard") and informed Obito that [Kakashi Hatake](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi_Hatake "Kakashi Hatake") and [Rin Nohara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rin_Nohara "Rin Nohara") were being attacked by [Kirigakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kirigakure "Kirigakure") shinobi. When the spiral-patterned Zetsu offered to help Obito by encasing himself around the young Uchiha in order to enable him to leave the cave, White Zetsu served to guide Obito telepathically with the information he received from other Zetsu stationed outside.[^7] [^8] When they returned to Madara, White Zetsu assured him that no one had seen them and relayed information on what had happened after the arrival of [Konoha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha "Konoha") reinforcements, noting that Obito didn't kill Kakashi.[^9]

![Black Zetsu imbuing itself onto White Zetsu.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/85/Will_Materialisation.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150910041153)

Black Zetsu imbuing itself onto White Zetsu.

When Madara was on the verge of death, he [imbued](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Body_Coating "Body Coating") Black Zetsu into White Zetsu's right half, believing the former was a manifestation of his [will](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Will_Materialisation "Will Materialisation").[^10] Zetsu later accompanied Obito to [Amegakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Amegakure "Amegakure") to locate [Nagato](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nagato "Nagato"), who had unknowingly been given Madara's [Rinnegan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rinnegan "Rinnegan") as a child, and watched as Obito introduced himself using Madara's identity.[^11]

## Personality

Unlike Black Zetsu, White Zetsu, nicknamed "Chatterbox" (饒舌, *Jōzetsu*), was carefree and playful with a habit of teasing others, traits shared by his clone siblings.[^3] In the Japanese version of the manga, White Zetsu used [kanji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanji "wikipedia:Kanji") and [kana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kana "wikipedia:Kana") regularly. This was often conveyed in the anime through a higher-pitched voice. While he and Black Zetsu sometimes had differing opinions, the two halves were still able to function as a single entity.

White Zetsu appeared to be extremely trustworthy, obedient and loyal, being entrusted with Akatsuki's secrets and knowledge of Madara's [Eye of the Moon Plan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eye_of_the_Moon_Plan "Eye of the Moon Plan"), which he believed to be beneficial to mankind.[^12] Because of his ties to Madara, White Zetsu worked with Obito for a long time, originally helping him find and convince Nagato to join them. He was shown to be quite friendly to most Akatsuki members, indicated by how he was saddened that so many had died in a conversation about them with Tobi; in the anime, one of his clones freed a captured Deidara, saying he was a fun guy to be around in Akatsuki. Though he annoyed Obito at first, White Zetsu eventually won the boy's friendship.[^7] Like [Tobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tobi_\(Zetsu\) "Tobi (Zetsu)"), White Zetsu believed Madara was a good man at heart and saw no harm in helping Obito without Madara's permission.[^13]

Zetsu's main role within Akatsuki was to function as a spy, watching the actions of both the Akatsuki and their enemies,[^14] with other duties that were similar to those of a [hunter-nin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hunter-nin "Hunter-nin"). He was able to control his body and take part in the [tailed beast](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_beast "Tailed beast") [sealing ritual](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sealing_Technique:_Phantom_Dragons_Nine_Consuming_Seals "Sealing Technique: Phantom Dragons Nine Consuming Seals") simultaneously, which, in addition to being unique to him, allowed him to act as a lookout while the sealing took place.[^15] When an agent of Akatsuki, or possibly even a member, died, Zetsu was sent to devour their bodies so as to not have their secrets revealed, further adding to his nature as a half- [plant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venus_flytrap "wikipedia:Venus flytrap") creature.[^16] Zetsu was also the one sent to retrieve the rings of dead Akatsuki members.[^17] Due to the role he played, Zetsu was the only member of Akatsuki to act without a partner on a regular basis. He operated on his own, often moving between the different locations where the other Akatsuki members were located. This resulted in other members reporting directly to him, rather than the figurehead leader, [Pain](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Pain "Pain"). Zetsu would in turn report directly to Pain as well as Obito, the organisation's benefactor and eventual leader.[^18]

## Appearance

When together, the two Zetsu were easily distinguished by the two [Venus flytrap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venus_flytrap "wikipedia:Venus flytrap") -like extensions that emerged from their sides, enveloping their head and upper body as a shell, which they were able to open and close. Beneath these extensions, White Zetsu had short green hair and a single yellow eye. The right half of his body was covered by Black Zetsu, whose leg was originally White Zetsu's own right leg. Under their Akatsuki cloak, they wore blue pants and sandals, and had both legs wrapped in bandages. Zetsu's green Akatsuki ring, which bore the kanji for "sign of the boar" (亥, *gai*), was worn on Black Zetsu's little finger. In the anime, Zetsu's fingernails were painted pink and his toenails, as seen in his [début](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/The_End_of_Tears "The End of Tears"), were blue.

Unlike Black Zetsu, White Zetsu had human-like facial features and a proper eye, as well as unusually rounded teeth. When he was first awakened by Madara, his Venus flytrap-like protrusions were not fully formed, being white in colour as well as sticking out all along the right side of his body. When the two halves split apart, their plant-like extensions would separate and hang from the waists of both. Any clothing they were wearing, including their Akatsuki mantle, would split along with them, usually causing them to discard their torn clothing. When either of the halves [emerged](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mayfly "Mayfly") from the ground while split, their plant-like extensions would surround them in a circular formation. The two also demonstrated the ability to grow an additional leg, though deformed, from their white body mass to substitute for their missing half while split.

## Abilities

White Zetsu was described by Obito as not being a "front line fighter" [^19] and thus never directly participated in battles. However, by using several supporting abilities, he proved to be of great use for his [allies](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki "Akatsuki") and [master](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Madara_Uchiha "Madara Uchiha").

### Ninjutsu

In addition to functioning as a single entity with Black Zetsu by communicating through thoughts, White Zetsu was able to split his body in two, allowing them to act independently. While separated, the two halves could extend roots underground to communicate with each other over long distances.[^20]

![Zetsu physically splitting in two.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/e/e2/Zetsu_dividing.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20151004011115)

Zetsu physically splitting in two.

He was able to create and remotely communicate with near-identical [clones](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Parasite_Clone "Parasite Clone") of himself, which could [absorb](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_Absorption_Techniques "Chakra Absorption Techniques") a person's chakra. With his [Spore Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Spore_Technique "Spore Technique"), White Zetsu was able to plant these clones on the bodies of others in the form of spores, which were undetectable even to [sensor type](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sensor_type "Sensor type") and [Kage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kage "Kage") -level shinobi. Once activated, these spores would grow into a white mass which surrounded the individuals they formed on and absorb their chakra while restricting their movements. The clones could also use this stolen chakra to [replenish](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_Transfer_Technique "Chakra Transfer Technique") an ally's chakra supply by attaching themselves to him or her.[^21] Obito had White Zetsu attach several of these spores to [Sasuke Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Uchiha "Sasuke Uchiha") in order to monitor and even assist him when necessary.[^22]

#### Substitute Technique

White Zetsu claimed to have the best transformation technique and was able to freely alter his form and chakra signature.[^2] Doing so allowed him to become or create a completely indistinguishable [substitute](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Substitute_Technique "Substitute Technique") of another individual, even becoming suited with their tools. By manipulating the nature of his chakra, he was also able to emulate techniques of which is known to him. Because White Zetsu was typically weaker than the one he was imitating, he mainly used this technique through his clones to serve as decoys or for infiltration and assassination.[^23] Even after suffering tremendous damage, or even death, the technique would not cancel until he chose to release it.

Despite not being suited for prolonged battle, White Zetsu showed considerable skill in emulating the personalities and battle tactics of his target. It was because of this characteristic that the entire [Allied Shinobi Forces](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Allied_Shinobi_Forces "Allied Shinobi Forces") was forced to be in constant paranoia and sometimes turned on one another. Though with keen analysis, one could exploit his lack of knowledge on certain subjects. Those who could [sense negative emotions](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Negative_Emotions_Sensing "Negative Emotions Sensing") could also take advantage of his true intentions.

#### Nature Transformation

![Zetsu merged with the ground.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/d/de/Mayfly.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150220084111)

Zetsu merged with the ground.

White Zetsu was capable of utilising all five basic [nature transformations](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nature_transformation "Nature transformation") along with [Yin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yin "Yin") and [Yang Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yang_Release "Yang Release").[^2] Because he was tethered to the [flowering tree](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hashirama%27s_Artificial_Body "Hashirama's Artificial Body") grown from Hashirama Senju's DNA, White Zetsu's genetic material and chakra was identical to that of the latter's. Like Hashirama, he was able to use [Wood Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Release "Wood Release"), albeit on a much weaker scale.[^24]

Zetsu's signature technique was [Mayfly](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mayfly "Mayfly"), which allowed him to merge with the ground to quickly travel to a new location, though not as quickly as Obito's [space–time travelling](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kamui "Kamui").[^25] This technique was ideal for Zetsu's function as a spy, as it erased his presence and prevented him from being sensed.

### Other Skills

Being a mutated human, White Zetsu was able to survive without food or water and did not need to carry out normal human bodily functions.[^26] He could, however, quickly devour another individual completely, as he was normally tasked to dispose of dead Akatsuki members or allies that way.[^27] Doing so also serves to supplement its own chakra reserves.[^2] Befitting his role as a spy, White Zetsu was able to put up a warning system, allowing him to monitor a vast area.[^1]

Being almost entirely composed with the cells of Hashirama Senju, White Zetsu had the capacity to [heal nearly any wound](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Regeneration_Ability "Regeneration Ability"), even without the use of hand seals.[^2] It's because of this that White Zetsu was able to aid his companions by grafting his body parts onto them, should they lose any major portion of their anatomy. Those aided in this way are also gifted White Zetsu's healing capabilities.[^2]

## Part II

### Kazekage Rescue Mission

Main article: [Kazekage Rescue Mission](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kazekage_Rescue_Mission "Kazekage Rescue Mission") During the extraction of the [One-Tail](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/One-Tail "One-Tail"), Zetsu is told by the [Akatsuki Leader](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nagato "Nagato") to use his real body to use as a lookout, in which he identifies several enemies such as [Team Guy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Guy "Team Guy"), [Team Kakashi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Kakashi "Team Kakashi"), as well as [Chiyo](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chiyo "Chiyo") who was with the latter.[^30] Later, he is sent to dispose the corpses of [Mukade](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mukade "Mukade") and [Yura](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yura "Yura"), finding Mukade a delicious meal.[^31]

After [Sasori](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasori "Sasori") and [Deidara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Deidara "Deidara") were defeated, Zetsu and [Tobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tobi "Tobi") were sent to retrieve their rings, and dispose of their corpses. However, he was unable to eat Sasori due to him being a puppet, but was amazed to finally see what the real Sasori looked like. Tobi found Sasori's ring, wishing to take his place. Black Zetsu thought this was a bad idea, but White Zetsu thought otherwise, noting that Tobi was a good person.[^32] They later found Deidara's severed right arm and believe him dead until he arrived and proved them wrong. This caused Black Zetsu to wonder why Deidara hadn't captured [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki"), to which Deidara replied that he had already fulfilled his quota either way.[^33]

Kakuzu, disagreeing stated that money was all one could count on. White Zetsu agreed that money was important, however Black Zetsu stated finding the next [one](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki "Jinchūriki") was much more important. Zetsu is later seen sealing the [Two-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Two-Tails "Two-Tails").[^34]

Main article: [Itachi Pursuit Mission](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Itachi_Pursuit_Mission "Itachi Pursuit Mission") After Deidara's death Zetsu was sent to recover his body, with White Zetsu wondering what happened to Tobi. Black Zetsu mentioned that based on the damaged area, he was most likely caught up in Deidara's explosion.[^35] Later, when Pain had encouraged the organisation to mourn Deidara's loss, White Zetsu took it on himself to mourn for Tobi's loss, while Black Zetsu thought such a notion was ridiculous.[^36]

Main article: [Tale of Jiraiya the Gallant](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tale_of_Jiraiya_the_Gallant "Tale of Jiraiya the Gallant") After Pain's fight with Jiraiya, Zetsu revealed himself to have been watching the whole battle. ### Fated Battle Between Brothers

Main article: [Fated Battle Between Brothers](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fated_Battle_Between_Brothers "Fated Battle Between Brothers") Both halves spectated and commented on the battle, with Black Zetsu offering knowledge on various Sharingan abilities. When Itachi died, Zetsu noted that Itachi should not have been that weak, believing him to have already been afflicted prior to the battle. He later appeared near Tobi while he was engaged in combat with the [Eight Man Squad](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eight_Man_Squad "Eight Man Squad"), to report to him the result of the fight.[^38] They then led Tobi to where Itachi and Sasuke were, with Black Zetsu confirming to Tobi that he had indeed *recorded* the fight.[^25] After a meeting with Taka and Kisame, Tobi met with Zetsu on a bridge where they discussed the fact that Itachi — who had been hampering their plans — was dead and Zetsu lamented on the fact that so many Akatsuki members had died. Tobi said their deaths were worth it because Sasuke was in his hands now.[^39]

### Five Kage Summit

Main article: [Five Kage Summit](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Five_Kage_Summit "Five Kage Summit") Zetsu finds Tobi after the latter had encountered Taka, revealing that Konoha had already been destroyed, and that [Danzō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Danz%C5%8D "Danzō") had been named the acting [Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage "Hokage"). He then mentioned to Sasuke that Naruto had defeated Pain, and was probably stronger than him. When Taka agreed to go to the [Kage Summit](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kage_Summit "Kage Summit"), Zetsu split in half, and White Zetsu proceeded to lead Sasuke and his team to the Kage Summit.

After identifying Danzō, White Zetsu managed to slip away from Taka unnoticed. White Zetsu later emerged in the conference room, telling the five Kage that Sasuke was somewhere around. After revealing himself, the [Fourth Raikage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Raikage "Fourth Raikage") grabbed the white Zetsu's neck, asked him about the whereabouts of Sasuke, and threatened to use force. Zetsu mockingly replied that he would give a hint, which prompted the Raikage, angered, to tighten his grip on Zetsu's neck. With his neck snapped, the White Zetsu collapsed on the floor and was presumed to be dead by everyone in the vicinity.[^41]

Before he could engage in combat however, Tobi tells him not to as he wasn't a 'front line' fighter, and instead gives him the task of checking up on [Kisame](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kisame "Kisame") and rejoining with Black Zetsu.[^43]

Black Zetsu then stated that it was their feeding time and that they should merge once again.[^44]

### Fourth Shinobi World War: Countdown

## Creation and Conception

![Concept design for Zetsu.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/7/7a/Zetsu_Concept.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/150?cb=20090404000142)

Concept design for Zetsu.

In *[Naruto Hiden: Sha no Sho Character Official Data Book](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sha_no_Sho "Sha no Sho")*, Kishimoto revealed that he had originally planned for Akatsuki to be a group of monsters, "with almost no human characteristics", and that Zetsu's design was modelled after an insectivorous plant. He also stated that he had decided to make Zetsu half black and half white to "better show his split personality".

## Trivia

- The name "Zetsu" (舌) means "tongue". A fitting name, as White Zetsu was referred to as "Chatterbox" (饒舌, *Jōzetsu*) based on his personality.[^3]
- According to the [databook](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sha_no_Sho "Sha no Sho"):
	- Zetsu's hobby is observing unique shinobi.
		- Zetsu wishes to fight people who are likely to leave behind a corpse that will make a good meal.
		- Zetsu's favourite food is ["chewy" people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cannibalism "wikipedia:Cannibalism"), while his least favourites are [konnyaku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Konjac "wikipedia:Konjac") and jelly.
		- Zetsu's favourite phrase is "Independence unbending" (独立不撓, *Dokuritsu Futō*), which roughly means "The only one you can count on is yourself".
		- Wherever Tobi is, Zetsu is never far behind.
- The White Zetsu Army referred to this White Zetsu as their "Original" (オリジナル, *Orijinaru*).[^51]
- In the [fourth databook](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jin_no_Sho "Jin no Sho"), [kekkei genkai](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kekkei_genkai "Kekkei genkai") is not listed among White Zetsu's special characteristics, despite his usage of Wood Release through [Hashirama's cells](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hashirama%27s_Living_Clone "Hashirama's Living Clone") in the manga.[^2]
- White Zetsu and his clones are not dissimilar to the legends told of the [mandrake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandrake_\(plant\) "wikipedia:Mandrake (plant)") plant used to create [artificial men](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homunculus "wikipedia:Homunculus").
- When White Zetsu is killed by Sasuke, his flytrap appendages are missing in both anime and manga.
- In the anime, White Zetsu's eye occasionally has a pupil not present in the manga.
