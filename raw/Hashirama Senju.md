**Hashirama Senju** (千手柱間, *Senju Hashirama*) was a member and leader of the famed [Senju clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Senju_clan "Senju clan") and a [reincarnation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Reincarnation "Reincarnation") and descendant of [Asura Ōtsutsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Asura_%C5%8Ctsutsuki "Asura Ōtsutsuki"). He himself was hailed as the "God of Shinobi" for his unmatched ninja prowess. Hashirama sought peace for the shinobi world, and to that end founded [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure") with his clan, his childhood friend and rival: [Madara Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Madara_Uchiha "Madara Uchiha"), and the [Uchiha clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uchiha_clan "Uchiha clan"). He later became the **First [Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage "Hokage")** (初代火影, *Shodai Hokage*, *literally meaning:* First or Founding Fire Shadow) of Konohagakure. Although he was not able to achieve peace during his lifetime, his legacy and vision for the world continued to shape the village and the entire shinobi world even after his death.

## Background

![Hashirama meeting Madara.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/1/14/Madara_and_hasirama2.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140703222619)

Hashirama meeting Madara.

Hashirama was born during the [Warring States Period](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Warring_States_Period "Warring States Period"), the eldest of [Butsuma Senju](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Butsuma_Senju "Butsuma Senju") 's four sons. Hashirama and his brothers grew up on the battlefield waging constant war with the Senju's rivals: the [Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uchiha "Uchiha"). During his infrequent downtime, Hashirama met a boy his own age named [Madara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Madara "Madara"). The two quickly developed a friendly rivalry, be it skipping stones or urinating in rivers, with Hashirama typically winning their contests.[^6] Hashirama did not approve of this practice of sending children to fight and die, believing it would only increase the bitterness between the Senju and Uchiha, thus causing more deaths.

After the deaths of their brothers, [Kawarama](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kawarama "Kawarama") and later [Itama](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Itama "Itama"), Hashirama and his remaining brother, [Tobirama](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tobirama "Tobirama"), decided a new shinobi system would need to be implemented to end the cycle of child deaths, a system that would require an alliance between the Senju and Uchiha. Like Hashirama, Madara was also a shinobi who had lost brothers on the battlefield. Together they imagined a world where children like themselves wouldn't need to fight and where their brothers would be safe from harm.[^7] As a precaution, Hashirama and Madara did not divulge their family names, but nevertheless discovered each other's identities: Hashirama was a Senju, Madara was an Uchiha; it was their duty to kill each other. Hashirama set this duty aside, unwilling to take his friend's life. Madara, however, decided their dreams of a peaceful world were impossible, and therefore ended their friendship so that they could kill each other without reservation.[^8]

![Hashirama and Madara clash on the battlefield.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/7/7d/Madara_and_Hashirama_combat.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20211022021902)

Hashirama and Madara clash on the battlefield.

Over the following years Hashirama and Madara continued to meet in combat. Hashirama could never bring himself to kill someone he still considered a friend, and Madara could never defeat Hashirama's superior abilities. In time, both Madara and Hashirama became leaders of their respective clans. Under Hashirama's leadership and extraordinary ninja prowess, the Senju began to best the Uchiha Clan until at one point Hashirama had both Madara and Izuna at his mercy. However, unable to find the resolve to kill his childhood friend, Hashirama tried to convince Madara to unite their two clans to make their childhood dreams a reality. A mortally wounded Izuna convinced Madara otherwise and they retreated, prompting Hashirama to send a cease-fire request. Although some Uchiha found the offer increasingly tempting, Madara was unwilling to accept after Tobirama killed his last remaining brother, [Izuna](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Izuna "Izuna"). This did not stop some Uchiha from defecting to the Senju out of self-preservation.[^9] Wracked with grief, Madara made one final stand against Hashirama and the Senju and was summarily defeated. Tobirama attempted to kill Madara to finally end the fighting, but Hashirama stopped him, knowing that would only renew hostilities between the Senju and Uchiha. At Madara's suggestion, Hashirama offered to kill himself in order to achieve a peaceful resolution. As he was about to end his own life, Madara stopped him, moved by the gesture, and assented to peace.[^10]

![Konohagakure is formed.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/d/d2/Senju_%26_Uchiha_truce.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140220202610)

Konohagakure is formed.

The Senju, the Uchiha, and all their affiliated clans came together to found a village of peace, where children would never need to die in battle. Hashirama and Madara, rekindling their childhood friendship, called that village [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure"). The other countries would soon follow Konohagakure's example and build their own hidden villages. It was his wish that Madara become [Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage "Hokage"), the village's leader and protector.[^11] At Tobirama's insistence, the Hokage position was democratically elected, and in the end it was Hashirama that was chosen for the office.[^12] [^13] Hashirama wanted Madara to work with him as a brother and as his right hand so that he could improve his standing among the villagers and someday succeed him as Second Hokage. Madara felt that position would inevitably go to Tobirama, which would in turn end badly for the Uchiha. With his position in Konoha rapidly diminishing and after being manipulated by the stone tablet [Zetsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Zetsu "Black Zetsu") altered,[^14] Madara decided the village was a failed experiment and abandoned it, intent on someday returning to face Hashirama in battle yet again.[^15]

![Hashirama fighting Madara and the Nine-Tails.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/6/64/Hashirama_vs_Madara.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150215010118)

Hashirama fighting Madara and the Nine-Tails.

True to his promise, Madara eventually attacked Konohagakure on multiple occasions.[^16] After countless fights and with the [Nine-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tails "Nine-Tails") under his control, Madara challenged Hashirama to one final contest.[^17] The Nine-Tails' power, as well as Hashirama's efforts to capture and contain it, devastated the landscape, carving out what would later be called the [Valley of the End](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Valley_of_the_End "Valley of the End"). Hashirama spent the duration of their battle trying to reason with Madara, but Madara continued fighting until he was too tired to keep even his [Sharingan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sharingan "Sharingan") active. This enabled Hashirama to distract him with a [wood clone](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_clone "Wood clone") while the real Hashirama stabbed him in the back. Madara collapsed and, before dying, noted how much Hashirama had changed from when they were children.[^18]

![Hashirama attending the first Kage Summit.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/2e/First_Gokage_summit.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20250902131617)

Hashirama attending the first Kage Summit.

In the aftermath of the battle, Tobirama hid away Madara's body and Hashirama's wife, [Mito](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mito "Mito"), sealed the Nine-Tails within herself in order to contain its great power to Konoha's benefit.[^19] For a time, things were peaceful: Hashirama helped train one of Tobirama's students, [Hiruzen Sarutobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hiruzen_Sarutobi "Hiruzen Sarutobi") [^20]; he got to spoil his first grandchild, [Tsunade](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tsunade "Tsunade");[^21] he locked away the [Scroll of Seals](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Scroll_of_Seals "Scroll of Seals"), the [kinjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kinjutsu "Kinjutsu") it contained no longer necessary in the world he'd made.[^22] But the peace Hashirama had worked so hard for was starting to unravel; at some point [Takigakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Takigakure "Takigakure") sent [Kakuzu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakuzu "Kakuzu") to assassinate him.[^23] Although he defeated Kakuzu, other wars began to break out.

In an unknown time, Hashirama convened a [Kage Summit](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kage_Summit "Kage Summit") so that he could share the [tailed beasts](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_beasts "Tailed beasts") Konoha had acquired with them, thus balancing power amongst them. But cooperation would not be so simple as Hashirama intended it to be, first by Tobirama's demand that the other villages pay for the tailed beasts they received, then by the [First Kazekage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/First_Kazekage "First Kazekage") 's counter-request of money and territory in lieu of a tailed beast. As unrest began to grow, Hashirama shared his fear that any agreement they made would only be temporary, but also that future generations could forge a lasting unity.[^24]

![Hashirama buried.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/c/c4/Hashirama%27s_Coffin.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20241219025911)

Hashirama buried.

Ultimately, Hashirama's wish for peace would not come to fruition during his lifetime and he died while Konohagakure began to flourish,[^1] with it being regarded as Hashirama dying in vein while in the midst of pursing his dream.[^25] Before his death he passed the title of Hokage to Tobirama, and instructed him not to mistreat the Uchiha.[^26] Hashirama's body was buried, and stayed intact long enough for [Orochimaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Orochimaru "Orochimaru") to extract DNA from.[^27]

## Personality

![Hashirama brooding about memories of Tsunade.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/3/3b/Hashirama_depressed.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20141026153705)

Hashirama brooding about memories of Tsunade.

Hashirama was an excitable man with a boisterous personality. He loved meeting new people, having friendly competitions, and gambling. Headstrong and often impulsive, it fell to others – such as his brother, Tobirama – to be more responsible or realistic about things that Hashirama had gotten carried away with.[^28] When taken down in this way, or even when simply presented with information that troubled him, Hashirama could do a near-immediate about-face, going from laughing to sulking. These moping periods were typically brief though, and he'd quickly go back to his usual high-spirited nature.[^29] Later generations upon meeting him, tended to remark that neither extremes of his personality were very fitting for a man called the " [God of Shinobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/God_of_Shinobi "God of Shinobi") ".[^21]

Despite the first impression he may give, Hashirama was capable of being serious when it came to matters that were important to him. He spent years opposing the practice of sending children to war and dreamed of a shinobi system where a child could be around others their age, never needing to "grow up" prematurely on the battlefield.[^7] To that end he became very devoted to Konoha upon its foundation, as it was the realisation of his dream for peaceful childhoods. He wanted the rest of the village to cherish what Konoha represented just as much as he did, encouraging them to regard every villager as part of one large family that should always look out for each other. As Hokage, his job was to protect that family at all costs, even if that cost was his life. This philosophy, known as the " [Will of Fire](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Will_of_Fire "Will of Fire") ", would become a cornerstone of Konoha teachings for decades after his death.

![Hashirama offering his life without hesitation for peace.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/28/Hashirama_ready_to_commit.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20141026172520)

Hashirama offering his life without hesitation for peace.

The Will of Fire is demonstrative of Hashirama's general optimism when it came to other people; he believed a village-first loyalty would naturally break down the mentality that shinobi had to look out for their clans.[^30] Hashirama lived by this philosophy long before Konoha's founding, having no qualms about killing himself if it meant peace between the Uchiha and Senju clans. In fact, his entire goal of gaining more power was to have enough just to stop violence. One example of this was he believed if the [two most](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Senju_Clan "Senju Clan") [powerful clans](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uchiha_Clan "Uchiha Clan") during the Warring States Period were to join forces, there would be no other shinobi clan to stand against them and would stop selling their services, effectively ending the Warring States Period. He always saw the best in others, either trusting that they acted in good faith or that they could, through non-violent means, be reasoned with. He was humble, not above lowering his head to his fellow village heads [^31] or claiming inferiority to those he'd never met but whose actions he approved of.[^32] If nothing else, Hashirama was kind, placing the needs of others before his own: he was even willing to take his own life if it would help create a world where his friends and family wouldn't need to die unnecessarily.[^33] It was on these points that Tobirama disagreed with Hashirama most ardently, as Tobirama felt that Hashirama should have a greater value of himself and a greater suspicion of others. It was, similarly, on these points that Hashirama was at his most forceful, asserting his views over Tobirama's; though Tobirama did not often agree, he knew better than to oppose his brother.[^34] Hashirama is also well-balanced on this regard as he didn't exert his force over Tobirama over past actions that cannot be changed such as when finding out Tobirama seemingly mistreated the Uchiha, he expressed anger, but didn't exert his force as it didn't do any good.

![Hashirama's intimidating demeanour.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/88/Hashirama%27s_intimidating_demeanour.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20211022021528)

Hashirama's intimidating demeanour.

Hashirama had a lifelong friendship and rivalry with Madara Uchiha, eventually considering him a brother.[^35] As children, they would spend much of their free time together, talking, playing, and planning for a future that they weren't sure would ever come. Both were willing to oppose their own fathers by refusing to kill each other, but only Hashirama kept up this refusal into adulthood;[^36] even as enemies, Hashirama would go out of his way for Madara, restraining himself (and Tobirama) so as not to hurt him and indulging Madara's desires at his own consequence.[^37] When they were able to rekindle their friendship by making Konoha, Hashirama placed a great deal of trust in Madara by desiring him to be Hokage, believing it was the best way for Madara to recover from the loss of his brothers. When this ultimately failed, Hashirama respected Madara's opinions and dissatisfaction with the village, but would not permit him to move against Konoha, killing him in the end with sadness and reluctance in order to protect what they'd made together.[^38] Despite everything, Hashirama considered Madara his friend to the very end while admitting that the two of them tried to do everything by themselves to fulfill their respective dreams instead of cultivating them across their generations.[^39]

## Appearance

- ![Hashirama as a child.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/2e/Kid_Hashirama.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/218?cb=20230322145136)
	Hashirama as a child.

1/2

Hashirama was a tall man with tanned skin and dark eyes. He had waist-length black hair typically styled in a centre-parted fringe that framed his face. He often wore the standard shinobi dress of his era, consisting of dark red armour worn over a simple black suit with sandals. This armour was constructed from numerous metal plates, formed into multiple protective guards along his body; each collar of his shoulder guards bore the Senju symbol. Prior to Konoha's formation he wore a white-coloured cloth bearing his clan's symbol on his forehead,[^40] which he later replaced with the village's [forehead protector](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Forehead_protector "Forehead protector").

![Hashirama in Hokage garb.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/24/Shodai_Hokage.PNG/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20100206180921)

Hashirama in Hokage garb.

Upon becoming Hokage, Hashirama tended to wear the traditional Kage attire consisting of the customary maroon and white hat and haori over a full-length maroon and white gown, along with a simple red headband.[^41] Otherwise, he wore a light-brown, short-sleeved, kimono shirt with mesh armour underneath, held closed by a red sash, and navy blue pants. In his Hokage portrait, he is seen wearing a kerchief.[^42]

As a child, Hashirama's hair was more bowl-cut in style, hanging around his face. His usual attire consisted of a dark kimono shirt, a short light green jacket with darker green linings, a white scarf around his neck, traditional pinstriped kimono pants of his clan, and a dark green sash around his waist.[^43]

## Abilities

![Hashirama clashing with his life-long rival, Madara.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/f/f1/Hashirama_and_Madara_clash.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140619121232)

Hashirama clashing with his life-long rival, Madara.

Hashirama was famed as the strongest shinobi of his era and one of the strongest in history, reputed as a [God of Shinobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/God_of_Shinobi "God of Shinobi").[^5] Madara, another legendary shinobi, openly admitted inferiority to Hashirama throughout their entire lifetime together, even with both the [Eternal Mangekyō Sharingan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Mangeky%C5%8D_Sharingan "Eternal Mangekyō Sharingan") and the [Nine-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tails "Nine-Tails") at his disposal.[^44] Through his [unique powers](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Release "Wood Release"), Hashirama had been able to subdue and capture eight of the nine free-roaming [tailed beasts](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_beasts "Tailed beasts") on his own.[^45] During the Fourth Shinobi World War, Kabuto proclaimed that the man's might as a ninja was so vast that it was held in a fantasy light similar to the [Sage of Six Paths](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sage_of_Six_Paths "Sage of Six Paths"), stating that no one, aside from Madara, had come close to matching him ever since his passing.[^46]

### Chakra and Physical Prowess

As a Senju, Hashirama possessed a powerful life-force and physical energy. This made his [chakra](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra "Chakra") remarkably strong, shown by his ability to release powerful shockwaves that damage his surroundings simply by flaring up his chakra.[^47] During the Fourth Shinobi World War, after seeing [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki") distribute his and the Nine-Tails' chakra to the entire [Allied Shinobi Forces](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Allied_Shinobi_Forces "Allied Shinobi Forces"), Hashirama claimed that the distributed chakra amount almost rivalled his own reserves.[^48] His chakra control is advanced enough to perform various complex and large-scale jutsu with only a single [hand seals](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hand_seal "Hand seal"), if any at all.[^49] Hashirama was also able to produce ten wood clones while maintaining a [Kage-level barrier](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Four_Red_Yang_Formation "Four Red Yang Formation").

Hashirama's life-force resilient nature also granted him enough stamina to fight nonstop for over 24 hours with no decline,[^50] and could resist the control of the perfected [Impure World Reincarnation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Impure_World_Reincarnation "Impure World Reincarnation") with ease, even after his cells were used to strengthen the technique's binding.[^51] His body was brimming with vitality, granting him considerable [regenerative powers](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Regeneration_Ability "Regeneration Ability") that could mend most injuries with no residual effects, an ability that only [Tsunade](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tsunade "Tsunade") 's [technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ninja_Art_Creation_Rebirth_%E2%80%94_Strength_of_a_Hundred_Technique "Ninja Art Creation Rebirth — Strength of a Hundred Technique") or Naruto's own healing when he accesses [Kurama](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kurama "Kurama") 's chakra have both came close to emulating.[^52] [^53] Even more, his very cells housed such potency that anyone who safely transplanted some into their bodies obtained considerable boosts in vitality and stamina reserves, his regeneration, self-sustainability, and the potential to manifest the [Wood Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Release "Wood Release").

### Ninjutsu

As a Senju, Hashirama is trained in a variety of shinobi skills. He could summon [multiple Rashōmon gates](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning:_Quintuple_Rash%C5%8Dmon "Summoning: Quintuple Rashōmon") to protect himself from nearly any attack.[^54] He could use [barrier ninjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Barrier_ninjutsu "Barrier ninjutsu") advanced enough to subdue the [Ten-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ten-Tails "Ten-Tails").[^55] He had unprecedented skill in [medical ninjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Medical_ninjutsu "Medical ninjutsu"), able to heal wounds without forming hand seals. His skill in [fūinjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/F%C5%ABinjutsu "Fūinjutsu") could store several different types of weapons for battle, with which he showed versatile skill in [bukijutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Bukijutsu "Bukijutsu"), able to fight on par with Madara. Likewise, Hashirama's fūinjutsu could reinforce sealing techniques that were not his own creation with his own techniques.[^56] In the anime, he could also [break](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Contract_Seal "Contract Seal") Madara's control over the Nine-Tails.[^57]

#### Nature Transformation

![Hashirama using Wood Release.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/9/9a/Hashirama%27s_wood_release.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240908160726)

Hashirama using Wood Release.

Hashirama was proficient in all five basic [nature transformations](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nature_transformation "Nature transformation"), along with [Yin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yin "Yin") and [Yang Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yang_Release "Yang Release").[^4] He was most famous for his use of [Wood Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Release "Wood Release"), a combination of [earth](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Earth "Earth") and [water](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water "Water") -natured chakra, creating wood and plants from the ground or his very being of various sizes and shapes that he manipulated to his purposes. This ability was apparently exclusive to him as no one else has naturally acquired this power; any replications are noticeably pale in comparison to Hashirama's capabilities.[^58]

![Hashirama's True Several Thousand Hands.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/a/af/Shin_S%C5%ABsenju.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140619122300)

Hashirama's True Several Thousand Hands.

Hashirama could completely change the [landscape](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Release_Secret_Technique:_Nativity_of_a_World_of_Trees "Wood Release Secret Technique: Nativity of a World of Trees") with his constructs. He also had more ingenious usages such as making [wood clones](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_clone "Wood clone") that are nearly indistinguishable from himself. He could make [flowering trees](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Release:_Advent_of_a_World_of_Flowering_Trees "Wood Release: Advent of a World of Flowering Trees") whose pollen rendered opponents unconscious, [protective structures](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Release:_H%C5%8Dbi_Technique "Wood Release: Hōbi Technique") able to withstand [Tailed Beast Balls](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Ball "Tailed Beast Ball"),[^59] [giant hands](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Release:_Hotei_Technique "Wood Release: Hotei Technique") to apprehend large targets,[^60] as well as a [giant humanoid creature](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Release:_Wood_Human_Technique "Wood Release: Wood Human Technique") able to clash with Madara's Complete Body — [Susanoo](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Susanoo "Susanoo").[^61] In the anime, his precision and dexterity with his Wood Release could wield and launch his various weapons from afar, allowing him to attack from multiple angles.[^57]

Hashirama's Wood Release also granted him a method of [subduing](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Control "Tailed Beast Control") and controlling the tailed beasts. By making direct hand contact or channelling it through his Wood Release, Hashirama could use the [Hokage-Style Sixty-Year-Old Technique — Kakuan Entering Society with Bliss-Bringing Hands](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage-Style_Sixty-Year-Old_Technique_%E2%80%94_Kakuan_Entering_Society_with_Bliss-Bringing_Hands "Hokage-Style Sixty-Year-Old Technique — Kakuan Entering Society with Bliss-Bringing Hands") to put the beast to sleep.[^62] He acquired several tailed beasts in this way and was able to maintain control over all of them at the same time. Likewise, he could create [wood dragons](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_dragon "Wood dragon") to wrap around any targets and [absorb](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_Absorption_Techniques "Chakra Absorption Techniques") their chakra. It was by this power that he earned fame as one of few people who could completely control a tailed beast.

### Senjutsu

![Hashirama in Sage Mode.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/20/Hashirama%27s_Sage_Mode.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150112025603)

Hashirama in Sage Mode.

Because of his large chakra reserves, Hashirama could use [senjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Senjutsu "Senjutsu") and enter [Sage Mode](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sage_Mode "Sage Mode") — a feat he could perform instantaneously.[^4] In this mode, he gains red (black in the anime, and dark green in the video games) markings around and under his eyes as well in the middle of his forehead. While it is not known where he learned it, Hashirama proved formidable in its usage, riding about on his wood techniques as a means to be mobile in battle while maintaining the stillness of one's self needed to continue building up natural energy to keep this mode activated. Hashirama could perform powerful [seals](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sage_Art:_Gate_of_the_Great_God "Sage Art: Gate of the Great God") to bind targets, even those as large as the Ten-Tails.[^55] The power of Sage Mode would also greatly augment his Wood Release, causing his constructs to become considerably larger and more dangerous, as seen with his largest creation, the [Sage Art Wood Release: True Several Thousand Hands](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sage_Art_Wood_Release:_True_Several_Thousand_Hands "Sage Art Wood Release: True Several Thousand Hands"): by using it to deliver an [enormous barrage of punches](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Top_Transformed_Buddha "Top Transformed Buddha"), it could strip Susanoo from the Nine-Tails plus was chiefly responsible for the formation of the [Valley of the End](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Valley_of_the_End "Valley of the End").[^63]

### Other Skills

Hashirama was skilled enough in [taijutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Taijutsu "Taijutsu") to match experts like Madara and [Hiruzen](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hiruzen "Hiruzen"),[^64] [^65] and could cast [genjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genjutsu "Genjutsu") to lock a target in [perpetual darkness](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Bringer-of-Darkness_Technique "Bringer-of-Darkness Technique") for the technique's duration.[^66] Hashirama was shown to be fast enough to outrun the Nine-Tails during his battle with Madara, allowing him to lead the battle to the shoreline.[^67]

## Part I

### Konoha Crush

Main article: [Konoha Crush](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Crush_\(Arc\) "Konoha Crush (Arc)")

![Orochimaru summons Hashirama and Tobirama.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/e/ed/Summoning_Impure_World_Reincarnation.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150126230450)

Orochimaru summons Hashirama and Tobirama.

[Orochimaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Orochimaru "Orochimaru") [invades Konoha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Crush "Konoha Crush") so that he can kill his master, [Hiruzen Sarutobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hiruzen_Sarutobi "Hiruzen Sarutobi"), who is now the Third Hokage. Because Orochimaru wants Hiruzen to experience the same thrills of fighting a teacher as he is, he reincarnates Hashirama and [Tobirama](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tobirama "Tobirama"). Recognising Hiruzen and seeing how old he has gotten, Hashirama realises how long he's been dead. Tobirama, meanwhile, realises that they've been brought back with the [Impure World Reincarnation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Impure_World_Reincarnation "Impure World Reincarnation"), and therefore will have no choice but to fight Hiruzen. Orochimaru suppresses their personalities and forces them to attack.

![Hiruzen's clone removing Hashirama's soul.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/d/de/Hiruzen_sealing_Hashirama.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150202074706)

Hiruzen's clone removing Hashirama's soul.

Hiruzen struggles to land an attack against Hashirama and Tobirama, their coordination and responsiveness offering him few openings. The few attacks he does land are merely regenerated by their reincarnated bodies. Because killing Orochimaru will not end the Impure World Reincarnation, Hiruzen uses the only option he has left: after creating two [shadow clones](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_clone "Shadow clone"), he has a clone capture each of the deceased Hokage and use the [Dead Demon Consuming Seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Dead_Demon_Consuming_Seal "Dead Demon Consuming Seal") on them. With their personalities restored, Hashirama and Tobirama apologise for being such troubles to him, just as Hiruzen apologises for the fate he has sentenced them to. He pulls the souls from their reincarnated bodies, feeding them to the [Shinigami](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shinigami "Shinigami").

## Part II

### Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax

Main article: [Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War:_Climax "Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax")

![The four Hokage are reincarnated.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/03/Hokage_reincarnated.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140612141309)

The four Hokage are reincarnated.

At the request of [Sasuke Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Uchiha "Sasuke Uchiha"), [Orochimaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Orochimaru "Orochimaru") [releases](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Dead_Demon_Consuming_Seal:_Release "Dead Demon Consuming Seal: Release") the souls of the first four Hokage from the stomach of the Shinigami so that he can reincarnate them. After taking in their surroundings, Hashirama and Tobirama are excited to meet [Minato](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Minato "Minato"), the Fourth, and take him as a sign of Konoha's continued prosperity. Hashirama asks who the Fifth is, and is informed that it is his granddaughter, [Tsunade](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tsunade "Tsunade"); Hashirama, accordingly, worries about the village's current fate. Sasuke asks the former Hokage about the history of the village, specifically with regards to the [Uchiha clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uchiha_clan "Uchiha clan"). From Tobirama's comments, Hashirama realises that Tobirama did not follow his instructions to be understanding of the Uchiha, which greatly angered him. When Tobirama prepares to attack Sasuke in order to protect the village from one of the last Uchiha, Hashirama uses force to stay his hand.

![Hashirama stops Tobirama from attacking.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/20/Hashirama_stops_Tobimara.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140619121510)

Hashirama stops Tobirama from attacking.

The Hokage are told about recent events, namely the outbreak of the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War") and the reincarnation of [Madara Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Madara_Uchiha "Madara Uchiha"). Tobirama wishes to go and help Konoha's forces in the war effort and asks for Hashirama's help. Although Orochimaru has perfected his control of the Impure World Reincarnation, Hashirama is still strong enough to break free if he chooses. Instead of doing that, however, he decides to tell Sasuke about his work to create Konoha and the dealings with the Uchiha, as that is why he has been reincarnated in the first place.

![The Hokage arrive on the battlefield.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/2c/The_Hokage_Arrive.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140814200218)

The Hokage arrive on the battlefield.

Hearing Hashirama's history and reflecting on his own experiences, Sasuke decides he doesn't want Konoha to be destroyed, as is Madara's current intention. Orochimaru releases the Hokage from his control so that they can help in this goal, and they set out for the site of the [Allied Shinobi Forces](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Allied_Shinobi_Forces "Allied Shinobi Forces") ' battle with the [Ten-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ten-Tails "Ten-Tails"). Although worried by Madara's current plans, Hashirama is at the same time curious how Madara managed to survive their last encounter and looks forward to seeing him. When they arrive on the battlefield, Madara, sensing Hashirama, excitedly asks for a chance to fight him, but Hashirama replies that he'll need to wait. Entering [Sage Mode](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sage_Mode "Sage Mode"), Hashirama uses [Sage Art: Gate of the Great God](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sage_Art:_Gate_of_the_Great_God "Sage Art: Gate of the Great God") to bind the Ten-Tails, then assists the other Hokage in erecting a [barrier](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Four_Red_Yang_Formation "Four Red Yang Formation") to confine it.

![Hashirama leads the Alliance.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/02/Hashirama_orders_attack.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140821153820)

Hashirama leads the Alliance.

Because he must stay with the barrier, Hashirama creates five [wood clones](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_clone "Wood clone") to act on his behalf. Four lead an Allied attack against the [Ten-Tails' minions](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ten-Tails_Fission "Ten-Tails Fission") while the fifth engages Madara; Madara is uninterested and offers to continue waiting until the real Hashirama is available. When [Obito Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Obito_Uchiha "Obito Uchiha") appears on the battlefield, Hashirama realises that Madara is forcing him to use the [Samsara of Heavenly Life Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Samsara_of_Heavenly_Life_Technique "Samsara of Heavenly Life Technique") to revive him. He creates six additional wood clones to go after Madara and stop him. Obito is able to resist Madara's control and instead seals the Ten-Tails into himself, becoming its [jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki "Jinchūriki").

### Birth of the Ten-Tails' Jinchūriki

Main article: [Birth of the Ten-Tails' Jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Birth_of_the_Ten-Tails%27_Jinch%C5%ABriki "Birth of the Ten-Tails' Jinchūriki")

![Hashirama battling Madara.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/07/Madara_and_Hashirama_fight_again.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20141002131518)

Hashirama battling Madara.

With his new power, Obito rips down the barrier, easily shrugs off another Gate of the Great God, and tears through one of the wood clones before it has a chance to respond. The clone is able to retain its form for long enough to create branches that restrain Obito, allowing one of Tobirama's [shadow clones](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_clone "Shadow clone") to use [Mutually Multiplying Explosive Tags](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mutually_Multiplying_Explosive_Tags "Mutually Multiplying Explosive Tags"). When this fails to have an effect, Hashirama decides to get personally involved. Madara, however, is done waiting, and forces Hashirama to fight him. Hashirama tries to convince him to postpone, but Madara won't hear of it. While he engages Madara, his remaining wood clones provide what support to the Allies they can and keep Hashirama apprised of how the battle against Obito is going.

![Hashirama communicates through Ino's technique.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/1/1f/Hashirama_communication.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20141016123211)

Hashirama communicates through Ino's technique.

After Obito creates the Ten-Tails' giant tree form, Madara explains to Hashirama the tree's function in the eventual implementation of the [Infinite Tsukuyomi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Infinite_Tsukuyomi "Infinite Tsukuyomi"). Needing to communicate this to the rest of the Alliance, Hashirama has [Ino Yamanaka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ino_Yamanaka "Ino Yamanaka") telepathically link one of his wood clones to the rest of the Alliance, which now includes Tsunade and the other current [Kage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kage "Kage"). After saying hello to his granddaughter, the clone relays his message and implores them not to give. When feelings of futility begin to grow amongst the Alliance's ranks, Hashirama shares his memories of the first [Kage Summit](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kage_Summit "Kage Summit") with them, helping to rally them all and, ultimately, defeat Obito.

![Madara captures Hashirama.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/a/aa/Madara_captures_Hashirama.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20141211124430)

Madara captures Hashirama.

The original Hashirama, meanwhile, manages to pin Madara with multiple Gate of the Great Gods and surrounds him with [wood dragons](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_dragon "Wood dragon") to prevent him from escaping. [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki") arrives to help seal Madara, thus ending the war, but Madara implements a final trump card before they can do so: successfully forcing Obito to restore him to life with the Samsara of Heavenly Life Technique. The full return of his original power allows Madara to break free of his restraints and, in the confusion, quickly immobilise Hashirama with [chakra receivers](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_receiver "Chakra receiver"). Madara is able to fight off several other immediate attackers, but his revival has left him without his eyes, diminishing his senses. He approaches Hashirama and absorbs his [senjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Senjutsu "Senjutsu") chakra so that he can find targets based on their chakra signatures. Before he leaves, Madara notes that the plan for peace he mentioned to Hashirama so long ago is about to be achieved.

As Sasuke is about to go after Madara, Hashirama, still unable to move, offers to give him a jutsu that can be used to cancel out the senjutsu chakra Madara has taken. Sasuke asks why Hashirama would trust him, an Uchiha, to which Hashirama replies that he resembles Madara's brother, [Izuna](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Izuna "Izuna"), and that maybe that likeness will remind Madara of the kind person he used to be before Izuna died. In the end, Sasuke fails and the [Infinite Tsukuyomi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Infinite_Tsukuyomi "Infinite Tsukuyomi") is cast, with the whole world – the reincarnated Hokage excluded – getting wrapped into [God: Nativity of a World of Trees](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/God:_Nativity_of_a_World_of_Trees "God: Nativity of a World of Trees").

![Hashirama speaks to Madara for the last time.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/ba/Madara%27s_Final_Moment.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160901115404)

Hashirama speaks to Madara for the last time.

Sometime later, the chakra receivers disappear, restoring Hashirama's mobility. He reunites with the other Hokage and they try to figure out what they can do to break the Infinite Tsukuyomi. ## Legacy

![The living clone cultivated from Hashirama's cells.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/c/c5/Hashirama%27s_Artificial_Body.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150826111731)

The living clone cultivated from Hashirama's cells.

The fact that only Hashirama possessed the ability to use [Wood Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Release "Wood Release") – and related abilities of controlling [tailed beasts](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_beasts "Tailed beasts") and [self-healing](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Regeneration_Ability "Regeneration Ability") – made his [DNA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DNA "wikipedia:DNA") highly coveted after his death:

- In the anime, [Konoha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha "Konoha") sanctioned several attempts to research and reproduce Hashirama's power. However, all met with disastrous failure as the powerful cells overwhelmed and killed the subjects. As such, any further study was forbidden.[^68]
- [Orochimaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Orochimaru "Orochimaru") exhumed Hashirama's body for his own experiments, where he infused sixty child test subjects with cells harvested from the remains in the hopes that one would prove able to use Wood Release. Only [Yamato](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yamato "Yamato") survived the process, albeit with considerably diminished might compared to the First Hokage.[^69]
	- [Danzō Shimura](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Danz%C5%8D_Shimura "Danzō Shimura") had a work relationship with Orochimaru and had his right arm infused with Hashirama's cells. In addition to granting him increased chakra levels and limited Wood Release abilities, it extended the amount of time he could keep [Izanagi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Izanagi "Izanagi") active and control how long it is used to conserve time with each different [Sharingan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sharingan "Sharingan"). It also increased how often he could use [Kotoamatsukami](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kotoamatsukami "Kotoamatsukami").
		- [Kabuto Yakushi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kabuto_Yakushi "Kabuto Yakushi") continued Orochimaru's studies, eventually learning how to create an implant that would grant whoever received it access to Hashirama's complete power. The only recipient of this implant was Madara, enhancing him "beyond his prime".
- [Madara Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Madara_Uchiha "Madara Uchiha") took his own sample of Hashirama's DNA during their fight at the Valley of the End, which he applied to his injuries afterwards to accelerate his recovery and likewise extended his longevity. Decades later, towards the end of his natural lifespan, the combination of his Uchiha essence with Hashirama's DNA allowed his Sharingan evolve into a [Rinnegan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rinnegan "Rinnegan"),[^70] though unknowingly to Madara, that was actually due to Indra's and Ashura's chakra combining, Madara and Hashirama being their respective reincarnations.
	- Using this DNA sample, Madara created a [mindless clone of Hashirama](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hashirama%27s_Living_Clone "Hashirama's Living Clone"), with which could produce a substance that enhanced recipients and even replace lost anatomy, as it saved [Obito Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Obito_Uchiha "Obito Uchiha") from maiming damage of his body's destroyed right half and likewise repair it. This substance also granted Madara and Obito Uchiha greatly enhanced chakra reserves and physical prowess, advanced healing, being unimpaired without food or water, and the Wood Release.[^71] [^72] This same substance would be used to enhance the [White Zetsu Army](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/White_Zetsu_Army "White Zetsu Army").[^73] Later, [Tsunade](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tsunade "Tsunade") used this substance to replace Naruto and Sasuke's respective lost arms (the latter however declining the offer).
- In the anime, [Root](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Root "Root") saw this as a means to create an ultimate weapon, researching for years to create [Gozu Tennō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gozu_Tenn%C5%8D "Gozu Tennō").[^74]
- In the anime, Hashirama's DNA became so highly coveted as rumors spread about his powerful life-force granting healing powers and heightened longevity could also restore one's youth, which [Mugino](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mugino "Mugino") claimed was false.
	- One sample was stolen from one of Orochimaru's laboratory and sold on the black market in the [Land of Silence](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_Silence "Land of Silence").[^75] It would eventually making its way to [Victor](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Victor "Victor"), who used it to induce a splinter of the [God Tree](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/God_Tree "God Tree") to blossom into an artificial tree.[^76]

A large part of Hashirama's legacy was his philosophy of the [Will of Fire](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Will_of_Fire "Will of Fire"), which became increasingly common amongst Konoha's ninja in the years after his death. Future [Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage "Hokage"), in particular, were expected to fully adopt the philosophy. In time, even members of the [Uchiha clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uchiha_clan "Uchiha clan") began demonstrating signs of the Will of Fire, with Hashirama claiming [Itachi Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Itachi_Uchiha "Itachi Uchiha") 's dedication to the village was even greater than his own.

- "Hashirama" (柱間) is a Japanese word referring to the space between two pillars. This might be a play on the word "pillar" (柱, *hashira*), as the Hokage is said to be the pillar that supports the village. His family name "Senju" literally means "thousand hands" (千手).
- Hashirama and his siblings all share names referring to the space between two things. Hashirama refers to the space between two pillars, Tobirama the space between two doors, Kawarama two roof tiles, and Itama the space between two wooden slats on a [duckboard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duckboard "wikipedia:Duckboard"), a type of platform built to create a dry passage over muddy, or wet terrain.
- His face on the [Hokage Rock](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage_Rock "Hokage Rock") has a crack across the bridge of the nose.[^77] This crack is not present in flashback depictions of the Rock.[^78]
- Hashirama's face appears within the fireworks in the [omake](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Omake "Omake") of [*Shippūden* episode 129](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Infiltrate!_The_Village_Hidden_in_the_Rain "Infiltrate! The Village Hidden in the Rain").
- Studio Pierrot's Settei sheets show that Hashirama was 147cm when he met Madara.
- According to the databook(s):
	- His hobbies were [bonsai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonsai "wikipedia:Bonsai") and [wood sculpting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sculpture "wikipedia:Sculpture").
		- His desired opponent was Madara Uchiha.
		- His favourite food was mushroom [zōsui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zosui "wikipedia:Zosui"), Japanese mushroom rice porridge made usually from leftovers.
		- His favourite phrases were "The vast lands fertile" (大地豊饒, *Daichi hōjō*) and "Heaven's garments are without seams" (天衣無縫, *Ten'i muhō*). They refer to perfect beauty without any artificial elements.
- In the English Dub, in Part I, Hashirama is voiced by [Jamieson Price](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamieson_Price "wikipedia:Jamieson Price") while Tobirama is voiced by [Peter Lurie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Lurie "wikipedia:Peter Lurie"). In Part II, ironically enough, their voice actors switched roles, with Lurie now voicing Hashirama while Price voiced Tobirama.
- Despite having cast [genjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Bringer-of-Darkness_Technique "Bringer-of-Darkness Technique") in the series, Yin Release is not listed as one of Hashirama's natures in the fourth databook.[^4]
- It seems Hashirama's blood type (type B) has no issue in compatibility when his DNA is transplanted to others of a different blood type, as both Madara and Obito have type O.
