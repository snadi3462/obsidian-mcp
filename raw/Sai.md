**Sai Yamanaka** (山中サイ, *Yamanaka Sai*) is the [Anbu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Anbu "Anbu") Chief of [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure") 's [Yamanaka clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yamanaka_clan "Yamanaka clan"). Prior to this, he was a [Root](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Root "Root") member. As per standard Root training, Sai was conditioned to remove all emotions and as such, had difficulty connecting with others. When he is added to [Team Kakashi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Kakashi "Team Kakashi") as a replacement for the rogue [Sasuke Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Uchiha "Sasuke Uchiha"), Sai begins to learn more about people's feelings and tries to develop his personality and identity by forming genuine bonds and rediscovering the emotions he was taught to suppress.

## Background

![Sai as a child.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/4/41/Sai_as_a_Child.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140511181127)

Sai as a child.

Sai was orphaned as a child and recruited into Root, a secret branch of the Anbu under the leadership of [Danzō Shimura](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Danz%C5%8D_Shimura "Danzō Shimura"). There, he was raised and conditioned to have no personality, personal connections, or a name; "Sai" being given to him for the purposes of joining Team Kakashi. Though largely successful in this regard, Sai retained an interest in art. When [Shin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shin "Shin"), an older Root member, discovered Sai's drawing ability, he encouraged Sai's talent and they became very close.

Over time, Sai came to think of Shin as an older brother and looked up to him. Unfortunately, the final step in Root training called for the two to fight to the death to eradicate all remaining emotions. Before that happened, however, Shin died of an unnamed illness and Sai's Root training suppressed most of the memories he had of him.[^5] In the anime, Sai and Shin were forced to fight by Danzō but Shin, knowing that he was dying anyway, told Sai to report to Danzō that he had killed him in battle. Before dying, Shin told his brother that he had to suppress his feelings, and to live for the both of them.[^6]

Before Shin's death, Sai created a picture book that would depict all the opponents they faced and illustrate the feelings that they shared for each other. Sai organised the book so that his and Shin's portraits would be located at opposite ends of the book, and in the centre pages would be a picture of them holding hands. Some time after the loss of Shin, Sai forgot that this was his intention, and the picture book was left unfinished, though he could never bring it upon himself to discard the book.

During his time in Root, Sai was ordered to eliminate a large number of individuals that posed a threat against Konoha. At the time he was assigned to Team Kakashi, the last assassination he performed was of [Unmo Samidare](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Unmo_Samidare "Unmo Samidare").[^7] In the anime, an attempt to gain a powerful [fūinjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/F%C5%ABinjutsu "Fūinjutsu") after seeing the [Third Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Hokage "Third Hokage") [sacrifice](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Dead_Demon_Consuming_Seal "Dead Demon Consuming Seal") his life in an attempt to defeat [Orochimaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Orochimaru "Orochimaru"), led Danzō to supervise Sai while he learned how to use the [Sealing Technique: Tiger Vision Staring Bullet](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sealing_Technique:_Tiger_Vision_Staring_Bullet "Sealing Technique: Tiger Vision Staring Bullet"), noting that it would be dangerous as he would have to use "emotional energy" in order to make the technique stronger. Sai struggled greatly with this endeavour as it conflicted with his training and, consumed by negative emotions, his training was seemingly left incomplete.[^8]

## Personality

![Sai's fake smile.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/e/e9/Sai2.PNG/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150825083158)

Sai's fake smile.

When first introduced, Sai was distinguished by his total lack of emotion which he often masks either with a blank expression or a small smile. After much mental conditioning and training while in Root, Sai had suppressed almost all of his emotions, laying all of his loyalty to whoever commands him, and thus making it easier for him to accomplish dangerous, unpleasant, or unethical missions. Due to this training and his lack of social experience and personal initiative, Sai has great trouble in understanding how bonds between people can motivate others. He often showcases this confusion after thinking about why Naruto would go so far to save Sasuke, a traitor, even though Naruto had not been ordered to do so.

Sai's social ineptitude tends to get people angry, because he often speaks without considering his words and their effects on people. This was due to his inability to realise the difference between a rude statement and a true statement. For example, Sai had read somewhere in a book that giving somebody a nickname would bring you closer to that person and develop your friendship, this resulted in Sai hurting [Sakura Haruno](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sakura_Haruno "Sakura Haruno") 's feelings, because he nicknamed her "Ugly", stating the first character trait that came to his mind. This also nearly led to enraging Chōji, while eating barbecue with [Team Asuma](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Asuma "Team Asuma") and [Team Kakashi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Kakashi "Team Kakashi") he nearly called him "Fat" before Naruto covered his mouth before he could finish. Realising he was offending people by saying characteristics that came to his head, Sai decided to take a different approach by saying the opposite of what came into his head. In this way, at the same event, Sai called Ino "Gorgeous" (although he truly meant to call her "Ugly"), much to Ino's surprise, Naruto's relief, and Sakura's outrage.

![Sai dreams of having the perfect smile.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/b6/Sai%27s_Dream.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160304062615)

Sai dreams of having the perfect smile.

After spending more time with Team Kakashi, Sai comes to appreciate bonds, particularly the bond that exists between Naruto Uzumaki and Sasuke Uchiha, though Sai would come to have serious doubts about Sasuke when he began to commit crimes on an international scale, which Naruto would suffer for, leading him to support assassinating Sasuke. This causes him to break out of his emotionless shell and start to remember the times he had with his own "brother", [Shin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shin "Shin"). In doing so, Sai was able to start giving genuine smiles. Wanting to experience personal bonds for himself, Sai sets out to make as many friends as he can. However, since he is learning most of this out of books and by trial and error, he often accidentally ends up offending people with his nicknames and conversation. He has come to develop a more genuine, pleasant, and thoughtful personality, while also showing signs of more emotion, such as enthusiasm and occasional nervousness. In the anime, it was shown from his [Infinite Tsukuyomi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Infinite_Tsukuyomi "Infinite Tsukuyomi") dream that his greatest desire is to fully rediscover his emotions and connect with as many people as possible through it.[^9]

![Sai becomes genuinely enraged that his brother is used as a bomb.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/f/f4/Sai_Enraged.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20211024164417)

Sai becomes genuinely enraged that his brother is used as a bomb.

Sai first shows genuine anger when explaining to Sakura about Naruto's feelings for her. This is further on display after he encounters his [reincarnated](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning:_Impure_World_Reincarnation "Summoning: Impure World Reincarnation") brother. When Shin is used as a living bomb by Deidara, Sai became truly enraged for the first time and attacked Sasori and Deidara mercilessly, showing genuine rage on his face. Sai also displayed genuine sadness, shedding tears when his brother was freed from the technique and disappears.

Sai is very observant, and is always focused on his missions. He pays a lot of attention to detail, allowing him to quickly notice if something is not as it appears, as shown from how he easily sees through Yamato's tricks. This awareness spans beyond the trials of duty, as Sai was later quick to understand the nature of Naruto's inner suffering without Naruto even telling him. What he failed to understand, however, was that Naruto was not trying to save Sasuke just for Sakura, something he mentions is due to his still lacklustre understanding of both his Team 7 teammates and emotions in general and he would come to regret when it lead to Sakura's suicidal attempt to stop Sasuke, feeling that as if he was a true friend, he should've found another way that didn't put his teammates at risk. Sai holds Naruto in high regard and tries to assist Naruto whenever he's going through hardships however he can.

This bond with Naruto led Sai to start to understand camaraderie, and by the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War"), he realised he understood it as he, [Rock Lee](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rock_Lee "Rock Lee"), and various others went to assist Naruto as he fought against [Tobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Obito_Uchiha "Obito Uchiha"). Growing up over the following the years, Sai came to truly understand compassion and love, forming a closer bond with Ino, showing more forwardness in such acts as asking her on a date and holding her hand affectionately. This ultimately led to them forming a family. Through all this, Sai has completely come to reject the teachings of Root, accepting that Danzō's actions, while genuine to protect the village, were misguided and only led to more suffering.[^10]

Sai is shown to care greatly for his family, as he has begun training his son in his artistic-ninjutsu, finding humour in Inojin's cruder drawing. Sai also has developed strong parenting skills, being the one who's reprimanding Naruto for not holding back enough when he tried to counter Boruto's shadow clone with his own. His compassion for his son's happiness was also shown from not wanting to hurt any of his friends, regardless of the circumstances.[^10] He also believes in an indirect teaching-method, feeling that while people need guidance, they cannot truly grow as people if they don't find the answers themselves.

## Appearance

- ![Sai's full appearance.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/7/75/Sai_full2.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/60?cb=20151231180402)
	Sai's full appearance.

Sai has short, straight black hair, and dark eyes which contrast with his translucent-looking pale skin. He is usually seen carrying a small backpack with his brush, scrolls, and ninja ink in it. He wears a short black-and-grey jacket with red straps — as customary of all Root members, to most likely show his allegiance to the organisation. He also carries a tip-less tantō on his back. The rest of his outfit consisted of a high-collared midriff shirt, black pants, shinobi sandals and gloves with his index and thumb fingers exposed, most likely to facilitate the use of his drawing based techniques. When first seen with Team Kakashi, his jacket has a long right and short left sleeves.[^11] He had Danzō's [cursed seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Cursed_Tongue_Eradication_Seal "Cursed Tongue Eradication Seal") on the back of his tongue until Danzō's death, which caused it to disappear.[^12] [^13] Despite his different hair-style and skin-tone, he is noted by many to physically resemble Sasuke,[^14] [^15] [^16] and likewise considered very handsome. When he's not on missions, Sai wears a casual outfit consisting of a long-sleeve shirt with a high collar, matching pants, and his regular shinobi sandals. This outfit was first depicted as being purple in colour and later, navy blue. He doesn't wear his forehead protector or gloves with this outfit.

In the anime, when Sai uses the Sealing Technique: Tiger Vision Staring Bullet and his negative emotions surface because of his use of "emotional energy", his eyes turn a translucent red colour and his sclerae turn black. This is apparently, as when Danzō countered the symptoms, ink splattered from Sai's body.[^8]

Two years after the Fourth Shinobi World War, Sai grew his hair longer with his bangs framing his face. His attire is similar to his old Root outfit but his jacket is now completely black with both sleeves of his shirt long, and less of his midriff exposed. He also has a large scroll strapped horizontally to his back and his shinobi sandals reach above his calves.

A decade after the Fourth Shinobi World War, his jacket now covers his entire upper body, with both sleeves long. His hair is shorter, and he wears an even larger scroll on his back to perform fūinjutsu.[^10] Sai keeps his hair in a short ponytail as customary of the [Yamanaka Clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yamanaka_Clan "Yamanaka Clan"). He has a different styled jacket with the sleeves rolled up and his collar is upturned. The large scroll he carries is attached to his belt and he also wears gloves. He occasionally still uses his forehead protector.

## Abilities

As a member of Root, Sai is a highly capable shinobi, who is trained in the organisation's [kenjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Assassination_Technique "Assassination Technique"). Being said to be the strongest Root member of his generation,[^17] Danzō had enough faith in Sai's abilities to task him with single-handedly killing Sasuke.[^18] By adulthood, his talents led him to become one of Konohagakure's top authorities as the head of Anbu and regular reporting directly to the Seventh Hokage and aiding him in various decisions.

### Ninjutsu

Sai is able to use [Earth Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Earth_Release "Earth Release") to [conceal himself underground](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Earth_Release:_Hiding_Like_a_Mole_Technique "Earth Release: Hiding Like a Mole Technique") without disrupting the surface of the earth.[^19] He can also use [Fire](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fire "Fire"), [Water](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water "Water") and [Yang Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yang_Release "Yang Release").[^2] In adulthood, since marrying into the Yamanaka clan, Sai appears to have become well-versed their hidden techniques, able to recognise the signs of people telepathically communicating and likewise to aid his son's training in them. In his own practice, he can easily [contact others](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_Body_Transmission_Technique "Mind Body Transmission Technique"). In the anime, he can also use [shadow clones](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_clone "Shadow clone").[^20]

#### Ink Techniques

![Sai using Super Beast Imitating Drawing.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/04/Super_Beast_Imitation_Drawing.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160108115900)

Sai using Super Beast Imitating Drawing.

Sai is able to [combine](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ink_Mist_Technique "Ink Mist Technique") ink with the [Body Flicker Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Body_Flicker_Technique "Body Flicker Technique") to create a more effective cover up. However, Sai's most notable talent is his skill as an artist, able to create drawings in mere moments. He uses these artistic skills as a source for his [Super Beast Imitating Drawing](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Super_Beast_Imitating_Drawing "Super Beast Imitating Drawing"). Using his custom scroll (which contains a compartment for Sai's [ink brush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ink_brush "wikipedia:Ink brush") and a supply of [ink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ink "wikipedia:Ink")), and applying [chakra](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra "Chakra") into the drawings, his creations come to life. In the anime, he is also ambidextrous, able to draw with either hand as quickly and skillfully.[^21]

![Sai's ink birds attached with self-drawn explosive tags.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/2e/Super_Beast_Imitating_Drawing_Birds_and_Tags.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150901091039)

Sai's ink birds attached with self-drawn explosive tags.

Sai typically creates giant birds to transport himself or others,[^22] lion-like monsters for attack,[^23] snakes for restraining enemies, and mice to scout an area. Sai can also create [ink clones](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ink_Clone_Technique "Ink Clone Technique") of himself for reconnaissance. For discreet communiques when he is undercover, Sai is able to turn the words he writes into small animals, allowing them to travel to their intended target unnoticed. He has also demonstrated the ability to create [large humanoids](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Super_God_Imitating_Drawing "Super God Imitating Drawing") that resemble the [Benevolent Kings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nio "wikipedia:Nio") (仁王, *Niō*), which possess enough strength to completely destroy Deidara's clay birds. Sai can also produce [explosive tags](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Explosive_tag "Explosive tag"), as seen when he had multiple birds fly down to bombard his enemies with a chain of explosions.[^24]

#### Fūinjutsu

![Sai sealing Jinin Akebino.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/bf/Tiger_Vision_Staring_Bullet1.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160205050136)

Sai sealing Jinin Akebino.

As noted by Kakashi, Sai is one of Danzō's prized soldiers and as such, his Root training also included learning [fūinjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/F%C5%ABinjutsu "Fūinjutsu").[^25] By animating a large tiger from his scroll, he can use it to grab and [seal the target in the painting](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sealing_Technique:_Tiger_Vision_Staring_Bullet "Sealing Technique: Tiger Vision Staring Bullet"). Due to the preparation time it would take to paint the tiger, he would need allies to protect him while in battle.[^26] In the anime, it is stated that Sai uses "emotional energy" to bolster the technique. This is a double-edged sword as Sai becomes susceptible to his negative emotions.[^8]

### Physical Prowess

Despite not being his speciality, Sai has shown to be proficient in [taijutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Taijutsu "Taijutsu"). He was able to react and block Naruto's attempt to stab him with a kunai very easily with his tantō.[^27] He also was able to subdue [Kabuto Yakushi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kabuto_Yakushi "Kabuto Yakushi") very easily [^28] and block [Sasuke Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Uchiha "Sasuke Uchiha") 's attempt to stab Naruto, with Sasuke commenting that he blocked the attack in the most appropriate manner.[^29] During the Fourth Shinobi World War, he was able to break Shin's neck with a flying knee kick in mid-air.[^30] Even without the use of his tantō, he has shown great skill with a bladed weapon and was able to fight equally with an opponent wielding a larger weapon.[^31] A distinct advantage he has in battle is to perform techniques with a single hand, leaving the other free for enemy counter-attacks.

## Part II

### Tenchi Bridge Reconnaissance Mission

Main article: [Tenchi Bridge Reconnaissance Mission](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tenchi_Bridge_Reconnaissance_Mission "Tenchi Bridge Reconnaissance Mission")

![Team Kakashi faces Kabuto and Orochimaru.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/04/Team_Yamato_at_Tenchi_Bridge.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20221003120655)

Team Kakashi faces Kabuto and Orochimaru.

Sai was assigned to join Team Kakashi by his superior Danzō to assist in meeting with a spy in [Orochimaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Orochimaru "Orochimaru") 's ranks to find out about Sasuke Uchiha. Sai was confrontational with his new teammates upon their first meeting, exasperating both Sakura and Naruto. On their way to the rendezvous point, Sai once again aggravated them, questioning Naruto's manhood and insulting Sasuke. The latter caused [Sakura Haruno](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sakura_Haruno "Sakura Haruno") to punch him in anger. As they continued on to the meeting point, Sai questioned why Naruto was loyal to a traitor like Sasuke, to which Naruto stated he was not a traitor, but a friend. Upon reaching the designated meeting place, Yamato went ahead disguised as [Sasori](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasori "Sasori") so that he could meet the spy. When the spy, [Kabuto Yakushi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kabuto_Yakushi "Kabuto Yakushi"), arrived with Orochimaru to kill Sasori, Sai, Naruto, and Sakura were called in for help.

![Sai delivering Danzō's message to Orochimaru.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/80/Sai%27s_real_mission.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20141011215728)

Sai delivering Danzō's message to Orochimaru.

When Naruto and Orochimaru began fighting, Sai left the rest of the team to begin his real mission. He briefly observed Naruto's fight with Orochimaru, unconcerned with the danger he left Yamato and Sakura in, and approached Orochimaru after he had repelled Naruto with a proposition from Danzō. Orochimaru accepted, and Sai accompanied him and Kabuto back to their hideout. Once arrived at Orochimaru's hideout, Sai was introduced to Sasuke, who had been training under Orochimaru for the past two-and-a-half years. When Sai mentioned that he might get along better with Sasuke than he did Naruto, Sasuke temporally traps Sai within a [genjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Coercion_Sharingan "Coercion Sharingan") which startles Sai to the ground due to the amount of fear it gave his emotionless self. He was led to his own room afterwards by Kabuto, who locked Sai in upon leaving. Soon afterwards, Team Kakashi tracked Sai down and confronted him about his real mission. Having been caught, Sai explained that he had been assigned to ally with Orochimaru on Danzō's behalf so that the two may join forces in destroying Konohagakure.

![Sai restrained by Team 7.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/a/ad/Sai_captured_by_Team_7.PNG/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160108123923)

Sai restrained by Team 7.

Disgusted by the ramifications of this plan, Team Kakashi restrained Sai before setting out to find and retrieve Sasuke. Sai, however, doubted their success, stating that Sasuke no longer cared about them. Naruto, in response, stated that he also used to hate Sasuke, but over time he had formed a bond with Sasuke through their shared experiences, and as such he would do whatever it took to bring Sasuke home. Naruto's dedication to retrieving Sasuke struck a chord with Sai, who was left without words as a result. When Kabuto arrived and freed Sai, Sai instead helped them capturing Kabuto, hoping to accompany Naruto to find out more about bonds. Naruto and Sai split away from Sakura and Yamato to go looking for Sasuke. They were soon found by Orochimaru, and as a result, Naruto distracted him so that Sai could have a chance to scout on ahead. When Orochimaru retreated and Naruto reunited with Sakura and Yamato, Yamato went through the belongings that Sai had left behind. In doing so he discovered Sai's actual mission: to kill Sasuke as a traitor to Konoha.

![Sai blocking Sasuke's attack.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/9/98/Sai_intercepts_Sasuke.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150825100633)

Sai blocking Sasuke's attack.

Team Kakashi raced through the base to find and stop Sai, but Sai had already found Sasuke. Sai, however, truly had a change of heart, and instead, tried to capture Sasuke in order to protect his bond with Naruto. Irritated by Sai's reasons for bothering him, Sasuke attacked Sai, drawing the rest of the team to their location. When they found out what Sai was trying to do, they all teamed up to restrain Sasuke. Although Sai was able to land a successful attack, Sasuke's abilities were too much for them and he prepared to kill them all. He was soon stopped by Orochimaru and Kabuto, who convinced him to spare the team before leaving. Defeated, Team Kakashi returned home. Sai requested that he be allowed to stay as a part of the team and, with Danzō's approval, began to spend time with his new friends. Upon returning to Konoha and failing their mission, Sai began reading about relationships in the hopes of making some for himself.

### Twelve Guardian Ninja

Main article: [Twelve Guardian Ninja](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Twelve_Guardian_Ninja_\(Arc\) "Twelve Guardian Ninja (Arc)") In the anime, Team Yamato are sent to help guard anymore tombs of the former [Twelve Guardian Ninja](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Twelve_Guardian_Ninja "Twelve Guardian Ninja") from being robbed. Shortly after arriving at the [Fire Temple](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fire_Temple "Fire Temple"), the team are notified that another tomb has been robbed. The team then set out to find the perpetrators, accompanied by [Chiriku](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chiriku "Chiriku") and [Sora](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sora "Sora"). Along the way they spot caskets moving across a field and onto a cliff where [four ninja](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Furido%27s_4-Man_Team "Furido's 4-Man Team") lied. When they confront the enemy ninja, [Fudō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fud%C5%8D "Fudō") traps them in a [rocky canyon](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Earth_Release:_Mutability "Earth Release: Mutability"), which separates everyone. As Sai tries to look for a way out, [Fuen](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fuen "Fuen") [halts](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Altering_Terrain_Technique "Altering Terrain Technique") his attempts each time. He soon figures out that he is being tracked with a chip and plants it on a [ink clone](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ink_clone "Ink clone"). This deceives Fuen, giving Sai the opportunity to sneak attack her from behind with ink lions. Although she is able to counter, Sai is able to escape.

Some time after, Sai stumbles into Sakura, arriving in time to save her from a surprise attack from a giant spider. While he manages to kill it, he gets injured in the process. Sakura tends to his wound, but he is forced to wear an arm brace. After the two meet up with Yamato, they find Naruto being [drained](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Execution_by_Kiss "Execution by Kiss") of his chakra by [Fūka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/F%C5%ABka "Fūka"). Main article: [Naruto Shippūden the Movie: Bonds](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Shipp%C5%ABden_the_Movie:_Bonds "Naruto Shippūden the Movie: Bonds") Sai, due to his ability to create flying mounts for himself with his Super Beast Imitating Drawing Technique, served a double function as a scout and first counterstrike against the [Land of the Sky](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_the_Sky "Land of the Sky") attackers by using taijutsu to jump and wreck different the Land of the Sky shinobi wing sets. He admits, soon after, that he is actually bored with the incompetence of the Sky ninja.

#### Naruto Shippūden the Movie: The Will of Fire

Main article: [Naruto Shippūden the Movie: The Will of Fire](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Shipp%C5%ABden_the_Movie:_The_Will_of_Fire "Naruto Shippūden the Movie: The Will of Fire") After being sent to scout the area, Sai is knocked out of the sky and is later found unconscious by Naruto. Although not officially given a mission, after recovering from previous injuries, Sai pursued the Konoha 11 on their way to Hiruko's base. Temporarily blinding [San](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/San "San") with a flashbomb, he picked up Naruto and Sakura in order to get them ahead of the following teams, leaving Shikamaru and his teammates to deal with San.

#### Naruto Shippūden the Movie: The Lost Tower

Main article: [Naruto Shippūden the Movie: The Lost Tower](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Shipp%C5%ABden_the_Movie:_The_Lost_Tower "Naruto Shippūden the Movie: The Lost Tower") When Naruto and Yamato are taken into the seal Mukade broke, Sai saves Sakura from being pulled into it as well. When Naruto and Yamato reappear, they all go back to Konoha.

#### Naruto the Movie: Blood Prison

#### Road to Ninja: Naruto the Movie

Main article: [Road to Ninja: Naruto the Movie](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Road_to_Ninja:_Naruto_the_Movie "Road to Ninja: Naruto the Movie") The [alternate world](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genjutsu_World "Genjutsu World") 's Sai, compared to the Sai that Naruto and Sakura know, has more emotions within himself and is more outgoing. His passion for art is the same, the only difference is that his alternate self is horrible at it. Also, due to the alternate Sasuke still being part of Team 7, the alternate Sai never became a member of Team 7 nor does he know the others.

## Trivia

- As an Anbu member, Sai refers to [Kakashi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi "Kakashi") as his [senpai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senpai_and_k%C5%8Dhai "wikipedia:Senpai and kōhai") out of respect.
- According to the databook(s):
	- Sai's hobbies are drawing and [calligraphy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/calligraphy "wikipedia:calligraphy").
		- Sai wishes to fight [Kakashi Hatake](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi_Hatake "Kakashi Hatake").
		- Sai's favourite word is " *comrades/companions* " (仲間, *nakama*).
		- Sai's favourite food is [momen tofu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tofu#Firm_tofu "wikipedia:Tofu"), while his least favourite is [mitarashi dango](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitarashi_dango "wikipedia:Mitarashi dango").
		- Sai has a frank, obedient and submissive personality.
- According to character trivia from *[Boruto: Naruto Next Generations](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Boruto:_Naruto_Next_Generations "Boruto: Naruto Next Generations")*:
	- His attributes are: 140 in strength, 144 in intelligence, 155 in perception, 160 in dexterity, 150 in chakra, and 120 in negotiation.
		- He was also given a skill rating in these particular areas:
		- Art (Painting): ★★★★★
				- Disinformation: ★★★★☆
- In the seventh character poll, Sai placed 12th place, alongside Sakura, with 2,188 votes each.
- Between the end of the Fourth Shinobi World War and the events of *[The Last: Naruto the Movie](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/The_Last:_Naruto_the_Movie "The Last: Naruto the Movie")*, Sai changes his rank from "Anbu" to **Chūbu** (柱部, *literally meaning:* Support Division). Currently, it is not known what exactly this change implies.
- In the [anime](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jiraiya_Shinobi_Handbook:_The_Tale_of_Naruto_the_Hero "Jiraiya Shinobi Handbook: The Tale of Naruto the Hero"), a dream version of Sai was still part of [Root](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Root "Root") and was one of its best members, alongside [Yūgao Uzuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Y%C5%ABgao_Uzuki "Yūgao Uzuki"), [Hayate Gekkō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hayate_Gekk%C5%8D "Hayate Gekkō"), and [Anko Mitarashi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Anko_Mitarashi "Anko Mitarashi"). He wore a standard-issue [Anbu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Anbu "Anbu") uniform with a black hooded cloak and a mask with abstract symmetrical designs.[^36]
- Sai is the only known male character in the series to take on his spouse's surname upon marriage.
