**Tsunade** (綱手) is a descendant of the [Senju](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Senju "Senju") and [Uzumaki Clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uzumaki_Clan "Uzumaki Clan") and is one of [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure") 's [Sannin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sannin "Sannin"). She is famed as the world's strongest [kunoichi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kunoichi "Kunoichi") and its greatest [medical-nin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Medical-nin "Medical-nin"). The repeated loss of her loved ones caused Tsunade to later abandon the life of a shinobi for many years. She is eventually persuaded to return to Konoha and take on the mantle of **Fifth [Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage "Hokage")** (五代目火影, *Godaime Hokage*, *literally meaning:* Fifth Fire Shadow), where her skills prove invaluable to the village.

## Background

![Hiruzen and his students.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/3/30/Team_hiruzen.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240908012838)

Hiruzen and his students.

Tsunade is the granddaughter of [Hashirama Senju](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hashirama_Senju "Hashirama Senju") and [Mito Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mito_Uzumaki "Mito Uzumaki"). Because Hashirama was the First [Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage "Hokage") of [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure"), Tsunade is called "Princess" (姫, *Hime*). Hashirama doted on Tsunade when she was very young because she was his first grandchild and found her deviance and her adoption of his own gambling habits highly amusing.[^7] Tsunade inherited his [necklace](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/First_Hokage%27s_Necklace "First Hokage's Necklace") when he died. After she graduated from Konoha's [Academy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Academy "Academy"), Tsunade was teamed with [Orochimaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Orochimaru "Orochimaru") and [Jiraiya](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jiraiya "Jiraiya") under the leadership of [Hiruzen Sarutobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hiruzen_Sarutobi "Hiruzen Sarutobi"). When Jiraiya failed to acquire a bell during the team's first [bell test](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Bell_test "Bell test"), Tsunade teased him, prompting him to criticise her flat chest.[^8] Despite this remark, Jiraiya had a certain interest in Tsunade from their first meeting,[^9] which only increased after her breasts developed. When she nearly killed him for spying on her while she bathed, he was forced to be more discreet about both his attraction to her and his interest in peeping.[^10]

Tsunade's younger brother, [Nawaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nawaki "Nawaki") dreamed of someday becoming Hokage so that he could protect Konoha, their grandfather's legacy. On Nawaki's twelfth birthday, Tsunade kissed his forehead and gave him Hashirama's necklace in the hopes that it would help him achieve his dream. He died the next day, a casualty of the ongoing [Second Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Shinobi_World_War "Second Shinobi World War"), thus returning the necklace to her. Tsunade began advocating for the inclusion of [medical-nin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Medical-nin "Medical-nin") on all four-man squads in order to avoid future deaths like Nawaki's. Hiruzen, the Third Hokage, agreed with her arguments, but could not dedicate resources to the necessary implementation due to the ongoing war. [Dan Katō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Dan_Kat%C5%8D "Dan Katō"), on the other hand, shared her views, and as their relationship developed he confided with her his dreams of becoming Hokage so that he could bring change to Konoha and protect his loved one from death. Tsunade kissed his forehead and gave him Hashirama's necklace in the hopes that it would help him achieve his dream.

![Hanzō dubs the Sannin.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/6/6f/Hanzo_and_Sannin.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150108211606)

Hanzō dubs the Sannin.

Although their team under Hiruzen's leadership had disbanded, Tsunade, Orochimaru, and Jiraiya continued fighting together during the Second Shinobi World War. During one such fight in [Amegakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Amegakure "Amegakure"), they were the only three to survive an encounter with [Hanzō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hanz%C5%8D "Hanzō"), an accomplishment for which Hanzō dubbed them Konoha's "Legendary [Sannin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sannin "Sannin") ".[^11] In the aftermath of the battle, Jiraiya opted not to return to Konoha so that he could train the [Ame orphans](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ame_orphans "Ame orphans"). Tsunade would go on to make her own independent contributions to the war, frequently counteracting [Chiyo](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chiyo "Chiyo") of [Sunagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sunagakure "Sunagakure") 's [poisons](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Poison "Poison").[^12] Although Tsunade's actions are said to have won the war for Konoha,[^13] Tsunade was unable to prevent Dan's death, and her failure to stop his severe blood loss caused her to develop [haemophobia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blood_phobia "wikipedia:Blood phobia"). With Dan's death, Hashirama's necklace returned to Tsunade once again, convincing some that the necklace was cursed to kill anyone who wore it other than Tsunade. Tsunade herself became convinced that achieving one's dreams was impossible and that to be Hokage was a "fool's job". She therefore left Konoha during the height of the [Third Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Shinobi_World_War "Third Shinobi World War"), taking Dan's niece, [Shizune](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shizune "Shizune"), with her to train her as a disciple. Due to her haemophobia, Tsunade retired from any direct involvement in healing or combat, instead relying on Shizune's skills whenever a need for either came up.

At one point during their travels, Tsunade and Shizune encountered the [Fourth Raikage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Raikage "Raikage") [A](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/A_\(Fourth_Raikage\) "A (Fourth Raikage)"), where the latter requested her help in removing the [Exploding Insect](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Exploding_Insect "Exploding Insect") implanted in his [subordinate](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Karai "Karai"). However, Tsunade was completely drunk and ended up falling asleep, completely forgetting about her conversation with A. After A insisted, Tsunade challenged him to an arm wrestling contest, promising to help should she lose. A managed to defeat Tsunade by using his speed rather than strength, and Tsunade complied and followed him to his subordinate's location. After Tsunade diagnoses the situation and realises that one of the insects was also inside A, she decided to conduct the surgery. However, Tsunade instead enlisted Shizune to conduct it, and once blood was splattered in her face, Tsunade left to regain her composure while Shizune performed the surgery and explained the origins of Tsunade's trauma to A and his team.[^14]

## Personality

Tsunade was very willing to believe in the attainability of dreams while her little brother Nawaki and her lover Dan were still alive, and that by supporting and encouraging those dreams, she might be able to help achieve them. When both died gruesomely shortly after receiving her support, Tsunade lost faith in the idea of dreaming for anything, believing that the mere pursuit of it would be doomed from the outset and retiring from the life of a ninja with the intention to spend the rest of her life away from her village in the company of her dear disciple Shizune, Dan's own niece. Tsunade becomes especially critical of those wanting to be Hokage, as both Dan and Nawaki died trying to attain the title and the past Hokage (including her grandfather Hashirama, her great-uncle [Tobirama](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tobirama "Tobirama"), and her former teacher Hiruzen) have all died prematurely and, as Tsunade argues, pointlessly because they held the position; she concludes that to be Hokage is a fool's job.[^15]

![Tsunade's traumatic loss develops hemophobia.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/6/6d/Tsunade%27s_Hemophobia.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240330035623)

Tsunade's traumatic loss develops hemophobia.

Tsunade is forced to reconsider her cynicism upon meeting [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki"), who demonstrates two things to her: just because a goal seems impossible does not mean it actually is, as Naruto is able to master the [Rasengan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rasengan "Rasengan") unprecedentedly quickly despite seemingly lacking any natural talent; and just because the dreamer dies does not mean the dream itself does, as Nawaki and Dan's desire to become Hokage lives on in Naruto. Because Naruto is so very much an exception to how she thought the world worked, Tsunade puts her faith in dreams once again, a desire so strong that she overcomes her fear of blood.[^16] She becomes Hokage herself, partly so that she can live Nawaki and Dan's dream for them, but also so that she can pave the way for Naruto inevitably holding the title. Becoming close to Naruto, he became the only person she allowed to refer to her as "Grandma", knowing it was out of affection. Later, when Tsunade is approached by [Sakura Haruno](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sakura_Haruno "Sakura Haruno"), who asks to be her disciple and be taught [medical ninjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Medical_ninjutsu "Medical ninjutsu"), Tsunade heartily agrees not only because she senses Sakura's potential but because she realizes Sakura has something she wants to protect ([Sasuke Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Uchiha "Sasuke Uchiha") and Naruto himself), just like she wanted to protect Nawaki and Dan. As a result, Tsunade spends the next years juggling between her roles as a village leader and as a mentor with great devotion, despite the constant stress the former position puts on her.

As Hokage, Tsunade is committed to protecting Konoha and its villagers, willing to give her life for her village and allies if necessary.[^17] Just as she once did, she supports all other dreams that she can; for example, when [Rock Lee](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rock_Lee "Rock Lee") tells her he dreams of being a ninja despite knowing he may die trying, she does not argue his decision and, in the anime, does all that she can to ensure he survives. She does this because Nawaki and Dan loved Konoha and so she keeps their dreams alive by doing so, but also because Konoha's continued survival honours all those who gave their lives creating it and keeping it safe. Although her reasons are somewhat different, the outcome is nevertheless consistent with the [Will of Fire](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Will_of_Fire "Will of Fire"). Tsunade is similarly optimistic about the [next generation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_11 "Konoha 11") of Konoha's ninja, especially Naruto and Sakura, convinced that the village's future will be in good hands because of them. Demonstrations of her faith include but are not limited to: congratulating [Shikamaru Nara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shikamaru_Nara "Shikamaru Nara") for his failed mission because at least none of his teammates died;[^18] and not labelling Sasuke a [missing-nin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Missing-nin "Missing-nin") because she trusts that Naruto can someday convince him to return to Konoha. She in fact becomes angry when others lack this same faith in their peers, believing it is better to let them try and then fail than to assume their failure is inevitable and thus prevent them from trying.[^19]

Even as a child, Tsunade had a love for gambling.

Despite her many noble qualities, Tsunade is not without her faults and vices. She is blunt and short-tempered, prone to striking Naruto for his immaturity or her old friend Jiraiya for his perversion; she is widely feared because of this tendency.[^20] Her own behaviour isn't much better than theirs, as she drinks constantly, sometimes sleeps on the job, and tries as much as she can to have other people do her work for her. One of her favourite vices is gambling, which she acquired from Hashirama. Despite how often and how irresponsibly she gambles, Tsunade has notoriously bad luck, causing those who have made huge profits off of her to mockingly call her "The Legendary Sucker" (伝説のカモ, *Densetsu no Kamo*).[^21] Tsunade is fully aware of how unlucky she is, and so whenever she hits a winning streak she takes it as a bad omen and prepares for the worst;[^22] she has at times tried using her bad luck to her advantage, betting on outcomes she doesn't want so that her unluckiness will prevent them from happening.[^23] Hashirama finds Tsunade's many bad habits highly amusing on a personal level, but when he is told that Tsunade is Hokage, he immediately worries about what misfortunes she could have brought upon Konoha.[^7]

![Tsunade dreams of being with her loved ones.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/c/c3/Tsunade%27s_Infinite_Tsukuyomi_Dream.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160226000051)

Tsunade has complex relationships with her fellow Sannin. From their first meeting, Jiraiya assumed that there was a mutual attraction between him and Tsunade, a statement that only served to annoy and anger her. Despite his many advances over the years and the genuine affection between each other, she never reciprocated his romantic feelings, only ever loving Dan, even decades after his death. Jiraiya wasn't discouraged by this and claimed that her repeated rejections had not only made him stronger, but they've provided excellent material for his *[Icha Icha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Icha_Icha "Icha Icha")* series of books. Though he concluded that to be happy wasn't his fate. she doubted he believed his own words and she commented that he did not need to put on a brave front when he returned from [Amegakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Amegakure "Amegakure").[^23] When Jiraiya ended up dying during that same mission, Tsunade privately mourned for the loss of her dear friend and teammate.[^9] In contrast to her affectionate friendship with Jiraiya, Tsunade is permanently at odds with their former teammate and friend Orochimaru due to to a long history of betrayal and distrust, from the danger he was to their home and team to his deal to bring Dan and Nawaki [reincarnated](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning:_Impure_World_Reincarnation "Summoning: Impure World Reincarnation"), a result that would make her happy but at the same would go against all they stood for, which she eventually realised. Even after Orochimaru contributed in their victory in the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War"), she still remains wary of her old teammate. This resentment influenced her thoughts of anyone directly related to him, and she was furious at Naruto, in his capacity as the Seventh Hokage, for allowing Orochimaru's child, [Mitsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mitsuki "Mitsuki"), to immigrate into Konohagakure. Mitsuki's heritage alone led Tsunade to develop a prejudiced view that he is as dangerous as his parent.[^24] As revealed in the [Infinite Tsukuyomi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Infinite_Tsukuyomi "Infinite Tsukuyomi"), however, Tsunade's greatest desire was to be with [her](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nawaki "Nawaki") [lost](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Dan_Kat%C5%8D "Dan Katō") [loved](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Orochimaru "Orochimaru") [ones](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jiraiya "Jiraiya") while supporting Dan as he lived out his dream of being Hokage, showing she still misses her time with the other Sannin, even Orochimaru.[^25]

## Appearance

Tsunade is a fair-skinned woman with brown eyes and straight blonde hair. Her hair parts above her forehead and has shoulder-length bangs that frame her face and the rest reaches her lower back. During her childhood and through her young-adult years, she wore her hair in a high ponytail, but she later keeps it tied in two loose ponytails. The [Strength of a Hundred Seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Strength_of_a_Hundred_Seal "Strength of a Hundred Seal") - taking the appearance of a violet diamond mark- is clearly visible on her forehead. As an adolescent, she was considered flat-chested, something which her teammate Jiraiya poked fun at. By young-adulthood however, she grew into a slender woman with noticeably large breasts and a curvaceous figure, which Jiraiya noted to be 106 centimetres;[^26] Tsunade is in fact widely regarded as the most beautiful kunoichi in the world.[^27] By her fifties, she appears much older, being heavily wrinkled and rather frail-looking (presumably from over-usage of her [Creation Rebirth](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Creation_Rebirth "Creation Rebirth")). However, she maintains her youthful appearance using a unique [Transformation Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Transformation_Technique "Transformation Technique").

In her childhood, Tsunade wore grey mesh shirt and over it a teal-coloured kimono with short sleeves and red-lining around the collars which she secured with a blue sash. She also wore red wristbands and white wrapping over her calves with blue sandals.

Tsunade often wears a grass-green haori with the kanji for "gamble" (賭, *kake*) on the back, inside a red circle. Underneath she wears a grey, kimono-style blouse with no sleeves, held closed by a broad, dark bluish-grey obi that matches her pants. Her blouse is closed quite low, revealing her sizeable cleavage. She wears open-toed, strapped black sandals with high heels. She has red nail polish on both her fingernails and toenails and uses a soft pink lipstick. She wore the [First Hokage's necklace](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/First_Hokage%27s_necklace "First Hokage's necklace") for many years until she gave it to Naruto.

## Abilities

![Tsunade shattering Madara's Susanoo ribcage.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/b3/Heaven_spear_kick.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150406161906)

Tsunade shattering Madara's Susanoo ribcage.

Like other students of the [Third Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Hokage "Third Hokage"), Tsunade is an exceptionally talented shinobi. She is considered the strongest kunoichi in [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure") history.[^28] She became well-known following the [Second Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Shinobi_World_War "Second Shinobi World War") when she, alongside Jiraiya and Orochimaru, were titled [Sannin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sannin "Sannin") by [Hanzō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hanz%C5%8D "Hanzō"). Her skills are popularly held as the reason for Konoha's victory during that war, and in the years following, she became known as the world's strongest kunoichi.[^27] Despite being rusty, her skills easily outclassed [Kabuto Yakushi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kabuto_Yakushi "Kabuto Yakushi") 's when they battled, with him having to rely on using a [Military Rations Pill](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Military_Rations_Pill "Military Rations Pill") to continue battling and using her fear of blood against her to gain the upperhand. Following the Third Hokage's death, she was unanimously recognised to become the Fifth [Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage "Hokage"), proving her worth by saving countless villagers when [Pain](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Pain "Pain") [decimated](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shinra_Tensei "Shinra Tensei") Konoha. Even the likes of [Madara Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Madara_Uchiha "Madara Uchiha") admits that Tsunade was worthy of being a Kage.[^29]

### Physical and Chakra Prowess

![Tsunade ruptures the ground with one finger.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/f/f9/Tsunade_defeats_Naruto.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150826191554)

Tsunade ruptures the ground with one finger.

While Tsunade believes it is vital for a medical-nin to avoid injury, as they cannot perform their duties if anything happens to them,[^30] she is equally famous for her immense prowess in taijutsu, said to wield the highest level of efficiency.[^31] This is mainly done as a deterrent to keep enemies from getting too close to her while she performs her medical-nin duties. Even more, belying her slender form, she wields inhuman raw strength, able to easily split the ground with a finger strike [^32] or lift and hurl [Gamabunta](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gamabunta "Gamabunta") 's sword with deadly accuracy.[^33] Only by severing her muscles is that strength inhibited.[^34]

![Tsunade releasing her seal.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/1/1e/Yin_Seal_Release.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240928165151)

Tsunade releasing her seal.

As a descendant of both the [Senju](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Senju "Senju") and [Uzumaki Clans](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uzumaki_Clan "Uzumaki Clan"), Tsunade inherited their life-force and physical energy, allowing her to still fight at peak performance despite her naturally frail form and battle the likes of [Madara Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Madara_Uchiha "Madara Uchiha") for over half a day without exhaustion. She can even endure several mortal stabs to the chest [^35] and retain consciousness after being bisected [^36] long enough to heal others before being healed herself. Her reserves of chakra are immense, enough to heal several thousand shinobi during the final battle with [Obito Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Obito_Uchiha "Obito Uchiha"). In addition, Tsunade has excellent control over her [chakra](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra "Chakra"), able to perform her various feats to the maximum efficiency with absolute minimum required chakra and build-up time. Her timing and precision for focusing her chakra is so advanced, by releasing it in parts of her body at the key point of contact, she can further her strength to decimate a target, even break through an " [absolute defence](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Absolute_defence "Absolute defence") " in [Susanoo](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Susanoo "Susanoo"), with a single blow, which led to Madara praising her as having greater raw power than the [Fourth Raikage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Raikage "Fourth Raikage").[^37]

### Ninjutsu

![Tsunade and Katsuyu healing injuries.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/b8/Katsuyu_healing.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150922214903)

Tsunade and Katsuyu healing injuries.

Tsunade has advanced skill with [fūinjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/F%C5%ABinjutsu "Fūinjutsu"). With her [Strength of a Hundred Seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Strength_of_a_Hundred_Seal "Strength of a Hundred Seal"), she can continuously store up additional chakra into it. When the seal is [released](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yin_Seal:_Release "Yin Seal: Release"), she has access to all the accumulated chakra, totaling a so-named hundred-times her usual chakra levels. While normally using this extra chakra to perform her techniques, she can also transfer the chakra to others, either to replenish or amplify their own.[^38]

Tsunade can [summon](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning_Technique "Summoning Technique") segments of [Katsuyu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Katsuyu "Katsuyu"), a [slug](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Slug "Slug") from the [Shikkotsu Forest](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shikkotsu_Forest "Shikkotsu Forest"). In addition to fighting alongside Tsunade, Katsuyu can use her [divisions](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Slug_Great_Division "Slug Great Division") as a form of long-distance communication or to move and shield Tsunade's injured allies. Should her allies be a distance away, she can [send](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Reverse_Summoning_Technique "Reverse Summoning Technique") Katsuyu to their location.[^39] By [linking](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Katsuyu:_Immense_Network_Healing "Katsuyu: Immense Network Healing") with Katsuyu, Tsunade can monitor those that Katsuyu has attached to, remotely healing them and replenishing their chakra as needed.

Tsunade's [natures](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nature "Nature") include [Lightning](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning "Lightning"), [Fire](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fire "Fire"), [Water](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water "Water"), and [Earth](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Earth "Earth"), as well as [Yang](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yang "Yang") [^3] and in the anime [Yin Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yin_Release "Yin Release").[^40] In the anime, Tsunade can make [shadow clones](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_clones "Shadow clones") and also shows proficiency in [space–time ninjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Space%E2%80%93time_ninjutsu "Space–time ninjutsu") and [barrier ninjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Barrier_ninjutsu "Barrier ninjutsu").[^41]

#### Transformation Technique

Despite being in her fifties, Tsunade constantly appears as if she were still in her twenties through her [Transformation Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Transformation_Technique "Transformation Technique"). Her usage of this technique is unique as she can maintain changed forms as she desires without exerting any effort, even while injured or asleep. However, the technique dispels when she runs out of chakra, reverting her to her natural appearance.[^42] She readily alters what age she appears as in order to avoid her gambling debts.[^21]

#### Medical Ninjutsu

Tsunade is hailed as the world's greatest [medical-nin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Medical-nin "Medical-nin"), able to identify any ailments at a glance and treat conditions that other medical-nin deem impossible to cure. Orochimaru believed only she could reverse the necrosis-like effects of the [Dead Demon Consuming Seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Dead_Demon_Consuming_Seal "Dead Demon Consuming Seal"). Amongst other things, she [healed](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mystical_Palm_Technique "Mystical Palm Technique") the psychological damage of [Tsukuyomi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tsukuyomi "Tsukuyomi");[^43] fixed the damage to [Rock Lee](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rock_Lee "Rock Lee") 's spine when he was deemed a helpless case;[^44] counteracted the effects of the [Three Coloured Pills](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Three_Coloured_Pills "Three Coloured Pills") with instructions from the [Nara Clan Medical Encyclopaedia](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nara_Clan_Medical_Encyclopaedia "Nara Clan Medical Encyclopaedia");[^45] and near-instantly healed Shikamaru's severe chakra depletion after he came into contact with a root of the [God Tree](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/God_Tree "God Tree"), the chakra cloak surrounding him noticeably reacting to her treatment.[^46]

Though Tsunade rarely uses her medical ninjutsu in combat, she is more than able to do so. She is notably capable of using the [Body Pathway Derangement](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Body_Pathway_Derangement "Body Pathway Derangement") technique, a high-level medical jutsu which injects chakra into an opponent, scrambling their body's electric signals so that commands go to the wrong body part, like making one's hand move when intending to move their leg.[^47] It is noted to be an extremely difficult technique to master, however, Tsunade was able to do so thanks to her expertise in medical treatment.[^48]

![Tsunade healing herself with Creation Rebirth.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/f/fe/Creation_Rebirth.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240928171334)

Tsunade healing herself with Creation Rebirth.

Undoubtedly, the pinnacle of Tsunade's medical prowess is her [Creation Rebirth](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Creation_Rebirth "Creation Rebirth"), which draws on her vast chakra supplies to instantaneously heal any wounds she receives. Because it requires no [hand seals](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hand_seal "Hand seal") to perform, she needs no conscious effort on her part once active and can heal any damage no matter how severe (her chakra levels permitting); [Orochimaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Orochimaru "Orochimaru") describes it as a form of immortality that even he is envious of.[^49] Despite how useful it is in battle, Creation Rebirth is a double-edged sword: the body's cells can only divide and be created a set amount of times, so whenever Tsunade uses the technique, she shortens her lifespan. Because of this, she only uses it in very desperate situations.

![Tsunade researching for a procedure.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/e/e8/Nara_Clan_Medical_Encyclopaedia.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150601122918)

Tsunade researching for a procedure.

Tsunade's skills extend beyond the typical [medical ninjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Medical_ninjutsu "Medical ninjutsu"). She earned fame during the Second Shinobi World War for being the only person able to make antidotes for the poisons of [Sunagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sunagakure "Sunagakure") 's elder [Chiyo](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chiyo "Chiyo").[^12] Similarly, she can prepare sleeping potions that are undetectable to even the most skilled ninja.[^50] She is more than knowledgeable enough to perform autopsies and examine damage on a cellular level. In addition, Tsunade is a skilled researcher. In the anime, she figured out Kabuto's [Yin Healing Wound Destruction](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yin_Healing_Wound_Destruction "Yin Healing Wound Destruction") and used what she found to raise the survival rate of Rock Lee's medical procedure.[^51] Following the Fourth Shinobi World War, she studies the material that the [White Zetsu Army](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/White_Zetsu_Army "White Zetsu Army") is made of and learns to make moveable replacement limbs from it. Tsunade has consistently proven to be a highly skilled teacher in medical ninjutsu, as all her disciples, Shizune, Sakura, and Ino each became very respected medical-nin in their own respective rights. Due to her vast medical knowledge, Tsunade was primarily responsible for setting up the modern system of medical-nin, though she exempts herself from many of the laws that govern them due to her Creation Rebirth.[^37]

## Part I

### Search for Tsunade

Main article: [Search for Tsunade](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Search_for_Tsunade "Search for Tsunade")

![Tsunade and Orochimaru meet for the first time in years.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/06/Tsunade_and_Orochimaru.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20251018192318)

Tsunade and Orochimaru meet for the first time in years.

Tsunade hits a jackpot while playing the slot machines in [Tanzaku Quarters](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tanzaku_Quarters "Tanzaku Quarters"). She takes this as a sign that something bad is about to happen and makes immediate plans to leave the city, but [Shizune](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shizune "Shizune") convinces her to visit a local castle before they go. While they're there, the castle is destroyed by a [snake](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Snake "Snake"), [Orochimaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Orochimaru "Orochimaru") 's way of saying hello. Tsunade immediately notices the damage to his arms and, guessing he wants her to heal them, preemptively refuses. Orochimaru's assistant, [Kabuto Yakushi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kabuto_Yakushi "Kabuto Yakushi"), explains that they've tried all other options and that only Tsunade can help. She asks how his arms were damaged, to which Orochimaru responds that the damage was received while he killed [Hiruzen Sarutobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hiruzen_Sarutobi "Hiruzen Sarutobi"). Tsunade becomes enraged and threatens to kill Orochimaru, but he piques her interest when he offers to [revive](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Impure_World_Reincarnation "Impure World Reincarnation") [Nawaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nawaki "Nawaki") and [Dan Katō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Dan_Kat%C5%8D "Dan Katō").

Tsunade asks what he would do if his arms were restored. Orochimaru replies that he would destroy [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure"), which is what he was trying to do when his arms were damaged in the first place. Shizune tries to convince Tsunade to help her kill Orochimaru and Kabuto, but they decide to leave instead, giving Tsunade a week to decide. While having dinner later, they are found by [Jiraiya](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jiraiya "Jiraiya"). Jiraiya asks her to become the Fifth [Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage "Hokage"), which she refuses, upsetting Jiraiya's companion, [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki"). Tsunade recognises Naruto as the [jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki "Jinchūriki") of the [Nine-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tails "Nine-Tails") and starts remarking on what a waste it was for the [Fourth Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Hokage "Fourth Hokage") to give his life saving Konoha from the Nine-Tails. She reflects further that all the past Hokage have wasted their lives for the village and concludes that only a fool would ever want the title.

![Tsunade and Naruto face-off.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/8d/Tsunade_vs_naruto.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240927015621)

Tsunade and Naruto face-off.

Naruto, who has his own dreams of being Hokage, is enraged and tries to attack Tsunade. Jiraiya holds him back, but Tsunade agrees to fight with him outside the restaurant. Tsunade easily outmatches Naruto using only one finger for offence and defence, but she's unsettled by how similar his resolve is to Nawaki and Dan's. While she's distracted by this, Naruto attacks her with an incomplete [Rasengan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rasengan "Rasengan"), which she once again avoids using only a finger. She berates Jiraiya for teaching Naruto the Rasengan at all, declaring it cruel to let someone as untalented as him believe he can master something as complicated as the Rasengan. Naruto vows to complete it in three days, prompting Tsunade to make a wager: if Naruto can master the Rasengan in a week, she'll give him the [First Hokage's necklace](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/First_Hokage%27s_necklace "First Hokage's necklace"); if he can't, she gets his [wallet](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gama-chan "Gama-chan"). Naruto agrees.

![Tsunade spikes Jiraiya's drink.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/09/Tsunade_drugs_Jiraiya.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240925022247)

Tsunade spikes Jiraiya's drink.

Tsunade and Jiraiya have drinks later, and the latter guesses that Orochimaru made some offer to Tsunade. He doesn't ask for details about the offer, but he promises to personally kill her if she does anything to endanger Konoha. She claims not to care about the village, so Jiraiya reminds her of how important the village was to Nawaki and Dan; he further notes how similar they, particularly Nawaki, are to Naruto. Tsunade keeps tabs on Naruto as he trains, but by the end of the week he has failed to master the Rasengan and the intense training leaves him bedridden. Shizune pleads with Tsunade not to accept Orochimaru's deal and, when this fails, she tries to use force, prompting Tsunade to knock her out. She meets with Jiraiya again and drugs his drink, knocking him out and, when he wakes up the next morning, making it difficult for him to use his [chakra](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra "Chakra").

![Tsunade preparing to "heal" Orochimaru.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/c/c5/Tsunade%27s_decision.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240925022027)

Tsunade preparing to "heal" Orochimaru.

Tsunade meets with Orochimaru and agrees to heal his arms on the condition that he would leave Konoha alone. Orochimaru agrees. She approaches him, apparently to heal him, but Kabuto notices at the last moment that she has [killing intent](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Killing_intent "Killing intent") and stops her. Orochimaru questions why she would deceive him, to which she replies that she knew he was lying about sparing Konoha. Although she desperately wants to see Nawaki and Dan again, she knows they wouldn't want to be brought back at the cost of Konoha's safety, something Naruto has reminded her of. Orochimaru and Kabuto state that they'll force her to heal his arms, causing her to attack them. They draw her away from the city and Kabuto, a [medic-nin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Medic-nin "Medic-nin"), tries neutralising her, but she heals all the debilitating damage he does. Unable to stop her through conventional means, Kabuto slits his own wrists and coats her with his blood, paralysing her due to her phobia.

![Tsunade protected by Naruto.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/f/f1/Naruto_protects_Tsunade.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150224122508)

Tsunade protected by Naruto.

Jiraiya, Shizune, and Naruto eventually arrive to help. Shizune looks after Tsunade as she shudders from the blood while Jiraiya fights Orochimaru and Naruto fights Kabuto. Naruto is quickly defeated by Kabuto, as is Shizune when she tries to face him afterwards. Kabuto approaches Tsunade and tries to restrain her, which she is unable to fend off in her condition. Naruto stops him and attacks with an incomplete Rasengan, but misses, causing Kabuto to start commenting on his lack of talent, just as Tsunade did the week before. Naruto persists and, after making a [shadow clone](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_clone "Shadow clone") to help him, he is able to strike Kabuto with a perfected Rasengan. Kabuto is defeated, but as his last act he severs Naruto's heart muscles. Tsunade tries healing him, fearing that he'll die just like Nawaki and Dan did. She succeeds, and in a brief moment of regained consciousnesses Naruto claims the necklace he's won.

![Tsunade defeats Orochimaru.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/9/90/Tsunade_punch_orochimaru.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240925005526)

Tsunade defeats Orochimaru.

Orochimaru decides to kill Naruto and attacks with his [Sword of Kusanagi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sword_of_Kusanagi "Sword of Kusanagi"). Tsunade uses her body to block the attack, her way of protecting Naruto, Konoha's future Hokage. Orochimaru keeps trying to attack him, which Tsunade keeps using her body to block. Her resolve to protect Naruto eventually overcomes her fear of blood, allowing her to go on the offensive: she kicks Orochimaru, activates [Creation Rebirth](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Creation_Rebirth "Creation Rebirth") to heal all the damage she's received, and declares her acceptance of the position of Fifth Hokage. She, Jiraiya, and Orochimaru each [summon](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning_Technique "Summoning Technique") [Katsuyu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Katsuyu "Katsuyu"), [Gamabunta](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gamabunta "Gamabunta"), and [Manda](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Manda "Manda"), respectively. When Manda proves too elusive for Katsuyu and Gamabunta, Tsunade uses Gamabunta's sword to pin Manda to the ground with his mouth shut. She then delivers a sequence of punches to Orochimaru's face, forcing him to escape with Kabuto; before he leaves, he warns that he has [other ways](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Living_Corpse_Reincarnation "Living Corpse Reincarnation") of fixing his arms.

Having used so much chakra during the fight, Tsunade's younger transformation fades after the battle, but it is restored before Naruto wakes up. When he hears about what happened while he was unconscious, Naruto expresses his concern that she isn't qualified to be Fifth Hokage, being vastly inferior to Hiruzen. Tsunade challenges him to a fight and once again outmatches him using a single finger. Rather than defeat him, however, she kisses his forehead to symbolise her faith in his achieving his dreams, just as she once did for Nawaki and Dan.

### Sasuke Recovery Mission

Main article: [Sasuke Recovery Mission](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Recovery_Mission "Sasuke Recovery Mission")

![Tsunade becomes the Fifth Hokage.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/a/aa/Tsunade_as_Hokage.PNG/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20260807013745)

Tsunade becomes the Fifth Hokage.

When they get back to Konoha, the villagers start making preparations to officially place Tsunade into office. Before that can happen, Naruto has Tsunade heal the [damage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tsukuyomi "Tsukuyomi") to the minds of [Kakashi Hatake](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi_Hatake "Kakashi Hatake") and [Sasuke Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Uchiha "Sasuke Uchiha"). She also looks at the damage to [Rock Lee](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rock_Lee "Rock Lee") 's spine; although she's the only one who can fix it, she confesses that there's a fifty percent chance he wouldn't survive the procedure and recommends he give up on life as a ninja. After thinking it over, Lee tells her plainly that being a ninja is his dream, which she cannot argue with. In the anime, Tsunade tries to improve his chances of survival by studying [one of Kabuto's jutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yin_Healing_Wound_Destruction "Yin Healing Wound Destruction"), and in doing so she improves Lee's odds to 58%. Lee ultimately survives the procedure.

Being Hokage comes with a heavy workload, especially in the aftermath of Orochimaru's [Konoha Crush](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Crush "Konoha Crush"), which left the village understaffed. For this reason, when Tsunade is informed that Sasuke has defected to Orochimaru, [Shikamaru Nara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shikamaru_Nara "Shikamaru Nara") - only recently promoted to [chūnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin "Chūnin") - is the highest ranking ninja she can assign to lead the [Sasuke Recovery Team](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Recovery_Team "Sasuke Recovery Team"); she allows him any [genin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genin "Genin") he can recruit for the mission, though recommends Naruto's inclusion. After the Recovery Team leaves, Tsunade is notified of the [Konoha Special Mission Platoon](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Special_Mission_Platoon "Konoha Special Mission Platoon") 's defeat by the [Sound Four](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sound_Four "Sound Four"), Sasuke's escorts. Having already suspected that a team of genin wouldn't be enough, she requests [Sunagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sunagakure "Sunagakure") 's support so that Orochimaru, a mutual enemy of both villages, can't gain Sasuke's [Sharingan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sharingan "Sharingan"). She is also informed that Lee, despite still recovering from his surgery, has also gone off to lend support.

![Tsunade trains Sakura.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/4/42/Sakura_training.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20110522004852)

Tsunade trains Sakura.

Despite the best efforts of the Recovery Team, Lee, and Suna's [Three Sand Siblings](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Three_Sand_Siblings "Three Sand Siblings"), they are unable to stop Sasuke from joining forces with Orochimaru. Tsunade sends the [Medic Corps](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Medic_Corps "Medic Corps") to retrieve the Recovery Team's members and tend to their injuries; Tsunade, by studying the [Nara Clan Medical Encyclopaedia](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nara_Clan_Medical_Encyclopaedia "Nara Clan Medical Encyclopaedia"), is able to cure [Chōji Akimichi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%8Dji_Akimichi "Chōji Akimichi") of the [Three Coloured Pills](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Three_Coloured_Pills "Three Coloured Pills") ' side-effects. Tsunade is later approached by [Sakura Haruno](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sakura_Haruno "Sakura Haruno"), who asks to be taught [medical ninjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Medical_ninjutsu "Medical ninjutsu"). Sensing that Sakura not only has potential but has something she wants to protect (Naruto and Sasuke) just like she did (Nawaki and Dan), Tsunade heartily accepts and puts Sakura through intense training sessions for the next two years.

### In Naruto's Footsteps: The Friends' Paths

Main article: [In Naruto's Footsteps: The Friends' Paths](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/In_Naruto%27s_Footsteps:_The_Friends%27_Paths "In Naruto's Footsteps: The Friends' Paths") Sakura progresses rapidly under Tsunade's tutelage. In the anime, [Ino Yamanaka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ino_Yamanaka "Ino Yamanaka") also asks to train with Tsunade. From seeing the rivalry that exists between Sakura and Ino and how that competition drives them each to do better, Tsunade agrees.[^52] However, she teaches Ino only medical ninjutsu and not the additional skills she teaches Sakura, believing it would be better for Ino to focus on her clan's [hiden jutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hiden_jutsu "Hiden jutsu").[^53]

![Tsunade and the Konoha Council meeting with the Suna delegation.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/c/c7/Suna_and_Konoha_meeting.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150108135730)

Tsunade and the Konoha Council meeting with the Suna delegation.

About two years after Naruto leaves Konoha to train with Jiraiya, Tsunade proposes to Suna's [Fifth Kazekage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fifth_Kazekage "Fifth Kazekage") that their two villages hold a [Chūnin Exam](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exam "Chūnin Exam"). Publicly, the exam will demonstrate the alliance that now exists between their two villages. Secretly, the exam will hopefully draw out [Akatsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki "Akatsuki"), who are after jinchūriki like the Kazekage. The Kazekage agrees and the two villages split responsibilities for the exams. She sends Kakashi Hatake to invite [Amegakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Amegakure "Amegakure") to the exams with instructions to investigate rumours that Ame's leader, [Hanzō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hanz%C5%8D "Hanzō"), has been overthrown.[^54] Ame accepts the invitation and Kakashi is unable to uncover anything, but both are convinced that something unusual is happening in Ame.

The first stage of the exams is completed in Konoha without incident. Before the examinees and proctors relocate to Suna for the second stage, Tsunade warns the Konoha personnel to be alert to attacks on the Kazekage.[^55] The Kazekage is indeed attacked during the second stage, forcing the exams to be cancelled prematurely. Performance reports on the remaining participants are sent to their respective villages to decide who will be promoted to chūnin; Tsunade promotes the members of [Team 8](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_8 "Team 8"), [Team 10](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Asuma "Team Asuma"), and [Team Guy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Guy "Team Guy"). She also promotes [Neji Hyūga](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Neji_Hy%C5%ABga "Neji Hyūga") to [jōnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/J%C5%8Dnin "Jōnin") because of his role in protecting the Kazekage.[^56]

## Part II

### Kazekage Rescue Mission

Main article: [Kazekage Rescue Mission](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kazekage_Rescue_Mission "Kazekage Rescue Mission")

![Tsunade and Shizune oversee Naruto and Sakura's bell test.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/7/71/Tsunade_and_Shizune_oversee_Team_7.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230313053200)

Tsunade and Shizune oversee Naruto and Sakura's bell test.

Tsunade is informed of Naruto's return to Konoha after two-and-a-half years of training. She shares this news with Sakura and together they welcome him back. Tsunade has Naruto and Sakura reform [Team 7](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Kakashi "Team Kakashi") under Kakashi's leadership. While trying to find a mission to send Team 7 on that Naruto won't complain about, Tsunade receives a report that Suna's [Fifth Kazekage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fifth_Kazekage "Fifth Kazekage") has been kidnapped by [Akatsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki "Akatsuki"). Tsunade decides to send Team 7 to lend assistance in the Kazekage's rescue in full awareness that Akatsuki wants to capture Naruto too. She isn't initially concerned, but when she suddenly wins the lottery she takes this as a bad sign and sends [Team Guy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Guy "Team Guy") to provide additional support.

A few days later they receive news that the Kazekage was successfully rescued and that Teams 7 and Guy will be returning to Konoha. ### Tenchi Bridge Reconnaissance Mission

Shizune once again questions Naruto's inclusion on the team, believing it would be safer to keep him in Konoha. Tsunade does ask Sakura to look after Naruto just in case, though.

![Tsunade meets with Homura and Koharu to discuss Naruto.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/6/60/Homura_and_Koharu_meet_with_Tsunade.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230505161444)

Despite how unsuccessful previous attempts to capture Akatsuki members have been, Tsunade approves the formation of a combined [Eight Man Squad](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eight_Man_Squad "Eight Man Squad") to find Itachi.

![Tsunade talks with Jiraiya.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/b1/Shushu-ya.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20211124031313)

### Pain's Assault

Main article: [Pain's Assault](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Pain%27s_Assault_\(Arc\) "Pain's Assault (Arc)")

![Tsunade berates Homura and Koharu for lacking faith.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/6/64/Homura_and_Koharu_accosted.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230412002436)

Tsunade berates Homura and Koharu for lacking faith.

[Fukasaku](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fukasaku "Fukasaku"), a former teacher of Jiraiya, reports to Tsunade that Jiraiya died while investigating the Akatsuki leader, [Pain](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Pain "Pain"). Tsunade summons Naruto to her office so that Fukasaku can also tell him the news, who they expect will have greater difficulty. Naruto is indeed upset and blames Tsunade for his death, saying she should not have let him go. Sakura tries to stop him from storming out in defense of her mentor, but Tsunade lets him go. Tsunade makes investigating Pain's identity using the information Jiraiya gathered top priority: she has Shizune examine the [Animal Path](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Animal_Path "Animal Path") 's body; she has [Ibiki Morino](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ibiki_Morino "Ibiki Morino") interrogate the prisoner Jiraiya captured; she has [Shikamaru Nara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shikamaru_Nara "Shikamaru Nara") decipher the coded message Jiraiya left behind. When Shikamaru tries objecting to the assignment, Tsunade ignores him, leaves, and, once she's alone, she cries for Jiraiya.

Knowing that Pain will be coming for him, Naruto goes to [Mount Myōboku](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mount_My%C5%8Dboku "Mount Myōboku") to train with Fukasaku. The investigations into Pain's identity continue, but none are not completed prior to [Pain's assault](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Pain%27s_assault "Pain's assault") on Konoha. Tsunade sends orders for Naruto to be recalled to Konoha, but she's stopped by the Konoha Council, who feel that Naruto is safest where he is. Tsunade becomes angry with both councillors and berates them for lacking even the barest faith in Naruto's ability to save himself. The councillors are affronted by how she handles them and leave, but tell her to do what she wants. Tsunade heads to the roof of the [Hokage Residence](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage_Residence "Hokage Residence") and summons Katsuyu, who she instructs to [divide](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Slug_Great_Division "Slug Great Division") and attach to all of Konoha's villagers so that Tsunade can [heal them remotely](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Katsuyu:_Immense_Network_Healing "Katsuyu: Immense Network Healing").

![Tsunade heals Konoha's villagers via Katsuyu.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/5/53/Healing_Chakra_Transmission.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150316101235)

Tsunade heals Konoha's villagers via Katsuyu.

As Katsuyu's divisions spread throughout the village, Tsunade starts to fully appreciate the extent of the damage that Pain's attack is doing. When she senses, through Katsuyu, that Kakashi has died, she destroys a nearby spire out of frustration. Pain's [Deva Path](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Deva_Path "Deva Path") eventually finds her. As her Anbu-bodyguards move in to protect her, Tsunade recognises the Deva Path as an [orphan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yahiko "Yahiko") that Jiraiya once trained. The Deva Path asks Tsunade where Naruto is, which she refuses to reveal. The Deva Path blames Konoha's past actions for the contemporary dire state of the world and Tsunade's refusal to cooperate with Akatsuki for the attack on the village. Tsunade is unmoved, but Pain is able to able to find out where Naruto is through other means. Before leaving, the Deva Path states it will show Konoha the pain that the rest of the world has known for too long.

![Tsunade in her weakened state.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/89/Tsunade_Ages.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150917021038)

Tsunade in her weakened state.

The Deva Path leaps into the air and Tsunade tries to follow. As it destroys Konoha with [Shinra Tensei](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shinra_Tensei "Shinra Tensei"), Tsunade transfers all of her chakra to Katsuyu to use in keeping the villagers safe. When the dust clears, Tsunade finds the village gone. She confronts the [Six Paths of Pain](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Six_Paths_of_Pain "Six Paths of Pain") and vows to personally defeat them. The [Asura Path](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Asura_Path "Asura Path") attacks her, which Naruto, newly returned, destroys in order to save her. Naruto asks Tsunade to trust Pain's defeat to him and has [Gamakichi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gamakichi "Gamakichi") take Tsunade to Sakura, who looks after her mentor. Having done all that she can and with her chakra gone, Tsunade's youthful transformation fades and she falls into a coma.

### Fourth Shinobi World War: Countdown

Main article: [Fourth Shinobi World War: Countdown](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War:_Countdown "Fourth Shinobi World War: Countdown")

![Tsunade awakens from her coma.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/4/4e/Tsunade_out_of_coma.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20210805230613)

Tsunade awakens from her coma.

After Tsunade regains consciousness many days later, she is immediately embraced by Shizune while Sakura goes around the village sharing the good news. As Tsunade eats to replenish her chakra reserves, she is told of all that's happened while she's been unconscious: Naruto's victory over Pain; [Tobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tobi "Tobi") of Akatsuki's declaration of a [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War"); the formation of the [Allied Shinobi Forces](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Allied_Shinobi_Forces "Allied Shinobi Forces"). Kakashi discusses these matters with her, and also thanks her for waking up in time so that he would not need to make her replacement as Hokage. Once she's done eating, she convenes a war council to discuss Konoha's strategies for the coming war and any preparations that will need to be made.

A few days later, Tsunade travels to [Kumogakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kumogakure "Kumogakure") to meet with the other heads of the Alliance and discuss topics of broader importance. Chief among these is what to do with Naruto and [Killer B](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Killer_B "Killer B"), whose capture Tobi started the war for. The Alliance's other leaders have already decided to send them into seclusion, which Tsunade strongly disagrees with, believing they would be invaluable on the battlefield. The [Fifth Kazekage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fifth_Kazekage "Fifth Kazekage") quickly disagrees, reminding her that Naruto has the tendency to be reckless for his friends, which in turn would place him too much at risk. Knowing she was overruled, Tsunade reluctantly agrees and Naruto and B are sent to the [Island Turtle](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Island_Turtle "Island Turtle"). In the anime, after the meeting with the other heads of the Alliance she requested that B teach Naruto to achieve control over the Nine-Tails in the event that they would have to be deployed, which A approved. As they move on to other matters, Tsunade takes particular interest in equipping the [Logistical Support and Medical Division](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Logistical_Support_and_Medical_Division "Logistical Support and Medical Division") with enough supplies.

### Fourth Shinobi World War: Confrontation

Main article: [Fourth Shinobi World War: Confrontation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War:_Confrontation "Fourth Shinobi World War: Confrontation") Most of the Alliance's leaders head to the battlefield when the war begins, but Tsunade and the [Fourth Raikage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Raikage "Fourth Raikage") remain at the Allied headquarters to discuss tactics, coordinate troop movements, and process new intel. On the first night of fighting, they receive reports that Naruto and B have escaped the Island Turtle. The Raikage takes Tsunade with him to head them off; she leaves behind one of Katsuyu's divisions in case she needs to be contacted about anything.

![Tsunade places her faith in Naruto and B.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/9/97/Tsunade%27s_Resolve.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20210721215131)

Tsunade places her faith in Naruto and B.

Once they locate Naruto and B, the Raikage blocks their progress and tries to convince them to return to the Island Turtle. Naruto implores Tsunade to let them pass, but she explains that she must do what is in the best interest of the entire Allied Shinobi Forces. Naruto's continuing obstinance prompts the Raikage to decide to kill him so that Akatsuki can't capture him. Tsunade cannot allow this and steps in, observing that Naruto's new [Nine-Tails Chakra Mode](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tails_Chakra_Mode "Nine-Tails Chakra Mode") indicates his unprecedented control over the Nine-Tails, something that may never be replicated in future jinchūriki of the Nine-Tails. The best course, she argues, is to let Naruto use his new power to try and win the war. The Raikage is not openly convinced by Tsunade's, B's, or Naruto's words and appears willing to fight, but when Naruto is finally able to get past him the Raikage doesn't pursue.

When Tsunade and the Raikage return to headquarters they are informed that the [White Zetsu Army](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/White_Zetsu_Army "White Zetsu Army") is cultivated from the DNA of [Hashirama Senju](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hashirama_Senju "Hashirama Senju"). Tsunade is upset by the continued use of her grandfather's abilities and what else Tobi may have used them for, but believes Naruto may be able to root out Zetsus that have [infiltrated](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Substitute_Technique "Substitute Technique") the Allied Forces.

### Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax

Main article: [Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War:_Climax "Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax")

![The five Kage confront Madara.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/c/c3/Gokage_Sh%C5%ABketsu.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130801135541)

The five Kage confront Madara.

Naruto, using [shadow clones](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_clone "Shadow clone"), does indeed help defeat the Zetsu Army, finding those that are disguised, defeating those that aren't, and helping capture any remaining reincarnated forces. The apparent victory is cut short by the sudden appearance of the reincarnated [Madara Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Madara_Uchiha "Madara Uchiha"), who proceeds to decimate the [Fourth Division](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Division "Fourth Division"). Wishing to join the battle, Tsunade has [Mabui](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mabui "Mabui") [transfer](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Heavenly_Transfer_Technique "Heavenly Transfer Technique") her to the site of Madara's battle with the Fourth Division, using [Creation Rebirth](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Creation_Rebirth "Creation Rebirth") to heal the damage that the transfer does to her body. The Raikage joins her and they arrive in time to save the Fifth Kazekage and [Third Tsuchikage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Tsuchikage "Third Tsuchikage") from Madara's attack. Tsunade heals them and, after being joined by the [Fifth Mizukage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fifth_Mizukage "Fifth Mizukage"), the [Five Kage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Five_Kage "Five Kage") face off with Madara. Confident they can defeat him, they tell one of Naruto's shadow clones to entrust Madara to them and to go defeat Tobi elsewhere.

![Tsunade attacking Madara.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/25/Tsunade_overwhelms_Madara.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20131003122949)

Tsunade attacking Madara.

Tsunade initially takes a supplementary role during the battle, healing the other Kage's injuries and replenishing their chakra as needed. She is surprised when Madara is able to use her grandfather's [wood release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_release "Wood release"), which they eventually discover to be due to a cloned graft of Hashirama's face that has been implanted in Madara's body. Madara senses that Tsunade is descended from Hashirama and decides to kill her first because she is so much weaker than her ancestor. She acknowledges that she did not inherit all of Hashirama's natural gifts, but she does have his [Will of Fire](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Will_of_Fire "Will of Fire"), which drove her to create the [Ninja Art Creation Rebirth — Strength of a Hundred Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ninja_Art_Creation_Rebirth_%E2%80%94_Strength_of_a_Hundred_Technique "Ninja Art Creation Rebirth — Strength of a Hundred Technique"). After activating the technique, she joins the other Kage in attacking him. Madara is unimpressed, but Tsunade is nevertheless able to punch through his body, giving the Kazekage a chance to try and [seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Desert_Layered_Imperial_Funeral "Desert Layered Imperial Funeral") him.

![The Kage against Madara's wood clones.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/3/3f/Gokage_vs_25_Susanoo.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20131121122857)

The Kage against Madara's wood clones.

Madara eludes capture and uses [Susanoo](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Susanoo "Susanoo") 's sword to stab Tsunade. Madara assumes she's dead, but Tsunade removes the sword from her body and attacks him with it, convincing him that maybe she did indeed inherit something from Hashirama. He in fact admits that all the Kage have posed him some challenge, but claims that it's because they outnumber him five-to-one; in order to make things "fair", he creates twenty-five [wood clones](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_clone "Wood clone"), five for each of the Kage to fight. The wood clones each use Susanoo and fight the Kage into the night. Despite the superior forces against them, the Kage are able to combine forces against the original Madara, impressing him enough to use Susanoo's "Complete Body" form. Before he can use it to destroy the Kage, the Impure World Reincarnation is released and Madara's soul starts returning to the [Pure Land](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Pure_Land "Pure Land").

![Tsunade sees Dan again.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/8b/Dan_%26_Tsunade.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20161117095004)

Tsunade sees Dan again.

Madara uses the remaining moments before his soul departs trying to finish off the Kage. Tsunade takes responsibility for blocking his attacks, expecting that her Creation Rebirth will heal the damage, but she runs out of chakra and is left at Madara's mercy. She is saved by [Dan Katō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Dan_Kat%C5%8D "Dan Katō") 's [Spirit Transformation Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_Transformation_Technique "Spirit Transformation Technique"), his soul having also been released from the Impure World Reincarnation. Tsunade uses the little time that they have apologising to him for abandoning his dreams for so many years after he died, but that she now tries to keep his dreams alive in her capacity as Hokage. He thanks her for continuing his dream, but tells her that the greatest service she can do to him is to stay alive, as she herself is his dream. He kisses her forehead before vanishing and transfers all of his chakra to her. Although Tsunade is rejuvenated, Madara has used this time removing the Impure World Reincarnation's user's control over him, thus allowing him to stay.

![Tsunade summons Katsuyu in her injured state.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/c/c3/Tsunade_summons_Katsuyu.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140109202449)

Tsunade summons Katsuyu in her injured state.

Now free to do what he wants, Madara decides he's lost interest in the Kage and states his intentions to go after Naruto. The Kage try to stop him and thus fulfil their promise to Naruto, but Madara defeats them all and leaves them near death. Tsunade, despite being bisected in the battle, is able to summon Katsuyu. She asks Katsuyu to bring the other Kage closer to her so that she can try and save their lives. Tsunade is later found by Orochimaru, [Suigetsu Hōzuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Suigetsu_H%C5%8Dzuki "Suigetsu Hōzuki"), and [Karin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Karin "Karin"). Tsunade has used almost all of her chakra on the other Kage and now lacks the energy to even speak. After assuring Katsuyu that he wants to help, Orochimaru has Karin [replenish](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Heal_Bite "Heal Bite") Tsunade's chakra and has Suigetsu assist Katsuyu with putting Tsunade's halves back together. Tsunade reluctantly thanks Orochimaru once she's restored and notes a change in him, a change that might have saved Jiraiya had it happened sooner. Orochimaru departs, leaving Tsunade to finish healing the Kage.

### Birth of the Ten-Tails' Jinchūriki

Main article: [Birth of the Ten-Tails' Jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Birth_of_the_Ten-Tails%27_Jinch%C5%ABriki "Birth of the Ten-Tails' Jinchūriki")

![Sakura and Tsunade work together.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/e/e5/Tsunade%26Sakura_Byakugo_no_In.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20250902135231)

Sakura and Tsunade work together.

Once all the Kage are healed, the Kazekage [transports](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Desert_Suspension "Desert Suspension") them to where the Allied Forces are fighting; along the way, Katsuyu informs them of what's been happening up until now. As they near the battlefield, a reincarnated Hashirama Senju [contacts](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_Body_Transmission_Technique "Mind Body Transmission Technique") all the Allied powers. After briefly saying hello to Tsunade, Hashirama reveals the purpose of the [God Tree](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/God_Tree "God Tree") recreation now looming over them: to cast the [Infinite Tsukuyomi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Infinite_Tsukuyomi "Infinite Tsukuyomi"). Much of the infantry is thrown into disarray from fear of what will happen, but when the Kage finally arrive they rally their forces against the God Tree. Tsunade locates Sakura, congratulates her for the work she's done with a pat on her head, and teams up with her to summon a large segment of Katsuyu, which dissolves across the battlefield so that Tsunade and Sakura can constantly rejuvenate everybody as they fight.

Although they aren't able to cut down the God Tree, the Allies are able to help Naruto defeat Tobi, real name [Obito Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Obito_Uchiha "Obito Uchiha"). Naruto leaves to fight Madara, leaving the rest of the Allied Forces to fight [another Zetsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tobi_\(Zetsu\) "Tobi (Zetsu)"). For this reason, when the Kazekage brings Naruto to her in need of emergency life support, Tsunade sends Sakura to help him.

### Kaguya Ōtsutsuki Strikes

Main article: [Kaguya Ōtsutsuki Strikes](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kaguya_%C5%8Ctsutsuki_Strikes "Kaguya Ōtsutsuki Strikes")

![Tsunade trapped in the Infinite Tsukuyomi.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/9/9a/Tsunade_Caught.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150827115618)

Tsunade trapped in the Infinite Tsukuyomi.

Despite the Allied Forces' efforts, they are unable to stop Madara from casting the Infinite Tsukuyomi, trapping the world in a dream. Tsunade dreams of a Konoha where Dan is Hokage, where Nawaki and Jiraiya are still alive, and where Orochimaru never defected from the village. In the anime, while waiting to join Dan for a Kage Summit, she reads a novel written by Jiraiya titled *[Jiraiya Shinobi Handbook: The Tale of Naruto the Hero](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jiraiya_Shinobi_Handbook:_The_Tale_of_Naruto_the_Hero "Jiraiya Shinobi Handbook: The Tale of Naruto the Hero")*, despite her telling him she had no interest in it earlier. In the novel, Konoha went through much less upheaval; the Fourth Hokage and his wife survived the [Nine-Tails' Attack on Konoha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tailed_Demon_Fox%27s_Attack "Nine-Tailed Demon Fox's Attack"), and the [Uchiha Clan Downfall](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uchiha_Clan_Downfall "Uchiha Clan Downfall") never happened, though Tsunade still meets Naruto and Sakura. Naruto and Sasuke eventually release the Infinite Tsukuyomi, freeing Tsunade and the rest of the world and thus ending the Fourth Shinobi World War. Since both lost their arms, Tsunade creates prosthesis arms for them once she returns to Konoha.

### Kakashi Hiden: Lightning in the Icy Sky

Main article: [Kakashi Hiden: Lightning in the Icy Sky](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi_Hiden:_Lightning_in_the_Icy_Sky "Kakashi Hiden: Lightning in the Icy Sky") Kakashi is appointed the Sixth Hokage after the Fourth Shinobi World War ends. Yet a year after the war is over, he still has yet to officially accept the duties of the office. Tsunade continues to carry out all the responsibilities until he's ready, but also pressures Kakashi to make a decision. Kakashi requests that she wait until he completes his mission to guard the [Tobishachimaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tobishachimaru "Tobishachimaru") on its top secret maiden voyage. The Tobishachimaru ends up being hijacked by the [Ryūha Armament Alliance](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ry%C5%ABha_Armament_Alliance "Ryūha Armament Alliance") during its voyage, and the hijackers threaten to kill the passengers if Konoha doesn't release their leader, [Garyō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gary%C5%8D "Garyō"), from the [Blood Prison](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Blood_Prison "Blood Prison") in [Kusagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kusagakure "Kusagakure"); they also stipulate that Naruto not be involved. After being informed that Kakashi is aboard the Tobishachimaru, Tsunade summons Konoha's available personnel, with the exception of Naruto.

While trying to plan what to do, Tsunade is contacted by the [Third Tsuchikage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Tsuchikage "Third Tsuchikage"), who informs her that the "secret" Tobishachimaru is visible from several countries, including his own [Iwagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Iwagakure "Iwagakure"). Although she assures him it is bound for Kusa, he warns her that he will need to destroy it if it threatens his village, which would complicate the relationship between Konoha and Iwa. Tsunade shares this threat with one of the hijackers, [Rahyō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rahy%C5%8D "Rahyō"), but it does not deter him. Having few options left, Tsunade decides that the ship must be destroyed before Iwa is forced to intervene. She has [Ino Yamanaka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ino_Yamanaka "Ino Yamanaka") [tell](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_Body_Transmission_Technique "Mind Body Transmission Technique") Kakashi to do what he can but also warn him of her decision. She leads the assembled available Konoha ninja to the Blood Prison to be on hand in case the worst should happen.

Kakashi ultimately crashes the Tobishachimaru just outside the Blood Prison, thus averting an incident with Iwa. However, several passengers died during the hijacking and the entire incident has been an embarrassment to Konoha. Tsunade is outraged by these developments and orders [Kahyō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kahy%C5%8D "Kahyō"), the highest-ranking hijacker to survive, taken into custody. Kakashi intervenes, asking that he be allowed to pass sentence on Kahyō instead. Tsunade allows it on the condition that Kakashi make it his first act as Sixth Hokage, which he agrees to.

After the war, Tsunade studies the material that the White Zetsu are made of, and from her research is able to create prosthetic arms for Naruto and Sasuke, though Sasuke declines his.

### Mitsuki's Disappearance Arc

Main article: [Mitsuki's Disappearance Arc](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mitsuki%27s_Disappearance_Arc "Mitsuki's Disappearance Arc")

![Tsunade angry with Naruto for hiding Mitsuki's lineage.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/e/ee/Tsunade_angry_at_Naruto.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20210714191723)

Tsunade angry with Naruto for hiding Mitsuki's lineage.

In the anime, when two chūnin [gate](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/U%C5%8D "Uō") [guards](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sa%C5%8D "Saō") were attacked by unknown assailants, the village was put on lockdown. Tsunade soon after returned from her trip to meet with Naruto about the events. She then joined him when Ino probed a guard's mind for answers, learning that [Mitsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mitsuki "Mitsuki") had left willingly with the attackers. When Tsunade asked who this boy is, Naruto revealed that Mitsuki was in fact Orochimaru's son. Tsunade was furious that Naruto would permit an offspring of the man who killed the Third Hokage live in Konohagakure, let alone keep it a secret from her. Naruto rationalised this on the grounds that Orochimaru's conduct had changed from before and that his aid in investigating the Ōtsutsuki was invaluable. Despite this, noting the unpredictable nature of Orochimaru, the meeting ended with deeming the Oto-born boy as a threat. As the council and Hokage continued to talk about how best to deal with this, Tsunade concluded that it was a waste of time to discuss the merits of Naruto's decision to allow Mitsuki into the village, and they should focus instead on how to deal with the current situation.

As Tsunade continued to keep word about Mitsuki quiet and the council from taking drastic actions, questions continued to build up on Mitsuki as Naruto and Shikamaru still felt the situation didn't make sense for Mitsuki to betray them. They began wondering if the enemy's goal was Mitsuki himself because of his unique background. They received word that one of the guards was recovering and would soon awake to give them answers. When Shikadai contacted his father via phone, he revealed that Iwagakure was taken over as part of a plan by Ōnoki. Knowing that they couldn't act hastily, Kakashi and Tsunade were sent to meet with the other great villages to get their support on engaging this personal matter.

Tsunade talks with a time-traveling Boruto and Sasuke.

## Trivia

| ![Springtime of Youth](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/3/39/Youth_symbol.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/40?cb=20130120020002)  Springtime of Youth | The [*Springtime of Youth* wiki](https://springtimeofyouth.fandom.com/wiki/ "w:c:springtimeofyouth") has an article about this topic:  **[Tsunade](https://springtimeofyouth.fandom.com/wiki/Tsunade "w:c:springtimeofyouth:Tsunade")** |
| --- | --- |

- Tsunade and the other Sannin are named after the characters in the Japanese folktale *[Jiraiya Goketsu Monogatari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jiraiya_Goketsu_Monogatari "wikipedia:Jiraiya Goketsu Monogatari")*. Tsunade of that story excels in slug magic.
	- "Tsunade" (綱手) means " [mooring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mooring_\(watercraft\) "wikipedia:Mooring (watercraft)") rope", which is not dissimilar to her brother's name, Nawaki, which means "rope tree".
- Tsunade is first mentioned by [Tenten](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tenten "Tenten") in [chapter 84](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chapter_84 "Chapter 84") of the *Naruto* manga, which was 55 chapters before her first formal appearance.
- Studio Pierrot's Settei sheets of Tsunade show that she was 145 cm when she was part of Hiruzen's genin team, and that her high-heeled sandals she wears as an adult add 5 cm to her height.
- According to the databook(s):
	- Tsunade's hobby is [gambling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gambling "wikipedia:Gambling").
		- Tsunade wishes to fight Naruto Uzumaki.
		- Tsunade's favourite foods are chicken breast and sake, while her least favourite food is liver [sashimi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sashimi "wikipedia:Sashimi").
		- Tsunade has completed 1,256 official missions in total: 40 D-rank, 236 C-rank, 467 B-rank, 418 A-rank, 95 S-rank.
		- Tsunade's favourite phrase is *"One grab, a thousand gold"* (一攫千金, *Ikkaku Senkin*), referring to getting rich without much effort.
- Tsunade shares her blood type with her former team-mates, [Jiraiya](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jiraiya "Jiraiya") and [Orochimaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Orochimaru "Orochimaru").
- While Tsunade's English anime voice actor is Debi Mae West, in some games, she is voiced by Mary Elizabeth McGlynn, who is [Kurenai Yūhi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kurenai_Y%C5%ABhi "Kurenai Yūhi") 's voice actor.
	- In *[Naruto: Clash of Ninja Revolution 2](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto:_Clash_of_Ninja_Revolution_2 "Naruto: Clash of Ninja Revolution 2")*, McGlynn voiced Tsunade throughout the storyline while West voiced Tsunade during the actual gameplay.
- Tsunade is the only Hokage to live long enough to see two generations of successors take office.
- Tsunade is the first Kage to be older than their predecessor, the second being Shikamaru Nara.
- Tsunade is the only member of Team Hiruzen to not have been killed by one of her students.
