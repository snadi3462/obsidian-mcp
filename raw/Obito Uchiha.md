This article is about the character who used the alias Tobi. For the Zetsu named Tobi, see [Tobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tobi_\(Zetsu\) "Tobi (Zetsu)"). For other uses, see [Obito Uchiha (disambiguation)](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Obito_Uchiha_\(disambiguation\) "Obito Uchiha (disambiguation)").

**Obito Uchiha** (うちはオビト, *Uchiha Obito*) was a member of [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure") 's [Uchiha clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uchiha_clan "Uchiha clan"). He was believed to have died during the [Third Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Shinobi_World_War "Third Shinobi World War"), his only surviving legacy being the [Sharingan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sharingan "Sharingan") he gave to his teammate, [Kakashi Hatake](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi_Hatake "Kakashi Hatake"). In truth, Obito was saved from death and trained by [Madara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Madara "Madara"), but the events of the war left Obito disillusioned with reality, and he inherited Madara's [plan to create an ideal world](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eye_of_the_Moon_Plan "Eye of the Moon Plan"). Resurfacing under the names of **[Tobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tobi_\(Zetsu\) "Tobi (Zetsu)")** (トビ, *Tobi*) and Madara Uchiha himself, Obito subtly took control of the [Akatsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki "Akatsuki"), using them as a means to advance his machinations, eventually going public and starting the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War"). However, towards the war's conclusion, Obito had a change of heart and, as atonement, sacrificed his life to save the same world he sought to replace.

## Background

### Early life

![Obito arrives late to his Academy entrance ceremony.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/5/5c/Obito%27s_year_group.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150616161326)

Obito arrives late to his Academy entrance ceremony.

Obito grew up not knowing who his parents were; in the anime, he was left in the care of his [grandmother](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Obito%27s_Grandmother "Obito's Grandmother").[^7] Feeling alone in the world, Obito dreamed of becoming [Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage "Hokage") so that the people of the village would acknowledge his existence.[^8] He enrolled in the [Academy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Academy "Academy") to help him achieve that goal, where he developed a one-sided rivalry with Kakashi, from jealousy over Kakashi's natural talent and popularity. He also became a close friend of [Rin Nohara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rin_Nohara "Rin Nohara"), whom he eventually fell in love with. After finally graduating some years later, Obito, Rin, and Kakashi were placed on a [team](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Minato "Team Minato") under the leadership of [Minato Namikaze](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Minato_Namikaze "Minato Namikaze").[^9]

| --- | --- |

In the anime, as a final qualifying test, Minato gave the team a [bell test](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Bell_test "Bell test") to test their cooperation skills. Obito could not accomplish this on his own, but by joining forces with Rin and Kakashi, they succeeded in taking the bells, teaching Obito the value of teamwork.[^10] The team later participated in the [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams "Chūnin Exams"), where Obito was defeated in the third round in a one-on-one match with [Might Guy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Might_Guy "Might Guy"). Kakashi would go on to defeat Guy in a subsequent match, promoting him to [chūnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin "Chūnin") and impressing Rin. Eager for Rin's attention, Obito trained relentlessly, eventually rising to the rank of chūnin himself. His excitement was short-lived as Kakashi soon afterwards became a [jōnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/J%C5%8Dnin "Jōnin"), once again earning Rin's praise and Obito's resentment.[^9]

During the [Third Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Shinobi_World_War "Third Shinobi World War"), Kakashi was placed in charge of the team for a mission to destroy the [Kannabi Bridge](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kannabi_Bridge "Kannabi Bridge"), which would hinder [Iwagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Iwagakure "Iwagakure") from using [Kusagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kusagakure "Kusagakure") as a relief point. Before beginning the mission, Minato and Rin gave gifts to Kakashi to celebrate his promotion to jōnin, though Obito had "forgotten", straining their already poor relationship. Minato was soon called to the front lines, leaving the team to complete the mission alone under Kakashi's command. The three were discovered by Iwa-nin along the way and Rin was captured. Kakashi elected to abandon Rin, believing it was more important to finish the mission before concerning themselves with her safety. Obito became enraged at the idea and insisted that they focus on her rescue. When Kakashi refused, Obito left on his own, remarking that Kakashi was worse than trash for abandoning his friends.[^11]

Obito located the cave the Iwa-nin were using as a hideout, but was found by a [camouflaged](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hiding_with_Camouflage_Technique "Hiding with Camouflage Technique") [Taiseki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Taiseki "Taiseki") before he could launch a rescue. Kakashi, moved by Obito's earlier words, arrived in time to save him from Taiseki's attack, but lost his left eye in the process. From his desire to help Kakashi, Obito awakened his [Sharingan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sharingan "Sharingan"), allowing him to see through Taiseki's camouflage and kill him. Obito and Kakashi infiltrated the cave and released Rin from her restraints. Her captor, [Kakkō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakk%C5%8D "Kakkō"), [caused the cave to collapse](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Earth_Release:_Rock_Lodging_Destruction "Earth Release: Rock Lodging Destruction") around them. As the team ran for the exit, Kakashi was struck in his blind spot and fell. When Obito noticed that Kakashi was about to be hit by a falling boulder, Obito pushed him out of the way and became trapped in his place.

![Obito trapped beneath a boulder.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/06/Obito_trapped.PNG/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150825143454)

Obito trapped beneath a boulder.

With the right side of his body crushed and no way to free himself, Obito accepted his fate and made an offering to give Kakashi his left Sharingan as an apology for not getting him a present earlier.[^12] Rin performed the transplant and, once the procedure was finished, Kakashi used his new Sharingan to kill Kakkō. Iwa reinforcements quickly began to [further compress the rubble](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Earth_Release:_Tearing_Earth_Turning_Palm "Earth Release: Tearing Earth Turning Palm"), forcing Kakashi and Rin to leave Obito behind. As the rocks tightened around him, Obito reflected that he had finally started to get along with Kakashi and that he couldn't confess to Rin that he loved her. Kakashi and Rin were rescued by Minato and, when they returned to Konoha, Obito's name was engraved on the village's [Memorial Stone](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Memorial_Stone "Memorial Stone").[^13]

### Saved from Death

![Obito meets Madara.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/a/a7/Madara_saves_Obito.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20250729153607)

Obito meets Madara.

In actuality, Obito was rescued by [White Zetsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/White_Zetsu "White Zetsu") under orders from an elderly Madara. He brought Obito to [Mountains' Graveyard](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mountains%27_Graveyard "Mountains' Graveyard") and tended to his injuries, removing those body parts too damaged to be healed and replacing them with limbs cultivated from the cells of [Hashirama Senju](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hashirama_Senju "Hashirama Senju"). Despite his injuries, Obito's right Sharingan had survived intact. Although frightened by Madara, Obito felt indebted to him for saving his life and was willing to render any assistance he could, an offer Madara made clear he would collect upon. Obito began a long rehabilitation process, eager to recover enough for him to return to Konoha and help his friends and the village with the still-ongoing war. With the help of [White Zetsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/White_Zetsu "White Zetsu") and another [spiral-faced Zetsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tobi_\(Zetsu\) "Tobi (Zetsu)") he nicknamed Guruguru, Obito became accustomed to his replacement limbs and the abilities they granted him.[^14] All the while, Madara would tell Obito about the harsh realities of the world and his plan to save it, which the young Uchiha disregarded.

![Obito mourning Rin's death.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/f/f8/Obito_mourns_Rin%27s_death.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230430043436)

Obito mourning Rin's death.

During the end of his recuperation process, White Zetsu informed Obito that Kakashi and Rin were elsewhere about to be killed by [Kirigakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kirigakure "Kirigakure") ninja. Obito was insistent on helping them, which Guruguru offered to help with by encasing Obito with its body. Before leaving, Obito thanked Madara for all his help but said he wouldn't be returning. Madara made clear his conviction that Obito would return to him. Guruguru directed Obito to Rin and Kakashi's location, along the way informing him of Minato's absence. When they arrived they found Rin and Kakashi surrounded by Kiri-nin and Kakashi plunging his [Chidori](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chidori "Chidori") through Rin's heart.[^15] Rin's death caused each of their Sharingan to mature into [Mangekyō Sharingan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mangeky%C5%8D_Sharingan "Mangekyō Sharingan"), a process that also caused Kakashi to pass out. Enraged by what had happened, Obito used a combination of his Mangekyō Sharingan's [Kamui](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kamui "Kamui") and the [Wood Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Release "Wood Release") of Guruguru's body to slaughter the Kirigakure ninja. When all of them were dead, Obito cradled Rin's lifeless body, ignoring the unconscious Kakashi.[^16]

Obito returned to Mountains' Graveyard, vowing to do anything for Madara if it could bring him together with Rin and Kakashi again. Madara explained his Eye of the Moon Plan, which would replace the contemporary world of violence and death with one where nobody ever needs to die. Obito was intrigued, determined to create a reality where he, Rin, and Kakashi could exist alongside each other. Madara imparted all of his knowledge and plans to Obito, taught him about abilities he would need moving forward, entrusted him with his possessions, and manifested [Black Zetsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Zetsu "Black Zetsu") to act as a guide. Having left almost all that he had to Obito, Madara disconnected himself from the [Demonic Statue of the Outer Path](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Statue_of_the_Outer_Path "Demonic Statue of the Outer Path") that was keeping him alive and told Obito that until his revival, he was to act as Madara Uchiha.[^16]

### Moving the Plan Forward

![Obito and Zetsu approach Nagato.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/4/4e/Obito_and_zetsu.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140123132646)

Obito and Zetsu approach Nagato.

Using Madara's name and concealing his identity, Obito moved in the shadows of the ninja world to acquire the remaining pieces of the Eye of the Moon Plan. Shortly after Madara's death, Obito and Zetsu went to [Amegakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Amegakure "Amegakure") and approached the fledgling Akatsuki with an offer of support in creating the world of peace they envisioned.[^17] In truth he only needed [Nagato](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nagato "Nagato"), in whom Madara had implanted his [Rinnegan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rinnegan "Rinnegan") several years earlier and who would be needed in the final stages of the Eye of the Moon Plan. While Obito was almost able to sway Nagato, the Akatsuki leader, [Yahiko](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yahiko "Yahiko"), declined; Obito claims he eventually agreed without informing Akatsuki's other members.[^18] In the anime, Obito learned of a conspiracy between [Hanzō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hanz%C5%8D "Hanzō") and [Danzō Shimura](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Danz%C5%8D_Shimura "Danzō Shimura") to eliminate Yahiko. He intercepted and killed the members of Akatsuki that tried to rescue Yahiko and, once Yahiko was dead, encouraged Nagato in a new direction for the organisation, one focused on acquiring the [tailed beasts](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_beasts "Tailed beasts").[^19] While Nagato became the Akatsuki leader and recruited powerful [missing-nin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Missing-nin "Missing-nin") for their cause, Obito took on the alias of "Tobi", and changed his personality around members to conceal his identity.

![Obito confronting Yagura.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/9/93/Obito_Confronts_Yagura.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160818112833)

Obito confronting Yagura.

In Kirigakure, Obito at some point [took control](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genjutsu:_Sharingan "Genjutsu: Sharingan") of the Fourth Mizukage, [Yagura Karatachi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yagura_Karatachi "Yagura Karatachi") (in the anime being accompanied by Pain and Konan) in effect making him the de facto Mizukage. After [Kisame Hoshigaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kisame_Hoshigaki "Kisame Hoshigaki") became disillusioned by the lies of the world, Obito (as "Madara") revealed himself to Kisame and promised to help make a world of truth. Kisame became his loyal servant, one of the few Kiri-nin to knowingly work for him.[^20] During this time Obito discovered the circumstances of Rin's death: that Kiri had sealed the [Three-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Three-Tails "Three-Tails") into her to make her a timebomb that would destroy Konoha. At Rin's insistence, Kakashi killed her to prevent this from happening.[^21] Obito's manipulation of the Mizukage was eventually discovered by [Ao](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ao "Ao") and he was forced to abandon it.

Twelve years before the start of the series, Obito visited Rin's grave in Konoha. Kakashi was already there when he arrived and Obito, watching secretly, heard him confide to Rin's grave that Minato's wife, [Kushina Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kushina_Uzumaki "Kushina Uzumaki"), would soon be giving birth. Knowing that Kushina was the [Nine-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tails "Nine-Tails") ' [jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki "Jinchūriki") and that the seal keeping the Nine-Tails contained within her would weaken during childbirth, Obito tracked her down on the night of October 10. He killed her [Anbu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Anbu "Anbu") bodyguards and midwives, which included the [Third Hokage's](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hiruzen_Sarutobi "Hiruzen Sarutobi") [wife](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Biwako_Sarutobi "Biwako Sarutobi"), and took her newborn son, [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki"), hostage to prevent Minato from interfering. Minato was able to take Naruto from him, which distracted him long enough for Obito to escape with Kushina.[^22] He extracted the Nine-Tails from her body, placed it under his control with his Sharingan, and ordered it to [destroy the village](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tailed_Demon_Fox%27s_Attack "Nine-Tailed Demon Fox's Attack").[^23]

![Tobi and Minato clash.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/e/e5/Minato_vs_Tobi.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20120210084331)

Tobi and Minato clash.

Minato soon afterwards arrived to help in the village's defence. Before Minato could contribute much or even tell anyone what had happened, Obito located him and tried to use Kamui to send him away and prevent further interference. Minato was able to escape with his [Flying Thunder God Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Flying_Thunder_God_Technique "Flying Thunder God Technique"), but Obito pursued him. Minato did not recognise Obito as they fought, instead suspecting he was Madara Uchiha. He initially struggled to successfully strike Obito but, after several failed attacks, Minato finally hit him with a [Rasengan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rasengan "Rasengan") and branded him with a Flying Thunder God seal, allowing him to teleport to Obito whenever he wanted. He then used a [Contract Seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Contract_Seal "Contract Seal") on Obito to release the Nine-Tails from his control. Wounded and deprived of his best weapon, Obito fled.[^24] Minato gave his life to save the village by sealing the Nine-Tails into his son, and thus never had the chance to inform anyone of Obito's involvement. Konoha's leadership nevertheless suspected an Uchiha's involvement, and to that end placed all members of the clan under heavy scrutiny.

![Obito and Itachi plotting to massacre their clan.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/85/Tobi_talks_to_Itachi.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140418015240)

Obito and Itachi plotting to massacre their clan.

Years after his attack, Obito attacked the [Fire Daimyō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fire_Daimy%C5%8D "Fire Daimyō") 's convoy en route to Konoha, placing most of the procession in a [genjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genjutsu "Genjutsu") and killing [Tenma Izumo](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tenma_Izumo "Tenma Izumo"), but swiftly retreats after sensing Kakashi approaching.[^25] [^26] Years later, the Uchiha, as a result of their mistreatment, began plotting a coup d'état. Obito returned to the village with the intention of exacerbating the conflict, but was discovered by [Itachi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Itachi "Itachi"). Believing Obito was Madara, Itachi asked for his help in [wiping out](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uchiha_Clan_Downfall "Uchiha Clan Downfall") their clansmen, offering revenge against them for their treatment of Madara decades earlier in exchange for Obito's agreement to spare the village.[^27] Obito accepted and offered Itachi a position in Akatsuki. In the anime, during the night of the massacre, he slaughtered the [Konoha Military Police Force](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Military_Police_Force "Konoha Military Police Force") and killed [Izumi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Izumi "Izumi"). Afterwards, he collected several Uchiha corpses in order to extract their Sharingan for his own use.[^28] He also met Danzō around this time, for unknown reasons.[^29] Following the attack, he cut his hair and brought Itachi into his organisation.

## Personality

![Obito aiding an elderly villager.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/6/6f/Obito_helps_old_woman.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20131219123523)

Obito aiding an elderly villager.

Because he grew up without knowing his parents, Obito's childhood was marked by dreams of being acknowledged. This motivated him to become a ninja so that he could, in turn, become Hokage and have the entire village finally recognise him. When Rin acknowledged him regardless, Obito fell in love with her and aimed to earn her love in addition to the Hokage title. But Kakashi's presence, both in the classroom and later on their team, became a wall for Obito, because of Kakashi's prodigy and that he had, without trying, gained Rin's affections. Though generally kind and of unwavering loyalty to his friends and allies, Obito nevertheless formed a one-sided rivalry with Kakashi. When not in competition with Kakashi, Obito would famously perform random good deeds around Konoha, especially for the elderly, which in turn made him notoriously late.[^30] This combination of factors and Obito's naturally just heart and optimism made him easy for Madara to exploit and ultimately corrupt.[^31]

![Obito falls victim to the Curse of Hatred.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/24/I%27m_in_Hell.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140116130913)

Obito falls victim to the Curse of Hatred.

After Rin's death, Obito's determination and idealism were [shattered](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Curse_of_Hatred "Curse of Hatred"). He became more calm and focused, no longer caring about his village, his friends, or even his name, all of them being worthless parts of a miserable world that had forced Rin to die at the hands of a person she loved.[^21] His sole commitment was to Madara's Eye of the Moon Plan and the new peaceful world it would create; every crime was a necessary evil, every murder was a sacrifice for the greater good and a life that would be restored in the new world. He was willing to sacrifice himself for the plan and, more often, his allies, turning on them the instant they no longer had any further use to him. Like Madara before him, Obito would gain these allies by preying on the darkness in their hearts and manipulating them, either by subtly corrupting their own goals or by appearing to share their beliefs. This was best seen in the existence of Akatsuki, its wildly-differing members working together because they believed the organisation would further their own ends; in actuality, they were mere tools that Obito used to forward his own agenda. He even treated Madara, his saviour, mentor and inspirator, with similar disregard, intending to leave the elder Uchiha as dead while taking over the role of mastermind for the Eye of the Moon Plan for himself, despite his former gratitude of being saved by him.

![Obito recalling his original dream.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/84/Obito_as_Hokage.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20141030115656)

Obito recalling his original dream.

Despite how different his adult personality seemed, it was at its basest level very much the same as the person he was as a child. Obito viewed his actions as a station beyond Hokage; where the Hokage does what is best for the village, Obito felt he did what was best for the world.[^32] Though Rin remained ever-present in his thoughts, Kakashi and Minato were also present in his dreams of a new world, and it was the hope of reuniting their team under happier circumstances that motivated him.[^33] Kakashi theorised that it was this conflict between his past and present selves that drove Obito the most, especially in the events leading up to and following the outbreak of the Fourth Shinobi World War; he became increasingly hasty with his plans, first moving Akatsuki from a secretive organisation to a force that directly challenged the [Five Great Shinobi Countries](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Five_Great_Shinobi_Countries "Five Great Shinobi Countries"), and later by prematurely reviving the [Ten-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ten-Tails "Ten-Tails").[^34]

![Obito showing Kakashi just how empty the world has left him.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/f/fa/Kazaana.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140807122431)

Obito showing Kakashi just how empty the world has left him.

As Obito grappled with his thoughts, he at different times took a special interest in [Sasuke](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke "Sasuke") and Naruto. With Sasuke, Obito began monitoring him after their first meeting, though he avoided further contact in honour of the agreement he made with Itachi. After Itachi's death, he approached Sasuke and brought him into his confidence, later remarking that the loss of five Akatsuki members was worth it to gain Sasuke's loyalty. Although Obito stated a number of times that he saw Sasuke as nothing more than a disposable pawn, he nevertheless kept an eye on him, intervening whenever Sasuke was at risk and always happy with signs of his ever-improving Sharingan and his growing isolation from all other allies.[^35] In a way, he saw in Sasuke the kind of person he was trying to be. With Naruto, however, he saw the person he once was: dreams of being Hokage and dedication to his friends.[^36] Because of these similarities, Obito became fixated on Naruto, hoping to demonstrate the naivety of his beliefs and the terribleness of the world so that he would come around to Obito's point of view. When Naruto refused, Obito became increasingly angry and determined to eliminate Naruto and his dreams so that he can bury the last remains of the old Obito.

![Obito professing his resolve to die for Naruto.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/2c/Obito%27s_resolve.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230323045615)

Obito professing his resolve to die for Naruto.

Through his inability to overpower Naruto's will, Obito came to understand that Naruto's way of thinking was right all along. Moreover, he realised the reason for his conflicting desires: he had been lying to himself about his dedication to the Eye of the Moon Plan to hide from his personal failings. The persona that projected him as someone without feelings or a heart was a mask Obito wore to escape the painful truths of his regrets. When Naruto and his allies defeated Obito, the lie was destroyed, causing his original personality to emerge. Obito felt guilty about his actions and declared himself unworthy of seeing Rin in the afterlife. He spent the remainder of his life opposing the Eye of the Moon Plan and Madara, wishing to keep the world as it was. Believing Naruto was vital to the world's preservation, Obito ultimately gave his life to protect Naruto and his team. His new conviction even allowed him to briefly return from death not only to assure Naruto's victory, but also to make amends with Kakashi one final time before fully departing to the afterlife.

In order to further conceal himself and his actions as Madara from the public eye, Obito adopted the name, personality and mannerisms of the aforementioned [Zetsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tobi_\(Zetsu\) "Tobi (Zetsu)") he befriended during his recovery; Which he played up the role of a happy-go-lucky idiot who annoyed most members of Akatsuki. [Deidara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Deidara "Deidara"), in particular, was easily provoked by his vanity and lack of respect, often retaliating against him with comical violence. Kisame, on the other hand, appreciated Tobi's ability to brighten up a gloomy organisation such as theirs.[^37] The anime showed that Tobi appeared to have a liking for [dango](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dango "wikipedia:Dango"),[^38] and continued to act like a fool even when nobody else was around.[^39] After first meeting Sasuke, Obito abandoned the Tobi persona – except for during a brief encounter with Konoha ninja. As "Tobi", Obito would deactivate his Sharingan to prevent suspicion from arousing whenever he didn't need it.[^40] Even without the Tobi persona, Obito had developed a morbid sense of humour, sometimes making jokes, if perverse ones.

## Abilities

![Obito clashing with Kakashi in hand-to-hand combat.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/8c/Obito_vs_Kakashi.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20141026172318)

Obito clashing with Kakashi in hand-to-hand combat.

As a [genin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genin "Genin"), Obito's abilities were mediocre at worst and average at best, struggling to match his classmates from the Academy. Determined to improve himself, he trained for several months at a time to eventually become a chūnin. Madara saw great potential in Obito and thus selected him as his disciple.[^47] Madara's hopes were proven correct, as under his tutelage, Obito dramatically improved his abilities as a late bloomer, with his skills leading to others questioning if he was Madara.[^48] As a teenager Obito posed a threat to the [Five Great Shinobi Countries](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Five_Great_Shinobi_Countries "Five Great Shinobi Countries"), having taken control of the [Fourth Mizukage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Mizukage "Fourth Mizukage") and effectively [Kirigakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kirigakure "Kirigakure"), as well as posing a challenge to the Fourth Hokage and nearly destroying Konoha. After fighting Obito and recalling Jiraiya's warning, Minato was convinced Obito would be the one to bring catastrophe to the world, and that Naruto would need the power of the Nine-Tails to stop him.[^49] As an adult, he defeated [Root](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Root "Root") 's [most](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Torune_Aburame "Torune Aburame") [skilled](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/F%C5%AB_Yamanaka "Fū Yamanaka") ninja, and simultaneously held his own against [Konoha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Might_Guy "Might Guy") 's [top](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi_Hatake "Kakashi Hatake") [shinobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki") and [Killer B](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Killer_B "Killer B"). As the [Ten-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ten-Tails "Ten-Tails") [Jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki "Jinchūriki"), Obito's power exceeded that of even [Hashirama Senju](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hashirama_Senju "Hashirama Senju"), he was able to effortlessly fight [three](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tobirama_Senju "Tobirama Senju") [powerful](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hiruzen_Sarutobi "Hiruzen Sarutobi") [Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Minato_Namikaze "Minato Namikaze") with ease, overwhelm both Naruto and Sasuke several times, and had the capability to [destroy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Ball "Tailed Beast Ball") the entire [Allied Shinobi Forces](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Allied_Shinobi_Forces "Allied Shinobi Forces").

### Chakra and Physical Prowess

Obito's chakra reserves were enhanced by the White Zetsu matter equipped to his body, enabling him to perform several chakra-taxing techniques without noticeable exhaustion.[^50] [^51] He showed the ability to share and [transfer his chakra](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_Transfer_Technique "Chakra Transfer Technique") to another person.[^52] [^53] Obito was proficient in taijutsu as he fought on par with Kakashi on several occasions.[^54] [^55] His physical strength and speed were great enough to lift Konan by her throat with one hand while gravely injured,[^56] impale someone by running his arm through their chest,[^26] stop [Suigetsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Suigetsu "Suigetsu") 's swing with [Kubikiribōchō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kubikirib%C5%8Dch%C5%8D "Kubikiribōchō") by using a single arm,[^57] and keep up with Naruto's [Nine-Tails Chakra Mode](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tails_Chakra_Mode "Nine-Tails Chakra Mode").[^58]

#### Body Modifications

![Obito's body modifications.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/6/6f/Obito_Hashirama%27s_cells.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150221063904)

Obito's body modifications.

The right side of Obito's body was badly damaged during the mission in Kusagakure, and he even lost his right arm. To heal his body and replace the limb, Madara outfitted Obito with the same substance that the [White Zetsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/White_Zetsu_Army "White Zetsu Army") are made of,[^59] enhancing his chakra reserves and eventually allowing him to perform Wood Release. He also gained certain physical augmentations of Hashirama Senju's DNA, such as great durability to attacks, rapid [healing](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Regeneration_Ability "Regeneration Ability"),[^60] considerable boosts in stamina and chakra reserves, and the ability to survive without food or water. His artificial limb was very strong, able to withstand an attack from [Kubikiribōchō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kubikirib%C5%8Dch%C5%8D "Kubikiribōchō") and punch through boulders.[^14] If this or another limb was damaged beyond repair, they were easily removed and replaced with the same material.[^61]

### Ninjutsu

Obito learned from Madara how to perform a variety of different types of ninjutsu: he could create [barrier ninjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Barrier_ninjutsu "Barrier ninjutsu"), using the [Uchiha Flame Formation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uchiha_Flame_Formation "Uchiha Flame Formation") to protect the Demonic Statue;[^62] he could [sense chakra](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sensing_Technique "Sensing Technique"), allowing him to predict attacks and locate others from great distances, even in different dimensions [^63] [^64] and he could perform [fūinjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/F%C5%ABinjutsu "Fūinjutsu") powerful enough to [seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Six_Paths_Ten-Tails_Coffin_Seal "Six Paths Ten-Tails Coffin Seal") the Ten-Tails.[^46]

#### Nature Transformation

![Obito using Fire Release: Blast Wave Wild Dance.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/5/5d/Blast_Wave_Wild_Dance.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150107135939)

Obito using Fire Release: Blast Wave Wild Dance.

Obito could utilise all five basic [nature transformations](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nature_transformation "Nature transformation") along with [Yin–Yang Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yin%E2%80%93Yang_Release "Yin–Yang Release").[^3] As an Uchiha, he had a natural aptitude for [Fire Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fire_Release "Fire Release") techniques, and could perform the [Great Fireball Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Fireball_Technique "Great Fireball Technique") at an early age.[^65] The size and strength of his flames increased in his adulthood, and through a combination with his Mangekyō Sharingan he could perform [Fire Release: Blast Wave Wild Dance](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fire_Release:_Blast_Wave_Wild_Dance "Fire Release: Blast Wave Wild Dance").[^66] He was also seen performing [Earth Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Earth_Release "Earth Release") techniques on occasion, though he exclusively did so in order to [burrow and move underground](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Earth_Release:_Hiding_Like_a_Mole_Technique "Earth Release: Hiding Like a Mole Technique"), letting him augment the battlefield from below or sneak up on opponents.[^67]

With the [White Zetsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/White_Zetsu_Army "White Zetsu Army") matter used as his replacement limbs, Obito could perform [Wood Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Release "Wood Release"), a combination of earth and [water](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water "Water") -based chakra. Instinctively, he was able to form branches and roots to bind,[^68] [crush](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sea_of_Trees_Erosion "Sea of Trees Erosion"),[^69] or [skewer](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Release:_Cutting_Technique "Wood Release: Cutting Technique") opponents. Because the White Zetsu material was partly derived from the DNA of [Hashirama Senju](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hashirama_Senju "Hashirama Senju"), Obito could control the Ten-Tails when he was connected to its body.[^70]

#### Bukijutsu

![Obito ejects multiple shuriken from Kamui's dimension.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/7/7b/Tobi_using_shuriken.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20131219122549)

Obito ejects multiple shuriken from Kamui's dimension.

Like many Uchiha, Obito was trained in [shurikenjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shurikenjutsu "Shurikenjutsu"), allowing him to throw shuriken with precision for offence and defence. When Madara died, a number of weapons were left to Obito which he used at different times over the years. Several of these weapons he kept stored within [Kamui's dimension](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kamui%27s_dimension "Kamui's dimension") so that he could access them anywhere and fire them at opponents; he was seen using [giant shuriken](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Giant_shuriken "Giant shuriken") and [chakra receivers](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_receiver "Chakra receiver") – fashioned into giant restraining stakes – for the latter purpose.[^71] [^72] When he fought Minato during the attack on Konoha, he used a [long chain](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kusari "Kusari") attached to braces on his wrists that, when used in conjunction with his intangibility, enabled him to harmlessly pass through his opponent before solidifying to restrain him with the trailing chain.[^73] Around the time of the Uchiha Massacre, Obito carried a [sword](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sword "Sword") at his waist.[^27] During the Fourth Shinobi World War he wielded Madara's iconic gunbai, using it both as a shield and as a sort of flail for attack.[^74] [^75] The gunbai can generate [wind](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wind "Wind") to [reflect attacks](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uchiha_Return "Uchiha Return").[^76]

### Dōjutsu

#### Sharingan

![Obito's Sharingan.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/d/d0/Obito%27s_Sharingan.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140115221737)

Obito's Sharingan.

When Obito first awakened his Sharingan, it already had two [tomoe](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tomoe "Tomoe"), which at that level he could use to track chakra signatures and predict movements.[^77] He acquired a third tomoe after witnessing Rin's death.[^78] Obito kept his Sharingan active seemingly all the time without any noticeable drain on his [chakra](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra "Chakra") levels. With his Sharingan he could perform [various genjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genjutsu:_Sharingan "Genjutsu: Sharingan") on targets he had eye contact with, either to create illusions,[^21] acquire information,[^79] or control their actions.[^20] Although he was seen controlling six [tailed beasts](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_beasts "Tailed beasts") with his Sharingan, his control with multiple targets was not as complete as it is with one and it is possible for some to break free from his control.[^80]

Obito had a number of Sharingan in his possession which he kept in [storage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Obito%27s_Laboratory "Obito's Laboratory") should he need them. He used one of these Sharingan to replace the one he gave to Kakashi, although he kept it covered with his mask most of the time. He later sacrifices this spare eye's vision in order to perform [Izanagi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Izanagi "Izanagi"), which allows the user to manipulate events around them. Izanagi ordinarily lasts for only the briefest of moments, but with his access to Hashirama's DNA he could maintain it for ten minutes using a single eye.[^81]

#### Mangekyō Sharingan

![Obito's Mangekyō Sharingan.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/4/49/Obito%27s_Mangekyo.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20251018185252)

Obito's Mangekyō Sharingan.

Obito awakened his Mangekyō Sharingan after witnessing Rin's death; the Sharingan he gave to Kakashi awakened its Mangekyō at the same time.[^78] Their Mangekyō's design was three stretched triangles evenly spaced around the pupil that each curve at the top around the eye to form a circle, making it similar to a pinwheel. Obito did not appear to suffer any adverse effects from using his Mangekyō Sharingan, such as the deteriorating eyesight or exhaustion experienced by others.

![Obito using the short-ranged Kamui.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/e/e2/Space_time_migration.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150125202556)

Obito using the short-ranged Kamui.

Obito's Mangekyō Sharingan allowed him to perform [Kamui](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kamui "Kamui"), a [space–time ninjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Space%E2%80%93time_ninjutsu "Space–time ninjutsu") with greater versatility than [Minato's](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Flying_Thunder_God_Technique "Flying Thunder God Technique").[^82] Kamui served as a gateway to another [dimensional space](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kamui%27s_Dimension "Kamui's Dimension"), which he can move all or parts of his body between at will. This granted Obito two distinct abilities: teleportation and what is best described as "intangibility". For teleportation, he absorbed himself through his right eye into the special dimension, and from there he could travel anywhere in the world instantly. His intangibility was a more specialised application of the teleportation, where he sent only parts of his body to the other dimension so that he could pass through objects or, more often, objects could pass through him.[^83] He could only make himself continuously intangible for about five minutes at a time before needing to rest.[^84]

![Obito using his intangibility.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/f/f2/Obito_vs_mist.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20141026171345)

Obito using his intangibility.

Kamui became Obito's signature fighting style to the point that he rarely resorted to more traditional forms of ninjutsu. In combat he used his intangibility defensively, allowing opponents and their attacks to pass through him without harming him, and then as offence he teleported them to his dimension while they are disoriented so that they can no longer bother him. The chakra signatures of those he sent to the other dimension cannot be sensed from outside of it.[^85] Despite how useful Kamui is, it isn't without its weaknesses. Because the two aspects of the technique were connected he couldn't use both at the same time; if he wanted to teleport he couldn't be intangible, leaving him open to attack, and if he wanted to be intangible he couldn't teleport, preventing him from escaping so long as he remained on the defensive.[^84]

![Obito using the long-ranged Kamui.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/8f/Obito%27s_Kamui.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160814174001)

Obito using the long-ranged Kamui.

Because Kakashi's Mangekyō Sharingan could also use Kamui, his eye remained the only reliable counter to Obito's abilities. Although Kakashi cannot use Kamui against Obito directly,[^86] he could use his own access to the other dimension to negate the advantages Obito made use of: by teleporting attacks or allies to the other dimension at the same moment that Obito's body resided there for defence, he could be attacked successfully.[^87] He regained the Sharingan he gave to Kakashi during the Fourth Shinobi World War, allowing him to use the eye to perform rapid, precise long-distance teleportation instead of the physical contact required with his other eye's teleportation. With effort, he could use his left Mangekyō to access other [dimensional planes](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kaguya%27s_Dimensions "Kaguya's Dimensions").[^88]

#### Rinnegan

![Obito's Rinnegan.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/d/df/Obito_Rinnegan.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20220217122122)

Obito's Rinnegan.

Obito took the [Rinnegan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rinnegan "Rinnegan") from Nagato's body after he died and implanted it in his left eye socket.[^89] Although he used only one eye and was not its original owner, Obito nevertheless gained a great deal of power from it. While he admitted that he could barely handle a single eye, almost losing himself,[^90] Obito was capable of performing all of the [Six Paths Techniques](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Six_Paths_Technique "Six Paths Technique") with the Rinnegan, though he was only ever seen performing abilities of the [Outer Path](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Outer_Path "Outer Path"): he could [summon](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning_Technique "Summoning Technique") the [Demonic Statue of the Outer Path](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Statue_of_the_Outer_Path "Demonic Statue of the Outer Path"), create [chakra chains](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Statue_Chains "Demonic Statue Chains") to restrain targets,[^91] use the [Samsara of Heavenly Life Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Samsara_of_Heavenly_Life_Technique "Samsara of Heavenly Life Technique") to revive the dead in exchange for his life, as well as using the [Preta Path](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Preta_Path "Preta Path") to [absorb chakra](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_Absorption_Techniques "Chakra Absorption Techniques") from others.[^92]

![Obito with his Six Paths of Pain.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/5/5c/Reincarnated_Jinch%C5%ABriki.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150826053626)

Obito with his Six Paths of Pain.

While in possession of the Rinnegan, Obito used it to create his version of the [Six Paths of Pain](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Six_Paths_of_Pain "Six Paths of Pain"), composed of the six [reincarnated](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning:_Impure_World_Reincarnation "Summoning: Impure World Reincarnation") jinchūriki with their respective tailed beasts sealed back into their bodies. He controlled them each with a single chakra receiver embedded into their bodies. Each jinchūriki had the same eye-pattern as him – a right Sharingan (which granted them its predictive abilities) and a left Rinnegan – allowing him to share their vision and coordinate their movements. The effort required to not only maintain this coordination but to keep the tailed beasts under his control prevents Obito from manifesting any of the Six Paths Techniques in his Six Paths of Pain.[^93]

### Intelligence

From Madara, Obito learned much about the world, individual people in it, and different abilities from throughout history. As such, it was difficult to surprise him in combat because he already knew tactics ninja from certain villages are likely to use, what weaknesses to take advantage of in different opponents, and how to counter most jutsu.[^94] On those infrequent occasions when he encountered something new, he calmly kept his distance and observed carefully until he devised the best approach to neutralising it. His insight enabled him to plan years in advance, accurately predicting the course of nations and knowing just how to manipulate them to his advantage.[^95] Likewise, he proved to be a master of deception and impersonation, able to throw off all suspicion on him with his goofy persona as "Tobi", or effectively masquerading as Madara himself, even perfectly imitating the legendary ninja's voice and speech patterns to sway people into following him. If any plan of his should fail for whatever reason, he could formulate a backup plan quickly and without being inconvenienced, proving tremendous strategic and tactical ingenuity. Indeed, Obito often kept multiple back-up plans, rarely pinning his hopes on a single outcome. Obito also demonstrated high abilities in manipulation, able to see the darkness in others and twist them to his own purposes with a clever mixture of truth and lies. As Tsunade noted, by taking on the identity of the infamous Madara Uchiha, Obito was able to command the influence that came with it, such as the entire world taking his simple declaration of the Fourth Great Shinobi War seriously.

Obito's Ten-Tails jinchūriki transformation.

Obito [sealed](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Six_Paths_Ten-Tails_Coffin_Seal "Six Paths Ten-Tails Coffin Seal") the Ten-Tails into his body during the Fourth Shinobi World War. Upon becoming its jinchūriki, all of the previous damage to his body was healed and he became stronger, faster,[^33] and nearly impervious to damage,[^96] displaying overwhelming strength.[^97] He initially lacked control of his mind after the sealing, causing him to literally swell with the Ten-Tails' chakra and react relentlessly to all threats.[^46] In this state, Obito had still became stronger than the incomplete Ten-Tails because he was able to focus its immeasurable power, leaving so-called [God of Shinobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/God_of_Shinobi "God of Shinobi") unable to compete with him.[^98] After pulling himself together, he became stronger still, able to use his abilities with further refinement. As the Ten-Tails' jinchūriki, Obito could fly and create [chakra arms](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Chakra_Arms "Tailed Beast Chakra Arms").[^99] [^100] He could produce trees through which he can fire [Tailed Beast Balls](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Ball "Tailed Beast Ball"), and later create and control the Ten-Tails' tree form in preparation for performing the [Infinite Tsukuyomi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Infinite_Tsukuyomi "Infinite Tsukuyomi").[^101] [^102] He was unable to use Kamui's intangibility while a jinchūriki, however.[^103]

#### Truth-Seeking Ball

As the Ten-Tails' jinchūriki, Obito was able to use [Truth-Seeking Balls](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Truth-Seeking_Ball "Truth-Seeking Ball"), ten black orbs comprised of all five elemental nature transformations,[^96] and Yin–Yang Release. The balls were his primary weapons and generally floated behind him in a halo-like formation. The black chakra was highly malleable, able to be shaped into various forms for different uses, such as high speed projectiles, protective barriers, explosives, and various weapons, including a [shakujō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shakuj%C5%8D "Shakujō") reminiscent of the [Sage of the Six Paths](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sage_of_the_Six_Paths "Sage of the Six Paths") '.[^33] He could even recreate the Sage's [Sword of Nunoboko](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sword_of_Nunoboko "Sword of Nunoboko"), with which Obito claims he could lay waste to the world.[^104] With his complete control over the Ten-Tails' power, Obito could apply Yin–Yang Release to nullify all ninjutsu the balls come in contact with, but can’t nullify the effects of [senjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Senjutsu "Senjutsu"), making it the only viable method to combat him.[^105] Unique to Obito, he was able to increase the size of his Truth-Seeking Ball by passing them through holes in his hand. After the Ten-Tails' extraction, Obito was able to manifest a single Truth-Seeking Ball after [absorbing](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_Absorption_Techniques "Chakra Absorption Techniques") some of Madara's [power](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Six_Paths_Chakra "Six Paths Chakra").[^106] [^107]

In the anime, Deidara treated Tobi to some dango before the mission, a ploy to get him to remove his mask. He turned away while he ate, preventing Deidara from seeing anything. Deidara retorted that it was actually his clay that defeated the Three-Tails. Tobi, unconvinced, fell asleep during Deidara's argument. Deidara "woke him up" by detonating a clay bomb next to him.

When they got the Three-Tails to an Akatsuki base, the Akatsuki members convened and sealed it. When they were done, they also sealed the [Two-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Two-Tails "Two-Tails") (the Two-Tails is sealed before the Three-Tails' capture in the anime).

Upset by yet more setbacks, Tobi assigns Kisame to capture B and tracks down Sasuke and Taka on their way to attack Konoha. While they are speaking, Zetsu reports that [Danzō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Danz%C5%8D "Danzō") is to be the next [Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage "Hokage") and that he will be attending the [Kage Summit](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kage_Summit "Kage Summit") in the [Land of Iron](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_Iron "Land of Iron").

As this could only mean that Kabuto has failed, Tobi realises that time is running out. With no other option he tosses the Benihisago and the Kohaku no Jōhei into the Demonic Statue's mouth which, in addition to the Eight-Tails' tentacle from the fake Killer B, completes the set of the nine tailed beasts, initiating the [Ten-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ten-Tails "Ten-Tails") ' revival.

Obito informs him of Nagato's betrayal before returning to him his [gunbai](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gunbai "Gunbai") and directing him to Naruto and B. While Madara goes after them, Obito fights Kakashi. He ignores Kakashi's pleas for an explanation and nearly kills him, stopped only by Naruto. When Kakashi gets over his initial shock and starts to fight seriously, Obito sends him away with Kamui. ## Legacy

Following Obito's apparent death, Kakashi was left greatly changed by the experience. The Sharingan that Obito gave to Kakashi helped him gain fame throughout the ninja world and earned him the nickname of "Copy Ninja Kakashi" (コピー忍者のカカシ, *Kopī Ninja no Kakashi*) for over a thousand [jutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jutsu "Jutsu") he copied with the Sharingan. Obito's philosophy about the importance of team-mates also guided Kakashi during all his future missions and, once Kakashi was old enough to begin training his own students, became the single most important lesson he wanted them to learn and demonstrate. On a more personal level, Obito's death remained ever in his thoughts and he would often spend his time visiting the [Memorial Stone](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Memorial_Stone "Memorial Stone") where Obito's name is engraved to reflect on events. In the anime, while the [Uchiha clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uchiha_clan "Uchiha clan") wanted to retrieve Obito's Sharingan from Kakashi, the head [Fugaku](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fugaku "Fugaku") allowed Kakashi to keep it in honour of Obito's wishes.

The [Nine-Tailed Demon Fox's Attack](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tailed_Demon_Fox%27s_Attack "Nine-Tailed Demon Fox's Attack") that Obito was responsible for would end up directing the course of Naruto and Sasuke's lives. Naruto's parents died that night, leaving him an orphan. Like Obito before him, Naruto came to dream of gaining Konoha's acknowledgment by becoming Hokage and pursued that goal against all odds. The suspicion that an Uchiha was behind the attack would ultimately lead to the clan's [downfall](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uchiha_Clan_Downfall "Uchiha Clan Downfall"). Sasuke, the only Uchiha to be spared from death, dedicated his life to taking vengeance against those behind the death of his family at any cost. Despite the dark path he took in his life, Obito's actions eventually led the [Five Great Shinobi Countries](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Five_Great_Shinobi_Countries "Five Great Shinobi Countries") aligning with one another to form the [Allied Shinobi Forces](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Allied_Shinobi_Forces "Allied Shinobi Forces"), and later the [Shinobi Union](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shinobi_Union "Shinobi Union"), ultimately fulfilling his promise of ending all wars and bringing peace to the world to Rin.

- Obito makes several cameos before his actual appearance in the series: He is seen in a picture of [Team Minato](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Minato "Team Minato") on the cover of [chapter 16](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Who_are_You%3F! "Who are You?!"); The [Third Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Hokage "Third Hokage") thinks of him in [chapter 122](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/The_Inherited_Will!! - "Obito" (首) is an archaic Japanese hereditary title for a commander from a noble family.
- "Tobi" can be translated as the ["i form"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_verb_conjugation#i_form "wikipedia:Japanese verb conjugation") of "to fly" (飛ぶ, *tobu*), or as " [kite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kite_\(bird\) "wikipedia:Kite (bird)") " (鳶). The latter translation connects Tobi with [Taka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Taka "Taka") through the Japanese proverb "a hawk born from a kite" (鳶が鷹を生む, *tobi ga taka o umu*), meaning that an extraordinarily gifted child can be born from ordinary parents.
	- In [Naruto Mobile](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Mobile "Naruto Mobile"), as Tobi, Obito could glide like a kite by unfurling his Akatsuki cloak, using it to rain down explosives.[^108]
- Obito's orange mask appears in the ending [omake](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Omake#Festival "Omake") of [*Naruto: Shippūden* episode 129](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Infiltrate!_The_Village_Hidden_in_the_Rain "Infiltrate! The Village Hidden in the Rain") along with [Baki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Baki "Baki") 's face and [Haku](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Haku "Haku") 's mask.
- According to the databook(s):
	- Obito's favourite words were "teamwork" (チームワーク, *chīmuwāku*) and "comrade" (仲間, *nakama*).
		- Obito had completed 135 official missions in total: 86 D-rank, 24 C-rank, 24 B-rank, 1 A-rank, 0 S-rank.
		- As " *Tobi* ", his hobby was accompanying "Deidara- [senpai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senpai_and_k%C5%8Dhai "wikipedia:Senpai and kōhai") ", and his favourite phrase was given as "secret (?)" (秘密 (?), *himitsu (?)*).
- In video games, such as *[Ultimate Ninja Impact](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ultimate_Ninja_Impact "Ultimate Ninja Impact")* and *[Ultimate Ninja Storm 2](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ultimate_Ninja_Storm_2 "Ultimate Ninja Storm 2")*, Obito can use [Fire Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fire_Release "Fire Release") techniques even though he wears a mask; the same happens with Kakashi.
- Despite Obito being a user of the Six Paths Senjutsu, he is not listed as a senjutsu user nor a user of Yang Release in the fourth databook.[^3]
- According to the third databook, Obito is said to be 175 cm as Tobi. However, when revealed to be Obito, the fourth databook concludes that he is 182 cm. Interestingly enough though, Studio Pierrot says Obito is 180 cm as an adult.
- Studio Pierrot's Settei sheets show that Obito was 125.4 cm when he first entered the Academy.
- Despite Hashirama's blood type being type B, and Obito's blood type being type O, Madara was able to transplant Hashirama's DNA onto Obito despite type B not being able to give blood to type O due to incompatibility. This is the same case with Madara, whose blood type is also type O.
