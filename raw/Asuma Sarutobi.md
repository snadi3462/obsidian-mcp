**Asuma Sarutobi** (猿飛アスマ, *Sarutobi Asuma*) was a [jōnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/J%C5%8Dnin "Jōnin") of [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure") 's [Sarutobi clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sarutobi_clan "Sarutobi clan") and a former member of the [Twelve Guardian Ninja](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Twelve_Guardian_Ninja "Twelve Guardian Ninja"). He was also the leader of [Team 10](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Asuma "Team Asuma") which consists of [Shikamaru Nara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shikamaru_Nara "Shikamaru Nara"), [Ino Yamanaka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ino_Yamanaka "Ino Yamanaka"), and [Chōji Akimichi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%8Dji_Akimichi "Chōji Akimichi").

## Background

![Asuma at his entrance ceremony.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/5/5c/Obito%27s_year_group.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150616161326)

Asuma at his entrance ceremony.

Asuma was born to [Biwako](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Biwako "Biwako") and [Hiruzen Sarutobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hiruzen_Sarutobi "Hiruzen Sarutobi"), the Third [Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage "Hokage"). During his [Academy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Academy "Academy") entrance ceremony, Asuma became friends with [Kurenai Yūhi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kurenai_Y%C5%ABhi "Kurenai Yūhi"). In the anime, during the [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams "Chūnin Exams"), Asuma was placed on a team with Kurenai and [Raidō Namiashi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Raid%C5%8D_Namiashi "Raidō Namiashi").[^5] He later cheered on his classmates during the third round of the Chūnin Exams.[^6] During the [Nine-Tailed Demon Fox's Attack](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tailed_Demon_Fox%27s_Attack "Nine-Tailed Demon Fox's Attack"), Asuma along with the other young ninja were barred from participating in the defence of the village by [Shinku Yūhi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shinku_Y%C5%ABhi "Shinku Yūhi") and the other senior shinobi. Asuma then told Kurenai to calm down.[^7] A few years after the event, Asuma would become an uncle to [Konohamaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohamaru "Konohamaru").

About seven years before the start of the series, he had a disagreement with his father and left Konoha to pursue his personal interests. During his absence, he became one of the Twelve Guardian Ninja, a group that protected the Land of Fire's daimyō, indicated by the sash on his waist.[^8] During this time he developed a strong friendship with the monk [Chiriku](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chiriku "Chiriku"), who would go on to become the head monk of the [Fire Temple](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fire_Temple "Fire Temple"). Prior to returning to Konoha to resume active military duty, he accumulated a bounty of 35 million [ryō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ry%C5%8D "Ryō") on the black market, something he seemed to be particularly proud of since it was a testament of his skill, even bragging that his bounty was 5 million more than Chiriku.[^9] After returning to the village, Asuma resumed his jōnin duties and became a teacher of [Inomatsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Inomatsu "Inomatsu") and presumably some more students. In the anime, about twelve years after the [Nine-Tailed Demon Fox's Attack](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tailed_Demon_Fox%27s_Attack "Nine-Tailed Demon Fox's Attack"), seeing his [classmate](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi_Hatake "Kakashi Hatake") fall into deeper self-pity from losing [Obito Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Obito_Uchiha "Obito Uchiha") and [Rin Nohara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rin_Nohara "Rin Nohara"), Asuma, along with [Might Guy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Might_Guy "Might Guy") and Kurenai decided to speak with the Third Hokage, voicing their disapproval of Kakashi being in Anbu with such bitterness and suggested instead that he be re-assigned as a jōnin sensei in the hopes of restoring Kakashi's kindness.[^10]

## Personality

![A young Asuma playing shōgi with Hiruzen.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/e/e3/Asuma_and_Hiruzen.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20210829005852)

A young Asuma playing shōgi with Hiruzen.

Asuma was typically a laid-back individual, and did not get himself involved in things unless absolutely necessary, as seen when he decided not to help save [Hinata Hyūga](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hinata_Hy%C5%ABga "Hinata Hyūga") during her fight in the Chūnin Exams. He was a heavy smoker, but, despite this, he would abruptly break the habit under certain circumstances, such as when his father died [^11] or learned that Kurenai was pregnant.[^12] [^13] Not always seeing eye to eye with his father, Asuma later came to respect his father's role as [Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage "Hokage"): referring to him as a great father, and was proud to have been born into the Sarutobi clan.[^14]

![Asuma and Shikamaru playing shōgi.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/1/16/Shikamaru_playing_shougi.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140621101053)

Asuma and Shikamaru playing shōgi.

Despite Asuma's very blunt personality, often calling his students what they were unreservedly, a "glutton" (Chōji), a "loud mouth" (Ino), and a "slacker" (Shikamaru), he had a very good relationship with all three. He often took them to [Yakiniku Q](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yakiniku_Q "Yakiniku Q") for lunch and dinner where more often than not Chōji would eat too much and Asuma had to pay more and apologise emphatically to the owner. To help them overcome their underachieving natures, he attempted to use bribery and outright trickery to manipulate them into doing what he wanted. This was seen most with Chōji, who Asuma was frequently able to entice into action with the promise of food and even Shikamaru whom he had to use games like [shōgi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/shogi "wikipedia:shogi") on to assess his skill level. Out of his students, Asuma spent the most time with and was closest to Shikamaru. Asuma and Shikamaru often played shōgi or Go in their free time, though Asuma was never able to beat Shikamaru.[^15] It was Asuma who first discovered that the lazy Shikamaru was actually a genius, with an I.Q. over 200.[^16]

Throughout the series it had been heavily hinted that Asuma was romantically involved with fellow jōnin [Kurenai Yūhi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kurenai_Y%C5%ABhi "Kurenai Yūhi"), having been close to her since childhood. The two appeared together in most of their scenes, and the other characters made it a point to note this fact on several occasions. When [Kakashi Hatake](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi_Hatake "Kakashi Hatake") found the two together at a dumpling shop, Kakashi asked if they were on a date, to which Asuma responded by quickly changing the subject.[^17] In a flashback, Asuma was shown buying flowers for a person he refused to name, though Ino asked that he say hello to Kurenai for her anyway, to which Asuma nervously asked how she knew about it.[^18] During Asuma's battle with [Hidan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hidan "Hidan") and [Kakuzu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakuzu "Kakuzu"), Kurenai was often shown wondering about his progress, appearing anxious. When she heard of his death, she fell to her knees grief stricken. She later laid flowers on his grave during his funeral. The truth of their relationship was then made clear when Kurenai revealed that she was pregnant with Asuma's child.

## Appearance

![Asuma's full appearance.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/89/Asuma_full.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/76?cb=20160124055358)

Asuma's full appearance.

Asuma was a tall man, with brown eyes, olive skin, short black spiky hair, and a beard. His clothing consisted of the standard Konoha ninja uniform with the sleeves rolled up halfway, a darker-green [flak jacket](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Flak_jacket "Flak jacket"), regular shinobi sandals and [forehead protector](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Forehead_protector "Forehead protector"). He also wore a sash that had the kanji for "Fire" (火) marked on it around his waist, a pair of black bangles, and bandages wrapped around the arms of his sleeves.

As a genin, he wore a white short-sleeved shirt with a blue and black collar over chain-mail armour, along with a simple white vest, a pair of black pants, and a white belt.[^6] [^19] In the anime, it was shown that while he was a member of the Twelve Guardian Ninja, he wore a simple, long-sleeved muted green shirt with bandages on the arms, dark gray pants with a tear on the right, that sash denoting he was a guardian and a sleeveless, dark olive-colored jacket with a brown fur collar. He also had his [trench knives](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_Blades "Chakra Blades") with him at that point.[^8]

## Abilities

![Asuma using Welcoming Approach: Thousand-Armed Murder against Hidan.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/c/cf/Welcoming_Approach_Thousand-Armed_Murder.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150816121633)

Asuma using Welcoming Approach: Thousand-Armed Murder against Hidan.

Asuma was a highly talented, well-respected [jōnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/J%C5%8Dnin "Jōnin") of his village, gaining praise from [Shikaku Nara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shikaku_Nara "Shikaku Nara") of being a great shinobi not easy to take down.[^20] He established a great record in military prowess — something many upcoming shinobi greatly admired.[^3] His abilities earned him a position into the Twelve Guardian Ninja — an elite hand-picked team which served to protect the [Land of Fire](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_Fire "Land of Fire") 's [daimyō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fire_Daimy%C5%8D "Fire Daimyō") — a role which later earned him a bounty of 35,000,000 [ryō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ry%C5%8D "Ryō").[^9] [^21] He could effortlessly take down nine [Oto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Oto "Oto") -nin during the [Konoha Crush](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Crush "Konoha Crush"), noted to have been [chūnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin "Chūnin") -level or above,[^22] and was able to compete against [Hidan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hidan "Hidan"), a powerful member of the [Akatsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki "Akatsuki"), while also protecting his team.

### Physical Prowess

![Asuma with his Chakra Blades.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/6/68/Asuma_With_His_Chakra_Blades.PNG/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160216223830)

Asuma with his Chakra Blades.

Asuma was hailed as Konoha's foremost skilled ninja in close-ranged melee combat.[^3] His battle style revolved mainly around [taijutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Taijutsu "Taijutsu"), combined with his special [trench knives](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_Blades "Chakra Blades") to create a melee-type fighting style. These knives are made of a special metal that is capable of easily being [infused](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_Flow "Chakra Flow") with [chakra](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra "Chakra"). He was also physically strong enough to block [Kisame Hoshigaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kisame_Hoshigaki "Kisame Hoshigaki") 's [Samehada](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Samehada "Samehada") using his trench knives.[^23]

### Ninjutsu

With his [Flying Swallow](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Flying_Swallow "Flying Swallow"), he could increase the cutting power of the blades to easily pierce through solid rocks and metal. He could also [extend the blades](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_Blade:_Straight_Line "Chakra Blade: Straight Line") with his chakra to injure an enemy several feet out of the blade's physical range. In the anime, Asuma was taught how to use the ultimate technique of the Fire Temple, the [Welcoming Approach: Thousand-Armed Murder](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Welcoming_Approach:_Thousand-Armed_Murder "Welcoming Approach: Thousand-Armed Murder"). While admitting to not be as skilled with it as [Chiriku](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chiriku "Chiriku"), he was able to effectively manifest the spirit to attack and defend to a certain extent.[^24]

#### Nature Transformation

![Asuma using Fire Release: Ash Pile Burning.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/4/40/Haisekish%C5%8D_1.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20211022053203)

Asuma using Fire Release: Ash Pile Burning.

Asuma possessed an affinity to [Wind Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wind_Release "Wind Release"). He would mainly combine it with his trench knives to increase their lethality. As explained by Asuma to Naruto, due to having an affinity for the nature, the trench knives will gain enough striking power to pierce through rock.[^25] He could also release a [powerful stream of wind](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wind_Release:_Dust_Cloud_Technique "Wind Release: Dust Cloud Technique"), and in the anime, use his wind-based chakra to [levitate](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wind_Release:_Verdant_Mountain_Gale "Wind Release: Verdant Mountain Gale") his trench knives and control them remotely.

Asuma was also able to use [Fire Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fire_Release "Fire Release"). With it, he can use the [Fire Release: Ash Pile Burning](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fire_Release:_Ash_Pile_Burning "Fire Release: Ash Pile Burning") technique to breathe out a thick cloud of gunpowder-infused ash that causes a devastating explosion when ignited, leaving the target with third-degree burns.[^26]

## Part I

### Chūnin Exams

Main article: [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams_\(Arc\) "Chūnin Exams (Arc)") When Chōji, fearing the competition, contemplated forfeiting his match during the preliminaries of the Chūnin Exam, Asuma convinced him not to by offering to take him out for "all you can eat" barbecue (yakiniku or Korean kalbi). Asuma also claimed that he'd stop the match if it got out of hand, as the other jōnin did during [Hinata Hyūga](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hinata_Hy%C5%ABga "Hinata Hyūga") 's match. Both Ino and Shikamaru noted to themselves that Asuma didn't actually help in that instance.

### Konoha Crush

Main article: [Konoha Crush](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Crush_\(Arc\) "Konoha Crush (Arc)")

![Asuma rescues Shikamaru.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/4/45/Asuma_rescues_Shikamaru.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140621101106)

Asuma rescues Shikamaru.

During the Konoha Crush, Shikamaru was left by himself to defend against a group of invading Oto-nin. Later, just as Shikamaru had run out of options, and the nine Oto-nin were about to finish him off, Asuma appeared and single-handedly defeated the group saving his student. After the invasion, he attended his [father](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hiruzen_Sarutobi "Hiruzen Sarutobi") 's funeral.

### Search for Tsunade

Main article: [Search for Tsunade](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Search_for_Tsunade "Search for Tsunade")

![Asuma clashes with Kisame.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/2c/Asuma_vs_Kisame.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240820181804)

Asuma clashes with Kisame.

During his brief battle with [Itachi Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Itachi_Uchiha "Itachi Uchiha") and [Kisame Hoshigaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kisame_Hoshigaki "Kisame Hoshigaki"), Asuma managed to catch Kisame off-guard with his trench knives, leaving a cut on Kisame's cheek below the eye. Witnessing the blade made of wind chakra, Kisame switches to ninjutsu using the [Water Release: Water Shark Bullet Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water_Release:_Water_Shark_Bullet_Technique "Water Release: Water Shark Bullet Technique") with Asuma seemingly still battle stationed with the blades. Kakashi Hatake arrived in time to copy the technique, and used it to block the attack, much to Asuma's surprise. Asuma, Kurenai, and Kakashi then faced off with the two [Akatsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki "Akatsuki") members, only for Kakashi to fall victim to Itachi's [Tsukuyomi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tsukuyomi "Tsukuyomi"). [Might Guy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Might_Guy "Might Guy") arrived just in time to stall the Akatsuki duo and buy time for the rest of his comrades who were about to get targeted by Kisame, leading to him coaching Asuma and Kurenai on the essentials on how to fight against a [Sharingan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sharingan "Sharingan") -using shinobi when the pair fled to pursue [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki"), their true target.

Asuma was later seen at [Tsunade](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tsunade "Tsunade") 's inauguration ceremony as the new [Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage "Hokage"), amongst the villagers.[^27]

### Sasuke Recovery Mission

Main article: [Sasuke Recovery Mission](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Recovery_Mission "Sasuke Recovery Mission") Prior to Chōji's setting out with the squad assembled by Shikamaru to track down the renegade [Sasuke Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Uchiha "Sasuke Uchiha") and the [Sound Four](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sound_Four "Sound Four"), Asuma berated him for eating so much and not training enough. After Chōji's recovery from his battle against Sound ninja [Jirōbō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jir%C5%8Db%C5%8D "Jirōbō"), he quickly regained his lost weight, but also approached Asuma for more intense training, which Asuma agreed to, with some admiration.

### Mizuki Tracking Mission

Main article: [Mizuki Tracking Mission](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mizuki_Tracking_Mission "Mizuki Tracking Mission") In the anime, Asuma and Kurenai were dispatched by [Tsunade](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tsunade "Tsunade") to bring the [chūnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin "Chūnin") criminal [Mizuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mizuki "Mizuki") in for questioning, regarding his suspected involvement with [Orochimaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Orochimaru "Orochimaru"). The pair arrived at the Leaf Village Maximum Security Prison, only to be greeted by a swarm of released inmates, including the [Legendary Stupid Brothers](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Legendary_Stupid_Brothers "Legendary Stupid Brothers"), [Fūjin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/F%C5%ABjin "Fūjin") and [Raijin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Raijin "Raijin"). Outnumbered, the two were beaten unconscious and later found by [Naruto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto "Naruto") who rushed them to the hospital for medical treatment.

### In Naruto's Footsteps: The Friends' Paths

Main article: [In Naruto's Footsteps: The Friends' Paths](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/In_Naruto%27s_Footsteps:_The_Friends%27_Paths "In Naruto's Footsteps: The Friends' Paths") About two years after Naruto left the village with [Jiraiya](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jiraiya "Jiraiya") for training, Konoha decided to host an early Chūnin Exams alongside Sunagakure. Later, as Team Asuma began discussing their joining the exams, it became noted that with Shikamaru already a chūnin and therefore ineligible to join, it left the rest of the team short.[^28] After the first exam concluded — held in Konoha — Asuma and Kurenai acted as the second exam's supervisors, informing the genin teams that weren't part of the first 30 teams to arrive at the checkpoint, that they already lost and couldn't participate in the second round. Later, after the second exam began, he, Kurenai and [Guy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Guy "Guy") received word from Tsunade to be on guard for potential assassins towards Gaara as many still rejected him as Kazekage.[^29]

## Part II

Asuma made his Part II début standing at the Third Hokage's grave, where he commented on how he was finally beginning to understand what the Third had taught him. Asuma confessed that he'd always been detached from Konoha, but after seeing how his father sacrificed himself for the village, he was now proud to be a member of the [Sarutobi clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sarutobi_clan "Sarutobi clan").

Later, when Naruto began training to master wind chakra, he came to Asuma, who was also a wind-type user, for help. Asuma agreed to give him pointers, if he agreed to pay for his team's next meal (which, Naruto forgot, included Chōji).

### Twelve Guardian Ninja

Main article: [Twelve Guardian Ninja](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Twelve_Guardian_Ninja_\(Arc\) "Twelve Guardian Ninja (Arc)") In the anime, when Team Kakashi returned to Konoha from their failed mission, he recognised a young monk named [Sora](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sora "Sora") from the [Fire Temple](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fire_Temple "Fire Temple") who accompanied them back. He invited Sora and the boys of the [Konoha 11](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_11 "Konoha 11") to join him at a restaurant to have a guys day. Some time after, he revealed to Sora that he knew his father and that he killed him for trying to kill the [Daimyō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Daimy%C5%8D "Daimyō"). An enraged Sora tried to attack him, only failing as a few Anbu members got in his way and Sora stormed off.

![Asuma confronting Kazuma.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/5/54/Asuma_vs_Kazuma.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20210829023007)

Asuma confronting Kazuma.

Later, the Konoha was attacked by members of [Furido's 4-Man Team](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Furido%27s_4-Man_Team "Furido's 4-Man Team"), reviving many shinobi. Asuma recognised some of the revived shinobi from the [Twelve Guardian Ninja](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Twelve_Guardian_Ninja "Twelve Guardian Ninja"), who planned on using a [powerful technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Limelight "Limelight") to destroy Konoha. He fought [Kitane](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kitane "Kitane") and ultimately overpowers him. With the three members of Furido's 4-Man Team killed, the leader, [Furido](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Furido "Furido"), remained. Asuma finds out that Fūrido was [Kazuma](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kazuma "Kazuma"), Sora's father, the man Asuma thought he killed. Having unleashed the seal he embedded on Sora around 10 years prior Kazuma informs Asuma and Naruto that Sora is a [Pseudo-Jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Pseudo-Jinch%C5%ABriki "Pseudo-Jinchūriki") of the nine tails. Asuma engages Kazuma in battle, and later with Sai's help, Asuma finished Kazuma off. Ultimately, Naruto is able to stop Sora by releasing the nine tails' chakra sealed inside him.

When the invaders didn't succeed, Sora having came to his senses, departed from Konoha. Afterwards, Asuma was seen playing shōgi with Shikamaru.

### Akatsuki Suppression Mission

Main article: [Akatsuki Suppression Mission](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki_Suppression_Mission "Akatsuki Suppression Mission")

![Asuma's team begin their search.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/9/93/Nij%C5%AB_Sh%C5%8Dtai_Asuma.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20211008012803)

Asuma's team begin their search.

Asuma was teamed up with [Shikamaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shikamaru "Shikamaru"), [Kotetsu Hagane](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kotetsu_Hagane "Kotetsu Hagane"), and [Izumo Kamizuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Izumo_Kamizuki "Izumo Kamizuki") as members of the [Twenty Platoons](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Twenty_Platoons "Twenty Platoons"). His team, along with the entire group, was ordered to search for and, if possible, capture a pair of Akatsuki members who had entered the Land of Fire. Asuma and his team eventually tracked down the Akatsuki members, [Hidan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hidan "Hidan") and [Kakuzu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakuzu "Kakuzu"), at a [Bounty Station](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_Fire_Bounty_Station "Land of Fire Bounty Station"), where they quickly discovered that Hidan was immortal after their first attack. Hidan ordered Kakuzu to stay out of it, wanting to do this on his own. Asuma decided to be the one to physically confront him and took him on in battle while Shikamaru backed him up from a distance with his shadows, keeping Hidan on his toes.

![Asuma decapitates Hidan.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/08/Asuma_beheads_Hidan.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20211024195155)

Asuma decapitates Hidan.

The situation worsened, when Hidan's counter-attack resulted in him becoming 'linked' to Asuma through a [ritual](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Curse_Technique:_Death_Controlling_Possessed_Blood "Curse Technique: Death Controlling Possessed Blood"). Hidan assumed a Grim Reaper-like form, causing any bodily damage to himself to be shared with Asuma, while at the same time not hindering himself at all. With some quick thinking by Shikamaru who managed to break Hidan's ritual and pin him down, Asuma was able to decapitate him with his chakra-extended trench knives. Surprisingly, Hidan's severed head started to talk, uttering expletives at Asuma and then Kakuzu for not helping him.

![Asuma dying amongst his team.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/9/95/Ino_healing_Asuma.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20141009141419)

Asuma dying amongst his team.

Kakuzu finally stepped in as he admonished his partner for letting his guard down, and briefly attacked Asuma so he couldn't stop him from sewing Hidan's head back onto his body, and to prevent him from escaping. With Hidan's body restored, Hidan resumed the ritual, and impaled himself in the heart. Though the blow did not instantly kill Asuma, it was still quite fatal. The rest of Team 10 arrived as back-up, which, combined with a telepathic call from [Pain](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Pain "Pain"), forced the Akatsuki duo to leave. Before dying, Asuma left some parting words with his students, and smoked one last cigarette.[^30]

Later, while Shikamaru exacted revenge upon Hidan for killing Asuma, Asuma appeared before Shikamaru, and congratulated him for finally defeating the immortal. After entrusting his [Will of Fire](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Will_of_Fire "Will of Fire") to Shikamaru, Asuma disappeared in a puff of cigarette smoke just before his student finished off Hidan with a huge explosion.[^31] Also, in his memory from the flashback, Shikamaru was finally able to understand who the "King" of Shōgi was in the real world. It is the next generation of shinobi, like Asuma's and Kurenai's unborn child, and how they would learn to care and live peacefully by the great shinobi in the present day. That was why Asuma entrusted his baby to Shikamaru, as apprentice and future sensei.

### Fourth Shinobi World War: Confrontation

Main article: [Fourth Shinobi World War: Confrontation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War:_Confrontation "Fourth Shinobi World War: Confrontation")

![Asuma reincarnated, alongside Hizashi and Dan.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/e/ec/Konoha_Jounin_Reincarnated.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20210819004324)

Asuma reincarnated, alongside Hizashi and Dan.

In preparation for the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War"), Asuma was [reincarnated](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning:_Impure_World_Reincarnation "Summoning: Impure World Reincarnation") by [Kabuto Yakushi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kabuto_Yakushi "Kabuto Yakushi") to fight against the [Allied Shinobi Forces](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Allied_Shinobi_Forces "Allied Shinobi Forces"). He was later mobilised alongside [Hizashi Hyūga](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hizashi_Hy%C5%ABga "Hizashi Hyūga") and [Dan Katō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Dan_Kat%C5%8D "Dan Katō"). Informed by Dan of their current situation, he stated that he had an ominous feeling of what was to come. Relocating to the [Land of Lightning](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_Lightning "Land of Lightning") 's coast, Asuma emerged from the sea amongst the [White Zetsu Army](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/White_Zetsu_Army "White Zetsu Army") and a few other reincarnated shinobi, before engaging the opposing [First Division](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/First_Division "First Division"). Confronting [Chōza Akimichi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%8Dza_Akimichi "Chōza Akimichi") with Dan initially, he soon separated from them and began fighting other shinobi, engulfing several in an enormous [explosion](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fire_Release:_Ash_Pile_Burning "Fire Release: Ash Pile Burning").

![Asuma attempts to attack Chōji.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/84/F%C5%ABton_F%C5%ABjin_no_Jutsu.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20120809113519)

Asuma attempts to attack Chōji.

Asuma is later confronted by his [former students](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Asuma "Team Asuma") where he praised them on their growth before he engaged them in combat. Moving involuntarily, Asuma attempted to provide what assistance he could by advising them on the best methods to counteract his attacks. Although hesitant, [Shikamaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shikamaru "Shikamaru") and [Ino](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ino "Ino") are able to fight regardless of their reluctance to harm their teacher, but [Chōji](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%8Dji "Chōji") could not bring himself to attack his beloved teacher. This created an opportunity for Asuma to use his [Wind Release: Dust Cloud Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wind_Release:_Dust_Cloud_Technique "Wind Release: Dust Cloud Technique"), however, Ino managed to push Chōji out of the path of the attack. Asuma tried in vain to provoke Chōji, and even went as far as to call him fat, yet ultimately forced his comrades to come to his aid once again.

![Asuma bids farewell to his students, before being sealed.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/1/1f/Asuma_Sarutobi_before_being_sealed_away.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20210818204402)

Asuma bids farewell to his students, before being sealed.

Ino took [control](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_Body_Switch_Technique "Mind Body Switch Technique") of Chōji's body in order to fight Asuma in his place, but when she attempts to press the importance of their actions to Chōji, Asuma exploited her distraction by attacking with his [Fire Release: Ash Pile Burning](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fire_Release:_Ash_Pile_Burning "Fire Release: Ash Pile Burning") technique. Chōza, however, dived in front of the attack, protecting them from harm and finally providing Chōji with the necessary motivation after remembering the oath he swore as the next head of the Akimichi. Observing Chōji's new found confidence and [metamorphosis](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Butterfly_Ch%C5%8Dji_Mode "Butterfly Chōji Mode"), Asuma happily encouraged them to defeat him. Overcome by Ino and Shikamaru's [combined](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_Clone_Switch_Technique "Mind Clone Switch Technique") [efforts](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_Sewing_Technique "Shadow Sewing Technique"), Asuma was unable to avoid Chōji's [attack](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Butterfly_Bullet_Bombing "Butterfly Bullet Bombing") and was sent flying into a nearby cliff, where he is ironically [immobilised](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_Imitation_Shuriken_Technique "Shadow Imitation Shuriken Technique") with his previous [weapons](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_Blades "Chakra Blades"). After complimenting his students' on their perfect [formation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Formation_Ino%E2%80%93Shika%E2%80%93Ch%C5%8D "Formation Ino–Shika–Chō") he thanked them, before being completely [bound](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Cloth_Binding_Technique "Cloth Binding Technique") and [sealed](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sealing_Tag "Sealing Tag") by the [Sealing Team](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sealing_Team "Sealing Team").

## Legacy

![Asuma's gravestone.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/1/1f/Asuma%27s_Grave.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20170614065151)

Asuma's gravestone.

Though somewhat understated, the legacy Asuma left behind was one of great value. Within Konoha, and the wider shinobi world he left a sterling record of military prowess which many young Konoha shinobi look up to, wanting to emulate. He is also forever recognised for this prowess having been selected as one of the twelve people deemed strong enough to protect the [leader](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Daimy%C5%8D "Daimyō") of their country. Though initially also confused about the role of the [Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage "Hokage") and the [Will of Fire](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Will_of_Fire "Will of Fire") which his father told him of, Asuma came to understand all of these in time and also came to appreciate his father, and the role the Hokage played in protecting the villagers. Along with this, Asuma was also responsible for training the current generation of the famed [Ino–Shika–Chō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ino%E2%80%93Shika%E2%80%93Ch%C5%8D "Ino–Shika–Chō") trio who are the future leaders of their respective clans as well as completing the all-important [ceremony](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yamanaka,_Nara,_and_Akimichi_Clan_Memorial "Yamanaka, Nara, and Akimichi Clan Memorial") concerning the three clans and the Sarutobi. He was also integral in assisting [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki") with his [nature transformation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nature_transformation "Nature transformation") training with [Wind Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wind_Release "Wind Release"). Asuma left behind lover Kurenai Yūhi whom he fathered a [child](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mirai "Mirai") with. This child he views as his " *king* " whom he asked his student Shikamaru to protect with his dying breath and years later his daughter would follow the footsteps of him and Asuma's father.

In the anime, Asuma's students continued his tradition of taking their respective [children](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_10_\(Moegi\) "Team 10 (Moegi)") to Yakiniku Q to celebrate accomplishments.[^33]

## Trivia

| ![Springtime of Youth](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/3/39/Youth_symbol.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/40?cb=20130120020002)  Springtime of Youth | The [*Springtime of Youth* wiki](https://springtimeofyouth.fandom.com/wiki/ "w:c:springtimeofyouth") has an article about this topic:  **[Asuma Sarutobi](https://springtimeofyouth.fandom.com/wiki/Asuma_Sarutobi "w:c:springtimeofyouth:Asuma Sarutobi")** |
| --- | --- |

- The name "Asuma" means "tomorrow truth" (明日真), paralleling his daughter Mirai's name which means "future". His family name "Sarutobi" means "monkey jump" (猿飛).
- Studio Pierrot's Settei sheets of Asuma show that he was 126 cm when enrolling in the Academy and 150 cm around the time of the Kannabi Bridge incident.
- Asuma and [his father](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hiruzen_Sarutobi "Hiruzen Sarutobi") both died on the 80th episode of their respective series: Hiruzen having died on the 80th episode of Part I, and Asuma having died on the 80th episode of Part II.
- During a question and answer session held at Tokyo's Jump Fest '09, [Masashi Kishimoto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Masashi_Kishimoto "Masashi Kishimoto") stated that he was thinking of making Kurenai and Asuma's child a girl, which he follows through with.[^34]
- Studio Pierrot has consistently given him the height of 190 cm, instead of 190.8cm as his Databook profiles gives him. In Shippuden Ep 76, [The Next Step](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/The_Next_Step "The Next Step"), he is listed in the collection centre Bingo Book as 6'5" or 195.58cm.
- According to the databook(s):
	- Asuma's hobby was to play shōgi.
		- Asuma wished to duel Shikamaru in a game of shōgi.
		- Asuma's favourite foods were [sausage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sausage "wikipedia:Sausage") and [tororo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dioscorea_opposita "wikipedia:Dioscorea opposita") [soba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soba "wikipedia:Soba"). His least favourite food was asparagus.
		- Asuma had completed 719 official missions in total: 111 D-rank, 193 C-rank, 217 B-rank, 178 A-rank, 20 S-rank.
		- Asuma's favourite phrase was "Not right now!" (今のナシ!, *Ima no nashi!*).
