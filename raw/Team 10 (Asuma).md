This article is about the team led by Asuma Sarutobi. For other uses, see.

**Team Asuma**, also known as **Team 10**, is a generation in the **Ino–Shika–Chō Trio** (いの しか ちょうトリオ, *Ino–Shika–Chō Torio*). As such, the three team members are good friends and [have exceptional team work](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Formation_Ino%E2%80%93Shika%E2%80%93Ch%C5%8D "Formation Ino–Shika–Chō"), although they tend to bicker from time to time. All three members of Team 10 are skilled at stalling opponents and excel at coordinated [jutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jutsu "Jutsu") attacks, with their ability as a four-man squad being raised even higher by the time of Part II with Shikamaru's improved tactics and Ino's newfound [medical ninjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Medical_ninjutsu "Medical ninjutsu") and improved detection skills.[^2] They were led by Asuma until his death in Part II. Kakashi led them temporarily after that to help them avenge Asuma's death. The team is assumed to still be active, although without a permanent leader.

During the time-skip in the anime, for the second [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams "Chūnin Exams"), Sakura (whose other teammates were not in the village) temporarily joined the formation, as a team of three is required. Asuma placed Shikamaru in charge, having already become a [chūnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin "Chūnin").

Along with the other members of the [Konoha 11](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_11 "Konoha 11"), Team 10 had regrettably decided to kill [Sasuke Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Uchiha "Sasuke Uchiha") after he attacked [Kumogakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kumogakure "Kumogakure") on behalf of [Akatsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki "Akatsuki") in order to stop him from sinking any lower as a [missing-nin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Missing-nin "Missing-nin") and risk causing a devastating international war as a result. However, [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki") persuaded them that he will deal with Sasuke alone.

Shikamaru was eventually promoted to jōnin and became Naruto's advisor and right-hand man when he became Hokage, and married [Temari](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Temari "Temari") and had a son named [Shikadai](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shikadai "Shikadai"). Ino married [Sai](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sai "Sai") after taking her father's duties for the [Shinobi Union](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shinobi_Union "Shinobi Union"), and had a son named [Inojin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Inojin "Inojin"). Chōji married [Karui](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Karui "Karui") and had a daughter named [Chōchō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%8Dch%C5%8D "Chōchō").

## Known Missions

[Capture Baji](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_10%27s_Teamwork "Team 10's Teamwork") (Anime Only)

- **Rank:** Unknown
- **Status:** Success

In a joint mission with Team 7, Team 10 was deployed to save the [granddaughter](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Headman%27s_Granddaughter "Headman's Granddaughter") of a [village headman](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Village_headman "Village headman") who was taken hostage by a bandit leader named [Baji](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Baji "Baji"). Allowing himself to be held captive so he can know the location of the hostages, Shikamaru enabled Ino and Chōji to locate his position so they can free the hostages and defeat the bandits.

[Rescue Kosuke](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/The_Legendary_Ino%E2%80%93Shika%E2%80%93Ch%C5%8D "The Legendary Ino–Shika–Chō") (Anime Only)

- **Rank:** Unknown
- **Status:** Success

While they were still genin, Team Asuma was sent to aid [Kosuke Maruboshi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kosuke_Maruboshi "Kosuke Maruboshi") who was being pursued by a group of [Iwa shinobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Three_Brothers "Three Brothers"). Using their teamwork in conjunction with ambush tactics, they managed to defeat the pursuers and save Kosuke.

[Hunting Akatsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki_Suppression_Mission#Akatsuki_vs._Team_10 "Akatsuki Suppression Mission")

- **Rank:** A-rank [^3]
- **Temporary Member:** [Kakashi Hatake](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi_Hatake "Kakashi Hatake") (Jōnin Leader)
- **Status:** Success
![Team Asuma with Kakashi Hatake in charge.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/be/Team_Asuma_with_Kakashi_Hatake_in_charge.PNG/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20110213033628)

Team Asuma with Kakashi Hatake in charge.

When [Asuma](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Asuma "Asuma") died fighting the [Akatsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki "Akatsuki") member [Hidan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hidan "Hidan"), the three swore revenge and stayed together under the name "Team 10", albeit without a [jōnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/J%C5%8Dnin "Jōnin") commander. When they prepared to depart on the day the duo had promised to return, Tsunade, however, objected to them recklessly seeking revenge even with Shikamaru's plan, and forbade them from undertaking the mission, as they do not qualify as the standard four-man cell. However, [Kakashi Hatake](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi_Hatake "Kakashi Hatake") opted to lead the squad on this mission. The team's revenge was consummated with the defeat of Hidan whom Shikamaru trapped within the [Nara Clan Forest](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nara_Clan_Forest "Nara Clan Forest"), and [Kakuzu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakuzu "Kakuzu") 's death, with backup from the rest of [Team Kakashi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Kakashi "Team Kakashi").

## Trivia

- Asuma gave all three of his students a pair of silver knob earrings,[^4] as part of a [tradition](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yamanaka,_Nara,_and_Akimichi_Clan_Memorial "Yamanaka, Nara, and Akimichi Clan Memorial") where a [Sarutobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sarutobi "Sarutobi") gives the members of the [Yamanaka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yamanaka "Yamanaka"), [Nara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nara "Nara") and [Akimichi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akimichi "Akimichi") clans earrings when they are promoted to the rank of chūnin.[^5]
- The name "Ino–Shika–Chō" is a reference to a card combination in the [Hanafuda card game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanafuda "wikipedia:Hanafuda"), *Koi-Koi*; Inoshikachō uses the Boar (猪, *Ino*), the Deer (鹿, *Shika*), and the Butterfly (蝶, *Chō*).
- According to [Sakura Haruno](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sakura_Haruno "Sakura Haruno"), [Team 7](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Kakashi "Team Kakashi") has the best teamwork with Team 10.[^6]
	- In the anime, Sakura became a temporary member of the team in order for her, Chōji, and Ino to take part in their second [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams "Chūnin Exams"), Shikamaru having already been promoted, and Sakura's team-mates not being in the village at the time.
		- When Team 10 was introduced properly during the Chūnin Exams, Naruto commented on them with the less-than-flattering nickname The Idiot (O-baka) trio, referring to each members' most obvious and annoying habit (food for Chōji, complaining for Shikamaru, and obsessing with [Sasuke](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke "Sasuke") for Ino).[^7]
