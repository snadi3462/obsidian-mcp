**Shino Aburame** (油女シノ, *Aburame Shino*) is a [shinobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shinobi "Shinobi") of [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure") 's [Aburame clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Aburame_clan "Aburame clan"). Quiet, and at times off-putting to some, Shino puts his clan's [insect-based](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Parasitic_Destruction_Insect_Technique "Parasitic Destruction Insect Technique") techniques to use on [Team Kurenai](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Kurenai "Team Kurenai"). In later years, Shino became a [jōnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/J%C5%8Dnin "Jōnin") and an [Academy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Academy "Academy") teacher.[^6]

## Background

![Shino with his adoptive brother Torune.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/06/Young_Torune_%26_Shino.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150825043745)

Shino with his adoptive brother Torune.

Shino at one time grew up alongside [Torune Aburame](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Torune_Aburame "Torune Aburame") as his family took him in under guardianship after Torune's father, [Shikuro Aburame](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shikuro_Aburame "Shikuro Aburame"), passed away. Despite their different views about friendship, Shino grew close to Torune, viewing him as an older brother. Torune even revealed to Shino his unique breed of [microscopic insects](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rinkaich%C5%AB "Rinkaichū"). One day, [Danzō Shimura](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Danz%C5%8D_Shimura "Danzō Shimura") approached the two young Aburame with the desire to recruit another young member of a secret technique-using clan for [Root](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Root "Root"). While Danzō had the authority to do so and expressed interest in Shino, not wanting Shino to live a life of loneliness, Torune offered himself, appealing to Danzō by revealing who his father was. Knowing that he would probably never see Torune again but wanting to remain close to his brother-figure in some way, Shino harvested some of Torune's insects and spent years refining his bug breeding technique to produce new beetles that inherited traces of Torune's insects.

After enrolling into the [Academy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Academy "Academy") and being placed in [Iruka Umino](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Iruka_Umino "Iruka Umino") 's class, he met [Kiba Inuzuka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kiba_Inuzuka "Kiba Inuzuka"), whom he numerously became irritated at due to the pair having polar opposite personalities. During breaks in between classes, he would let his bugs walk on his desk.[^7] During a class training session, he watched on alongside Kiba as [Shikamaru Nara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shikamaru_Nara "Shikamaru Nara") and [Chōji Akimichi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%8Dji_Akimichi "Chōji Akimichi") refused to fight each other even during [training](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Traditional_Shinobi_Sparring "Traditional Shinobi Sparring"). When Kiba commented that Shikamaru, who had found the process to be too troublesome, would be a [genin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genin "Genin") for the rest of his life, Shino noted that that may not be so as Shikamaru could go on to live a long life and many things could happen, but before he could finish his statement Kiba cut him off.

## Personality

Since his early childhood, Shino has been a calm, collected, and solitary person, who is quite mysterious. Shino is portrayed as being somewhere between merely stoic and matter-of-fact (becoming even more taciturn in Part II) [^8], rarely ever smiling or showing emotion at all; rather than thank his teammates for congratulating him on his victory in the preliminaries, he told them that he expected them to do the same. He also has a tendency of holding grudges and being rather intimidating, such as seen when [Naruto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto "Naruto") failed to recognise him at the start of Part II (which is fair as the only visible part of his face were his sunglasses), but easily recognised Kiba and [Hinata](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hinata "Hinata") regardless. He still holds that grudge to this day. Shino seems to be aware of these odd personality traits; in the anime, when he ate food poisoned with a chemical that forced him to laugh, he menacingly told Naruto to forget it had ever happened. In his adulthood, however, Shino seems to have outgrown these traits, openly smiling and laughing in front of others.

![Shino's dream of discovering new insect breeds.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/1/1e/Shino%27s_Infinite_Tsukuyomi_Dream.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160304060951)

Shino's dream of discovering new insect breeds.

His interest in insects only helps him strengthen this viewpoint; by spending most of his spare time watching them, and often making analogies to insects when talking, people have come to identify Shino as "creepy", and tend to dislike being in his company for prolonged periods of time. In his [Infinite Tsukuyomi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Infinite_Tsukuyomi "Infinite Tsukuyomi") dream, Shino dreamt that he found a new giant-sized species of bugs and tamed it as his own. Despite occasionally arguing with Kiba due to their personality differences, Shino has a very strong sense of connection with his teammates and allies. This was first seen when he promised [Sasuke Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Uchiha "Sasuke Uchiha") to help him fight Gaara after dealing with [Kankurō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kankur%C5%8D "Kankurō"), but regretted not being able to uphold that promise after being poisoned.

Shino is also very brave as he and his teammates were terrified after seeing Gaara's powers during the second stage of the Chūnin Exams, but he still resolved to help Sasuke in any way he could with Gaara. He also regretted being unavailable and therefore unable to help with retrieving Sasuke. From that point onwards, he resolved to work on his teamwork skills with Kiba and Hinata. He even helped Hinata to improve her abilities, and is one of the few people, besides Naruto, who openly professes confidence in her. The fruits of his efforts are seen in the anime, where Shino was able to carry out a mission successfully while also keeping others safe. He also proves to be able to understand his teammates' inner workings, sensing when the slightest of things is wrong with either of them. During the Fourth Shinobi World War, he also went out of his way to keep his teammate's chakra flow active through the use of his insects.

Shino speaks in a curious fashion, as noted by both Naruto and Kiba. He speaks in a manner most simply described as "nitpicking" and uses the word 'because' (なぜなら, *nazenara*) a great deal. To others, his manner of speaking seems like over concern with the trivial points of a conversation which could be left unsaid, or, providing information that will leave questions that could possibly be asked — already answered.[^9] [^10]

Shino is fairly confident in his abilities and that he will win a battle, and tends to try to communicate this to an opponent for their own well-being. Still, Shino enjoys fighting, and dislikes being denied a chance to battle a skilled opponent, as seen when he went out of his way during the Konoha Crush to fight Kankurō, after the latter had forfeited to avoid revealing his puppets' secrets.

In the anime, it was shown that Shino will panic when confronted with insectivorous animals or plants (with Shino himself likening it to psychological torture).[^11] He will also fall into despair showing saddened expressions — atypical of him — to the point where he almost cries and even refuses to continue his mission to avoid losing any more insects. Similarly, in adulthood, Shino's passion for teaching shows a noticeable distress when his students fail to listen to him or damage happens to the Academy.[^12] Also, while still generally maintaining a composed and straightforward attitude, is shown able to relax and enjoy himself in more common activities. As a teacher, Shino is very compassionate with his students, as he insisted on caring for [Sumire Kakei](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sumire_Kakei "Sumire Kakei") despite her actions that harmed many people.

## Appearance

- ![Shino in Part I.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/a/a7/Shino_p1.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/152?cb=20160425065309)
	Shino in Part I.

1/4

Shino is regularly seen sporting the same style as the rest of his clan, consisting of dark round sunglasses and a sea-green jacket with a high, upturned collar. While Shino was in the Academy, he wore a similar outfit to the one he does now in Part II except his jacket then was of a light grey colour. Shino is a fair-skinned man and the tallest member of his graduating class. He has dark, bushy, brown hair and dark, sharp eyes (which has only been shown in the anime).[^13] As with other members of his clan, Shino constantly keeps his eyes covered to others, as even his teammate did not know what his eyes truly looked like.[^14]

During Part II, his appearance became even more mysterious, with his jacket hanging down to his knees and the addition of a hood that obstructs his face even more and a satchel on his back. This current appearance makes it harder for people like Naruto to recognise him right away. During the Fourth Shinobi World War, he wears a flak jacket under his hooded-one.

Two years after the [Fourth Great Shinobi War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Great_Shinobi_War "Fourth Great Shinobi War"), Shino sports a flak jacket with his regular shinobi attire and a new set of sunglasses. His outfit consists of a grey shirt with matching pants under his light-grey button-up jacket. The jacket also has a hood which he keeps over his head. He also changes his forehead protector to a bandanna-style.

As an adult, he wears a flak jacket with two bug-like emblems on the neck, and a full-body suit underneath. While on duty he wears a three-strip optic visor which wraps around his head, and while off duty he wears sunglasses. He also wears his hair as a topknot.

## Abilities

![Shino preparing to use his insects for battle.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/e/e9/Shino_using_his_bugs.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150320121537)

Shino preparing to use his insects for battle.

Shino showed great potential as a shinobi since early age, having drawn the attention of Danzō, a man with exceedingly high standards, in the anime.[^15] As a [genin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genin "Genin"), he easily managed to defeat [Zaku Abumi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Zaku_Abumi "Zaku Abumi"), and later fight Kankurō, a skilled shinobi to a stalemate.[^16] During the time-skip, he was promoted to [Chūnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin "Chūnin") alongside his teammates. During the [War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War"), he was able to fight and defeat several [White Zetsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/White_Zetsu "White Zetsu") despite them being [jōnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/J%C5%8Dnin "Jōnin") -calibre in power, and was able to defeat the reincarnated [Torune Aburame](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Torune_Aburame "Torune Aburame").

### Ninjutsu

Shino has shown considerable skill in [shurikenjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shurikenjutsu "Shurikenjutsu"), able to [manipulate](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Manipulated_Shuriken_Technique "Manipulated Shuriken Technique") his shuriken's trajectory mid-flight. By the Fourth Shinobi World War, he possessed [Earth](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Earth "Earth"), [Fire](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fire "Fire") and [Yang Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yang_Release "Yang Release").[^4] He can also use the [Summoning Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning_Technique "Summoning Technique"),[^4] which in the anime, can summon a [giant insect](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Giant_Insect "Giant Insect").

#### Aburame Clan Techniques

![Shino and the insects inside him.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/c/c3/Destruction_Bug_Host_Technique.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140621103150)

Shino and the insects inside him.

As a member of the [Aburame clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Aburame_clan "Aburame clan"), Shino was [infused at birth](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Parasitic_Destruction_Insect_Technique "Parasitic Destruction Insect Technique") with a special breed of insects, called [kikaichū](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kikaich%C5%AB "Kikaichū"), that use his body as a nest and feed on his [chakra](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra "Chakra"), living in a form of [symbiosis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symbiosis "wikipedia:Symbiosis"). In return, they attack and do other tasks as he commands forming his main [fighting style](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fighting_style "Fighting style"): in battle, he boxes in opponents with his bugs and then consumes their chakra once they cannot escape. In the anime, Shino is able to [transfer](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_Transfer_Technique "Chakra Transfer Technique") this accumulated chakra into a new target.[^17]

Shino has shown to be able to create [insect replicas of himself](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Insect_Clone_Technique "Insect Clone Technique"), which can reform once struck. Offensively, Shino could encase his enemies in a [cage of insects](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Secret_Technique:_Insect_Sphere "Secret Technique: Insect Sphere"), preventing them from moving and depriving them of their chakra; as well as could use a special breed of insects called [kidaichū](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kidaich%C5%AB "Kidaichū") to [devour](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Parasitic_Giant_Insect_%E2%80%94_Bug_Bite "Parasitic Giant Insect — Bug Bite") the chakra and even flesh of their victim. Being able to house the kidaichū in his body shows his skills in chakra control, as feeding them an incorrect amount will cause them to devour their host's flesh.[^18] In the anime, Shino could also use his bugs defensively by having them swarm around himself and his allies, creating a [dome](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Insect_Jar_Technique "Insect Jar Technique").[^19]

Shino could use his bugs to spy and gather information. Since the bugs make little to no noise, Shino have them scout an area and return with any useful information. Shino could leave a female bug on his target to track them down by the scent picked up from a male bug. To stop himself from being tracked, Shino could use the [Insect Jamming Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Insect_Jamming_Technique "Insect Jamming Technique") to create false chakra patterns, which would confuse [sensor](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sensor "Sensor") -type opponents.[^20]

![Shino using Secret Technique: Insect Gathering.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/6/63/Secret_Technique_Insect_Gathering.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20170309115209)

Shino using Secret Technique: Insect Gathering.

While the kikaichū only have a lifespan of a few hours, their life-cycle ensures that there will always be females with eggs. In the anime, it was demonstrated that his bugs are always undergoing [evolutionary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evolution "wikipedia:Evolution") adaptation through [mutation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mutation "wikipedia:Mutation") and [natural selection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_selection "wikipedia:Natural selection");[^21] so to cultivate for this ability, Shino can [accelerate the growth of his insects](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Secret_Technique:_Insect_Cocoon "Secret Technique: Insect Cocoon").[^21]

Also, having bred insects with the [rinkaichū](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rinkaich%C5%AB "Rinkaichū") of Torune, Shino cultivated insects that can simulate the same toxic effect as Torune's, or even produce antibodies against the said potent toxins.[^15]

### Taijutsu

![Shino attacks Suika with Iron Mountain Leaning.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/f/f6/Iron_Tackle.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20151201022434)

Shino attacks Suika with Iron Mountain Leaning.

Although favouring the use of his insects in battle, Shino is shown to be quite capable in taijutsu. During the [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams "Chūnin Exams") preliminary matches, he calmly blocked an incoming strike from Zaku and later defeated him with one punch.[^22] Shino was also fast enough to avoid a sneak attack from Kankurō's [Karasu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Karasu "Karasu") puppet.[^23] In the anime, after training more with Kiba and Hinata, Shino's taijutsu greatly improved to the point where he could fight on par with a [Jōnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Suika "Suika") and [overpower](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Iron_Mountain_Leaning "Iron Mountain Leaning") him in their second encounter.[^24]

### Intelligence

Shino has shown himself to be quite intelligent, being able to use his analytical and observational skills to make shrewd decisions with pinpoint reasoning. Like Shikamaru, Shino prevails in battles by systematically staying several steps ahead of the enemy,[^25] [^26] as well as keeping a good distance away to avoid his enemies' attacks. Shino can also tell the number of people in an area by putting his ear to the ground.[^27] Since becoming an Academy teacher, Shino has become well-versed in the various basic attack patterns and battle methods of shinobi combat, able to easily anticipate most tactics his opponents launch on him.[^28]

## Part I

### Chūnin Exams

Main article: [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams_\(Arc\) "Chūnin Exams (Arc)")

![Team 8 hides from Gaara.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/8b/Team_8_hides_from_gaara.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20250815195030)

Team 8 hides from Gaara.

While Shino originally made several background appearances during [Prologue — Land of Waves](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Prologue_%E2%80%94_Land_of_Waves "Prologue — Land of Waves") in the anime, his official debut was just before the beginning of the [Chūnin Exams arc](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams_\(Arc\) "Chūnin Exams (Arc)") when the [Rookie Nine](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rookie_Nine "Rookie Nine") came together. In the first part of the [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams "Chūnin Exams"), Shino used his [insects](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kikaich%C5%AB "Kikaichū") to read the answers on other people's tests. During the second part, his knowledge of insects allowed his team to take another team's scroll with little effort; when leeches attacked another team (from the [Amegakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Amegakure "Amegakure") in the anime, from the [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure") in the manga), they stumbled into a net trap. Kiba wanted to acquire yet another scroll but Shino and Hinata disapproved, however they went along. While keeping cover, he and his teammates soon spotted the [Three Sand Siblings](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Three_Sand_Siblings "Three Sand Siblings") confronting some [Ame-nin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Shigure "Team Shigure"). They watched as [Gaara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gaara "Gaara") killed the Ame-nin's [leader](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shigure "Shigure") and despite the other Ame-nin surrendering, he killed them as well. Gaara apparently knew they were there but his siblings persuaded him to move onwards. Later, when his team reached the tower, they saw the Sand Siblings once again leaving a chilly vibe as they walked by.

![Shino vs. Zaku.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/9/91/Shino_vs_Zaku.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140621103252)

Shino vs. Zaku.

During the preliminary matches of the Chūnin Exams, Shino was matched against [Zaku Abumi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Zaku_Abumi "Zaku Abumi"). Because one of Zaku's arms was in a sling, Shino advised him to forfeit the match. Zaku refused, and instead attacked Shino with his one good arm. In attacking Shino, Shino was given an opportunity to box in Zaku with bugs, forcing Zaku to choose between attacking him or attacking the bugs, leaving the other to attack Zaku from behind. Zaku chose to attack both, and pulled his other arm out of its sling. As Zaku prepared to blast the two opposing forces, his arms detonated, making them unusable and putting him in immense pain (in the manga, his right arm was even blown off of his body). As Zaku writhed, Shino revealed that the tubes in Zaku's arms had been filled with his bugs, causing the pressure of Zaku's attack to go inward when it couldn't escape.

Shino was scheduled to fight against [Kankurō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kankur%C5%8D "Kankurō") in the final rounds, but Kankurō forfeited before their match began to avoid revealing the secrets of his [puppet](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Puppet "Puppet") [Karasu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Karasu "Karasu"). The [Konoha Crush](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Crush "Konoha Crush") started soon afterwards.

### Konoha Crush

Main article: [Konoha Crush](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Crush_\(Arc\) "Konoha Crush (Arc)")

![Shino clashes with Kankurō.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/5/57/Shino_vs_Kankur%C5%8D.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20141014120105)

Shino clashes with Kankurō.

When Kankurō fled the village, Shino followed him, apparently having managed to dispel the [sleep-inducing genjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Temple_of_Nirvana_Technique "Temple of Nirvana Technique") that spread throughout the stadium and wanting the fight he had been denied. Shino caught up with Kankurō about to fight with [Sasuke](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke "Sasuke"), allowing Sasuke to continue on pursuing Gaara while they had their match. Kankurō attacked Shino from a distance with his puppet, and Shino used his bugs to make otherwise impossible evasions. A poisonous gas attack, however, caught Shino by surprise. Although he was able to avoid the worst of it, Shino breathed in some of the gas by accident. As the battle progressed, Shino used his bugs to eat away the chakra strings that Kankurō used to control his puppet, forcing Kankurō to relinquish and reacquire control of his only weapon. All the while, Shino directed his bugs to the scent of a female bug planted upon Kankurō's forehead protector, and upon reaching Kankurō, they consumed his chakra. As Kankurō collapsed due to the sapping of his strength, Shino collapsed too, the poisonous gas taking effect on his system. After regaining consciousness, Shino found his father, [Shibi Aburame](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shibi_Aburame "Shibi Aburame"), standing over him, using his own bugs to draw out the poison in Shino's body.

He is later seen at the [Third Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Hokage "Third Hokage") 's funeral alongside the other villagers, mourning his death.

### Sasuke Recovery Mission

Main article: [Sasuke Recovery Mission](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Recovery_Mission "Sasuke Recovery Mission") Shino regretted being away with his father on a mission and therefore unable to help. Shino felt that, had he been able to help, the mission's outcome might have been different, and Sasuke might not have been able to abandon the village. Putting the past behind him, Shino resorted to working on his teamwork skills with Kiba and Hinata, helping the latter to improve her abilities.

Team 8 and Naruto searching for the bikōchū.

In the anime, Shino led Team 8 along with Naruto to find the [bikōchū](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Bik%C5%8Dch%C5%AB "Bikōchū") beetle, a rare insect with legendary tracking abilities, in the hopes that it could be used to find Sasuke although this was in part due to Hinata, who before displayed very little confidence for any mission, strongly encouraging the idea. During this arc, he displayed his improved teamwork skills, recognising how much effort Hinata was putting into her training. Despite their best efforts, defeating the [Kamizuru clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kamizuru_clan "Kamizuru clan") and even finding the bikōchū, Naruto passed wind on the bikōchū, causing it to follow his scent, and rendering it useless.

The day of the festival, Shino was proud of the effort the students were putting into the event. When the festival received a terrorist threat, Shino discussed it with other Academy staff, and later used his kikaichū to disarm explosive tags inside the ventilation system.

Following the terrorist attack, Hana was injured from an explosion. During her recovery, Shino was placed in charge of her class. Upon Hana's return, Shino and Anko welcomed her back. They also decided to inform her about a thwarted assassination attempt on Kae.

Shino later listened to Hana in the hospital as she explained how her other personality had been the assassin after Kae, and her decision to live with the other personality, teach her, and befriend her. Shino attended the class' Sannin play as a send off for Kae, who had to return to her country.

## Trivia

| --- | --- |

- The name "Shino" means "of faith" (志之), which is also the given name of the samurai Inuzuka of *[Nansō Satomi Hakkenden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nans%C5%8D_Satomi_Hakkenden "wikipedia:Nansō Satomi Hakkenden")*, a novel about a dog samurai. His teammate, [Kiba Inuzuka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kiba_Inuzuka "Kiba Inuzuka") is also based off Inuzuka from the novel. His family name "Aburame" literally means "oil woman" (油女), while it may also be a variation of "Aburamushi" which means "cockroach" (油虫), referring to his family's use of insects as weapons.
- In *Naruto* character popularity polls, Shino was ranked 12th in the third, 25th in the fourth, 21st in the fifth. He finished 26th in the sixth and most recent.
- In the anime, Shino has been shown to name his insects.[^39]
![Shino's eye profile.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/c/c9/Shino_Eyes.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150528190221)

Shino's eye profile.

- In the anime, Shino's eyes are momentarily seen.[^40]
- Shino is the only one of the Rookie Nine not to be on a manga cover before the time-skip.
- According to the databook(s):
	- Shino's hobbies are [entomology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entomology "wikipedia:Entomology"), and collecting new specimens for his insect collection.
		- Shino wishes to fight someone strong.
		- Shino's favourite foods are wild grass [salad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salad "wikipedia:Salad") and [winter melon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winter_melon "wikipedia:Winter melon"), whilst his least favourite are foods that are strong-smelling.
		- Shino has completed 44 official missions in total: 17 D-rank, 17 C-rank, 9 B-rank, 1 A-rank, 0 S-rank.
		- Shino's favourite phrase is " *trump card* " (切り札, *kirifuda*).
- According to the [seventh OVA](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto,_the_Genie,_and_the_Three_Wishes,_Believe_It! "Naruto, the Genie, and the Three Wishes, Believe It!"), Shino's favourite colour is moss green and his favourite flower is the chameleon flower.
- According to *[Hyō no Sho](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hy%C5%8D_no_Sho "Hyō no Sho")*, while in the [Academy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Academy "Academy"), Shino had an A in ninjutsu, a B in taijutsu, genjutsu, cooperation, and classroom attitude, and a C in positivity.
- According to *Boruto* novel 1, Shino is stated to be on par with a jōnin, while only still being a chūnin himself.
