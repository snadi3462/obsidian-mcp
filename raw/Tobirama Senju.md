**Tobirama Senju** (千手扉間, *Senju Tobirama*) was a member of the renowned [Senju clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Senju_clan "Senju clan"), who, together with his elder [brother](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hashirama_Senju "Hashirama Senju") and the [Uchiha clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uchiha_clan "Uchiha clan"), founded the first shinobi village: [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure"). Throughout his lifetime, Tobirama would work tirelessly to achieve political stability and implement the institutions that made the village system work, thus ensuring Konoha's continuity and prosperity. After his brother's death, he would earn the title of **Second [Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage "Hokage")** (二代目火影, *Nidaime Hokage*, *literally meaning:* Second Fire Shadow).

## Background

![Tobirama and his family.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/02/Hashirama_family.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140703222815)

Tobirama and his family.

Tobirama was born during the [Warring States Period](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Warring_States_Period "Warring States Period"), and was the second of [Butsuma Senju](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Butsuma_Senju "Butsuma Senju") 's four sons. He and his brothers grew up on the battlefield waging constant war with their rivalling clans especially the Uchiha. After the deaths of their brothers: [Kawarama](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kawarama "Kawarama") and later [Itama](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Itama "Itama"), Tobirama and his older brother, [Hashirama](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hashirama "Hashirama"), decided a new shinobi system would need to be implemented to end the cycle of child deaths — a system that would require an alliance between the warring clans.[^5] At some point in time, it became apparent that Hashirama got into the habit of disappearing into the forest for extended periods, and as such, Tobirama accepted his father's assignment to follow Hashirama and find out what he was doing.

From tracking Hashirama, Tobirama discovered that he was meeting with a boy named [Madara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Madara "Madara"): a young Uchiha. After Tobirama reported this to their father, Butsuma pressured Hashirama to lead Madara into an ambush to be carried out by himself and Tobirama. Upon springing the trap, however, they were met by Madara's family — who had similar plans and while their fathers clashed — Tobirama fought [Izuna Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Izuna_Uchiha "Izuna Uchiha"): Madara's younger brother.[^6] Hashirama and Madara were able to negotiate a temporary ceasefire, allowing all of them to walk away without bloodshed. In the years following their families would continue to clash, Hashirama against Madara and Tobirama against Izuna. On one occasion Tobirama eventually [mortally wounded](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Flying_Thunder_God_Slash "Flying Thunder God Slash") Izuna.

![Tobirama about to kill Madara.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/b2/Tobirama_prepares_to_kill.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140710151209)

Tobirama about to kill Madara.

Hashirama, by then the leader of the Senju, tried to use Izuna's fate to broker peace between their clans, but Madara — at Izuna's behest — refused. His refusal increasingly became the minority view, with individual Uchiha siding with the Senju in growing numbers. After Izuna succumbed to his injuries, Madara, wracked with grief, made one final stand against the Senju and was summarily defeated. Tobirama attempted to kill Madara to finally end the fighting, but Hashirama stopped him, knowing that would only renew hostilities between the Senju and Uchiha. Madara offered to surrender if Hashirama would kill himself or Tobirama.[^7] Hashirama agreed to take his own life, and as he made preparations he instructed Tobirama, despite his protests, not to seek retribution. Madara ultimately withdrew his request and agreed to peace.

The Senju, the Uchiha, and all their affiliated clans came together to found Konoha, a village of peace where children would never need to die in battle.[^8] Although Hashirama was optimistic about the village's future, Tobirama remained realistic about how that future would be achieved knowing that it was not enough to simply bring all these clans together. He believed the Uchiha — Madara in particular — threatened that future. When Hashirama wanted to give Madara the position of Hokage, Tobirama forced him to open the selection to a democratic process rather than appointing a leader, leading to Hashirama's election instead.[^9] Tobirama would continue to act as the voice of reason throughout Hashirama's tenure; during the first [Kage Summit](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kage_Summit "Kage Summit"), Hashirama wanted to give Konoha's [tailed beasts](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_beasts "Tailed beasts") to the other newly-formed villages for free, but Tobirama insisted that the villages pay for the tailed beasts they received.[^10]

![Tobirama with his students.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/3/37/Team_Tobirama.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240908014426)

Tobirama with his students.

Under Hashirama's leadership, the new system of ninja villages achieved peace for a time. Eventually, Tobirama started training his own [three-man team](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Tobirama "Team Tobirama") consisting of [Hiruzen Sarutobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hiruzen_Sarutobi "Hiruzen Sarutobi"), [Homura Mitokado](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Homura_Mitokado "Homura Mitokado"), and [Koharu Utatane](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Koharu_Utatane "Koharu Utatane").[^11] Techniques he had created, such as the [Shadow Clone Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_Clone_Technique "Shadow Clone Technique") and [Summoning: Impure World Reincarnation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning:_Impure_World_Reincarnation "Summoning: Impure World Reincarnation"), he [outlawed](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kinjutsu "Kinjutsu"), their use no longer being needed. But peace would ultimately be temporary as Madara challenged Hashirama's authority and the two fought at what would become the [Valley of the End](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Valley_of_the_End "Valley of the End"). When Madara was killed in the battle, Tobirama took his body in order to study the [Sharingan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sharingan "Sharingan"), and then buried it in an unmarked grave.[^12] Conflicts would start emerging elsewhere in the world, and it was during one of these that Hashirama would die.[^1] Before his death, he passed the title of Hokage to Tobirama and left him instructions to not mistreat the Uchiha.[^13]

Tobirama's role as Hokage included helping Konoha's shinobi transition from valuing their [clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Clan "Clan") above all else to valuing the village and everyone in it.[^14] To this end he created such institutions as the [Academy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Academy "Academy"), the [Anbu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Anbu "Anbu"), and the [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams "Chūnin Exams"). While largely successful, enough that other villages adopted the same organisations,[^3] the Uchiha resisted the loss of their autonomy. Already wary of the Uchiha because of his history with Madara, Tobirama created the [Konoha Military Police Force](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Military_Police_Force "Konoha Military Police Force") for the Uchiha to run, publicly as a sign of trust but primarily so that the Uchiha could focus their energies into something beneficial. [Others](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Setsuna_Uchiha "Setsuna Uchiha"), however, would come to see this as a way to marginalise the clan.[^15]

![Tobirama and his team during the First Shinobi World War.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/f/f5/1st_World_War.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150214204648)

Tobirama and his team during the First Shinobi World War.

Like Hashirama before him, Tobirama tried to foster good relations with the other villages. He planned an alliance between Konoha and [Kumogakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kumogakure "Kumogakure"), but during a formal ceremony he and the [Second Raikage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Raikage "Second Raikage") were attacked by the [Gold and Silver Brothers](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gold_and_Silver_Brothers "Gold and Silver Brothers") and left near death.[^16] During the [First Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/First_Shinobi_World_War "First Shinobi World War"), Team Tobirama, as well as [Danzō Shimura](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Danz%C5%8D_Shimura "Danzō Shimura"), [Torifu Akimichi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Torifu_Akimichi "Torifu Akimichi"), and [Kagami Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kagami_Uchiha "Kagami Uchiha") found themselves surrounded by Kumo's [Kinkaku Force](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kinkaku_Force "Kinkaku Force"). With no way for all of them to escape, Tobirama volunteered to act as a decoy in place of Hiruzen. As he departed, he passed the title of Hokage to Hiruzen,[^17] before being killed by [Kinkaku](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kinkaku "Kinkaku").[^18]

## Personality

Tobirama was a stern man who spoke bluntly about most topics, never sugar-coating facts or the matter at hand. He had a pragmatic approach to life, believing that many of the world's problems could be solved through well-defined rules and laws.[^5] Fitting his pragmatism and adherence to order, Tobirama believed the [Shinobi Rules](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shinobi_Rules "Shinobi Rules") were absolute, and despised any deviation from their edicts. Though his words could often come off as rude or insensitive, Tobirama himself was relatively calm and composed, rarely getting riled up or reacting emotionally. It was this that allowed him to act as a sort of counter-weight to his older brother, [Hashirama](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hashirama "Hashirama") 's ideals, when Hashirama would get carried away with his headstrong optimism, it fell to Tobirama to mediate and force a more realistic alternative in order to achieve those idealistic goals. Hashirama was typically disappointed by Tobirama's intervention, but he usually agreed without much protest.[^19] But Hashirama would, at times, push back against Tobirama, often because Tobirama's methods were becoming too forceful; Hashirama's rare displays of anger on such occasions were enough to make Tobirama back down.[^20] Despite how often he needed to undermine his brother and his personal opinion that Hashirama was something of an idiot,[^21] Tobirama greatly loved and respected him, and if anything wanted Hashirama to respect himself the same way he and others did.[^22]

![Tobirama enraged by a threat to destroy Konoha.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/29/Tobirama%27s_Rage.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140619121343)

Tobirama enraged by a threat to destroy Konoha.

Tobirama was a firm believer in the [Will of Fire](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Will_of_Fire "Will of Fire") — a philosophy that held that all villagers of Konoha were part of a family. As Hokage, it was Tobirama's responsibility to protect that family, and any who threatened Konoha were subject to his rarely-seen but unbridled rage.[^23] He modelled his own leadership after both Hashirama and [Madara Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Madara_Uchiha "Madara Uchiha"), trying to strike a balance between Hashirama's indulgent nature and Madara's ruthlessness in order to protect the village. Tobirama wished for the villagers, with their disparate backgrounds, to move away from the tendency to only think about their [clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Clan "Clan") and instead think of the village as a whole.[^14] It was for this reason that he came into conflict with the [Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uchiha "Uchiha") so frequently, as he felt they too often valued their own desires over others'; he believed Madara was the worst example of this tendency.[^24] Although Tobirama truly hoped that the [Konoha Military Police Force](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Military_Police_Force "Konoha Military Police Force") would help the Uchiha focus on the village, he was not surprised when they instead took it as an intentional slight, when that in turn led them to plan a coup against Konoha, and when the planned coup brought about their near-extinction. Despite this distrust against the Uchiha,[^25] Tobirama was ready to cooperate with any clan member that could overcome their " [Curse of Hatred](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Curse_of_Hatred "Curse of Hatred") "; he cited [Kagami Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kagami_Uchiha "Kagami Uchiha") as a prime example of such an Uchiha.[^26]

Though they only knew each other for a short time, Tobirama and [Minato Namikaze](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Minato_Namikaze "Minato Namikaze"), the Fourth Hokage, got along superbly. Their mutual respect for each other seemed founded on their similarities: both of them famously moving and thinking quickly on the battlefield.[^27] [^28] While their actual personalities were quite different, Tobirama was nevertheless amused by Minato's quirks, such as his complicated names for techniques, notably the " [Spiralling Flash Super Round Dance Howl Style Three](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Spiralling_Flash_Super_Round_Dance_Howl_Style_Three "Spiralling Flash Super Round Dance Howl Style Three") ".[^29] With Minato's son, [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki"), Tobirama was more frustrated than anything else: Naruto did not address him with the proper honourifics and failed to recognise him as the creator of certain [jutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jutsu "Jutsu"), such as the [Flying Thunder God Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Flying_Thunder_God_Technique "Flying Thunder God Technique"), which Naruto instead believed to be Minato's creation.

## Appearance

- ![Tobirama as a child.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/7/70/Kid_Tobirama.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/208?cb=20230322145433)
	Tobirama as a child.

1/2

Tobirama was a fair-skinned man with silver, shaggy hair and dark-coloured eyes (depicted as red in the anime). He had three red markings on his face, one under each of his eyes and one on his chin. In combat, he wore blue armour with a distinctive white fur collar over a simple black suit. This armour was constructed from numerous metal plates, formed into multiple protective guards along his body. Beneath his shoulder armour he wore two extra shoulder guards on each arm. This clothing was accompanied by sandals and a [happuri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mengu_\(Japanese_facial_armour\)#Happuri "wikipedia:Mengu (Japanese facial armour)") in place of the more traditional [forehead protector](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Forehead_protector "Forehead protector"). The happuri was initially engraved with the Senju's emblem, which was later replaced with Konoha's.

In his official Hokage portrait, Tobirama was seen wearing the usual all-white haori with a blue kimono underneath.[^30] His casual outfit was a simple, short-sleeved, blue shirt kimono with mesh armour underneath and held closed by a pale yellow sash. This casual attire included blue pants and a bracelet of sorts on his left hand.

As a child, he wore a pair of pin-striped pants and shirt which left his arms exposed. He also did not have the markings on his face.[^31] In battle, his armour was constructed from green metal plates that covered his torso, lower behind, and groin alongside fishnet armour around his wrists and shins.[^32]

## Abilities

![Tobirama defeating Izuna.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/4/4f/Hiraishingiri.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140710150949)

Tobirama defeating Izuna.

Tobirama was renowned as one of the most powerful shinobi in history, earning praise from even his rivals.[^33] He was able to kill [Izuna Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Izuna_Uchiha "Izuna Uchiha") — Madara Uchiha's brother and equal in both skills and power.[^34] [Danzō Shimura](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Danz%C5%8D_Shimura "Danzō Shimura") stated that Tobirama's prowess was unmatched during his time as Hokage.[^35] He was able to survive an ambush from the [Gold and Silver Brothers](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gold_and_Silver_Brothers "Gold and Silver Brothers") — a fearsome duo that were able to fully demonstrate the power of the [Treasured Tools of the Sage of Six Paths](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Treasured_Tools_of_the_Sage_of_Six_Paths "Treasured Tools of the Sage of Six Paths"), which is said to surpass the [Five Kage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Five_Kage "Five Kage") along with the [Nine-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tails "Nine-Tails") 's chakra that allowed them to enter [version 1 and 2 forms](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki_Forms "Jinchūriki Forms").[^4] [^16]

### Chakra and Physical Prowess

During his lifetime, Tobirama was hailed as the fastest shinobi.[^33] When [reincarnated](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning:_Impure_World_Reincarnation "Summoning: Impure World Reincarnation") without his full power, he was capable of moving fast enough to cover [Obito Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Obito_Uchiha "Obito Uchiha") who had just become the [Ten-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ten-Tails "Ten-Tails") ' [Jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki "Jinchūriki") with [explosive tags](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Explosive_tags "Explosive tags"), as well as mark him for his Space–Time Ninjutsu all unnoticed.[^28] His reflexes were equally impressive as his [Shadow Clone](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_Clone_Technique "Shadow Clone Technique") was able to grab a [Truth-Seeking Ball](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Truth-Seeking_Ball "Truth-Seeking Ball") mid detonation and teleport it back on Obito before it could explode.[^36] In the anime, Tobirama is shown moving equally quickly while underwater.[^37] He was adept enough in taijutsu to compete against his former student, Hiruzen, who is known for his absolute mastery of all shinobi skills.

His chakra reserves were large enough to teleport two [Tailed Beast Mode](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Mode "Tailed Beast Mode") users simultaneously,[^38] and strong enough to visibly affect the surrounding terrain when releasing it.[^39] His chakra control was advanced enough to perform complex techniques with only one [hand seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hand_seal "Hand seal").[^40] His will was also so strong that nearly-perfected [Impure World Reincarnation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Impure_World_Reincarnation "Impure World Reincarnation") required enhancements from Hashirama's cells to control him.[^41]

### Ninjutsu

![Tobirama using his invented Shadow Clone Technique.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/07/Tobirama_creating_Shadow_Clones.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140904190251)

Tobirama using his invented Shadow Clone Technique.

As a Senju, Tobirama was skilled in a variety of [ninjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ninjutsu "Ninjutsu") forms. This included [shurikenjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shurikenjutsu "Shurikenjutsu") and [^42] [barrier ninjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Barrier_ninjutsu "Barrier ninjutsu").[^43] He was highly skilled in [Kenjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kenjutsu "Kenjutsu"), which allowed him to clash with Izuna on multiple occasions [^34], and in the anime, even mastered wielding the [Sword of the Thunder God](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sword_of_the_Thunder_God "Sword of the Thunder God"), a blade with electrical abilities.[^44] Developing a variety of ninjutsu, his creations all share the quality of eliminating the need for allies to risk their lives while also weakening the enemy efficiently.[^45] This is reflected in his creation of the [Shadow Clone Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_Clone_Technique "Shadow Clone Technique"), which could aid him in battle to perform actions while he was otherwise preoccupied.[^46]

Since childhood, Tobirama was an adept [sensor](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sensor "Sensor").[^47] By [touching the ground](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Area_Scanning_Technique "Area Scanning Technique") with his finger, he could accurately detect the presence and location of targets in the surrounding area.[^48] He could also sense persons countries away,[^49] identify a target's ancestry from their chakra signature,[^50] and even pinpoint a target's exact location while blinded.[^51]

In the anime, Tobirama was shown to be able to cast [genjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genjutsu "Genjutsu") to lock a target in [perpetual darkness](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Bringer-of-Darkness_Technique "Bringer-of-Darkness Technique") that even [Hiruzen Sarutobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hiruzen_Sarutobi "Hiruzen Sarutobi") could not dispel.[^52] He also mentioned that he had the ability to send his soul to help a dying Sasuke, and would have, had he not been immobilised by Madara's [black receivers](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Black_receiver "Black receiver").[^53]

#### Nature Transformation

![Tobirama using a powerful Water Release technique without a pre-existing water source.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/4/47/Tobirama%27s_Water_Release.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20161028054225)

Tobirama using a powerful Water Release technique without a pre-existing water source.

Tobirama was able to use all five basic [nature transformations](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nature_transformation "Nature transformation"), along with [Yin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yin "Yin") and [Yang Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yang_Release "Yang Release").[^4] He excelled at [Water Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water_Release "Water Release") and was able to create vast quantities of the substance without a present water source enabling him to use high-level Water Release techniques at any given time.[^54] He was renowned for his ability to create [tsunamis](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water_Release:_Water_Colliding_Wave "Water Release: Water Colliding Wave") to flood the battlefield.[^55] He was able to form [defensive walls](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water_Release:_Water_Formation_Wall "Water Release: Water Formation Wall") or [large water dragons](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water_Release:_Water_Dragon_Bullet_Technique "Water Release: Water Dragon Bullet Technique") with a single [Hand Seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hand_Seal "Hand Seal") each and without a pre-existing water source.[^54] He could also shoot high-pressured streams from his mouth [to slice through targets](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water_Release:_Water_Severing_Wave "Water Release: Water Severing Wave"),[^56] or high velocity [needle-shaped projectiles](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Heavenly_Weeping "Heavenly Weeping") to catch an opponent off-guard while attacking their vital points.[^57] In the anime, he could shape water into [tendrils](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water_Release:_Water_Binding "Water Release: Water Binding") to pull targets down with him to drown them.[^37] While not shown he was also capable of forming a large concentrated [water lance](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water_Release:_Hard_Whirlpool_Water_Blade "Water Release: Hard Whirlpool Water Blade") with tremendous piercing power and attack range.[^58]

#### Space–Time Ninjutsu

![Tobirama teleports behind Obito.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/f/fa/Tobirama_teleporting.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20141021115857)

Tobirama teleports behind Obito.

Tobirama's skill with various [space–time ninjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Space%E2%80%93time_ninjutsu "Space–time ninjutsu") is well-remembered.[^59] Using the [Flying Thunder God Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Flying_Thunder_God_Technique "Flying Thunder God Technique"), he could instantly teleport himself and/or other targets to any location marked with his seal or connected to his chakra. Connecting his chakra with others has also allowed Tobirama to monitor a battlefield using their chakra as a source for his technique. When this chakra is [shared](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_Transfer_Technique "Chakra Transfer Technique") among many individuals, he can teleport any number of people away from harm.[^60] Should he connect his chakra to other users of the technique, Tobirama can make use of their Flying Thunder God seals.[^61] [^62] By using a marked area or throwing a marked kunai, with precise timing, he could move around an area quickly and strike at the most opportune moment [before his opponent could react](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Flying_Thunder_God_Slash "Flying Thunder God Slash").[^7]

#### Summoning

Tobirama was especially proficient in [Summoning Techniques](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning_Technique "Summoning Technique").[^4] His most infamous technique was the [Summoning: Impure World Reincarnation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning:_Impure_World_Reincarnation "Summoning: Impure World Reincarnation"), which returned the souls of the dead into immortal, regenerating bodies.[^63] [^64] Tobirama employed this technique to revive fallen enemies on the battlefield, either to extract information or to have them walk back into their own lines and carry out [kamikaze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamikaze "wikipedia:Kamikaze") -style attacks using the [Mutually Multiplying Explosive Tags](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mutually_Multiplying_Explosive_Tags "Mutually Multiplying Explosive Tags"),[^65] another technique he invented which took advantage of the revived bodies' regeneration to create near-infinite chains of explosions.[^28] He was said to use it whenever he needed to clear a map.[^66] Tobirama labelled it a [kinjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kinjutsu "Kinjutsu") in hopes it wouldn't be used by anyone again.[^67] It was implied that he never fully-refined this technique as he could only control a small number of reincarnated bodies at once [^68] and whose power was limited compared to themselves while alive,[^41] both of which other shinobi would improve upon in the future.

### Intelligence

Tobirama was highly intelligent, serving as the strategic leader of his clan during the [Warring States Period](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Warring_States_Period "Warring States Period") and later as the Hokage’s aide following the establishment of [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure"). His political acumen strengthened Konoha’s diplomacy and tempered his brother’s idealism, providing the stability necessary for the village’s growth.[^69] During his time, he created systems such as the [Academy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Academy "Academy"), the [Anbu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Anbu "Anbu"), and the [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams "Chūnin Exams") that were so successful, that other villages adopted the same organizations.[^3]

His innovative mind also extended to [Ninjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ninjutsu "Ninjutsu"), having created numerous original and advanced techniques, including the [Impure World Reincarnation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Impure_World_Reincarnation "Impure World Reincarnation"), [Flying Thunder God Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Flying_Thunder_God_Technique "Flying Thunder God Technique"), and [Shadow Clone Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_Clone_Technique "Shadow Clone Technique"). These techniques were designed to efficiently weaken opponents while minimizing the risks faced by his allies during wartime.[^70]

In battle, he was calculating, striking only when he's confident of success and without any thought of mercy.[^71] He was known for launching surprise attacks on opponents who think they've won and have let their guard down.[^33] In order to capitalise on every possible advantage, he pays careful attention to every aspect of a battle, analysing his opponents' strategies and techniques to discern their purpose, mechanics, and ultimately their weaknesses. He discovered that the [Truth-Seeking Balls](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Truth-Seeking_Ball "Truth-Seeking Ball") were capable of permanently damaging reanimated shinobi, nullifying their regeneration, and advising the [Fourth Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Minato_Namikaze "Minato Namikaze") to steer clear of them.[^72] He also analysed his comrade's abilities to create counterattacks, using strategies that made maximum use of the techniques he created. He was similarly knowledgeable of skills he has no training in, being able to recognise a toad from [Mount Myōboku](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mount_My%C5%8Dboku "Mount Myōboku") and their [senjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Senjutsu "Senjutsu") [^73] or different types of advanced [nature transformations](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nature_transformation "Nature transformation").

## Legacy

Although Tobirama created the [Konoha Military Police Force](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Military_Police_Force "Konoha Military Police Force") with the intention of pacifying the [Uchiha clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uchiha_clan "Uchiha clan"), it only exacerbated their feelings of marginalisation and, in effect, cut them off from positions of influence in [Konoha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha "Konoha"). Dissatisfaction with Konoha grew amongst the Uchiha for decades following, eventually culminating in the [Uchiha Clan Downfall](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uchiha_Clan_Downfall "Uchiha Clan Downfall").[^74]

Tobirama would also be responsible in creating several vital institutions for Konoha such as the Academy, the Anbu, and the Chūnin Exams which would help Konoha bond many of the clans together to fight for Konoha and would also help Konoha foster relationships with the other villages. These institutions were so successful that other villages began implementing their own versions of them in their own nations.

His invention of the [kinjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kinjutsu "Kinjutsu") [Summoning: Impure World Reincarnation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning:_Impure_World_Reincarnation "Summoning: Impure World Reincarnation") would play a major role in the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War") and ironically would allow Tobirama as well as the other deceased Hokage the ability to assist the [Allied Shinobi Forces](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Allied_Shinobi_Forces "Allied Shinobi Forces") in the war after they were reincarnated by Orochimaru at the behest of Sasuke Uchiha. The reincarnated [Madara Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Madara_Uchiha "Madara Uchiha") himself begrudgingly admitted he was thankful to Tobirama for inventing the technique while fighting the reincarnated [Hashirama Senju](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hashirama_Senju "Hashirama Senju").

- Tobirama and his siblings all share names referring to the space between two things. Hashirama refers to the space between two pillars, Tobirama the space between two doors, Kawarama two roof tiles, and Itama the space between two wooden slats on a [duckboard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duckboard "wikipedia:Duckboard"), a type of platform built to create a dry passage over muddy, or wet terrain.

- Tobirama is the only Hokage never to be shown wearing the hat of office.
- According to the databook(s):
	- His favourite words were "Mind – technique – body" (心・技・体, *Shin – gi – tai*).
		- His favourite food were the fresh fish caught in the clean rivers of Konoha.
- In the English Dub, in Part I, Tobirama is voiced by [Peter Lurie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Lurie "wikipedia:Peter Lurie") while Hashirama is voiced by [Jamieson Price](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamieson_Price "wikipedia:Jamieson Price").
