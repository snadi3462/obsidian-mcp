**Chōji Akimichi** (秋道チョウジ, *Akimichi Chōji*) is a member of [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure") 's [Akimichi Clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akimichi_Clan "Akimichi Clan"). Though sensitive about his weight, Chōji is nevertheless dedicated to his friends, especially in [Team Asuma](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Asuma "Team Asuma").

## Background

![Chōji as a child.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/d/df/Child_Choji.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160618201937)

Chōji as a child.

Chōji was born into the Akimichi clan, and is slated to become the **Sixteenth Head of the Akimichi** (秋道16代目当主, *Akimichi Jūrokudaime Tōshu*) after his father, [Chōza](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%8Dza "Chōza"). When Chōji was younger, he was often put down for not being good at a child's game called " *Ninja* ", which appears to be a cross between [tag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tag_\(game\) "wikipedia:Tag (game)") and [hide and seek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hide_and_Seek "wikipedia:Hide and Seek"), often employing the use of [shuriken](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shuriken "Shuriken") made out of cardboard and sticks. [His peers](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shinta "Shinta") would always berate him, telling him that any team that he was on was bound to lose. One day, they simply refused to let him play at all. However, [Shikamaru Nara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shikamaru_Nara "Shikamaru Nara") stuck up for him, saying that the teams would be uneven if Chōji didn't join them. This gave Chōji a little hope, although, in the end, they still refused to let him play.[^6]

| --- | --- |

Chōji and his father were later seen talking on the roof of a building while Chōji was still upset over the way he was treated by the other children, saying the Akimichi were all fat and stupid. Comforting him, his father told him that few people have a heart as kind and caring as his, and one day he would meet someone who would see that in him and respect him for it, and that eventually they would be the best of friends. His father proved to be correct as soon after, Shikamaru arrived. Chōji recognised him as the one who had defended him earlier, and asked Shikamaru why he wasn't playing the game, to which Shikamaru responded that it was too troublesome. He then told Chōji that he liked to come up there to watch the clouds and that Chōji happened to be sitting in his spot. Chōji moved over to give him space. Shikamaru proceeded to lie down before telling Chōji that he should watch the clouds with him. This delighted Chōji, and he eagerly asked Shikamaru if he wanted to eat some snacks that he had brought. Shikamaru took him up on the offer, causing Chōji to be overjoyed, and then Shikamaru said "This is the best, watching clouds and eating chips". From that day on, the two became best friends.[^7]

During Chōji's time at the Academy, he was often caught eating in class otherwise getting into trouble along with fellow deviants Shikamaru, [Kiba Inuzuka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kiba_Inuzuka "Kiba Inuzuka"), and [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki"). Together, these four would cut class or otherwise be testing the patience of [Iruka Umino](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Iruka_Umino "Iruka Umino"), their Academy teacher. During the Search for Tsunade, when Naruto was struggling to learn the second step of training for the [Rasengan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rasengan "Rasengan"), he recalled how once, during his days in the Academy, he, along with Shikamaru, Kiba, and Naruto were held back in class, and lectured about their all around lack in concentration — in Chōji's case, he was thinking more about food than about doing his school work. During the arc involving the trap master [Gennō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genn%C5%8D "Gennō") from the long-gone [Kagerō Village](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kager%C5%8D_Village "Kagerō Village"), it was mentioned how Shikamaru, Chōji, Kiba, and Naruto would skip class together and hang out at the kunai practice course, which was situated under the Academy, to hide from Iruka.

## Personality

![Chōji's dreams of a girl who admires him for his appetite.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/9/93/Ch%C5%8Dji%27s_Dream.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160303120127)

Chōji's dreams of a girl who admires him for his appetite.

Chōji is a very kind, polite, and caring person. These characteristics are said to be his greatest strength by both his father Chōza and his former teacher [Asuma Sarutobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Asuma_Sarutobi "Asuma Sarutobi") while his greatest weakness was his lack of self-confidence.[^8] He is easily motivated by the prospect of food, and becomes highly agitated when somebody calls him "fat" (he refers to himself as "big-boned" and "chubby"). Asuma would say that he is just "pleasantly plump". However, he does not hide his love for food, and his near constant hunger is also an annoyance to his teammates, as eating often comes before other necessities such as stealth and teamwork. Food can also make him over-react about silly things, such as who gets the last chip, or when Akamaru tried to take his chips, and he refuses to let anyone else eat the last chip in the bag, even in battle-situation.[^9] Chōji also tends to eat a lot when he's angry. Since Part II of the series, however, his eating habits are more controlled and no longer interfere with his duties as a shinobi.

| --- | --- |

Chōji has a long-standing friendship with Shikamaru. The two have complete trust in each other, with Shikamaru being one of the few people to look past Chōji's lack of athleticism, and see that he had a kind heart; recognising Chōji's true strength. Because of this, Chōji has unwavering faith and loyalty to Shikamaru and, if necessary, would give his life for him, which nearly happened during his battle with [Jirōbō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jir%C5%8Db%C5%8D "Jirōbō"). Chōji also cares deeply for his mentor, Asuma, and had a difficult time in fighting him during the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War"), due to not wanting to hurt his own teacher,[^10] even when Asuma called Chōji "fat" in an attempt to anger him.[^11]

As stated by his father and teacher, Chōji greatly lacked confidence in himself and his abilities which often times hindered his actions or even progression in some aspects.[^8] During the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War"), however, Chōji was able to overcome these issues and become one of the major contributing factors in the [Allied Shinobi Forces](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Allied_Shinobi_Forces "Allied Shinobi Forces") turning the tide of the battle in their favour.[^11]

As an adult, Chōji is shown to be very laid back and jovial. He is also shown to be a loving husband towards Karui, and an extremely doting father towards his eccentric daughter Chōchō. However, he maintains a competitive side, with Chōchō frequently challenging him to eating contests. During her Chūnin exam, Chōji is shown to gleefully cheer her on while also expressing concern for her well-being when she faces the skilled genin Shinki.

## Appearance

Like other Akimichi, Chōji has a rotund build and markings on his cheeks in the form of swirls. He has spiky, light brown hair that sticks upward in Part I and grows to waist-length in Part II. In *[The Last: Naruto the Movie](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/The_Last:_Naruto_the_Movie "The Last: Naruto the Movie")*, Chōji cuts his hair short and sports a goatee, both of which grow further when he becomes an adult, with his hair slicked back and braided.

	Chōji in Part I.

1/4

During Part I, Chōji donned a green, short-sleeved haori with a long, white scarf over a light-green shirt (white in the anime), with the kanji for "food" (食, *shoku*) on it. He also wore black shorts, hoop earrings, and bandages around his legs and forearms. His blue forehead protector was styled in a manner that allowed two tufts of hair to poke out at each side. In Part II, Chōji sports a red suit with plated armour on his torso, arms, and upper legs, having the same kanji for "food" (食, *shoku*) on the front. He switches to a standard red forehead protector and replaces his hoop earrings with studs, a gift from [Asuma Sarutobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Asuma_Sarutobi "Asuma Sarutobi"). During the Fourth Shinobi World War, he wears the standard Konoha flak jacket over his outfit.

In *[The Last: Naruto the Movie](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/The_Last:_Naruto_the_Movie "The Last: Naruto the Movie")*, Chōji's overall appearance slims down somewhat, being more plump as he considers himself. His outfit is mostly the same, but he wears larger wrist-guards and his boots reach his calves.[^12] Years later, Chōji's attire consists of elements from Parts I and II: a green haori over chest plate armour, brown pants with a red belt, bandages around his arms, and black sandals. He seems to have stopped wearing a forehead protector.

## Abilities

![Chōji entering his Butterfly Chōji Mode.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/9/97/Choji_Thin.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150520200205)

Chōji entering his Butterfly Chōji Mode.

Chōji's low self-confidence belied his great strength and immense power;[^13] he became agitated to fight an opponent mainly if they insulted him or his friends. It was during these instances that he proved to very strong, enough to defeat the [Sound Four](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sound_Four "Sound Four") member, [Jirōbō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jir%C5%8Db%C5%8D "Jirōbō"). His father [Chōza](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%8Dza "Chōza"), Shikamaru, and [Asuma Sarutobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Asuma_Sarutobi "Asuma Sarutobi") all believe that Chōji's greatest strength is his kindness and concern for his friends, which the latter states could someday make him stronger than anyone.[^14] Chōji helps fight members of [Akatsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki "Akatsuki") such as [Kakuzu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakuzu "Kakuzu") and [Pain](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Pain "Pain"), though his self-confidence reaches its peak during the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War"): Chōza states Chōji is now worthy of being the Akimichi clan's sixteenth head, every ally and foe on the battlefield become astonished at his display of strength, and he defeats the reincarnated Kakuzu and [Hizashi Hyūga](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hizashi_Hy%C5%ABga "Hizashi Hyūga").[^15] [^16]

### Ninjutsu

#### Akimichi Clan Techniques

![Chōji using the Human Bullet Tank.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/85/Human_bullet_tank.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150225060708)

Chōji using the Human Bullet Tank.

As an Akimichi, Chōji converts the calories he consumes into chakra, hence his husky physique and frequent eating habits. He uses the chakra he stores to perform the body-expanding [Multi-Size Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Multi-Size_Technique "Multi-Size Technique"), allowing him to use the [Human Bullet Tank](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Human_Bullet_Tank "Human Bullet Tank") to run down his opponents. He can increase the damage by [wrapping spikes](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Spiked_Human_Bullet_Tank "Spiked Human Bullet Tank") around his body, using either [kunai](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kunai "Kunai") or, in Part II, his long hair after he sharpens it. Chōji can expand other [parts of his body](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Partial_Multi-Size_Technique "Partial Multi-Size Technique") to enhance his physical strikes, grab and crush targets, or make himself [gigantic](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Super_Multi-Size_Technique "Super Multi-Size Technique") to [smash them](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chubby_Dive "Chubby Dive") [from above](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Super_Open_Hand_Slap "Super Open Hand Slap"). After the Sasuke Retrieval Mission, Chōji abandoned his initial brute-force fighting-style, perfecting his usage of his clan's Multi-Size Technique, along with bolstering his reaction speed, developing sharp movements, culminating in overall greater agility.[^17]

![Three Coloured Pills.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/b9/Three_Coloured_Pills.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20141010122500)

Three Coloured Pills.

The [Three Coloured Pills](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Three_Coloured_Pills "Three Coloured Pills"), when eaten, convert Chōji's own body fat into chakra, increasing his physical attributes and facilitating the use of his clan's more advanced techniques. After consuming the red pill, the large amount of [chakra](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra "Chakra") manifests into two massive [butterfly wings](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Butterfly_Mode "Butterfly Mode") on Chōji's back, making his power a hundredfold. Taking each pill damages Chōji's body, with the red pill not only emaciating the user's body, but also causing eventual death. Therefore, the pills are considered a last resort to use. However, Chōji survived thanks to the [Nara clan's medical book](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nara_Clan_Medical_Encyclopaedia "Nara Clan Medical Encyclopaedia") and Tsunade's medical expertise. He overcomes these issues by Part II, using his own chakra to perform his clan's techniques without consuming the pills. During the Fourth Shinobi World War, he becomes able to form the butterfly wings on his own, bypassing the pills' dangerous side effects.

#### Nature Transformation

Chōji's [nature transformations](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nature_transformation "Nature transformation") include [Fire](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fire "Fire") and [Earth Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Earth_Release "Earth Release"), though he almost exclusively uses the [Yang Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yang_Release "Yang Release") for his body-expanding techniques.[^4] With Earth Release, he could erect a [rock wall](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Earth_Release:_Practice_Brick_Technique "Earth Release: Practice Brick Technique") to protect against attacks.[^18]

### Physical Prowess

![Chōji intercepts Jirōbō's attack after using the Butterfly Chōji Mode.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/7/7e/Ch%C5%8Dji_stops_Jir%C5%8Db%C5%8D.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140621102544)

Chōji intercepts Jirōbō's attack after using the Butterfly Chōji Mode.

Naturally, Chōji has massive physical strength that he can combine with his clan's techniques to inflict powerful strikes on his opponent. When he manifests chakra butterfly wings, he can compress the chakra into his fist to use the [Butterfly Bullet Bombing](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Butterfly_Bullet_Bombing "Butterfly Bullet Bombing"), powerful enough to kill a [Cursed Seal Level 2](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sound_Four%27s_Cursed_Seals "Sound Four's Cursed Seals") -enhanced Jirōbō in one strike. His strength is necessary for important tasks, being selected as a member of the [Sasuke Recovery Team](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Recovery_Team "Sasuke Recovery Team") and helping to restrain the Deva Path during [Pain's Assault](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Pain%27s_Assault "Pain's Assault"). Aside from strength, Chōji has great durability and speed, enough to survive being hit by the [Demonic Statue of the Outer Path](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Statue_of_the_Outer_Path "Demonic Statue of the Outer Path") 's chakra blasts and save Shikamaru from being stomped by the statue. He is eventually considered to have the greatest destructive force in the shinobi world [^19] and in the anime, by adulthood, he is recognised as the physically strongest man in Konoha.[^20]

## Part I

### Chūnin Exams

Main article: [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams_\(Arc\) "Chūnin Exams (Arc)") During the first phase, he and his teammates met with their old classmates of [Team 7](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Kakashi "Team Kakashi") and [Team 8](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_8 "Team 8") until the written exams begun. After Ino cheated off of [Sakura](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sakura "Sakura") 's test answers using [Mind Body Switch Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_Body_Switch_Technique "Mind Body Switch Technique"), she went into Shikamaru and his body to pass on the answers to them. They ended up passing regardless because the true test was to overcome pressure and intensity.

![Chōji attacking Zaku.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/7/77/Choji_vs_Zaku.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20141010122732)

Chōji attacking Zaku.

During the second phase of the Chūnin Exams, Chōji was reluctant to join his team in helping Sakura fend off a team of Oto genin, fearing for his own safety, and continuously asking to run. However, when [Zaku Abumi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Zaku_Abumi "Zaku Abumi") called Chōji "fatso", Chōji proceeded to go berserk as he defended his size, and yelled that Konoha and the Sound ninja were now at war. Properly motivated, Chōji fought against the Oto genin, but were unable to defeat them. His actions, however, bought time until [Sasuke Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Uchiha "Sasuke Uchiha") awakened and forced them to retreat with the power of the [Cursed Seal of Heaven](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Cursed_Seal_of_Heaven "Cursed Seal of Heaven").

![Chōji being defeated by Dosu.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/1/15/Ch%C5%8Dji_vs_Dosu.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150128181720)

Chōji being defeated by Dosu.

In the preliminaries of the exam, Chōji began feeling hesitant about fighting, but Asuma convinced him to fight, promising to treat him to barbecue if he won, and to intervene if he was in danger. In the last match of the preliminaries, Chōji was matched against [Dosu Kinuta](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Dosu_Kinuta "Dosu Kinuta"). At the start of the match, [Ino Yamanaka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ino_Yamanaka "Ino Yamanaka") yelled to him that he was fat, so that he would be spurred into fighting. Figuring Dosu's sound based attack won't work on him if he doesn't hear it, he uses the Multi-Size Technique and uses the Human Bullet Tank to charge at Dosu. After Dosu manages to avoid Chōji's attack a few times, Chōji gets lodged in a wall. With Chōji unable to get out the wall, Dosu takes the initiative of sending a [blast of sound](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Resonating_Echo_Drill "Resonating Echo Drill") into Chōji's bulk, with its effectiveness increased as sound travels better through water. As a reward to Chōji for getting so far in the Chūnin Exams, and as a bribe to help with Shikamaru's training, Asuma takes Chōji to a buffet, but Chōji ends up eating too much and gains stomach pains, forcing him to spend some time in the hospital. He recovered in time to watch Shikamaru and Sasuke's matches, but was put to [sleep](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Temple_of_Nirvana_Technique "Temple of Nirvana Technique") during the ensuing [invasion of Konoha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Crush "Konoha Crush").

### Sasuke Recovery Mission

Main article: [Sasuke Recovery Mission](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Recovery_Mission "Sasuke Recovery Mission")

![The Sasuke Recovery Team.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/03/Shikateam.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20260701132713)

The Sasuke Recovery Team.

Shortly after Shikamaru's promotion to [chūnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin "Chūnin"), the team celebrated at Yakiniku Q. After a brief argument over the meat, Asuma told Chōji that he should eat less and train more. While Chōji was briefly depressed, Shikamaru convinced him to be himself.

When Sasuke Uchiha abandoned Konoha, Shikamaru asked Chōji to join the [Sasuke Recovery Team](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Recovery_Team "Sasuke Recovery Team"), to which Chōji refused. Determined to have Chōji come with them, Shikamaru and Naruto Uzumaki ate some of Chōji's favourite snacks in front of his house. When they got to the last chip, Chōji burst through the front door to eat it, saying that the last chip was sacred and he cannot let someone else have it. Having fallen for Shikamaru's trick, Chōji joined the team to retrieve Sasuke.

![Chōji finishes off Jirōbō.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/3/32/Chodan_Bakugeki.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140621102522)

Chōji finishes off Jirōbō.

At the start of the mission, Chōji took on the Sound Four member Jirōbō. Jirōbō initially proved to be too strong for Chōji to beat, forcing Chōji to use the Akimichi clan's special [Three Coloured Pills](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Three_Coloured_Pills "Three Coloured Pills"). Although reluctant to use the pills, Jirōbō's constant mocking of Shikamaru as a worthless, selfish, and uncaring leader convinced Chōji to take all three pills. With the accompanying increase in strength, he was able to kill Jirōbō noting that while it was fine for people to mock him and even eat his food, it was unforgivable to speak ill of his best friend. With that, Chōji sends a fist crashing into Jirōbō's stomach, killing the Oto-nin.

![Chōji undergoing treatment and observation in intensive care.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/9/9e/Ch%C5%8Dji_treated.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130321164719)

Chōji undergoing treatment and observation in intensive care.

As he headed through the forest, suffering the effects of the pills and severely emaciated, Chōji sees the arrow left by his comrades, he collapses beneath it, and tearfully mused to his father that he had finally found good friends. Near death, Chōji was found by members of the [Medic Corps](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Medic_Corps "Medic Corps") and rushed back to the village for emergency treatment. Thanks to the efforts of the [Nara clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nara_clan "Nara clan") 's [medical encyclopaedia](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nara_Clan_Medical_Encyclopaedia "Nara Clan Medical Encyclopaedia"), where they had listed their research on their comrade's pills and their effects, along with [Tsunade](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tsunade "Tsunade") 's medical expertise, Chōji was able to evade death and make a full recovery.

Later, when [Urashiki Ōtsutsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Urashiki_%C5%8Ctsutsuki "Urashiki Ōtsutsuki") was spotted in the area, Chōji joined several other Konoha-nin in pursuing him. However, this turned out to be division by the alien, who placed a genjutsu on them to let him sneak into the village.

Meanwhile, the grown-up Sasuke and Boruto were sent back in time to shortly after Sasuke's defection. There, the two pretended to be travelling performers and were put under watch by Naruto. During which, past-Chōji and his friends helped Boruto and Naruto clean up a bathhouse after the commotion Naruto and Jiraiya caused. Days later, Chōji and the rest of his team invited Boruto and Naruto to join them in an after-mission barbecue dinner at [Yakiniku Q](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yakiniku_Q "Yakiniku Q").

Days later, [Konohamaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohamaru "Konohamaru"), [Moegi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Moegi "Moegi"), and [Udon](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Udon "Udon") approached Chōji and his friends for help, telling how Naruto and Boruto were battling a [dangerous foe](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Urashiki_%C5%8Ctsutsuki "Urashiki Ōtsutsuki"). They quickly agreed to help. By the time they arrived however, Urashiki was already defeated. The group then escorted their injured friends to the hospital. Days later, after Sasuke and Boruto recovered and prepared to return to their time, Sasuke first used his Sharingan to erase the memories of everyone he and Boruto came in contact of the recent events with to protect the timeline.

Shikamaru takes this opportunity to apologise for his son, Shikadai, who recently declined an [S-rank](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/S-rank "S-rank") mission that Inojin and Chōchō could have benefited from. Even though the Summit could end with the outbreak of war, Chōji is unconcerned. He tells Chōchō that Shikamaru will be there, and that because of Shikamaru's presence the war will be averted.

## Trivia

| ![Springtime of Youth](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/3/39/Youth_symbol.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/40?cb=20130120020002)  Springtime of Youth | The [*Springtime of Youth* wiki](https://springtimeofyouth.fandom.com/wiki/ "w:c:springtimeofyouth") has an article about this topic:  **[Chôji Akimichi](https://springtimeofyouth.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C3%B4ji_Akimichi "w:c:springtimeofyouth:Chôji Akimichi")** |
| --- | --- |

- The name "Chōji" is made up of the kanji for "butterfly" (蝶) and "second" (二). Butterflies are insects known to eat sweets. Chōji also manifests butterfly wings made of [chakra](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra "Chakra") after eating the third red pill during his fight against Sound Four ninja Jirōbō. His family name "Akimichi" literally means "autumn road" (秋道), referring to the time of harvest as well as the streets where vendors sell food.
	- His given name could also be a reference to [Shio no Chōjirō](http://yokai.com/shionochoujirou/), also known as "Shio no Chōji", a man known for his legendary fondness of horse meat, which led to him devouring all 300 of his horses. Shio no Chōjirō in turn may have been inspired by Chōjirō Shioya, a late 17th century magician known for his "horse-swallowing technique".
- Chōji was the only member of the original 15 genin to not be ranked within the top 30 characters in the sixth and most recent Naruto character popularity poll. He was not ranked among the top 30 in the fifth poll either. The only poll he placed in was the fourth where he was ranked 21st most popular character.
- According to the databook(s):
	- Chōji's hobby is buying and eating snacks.
		- While Chōji doesn't wish to fight anyone in particular, Chōji will fight anyone as long as there is food involved.
		- Chōji's favourite foods are [Korean barbecue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_barbecue "wikipedia:Korean barbecue") and [junk food](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snack_food "wikipedia:Snack food"), whilst his least favourite food is anything inedible.
		- Chōji has completed 39 official missions in total: 17 D-rank, 13 C-rank, 6 B-rank, 3 A-rank, 0 S-rank.
		- Chōji's favourite word is 'meat' (肉, *niku*).
		- Chōji is the only character to have a stat go down between databooks: his hand seal score goes down by half a point between the second and third databooks.
- According to [Hyō no Sho](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hy%C5%8D_no_Sho "Hyō no Sho"), while in the [Academy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Academy "Academy"), Chōji had a B in ninjutsu, taijutsu, cooperation, and positivity, and a C in genjutsu and classroom attitude.
- In *[Naruto: Uzumaki Chronicles 2](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto:_Uzumaki_Chronicles_2 "Naruto: Uzumaki Chronicles 2")*, it is revealed that Chōji has a fear of lizards after he sees [Ibushi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ibushi "Ibushi") 's puppet lizard.
- In the [omake](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Omake "Omake") of [*Naruto: Shippūden* episode 163](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Explode!_Sage_Mode "Explode! Sage Mode"), Chōji seemingly uses [Wind Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wind_Release "Wind Release") by sucking up Potato chips, calling it "Wind Release: Great Absorption" (風遁・大吸引, *Fūton: Daikyūin*, *English TV:* Wind Style: Great Suction) which angers Naruto questioning what kind of Wind Release was that.
- In a 2012 Shōnen Jump interview, [Masashi Kishimoto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Masashi_Kishimoto "Masashi Kishimoto") stated that Chōji and [Killer B](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Killer_B "Killer B") were currently his favourite characters in the *Naruto* series.
- Despite Chōji using [Earth Release: Practice Brick Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Earth_Release:_Practice_Brick_Technique "Earth Release: Practice Brick Technique") in the manga, Earth Release is omitted from his list of natures in the fourth databook.[^4]
- On the back of [Hyō no Sho](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hy%C5%8D_no_Sho "Hyō no Sho"), Choji's hair colour is blond in colour.
