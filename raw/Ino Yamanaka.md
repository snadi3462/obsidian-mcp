**Ino Yamanaka** (山中いの, *Yamanaka Ino*) is a kunoichi of [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure") 's [Yamanaka clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yamanaka_clan "Yamanaka clan"). She and her fellow members of [Team Asuma](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Asuma "Team Asuma") are their generation's [Ino–Shika–Chō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Formation_Ino%E2%80%93Shika%E2%80%93Ch%C5%8D "Formation Ino–Shika–Chō") trio. She later becomes the head of the [Konoha Barrier Team](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Barrier_Team "Konoha Barrier Team") and a member of the [Analysis Team](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Analysis_Team "Analysis Team").

## Background

![Ino as a child.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/f/fa/Kid_Ino.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230317040234)

Ino as a child.

Ino was the only child of [Inoichi Yamanaka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Inoichi_Yamanaka "Inoichi Yamanaka") and [her mother](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ino%27s_Mother "Ino's Mother"). She was a popular student during her time in the Academy. There, she met [Sakura Haruno](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sakura_Haruno "Sakura Haruno"), who was teased by other kids for her large forehead. Ino defended Sakura from the bullies, throwing a flower she claimed was poisionous into the mouth of [one of the bullies](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ami "Ami"), making the group run away. Ino then encouraged Sakura to embrace her forehead rather than hide it, becoming good friends with her. Over the following years, Ino's guidance and friendship helped Sakura become more confident and develop into her own person. However, when Sakura found out that they had a crush on the same boy, [Sasuke Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Uchiha "Sasuke Uchiha"), Sakura approached Ino, telling her of her liking towards Sasuke. This caused them to end their friendship, starting their dislike towards each other in the beginning of the series.[^8]

## Personality

From a young age, Ino has been a confident, bold, and outspoken girl who had a mild-temper and occasionally lashed out at others if their personal habits bothered her.[^9] At the same time, she was by nature very friendly and loyal to her allies, usually more motivated than her team-mates, and tended to take charge of them, a characteristic she would later pass on to her son, Inojin. Shikamaru, despite finding girls "troublesome", offers no resistance to Ino's forceful behaviour, being unwilling to deal with her reaction if things don't happen her way.[^10] She is also prideful in her appearance and often tries to lose weight by dieting in the hopes that it will make her more attractive to boys; she even jokingly suggests that Chōji should do the same to attract girls.[^11] She is quite knowledgeable about different types and meanings of flowers, sometimes making analogies to them when talking.[^12]

Despite her assertiveness and uppity demeanour, Ino is a compassionate and kind person at heart, having been raised to embody the meaning of the [bush clover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lespedeza "wikipedia:Lespedeza"): a blunt, candid love who treasures the bonds with her friends.[^13] It is these qualities that earned Sakura's friendship, defending Sakura from bullies who teased her and helping her become confident. Their warm friendship developed into a bitter rivalry over the following years, fuelled by competition for Sasuke's affections, ninjutsu and even trading insults, with Ino as " **Ino-pig** " (いのぶた, *Ino-buta*) and Sakura as " **Forehead Girl** " (デコリーン, *Dekorīn*, *English TV:* Billboard Brow). After the [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams "Chūnin Exams"), Ino becomes impressed by Sakura's growth and they rekindle their friendship, while maintaining their rivalry on more amiable terms. She cares greatly for her teammates as well, being relieved that they survived after the failed [Sasuke Recovery Mission](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Recovery_Mission "Sasuke Recovery Mission"),[^14] promising her sensei [Asuma Sarutobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Asuma_Sarutobi "Asuma Sarutobi") to look after them after he dies,[^15] and during the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War"), encouraging them to stay strong in the face of danger.[^16] By the time of his death, Inoichi believed that Ino had truly blossomed into the bush clover of the Yamanaka Clan, and is proud of her bonds with her friends.

![Ino dreams of handsome young men like Sasuke and Sai fighting over her.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/3/39/Ino%27s_dream.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160303115538)

Ino dreams of handsome young men like Sasuke and Sai fighting over her.

Ino had the same infatuation for Sasuke that most other girls in their Academy class had, due to his good looks and cool personality. Even after Sasuke's defection from Konoha, Ino largely retained her feelings for him in Part II and she cried when he was declared an international criminal,[^17] and pictured him as the "love" the bush clover symbolism during the Fourth Shinobi World War.[^18] Nevertheless, Ino meets [Sai](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sai "Sai") in Part II, who not only replaces Sasuke on Team 7, but also strongly resembles him in appearance. For this reason, Ino becomes immediately smitten with him, flirting with him when first introduced and blushing when Sai called her beautiful.[^19] Although her [Infinite Tsukuyomi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Infinite_Tsukuyomi "Infinite Tsukuyomi") dream reveals she likes the idea of Sasuke and Sai fighting over her, Ino's affections ultimately shift toward Sai, showing a deeper desire to know more about him as she risks her life to save him in *[Shikamaru Hiden](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shikamaru_Hiden "Shikamaru Hiden")*. Sai reciprocates Ino's feelings and the two marry years later, having a son, [Inojin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Inojin "Inojin"). Even after many years together Ino's love for Sai is as strong as ever, occasionally acting as love-struck as she did when they first met, particularly when Sai gets affectionate with her.

As a mother, Ino's personality is relatively the same, but she has greater respect for her clan and its traditions, insisting they be passed down to the next generation to form a new Ino–Shika–Chō. However, she firmly believes in not pushing her son Inojin in a career path he does not want, recognising that all people mature at their own rate and in their own ways, openly trusting her son to find his own path as a ninja. Ino strongly respects her late father and tries her best to carry on his will in leading their clan. [^20]

## Appearance

Ino is a fair-skinned woman of average height with light green eyes (blue in the anime) and long, light blonde hair with bangs framing the right side of her face. As a young child, her hair was short. In the beginning of Part I, her hair grew to hip-length and is tied into a high ponytail. During the Chūnin Exams, Ino cropped her hair to shoulder-length, often keeping it up in a bun. By Part II, her hair grew back to hip-length ponytail with her bangs became thicker, hiding most of her right eye. In the Blank Period, her hair is hung loosely and extends to her calves; as an adult, her hairstyle returns to its shape in Part II, but her ponytail reaches her upper-back and sports a black clip.

- ![Ino in the Blank Period.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/f/f1/Ino_-_The_Last.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/56?cb=20160302071654)

Ino regularly wears a purple outfit consisting of a high-collared blouse and matching apron skirt. In Part I, she sports this attire with bandages on her abdomen and legs, small hoop-earrings, her [forehead protector](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Forehead_protector "Forehead protector") worn as a belt, and for a time, white arm warmers. In Part II, Ino's outfit is slightly more revealing as she discards her forehead protector and bandages while exposing her midriff and wears mesh armour on her thighs, elbows and knees. She also replaces her hoop earrings with studs, a gift from Asuma, and sports a red hair clip on her left temple. During the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War"), Ino dons the standard Konoha infantry [flak jacket](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Flak_jacket "Flak jacket") and assorted clothing. In the Blank Period, she wears a longer skirt that reaches her ankles, high-heeled sandals and pink lipstick.[^21] As an adult, Ino wears her purple outfit again exposing her midriff and open-toed sandals. She also dons the standard Konoha uniform while on missions.[^22]

## Abilities

As a [genin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genin "Genin"), Ino was noted to be a kunoichi with the potential to become the strongest member of the Yamanaka clan.[^23] During the time-skip, she becomes a [chūnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin "Chūnin"), and her improved skills ultimately earned further praise from her father in the Fourth Shinobi World War.[^24] Around the era of the Seventh Hokage, her talents made her the leader of the [Konoha Barrier Team](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Barrier_Team "Konoha Barrier Team"). Many shinobi in the village and of her own clan look up to her.[^20] In the anime, she also works in the [Analysis Team](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Analysis_Team "Analysis Team") as an interrogator.[^25]

### Chakra and Physical Prowess

While a genin, Ino showed to be skilled at [chakra control](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_control "Chakra control"), as seen when she used her own cut hair to [bind](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_Hair_Trap_Technique "Chakra Hair Trap Technique") a target. In Part II, her chakra control improved greatly, having been made a [medical-nin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Medical-nin "Medical-nin"), and being chosen to help [seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Four-Corner_Sealing_Barrier "Four-Corner Sealing Barrier") the [Three-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Three-Tails "Three-Tails") in the anime,[^26] She could also [transfer](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_Transfer_Technique "Chakra Transfer Technique") her chakra to others.[^27]

Although Ino rarely uses taijutsu, she has proven to be skilled enough in her battle against Sakura during the Chūnin Exams,[^28] and against a reincarnated Asuma in the Fourth Shinobi World War, a proficient taijutsu user himself.[^29] Ino has also shown to have strength and speed, able to tackle Chōji (with increased weight from his [enlarged arm](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Partial_Multi-Size_Technique "Partial Multi-Size Technique")) away from Asuma's [Wind Release: Dust Cloud Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wind_Release:_Dust_Cloud_Technique "Wind Release: Dust Cloud Technique").[^30] Two years after the war, while usually positioned near the back lines during close-ranged combat situations, her taijutsu has improved enough to support [Sai](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sai "Sai") in defeating multiple amounts of enemies.[^31]

### Ninjutsu

By the Fourth Shinobi World War, Ino possessed the [Earth](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Earth "Earth"), [Water](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water "Water"), [Yin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yin "Yin") and [Yang Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yang_Release "Yang Release") [nature transformations](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nature_transformation "Nature transformation").[^4] With Earth Release, she can erect a [rock wall](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Earth_Release:_Practice_Brick_Technique "Earth Release: Practice Brick Technique") to protect against attacks. She would later acquire [Fire Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fire_Release "Fire Release") as well in the anime.[^32]

#### Sensory Perception and Mind Reading Techniques

![Ino senses chakra around her.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/7/73/Kanchi.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140821155341)

Ino senses chakra around her.

Ino has a great [sensory](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sensor "Sensor") perception, able to [detect](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sensing_Technique "Sensing Technique") changes in another person's chakra.[^33] [^34] She can lock onto an enemy's chakra signature and [transfer](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sensing_Transmission "Sensing Transmission") the information to her allies for their benefit.[^35] Likewise, she became able to instinctively detect a person's chakra signature without first moulding chakra and also determine the potency of a chakra, becoming the leader of [Konoha Barrier Team](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Barrier_Team "Konoha Barrier Team").[^36] Her sensory skills are currently noted to be the best of her clan.[^20]

By the era of the Seventh Hokage, Ino could skilfully [probe people's minds](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Psycho_Mind_Transmission "Psycho Mind Transmission") for information. She could delve into the mind of a severely injured individual to extract his memories without the help of a machine.[^37] Her talents for it also aided in interrogating people to learn their true nature and determine whether they are under the influence of someone else.[^25]

#### Medical Ninjutsu

![Ino using the Mystical Palm Technique.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/0a/Ino_and_sakura_mystical_palm.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20151016035554)

Ino using the Mystical Palm Technique.

Ino learned [medical ninjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Medical_ninjutsu "Medical ninjutsu") over the time-skip, and was noted to have mastered it by Part II. She is skilled enough to make an immediate judgement on the condition of a patient.[^3] She also has employed anaesthetic-laced weapons.[^31] In the anime, she was a surrogate apprentice of Sakura's for three months under Tsunade's guidence where she learnt to [heal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mystical_Palm_Technique "Mystical Palm Technique") fatal wounds and to aid in surgeries.[^38] [^39]

#### Yamanaka Clan Techniques

![Ino using the Mind Body Switch Technique.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/e/e4/Mind_Body_Switch_Technique.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150923164110)

Ino using the Mind Body Switch Technique.

As a Yamanaka, Ino specialises in mind-affecting techniques, such as the [Mind Body Switch Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_Body_Switch_Technique "Mind Body Switch Technique"), which allows her to possess a target and take control of their actions. She can also use this technique on animals for reconnaissance and espionage, as it was originally intended.[^40] Ino's body, however, is left vulnerable to attack while the technique is active, and therefore it is not suited for battle.[^41] She can also be forced out of the body if the opponent's will is strong enough. In the anime, she learns the [Mind Body Disturbance Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_Body_Disturbance_Technique "Mind Body Disturbance Technique"), which allows her to force her enemies to attack each other.[^42]

![Ino using the Mind Clone Switch Technique.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/c/c4/Mind_Clone_Technique.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160121003628)

Ino using the Mind Clone Switch Technique.

During the Fourth Shinobi World War, Ino's speed with the Mind Body Switch Technique has improved greatly;[^43] able to quickly save her allies from incoming danger on multiple occasions.[^44] [^45] Ino has also learned the [Mind Clone Switch Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_Clone_Switch_Technique "Mind Clone Switch Technique"), which allows her to control multiple targets at once.[^46] With the [Mind Body Transmission Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_Body_Transmission_Technique "Mind Body Transmission Technique"), she can communicate telepathically with someone, and convey memories and feelings to them.[^47] She is quite skilled with this technique, able to connect the entire [Allied Shinobi Forces](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Allied_Shinobi_Forces "Allied Shinobi Forces") through a telepathic link. She can also [transmit](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sensing_Transmission "Sensing Transmission") what she senses into a target's mind. By making physical contact with a person, they can also communicate with one another while the technique is active.[^48] In *[The Last: Naruto the Movie](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/The_Last:_Naruto_the_Movie "The Last: Naruto the Movie")*, Ino's telepathic range has expanded enough to receive and broadcast messages across the [Five Great Shinobi Countries](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Five_Great_Shinobi_Countries "Five Great Shinobi Countries") in a short period of time with the aid of a [Chakra Transmission Communication Device](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_Transmission_Communication_Device "Chakra Transmission Communication Device"). In later years, she could perform this technique to connect as far as 100 kilometres beyond the distance of the village unaided,[^49] and likewise can maintain multiple independent channels.[^50]

### Intelligence

![Sakura falls prey to Ino's trap.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/9/99/Ino_Hair_Trap.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150211045020)

Sakura falls prey to Ino's trap.

Ino is quite intelligent, graduating at the top of her class in the [Academy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Academy "Academy"), alongside Sasuke and [Shino Aburame](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shino_Aburame "Shino Aburame"). She can be very deceptive in battle, able to get Sakura into lowering her guard by cutting her hair and seemingly using the Mind Body Switch Technique, despite Sakura being a moving target. While Sakura thought Ino lost her mind, Ino actually didn't use the technique and scattered her hair to create a chakra rope to restrain Sakura's movements, and thus, guaranteed that her Mind Body Switch Technique wouldn't miss.[^51] The anime shows that Ino is very attentive, able to determine a person's occupation by glancing at the condition of their hands.[^52] During [Pain's Assault](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Pain%27s_Assault "Pain's Assault"), she found a connection between [Pain](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Pain "Pain") 's technique and the [Yamanaka Clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yamanaka_Clan "Yamanaka Clan") 's mind techniques and concluded that Pain's real body must be around the village for his technique to work.[^53]

## Part I

### Prologue — Land of Waves

Main article: [Prologue — Land of Waves](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Prologue_%E2%80%94_Land_of_Waves "Prologue — Land of Waves") In the anime, Ino races Sakura toward the Academy, where they will be added to new teams along with other graduates of her class. Despite hoping to be on the same team as Sasuke, she is devastated that not only Sakura becomes Sasuke's teammate along with [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki"), but she herself is added to Asuma's Team 10 with Shikamaru and Chōji. Ino immediately lays ground rules for her new teammates to follow.

### Chūnin Exams

Main article: [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams_\(Arc\) "Chūnin Exams (Arc)") Despite how recently they graduated from the Academy, Asuma enters Team 10 in the [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams "Chūnin Exams") being held in Konoha. Shortly before the exams begin, Ino greets Sasuke by cheerfully hugging him, much to his, and of course, Sakura's chagrin. For the first stage of the exams, they are given a written test with ten questions too difficult for the standard genin to be able to answer, and for which reason participants are expected to cheat without getting caught. Ino uses her [Mind Body Switch Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_Body_Switch_Technique "Mind Body Switch Technique") to copy answers from Sakura's exam sheet and transfer the information to Shikamaru and Chōji.

![Ino infiltrates Kin's body.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/2e/Ino_infiltrates_Kin%27s_body.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20170829000712)

Ino infiltrates Kin's body.

For the second stage of the exams, Team 10 enters the [Forest of Death](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Forest_of_Death "Forest of Death") to take part in a five-day survival exercise. Team 10 initially spends their time avoiding conflict, hiding whenever they come across another team. When they are soon noticed by [Neji Hyūga](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Neji_Hy%C5%ABga "Neji Hyūga"), Ino tries using her charm to convince Neji into giving her team the scroll, though she fails miserably and her team immediately retreats from Neji's intimidation. When they discover Sakura defending her teammates from a [team](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Dosu "Team Dosu") of [Oto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Oto "Oto") -nin by herself, Ino recalls her childhood memories of their friendship and finally decides to help her. Team 10 initiates [Formation Ino–Shika–Chō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Formation_Ino%E2%80%93Shika%E2%80%93Ch%C5%8D "Formation Ino–Shika–Chō"): Chōji attacks [Zaku Abumi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Zaku_Abumi "Zaku Abumi") with [Human Bullet Tank](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Human_Bullet_Tank "Human Bullet Tank"), Shikamaru immobilises [Dosu Kinuta](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Dosu_Kinuta "Dosu Kinuta") with his [Shadow Imitation Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_Imitation_Technique "Shadow Imitation Technique"), and Ino uses her Mind Body Switch on [Kin Tsuchi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kin_Tsuchi "Kin Tsuchi"). Ino tries to use Kin as a hostage, but Zaku opts to attack Kin himself rather than let her be used against him. Too tired to offer much more of a fight, Team 10 wonders what to do. When Sasuke regains consciousness and activates his new [cursed seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Cursed_Seal_of_Heaven "Cursed Seal of Heaven"), Ino becomes frightened by the dark change in Sasuke's chakra and released herself from Kin's body. After the Oto-nin retreat, the Konoha genin take their leave, but not before Ino fixes Sakura's hair.

![Ino and Sakura knock each other out.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/e/eb/Double_Knockout.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150227214215)

Ino and Sakura knock each other out.

After passing the second stage, the remaining genin take part in one-on-one preliminary matches. For her match, Ino is paired against Sakura. They start by trading insults and then punches, surprising Ino because Sakura proves her equal in both categories. Frustrated, Ino follows Sakura's earlier example by cutting her hair, a ruse that allows her to immobilise Sakura while she uses her Mind Body Switch Technique. In control of Sakura's body, Ino tries to make her forfeit the match, but she is stopped and exorcised by [Inner Sakura](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Inner_Sakura "Inner Sakura"). They are both exhausted by this, but exchange one final blow, knocking both out. When they later wake up, Ino informs her that their match was ruled a tie, and that therefore neither of them will continue to the finals. Despite this, they decide to rekindle their friendship, though they can't help but continue to bicker over Sasuke.

A few days later, Ino accompanies Sakura to visit Sasuke at the hospital with flowers, but discover he is gone. Instead, they see Lee training despite his injuries, causing him to fall unconscious as a consequence. On the day of the finals, Ino sat with Sakura in the audience and tried to cheer her up when Sasuke hadn't arrived yet. She becomes impressed by Naruto's victory over Neji and cheers for Shikamaru in his match with [Temari](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Temari "Temari"), though is left shocked when he voluntarily gives up. Sasuke finally arrives for his match with [Gaara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gaara "Gaara"), and Ino watches the fight with interest, noting to Sakura that her teammates are amazing.

### Konoha Crush

Main article: [Konoha Crush](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Crush_\(Arc\) "Konoha Crush (Arc)") Ino and the rest of the audience are [put to sleep by genjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Temple_of_Nirvana_Technique "Temple of Nirvana Technique") as the [Konoha Crush](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Crush "Konoha Crush") begins. Several days after the invasion is successfully repelled, she attends the [Third Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Hokage "Third Hokage") 's funeral.

### Sasuke Recovery Mission

### In Naruto's Footsteps: The Friends' Paths

Main article: [In Naruto's Footsteps: The Friends' Paths](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/In_Naruto%27s_Footsteps:_The_Friends%27_Paths "In Naruto's Footsteps: The Friends' Paths")

Team 10 during the second Chūnin Exams.

In the anime, about two years after Naruto leaves Konoha to train, another Chūnin Exams is held. Because participants must enter as part of three-man teams, Ino invites Sakura to be Shikamaru's replacement on Team 10 since Shikamaru is already a chūnin. During the first exam, Ino, Sakura, and Chōji are seated in different rooms and are tasked with getting a combined score of exactly 100 points on their written test despite their separation. Ino telepathically contacts Sakura and Chōji in order to assign which questions to answer. After the initial testing period is over, they are given a bonus question: each team must unanimously select one of their members to disqualify from the rest of the exams. Team 10 selects nobody, which is the correct answer and which qualifies them for the next phase.

![Ino links with teammates to help them sense enemy.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/26/Ino_Links_with_Teammates.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20211220232906)

Ino links with teammates to help them sense enemy.

Those who pass the first exam must reach the [Demon Desert](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demon_Desert "Demon Desert") within three days in order to participate in the second exam. Team 10 successfully does so and they are given the same objective as they had in the exam several years ago: obtain a scroll from another team. They wander through the desert for three days, losing their provisions to an [Ame](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ame "Ame") -team. While recuperating at an oasis, they are confronted by [Team Ameno](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Ameno "Team Ameno"), and Ino is knocked out on her first attempt to attack them. Awakening and remembering her training, she uses her sensing abilities to locate their attackers and [telepathically links](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_Body_Transmission_Technique "Mind Body Transmission Technique") with Sakura and Chōji once more, allowing them to coordinate their attacks and defeat Team Ameno. Once Sakura and Ino heal their injuries, Team Ameno offers them their scroll. However, theirs is the same scroll that Team 10 already has, so they are allowed to keep it. They go their separate ways, agreeing to meet again in the third exam.

Team 10 is later attacked by [Team Saya](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Saya "Team Saya"). [Saya](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Saya "Saya") [possesses](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Puppet_Imitation_Technique "Puppet Imitation Technique") Ino's mind and forces her to attack Sakura. When Chōji, meanwhile, begins overwhelming Saya's teammates, Saya possesses his mind instead, and she forces him to attack Sakura using his [Super Multi-Size Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Super_Multi-Size_Technique "Super Multi-Size Technique"). Ino is able to release Chōji using her Mind Body Switch Technique, forcing Team Saya to retreat. Team 10 is afterwards trapped in a [sandstorm](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sandstorm_Technique "Sandstorm Technique"), during which Ino and Chōji are poisoned by a [cloaked](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sand_Cloak_Technique "Sand Cloak Technique") [Mamushi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mamushi_\(Genin\) "Mamushi (Genin)"). Sakura escapes the sandstorm, defeats Team Saya, and then returns to heal Ino and Chōji.

![Ino and the other genin being promoted to chūnin.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/9/91/Tsunade_Promoting_Genin.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20250924233621)

Ino and the other genin being promoted to chūnin.

Team 10 is eventually found by one of the exam's proctors, who brings them to where all of the other genin are being assembled. They are informed that the Chūnin Exams have been cancelled; reports on the participants' performances will be sent back to their villages, leaving their promotion up to their superiors. When they get back to Konoha, Tsunade promotes Ino, Sakura, and Chōji to chūnin.

### Akatsuki Suppression Mission

Main article: [Akatsuki Suppression Mission](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki_Suppression_Mission "Akatsuki Suppression Mission")

![Ino tries to heal Asuma.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/9/95/Ino_healing_Asuma.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20141009141419)

Ino tries to heal Asuma.

Ino and Chōji are assigned to one of the [Twenty Platoons](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Twenty_Platoons "Twenty Platoons"), a task-force assigned to hunt down [Akatsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki "Akatsuki") members within the [Land of Fire](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_Fire "Land of Fire"). Led by [Raidō Namiashi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Raid%C5%8D_Namiashi "Raidō Namiashi") and [Aoba Yamashiro](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Aoba_Yamashiro "Aoba Yamashiro"), the team is called in for back-up when Asuma's team engages [Hidan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hidan "Hidan") and [Kakuzu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakuzu "Kakuzu") of Akatsuki. When they arrive, however, the Akatsuki members flee and Asuma's injuries are too severe for Ino to heal. Sensing his death, Asuma gives parting words to his students: he praises Ino's confident personality and tells her to look after Shikamaru and Chōji, as well as to not lose to Sakura in any pursuit. Ino tearfully mourns Asuma's death and later attends his funeral in Konoha with Choji, asking him why Shikamaru hasn't arrived.

Once Shikamaru gets everything ready, he sets out with Ino and Chōji to hunt down Hidan and Kakuzu. Tsunade tries to persuade them not to go, but they are all confident in what they plan to do. She tries to hold them back on the grounds that they need a fourth member, at which point [Kakashi Hatake](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi_Hatake "Kakashi Hatake") appears and volunteers to be their fourth, causing Tsunade to relent. After the team review their battle strategy, Ino uses her Mind Body Switch Technique on a hawk to locate Hidan and Kakuzu. The team engages the Akatsuki duo in battle, though Ino cannot contribute much to the fight as her Mind Body Switch alone is too risky to use in battle and due to her status as a [Medical-nin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Medical-nin "Medical-nin"). Since Hidan and Kakuzu are formidable working together, Team 10 realises the duo need to be separated if they're to be defeated. Ino volunteers to lead Hidan away, but Shikamaru insists her not to and goes in her place.

![Kakuzu attempts to kill Team 10.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/4/4f/Kakuzu_chokes_Team_Asuma.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20211220232539)

Kakuzu attempts to kill Team 10.

Even with each side one member short, Kakuzu still manages to easily overwhelm and capture Kakashi and Team 10. His attempt to kill them is blocked by the [combined efforts](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Typhoon_Water_Vortex_Technique "Typhoon Water Vortex Technique") of Naruto and [Yamato](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yamato "Yamato"), who arrive with Sakura and Sai to lend assistance. While Sakura and Sai set out to help Shikamaru, Naruto fights Kakuzu and eventually defeats him with his newly-created [Wind Release: Rasenshuriken](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wind_Release:_Rasenshuriken "Wind Release: Rasenshuriken"). Afterwards, Ino heals Naruto's injuries and praises him for his improved skills before the teams return to Konoha. In the anime, Ino visits Neji and [Hinata Hyūga](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hinata_Hy%C5%ABga "Hinata Hyūga") and tells them about the mission's events and Naruto's new technique.[^54]

As they near the First Division's location, Shikaku contacts Shikamaru, Chōji, and Ino and tells them about the first threat they'll need to deal with: [Kinkaku](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kinkaku "Kinkaku"), who possesses some of the [Nine-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tails "Nine-Tails") ' chakra. When they arrive, they use Formation Ino–Shika–Chō on Kinkaku, allowing his capture in the [Kohaku no Jōhei](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kohaku_no_J%C5%8Dhei "Kohaku no Jōhei"). After Kinkaku's defeat, Team 10 is immediately confronted by a reincarnated Kakuzu, who is interested in fighting them. Shikaku contacts them and instructs them to deal with another threat instead: a reincarnated Asuma.

![Ino saves Chōji from Asuma's attack.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/84/F%C5%ABton_F%C5%ABjin_no_Jutsu.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20120809113519)

Ino saves Chōji from Asuma's attack.

Although none of them want to fight Asuma, they, as his former students, are experts on his fighting style and thus the best-qualified to defeat him. When he notices their presence, Asuma congratulates them for their clear growth since he last saw them. They attack him and he's forced to counter, but he gives pointers about how to avoid his attacks and ultimately defeat him. Although Ino and Shikamaru are committed to the fight, Chōji has difficulty bringing himself to strike Asuma, to everyone's frustrations, Asuma's included. Ino saves Chōji's life twice from Asuma's attacks and, using the Mind Body Switch Technique on Chōji, tells him he needs to pull himself together so they can defeat Asuma. Once Chōji is able to do so, Ino leaves Chōji's body and [takes control of two White Zetsu soldiers](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_Clone_Switch_Technique "Mind Clone Switch Technique") to attack Asuma, moving him toward Shikamaru and Chōji so they can restrain him. Defeated, Asuma gives them new parting words: that he has nothing more to teach them since their teamwork is now perfect.

By nightfall, the First Division's battle appears won: all the reincarnated forces have been captured and all present elements of the [White Zetsu Army](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/White_Zetsu_Army "White Zetsu Army") have been defeated. The [Demonic Statue of the Outer Path](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Statue_of_the_Outer_Path "Demonic Statue of the Outer Path") suddenly appears on the battlefield and starts devastating the First Division, causing Ino and her team to take cover. The statue later vanishes after [Tobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tobi "Tobi") escapes with the [Benihisago](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Benihisago "Benihisago") and the Kohaku no Jōhei, which contain the reincarnated [Gold and Silver Brothers](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gold_and_Silver_Brothers "Gold and Silver Brothers"). Later, Ino was sent with the rest of [Konoha 11](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_11 "Konoha 11") to retrieve back Naruto and [Sakura](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sakura "Sakura"). Ino's team caught up with Naruto and Sakura, but [Sai](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sai "Sai") came and took the two with him to catch up with Kakashi. They were left behind to take down [Hiruko](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hiruko_\(missing-nin\) "Hiruko (missing-nin)") 's henchmen, [San](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/San "San"). They used Ino–Shika–Chō combination and easily took down the enemy. [Shikamaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shikamaru "Shikamaru") had Ino tell San that they aren't here to fight but to bring back Naruto and the others, after Ino used [Mind Body Switch Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_Body_Switch_Technique "Mind Body Switch Technique") and told San, he refused and said that he understands but he can't just pass the information on, forcing Team 10 to fight him. He then summoned his two teammates, [Ichi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ichi "Ichi") and [Ni](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ni "Ni"), and used [Chimera Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chimera_Technique "Chimera Technique"), which made them turn into a beast, Team 10 was defeated quickly. The rest of Konoha 11 comes to back up Team 10 and while [Tenten](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tenten "Tenten") summoned out chains everyone grabs onto one and as Shikamaru was about to use a technique to bind the beast Ino urges Shikamaru to go on and that the rest of them can handle it. Ino is later seen at Hiruko's temple saving Sakura from a collapsing building while the other members of the Konoha 11 defeated the beast.

#### Road to Ninja: Naruto the Movie

Main article: [Road to Ninja: Naruto the Movie](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Road_to_Ninja:_Naruto_the_Movie "Road to Ninja: Naruto the Movie") Ino first appears with her friends battling several [Zetsu clones](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/White_Zetsu_Army "White Zetsu Army") disguised as deceased Akatsuki members. Later, in the [alternate world](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genjutsu_World "Genjutsu World"), a different Ino is seen. Compared to the Ino that Naruto and Sakura know, she is more timid, calm, conservative in her appearance, and shy with boys. She also isn't bossy with her team, trying to end things peacefully with them rather than lashing out at them like she usually does.

## Trivia

| ![Springtime of Youth](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/3/39/Youth_symbol.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/40?cb=20130120020002)  Springtime of Youth | The [*Springtime of Youth* wiki](https://springtimeofyouth.fandom.com/wiki/ "w:c:springtimeofyouth") has an article about this topic:  **[Ino Yamanaka](https://springtimeofyouth.fandom.com/wiki/Ino_Yamanaka "w:c:springtimeofyouth:Ino Yamanaka")** |
| --- | --- |

- The name "Ino" can be short for "Inoshishi" which means "boar" (猪), hence Sakura calling her Ino-pig-chan (いのブタちゃん, *Ino-buta-chan*). Her family name "Yamanaka" means "among the mountains" (山中). Wild boar are heavily associated with mountains in Japan, and are even called the whales of the mountains (山鯨, *yamakujira*), because of the amount of meat they produce.
- The name "Ino" (偉野, 偉埜) is sometimes used as a given name meaning "Great Field".
- "Ino" (いの町 *Ino-chō*) is also a name of a town in the Agawa District, Kōchi Prefecture, Japan.
- Her family owns a flower shop called [Yamanaka Flowers](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yamanaka_Flowers "Yamanaka Flowers"), where she works part-time.
- In the manga, it was never revealed how or why Ino became a medical-nin. Her choice to study under Tsunade was anime-exclusive.
- Ino was ranked as the 29th most popular character in the seventh Naruto character popularity poll. She placed 14th in the fifth poll, 17th in the sixth poll and 23rd in the fourth poll. She finished 30th in the first poll.
- According to the databook(s):
	- Ino's hobby is [shopping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shopping "wikipedia:Shopping").
		- Ino wishes for a rematch with [Sakura](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sakura "Sakura").
		- Ino's favourite foods are cherry tomatoes and pudding, while her least favourite is [sashimi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sashimi "wikipedia:Sashimi").
		- Ino has completed 40 official missions in total: 23 D-rank, 9 C-rank, 6 B-rank, 2 A-rank, 0 S-rank.
		- Ino's favourite phrase is "The many [meanings of flowers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanakotoba "wikipedia:Hanakotoba") " (花言葉の数々, *Hanakotoba no kazukazu*).
		- Ino's favourite thing is the flower she gave Sakura when they were children.
- According to [Hyō no Sho](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hy%C5%8D_no_Sho "Hyō no Sho"), while in the [Academy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Academy "Academy"), Ino had an A in ninjutsu and positivity, and a B in taijutsu, genjutsu, cooperation, and classroom attitude.
