**Kiba Inuzuka** (犬塚キバ, *Inuzuka Kiba*) is a member of [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure") 's [Inuzuka clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Inuzuka_clan "Inuzuka clan") and a member of [Team Kurenai](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Kurenai "Team Kurenai"). Despite his headstrong, and at times egotistic attitude, Kiba is loyal to his comrades and will do anything to protect them with his trusted canine companion, [Akamaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akamaru "Akamaru"), by his side. After the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War"), Kiba became an investigator of the [Konoha Military Police Force](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Military_Police_Force "Konoha Military Police Force") and a famed dog breeder.

## Background

![Kiba as a child.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/7/76/Kid_Kiba.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20141014110851)

Kiba as a child.

At a very young age, Akamaru was given to Kiba by his mother, [Tsume](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tsume "Tsume"), and the two became very close soon afterwards. Before entering the [Academy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Academy "Academy"), Kiba and Akamaru played together a great deal, resulting in Tsume getting angry because they weren't training enough.[^6] During Kiba's time at the Academy, he often cut class with Akamaru, stating that he wasn't suited to sit still at a desk, or otherwise getting into trouble, along with [Chōji Akimichi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%8Dji_Akimichi "Chōji Akimichi"), [Shikamaru Nara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shikamaru_Nara "Shikamaru Nara"), and [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki"). Together, these four would skip class and go to the practice hall (not to practice). They thought that it was fun to also test the patience of [Iruka Umino](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Iruka_Umino "Iruka Umino"), their Academy instructor. One day, Kiba fell asleep during class and, upon waking, saw Naruto attempting to use the [Transformation Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Transformation_Technique "Transformation Technique") to turn into the [Third Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Hokage "Third Hokage") for the first time, and failing twice. This caused Kiba to find Naruto incompetent, and he became among those who ridiculed him.

At some time in the past, Kiba knew his father; but because of his mother's attitude, Tsume scared him away, leaving him just with her and his sister [Hana Inuzuka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hana_Inuzuka "Hana Inuzuka"). Despite this, Kiba stays in a close relationship with his sister and mother,[^7] although he seems to agree that Tsume is rather frightening.

## Personality

Kiba is often short-tempered and impulsive (in stark contrast to his teammates [Shino](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shino "Shino") and [Hinata](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hinata "Hinata")), and can be prone to making mistakes when he becomes agitated in battle although he wished to avoid fighting opponents like [Gaara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gaara "Gaara") in the chunin exams. He also enjoys combat even when facing particularly strong opponents. He cares deeply for Akamaru, and is willing to do whatever is necessary to protect him. And, despite his rather gruff attitude, many of Kiba's actions reveal that he is very close to his older sister, Hana, and remains fiercely loyal to his clan and has great pride in it.

Back at his academy days, Shino always considered Kiba a "problem child", and wanted to be assigned to a team with anyone but him because of how different their personalities were to each other.[^8] Even after becoming a team, Kiba often argues with Shino over what course of action the group should take, particularly when Shino advises caution. Kiba often sees himself as the group leader, performing with exceptional leadership within Team Kurenai,[^9] and becomes offended at any of Shino's actions that he perceives as challenging his leadership. It seems that there was a contest for leadership of Team 8 during the Chūnin Exams, as Kiba said Hinata voted for him. Despite his disagreements with Shino, he is very close to him, and respects his fighting capabilities. Shino on the other hand came to consider Kiba his best friend, and a partner whom he could trust more than anyone else by his side.[^8] Despite Shino's own stoic personality, he has shown a rather humorous side of his around Kiba, especially when he occasionally teases Kiba whenever he's boasting about how he's a suitable candidate for Hokage.

Kiba has became extremely close to his other teammate, Hinata, and acts rather brotherly toward her, but tends to worry about her, urging her to be strong before the second phase of the exam,[^10] and to forfeit rather than face a dangerous opponent.[^11] Kiba also seems to be quite protective of her, although he insists that Hinata is not someone who can't take care of herself.[^12] Kiba is also one of the first few people to get to know about Hinata's crush on [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki"), and has been quite supportive of that. Kiba likes to playfully tease Hinata about her affections for Naruto. This usually includes telling her that Naruto is nearby, or just mentioning her reactions to him to other people, much to her embarrassment. Despite this, they have a very strong friendship. Kiba also seemed to be quite excited over Hinata and Naruto's wedding, and wanted to be the one, along with Shino and Akamaru, to bring them the best present out of all the people attending their wedding, so they could make Hinata happy. Kiba dubbed it as the Team 8's last mission, something that only members of their team would be involved in, who had been through all the happiness and sorrow with each other.[^13]

Kiba is also quite close to his sensei and her [daughter](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mirai_Sarutobi "Mirai Sarutobi"). Although he gets a little annoyed by Mirai's habit of mixing up his and Akamaru's names, calling him and Akamaru "Akakiba" and "Kibamaru" respectively, which is rather weird considering Kiba and Akamaru frequently came to play with Mirai during their walks.[^13] Kurenai also seems to act rather playful with Kiba, teasing him about his newly grown goatee and pinching his cheeks.

In the anime, mostly in the filler arcs, Kiba is shown to also get along well with Naruto, despite their arguments. This seems to be proven when it was shown that Kiba, Akamaru, Naruto, Chōji, and Shikamaru skipped classes together and play in the park. Those three seem to be some of the first true friends he made in the village. Although Kiba was rather rude to Naruto during their fight in Chūnin exams, Kiba grew to respect him after being defeated, cheering him on during his battle against Neji and even stating that Naruto is not someone to be underestimated, showing more confidence in him than Hinata, who looked rather worried.[^14] They also have somewhat of a rivalry that stems from childhood. He was shown to have become frustrated with Naruto's growth and is determined to surpass him. He even showed admiration towards Naruto, stating that he wanted to become like him.

![Kiba's dream of being Hokage.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/05/Kiba%27s_dream.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160303121436)

Kiba's dream of being Hokage.

Kiba also dreams of becoming the Hokage someday and expressed this desire both during his fight with Naruto during the [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams "Chūnin Exams") [^15] as well as during the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War"), where he deduced that if he fought and gained a name for himself during the war, it would be a step in the direction of becoming the Hokage,[^16] an ideal he mentions again later on.[^17] In his Infinite Tsukuyomi, Kiba dreamed he was Hokage and also desired to have a national holiday for all dogs.

Despite all of Kiba's wildness and impulsiveness, when Kiba sees [Tamaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tamaki "Tamaki"), he is usually left in a love-struck stupor where he freezes and is left at a loss for words. This is then immediately followed by Kiba politely boasting and controlling his wild personality while making connections between the two that usually make no real sense. His love-struck demeanour is enough to make Akamaru and [Ninneko](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ninneko "Ninneko") disgusted and Shino speechless (who sees a "dog-boy" and a "cat-girl" romantically involved).

## Appearance

Kiba's personality and fighting style is further complemented by his wild appearance as, while clearly human, he has several physical traits more akin to animals. Like most of his clan, he has messy brown hair, sharp black eyes with vertical slit-like pupils, pronounced canine teeth, and nails that he can change into claws. He also has the distinctive red fang markings of the Inuzuka clan on his cheeks.

During Part I, Kiba's attire consisted of dark greyish pants reaching to his calves and a grey, hooded fur-lined coat, with the hood usually placed on his head, over an apparent plate of armour and fishnet undershirt seen in his fight with [Sakon and Ukon](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sakon_and_Ukon "Sakon and Ukon"), with a blue [forehead protector](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Forehead_protector "Forehead protector") and blue sandals.

During Part II, each piece of his outfit, including his forehead protector, switched to a black colour, with his coat being replaced by what looks like a nowadays leather form fitting black jacket, with zips over the chest and sleeves, along with black pants and sandals, also zipped, matching the jacket. He, along with the rest of the [Konoha 11](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_11 "Konoha 11"), dons the standard [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure") shinobi outfit during the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War").

Two years after the war, he has grown a goatee and his hair has gotten a bit longer, although less spiky. He wears the new standard flak jacket underneath a open fur coat, and black fingerless gloves. He wears grey pants reaching his calves and new sandals with a slight difference to his previous ones.

As an adult, Kiba no longer wears his forehead protector. His hair has also gotten longer and is less unkempt. He wears a jacket similar to his genin outfit, with a mesh shirt underneath. He wears grey pants reaching down to his calves and regular shinobi sandals.

## Abilities

![Kiba and Akamaru displaying one of their advanced collaboration techniques.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/b2/Double_headed_wolf.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150108115531)

Kiba and Akamaru displaying one of their advanced collaboration techniques.

Kiba's [fighting style](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fighting_style "Fighting style") revolves around collaboration techniques alongside his companion Akamaru, but he can also perfectly synchronise with his teammates, as demonstrated with Naruto during their fight against Tobi. Due to his senses, as well as his partnership with Akamaru, he was the only genin to notice an [Anbu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Anbu "Anbu") stationed in the Chūnin Exams arena.[^18] He also favours using military pills to increase his chakra and that of Akamaru's, as well as smoke bombs, which robs the enemy of their vision, and due to his enhanced smell, has no problem pinpointing them. He could also use the smoke bombs to set up a trap, like creating a fake Akamaru that explodes and releases dozens of kunai, as seen during his fight against Ukon. By adulthood, Kiba is regarded as the closest [Ninken](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ninken "Ninken") -specialist to becoming [Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage "Hokage"),[^19] with his skills being respected since he was in the Academy.[^20]

### Ninjutsu

He can use the [Shadow Clone Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_Clone_Technique "Shadow Clone Technique"),[^21] and an [advanced version](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Combination_Transformation "Combination Transformation") of the [Transformation Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Transformation_Technique "Transformation Technique"), using them to perform his clan's advanced techniques. He is also capable of using [Earth](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Earth "Earth") and [Yang Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yang_Release "Yang Release").[^4]

#### Inuzuka Clan Abilities

![Kiba and Akamaru using Fang Passing Fang.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/a/a1/Fang_passing_fang.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240908045504)

Kiba and Akamaru using Fang Passing Fang.

Kiba fights and attacks with the ferocity of a beast, utilising his sharp claws and other attributes he gains from his [Four Legs Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Four_Legs_Technique "Four Legs Technique"). He can also turn Akamaru into an [identical clone of himself](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Beast_Human_Clone "Beast Human Clone") so they can use the [Fang Passing Fang](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fang_Passing_Fang "Fang Passing Fang"), attacking the target at high-speed with excellent coordination. In dire circumstances, they can transform together into a [two-headed wolf](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Human_Beast_Combination_Transformation:_Double-Headed_Wolf "Human Beast Combination Transformation: Double-Headed Wolf"), and attack by using the [Fang Wolf Fang](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fang_Wolf_Fang "Fang Wolf Fang"), which is almost impossible to avoid, let alone block. By adding a shadow clone, they can transform into a [three-headed wolf](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Human_Beast_Mixture_Transformation_%E2%80%94_Three-Headed_Wolf "Human Beast Mixture Transformation — Three-Headed Wolf") and use the more powerful [Tail Chasing Fang Fang Rotating Fang](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tail_Chasing_Fang_Fang_Rotating_Fang "Tail Chasing Fang Fang Rotating Fang").[^22]

According to the third databook, Kiba learned how to use the Whirlwind Wolf Fang (旋風狼牙, *Senpū Rōga*) between Part I and Part II. Other than giving him the power to protect someone he cares about, it is unknown what it does or even if it is a weapon or a technique.[^3] In [Konoha Hiden](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Hiden:_The_Perfect_Day_for_a_Wedding "Konoha Hiden: The Perfect Day for a Wedding"), Kiba had developed a new powerful jutsu, which he used to destroy one of the meteorites breaking off from the Moon.[^13]

### Enhanced Senses

As an Inuzuka, Kiba has enhanced canine-related senses, his most powerful trait being his sense of smell, surpassing that of a ninja hound.[^23] [^24] By concentrating his chakra to his nose, Kiba can increase his sense of smell a thousand fold,[^25] allowing him to easily distinguish people by their scent. It can clearly determine the physical condition of an individual, even from a considerable distance. Naturally, Kiba is the ideal choice to be included in all reconnaissance and tracking missions, as he can actively keep track of targets, identify traps that others would miss, and detect any nearby enemy shinobi. However, Kiba's powerful sense of smell can't detect stuff like a double-edged sword, as being exposed to strong odours can incapacitate him. This weakness resulted in his defeat by Naruto during the Chūnin Exams after the latter farted in Kiba's face. In his two-headed wolf transformation, his revolution speed is so fast that he cannot see anything, but he can target his enemy with his sense of smell after Akamaru uses Dynamic Marking.[^26]

### Physical Prowess

![Kiba using his clan's fighting style.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/2b/Four_Legs_Technique.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240908045016)

Kiba using his clan's fighting style.

Because of his clan's practised fighting style, Kiba has a quick reaction time, honed reflexes, and great physical strength and speed. After training prior to Part II, his speed became greater than that of a ninken.[^27] He practises his clan's famed "hit-and-run" tactics where he delivers powerful attacks to the opponent, moving at speeds that they can hardly keep up with. In Part II, during his fight against [Tobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tobi "Tobi"), not only was Kiba the only one able to discover where Tobi had teleported to, but fast enough to surprise Tobi and attack him from behind, without anyone noticing.[^28] In the anime, he had shown impressive strength during the Fourth Shinobi World War, being able to take out a [disguised](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Substitute_Technique "Substitute Technique") [White Zetsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/White_Zetsu "White Zetsu") clone with only a few blows, despite the latter being extremely durable.[^29]

## Part I

### Chūnin Exams

Main article: [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams_\(Arc\) "Chūnin Exams (Arc)")

![Team 8 hides from Gaara.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/8b/Team_8_hides_from_gaara.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20250815195030)

Team 8 hides from Gaara.

During the first phase of the [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams "Chūnin Exams"), Kiba used Akamaru to spy on the other examinees' tests and tell him the answers. During the second phase of the exam, while Team 8 easily took an Earth scroll from a rival team, Kiba wished to acquire yet another to narrow the competition, despite Shino and Hinata's reservations. While investigating a confrontation between two teams, they witnessed [Gaara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gaara "Gaara") killing three [Amegakure genin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Shigure "Team Shigure"), with Akamaru being traumatised by the amount of power he sensed from Gaara.

![Kiba and Akamaru vs. Naruto.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/5/5a/Kiba_vs_Naruto.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240908051408)

Kiba and Akamaru vs. Naruto.

Kiba was matched against [Naruto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto "Naruto") in the seventh match of the preliminaries. Kiba thought that he would easily win, since Naruto had been the worst student at the academy. Naruto then became extremely annoyed at Kiba's low opinion of him. While Kiba initially had an advantage, due to his superior speed and strengthening Akamaru with a [Military Rations Pill](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Military_Rations_Pill "Military Rations Pill"), Naruto tricked him into knocking Akamaru unconscious, then, after farting in Kiba's face (overwhelming his sense of smell and disorienting him), defeated him with the [Naruto Uzumaki Combo](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki_Combo "Naruto Uzumaki Combo"). Kiba regained consciousness on the stretcher, and urged Hinata to forfeit if she was matched against Gaara or [Neji Hyūga](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Neji_Hy%C5%ABga "Neji Hyūga").

A month later, at the finals, Kiba watched the match between Naruto and Neji with Hinata. He appeared to have gained respect for Naruto at this point, as, when he heard [Izumo](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Izumo "Izumo") and [Kotetsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kotetsu "Kotetsu") doubting Naruto's abilities to win, Kiba remembered how he had thought the same thing during his own match with Naruto, and had ended up losing. He also loudly cheered for Naruto during the match. After [Kabuto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kabuto "Kabuto") healed Hinata when she began coughing up blood, he rendered Kiba unconscious (in the anime, he was about to recognise him), leaving him unable to assist the Konoha ninja during the invasion.

### Sasuke Recovery Mission

Main article: [Sasuke Recovery Mission](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Recovery_Mission "Sasuke Recovery Mission")

![Kiba and Akamaru attack Sakon and Ukon.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/09/Kiba_and_Akamaru%27s_Dual_wolf_fang.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20250331224853)

Kiba and Akamaru attack Sakon and Ukon.

As a request from [Shikamaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shikamaru "Shikamaru"), Kiba was the only member from his team to participate in the task of stopping [Sasuke Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Uchiha "Sasuke Uchiha") from defecting from Konoha. Kiba was lined in front of the group due to the fact that he knows most of the Land of Fire's terrain and with his sense of smell, he could not only track Sasuke's scent, he could also detect the enemies' scent to avoid any traps in the way. Kiba smelled the [Sound Four](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sound_Four "Sound Four") and proceeded to attack, but they were trapped by [Jirōbō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jir%C5%8Db%C5%8D "Jirōbō"). After escaping, [Neji](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Neji "Neji") and [Chōji](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%8Dji "Chōji") stayed behind fighting their corresponding opponent. Kiba and the others continued their pursuing for Sasuke, and soon they caught up with [Sakon](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sakon "Sakon") and [Tayuya](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tayuya "Tayuya") and engaged them in battle. After Naruto seized the container holding Sasuke, Akamaru set off a bunch of exploding tags, but one with the result of knocking Kiba along with [Sakon and Ukon](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sakon_and_Ukon "Sakon and Ukon") off a cliff. Kiba and Akamaru started fighting them, but when Sakon and Ukon pushed their [cursed seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Cursed_seal "Cursed seal") into level two, their power increased in such a huge way leaving Kiba and Akamaru with the only option to use their new technique, [Human Beast Combination Transformation: Double-Headed Wolf](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Human_Beast_Combination_Transformation:_Double-Headed_Wolf "Human Beast Combination Transformation: Double-Headed Wolf"). Despite using this technique and their [Fang Wolf Fang](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fang_Wolf_Fang "Fang Wolf Fang"), the brothers were able to block their attack with [Summoning: Rashōmon](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning:_Rash%C5%8Dmon "Summoning: Rashōmon").

![Kiba stabs himself to hurt Ukon as well.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/f/f8/Kiba_vs_Ukon.PNG/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150220150259)

Kiba stabs himself to hurt Ukon as well.

With the wolf transformation dispersed, Akamaru was attacked and left heavily injured along with Sakon for the meantime. Ukon merged himself with Kiba's body thanks to his special technique. Not having any options, Kiba stabbed himself in the stomach, causing him and Ukon to start coughing up blood. Kiba found Akamaru and retreated frantically, fearing for their lives, hoping to escape the brothers. Kiba smelled three other individuals, which were later shown to belong to the [Three Sand Siblings](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Three_Sand_Siblings "Three Sand Siblings"). Kiba and Akamaru were saved by [Kankurō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kankur%C5%8D "Kankurō"), who trapped and killed the brothers with his [puppets](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Puppets "Puppets"). Kiba was wounded with severe, but not life-threatening, lacerations, but Akamaru had suffered much harsher damage; tendons in all four of his legs had been ripped and his sister, [Hana Inuzuka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hana_Inuzuka "Hana Inuzuka"), told him that they wouldn't be able to take walks for a while. After Akamaru recovered, Kiba swore to never let anything happen to him again.

Team 8 and Naruto searching for the bikōchū.

![Kiba telling Naruto he intends to find a doll for Tamaki.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/5/57/Kiba_and_Naruto.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20220124041856)

Kiba telling Naruto he intends to find a doll for Tamaki.

Kiba along with Akamaru and [Akemaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akemaru "Akemaru") met Naruto and [Himawari Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Himawari_Uzumaki "Himawari Uzumaki") outside the opening of a toy store where all looked to purchase a limited edition [Kurama](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kurama "Kurama") doll. Kiba hoped to surprise Tamaki with the gift and he joined Naruto in the rush of the store opening but both failed in finding the Kurama doll. Kiba suggested he and Naruto split up and search other stores so they could hopefully find a doll for Himawari. Naruto was unsuccessful in his search, but Kiba succeeded in finding the last toy in a kiosk. They realised the toy was stolen whilst Akamaru was in possession of it, and Naruto gave chase to the culprit who he successfully detained.

### Versus Momoshiki Arc

Main article: [Boruto: Naruto the Movie](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Boruto:_Naruto_the_Movie "Boruto: Naruto the Movie") Kiba later watched the Chūnin Exams in Konoha, beside Tamaki and Akamaru.

### Time Slip Arc

Main article: [Time Slip Arc](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Time_Slip_Arc "Time Slip Arc") In the anime, after Kiba and Shino stopped a shoplifter from escaping, they bumped into Boruto and Sarada, who were hoping to find the [Icha Icha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Icha_Icha "Icha Icha") books by Jiraiya in hopes understand his influence on Naruto. Concerned about such young kids reading such an adult book, the older ninja tried to get them off the topic by telling them from his own experience the kind of man Jiraiya was. When that failed, they simply told the kids that they were too young to read the books.

Later, when [Urashiki Ōtsutsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Urashiki_%C5%8Ctsutsuki "Urashiki Ōtsutsuki") was spotted in the area, Kiba joined several other Konoha-nin in pursuing him. However, this turned out to be diversion by the Ōtsutsuki, who placed a genjutsu on them to let him sneak into the village.

Meanwhile, the grown-up Sasuke and Boruto were sent back in time to shortly after Sasuke's defection. There, the two pretended to be travelling performers and were put under watch by Naruto. During which, past-Kiba and his friends helped Boruto and Naruto clean up a bathhouse after the commotion Naruto and Jiraiya caused.

Days later, [Konohamaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohamaru "Konohamaru"), [Moegi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Moegi "Moegi"), and [Udon](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Udon "Udon") approached Kiba and his friends for help, telling how Naruto and Boruto were battling a [dangerous foe](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Urashiki_%C5%8Ctsutsuki "Urashiki Ōtsutsuki"). They quickly agreed to help. By the time they arrived however, Urashiki was already defeated. The group then escorted their injured friends to the hospital. Days later, after Sasuke and Boruto recovered and prepared to return to their time, Sasuke first used his Sharingan to erase the memories of everyone he and Boruto came in contact with to protect the timeline.

### Kara Actuation Arc

Main article: [Kara Actuation Arc](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kara_Actuation_Arc "Kara Actuation Arc") In the anime, after learning about [Kara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kara "Kara") and how there could be a spy in the village, Naruto met with Kiba and other officials on whether or not they should close the gates to the village. Kiba noted that the train system was an easy way for a spy to move about.

### Ao Arc

Main article: [Ao Arc](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ao_Arc "Ao Arc") In the anime, Kiba attended the memorial service for the fallen people of the Fourth Shinobi World War.

### Kawaki Arc

Main article: [Kawaki Arc](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kawaki_Arc "Kawaki Arc") In the anime, Kiba participated in the coordination efforts lead by Shikamaru in preparation for [Isshiki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Isshiki "Isshiki") 's invasion. He was among the first to fight Isshiki upon his arrival, but was quickly defeated.

## Trivia

| ![Springtime of Youth](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/3/39/Youth_symbol.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/40?cb=20130120020002)  Springtime of Youth | The [*Springtime of Youth* wiki](https://springtimeofyouth.fandom.com/wiki/ "w:c:springtimeofyouth") has an article about this topic:  **[Kiba Inuzuka](https://springtimeofyouth.fandom.com/wiki/Kiba_Inuzuka "w:c:springtimeofyouth:Kiba Inuzuka")** |
| --- | --- |

- The name "Kiba" (牙) means "fang", while his family name "Inuzuka" means "dog hill".
	- His family name is a reference to the dog samurai Inuzuka from [Kyokutei Bakin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyokutei_Bakin "wikipedia:Kyokutei Bakin") 's *[Nansō Satomi Hakkenden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nans%C5%8D_Satomi_Hakkenden "wikipedia:Nansō Satomi Hakkenden")*.
- According to the databook(s):
	- Kiba's hobby is to [take walks with Akamaru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dog_walking "wikipedia:Dog walking"). Kiba also eats with Akamaru, and personally prepares Akamaru's food. He also bathes with Akamaru, and lets Akamaru sleep in his bed with him, and while on missions, Kiba used to let Akamaru sleep in his sleeping bag — these days Kiba will just huddle up with Akamaru and share a blanket with him.
		- Kiba wishes for a rematch with [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki").
		- Kiba's favourite foods are [beef](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beef "wikipedia:Beef"), [steak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steak "wikipedia:Steak"), [jerky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerky "wikipedia:Jerky"), and [cartilage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartilage "wikipedia:Cartilage"), whilst his least favourite foods are anything that is not chewy.
		- Kiba has completed 50 official missions in total: 21 D-rank, 19 C-rank, 8 B-rank, 2 A-rank, 0 S-rank.
		- Kiba's favourite word is " *Akamaru* " (赤丸).
- According to [Hyō no Sho](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hy%C5%8D_no_Sho "Hyō no Sho"), while in the [Academy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Academy "Academy"), Kiba had an A in taijutsu and positivity, a B in ninjutsu, and a C in genjutsu, cooperation, and classroom attitude.
- Kiba was ranked as 15th most popular character in the sixth and most recent *Naruto* character popularity poll. He was 12th in the fifth poll and 17th in the fourth.
- Kiba shares the same birthday with Akamaru, which is also the day when [Tanabata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanabata "wikipedia:Tanabata") starts in Japan.
- While stating that he hasn't noticed the increased size of Akamaru, in an [omake](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Omake#Akamaru "Omake") of the *[Naruto: Shippūden](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto:_Shipp%C5%ABden "Naruto: Shippūden")* [episode 94](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/A_Night_of_Rain "A Night of Rain"), Kiba did admit to noticing Akamaru needing more shampoo for baths, accumulating more loose fur while being brushed, and a painful experience of the larger Akamaru jumping on Kiba's head.
- Kiba was exposed to Naruto's farting three times. First during the Chūnin Exams, then on the mission to find the bikōchū beetle, and third during the Three-Tails' Appearance in Part II.
- On the back of [Hyō no Sho](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hy%C5%8D_no_Sho "Hyō no Sho"), Kiba was seen wearing a light orange jacket and having light orange markings on his cheeks instead of the usual red ones and the grey coloured jacket with black fur.
- Studio Pierrot has consistently given him the height of 168 cm at 16yrs, instead of 169.1cm as his Databook profile gives him.
