**Orochimaru** (大蛇丸) is one of [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure") 's legendary [Sannin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sannin "Sannin"). With a life-ambition to learn all of the world's secrets, Orochimaru seeks immortality so that he might live all of the lives necessary to accomplish his task. After being caught red-handed performing unethical experiments on his fellow citizens for the sake of this immortality, Orochimaru defected from Konoha rather than be persecuted for his ambitions, and for many years sought the village's destruction in order to take revenge and demonstrate what he had learned. After realising the flaws of his approach in life from witnessing it emulated by [Kabuto Yakushi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kabuto_Yakushi "Kabuto Yakushi"), his former associate, Orochimaru began focusing his research on the development of life rather than its manipulation after seeing the actions of his former apprentice, [Sasuke Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Uchiha "Sasuke Uchiha").

## Background

![Orochimaru as a child.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/6/6f/Orochimaru_As_A_Kid.PNG/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20210915212747)

Orochimaru as a child.

Orochimaru was an orphan who became a pupil of [Hiruzen Sarutobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hiruzen_Sarutobi "Hiruzen Sarutobi") alongside [Jiraiya](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jiraiya "Jiraiya") and [Tsunade](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tsunade "Tsunade"). Compared to the more laid back Jiraiya, Orochimaru stood out as a genius — his talents, insight, and determination were considered by Hiruzen to be that of a prodigy seen only once in a generation.[^5] According to Tsunade, Orochimaru had a twisted personality even as a child. His sadistic attitude was presumably due to the death of his parents. At some point after losing them, Orochimaru found a white [snake](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Snake "Snake") near his parents' grave, with Hiruzen's explanation of it representing fortune and rebirth inspiring Orochimaru to study the secrets of life and obtain knowledge of all techniques.[^6] Jiraiya theorised that Orochimaru went down this path in an attempt to forget his painful memories.[^7]

During the [Second Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Shinobi_World_War "Second Shinobi World War"), Orochimaru joined Jiraiya and Tsunade in fighting [Hanzō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hanz%C5%8D "Hanzō"), who gave them the title of Konoha's "Three Legendary Shinobi" (伝説の三忍, *Densetsu no Sannin*) as a reward for surviving their battle with him, while the rest of their group were easily slaughtered in the process.[^8] On their way back to Konoha, they encountered three [Amegakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Amegakure "Amegakure") [orphans](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ame_Orphans "Ame Orphans"), with Orochimaru proposing a mercy killing before Jiraiya chose to train them instead.[^9] [^10] In the anime, Orochimaru orchestrated the last known rampage of the [Eight-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eight-Tails "Eight-Tails") in [Kumogakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kumogakure "Kumogakure") by infiltrating the village while disguised as a Kumo-nin and posing as [Blue B](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Blue_B "Blue B") 's doctor in order to trick him into taking special [genjutsu inducing pills](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genjutsu_Pill "Genjutsu Pill"), allowing him to extract the tailed beast. After its rampage, Orochimaru obtained the severed horn of the Eight-Tails and harvested Blue B's DNA from it.[^11]

Orochimaru would go on to fight in the [Third Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Shinobi_World_War "Third Shinobi World War"), with his performance in the war being so great that he was considered as a candidate for [Fourth Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Hokage "Fourth Hokage") alongside fellow war hero [Minato Namikaze](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Minato_Namikaze "Minato Namikaze").[^12]

![Orochimaru working under Danzō as a Root operative.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/3/37/Kabuto_joins_Root.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20131031122241)

Orochimaru working under Danzō as a Root operative.

Sometime after Team Hiruzen was disbanded, Orochimaru became an [Anbu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Anbu "Anbu") member and joined [Root](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Root "Root") to work directly under [Danzō Shimura](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Danz%C5%8D_Shimura "Danzō Shimura"), while also mentoring future legendary shinobi such as [Anko Mitarashi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Anko_Mitarashi "Anko Mitarashi").[^13] [^14] It was during that time that Orochimaru met Kabuto Yakushi while accompanying Danzō in forcing the boy's guardian, [Nonō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Non%C5%8D "Nonō"), into a mission.[^15] While Orochimaru's ambitions did include becoming the [Fourth Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Hokage "Fourth Hokage"), he intended to use the title for his own agenda.[^16] Though Hiruzen knew the potential evil his former pupil had in him, he hoped to stir Orochimaru from the path before slowly realising that he was beyond anyone's help.[^5] When Hiruzen finally named Minato his successor, Orochimaru saw no more reason to remain in Konoha and began to be less discreet with his actions,[^17] resulting in him beginning to experiment on humans to further his research.[^13] This led to his implanting of [Hashirama Senju](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hashirama_Senju "Hashirama Senju") 's DNA into sixty children he kidnapped to recreate the First's [Wood Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Release "Wood Release"), having help from Danzō.[^18] Believing it to be a failure, Orochimaru was unaware that [one child](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yamato "Yamato") had survived his Wood Release experiment. Orochimaru also learned the [Second Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Hokage "Second Hokage") 's forbidden [Summoning: Impure World Reincarnation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning:_Impure_World_Reincarnation "Summoning: Impure World Reincarnation") during this time.

Around this time, he also met a mysterious young man named [Shin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shin_Uchiha "Shin Uchiha"), taking him in as another subordinate and research subject. With Shin's uniquely adaptive biology, harvesting much of his anatomy, Orochimaru was able to unlock many great truths of genetics and further his own biological development for general enhancements and new methods of [cloning](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shin_Uchiha_\(Clones\) "Shin Uchiha (Clones)"). This also refined his Wood Release researched enough to inject Hashirama's cells into the [Sharingan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sharingan "Sharingan") -infused right arm of Shin, which he transplanted to Danzō.[^19] [^20] [^21] Sometime after, Shin betrayed Orochimaru and left with almost all the independent clones of Shin that Orochimaru made.

Orochimaru performed various experiments on his prisoners, some being fellow Konoha shinobi he kidnapped. He used them as human guinea pigs to develop techniques that would grant him immortality, the end result being [Living Corpse Reincarnation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Living_Corpse_Reincarnation "Living Corpse Reincarnation"). Some time later, Orochimaru was at the outskirts of [Kirigakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kirigakure "Kirigakure") as he found [Kimimaro](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kimimaro "Kimimaro"), the sole survivor of the [Kaguya clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kaguya_clan "Kaguya clan"). Soon after, thanks in part to Kimimaro, Orochimaru's research on the source of [Jūgo](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/J%C5%ABgo "Jūgo") 's powers led him to find the [Ryūchi Cave](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ry%C5%ABchi_Cave "Ryūchi Cave") and master [senjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Senjutsu "Senjutsu").[^22] Unable to enter [Sage Mode](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sage_Mode "Sage Mode") because of the fragility of his host body, Orochimaru instead developed an alternate method of harnessing his senjutsu chakra through [cursed seals](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Orochimaru%27s_Juinjutsu "Orochimaru's Juinjutsu"), which he tested on Anko and several others before eventually using the finalised versions on Kimimaro and the [Sound Four](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sound_Four "Sound Four"). In the anime, Orochimaru found the [Iburi clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Iburi_clan "Iburi clan") living underground near Konoha. Taking an interest in their ability to [become smoke](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Iburi_Clan%27s_Kekkei_Genkai "Iburi Clan's Kekkei Genkai"), Orochimaru took advantage of the clan's fear of their imperfect and potentially fatal transformation by partially stabilising it with his cursed seals. Telling them that he needed more research to perfect the process, the clan willingly gave Orochimaru test subjects.[^23] In the anime, Orochimaru witnessed [Itachi Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Itachi_Uchiha "Itachi Uchiha") performing a training run normally meant for older and more experienced users due to the danger of it. After the young Uchiha flawlessly completed it, Orochimaru instantly became amazed and excited at Itachi's advanced growth rate. In the anime, he also allied himself with [Amachi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Amachi "Amachi") out of interest for creating [a shinobi capable of underwater combat](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kaima_Form "Kaima Form"), kidnapping [Land of the Sea](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_the_Sea "Land of the Sea") villagers for their experiments, while introducing Anko to the project. Sometime after, Orochimaru lost interest in the project, and ordered Amachi to cancel it, while also seeing Anko as a lost cause for her refusal to use the cursed seal, and wiped her memories as she left him.[^24]

Orochimaru was later sent on a mission by Danzō to [Iwagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Iwagakure "Iwagakure") to ensure that both Kabuto and Nonō, the former having joined Root as a spy, killed each other as they had become major liabilities. Orochimaru instead brought Kabuto to his [hideout](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Orochimaru%27s_Hideouts#Island_Laboratory "Orochimaru's Hideouts"), which would become part of his personal hidden village, [Otogakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Otogakure "Otogakure"), and revealed Root's intent for Kabuto's life and the role he was to play in it. Though Orochimaru revealed that he was also sent to kill the one who survived this ordeal, he told Kabuto that he saw a bit of himself in the boy and decided killing him would be a waste of great talent. Therefore, Orochimaru offered Kabuto a position as his right hand man and spy with the promise of an identity.[^25]

![Hiruzen discovers Orochimaru's criminal activities.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/5/55/Orochimaru_Caught.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150303185807)

As his test subjects began to die off, Orochimaru was caught red-handed by Hiruzen. Though he knew it was his duty, Hiruzen lacked the will to kill his student, allowing him to escape.[^26] In the anime, during his escape, Orochimaru was found by [Kakashi Hatake](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi_Hatake "Kakashi Hatake"). Despite the young prodigy's best efforts, Orochimaru swiftly defeated him. Letting his guard down at the approach of a snake, Orochimaru was greatly injured by an [explosive tag](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Explosive_tag "Explosive tag") concealed in the snake's mouth. Cursing Hiruzen for ruining his plans, though greatly injured, Orochimaru quickly regained his composure. He then resumed his retreat from the country, leaving a terrified Kakashi frozen in place from his [malicious glare](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Killing_Intent "Killing Intent") alone.[^23] Seriously wounded, Orochimaru visited the cave where the Iburi clan lived and killed them all, trying to restore his power. Upon the arrival of the youngest member, [Yukimi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yukimi "Yukimi"), Orochimaru pursued her while being chased by Kakashi Hatake and [Kinoe](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kinoe "Kinoe"). Eventually, he managed to take a sample of Yukimi's blood, intending to inject it into himself to temporarily gain her ability and slip across the border, but he was attacked before he could do so and quickly escaped.[^27] Orochimaru managed to destroy most of his research facilities and booby-trap the rest.[^28]

![Orochimaru attacks Itachi.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/7/7f/Orochimaru_attacks_Itachi.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150131224153)

Orochimaru attacks Itachi.

Orochimaru ultimately escaped Konoha after fighting Jiraiya, who attempted to reason with him one final time to no avail, leaving his emotionally broken mentor, and former friend's plea for him to reconsider falling on deaf ears.[^29]. Orochimaru soon became affiliated with and joined the elite criminal organisation [Akatsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki "Akatsuki"). Orochimaru was then partnered with [Sasori](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasori "Sasori"), with whom he did much good for the organisation.[^30] He later watched in amazement when Itachi Uchiha joined the organisation. Later, when Orochimaru saw his opportunity to steal his body to gain possession of the Sharingan after gaining intel on him through Sasori via Kabuto,[^13] Itachi used his Sharingan to stop him from doing so, while also cutting Orochimaru's left hand off to prevent him from breaking the [genjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Illusion:_Shackling_Stakes "Demonic Illusion: Shackling Stakes") he was under, which lead to him leaving Akatsuki to acquire a new body.[^31] For Orochimaru's defection, Sasori developed a grudge against him and used Kabuto, whom Orochimaru had sent to serve as his spy within Akatsuki, to counter spy as his [sleeper agent](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Memory-Concealing_Manipulative_Sand_Technique "Memory-Concealing Manipulative Sand Technique"). However, Orochimaru discovered and undid the technique Sasori had used on him.[^32] In the anime, when tipped off by Kabuto that Sasori was on his trail, Orochimaru chose to use this as an opportunity to test his development of the Summoning: Impure World Reincarnation. Against Sasori's [Third Kazekage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Kazekage_\(puppet\) "Third Kazekage (puppet)") [puppet](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Puppet "Puppet"), Orochimaru summoned the actual [Third Kazekage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Kazekage "Third Kazekage") through his technique. While proving his reincarnated ninja being stronger than the [human puppet](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Human_puppet "Human puppet"), the technique ultimately proved incomplete as the Third Kazekage was able to regain control and break the summoning contract.[^33]

After leaving the Akatsuki, Orochimaru continued to build up his network of hideouts, eventually culminating in the formation of [Otogakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Otogakure "Otogakure") which greatly grew in influence after just a few years.[^13] In the anime, Orochimaru had wormed his influence into the **Land of Rice Fields** (田の国, *Ta no Kuni*, *English TV:* Land of Rice Paddies) and convinced its [daimyō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sound_Daimy%C5%8D "Sound Daimyō") into establishing his own hidden village at the newly rechristened [Land of Sound](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_Sound "Land of Sound").[^34] Taking advantage of the country's many local shinobi [clans](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Clans "Clans") having suffered from war, many survivors joined Orochimaru's cause, being lured by his power and the promise to help revive their clans, including [Arashi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Arashi "Arashi") 's faction of the [Fūma Clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/F%C5%ABma_Clan_\(Land_of_Sound\) "Fūma Clan (Land of Sound)").[^35]

Orochimaru had also been on a life-long search for the [Sword of Totsuka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sword_of_Totsuka "Sword of Totsuka"), an ethereal sword, but was unaware that it was in the possession of Itachi's [Susanoo](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Susanoo "Susanoo").[^36] In the anime, Orochimaru witnessed [Mizuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mizuki "Mizuki") killing a comrade during a mission. Intrigued by Mizuki's view of doing what was needed for a mission, under the pretence as a gift for Mizuki's bold actions, Orochimaru granted him a [specialised cursed seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Animal_Cursed_Seal "Animal Cursed Seal"). In truth however, it was an incomplete experimental tool that would destroy whoever used it, to which Orochimaru was merely curious as to how the effects would happen on Mizuki.[^37]

## Personality

![Orochimaru upset over Tsunade's loss.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/7/73/Orochimaru_cries.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230517171800)

Orochimaru upset over Tsunade's loss.

Orochimaru possesses a complex personality; however, more often than not, he was described as "twisted" by a large number of people, including Tsunade. Jiraiya's speculation that the death of his parents while he was young scarred him, along with his sympathy for Tsunade losing [Dan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Dan "Dan") in the anime, implies that Orochimaru grew to disdain the fragility of human life and how it affected those still living, which led him to desire immortality. To that end, the primary purpose of Orochimaru's human experimentation is to test what modifications the human body can endure and harness a subject's unique abilities for himself. Once accomplishing this goal, Orochimaru had hoped to be worthy of the title of " *ultimate being* ", unable to die and able to achieve his secondary goal to learn every ninja technique in the world, which would normally take many lifetimes to obtain.

![Orochimaru's intimidating behaviour.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/0b/Orochimaru_Intimidating.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150927095249)

Orochimaru's intimidating behaviour.

According to Sasuke, becoming cruel with any good he had as a member of Team Hiruzen long gone, Orochimaru's agenda made him develop a [god complex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/God_complex "wikipedia:God complex") and highly value himself while delighting in striking terror in his first impressions. Though causing conflict to ease his boredom, Orochimaru seems to have no interest in wars started by others.[^38] He is also somewhat of a perfectionist, always striving to make his various projects succeed, and likewise looks down on those who are sloppy and/or reckless in their own projects.[^39] His charisma also attracts a number of followers, many of which he would not hesitate to send on potential suicide missions if for his own personal benefit, expressing some remorse if they're unable to properly complete a task, and having no sentimental attachments to any of them despite their value to his goals. Despite this, Orochimaru has genuine interest in the development of his underlings and experiments. Likewise, Orochimaru is treacherous by nature, unhesitant in double-crossing any allies if it would benefit him, having assassinated the [Fourth Kazekage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Kazekage "Fourth Kazekage") to impersonate him so as to making it easier to manipulate [Sunagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sunagakure "Sunagakure"), while using [Akatsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki "Akatsuki") to help further his own goals and abandoning them once he failed to take [Itachi Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Itachi_Uchiha "Itachi Uchiha") 's body.

Taking Sasuke as an apprentice, and seeing him as a prodigy like himself, Orochimaru intended to take the youth's body to obtain the Sharingan to reach another step in his goal of immortality and learning every technique in the world. This goal has developed to the point of obsession. However, despite knowing that Sasuke was willing to do anything for revenge, including sacrificing his own body, Orochimaru did anticipate treachery from his disciple and showed only a small level of surprise when it actually occurred. What truly surprised Orochimaru was Sasuke's decision to follow his own path and his eventual decision to defend the village he initially swore to destroy, one that Orochimaru openly approved of and was curious to see what would be the end result of.

He also had an initial disdain toward [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki"), believing him to be a poor excuse for a ninja until he defeated Kabuto with the Rasengan. Despite attempting to kill Naruto at the time after coming to the realisation that he would become a threat to him in the future, Orochimaru became interested in Naruto's development, provoking Naruto during their battle to draw out his full abilities at the time. During the [war](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War") seeing Naruto's indomitable willpower and his memories with Jiraiya caused Orochimaru to begin to respect him. In the anime, he was one of people who congratulated Naruto and Hinata on their wedding.[^40] Though coming to hate his home village, orchestrating a few attempts to destroy it, Orochimaru despised his former Akatsuki colleagues more. Throughout Part II, if Orochimaru was not busy with Sasuke's development, he would try to cripple Akatsuki's operations, as seen in the "Tenchi Bridge Reconnaissance Mission" arc and "Three-Tails' Appearance" arc. He expresses delight over the death of Sasori and later convinced Sasuke against killing Team Kakashi so they would thin out Akatsuki's membership.

An aspect of Orochimaru's personality is also his greatest weakness: his arrogance. He truly believed he was immortal at times, attacking recklessly against powerful opponents such as his former teacher and Itachi Uchiha. Both times, he was left crippled in some way since he either wanted to make his opponent suffer, like against Hiruzen, or because he was too uninformed to face his opponent, like against Itachi. Despite his distinct lack of morals, Orochimaru seems to be well-versed in psychological warfare and displays insightful wisdom when disparaging Tobirama for the actions resulting from his policies. He is also amusingly afraid of insects and bugs as seen during Naruto and Hinata's wedding.

After being revived by Sasuke, his personality changed a bit. While still as self-serving as ever, he also realised that copying someone completely was a mistake from his observation of Kabuto so utterly emulating him. At the same time, he displayed interest in Sasuke's "different path". Intrigued by Sasuke's pursuit of independence despite all he learned from the Sannin and wanting to see where it would lead, Orochimaru chose to fight alongside the Uchiha instead of ignoring Madara's war like he planned, and even made it a priority to heal Tsunade and the other Kage. Orochimaru also seems to genuinely care about Sasuke's wellbeing, as he originally thought it was best to take his time figuring out how to battle Tobi but quickly decided against taking his time when Karin sensed Sasuke was on the verge of death. Orochimaru has also shown signs of regret, seemingly saddened by his childhood friend Jiraiya's death.

Years later, while still as driven as ever to uncover all the secrets of life, he apparently now does so in a relatively more humane manner, including reforming his personal shinobi village and likewise "cleaning up" the damage he caused to society. Also, while his relationship with the rest of the world is still dubious at best, he has at least developed a relatively trusting relationship between the higher-ups of Konohagakure, and was likewise able to have a "casual" parent-teacher conference about Mitsuki. He is willingly aiding Konoha in research on pressing matters and likewise feels responsible enough to make amends for past mistakes.[^41] He apparently is on amicable terms with Konoha by sending his son, [Mitsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mitsuki "Mitsuki"), to the village. In the anime, he also feels indebted to Naruto as the Hokage for watching over Mitsuki.[^42] Ultimately, a key change in his nature comes from wanting to better understand the natural development of life, as Orochimaru wishes for Mitsuki to make his own decisions. He admits to having high hopes for the boy's natural growth, though he at times used manipulation and deception to put him in a situation where important decisions must be made. He also shows some interest in the respective children of Naruto and Sasuke, particularly intrigued by Boruto sharing the same unwavering spirit and loyalty as his father. These changes have made even the [White Snake Sage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/White_Snake_Sage "White Snake Sage") view Orochimaru as going "soft".

## Appearance

Orochimaru's most conspicuous characteristic is his snake-like appearance: he has very pale skin, golden eyes with slitted pupils, purple markings around his eyes and fang-like teeth. He also has pronounced cheekbones and straight waist-length black hair with some locks covering and framing his face or to his shoulders. The various bodies he takes over have each been modified to resemble his original form, except for the height differences. In *Naruto Gaiden*, Orochimaru's facial features soften and somewhat androgynous with his eye markings becoming black. He also wears his hair in a ponytail, allowing two locks to frame the sides of his face. In the manga, his skin-tone becomes noticeably fairer in complexion.[^43]

Orochimaru usually wears plain beige garbs with a black polo and pants underneath, a thick purple rope belt tied in a large knot behind his back, blue [tomoe](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tomoe "Tomoe") -shaped earrings, and shinobi sandals with bandages around his calves. He is seen wearing a kimono with different colours on several occasions, shown usually in flashbacks or inside his hideouts. During his time with [Akatsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki "Akatsuki"), he wore the standard red and black Akatsuki robes with a slate blue ring, which bears the kanji for "sky" or "void" (空, *kū*), on his left pinky finger. After countless experiments with his body, his true form became a giant white snake made up of many smaller snakes, with longer, spikier hair, a scaled face with snake-like teeth and a long tongue, black eye markings, and a pointed chin. Years later, he wears a white robe tied at his waist by a dark-coloured sash and a purple rope, blue pants and shirt.

## Abilities

![Orochimaru using his most powerful technique, the Eight Branches Technique.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/23/Eight_Branches_Technique.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20220202130714)

Orochimaru using his most powerful technique, the Eight Branches Technique.

Orochimaru is an exceptional shinobi, recognised as one of the strongest to ever exist. One of the only people who could compete against [Hanzō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hanz%C5%8D "Hanzō") in his prime, he became a [Sannin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sannin "Sannin").[^44] At the same time, amongst his team, Orochimaru stood out the most in sheer aptitude as a natural prodigy and the best of his generation,[^45] earning praise from even [Hashirama Senju](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hashirama_Senju "Hashirama Senju").[^46] Easily [Kage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kage "Kage") -calibre, Orochimaru was once a candidate to be Fourth Hokage. His power is feared throughout the world, such that [Akatsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki "Akatsuki"), which he was once a member, saw him as a threat.[^47] Following the [Konoha invasion](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Crush "Konoha Crush"), while failing, Orochimaru's actions left the village crippled for years and the Third Hokage dead. Even crippled and gradually weakening, Orochimaru remained extremely formidable, able to compete against either of his fellow Sannin (albeit with Tsunade being mentally hampered by her fear of blood and Jiraiya unable to properly infuse his chakra) [^48] and even toy with a [Version 2](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Version_2 "Version 2") [jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki "Jinchūriki") respectively.[^49] He would later regain his original strength upon [undoing](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shinigami_Mask "Shinigami Mask") the [seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Dead_Demon_Consuming_Seal "Dead Demon Consuming Seal") that prevented him access to his arms.[^50] In the anime, he easily overwhelmed an [Inner](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Victor "Victor") of [Kara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kara "Kara"), despite their monstrous might and [Shinobi-Ware](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shinobi-Ware "Shinobi-Ware") modifications.[^51]

### Chakra and Physical Prowess

![Orochimaru's killing intent was powerful enough to paralyze Kakashi with fear.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/28/Kakashi_being_afraid_of_Orochimaru.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140309151141)

Orochimaru's killing intent was powerful enough to paralyze Kakashi with fear.

Orochimaru has large reserves of strong chakra, powerful enough to stir up winds and crack stone.[^52] [^53] Likewise, the intensity of his [killing intent](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Killing_intent "Killing intent") can reduce most foes petrified in fear. He can [absorb chakra](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_Absorption_Technique "Chakra Absorption Technique") through physical contact, which also grants him their skills and knowledge.[^54] His chakra control is very advanced, excelling in various chakra applications. Even with his hands [sealed](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Dead_Demon_Consuming_Seal "Dead Demon Consuming Seal"), leaving him unable to perform handsigns, he can use various powerful techniques with ease. Orochimaru is very adept in taijutsu, able to overwhelm Sasuke in Part I despite his Sharingan-enhanced clarity. He is very strong, able to knock down a tree with a single kick,[^55] and temporarily stunned a Four-Tailed Naruto with his punch.[^49] He is also blazingly fast, able to move with such sudden change in direction to leave his opponent's disoriented.

#### Body Modifications

In his quest for immortality and the perfect body, Orochimaru gained numerous modifications over the years. He can heal from most damage in short periods of time. For more grievous injuries, like bisection, he can [reconnect himself](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Snake_Reconnection_Technique "Snake Reconnection Technique") or [regurgitate a new, fully-healed body](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Orochimaru-Style_Body_Replacement_Technique "Orochimaru-Style Body Replacement Technique"). Orochimaru's body is also able to [stretch and manipulate its form](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Soft_Physique_Modification "Soft Physique Modification") for greater manoeuvrability. His durability allows him to endure being stabbed by the giant Sword of Totsuka and shrug the damage off, and even resist multiple solidly-landed [enhanced](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_Enhanced_Strength "Chakra Enhanced Strength") punches from the already-super strong [Tsunade](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tsunade "Tsunade") essentially unharmed.[^56]

![Orochimaru's true form after many experiments.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/06/Orochimaru%27s_True_Form.PNG/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240615174452)

Orochimaru's true form after many experiments.

Orochimaru's modifications have resulted in his true form becoming a [giant white snake](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Power_of_the_White_Snake "Power of the White Snake") and a body composed of many smaller snakes that can attack. Its blood is poisonous, evaporating in the air to paralyse all who inhales it. This form is used for the [Living Corpse Reincarnation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Living_Corpse_Reincarnation "Living Corpse Reincarnation"), letting him transfer his [soul](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") and physical essence into a new body by swallowing that person whole, thereby gaining control of all the new body's abilities while retaining his previous abilities and extending his lifespan. This can only be used once every three years. The souls of the bodies he has taken remain in Orochimaru's subconscious and he can modify his body as he sees fit.

After Orochimaru physically died, as [Kabuto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kabuto "Kabuto") heavily modified his body with various genetic sources, including Orochimaru himself, Sasuke used some of Kabuto's flesh as a basis to reconstitute Orochimaru.[^57] This gave Orochimaru the added benefit of heightened chakra reserves, access to Kabuto’s medical ninjutsu capabilities, as well as the other various techniques and abilities gained from the genetic sources of [Taka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Taka "Taka") and the [Sound Five](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sound_Five "Sound Five"). He also later gained the abilities of a [Hashirama cell](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hashirama%27s_Artificial_Body "Hashirama's Artificial Body") -enhanced [White Zetsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Parasite_Clone "Parasite Clone") after taking over its body; this included further enhanced power,[^58] [accelerated healing](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Regeneration_Ability "Regeneration Ability"), [perfect shapeshifting into others](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Substitute_Technique "Substitute Technique"), and no need of nourishment.[^59] Some point after the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War"), Orochimaru's body became able to [instantly regenerate](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Self-Healing_Technique "Self-Healing Technique") his lost anatomy unfazed.[^51]

### Ninjutsu

Orochimaru's depth of knowledge and skill in ninjutsu is greater than any other shinobi in history,[^60] leading to him wielding a vast and unmatched arsenal of styles and techniques. His proficiency with the [Transformation Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Transformation_Technique "Transformation Technique") can let him stay undetected for long periods; his [fūinjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/F%C5%ABinjutsu "Fūinjutsu") skill includes the ability to use the [Five Elements Seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Five_Elements_Seal "Five Elements Seal") to seal off a person's chakra; he can summon [Rashōmon](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning:_Rash%C5%8Dmon "Summoning: Rashōmon") [gates](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning:_Triple_Rash%C5%8Dmon "Summoning: Triple Rashōmon") to protect himself from enemy attacks; he can [hide within surfaces](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hiding_in_Surface_Technique "Hiding in Surface Technique") to avoid damage, or use [juinjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Juinjutsu "Juinjutsu") to temporarily paralyse targets.[^61] The anime shows Orochimaru's advanced prowess in [clone techniques](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_techniques "Clone techniques"),[^62] [medical ninjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Medical_ninjutsu "Medical ninjutsu"),[^63] [space–time ninjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Space%E2%80%93time_ninjutsu "Space–time ninjutsu"),[^64] and [barrier ninjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Barrier_ninjutsu "Barrier ninjutsu").[^51]

Orochimaru is masterful in [wielding](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kenjutsu "Kenjutsu") the legendary [Sword of Kusanagi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sword_of_Kusanagi_\(Orochimaru\) "Sword of Kusanagi (Orochimaru)"), able to compete against his former teacher while wielding his [mighty bo-staff](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Monkey_King:_Enma "Monkey King: Enma"). The blade is able to extend great distances, cut through almost anything, and even be [remotely controlled](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sword_of_Kusanagi:_Longsword_of_the_Sky "Sword of Kusanagi: Longsword of the Sky"). He normally keeps the blade within a snake's mouth inside his own throat and retrieves it by having it turn into a snake after use.

#### Nature Transformation

![Orochimaru using Wind Release.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/5/5c/Orochimaru%27s_Wind_Release.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20201122151820)

Orochimaru using Wind Release.

Orochimaru can use all five basic [nature transformations](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nature_transformation "Nature transformation"), along with [Yin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yin "Yin") and [Yang Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yang_Release "Yang Release").[^4] With [Wind Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wind_Release "Wind Release"), he can create a powerful [gust of wind](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wind_Release:_Great_Breakthrough "Wind Release: Great Breakthrough") and with [Earth Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Earth_Release "Earth Release") can [travel underground](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Earth_Release:_Hiding_Like_a_Mole_Technique "Earth Release: Hiding Like a Mole Technique"). In the anime, he can create [mud-based clones](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Earth_Release:_Shadow_Clone "Earth Release: Shadow Clone").[^65] With [Fire Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fire_Release "Fire Release") he can easily unleash powerful fire streams. After taking over a White Zetsu body, modified with Hashirama's DNA, Orochimaru gained access to the latter's [Wood Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Release "Wood Release") — a simultaneous mixture of earth and [water](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water "Water").[^59]

#### Summoning

![Orochimaru summons Manda.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/e/e0/Manda_Summoned.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150131221107)

Orochimaru summons Manda.

Orochimaru has an affinity for snakes, using the unique arm tattoo to quickly [summon](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning_Technique "Summoning Technique") them for assistance, his largest and strongest being [Manda](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Manda "Manda"). He can eject snakes from [his sleeves](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hidden_Shadow_Snake_Hands "Hidden Shadow Snake Hands") or [mouth](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Formation_of_Ten_Thousand_Snakes "Formation of Ten Thousand Snakes") to strike with; the snakes can sprout blades from their own mouths for deadlier effect.[^66] He can even turn his lower body into a [snake's tail](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Slithering_Snake_Mode "Slithering Snake Mode"), increasing his speed and mobility in battle.[^67] Orochimaru's ultimate snake-related technique is the [Eight Branches Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eight_Branches_Technique "Eight Branches Technique"), where he transforms into an eight-headed, eight-tailed giant serpent with size superior to that of Manda.

![Orochimaru reincarnates the deceased Hokage.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/03/Hokage_reincarnated.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140612141309)

Orochimaru reincarnates the deceased Hokage.

Orochimaru can perform the [Summoning: Impure World Reincarnation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning:_Impure_World_Reincarnation "Summoning: Impure World Reincarnation"), having further refined the [kinjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kinjutsu "Kinjutsu") developed by [Tobirama Senju](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tobirama_Senju "Tobirama Senju"). Upon reviving the dead, he can implant them with special tags that erase their personalities and/or free will, granting him complete control over their actions. Originally, despite improving this technique's quality compared to its creator, Orochimaru's reincarnated soldiers were still considerably diminished in overall capacities compared to when alive. During the Fourth Shinobi World War, after Orochimaru's own capacities and knowledge of the technique were further augmented from Kabuto's essence, his prowess was also increased, as he revived the first four Hokage with nearly their full power and could use Hashirama's cells to strengthen his control.[^68]

### Senjutsu

![Orochimaru's juinjutsu.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/be/Orochimaru%27s_juinjutsu.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160526024718)

Orochimaru's juinjutsu.

During his research of [Jūgo's clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/J%C5%ABgo%27s_clan "Jūgo's clan"), Orochimaru eventually tracked down the power source at the [Ryūchi Cave](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ry%C5%ABchi_Cave "Ryūchi Cave") and [senjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Senjutsu "Senjutsu"). Although his host body was not strong enough to enter [Sage Mode](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sage_Mode "Sage Mode"),[^22] Orochimaru developed another use for senjutsu chakra: his [cursed seals](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Orochimaru%27s_Juinjutsu "Orochimaru's Juinjutsu").[^69] These seals contain some of Orochimaru's senjutsu chakra mixed with the enzyme produced by Jūgo, triggering a change similar to [Sage Transformation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sage_Transformation "Sage Transformation"), but with different side-effects. These cursed seals also enhance the power of one's techniques,[^70] and serve as an extension of and anchor to Orochimaru's consciousness, allowing him to observe events through anyone branded by the seal. He can even be revived any number of times from a cursed seal by using the [Evil Releasing Method](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Evil_Releasing_Method "Evil Releasing Method"), provided there is a proper DNA source as a basis.[^69] He also created the [Mind Awakening Pill](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_Awakening_Pill "Mind Awakening Pill") to help speed up the cursed seal's progression.

### Intelligence

![Orochimaru performing an experiment to study jutsu.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/27/Orochimaru%27s_experimentation.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150222111915)

Orochimaru performing an experiment to study jutsu.

Orochimaru has acquired a vast amount of information of the shinobi world from his time with organisations such as [Root](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Root "Root") and [Akatsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki "Akatsuki"), having set up a network of fully supplied hideouts throughout various countries. He has great charisma, enough to manipulate people into becoming his willing servants, providing insight into their past and promising them power in order to gain their loyalty. In battle, he can quickly deduce the workings of an enemy's fighting style and respond accordingly.

Wanting to decipher all of the world's secrets, Orochimaru has conducted extensive research and numerous experiments on himself and the DNA of other individuals to comprehend, attain, or reproduce their abilities. Research of abilities such as [Hashirama Senju](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hashirama_Senju "Hashirama Senju") 's [Wood Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Release "Wood Release") and [Jūgo](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/J%C5%ABgo "Jūgo") 's [Sage Transformation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sage_Transformation "Sage Transformation") have allowed him to recreate those same abilities, or at least produce [techniques](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Orochimaru%27s_Juinjutsu "Orochimaru's Juinjutsu") based off of them, into [other](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yamato "Yamato") [people](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Danz%C5%8D_Shimura "Danzō Shimura").[^71] [^69] From experimentation on [Shin Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shin_Uchiha "Shin Uchiha"), Orochimaru has become more knowledgeable of human genetics, facilitating his overall research in developing perfect [clones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_cloning "wikipedia:Human cloning") of himself, which he considers to be of a higher class than the [Shadow Clone Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_Clone_Technique "Shadow Clone Technique") as they cannot be dispersed.[^72] This research eventually resulted in genetically perfect [synthetic](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mitsuki "Mitsuki") [humans](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Log "Log").[^73] Designed to be stronger than normal humans, these synthetic humans were produced with unique capabilities: heightened immune systems,[^74] accelerated healing [^75] and regeneration,[^51] self-sustaining,[^76] infrared ray detection, low-frequency hearing,[^77] and animal communication.[^78] He has also devised a [juinjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Juinjutsu "Juinjutsu") in order to conceal the secrets of their composition.[^79]

In the anime, based on his research of the [Demonic Statue of the Outer Path](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Statue_of_the_Outer_Path "Demonic Statue of the Outer Path"), Orochimaru designed build a [sophisticated robot](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mecha-Naruto "Mecha-Naruto") with advanced weaponry and the ability to consume tailed beast chakra.[^80] He could also create [genjutsu pills](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genjutsu_Pill "Genjutsu Pill") that can subdue a jinchūriki and their tailed beast simultaneously, and even cultivate cells of the tailed beast to create pseudo-jinchūriki.[^11]

## Part I

### Chūnin Exams

Main article: [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams_\(Arc\) "Chūnin Exams (Arc)")

![Orochimaru branding Sasuke.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/5/56/Sasuke_receives_curse_seal.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150125204346)

Orochimaru branding Sasuke.

For a while, since leaving Konoha, Orochimaru left his hometown alone until the chance to destroy it presented itself. The opportunity presented itself when he forms a partnership with the [Fourth Kazekage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Kazekage "Fourth Kazekage") of [Sunagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sunagakure "Sunagakure"), who desired Konoha's destruction like him, planning their invasion during the [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams "Chūnin Exams"). Orochimaru also used the invasion as an opportunity to test [Sasuke Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Uchiha "Sasuke Uchiha"), Itachi's younger brother, if he can be of use as a vessel. Prior to the commencement of the second part of the Chūnin Exams, Orochimaru murdered a team of [Kusagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kusagakure "Kusagakure") genin to impersonate its leader [Shiore](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shiore "Shiore") and infiltrate the [Forest of Death](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Forest_of_Death "Forest of Death"). Using a snake summon to separate Naruto from [Team 7](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Kakashi "Team Kakashi"), the disguised Orochimaru subjected Sasuke to his [Killing Intent](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Killing_Intent "Killing Intent") with the youth unable to fight due to the fear instilled into him. However, the fear subsided with Naruto's timely appearance and convincing Sasuke to muster his courage before Orochimaru interferes with the [jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki "Jinchūriki") 's seal while knocking him out for the duration of the fight. After being subjected to Sasuke's [Dragon Fire Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Dragon_Fire_Technique "Dragon Fire Technique") as it partially burned off his disguise, Orochimaru expresses pride in Sasuke's abilities before branding his target with his [Cursed Seal of Heaven](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Cursed_Seal_of_Heaven "Cursed Seal of Heaven") and taking his leave.

As he sends Team Dosu as sacrificial lambs to test Sasuke, having Kabuto accompany Team 7 to witness Sasuke use the curse seal's power, Orochimaru is confronted by Anko as he tells her that the Konoha will continue the exams under threat of his retaliation if they cancel it. During the preliminary rounds of the Chūnin Exams, Orochimaru disguised himself as the leader of his genin team to watch Sasuke's performance against [Yoroi Akadō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yoroi_Akad%C5%8D "Yoroi Akadō") and more obsessive upon seeing him suppress the Cursed Seal with sheer will. After [Kakashi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi "Kakashi") was able to seal away the mark on Sasuke, Orochimaru confronted him. He made no attempt to hide his envy of Kakashi's Sharingan, and noted before leaving that Sasuke will eventually come to him seeking power.

### Search for Tsunade

Main article: [Search for Tsunade](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Search_for_Tsunade "Search for Tsunade")

## Influence

Orochimaru apparently kept tabs on [Akatsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki "Akatsuki"), as Kabuto was knowledgeable about [Zetsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Zetsu "Zetsu"), and Tobi's machination to revive the [Ten-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ten-Tails "Ten-Tails").[^87] Even when removed from reality by Itachi, being an extensive researcher, the notes he left behind were still coveted as vast stores of knowledge to those fortunate enough to come across them, as seen when one of his hidden rooms is found by Suigetsu and Jūgo, the former of which finds information he believes can impact the war.[^88]

In [the anime](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Power_\(Arc\) "Power (Arc)"), Kabuto used Orochimaru's cells in order to create [special snakes](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_Snakes "Clone Snakes") which could multiply at a rapid rate and merge into either [a clone of another living being using a genetic sample](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Body_Recreation_Technique "Body Recreation Technique") or [a larger serpent](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_Snakes:_Serpent_Technique "Clone Snakes: Serpent Technique") for combat. The clone snakes are first used to create a clone of [Hidan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hidan "Hidan"), and then later [a clone of Naruto with Kurama's chakra](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tailed_Naruto_Clone "Nine-Tailed Naruto Clone").

In his absence, Kabuto used Orochimaru's remains from his battle with Sasuke to integrate his master's DNA into himself in order to surpass him.[^89] Having also assimilated the DNA of [Sakon and Ukon](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sakon_and_Ukon "Sakon and Ukon") to procure their abilities, Kabuto uses the twins' [Attack of the Twin Demons](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Attack_of_the_Twin_Demons "Attack of the Twin Demons") to produce a construct of Orochimaru that transforms into his White Snake form and lunges at an opponent. However, Orochimaru lived on, in a sense, through [cursed seals](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Cursed_seal "Cursed seal") and Kabuto after the latter integrated Orochimaru's DNA into himself.[^90] Knowing the nature of the seal to contain parts of Orochimaru's consciousness, Sasuke revived Orochimaru by transferring a portion of Orochimaru's DNA which was retrieved from Kabuto's modified body into Anko's [Cursed Seal of Heaven](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Cursed_Seal_of_Heaven "Cursed Seal of Heaven").[^91]

## Creation and Conception

As one of the villains in the story, Kishimoto created Orochimaru as an antithesis to the protagonists' values. His snake-like appearance and related features were intended to make it easier for the reader to recognise that he is a villain. In an interview, Kishimoto asserted that making the villains "flamboyant" was one of his "guiding principles", and attributed this to his desire to have the villains possess a "powerful aura".

Following this theme of distinguishing villains, Kishimoto attempted to make Orochimaru's face appear "pasty and sickly," which serves to emphasise the "scary looks" that Kishimoto frequently has Orochimaru performing in the manga. As these occurrences are his "trademark", Kishimoto modifies Orochimaru's appearance and the scene to focus on Orochimaru's face; for instance, highlights that are normally added to Orochimaru's hair are removed and the background is blanked out to create a "creepy atmosphere".

- The name "Orochi" literally means "great snake" (大蛇) while "maru" (丸) is a common suffix in male names.
	- It is possible that Orochimaru has a last name, since both of his parent's names are written with two words. However, reading them is impossible due to the [kanji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanji "wikipedia:Kanji") 's bad quality.
- Orochimaru borrows heavily from Japanese myth and literature:
	- Literature — Orochimaru's name, as well as those of [Jiraiya](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jiraiya "Jiraiya") and [Tsunade](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tsunade "Tsunade"), come from the ["The Tale of the Gallant Jiraiya"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jiraiya "wikipedia:Jiraiya") (児雷也豪傑物語, *Jiraiya Goketsu Monogatari*). The Orochimaru in that story was originally a student of Jiraiya's called Yashagorō, until he was corrupted by snake magic and took on the name Orochimaru.
		- Myth — [Yamata-no-Orochi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yamata_no_Orochi "wikipedia:Yamata no Orochi") (八岐大蛇, *Eight-forked Giant Snake*), a giant eight-tailed, eight-headed snake killed with the Totsuka Sword by the god [Susanoo-no-Mikoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Susanoo "wikipedia:Susanoo"), who found the [Kusanagi Sword](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kusanagi "wikipedia:Kusanagi") in one of its tails. This mirrors almost exactly how Itachi Uchiha using Totsuka Sword wielding [Susanoo](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Susanoo "Susanoo") sealed Orochimaru, while in his [Yamata](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eight_Branches_Technique "Eight Branches Technique") form, and wielding the [Kusanagi Sword](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sword_of_Kusanagi:_Longsword_of_the_Sky "Sword of Kusanagi: Longsword of the Sky") kept inside of him.
![Orochimaru's signature.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/1/1a/Orochimaru%27s_Signature.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20201101165221)

Orochimaru's signature.

- In the English VIZ translation of [chapter 49](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Coward%E2%80%A6!! "Coward…!!"), Orochimaru introduces himself to Sasuke as "Orochimaru, the Giant Snake". This was not present in the original version.
- The symbolism of Orochimaru's snake affiliation can be also tied to how many cultures, Eastern and Western, perceived snakes as symbols of immortality and rebirth via the shedding of their skin.
- Orochimaru shares his blood type with his former teammates, Jiraiya and Tsunade.
- After the preliminaries conclude, he is never shown again wearing an Otogakure [forehead protector](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Forehead_protector "Forehead protector").
- Studio Pierrot's Settei sheets of Orochimaru show that he was 153 cm when he was part of Hiruzen's genin team, and as an adult he is always 179.5 cm even after taking bodies, even though the databook puts him as 179.4 cm.
- Orochimaru's second listed databook height of 172 cm exactly matches Gen'yūmaru's,[^92] suggesting Orochimaru takes on the height of his hosts after transferring into them. If that is the case, his height should be 177 cm after taking a Zetsu body.[^93]
- According to the databook(s):
	- Orochimaru's hobby is creating techniques.
		- Orochimaru wishes to fight those with techniques that can be used in the future.
		- Orochimaru's favourite food are [eggs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egg_\(food\) "wikipedia:Egg (food)") (likely a gag to his snake motif), while his least favourites are anything cold.
		- During his time as a Konoha-nin, Orochimaru has completed 1,468 official missions in total: 16 D-rank, 332 C-rank, 521 B-rank, 491 A-rank, 108 S-rank.
		- Orochimaru's favourite words are "destruction" (破壊, *hakai*) and "chaos" (混沌, *konton*).
- In the [fourth databook](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jin_no_Sho "Jin no Sho"), kekkei genkai is not listed among Orochimaru's special characteristics, despite his current [host](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Parasite_Clone "Parasite Clone") granting him [Wood Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Release "Wood Release").
- In *[Naruto Shippūden the Movie: Bonds](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Shipp%C5%ABden_the_Movie:_Bonds "Naruto Shippūden the Movie: Bonds")*, Orochimaru claimed that [Shinnō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shinn%C5%8D "Shinnō") had taught him many [Reincarnation Ninjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Reincarnation_Ninjutsu "Reincarnation Ninjutsu").
- Throughout the [anime adaption](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Hiden:_The_Perfect_Day_for_a_Wedding_\(Arc\) "Konoha Hiden: The Perfect Day for a Wedding (Arc)") of *[Konoha Hiden: The Perfect Day for a Wedding](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Hiden:_The_Perfect_Day_for_a_Wedding "Konoha Hiden: The Perfect Day for a Wedding")*, Orochimaru makes comedic cameos throughout Konoha while being pursued by Yamato.
- Orochimaru's purple markings around his eyes and nose seem to be congenital, as the same markings appeared on [Kabuto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kabuto "Kabuto") 's face after transplanting Orochimaru's cells. In the spin-off series, *[Rock Lee's Springtime of Youth Full Power Ninja Chronicles](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rock_Lee%27s_Springtime_of_Youth_Full_Power_Ninja_Chronicles "Rock Lee's Springtime of Youth Full Power Ninja Chronicles")*, however, these are humorously portrayed as being eyeliner.
