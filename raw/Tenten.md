**Tenten** (テンテン) is a [kunoichi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kunoichi "Kunoichi") from [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure") and a member of [Team Guy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Guy "Team Guy"). Wanting to be a powerful kunoichi, she puts her all into perfecting her [weaponry](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Bukijutsu "Bukijutsu") and [fūinjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/F%C5%ABinjutsu "Fūinjutsu") skills. Years after the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War"), she opened her own weapon shop and became a proctor of the [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams "Chūnin Exams").

## Background

![Tenten as a child.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/c/cd/Tenten_child.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160428000207)

Tenten as a child.

Since her time as a student in the [Academy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Academy "Academy"), Tenten had a goal to become a legendary kunoichi like [Tsunade](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tsunade "Tsunade"), one of the [Sannin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sannin "Sannin"), which she openly shared to others. After graduating, she was assigned to the [jōnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/J%C5%8Dnin "Jōnin") tutelage of [Might Guy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Might_Guy "Might Guy"), alongside her classmates [Rock Lee](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rock_Lee "Rock Lee") and [Neji Hyūga](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Neji_Hy%C5%ABga "Neji Hyūga"). For their final test to become genin, Guy forced his potential students to spar him and see their commitment to succeed. Ultimately, all three passed.[^6] During this time of trial and failure, trying to emulate the legendary [Sannin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sannin "Sannin") [Tsunade](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tsunade "Tsunade"), Tenten attempted to become a [medical-nin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Medical-nin "Medical-nin"), building up her raw strength and chakra, and such, but she failed due to her lack of [chakra control](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_control "Chakra control"). While each failed attempt was damaging to her pride, Tenten discovered her aptitude for [fūinjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/F%C5%ABinjutsu "Fūinjutsu") and thus developed her unique fighting style. Realising her own nature, she stopped trying to become like Tsunade, but still has not given up on her dream of becoming a great kunoichi in her own right.[^7] Later, Tenten and her team were offered the chance to enter the [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams "Chūnin Exams") in the possibility of being promoted to said rank. However, all three decided to pass on the exams for the time being to further hone their skills.[^6]

## Personality

![Tenten dreams of Guy and Lee being mature.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/2e/Tenten%27s_Dream.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140604062413)

Tenten dreams of Guy and Lee being mature.

Tenten is depicted as naturally inquisitive and observant, with a personal dream to become a legendary kunoichi like Tsunade. This has led to her to become strongly opinionated about women, defending that girls can be just as skilled as boys in [taijutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Taijutsu "Taijutsu") when [Neji Hyūga](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Neji_Hy%C5%ABga "Neji Hyūga") initially thought otherwise. She also exhibits a distaste for weakness or being overly dependent on other people. Though she can be quite fierce at times,[^8] she is kind and caring toward others, comforting her friends when they are going through a hard time.[^9]

Being around her overly enthusiastic teammates, particularly [Might Guy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Might_Guy "Might Guy") and [Rock Lee](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rock_Lee "Rock Lee"), Tenten is often left exasperated at their antics and wishes they would behave more maturely; her [Infinite Tsukuyomi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Infinite_Tsukuyomi "Infinite Tsukuyomi") dream actually depicts this. She is more easy-going and dislikes having to travel for whole days [^10] or continue training when she is already tired, for which reason she enjoys her days-off from missions. However, she cares greatly about her team, helping to go save Lee during the Chūnin Exams when he didn't report back at their base. Despite her personality, she is shown to strongly care for both her teammates.

Naturally Tenten, who was a ninja-tool user, loves ninja weapons to the point that she collects every ninja tool from all over the world as a hobby. Moreover, her hobby developed so much that she even opened a full-fledged ninja tool shop.[^11] As seen during her friendly interaction with the younger kunoichi [Mirai Sarutobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mirai_Sarutobi "Mirai Sarutobi"), Tenten also appears to have a fascination with the supernatural spirits hiding in old houses, believing in ghosts and the like.[^12]

## Appearance

Tenten has black hair and grey eyes, both of which are depicted as dark brown in the anime. She wears her hair in two Chinese-style buns on her head with short fringe-bangs framing her face. She keeps this hairstyle consistent throughout Parts I and II. In *[The Last: Naruto the Movie](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/The_Last:_Naruto_the_Movie "The Last: Naruto the Movie")*, her hair buns are braided and held together by a short plait that falls just below her shoulders. As an adult, her braided-buns are still kept, but her straight bangs cover her forehead.

Tenten's outfits serve as a reference to Chinese culture. In Part I, she wears a pink sleeveless qipao-style blouse (initially depicted as blue in the manga) with red sleeve trimmings and yellow fastening buttons and dark green pants. A pouch is adjusted to her thigh and her forehead protector and sandals are both blue. In Part II, Tenten sports a long-sleeved, high-collared white blouse with maroon edges, black fingerless gloves, low-heeled sandals and a black forehead protector. She wears maroon, puffy [hakama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hakama "wikipedia:Hakama") -styled pants with the exposed parts covered in bandages and the shuriken holster removed. She usually carries a large scroll behind her back. During the Fourth Shinobi World War, Tenten wears the standard Konoha infantry [flak jacket](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Flak_jacket "Flak jacket") and assorted clothing, along with a holster around her waist that carries two small scrolls.

In *The Last: Naruto the Movie*, Tenten wears a white sleeveless, high-collared qipao dress with red trimmings and a pink flame pattern on the left, tied with a maroon obi. Underneath, she has fishnet leg stockings and black high-heeled boots. She no longer wears her gloves and forehead protector, but wears a pair of black scrolls around her wrists like bangles, and has two scrolls in the colour of green and light grey strapped on her lower back.

As an adult, Tenten once again wears the similar outfit she wore in Part II with the pants being peach-coloured. She also sports red dangling tassel-like earrings.

## Abilities

![Tenten destroying one of Kakuzu's elemental masks.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/9/9e/Hi_no_Maki.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150202192528)

Tenten destroying one of Kakuzu's elemental masks.

Tenten is a highly skilled and capable kunoichi; her skills being held in high regard by her team. By Part II, she became a [chūnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin "Chūnin"),[^3] capable of pressuring the likes of [Kisame Hoshigaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kisame_Hoshigaki "Kisame Hoshigaki") with the aid of her teammates.[^13] As an adult, Tenten is remarked as a kunoichi who excelled at [space–time ninjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Space%E2%80%93time_ninjutsu "Space–time ninjutsu").[^11]

### Ninjutsu

As an adult, Tenten learned to [manipulate](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Puppet_Technique "Puppet Technique") a multitude of [puppets](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Puppets "Puppets") to coordinate surprise attacks, even without the use of chakra threads.[^14] Having also been remarked as a kunoichi who excelled at [space–time ninjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Space%E2%80%93time_ninjutsu "Space–time ninjutsu"),[^11] she can [summon](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning_Technique "Summoning Technique") dozens of them over [vast distances](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Reverse_Summoning_Technique "Reverse Summoning Technique").[^14]

#### Fūinjutsu

![Tenten's arsenal of weapons.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/5/54/Tenten%27s_tools.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150323115352)

Tenten's arsenal of weapons.

Tenten is very skilled in the use of [fūinjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/F%C5%ABinjutsu "Fūinjutsu"). Her usual method is to [store](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Enclosing_Technique "Enclosing Technique") a numerous amount of weapons into her scrolls, which she can later [unseal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Unsealing_Technique "Unsealing Technique") with varying degrees of force to bombard her opponents with.[^15] She could attach [explosive tags](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Explosive_tags "Explosive tags") to the weapons to make them more lethal, and use [wire strings](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wire_strings "Wire strings") to better [guide](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Manipulated_Shuriken_Technique "Manipulated Shuriken Technique") the weapons' trajectory.[^16]

In the anime, Tenten can use her two small scrolls to perform the [Twin Rising Dragons](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Twin_Rising_Dragons "Twin Rising Dragons") to launch a variety of weapons in rapid succession;[^17] which she was limited to physically having to touch in order to execute until Part II. She equips a large scroll in Part II, which she can launch above her opponent to [rain a multitude of weapons](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Manipulated_Tools:_Heavenly_Chain_Disaster "Manipulated Tools: Heavenly Chain Disaster") down onto them. In addition, she can store and release a [fire dragon](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Exploding_Dragon_Strike "Exploding Dragon Strike") that explodes upon contact [^18] and a [metal dome](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Unsealing_Technique:_Segmented_Iron_Dome "Unsealing Technique: Segmented Iron Dome") to defend against attacks.[^19] She can even seal objects as large as a ship,[^20] or a large body of water; the latter of which she can use to wash obstacles away.[^21]

### Bukijutsu

![Tenten's fighting style.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/5/5c/Tenten_ep237.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160425215024)

Tenten's fighting style.

Tenten is able to wield a variety of weapons with great proficiency, such as the [manriki-gusari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manriki-gusari "wikipedia:Manriki-gusari") and bō.[^22] [^7] She could even effectively wield a weapon that she had no prior knowledge on, like the [Jidanda](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jidanda "Jidanda") and [Bashōsen](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Bash%C5%8Dsen "Bashōsen").[^23] [^24] Also, as noted by her teammates, she is an excellent marksman, hitting 100/100 marks each time.[^17] By adulthood, she is considered to be Konoha's top weapon user.[^11] Her skills make her an excellent support for other taijutsu users, as well as aiding them as a strategist.[^25]

## Part I

### Chūnin Exams

Main article: [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams_\(Arc\) "Chūnin Exams (Arc)")

![Team Guy finishes their training.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/87/Team_Guy_Part_1.PNG/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150126171001)

Team Guy finishes their training.

By having not been allowed to participate the year before, Team Guy took part in the Chūnin Exams along with the [Rookie Nine](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rookie_Nine "Rookie Nine"). They initially tried to avoid attention by acting weak by being beaten around by the two Chūnin that weren't letting them pass through, but [Lee](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Lee "Lee") accidentally let slip his true strength by interfering with a fight between Sasuke Uchiha and [Kotetsu Hagane](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kotetsu_Hagane "Kotetsu Hagane"). After Neji asked Sasuke to present who he was, Sasuke didn't answer, to which Tenten giggled to herself, thinking Sasuke was cute. During the written exam, Tenten assisted Lee in gathering answers. Using strings to adjust hidden mirrors on the ceiling, Tenten allowed Lee to see his fellow ninja's answers and copy them without being caught. To know that Lee saw the answer she sneaked in for him, Lee had to adjust his forehead protector.

She was seen again during the second part of the exam in the [Forest of Death](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Forest_of_Death "Forest of Death") with her teammates. Their team decided to split up to find weaker teams to corner and take scrolls from. She and Neji met back up at the assigned destination, but became worried when Lee didn't return. They went looking for Lee and arrived in time to find an unconscious Lee surrounded by [Team Dosu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Dosu "Team Dosu"). Tenten was seen taking over watching an unconscious Lee for [Ino](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ino "Ino"), and then proceeded to wake him by violently shaking him. During Sakura's and Ino's match, Neji not being impressed commented on the skill of their hand to hand combat, wondering whether this was as far all kunoichi can fight, to which Tenten replied that it didn't matter whether a man or woman was in battle.

![Temari finishes off Tenten.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/4/48/Temari_vs_Tenten.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150209080346)

Temari finishes off Tenten.

Tenten was pitted against [Temari](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Temari "Temari") during the preliminaries of the [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams "Chūnin Exams"). Since their fight went unseen in the manga, the anime adaptation explained the circumstances that occurred. Tenten began the match by throwing a few of her weapons at Temari while keeping the best distance for offence and defence, though Temari easily blew the weapons away with her fan. She was surprised that not one weapon reached Temari. Temari explained the power of her fan and that once Tenten had seen all three moons the match would be over. Since nothing had even come close to hitting Temari, Tenten used one final technique that she was saving for the finals, the [Twin Rising Dragons](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Twin_Rising_Dragons "Twin Rising Dragons"), but ultimately ineffective against Temari's fan. As Tenten ran out of weapons, she used strings to control the weapons and fling them once again back at Temari, in an attempt to surprise her. Temari however quickly used her fan to blast Tenten, along with her weapons and she hit the floor. Temari finally used her fan to trap Tenten in a [cyclone](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sickle_Weasel_Technique "Sickle Weasel Technique") and propel her into the air. As the cyclone dispersed and Tenten fell to the ground, Temari had Tenten land on her closed fan, knocking her unconscious and calling the match she just had boring. Even after she was declared the winner of the match, she threw Tenten off of the fan in an attempt to have her land on the many weapons on the ground to injure her more. However, Lee jumped in to save Tenten before she hit the ground.

Before the finals began, Tenten helps Neji in his training to perfect his techniques. She was seen using her weapons to help Neji perfect his [Eight Trigrams Palms Revolving Heaven](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eight_Trigrams_Palms_Revolving_Heaven "Eight Trigrams Palms Revolving Heaven") technique and that his technique was 'perfect.' Later, she watched Neji's fight against [Naruto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto "Naruto") in the finals, where she narrated Neji's skills for the audience and thought it was impossible that Naruto would beat a genius like him. Tenten was shown to be shocked, praising Naruto and his abilities for defeating Neji. She was later seen again during Temari and [Shikamaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shikamaru "Shikamaru") 's match and commented that she could never beat that kind of opponent.

### Kurosuki Family Removal Mission

Main article: [Kurosuki Family Removal Mission](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kurosuki_Family_Removal_Mission "Kurosuki Family Removal Mission")

![Tenten's team discover Ranmaru.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/4/4e/The_Enemy_of_the_Byakugan.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150405073245)

Tenten's team discover Ranmaru.

In the anime, Team Guy and Naruto were sent to get rid of the Kurosuki family. After taking care of most of the enemy members, they ran into their leader, [Raiga Kurosuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Raiga_Kurosuki "Raiga Kurosuki"), who was more than enough to handle her and teammates. Ultimately, Raiga was defeated. She and the others soon found a young boy named [Ranmaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ranmaru "Ranmaru") who was not only working alongside Raiga by using his [dōjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ranmaru%27s_Kekkei_Genkai "Ranmaru's Kekkei Genkai"), but depended on Raiga as his arms and legs, and developed an unhealthy attachment to Raiga. They listened to Ranmaru state that without Raiga he was useless and has no reason to live. Before they continued the mission, they tied up Ranmaru inside a shed, and Tenten pointed out that the only threat Ranmaru posed was possibly being a danger to himself. Later, they came back to Ranmaru and heard him state that it didn't matter if he felt alright or not, he couldn't keep living all alone. Tenten was frustrated by all of Ranmaru's self loathing and referred to him as a weakling, affirming that everyone should be able to live even if they are alone. She couldn't stand weak, indecisive people. After this, she made a formidable opponent for [Raiga Kurosuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Raiga_Kurosuki "Raiga Kurosuki") when she faced him in a one-on-one fight. Knowing that he used [Lightning Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning_Release "Lightning Release") techniques, she quickly deduced that her metal weapons would be a bad idea to use, and took on the [missing-nin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Missing-nin "Missing-nin") using her wooden based melee weapons. She appeared to gain the upper hand in the battle when she succeeded in disarming Raiga until he ultimately overwhelmed her with his techniques. Tenten noticed that Ranmaru was hurt and took care of him in the shed. Ranmaru told her to not bother because he knew he'd be dead soon anyway. Tenten yelled that she wouldn't let that happen and that he was not allowed to get out that easy. She stated that all the people he and Raiga killed wanted to live, that he didn't get to decide who dies this time, and that he was going to live and take some responsibility for all those people whose lives he cut short. Later, she saved his life by taking him to the [Curry of Life](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Curry_of_Life "Curry of Life") shop, and once he ate the curry his body started healing. She brought Ranmaru out of his depression and convinced him to live and to stand on his own two feet.

Carrying out the plan spot on, Tenten and the rest of the [Konoha 11](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_11 "Konoha 11") break through Obito's defences using the Rasengan Naruto had created giving him and Sasuke the opportunity to attack Obito directly with landing a final blow. Tenten and her comrades later assisted Naruto with wresting control of all the tailed beasts' chakra from Obito's control in order to stop the holy tree from blooming and succeed.

Tenten regrouped with Guy and Lee. A weakened Guy went on about the power of youth, which Tenten commented if only his body kept up with his heart. Then the team saw Gaara, Sakura, and a dying Naruto fly over them from above with Gaara's sand, and wonder what is going on in the place they were headed to. She is soon exasperated with the pair as Guy is determined to still fight and head over to Kakashi's location, even though severely exhausted and Lee, adamant to follow his sensei to the letter. As she asked why Neji had to leave her alone with these two extreme characters, referring to them as bone-heads, Guy and Lee are struck down by the falling Kohaku no Jōhei and [Benihisago](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Benihisago "Benihisago") that was discarded by [Madara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Madara "Madara"). This caused Tenten to fall over as well due to the impact. After she regained her conciseness, Tenten asked weather Guy and Lee were alright or not, she then recognised the Treasured Tools after Madara had expelled them from within him, leaving her in shock to their reappearance. Guy went on ahead to where the main battle was occurring later on followed by Lee. When Lee saved his sensei, Guy asked where Tenten was. The chaotic tremors later felt across the battlefield caused Tenten to fall over and drop her scroll, which was shown to be unrolled with the two treasured tools exposed. As she slowly started getting up, she noted that she needed to ask someone from the [Kumogakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kumogakure "Kumogakure") to tell her the Treasured Tools work. The scroll is then pierced by a piece of Madara's discarded forehead protector which left her in shock.

![Tenten falls victim to the Infinite Tsukuyomi.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/c/c7/Tenten_Caught.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150903110243)

Tenten falls victim to the Infinite Tsukuyomi.

Later, when Madara Uchiha finally succeeded with the activation of the [Infinite Tsukuyomi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Infinite_Tsukuyomi "Infinite Tsukuyomi"), much like the rest of the world, she too was caught by the roots of the God Tree and fell under the genjutsu's power. Inside the genjutsu, Tenten dreamed of Guy and Lee acting more mature and "cool" as well as wearing mature clothing, proudly speaking about it with a still-alive Neji. In the anime, her possession of the Six Path Sage's tools appeared to have caused a unique affect to Tenten's illusion, allowing her to retain her memories of the war. Finding herself seemingly back in Konoha, she was shocked at how different all her friends and allies were acting, including Naruto being known as " [Menma](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Menma_Namikaze "Menma Namikaze") ". Concluding that she was stuck in a very elaborate genjutsu she attempted to dispel it but to no avail. Ultimately, she decided to avoid conversing with any of her friends, believing they may in fact be agents of Madara.[^38]

After failing to get help from Kurenai, who in this world was only an avid fan of genjutsu but lacked the talent to perform it, she was approached by a travelling merchant, who said there was a shinobi in [Amegakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Amegakure "Amegakure") with advanced genjutsu prowess who could help her. Accepting his map to Amegakure, Tenten decided to leave without asking for approval. This lead to her being captured and branded a traitor as the map was revealed to have secret information on Konoha concealed. While locked up, Neji and Lee secretly met with Tenten, promising to prove her innocence. Later, the village was attacked by Amegakure. As Tenten watched in horror, Menma came and save her.

Deciding to help, Tenten joined Menma in fighting off the Ame-nin, using the two Six Paths Sage tools. After the enemies were defeated, Tenten was applauded by all, convincing her that she belonged there.[^39]

## Trivia

| ![Springtime of Youth](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/3/39/Youth_symbol.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/40?cb=20130120020002)  Springtime of Youth | The [*Springtime of Youth* wiki](https://springtimeofyouth.fandom.com/wiki/ "w:c:springtimeofyouth") has an article about this topic:  **[Tenten](https://springtimeofyouth.fandom.com/wiki/Tenten "w:c:springtimeofyouth:Tenten")** |
| --- | --- |

- The name "Tenten" literally means "heaven, heaven" (天天). Written as 点点, it can also mean "little by little", "this and that", "here and there" or "dot" in Japanese. Written as 転転, it means "roll", relating to her shop. It is also a possibility that her name insinuates "ten out of ten", as a reference to her [marksmanship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marksman "wikipedia:Marksman") or one of the names for a Japanese ellipsis meaning "mark-mark", referring to her use of targets with [bukijutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Bukijutsu "Bukijutsu").
- Tenten has slowly been improving her ranking in the Naruto character popularity polls. She placed 24th in the first and fifth, 30th in the second, 29th in the third and ninth and most recent poll, 22nd in the third and 15th in the seventh. That is her highest rank in all of the polls. She is not in the top 30 in the eighth poll.
- In an interview, Kishimoto said he preferred Tenten out of the four [Konoha 11](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_11 "Konoha 11") girls because he liked her design.[^40]
- Like the rest of her team, Tenten's character seems to draw inspiration from Chinese culture as seen from her attire and hairstyle, the Chinese style Chignon and Odango.
- According to the databook(s):
	- Her hobby is [fortune-telling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortune-telling "wikipedia:Fortune-telling").
		- Tenten would like to have a rematch with [Temari](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Temari "Temari"), and she would also like to fight [Tsunade](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tsunade "Tsunade") 's apprentice, [Sakura Haruno](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sakura_Haruno "Sakura Haruno").
		- Her favourite foods are [Sesame dumplings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dumpling#Asian_cuisine "wikipedia:Dumpling") and [Chinese food](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_cuisine "wikipedia:Chinese cuisine"), while her least favourite are pickled plums.
		- Tenten's favourite phrase is "A hundred shots, a hundred bull's-eyes" (百発百中, *Hyappatsu Hyakuchū*), an idiom for "always hitting the bulls-eye".
		- Tenten has completed 62 official missions in total: 28 D-rank, 23 C-rank, 3 B-rank, 7 A-rank, 1 S-rank.
		- Tenten loves to investigate.
- In original colour illustrations of the manga, Tenten's costume is blue. This is changed to pink in the anime and later manga images.
- In the [omake](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Omake#Mini_Corner "Omake") for episode [209](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Danz%C5%8D%27s_Right_Arm "Danzō's Right Arm"), her name is written in Chinese: 天天.
- On the back of *[Hyō no Sho](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hy%C5%8D_no_Sho "Hyō no Sho")*, her hair colour is black rather then brown.
- In the *Road to Boruto* downloadable content for *[Naruto Shippūden: Ultimate Ninja Storm 4](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Shipp%C5%ABden:_Ultimate_Ninja_Storm_4 "Naruto Shippūden: Ultimate Ninja Storm 4")*, Tenten is a [jōnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/J%C5%8Dnin "Jōnin").
- According to her *[Naruto Mobile](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Mobile "Naruto Mobile")* profile, Tenten destroyed all four of Kakuzu's masks using the Bashōsen before succumbing to exhaustion.[^41]
