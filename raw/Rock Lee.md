**Rock Lee** (ロック・リー, *Rokku Rī*) is a [shinobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shinobi "Shinobi") of [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure") 's [Lee clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Lee_clan "Lee clan") and a member of [Team Guy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Guy "Team Guy"). Unlike most shinobi, he lacked the skills necessary to use [ninjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ninjutsu "Ninjutsu") or [genjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genjutsu "Genjutsu"). However, he overcomes those shortcomings by undergoing special training from [Might Guy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Might_Guy "Might Guy") that would make him a [taijutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Taijutsu "Taijutsu") master while still a [genin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genin "Genin").

## Background

During his time in the [Academy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Academy "Academy"), Lee proved to have no talent for ninjutsu and genjutsu. When he was mocked by his peers because of this, Lee persevered, focusing on taijutsu.[^10] After he succeeded in graduating he was added to Team Guy along with [Neji Hyūga](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Neji_Hy%C5%ABga "Neji Hyūga") and [Tenten](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tenten "Tenten"). During the team's first meeting, Lee vowed to become a powerful ninja without using ninjutsu or genjutsu. Neji laughed at him, but their sensei, [Might Guy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Might_Guy "Might Guy"), took a special interest in Lee.[^11] He encouraged Lee to keep at it and, in time, began teaching him powerful forms of taijutsu.[^12] [^13]

## Personality

![Lee's "nice guy" pose.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/9/9d/Lee_Pose.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150124171221)

Lee's "nice guy" pose.

Under Guy's leadership, Lee has acquired a number of his sensei's personality traits: he is energetic, optimistic, and hot-blooded, and shows the epitome of a "nice guy". He believes that one cannot dislike food and shows strong dedication to his promises; when this promise is broken, just barely, he assigns himself arduous training to prevent it from happening again. Lee speaks respectfully to others, never using contractions or swear words and always addressing people with appropriate honourifics (except his teammates, since Lee feels they are close enough to be spoken to informally). He also carries a small book and pencil to recall advice that Guy gives him over time.

Despite his passionate nature, Lee has also shown strong instincts, staying focused at the task at hand and rarely losing his composure. He has a keen sense of honour and respects his opponents, refusing to hold grudges against those who have defeated him, and returning favours to those that have helped him. This was shown by his befriending of [Gaara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gaara "Gaara"), forgiving him for crushing his arm and leg — injuries that nearly ended his career as a shinobi. Lee feels others should show the same respect towards their opponents, and is disgusted whenever someone is unnecessarily cruel to their enemies.

One of Lee's recurring themes throughout the series is his drive to succeed, believing with enough hard work and perseverance, he can surpass a natural genius. He can be prone to depression when faced with setbacks. Neji, Lee's more talented teammate and rival, found Lee's efforts to be in vain, believing a "failure" could never defeat a "genius". As such, Lee became determined to defeat Neji in order to prove him wrong, though was met with defeat every time. When Neji himself was defeated by [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki"), another so-called "failure", he rejects his belief in fate and now holds great respect for Lee, believing that he will surpass the gifted with enough work.

![Lee dreams of victory in combat and love.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/c/cc/Lee%27s_dream.PNG/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20180826044610)

Lee dreams of victory in combat and love.

During Part I, Lee revealed he had feelings for [Sakura Haruno](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sakura_Haruno "Sakura Haruno"). Although she did not reciprocate his feelings and even considered him weird, she later become more appreciative and friendly towards him after he helped saved her from the [Otogakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Otogakure "Otogakure") genin during the Chūnin Exams. After the exams, Lee's interest in Sakura went largely unaddressed in the manga, though it is sometimes referenced in the anime; while having a match with Lee, Naruto transformed into Sakura, distracting Lee long enough for Naruto to attack him.[^14] However, Lee's [Infinite Tsukuyomi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Infinite_Tsukuyomi "Infinite Tsukuyomi") dream was of winning Sakura's affections, implying he still had romantic feelings for her. These feelings apparently have changed by adulthood, as Lee ended up marrying someone else and had a son named [Metal Lee](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Metal_Lee "Metal Lee").

## Appearance

Lee has shiny black hair, perfectly round eyes with prominent lower eyelashes, and very thick eyebrows; as a result, Naruto calls him " **Centipede Brows** " (ゲジマユ, *Gejimayu*, *English TV:* Bushy Brows). As a child, his hair was quite long and fastened in a braid, though he cuts it short by the time he joins Team Guy, letting it curve out and upward. Lee originally wore a white, short-sleeved kimono-shirt with a martial arts belt and dark pants, giving him a more traditional Chinese appearance. He also sported a forehead protector and shinobi sandals.

Lee later changes his appearance considerably to emulate his idol and sensei, Guy: his hair is now in a bowl-cut style; he wears a green jumpsuit, orange leg warmers, and a red forehead protector worn as a belt. He has bandages around his forearms up to his fingers, covering a set of stitches on his hands that were caused by his rigorous training.[^15] Like Guy, Lee's appearance remains largely the same over the years, though he adds a standard Konoha [flak jacket](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Flak_jacket "Flak jacket") as a [chūnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin "Chūnin") in Part II, and its redesigned version in *[The Last: Naruto the Movie](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/The_Last:_Naruto_the_Movie "The Last: Naruto the Movie")*. As an adult, Lee grew noticeably muscular with more-defined cheekbones, and now wears a sleeveless jumpsuit with an orange neckwarmer.

## Abilities

![Lee destroying half of a meteorite with the Suicide Corps.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/1/18/Kakuyoku_no_Jin.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150712135159)

Lee destroying half of a meteorite with the Suicide Corps.

While in the Academy, Lee was looked down upon for his inability to use either ninjutsu or genjutsu. However, with the guidance and support of Might Guy, Lee conditioned himself into a superb ninja who relies exclusively on taijutsu.[^16] While originally very limited in even that skill, Lee proved to be a late bloomer by Part I: His greatly improved skill was evident when he defeated [Sasuke Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Uchiha "Sasuke Uchiha") (a natural prodigy), posed a challenge against Gaara in the Chūnin Exams, and held his own against [Kimimaro](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kimimaro "Kimimaro"). As a [chūnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin "Chūnin"), he was capable of defeating the legendary taijutsu master [Chen](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chen "Chen"),[^17] as well as destroy a meteorite by himself.[^18] By adulthood, he had advanced to the rank of [jōnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/J%C5%8Dnin "Jōnin").

### Physical Prowess

![Lee easily defeating Naruto.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/2d/Leaf_Gale.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150124172048)

Lee easily defeating Naruto.

By age 13, Lee's arduous training conditioned him into essentially a taijutsu master, showing great proficiency in the [Strong Fist](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Strong_Fist "Strong Fist") fighting style. His strength could unearth a massive tree root from the ground,[^19] and even though Sasuke could predict his moves with the [Sharingan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sharingan "Sharingan") in their first fight, his body wasn't fast enough to react to them. As per his training Lee has worn heavy ankle weights (重り, *Omori*) [^20] even in fights and became much faster when they're removed, enabling him to become the first person ever to bypass Gaara's [Shield of Sand](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shield_of_Sand "Shield of Sand") and strike him.[^21] As a chūnin, Lee learned to perform the Leaf Great Gale (木ノ葉大烈風, *Konoha Daireppū*).[^22] In the anime, due to Lee's extensive training, he has shown to be able to run and fight even while unconscious or asleep, using nothing but pure [muscle memory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muscle_memory "wikipedia:Muscle memory").[^23] As an adult, Lee is considered to be one of Konoha's best taijutsu users.[^24]

#### Drunken Fist

![Lee using the Drunken Fist.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/d/db/Drunken_Fist.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150124172754)

Lee using the Drunken Fist.

Lee's very low tolerance for alcohol gives him a natural predisposition for the [Drunken Fist](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Drunken_Fist "Drunken Fist") style.[^25] When he consumes as little as one drop of alcohol, Lee becomes an unpredictable and nearly unstoppable force of destruction and chaos, attacking anyone whether they are friend or foe. This gives him considerably greater speed and dexterity, allowing him to strike and effectively dodge from various positions. Kimimaro, one of [Orochimaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Orochimaru "Orochimaru") 's strongest subordinates, was ultimately overwhelmed by Lee's attack patterns with Drunken Fist, noting that they were too unpredictable to follow.[^26]

#### Eight Gates

![Lee opening the first gate.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/7/71/Gate_Of_Opening.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20180830104553)

Lee opening the first gate.

Through Guy's teachings, Lee learned to open five of the [Eight Gates](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eight_Gates "Eight Gates") at age 12,[^27] the sixth gate at age 17,[^28] and (in the anime) the seventh gate some time after the Fourth Shinobi World War.[^29] By adulthood, Lee mastered the final gate.[^30] Using the 6th gate Lee is able to move so fast he can save Guy from Madara's Truth Seeking Ball, which moved fast enough that Obito couldn't use Kamui to escape from them. He is recognised as a genius for this feat by [Kakashi Hatake](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi_Hatake "Kakashi Hatake"), who notes it is highly difficult for even the most talented ninja to open even one gate.[^31] With each gate he opens, Lee's strength, speed, chakra levels, and bodily functions increase tremendously. However, the longer he maintains any of these gates, the more strain and potential physical damage his puts on himself from pushing his performance so far past his natural limits. For this reason, Lee is only permitted to open any of the gates during the most dire situation or to protect something very dear to him.[^32] Through his continued training, Lee has become able to open some of the Eight Gates instantly and shows a faster recovery rate from using them, but he nevertheless succumbs to the pain or exhaustion they cause.[^33] [^34]

Lee has learned to use a number of taijutsu techniques that he can perform once certain gates have been opened; with the first gate, he can use the pile-driving [Front Lotus](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Front_Lotus "Front Lotus"); with the third gate, he can use the more destructive [Reverse Lotus](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Reverse_Lotus "Reverse Lotus"); with the sixth gate, he can perform the [Crane Wing Formation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Crane_Wing_Formation_\(Eight_Gates\) "Crane Wing Formation (Eight Gates)") in collaboration with members of the [Suicide Corps](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Suicide_Corps "Suicide Corps"), which proved to be powerful enough to destroy half of a giant meteorite.[^35]

### Other Skills

Although Lee possess very little skill in ninjutsu, he can still perform basic chakra control skills, like using [chakra](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra "Chakra") to walk on vertical surfaces, as well as water. In the anime, he also developed enough skill to dispel genjutsu.[^36]

## Part I

### Chūnin Exams

Main article: [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams_\(Arc\) "Chūnin Exams (Arc)")

![Lee attempting to use the Front Lotus on Sasuke.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/f/f1/Kage_Buyou.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150125160205)

Lee attempting to use the Front Lotus on Sasuke.

Having opted not to participate the year before, Team Guy took part in the [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams "Chūnin Exams") along with the [Rookie Nine](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rookie_Nine "Rookie Nine"). They initially tried to avoid attention by acting weak, but Lee accidentally let slip his true strength by interfering with a fight between Sasuke and [Kotetsu Hagane](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kotetsu_Hagane "Kotetsu Hagane"). He also fell in love with Sakura. Soon afterwards, he confronted Sasuke, challenging him to a fight to see how he fared against the Sharingan, but not before knocking out Naruto, who tried to fight with Lee himself. After battling Sasuke, Lee easily gained the upperhand but Guy interrupted Lee and Sasuke's fight as Lee tried to use the [Front Lotus](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Front_Lotus "Front Lotus"), and Lee was forced to do laps around the practice range before he could proceed onto the first phase of the exams. With Tenten's help, he gained the answers for the written portion, and proceeded to the second round.

After spending their first few days in the [Forest of Death](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Forest_of_Death "Forest of Death") gathering supplies, Team Guy split up in search of other teams. Lee came across Sakura being attacked by [Team Dosu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Dosu "Team Dosu"), and went to her aid. He used his Front Lotus on [Dosu Kinuta](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Dosu_Kinuta "Dosu Kinuta"), but he survived the attack because of [Zaku Abumi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Zaku_Abumi "Zaku Abumi") using his [Decapitating Airwaves](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Decapitating_Airwaves "Decapitating Airwaves") in the ground, and Lee was left unable to defend himself from a counterattack from Dosu. Neji and Tenten soon arrived to help, but didn't have to because [Sasuke](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke "Sasuke") woke up and took care of Team Dosu. Once Lee recovered and they collected the specific scroll, they moved onto the preliminaries.

![Lee using Front Lotus on Gaara.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/9/9c/Front_Lotus_Lee.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240901230614)

Lee using Front Lotus on Gaara.

In the preliminaries, Lee was matched against Gaara. When his standard attacks proved unable to bypass Gaara's defences, he removed his ankle weights to increase his speed and managed to land a couple of hits on Gaara. Then, he attempted the Forward Lotus, but Gaara was able to escape at the last minute revealing a sand clone instead. His options running out if he was to win and prove himself, Guy allowed him to use the [Reverse Lotus](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Reverse_Lotus "Reverse Lotus"). Gaara barely survived by turning his gourd to sand to cushion him, and the techniques took a massive toll on Lee's body. Gaara added to Lee's injury by crushing his left arm and leg. Though Guy intervened to stop Gaara from killing him, Lee got to his feet to continue the battle, despite being completely unconscious.

Because of his injuries, [medics](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Medic-nin "Medic-nin") predicted, that even if Lee recovered, his injuries would prevent him from ever being able to be a shinobi again. He nevertheless tried to train while he was in the hospital, something that only worsened his condition. When he was asleep, Gaara came to his room and tried to kill him, but he was stopped by Naruto and [Shikamaru Nara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shikamaru_Nara "Shikamaru Nara") and later by Guy. To try and cheer Lee up, Guy took him to watch the final round matches, but they were surprised when they arrived to find out that Neji had been defeated by Naruto. Lee silently congratulated Naruto and then watched Gaara's fight with Sasuke, and marvelled at how quickly he had learned to emulate Lee's speed. Before the fight could finish, however, the [Konoha Crush](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Crush "Konoha Crush") began, and Lee was knocked unconscious.

### Sasuke Recovery Mission

Main article: [Sasuke Recovery Mission](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Recovery_Mission "Sasuke Recovery Mission")

![Lee attacks Kimimaro.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/7/7f/Leaf_Whirlwind.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150121174411)

Lee attacks Kimimaro.

Over the coming weeks, Guy tried a number of his own remedies to heal Lee, though none of his efforts worked. It was not until news of [Tsunade](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tsunade "Tsunade") becoming [Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage "Hokage") reached them that they found hope. But, when she examined Lee, she informed him of the dire situation: his broken bones and torn muscles have healed, but fragments of bone had lodged in his spinal column, and removing them would involve an extensive operation that only she was capable of. When Tsunade offered him an operation that carried a fifty percent chance of returning him to his former state, or dying in the process, Lee was hesitant to accept the operation. Though Lee was worried he would die in the operation, thanks to Guy's influence, he accepted the surgery. Upon overhearing Naruto, [Chōji Akimichi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%8Dji_Akimichi "Chōji Akimichi"), [Kiba Inuzuka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kiba_Inuzuka "Kiba Inuzuka"), and Shikamaru talking about the mission to bring Sasuke back to Konoha, Lee suggested that Neji go with them. Lee was disappointed that he couldn't go with them, still needing to go through his surgery that was scheduled for later that day [^37] (In the anime, Lee was recovering after having his surgery done the day before [^38]). In the end, Lee successfully survived the operation.

Once Lee was well enough to move, he left the hospital to follow them and provide assistance. He caught up with Naruto, who was in the middle of a battle with Kimimaro, and told him to go after Sasuke while he dealt with Kimimaro. At first, it seemed Kimimaro overmatched him, but after Lee drank some sake that he had mistaken for his medicine, he began to outclass Kimimaro. Though Lee's anticipated moves were unpredictable to Kimimaro, he revealed his [Kekkei Genkai](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kekkei_Genkai "Kekkei Genkai"), [Shikotsumyaku](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shikotsumyaku "Shikotsumyaku"), which, combined with Lee sobering up, easily took over the battle. Before his attack landed on Lee, Gaara arrived just in time to save him. Even though Lee insisted to fight side-by-side with him, he knew he was only getting in Gaara's way.

![The final moments of the fight with Kimimaro.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/6/6c/Kimimaro_vs_Gaara_Rock_Lee.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20260811022134)

The final moments of the fight with Kimimaro.

For the rest of the battle, Lee watches and is amazed by Gaara's new abilities. Even though Gaara's new techniques completely buried Kimimaro, Kimimaro counters by turning the battlefield into a forest of bones, which Lee and Gaara escaped by hanging onto [a pair of small clouds made out of sand](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Desert_Suspension "Desert Suspension"). Kimimaro then emerged from one of the bones, preparing to kill the two at point blank range. They were saved, however, as Kimimaro finally succumbed to his disease and died. After the battle, Lee and Gaara reflect upon Kimimaro's purpose, then the two return to Konoha.

### Kurosuki Family Removal Mission

Main article: [Kurosuki Family Removal Mission](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kurosuki_Family_Removal_Mission "Kurosuki Family Removal Mission")

![Ranmaru is discovered.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/4/4e/The_Enemy_of_the_Byakugan.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150405073245)

Ranmaru is discovered.

In the anime, Lee revealed that he once ran a marathon with Guy near the village of Katabami Kinzan shortly after being assigned to his team, and when he collapsed from exhaustion near the [Curry of Life](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Curry_of_Life "Curry of Life") shop, the owner, Sanshō, and her son Karashi fed him curry and saved his life. Lee's dedication caused Karashi to become enthusiastic, and he later left. A year later, when he came to the village with his teammates and Naruto to defeat the Kurosuki family, he found that Karashi had joined, and was upset that Karashi had sought strength in this way. He allowed Karashi to seek redemption, but Karashi used this opportunity to lure Lee's group into a trap after their first battle with [Raiga](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Raiga "Raiga"). The trap failed due to Naruto's use of [shadow clones](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_clone "Shadow clone"), and while the villagers were willing to seek vengeance on Karashi, Lee stepped in, promising to take responsibility for Karashi's actions. Karashi used the opportunity to take [Ranmaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ranmaru "Ranmaru") to revive Raiga. Lee went to fight Raiga with Naruto and Neji, and was knocked out but brought back to consciousness with the Curry of Life. The curry, however, accidentally contained wine, and Lee was intoxicated and performed the [Drunken Fist](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Drunken_Fist "Drunken Fist"). He fought Raiga drunkenly, but was defeated, and Naruto finished Raiga off. Later, when the group went back to the Curry of Life shop, they found out that Karashi had made a new curry that looked so bad that even Naruto didn't want to eat it. Lee, however, couldn't wait to try it, so the rest of the group had to literally drag him back to Konoha while he cried for the curry.

### Viva Dojo Challenge! Youth is All About Passion!

Main article: [Viva Dojo Challenge! Youth is All About Passion!](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Viva_Dojo_Challenge!_Youth_is_All_About_Passion! "Viva Dojo Challenge! Youth is All About Passion!")

![Rock Lee's Gutz Dōjō.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/5/5c/Episode_193.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150626140444)

Rock Lee's Gutz Dōjō.

In the anime, Lee, seeing that he had little time to train with other people due to the many missions that must be done, sought a new means of training. After having seen a man challenging a dōjō, ready to take the sign if he won, and ready to join if he lost; Lee founds his own dōjō (which he names the "Gutz Dōjō"), believing that a dōjō would draw-in many strong opponents. ### Konoha Hiden: The Perfect Day for a Wedding

Main articles: [Konoha Hiden (novel)](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Hiden:_The_Perfect_Day_for_a_Wedding "Konoha Hiden: The Perfect Day for a Wedding") and [Konoha Hiden (anime)](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Hiden:_The_Perfect_Day_for_a_Wedding_\(Arc\) "Konoha Hiden: The Perfect Day for a Wedding (Arc)") When Lee is assigned to bring a present to Naruto and [Hinata](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hinata "Hinata") 's wedding, he takes the mission as seriously as he takes all his missions: very. ## Creation and conception

According to an interview with Masashi Kishimoto in Shōnen Jump's 'Naruto Anime Profiles Episodes 1-37', Lee is his most fun character to draw. He also has stated that [Sakura Haruno](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sakura_Haruno "Sakura Haruno") and Lee were meant to symbolise human weakness, at least in the beginning.

## Trivia

| ![Springtime of Youth](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/3/39/Youth_symbol.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/40?cb=20130120020002)  Springtime of Youth | The [*Springtime of Youth* wiki](https://springtimeofyouth.fandom.com/wiki/ "w:c:springtimeofyouth") has an article about this topic:  **[Rock Lee](https://springtimeofyouth.fandom.com/wiki/Rock_Lee "w:c:springtimeofyouth:Rock Lee")** |
| --- | --- |

- Rock Lee's name is derived from the [rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_music "wikipedia:Rock music") musical genre and is a play on [Bruce Lee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruce_Lee "wikipedia:Bruce Lee"), whose given name is pronounced the same as [blues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blues "wikipedia:Blues") in Japanese. The pun continues with his son, [Metal Lee](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Metal_Lee "Metal Lee").
- Rock Lee has his own spin-off series called *[Rock Lee's Springtime of Youth Full Power Ninja Chronicles](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rock_Lee%27s_Springtime_of_Youth_Full_Power_Ninja_Chronicles "Rock Lee's Springtime of Youth Full Power Ninja Chronicles")* by [Kenji Taira](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kenji_Taira "Kenji Taira"), an assistant of [Masashi Kishimoto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Masashi_Kishimoto "Masashi Kishimoto").
- Like his sensei, Rock Lee's character seems to be inspired by [Bruce Lee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruce_Lee "wikipedia:Bruce Lee"), as shown from his name and having the same birthday and hair style. The outfit Rock Lee wears in part one may have been based of Bruce Lee's jumpsuit from the 1973 film *Game of Death*.
	- Rock Lee's injury is also similar to the one Bruce Lee once received; both were also expected to never walk again.
- Rock Lee placed very high in the first four Naruto character popularity polls. He finished 5th in the first and second polls, 8th in the third poll and 7th in the fourth poll. However, his popularity slipped in the last two polls, finishing 13th in the fifth poll and 18th in the sixth and most recent. In the 3rd Databook, a combination of the previous six polls ranked him as the 9th most popular character overall.
- Studio Pierrot has consistently given him the height of 158 cm at 13yrs, instead of 158.5 cm as his Databook profile gives him.
- [Kishimoto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kishimoto "Kishimoto") 's official sketches of Rock Lee show that he is 177cm in *[The Last: Naruto the Movie](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/The_Last:_Naruto_the_Movie "The Last: Naruto the Movie")*.
- According to the databook(s):
	- Rock Lee's hobbies are his and [Might Guy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Might_Guy "Might Guy") 's intense [taijutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Taijutsu "Taijutsu") training regimes.
		- Lee wishes for rematches with [Neji](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Neji "Neji"), [Sasuke](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke "Sasuke"), and [Gaara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gaara "Gaara"), and for an all-out match with [Naruto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto "Naruto").
		- Lee's favourite foods are medium spicy curry rice and curry pilaf, though, like his sensei, he finds it impossible to dislike any sort of food.
		- Lee has completed 61 official missions in total: 25 D-rank, 21 C-rank, 4 B-rank, 10 A-rank, 1 S-rank.
		- Lee's favourite words are "Endeavour" (努力, *doryoku*), "Guts" (根性, *konjō*), and "Love" (愛, *ai*).[^3]
- In *[Naruto: Ultimate Ninja Heroes](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto:_Ultimate_Ninja_Heroes "Naruto: Ultimate Ninja Heroes")*, Lee calls himself "Leaf Village's Noble Savage".
