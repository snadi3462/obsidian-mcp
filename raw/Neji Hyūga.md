**Neji Hyūga** (日向ネジ, *Hyūga Neji*) was a [shinobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shinobi "Shinobi") of [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure") 's [Hyūga clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hy%C5%ABga_clan "Hyūga clan"). Though a prodigy even by the Hyūga's standards, Neji was a member of the clan's branch house; no matter how skilled he became, he would always be in service to the Hyūga's main house, a fact that convinced him fate was predetermined. After experiencing [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki") 's refusal to be limited by such an ideology, Neji realised his fate was what he chose it to be, and as a member of [Team Guy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Guy "Team Guy") he sought the strength necessary to make the future he wanted for his family and friends.

## Background

![A young Neji before receiving his cursed seal.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/1/1f/Young_Neji.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150128190014)

A young Neji before receiving his cursed seal.

Neji was born the son of [Hizashi Hyūga](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hizashi_Hy%C5%ABga "Hizashi Hyūga"), placing him in one of the [Hyūga clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hy%C5%ABga_clan "Hyūga clan") 's branch houses. When the heiress of the main house, [Hinata](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hinata "Hinata"), turned three years old, Neji's forehead was branded with the [customary cursed seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hy%C5%ABga_Main_Family%27s_Juinjutsu "Hyūga Main Family's Juinjutsu") by his uncle, [Hiashi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hiashi "Hiashi").[^6] Despite how young he was at the time, Neji's natural talent with the Hyūga's trademark techniques were apparent to his father. For this reason, Hizashi resented that Neji was marked with a cursed seal, as he felt it bound him to a life of service to the main house when he should be destined for greater things. His anger would often [manifest](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Killing_Intent "Killing Intent") subconsciously, prompting Hiashi to activate Hizashi's cursed seal and punish him with pain, an act that traumatised Neji.[^7]

Shortly after Neji received his cursed seal, a [Kumogakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kumogakure "Kumogakure") [Head Ninja](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Head_Ninja "Head Ninja") visiting [Konoha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha "Konoha") took advantage of a peace treaty and [attempted to kidnap Hinata](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hy%C5%ABga_Affair "Hyūga Affair") in order to gain the secrets of the [Byakugan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Byakugan "Byakugan"). Hiashi stopped and killed the Head Ninja.[^8] In what became known as the [Hyūga Affair](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hy%C5%ABga_Affair "Hyūga Affair"), Kumo denied the allegations made against its Head Ninja and insisted Hiashi's actions were a declaration of war by Konoha; the only way to avoid hostilities between the two villages would be if Hiashi's body were turned over to Kumo as compensation. Hiashi was willing to do so if it meant protecting Konoha, but that in turn would give Kumo the Byakugan. Hizashi, Hiashi's twin brother, volunteered to be his body double since his cursed seal would destroy his Byakugan at the time of his death, a fate he insisted upon over Hiashi's protests. Neji was too young to understand what was happening at the time, and over the years came to believe his father had been forced to die for the main house.[^9]

Neji became spiteful towards the members of the main house for what he believed they'd done to his father; in the anime, he missed no opportunity to try and harm Hinata.[^10] He also came to believe that the course of a person's life was determined from the moment of their birth and could be altered under no circumstances. When he graduated from the [Academy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Academy "Academy") at the top of his class he was added to [Team Guy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Guy "Team Guy"), where his philosophy placed him at odds with one of his teammates, [Rock Lee](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rock_Lee "Rock Lee"): Lee wanted to become a great ninja despite his limitations, which Neji believed was impossible. [Tenten](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tenten "Tenten") was easily wowed by Neji, but Lee became determined to defeat Neji in a fight to prove him wrong. Their instructor, [Might Guy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Might_Guy "Might Guy"), encouraged this, believing competition and the drive to succeed would push them to new heights.

## Personality

![Neji's branch family seal, his perceived inescapable fate.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/a/aa/Neji_cursemark.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150128190233)

Neji's branch family seal, his perceived inescapable fate.

As a child, Neji was kind and prone to smiling; on Hinata's third birthday, he confided to his father that he thought she was cute.[^11] He lost these qualities after his father's death, growing serious and cold, more mature than most his age. For years he was driven by a [fatalistic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fatalism "wikipedia:Fatalism") belief that his and everyone else's fates were determined from birth and were inescapable. He perceived his own destiny as being that of a rare talent, causing him to arrogantly place himself above those he believes are "weaker" than himself. He also perceived that his talents would always be handicapped by mandatory servitude to the Hyūga's main house, which he resented—and this belief further fuelled his animosity towards the main house and its members.

Neji shared his philosophy about fate with those he met, gladly telling them when they are straying from the paths that have been set out for them by, for example, trying to exceed their natural limitations or trying to defeat him in combat when they lack any obvious ability to do so, denying the value of hard work,[^12] despite the effort he put into his own training. When he shared his philosophy with Hinata, she concluded that his insistence upon destiny was actually a sore spot for him that caused him inner suffering: he so badly wanted to prove himself to the main house yet feels incapable of doing so. Neji attempted to kill her in anger for her words. [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki") also disagreed with Neji, claiming that Neji was not realising his destiny because he was in fact holding himself back. Neji initially scoffed at Naruto's suggestion, but he was forced to reevaluate when Naruto, a "failure", defeats him, a "genius", in a fight.

From his experience with Naruto, Neji came to view fate as not something inescapable but that, rather, one's destiny is what one makes of it; he felt indebted to Naruto for releasing him from his prior "darkness". The destiny Neji ended up selecting for himself was to become strong enough to never lose again, although his inevitable destiny would soon come to hold true. In pursuit of this goal he trained with his teammates, whose own dreams he became supportive of, and later with members of the main house, whom he gradually started mending relations with. Over time his desire to get stronger became as much a motivation to never lose as it is to protect his friends and family. In particular, he embraced his responsibility as a member of a branch house to keep Hinata safe, showing greater concern for her than anyone else in combat situations and,[^13] ultimately, giving his life to save hers.

Birds were often used to characterise Neji's philosophy and growth: while he felt bound by destiny and the cursed seal on his forehead, he described his personal experiences as being like a caged bird;[^14] after being defeated by Naruto, he observed to his father's memory how many birds there were freely flying in the world;[^15] when trying to overcome the limitations of his [Byakugan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Byakugan "Byakugan"), he observed birds at a distance but could never spot the one at his blind-spot;[^16] after his death, the [Allied Shinobi Forces](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Allied_Shinobi_Forces "Allied Shinobi Forces") took on a bird formation in his memory.[^17]

## Appearance

- ![Neji in Part I.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/25/Neji_body.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/70?cb=20160304052819)
	Neji in Part I.

1/3

Neji had fair skin and long black hair, which the anime usually depicted as dark brown. When his Byakugan was not active he has featureless white eyes; the anime gave them a slight lavender tint. In Part I, Neji wore a beige-coloured shirt, a dull blue shirt beneath that, and mesh armour beneath that. He wore dark purple (dark brown in other art and media), blue shinobi sandals, and wrapped bandages around his right arm, chest, and right leg. He tied his hair back in a loose ponytail tied a few inches above the end. He wore a black [forehead protector](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Forehead_protector "Forehead protector"), under which was a smaller headband with two straps that frame the sides of his face, both of which hide his [Hyūga main family's juinjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hy%C5%ABga_main_family%27s_juinjutsu "Hyūga main family's juinjutsu").

In Part II, Neji changed the clothing and bandages of his former wardrobe for a white kimono-shirt with long, loose sleeves, matching pants, a navy-grey apron tied around his waist, and black shinobi sandals. He stopped wearing the straps that framed his face, instead allowing his now longer hair to do that instead. When on missions, he usually carried a tan and black drawstring bag over his shoulder. During the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War"), he swapped much of his wardrobe for the standard Konohagakure [flak jacket](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Flak_jacket "Flak jacket") and other attire. At the war's start, he kept his hair tied in a ponytail, but as the war progresses, he allowed it to hang loose.

## Abilities

![Neji's mastery of one his clan's secret techniques.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/3/3f/Neji_vs_Naruto.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150109173830)

Neji's mastery of one his clan's secret techniques.

From a very young age, Neji's aptitude as a natural genius was evident, even by the [Hyūga clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hy%C5%ABga_clan "Hyūga clan") 's high standards.[^18] As a member of the branch house, Neji was technically forbidden to learn any of the clan's advanced techniques. However, even without formal instruction, he learned how to perform many of these techniques from observation alone, his talent surpassing the Main Branch.[^19] Hiashi, so deeply impressed by Neji's raw talent and rapid growth despite being self-taught, came to believe that Neji should have been the successor of the Main Branch and would eventually surpass all other Hyūga to ever exist,[^20] the latter of which he succeeded in by Part II despite his still young age,[^3] considered the greatest genius and strongest member of his clan.[^21] [^22] Besides his advanced clan traits, Neji graduated from the [Academy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Academy "Academy") at the top of his class, which in turn convinced [Rock Lee](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rock_Lee "Rock Lee") that Neji was the top competitor during his first [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams "Chūnin Exams"),[^23] hailed as the strongest genin,[^19] with parameters already at the chūnin-level.[^1] He was the only [Konoha 11](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_11 "Konoha 11") member to become a [jōnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/J%C5%8Dnin "Jōnin") during the timeskip. By the end of Part I, Hiashi started training Neji in all of the Hyūga's secret techniques. He could also use [Fire](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fire "Fire"), [Water](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water "Water"), and [Earth Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Earth_Release "Earth Release").[^4]

### Byakugan

![Neji's Byakugan.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/1/18/Byakugan.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150314104523)

Neji's Byakugan.

As a Hyūga, Neji possessed the [Byakugan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Byakugan "Byakugan"). While a common [dōjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/D%C5%8Djutsu "Dōjutsu") in his clan, Neji's was noted to be especially powerful. With these eyes, he had a near-360° penetrative field of vision. A small blind-spot exists behind his third upper-thoracic vertebrae — a weakness he was well aware of and trained extensively to overcome, to no avail. Instead, he took extra precautions to compensate for it by emitting [chakra](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra "Chakra") around the area, giving him a way to sense any approaching attacks and slowing them enough to give him time to respond.

Neji’s Byakugan allowed him to see [chakra pathway systems](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_pathway_system "Chakra pathway system") and the 361 [tenketsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tenketsu "Tenketsu") that run along it. He was able to increase his Byakugan's range over time: whereas he could only see things 50 metres away in Part I,[^24] by Part II he could see things from at least 800 metres.[^25]

### Taijutsu

![Neji using the Eight Trigrams Palms Revolving Heaven.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/b4/Eight_Trigrams_Palms_Revolving_Heaven.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150316094721)

Neji using the Eight Trigrams Palms Revolving Heaven.

As with most members of the Hyūga clan, Neji specialised in [taijutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Taijutsu "Taijutsu"). He excelled at systematically taking down his opponents with precise strikes to key points in the body. Even more, he demonstrated blazing speed and combined with superb reflexes, coordination, and agility, he could seamlessly dodge nearly any attack while simultaneously being able to counterattack his opponent. These traits made him especially formidable at close range, forcing enemies to keep their distance, where Neji's skill made him difficult to hit.

Neji was trained from an early age in his clan's secret signature [Gentle Fist](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gentle_Fist "Gentle Fist") fighting style, which takes advantage of the Byakugan's piercing vision to strike at the opponent's chakra system directly, constricting or cutting off their chakra flow with only minimal physical contact. Because the chakra pathway system is closely intertwined with many of the body's major organs, debilitating and even fatal damage can be done just as easily. While Gentle Fist strikes are by default difficult to defend against, Neji's ingenuity and methods made his usage of the style especially effective.

![Neji and Hinata combining their Eight Trigrams Vacuum Palms.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/a/ae/Hakke_K%C5%ABhekish%C5%8D.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150221070203)

Neji and Hinata combining their Eight Trigrams Vacuum Palms.

Implementing several of the style's most powerful techniques, Neji was very versatile in battle. With the [Eight Trigrams Sixty-Four Palms](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eight_Trigrams_Sixty-Four_Palms "Eight Trigrams Sixty-Four Palms"), he could attack multiple targets at once, either several opponents' tenketsu to restrain them concurrently or deflect incoming volleys of projectiles; in the anime, he improved the technique to [double the number of strikes](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eight_Trigrams_One_Hundred_Twenty-Eight_Palms "Eight Trigrams One Hundred Twenty-Eight Palms"). Defensively, he could use [Eight Trigrams Palms Revolving Heaven](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eight_Trigrams_Palms_Revolving_Heaven "Eight Trigrams Palms Revolving Heaven") to expel chakra from his body and deflect all incoming attacks from all directions at once. If restrained and thus unable to use Revolving Heaven, he could instead [release chakra from his tenketsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gentle_Fist_Art_One_Blow_Body "Gentle Fist Art One Blow Body") to free himself. In Part II, Neji began using the [Eight Trigrams Vacuum Palm](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eight_Trigrams_Vacuum_Palm "Eight Trigrams Vacuum Palm") to attack targets outside his physical range or push away incoming attacks; when performed in [combination with others](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eight_Trigrams_Vacuum_Wall_Palm "Eight Trigrams Vacuum Wall Palm"), its strength is accordingly increased.[^26]

## Part I

### Chūnin Exams

Main article: [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams_\(Arc\) "Chūnin Exams (Arc)")

![Team Guy preparing for the Chūnin Exams.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/87/Team_Guy_Part_1.PNG/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150126171001)

Team Guy preparing for the Chūnin Exams.

After being held back from entering the [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams "Chūnin Exams") the year before in order to hone their skills, [Team Guy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Guy "Team Guy") takes part in the Chūnin Exams occurring in Konoha. Neji, [Tenten](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tenten "Tenten"), and [Rock Lee](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rock_Lee "Rock Lee") decide to try and avoid drawing attention to themselves so that the other competitors will not team up against them, something they accomplish by allowing themselves to be bullied and acting defenceless. During the first exam, Neji uses his [Byakugan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Byakugan "Byakugan") to copy off of other examinees' tests.

After they pass to the second stage, taking place in the [Forest of Death](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Forest_of_Death "Forest of Death"), Team Guy splits up to search for weak, rival teams that they can take one of the scrolls they need from. Neji comes across [Team 10](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Asuma "Team Asuma") during his search who, although he's isn't willing to pretend he doesn't know where they're hiding, he decides are too weak for him to bother with. When he returns to Team Guy's rendezvous point, he and Tenten find that Lee is uncharacteristically late. They go looking for him and find he's been defeated by [Team Dosu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Dosu "Team Dosu"). Neji prepares to fight them, confident he can beat them, but decides to let [Sasuke Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Uchiha "Sasuke Uchiha") fight them instead after he senses Sasuke's chakra. Sasuke drives Team Dosu off and, while Tenten collects Lee, Neji observes Sasuke, intrigued by his skills.

![Neji fights Hinata.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/5/5a/Gentle_fist.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150829085939)

Neji fights Hinata.

Team Guy completes the second test, but in order to qualify for the finals they must take part in one-on-one preliminary matches. Neji observes the early matches to learn about people he may face in the finals before he is finally matched against [Hinata Hyūga](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hinata_Hy%C5%ABga "Hinata Hyūga"). Neji, who previously promised to [Might Guy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Might_Guy "Might Guy") that he would not take any actions against Hinata during the exams, encourages Hinata to give up not only the match but also the life of a ninja, citing her meek personality and her lack of talent. His piercing analysis of her brings Hinata to tears, but she is motivated to continue by the encouragement of [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki"). In their first exchange of [Gentle Fist](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gentle_Fist "Gentle Fist") -blows, Neji seals her [tenketsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tenketsu "Tenketsu"), causing her attacks to have no effect on him.

Despite no longer having a way to damage Neji, Hinata keeps fighting. He continues encouraging her to forfeit and increases the amount of damage his blows do to her, but Hinata refuses to back down. Finally, after her internal organs are left badly injured and she has difficulty standing, Hinata uses Neji's words against him, arguing that he cares so much about destiny and being worthy of the Hyūga because his own destiny deprives him of the Hyūga's recognition. Neji is enraged and moves to kill her, but he is held back by Guy and the other observing [jōnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/J%C5%8Dnin "Jōnin"). Nevertheless, Hinata collapses and is left in critical condition from her injuries, Neji is declared the winner, and Naruto, moved by Hinata's perseverance and angered by Neji's actions, vows to defeat him during the finals. Neji watches the remaining matches and is amazed by Lee's performance against [Gaara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gaara "Gaara"). After the preliminaries conclude, the first round matchups for the finals are selected and Neji is matched against Naruto.

![Neji fights Naruto in the finals.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/e/ef/Naruto_vs_Neji.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150109174006)

Neji fights Naruto in the finals.

After a month of preparation, Naruto and Neji face each other. Naruto starts by using [shadow clones](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_clone "Shadow clone") to engage Neji, trying to overwhelm his taijutsu skills with sheer numbers. Neji's Byakugan is unable to tell the shadow clones from the real Naruto and for that reason Naruto is able to land some blows, but Neji deflects them with [Revolving Heaven](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Revolving_Heaven "Revolving Heaven") and eventually seals Naruto's chakra with [Sixty-Four Palms](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sixty-Four_Palms "Sixty-Four Palms"). Neji declares Naruto a failure who is destined to lose to him, a genius. When Naruto is unwilling to believe him, Neji explains [his father](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hizashi "Hizashi") 's death and the differences between the Hyūga's houses. Rather than convince Naruto that he is right, his words convince Naruto that Neji is wrong, as Naruto has personal experience with persevering despite others' expectations.

Although his own chakra is cut off, Naruto has another source: the [Nine-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tails "Nine-Tails") '. Neji is surprised by it and, unaware of what it is, is startled by the chakra's ferocity. Naruto's speed and strength are heightened dramatically, allowing him to keep up with Neji. Neji uses Revolving Heaven to deflect a particularly powerful blow, flinging both him and Naruto apart. When the smoke clears, Naruto appears defeated and Neji, assuming he's won, begins boasting. As he talks, Naruto bursts from the ground beneath him, knocking him out with an uppercut to the chin. Realising that the Naruto he saw defeated was a shadow clone covering the hole the real Naruto was digging towards him, Neji scolds himself for being defeated by Naruto's signature ability. In response, Naruto reveals that [clone techniques](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_techniques "Clone techniques") were once his worst ability. Before he's declared the winner, Naruto tells Neji to stop holding his natural genius back with trivial concerns about fate.

![Hiashi apologises to Neji.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/03/Hiashi_bows_to_Neji.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140719080221)

Hiashi apologises to Neji.

As Neji recovers, he is visited by his uncle, [Hiashi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hiashi "Hiashi"), who was observing his match. Hoping to correct Neji's misconceptions, he explains the truth surrounding Neji's father's death: that Hizashi chose to die not in service to the main house as Neji has long thought, but because he wanted to protect Hiashi, his brother; in doing so, Hizashi selected his own fate. (In the anime, this is revealed to Neji in a letter written by his father before his death, Neji recognising his father's handwriting.) Neji does not believe the story since he's only hearing it now. Hiashi, having expected this and having no further proof, bows to Neji and begs his forgiveness for not telling him sooner. Neji is moved to tears by the gesture and bids Hiashi to stand, finally believing him. While watching the birds outside, Neji decides he'd like to choose his destiny just like his father did, and that he wants his destiny to be to never lose again.

### Konoha Crush

Main article: [Konoha Crush](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Crush_\(Arc\) "Konoha Crush (Arc)") In the anime, two [Kumogakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kumogakure "Kumogakure") ninja attempt to kidnap an unconscious Hinata during the confusion caused by the [invasion of Konoha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Invasion_of_Konoha "Invasion of Konoha"). The [Hyūga Elder](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hy%C5%ABga_Elder "Hyūga Elder") sends Neji, Tenten, and [Kiba Inuzuka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kiba_Inuzuka "Kiba Inuzuka") to rescue her. Once they catch up with them, Neji draws the Kumo-nin away while Tenten and Kiba escape with Hinata. He is able to defeat one, but is no match for [Sekiei](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sekiei_\(spectator\) "Sekiei (spectator)") 's abilities and can only keep him busy until Hiashi arrives to defeat him.

### Sasuke Recovery Mission

Main article: [Sasuke Recovery Mission](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Recovery_Mission "Sasuke Recovery Mission") Although Konoha emerges from the invasion victorious, the village suffers numerous casualties that leave it understaffed in the aftermath. ![Neji fights Kidōmaru.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/d/da/Your_Opponent_is_Me%21.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150128190359)

Neji fights Kidōmaru.

Once they catch up with the Sound Four, Neji uses his Byakugan to help them plan their attack, which is foiled only by the unforeseen actions of [Ukon](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ukon "Ukon"); the Recovery Team is trapped in [Jirōbō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jir%C5%8Db%C5%8D "Jirōbō") 's [Earth Prison Dome of Magnificent Nothingness](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Earth_Prison_Dome_of_Magnificent_Nothingness "Earth Prison Dome of Magnificent Nothingness"). Neji is able to locate a weakness in the prison's fortifications and they escape, but they must leave [Chōji Akimichi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%8Dji_Akimichi "Chōji Akimichi") behind to delay Jirōbō while they go after the remaining Sound Four. When they catch up again, [Kidōmaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kid%C5%8Dmaru "Kidōmaru") uses his webbing to bind them. Only Neji, with the refined chakra control courtesy of his Gentle Fist training, is able to break Kidōmaru's webs. He tells the others to go on without him, but first warns them that one-on-one fights are probably going to be a continuing trend.

Kidōmaru tries to toy with Neji, binding his movements to the point that he cannot use his hands to break the webbing. Thinking Neji is defeated, Kidōmaru moves closer to finish him off. Neji takes that opportunity to release chakra from his entire body, loosening the webs and giving him an opening to use Sixty-Four Palms. Kidōmaru prevents the sealing of his tenketsu with an [Armour of Sticky Gold](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Armour_of_Sticky_Gold "Armour of Sticky Gold") and falls back, unwilling to let Neji trick him like that again. He starts attacking Neji from afar to determine the range of his vision and the completeness of his defences. From this observation, he starts to suspect Neji's Byakugan has a blind-spot, something he confirms upon further testing.

![Neji finishes Kidōmaru.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/c/cc/Neji_vs_kidomaru.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150125201405)

Neji finishes Kidōmaru.

Kidōmaru recognises Neji as a formidable opponent and makes to finish him off with his [Spider War Bow: Terrible Split](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Spider_War_Bow:_Terrible_Split "Spider War Bow: Terrible Split"), firing it directly at Neji's blind spot. However, Neji, guessing his blind spot has been discovered, is able to dodge enough to prevent being struck in a vital place. Kidōmaru modifies his arrows, increasing their speed and strength so that Neji won't be able to dodge and attaching a thread to them so he can guide them in flight. Neji realises he can't avoid it and allows himself to be hit. He immediately afterwards sends a charge of chakra through the connecting thread back to Kidōmaru, paralysing him. Neji then personally locates Kidōmaru and delivers a fatal blow. As he dies, Kidōmaru warns that the Recovery Team won't be able to bring Sasuke back to Konoha. Neji disagrees, believing that Naruto alone will be able to get through to Sasuke, just as he got through to Neji during the Chūnin Exams.

Neji is left in a critical condition due to his injuries and near death. Konoha eventually sends the [Medic Corps](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Medic_Corps "Medic Corps") after the Recovery Team. Neji is found, brought back to Konoha, and a team of medics mend his wounds using the [Healing Resuscitation Regeneration Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Healing_Resuscitation_Regeneration_Technique "Healing Resuscitation Regeneration Technique").

### Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax

Main article: [Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War:_Climax "Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax")

![The Hyūga protecting Naruto.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/c/ce/Hyuga_shield.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140612025324)

The Hyūga protecting Naruto.

Neji and the rest of the [Allied Shinobi Forces](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Allied_Shinobi_Forces "Allied Shinobi Forces") are sent to assist Naruto in his battle with the [Ten-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ten-Tails "Ten-Tails"). Recognising how crucial Naruto is to the war's victory, Neji, Hinata, and [Hiashi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hiashi "Hiashi") join forces to block attacks directed at him. The Ten-Tails' attacks rapidly become too numerous and Neji and Hiashi must break away to protect the other Allies from the Ten-Tails too. In their absence, the Ten-Tails fires several volleys of [wood](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood "Wood") projectiles at Naruto, which Hinata uses her body to defend him from. Neji arrives in time to protect her in turn with his own body, mortally wounding him.

![Neji dying on Naruto's shoulder.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/3/34/Neji_dies.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140606005510)

Neji dying on Naruto's shoulder.

Naruto calls for a medic but Neji accepts the fact that he can't be saved. He collapses over Naruto's shoulder and tells him to not to put his life at risk in the future, as he is now responsible for many other lives in addition to his own, one of which is Hinata's. As Neji watches Hinata cry for him, Naruto questions why Neji would give his life for him, to which Neji responds that it is because Naruto once called him a "genius". Neji dies, noting that he finally knows what it was like for [his father](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hizashi "Hizashi") to choose to die for his friends. Upon death, the [Hyūga main family's juinjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hy%C5%ABga_main_family%27s_juinjutsu "Hyūga main family's juinjutsu") activates, sealing his Byakugan.

## New Era

### Time Slip Arc

Main article: [Time Slip Arc](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Time_Slip_Arc "Time Slip Arc") In the anime, when the grown-up Sasuke and [Boruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Boruto_Uzumaki "Boruto Uzumaki") were sent back in time to shortly after Sasuke's defection, the two pretended to be travelling performers and were put under watch by Naruto. Past-Neji and his friends helped Boruto and Naruto clean up a bathhouse after the commotion Naruto and Jiraiya caused.

![Neji and Boruto talking about Naruto.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/bd/Boruto_and_Neji_talk.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20191117050844)

Neji and Boruto talking about Naruto.

Days later, after completing a training session with Hinata, Neji noticed Boruto eavesdropping and confronted him, having noticed Boruto through his Byakugan, and seeing that he was troubled, offered to talk to him. The two head to a nearby bridge, where Boruto admitted to having been exposed to the [berserk power](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kurama "Kurama") inside of Naruto and talked about being attacked. Neji insisted that Naruto is not one to be pitied and that he could be trusted. Neji told Boruto of his own experience and growth with Naruto, insisting that he learned from Naruto's example to not let one's past define them, and claimed that despite Naruto's lonely and sorrowful life, he was capable of fighting his fate of being deemed a monster, and perservered to become a trusted friend and comrade. Neji's words helped cheer up Boruto, who decided to continue his training with Naruto and refuse to allow his fears to overwhelm him, and as Boruto runs off, Neji mumbles that he should've asked for Boruto's name.

Later, after Sasuke and Boruto completed their mission to defeat [Urashiki Ōtsutsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Urashiki_%C5%8Ctsutsuki "Urashiki Ōtsutsuki"), Sasuke used his Sharingan to [erase](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genjutsu:_Sharingan "Genjutsu: Sharingan") Neji and everyone else's memories of the events to protect the timeline.

## Legacy

![Neji's grave.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/be/Neji%27s_Gravestone.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20161013121210)

Neji's grave.

[Obito Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Obito_Uchiha "Obito Uchiha") uses Neji as an example of the type of countless death that will keep occurring if [Naruto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto "Naruto") and the [Allied Shinobi Forces](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Allied_Shinobi_Forces "Allied Shinobi Forces") continue fighting. Rather than mourn Neji by surrendering, they celebrate his sacrifice with a counterattack in his memory after Hinata convinces Naruto that Neji's sacrifice will be for nothing if they give up: Naruto gives [version 1](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Version_1 "Version 1") cloaks to the entire Allied Forces and they manoeuvre into a bird-shaped formation, together breaking out of a prison created by the [Ten-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ten-Tails "Ten-Tails") and subsequently releasing it from Obito and [Madara Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Madara_Uchiha "Madara Uchiha") 's control.

After the war is won, Neji's body is taken back to Konoha and he is buried alongside the many other casualties. Though he is gone, he remains in the thoughts of those who knew him: in *[Konoha Hiden](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Hiden "Konoha Hiden")*, [Rock Lee](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rock_Lee "Rock Lee") regularly dreams of Neji, but the dreams never last long enough for Lee's liking; at Naruto and [Hinata](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hinata "Hinata") 's wedding, [Hanabi Hyūga](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hanabi_Hy%C5%ABga "Hanabi Hyūga") holds a picture of Neji during the ceremony; years later, Hinata regularly visits Neji's grave with her daughter, [Himawari Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Himawari_Uzumaki "Himawari Uzumaki"), who, though she never met him, regards him as her uncle.

This Shion, however, was actually Taruho, who had disguised himself as her to protect the real Shion from harm.

Neji and Sakura reunite with Lee and they distract the Gang of Four while Naruto helps Shion seal [Mōryō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/M%C5%8Dry%C5%8D "Mōryō"). From hearing about Lee's fight with the gang's other members, Neji theorises that they received their chakra from an external source. They provoke the gang into depleting their chakra and, when Kusuna moves in to replenish the chakra they've used, Neji kills him, leaving the rest of the Gang of Four unable to fight. With their mission accomplished, the team waits for Naruto to return with Shion.

## Trivia

- The name "Neji" means "screw/whirl" (螺子) or to "twist/wrench" (捩), which may be referring to his Eight Trigrams Palms Revolving Heaven. It's also possibly a play on "Nejia", an internal Chinese martial art that focuses on developing internal chi through whirling and spinning techniques. His family name "Hyūga" means "place in the sun" (日向).
- According to the databook(s):
	- Neji's hobby was [meditation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meditation "wikipedia:Meditation").
		- Neji's desired opponents were [Hiashi Hyūga](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hiashi_Hy%C5%ABga "Hiashi Hyūga") and [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki").
		- Neji's favourite food was [herring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herring_\(food\) "wikipedia:Herring (food)") [soba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soba "wikipedia:Soba"), while his least favourite was [pumpkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pumpkin "wikipedia:Pumpkin"). In addition to the aforementioned, Neji has also been demonstrated to have low capsaicin tolerance in the anime when he would be knocked out cold each time he was fed the [Curry of Life](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Curry_of_Life "Curry of Life").
		- Neji had completed 62 official missions in total: 26 D-rank, 19 C-rank, 4 B-rank, 11 A-rank, and 2 S-rank.
		- Neji's favourite phrase was "Flying leap" (飛躍, *hiyaku*) which refers to making rapid progress in something.
		- Neji's personality had been described as cool and realistic.
- In the [fourth databook](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jin_no_Sho "Jin no Sho"), [hiden](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hiden "Hiden") is not listed among Neji's special characteristics, despite his usage of at least one [hiden technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gentle_Fist_Art_One_Blow_Body "Gentle Fist Art One Blow Body").
- In an interview, [Masashi Kishimoto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Masashi_Kishimoto "Masashi Kishimoto") stated that the origin of the name [Boruto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Boruto "Boruto") is from Neji, who brought Naruto and Hinata together, and can have the meaning of connecting. Boruto (bolt) is another way of saying Neji (screw).[^28]
- Neji places well in the [Naruto Character Popularity Polls](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Character_Popularity_Polls "Naruto Character Popularity Polls"), reaching an all-time high of sixth most popular; he and [Rock Lee](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rock_Lee "Rock Lee") are roughly matched in overall popularity.
- In the [omake](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Omake#Neji_Chronicles "Omake") for [*Naruto: Shippūden* episode 156](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Surpassing_the_Master "Surpassing the Master"), Naruto points out how Neji doesn't appear in *Naruto: Shippūden* very often, whereas Lee and Tenten frequently do. Neji argues that, as a jōnin, he is constantly away on missions and never has free time.
- Studio Pierrot has consistently given him the height of 158 cm at 13yrs, instead of 159.2cm as his Databook profile gives him.
- Despite technically being cousins with Hinata, she would commonly refer to him as "brother" (Nii-san). This is possibly because their respective fathers are twins, which could arguably also make them half-siblings, making it fit. To which, Hinata's children would refer to their late cousin-once-removed as "uncle" (Oji-san).
