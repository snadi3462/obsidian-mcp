**Kakashi Hatake** (はたけカカシ, *Hatake Kakashi*) is a [shinobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shinobi "Shinobi") of [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure") 's [Hatake clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hatake_clan "Hatake clan"). Famed as **Kakashi of the [Sharingan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sharingan "Sharingan")** (写輪眼のカカシ, *Sharingan no Kakashi*) and the **Copy Ninja** (コピー忍者, *Kopī Ninja*), he is one of Konoha's most talented ninja, regularly looked to for advice and leadership despite his personal dislike of responsibility. To his students on [Team 7](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Kakashi "Team Kakashi"), Kakashi emphasises the importance of teamwork; he himself received this lesson, along with the Sharingan, from his childhood friend, [Obito Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Obito_Uchiha "Obito Uchiha"). After the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War"), Kakashi becomes Konoha's **Sixth [Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage "Hokage")** (六代目火影, *Rokudaime Hokage*, *literally meaning:* Sixth Fire Shadow).

## Background

### Early life

![Kakashi finds his father dead.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/0c/Sakumo_commits_suicide.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20131220220748)

Kakashi finds his father dead.

Because his mother died when he was very young,[^15] Kakashi was raised during his early years by his father, [Sakumo](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sakumo "Sakumo"). Sakumo was famed throughout the shinobi world, having saved Konoha on at least one occasion; Kakashi in particular revered his father.

In the Academy, Kakashi earned top grades, earning him recognition as a prodigy and the best of his generation.[^16] [^4] Ultimately, he became very popular amongst his peers. With his talents soon being recognised, at age 5, Kakashi graduated from the Academy at the top of his class in a single year. When his classmates, [Rin Nohara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rin_Nohara "Rin Nohara") and Obito Uchiha graduated three years later, they were teamed together, with Kakashi, under the leadership of [Minato Namikaze](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Minato_Namikaze "Minato Namikaze"). In the anime, Minato gave the team a [bell test](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Bell_test "Bell test") at its formation, assigning the three to take the two bells he kept on his person. Minato often held back less against the prodigious Kakashi than with Rin and Obito so they couldn't obtain the bells without teamwork. While realising this, Kakashi unfortunately only used Obito and Rin as a means to an end to obtain the bells. Nonetheless, Minato passed them because they accomplished the goal of the test by working as a team. Minato did encourage them to improve their teamwork afterwards, a message that Obito and Rin took to heart, but fell upon deaf ears with Kakashi.[^17] [Team Minato](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Minato "Team Minato") would go on many missions during its career, but Kakashi's devotion to the rules often made him difficult to work with;[^18] Obito, already jealous of Kakashi's natural talent and popularity, was frequently at odds with him about this behaviour. At age 6, Konoha officials allowed Kakashi to compete in the [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams "Chūnin Exams") with his team, which he passed by defeating [Might Guy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Might_Guy "Might Guy") and became a [chūnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin "Chūnin").[^19]

During one of Sakumo's missions - after Kakashi was promoted to chūnin - [^20] Sakumo made the decision to save the lives of his teammates rather than complete the assignment. The mission's failure had disastrous consequences for the [Land of Fire](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_Fire "Land of Fire"), causing many in Konoha, including the teammates he saved, to vilify him for abandoning his duties. Disgraced, Sakumo committed suicide. Seeing what his father went through and determined not to make the same mistakes, Kakashi decided that following the [Shinobi Rules](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shinobi_Rules "Shinobi Rules") must always take priority.[^21]

### Third Shinobi World War

Konoha eventually became embroiled in the [Third Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Shinobi_World_War "Third Shinobi World War"). As part of the war effort, [Team Minato](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Minato "Team Minato") was assigned to destroy the [Kannabi Bridge](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kannabi_Bridge "Kannabi Bridge") in [Kusagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kusagakure "Kusagakure") in order to cut off [Iwagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Iwagakure "Iwagakure") 's supply line. Minato was needed on the front lines at the time, leaving Kakashi, recently promoted to [jōnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/J%C5%8Dnin "Jōnin") at age 12,[^22] [^23] in charge. Before embarking on the mission, Minato and Rin gave him presents to celebrate his promotion; Obito forgot to get him anything. Shortly after entering Kusa, they encountered an Iwa-scout, [Mahiru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mahiru "Mahiru"). Kakashi tried to eliminate him with his new jutsu, [Chidori](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chidori "Chidori"), but the attack speed left him vulnerable to counter-attack, forcing Minato to step in, save Kakashi, and kill Mahiru himself. Before leaving them, Minato advised Kakashi to not use the Chidori again.

![Kakashi saving Obito.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/21/Kakashi_White_Light_Chakra_Sabre.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20131220214339)

Kakashi saving Obito.

Kakashi, Rin, and Obito continued further in to Kusa. They were eventually found by Mahiru's teammates, [Kakkō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakk%C5%8D "Kakkō") and [Taiseki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Taiseki "Taiseki"), who kidnapped Rin in order to find out what their mission was. Obito immediately suggested that they rescue her, but Kakashi elected to abandon Rin, believing it was more important to finish the mission before concerning themselves with her safety. Obito refused to go along with this and went off to save Rin by himself. Before he left, he told Kakashi that Sakumo had been a hero and that, although it was bad to abandon one's mission, it was worse to abandon one's teammates. Kakashi began carrying out the mission alone, but ultimately decided that Obito was right and went to join him. He arrived in time to save Obito from Taiseki with his [White Light Chakra Sabre](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/White_Light_Chakra_Sabre "White Light Chakra Sabre"), which he inherited from his father.

Taiseki [turned invisible](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hiding_with_Camouflage_Technique "Hiding with Camouflage Technique") and tried launching a sneak attack on Obito. Kakashi protected him, but his left eye was badly damaged in the process. In that moment, Obito awakened his Sharingan and used it to kill Taiseki. They then entered the cave where Rin was being held, drove off Kakkō, and released Rin from the [genjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genjutsu "Genjutsu") he placed her under. Kakkō retaliated by forcing a cave-in and Kakashi, due to his damaged eye, was struck in his blind spot and had difficulty avoiding the falling rocks. Before he could be crushed by a large boulder, Obito pushed him out of the way, becoming trapped instead. Unable to get free and knowing his injuries were too serious to survive, Obito decided to make his last act giving Kakashi the present he forgot to give him earlier: his Sharingan to replace the eye Kakashi lost.

After Rin transplanted Obito's Sharingan into him, Kakashi confronted Kakkō. His White Light Chakra Sabre was destroyed during the ensuing fight, but he succeeded in killing Kakkō with Chidori; due to the Sharingan's heightened vision, Kakashi was finally able to handle its speed. He went back to where Obito and Rin were, but Iwa reinforcements soon arrived and started constricting the rubble. Obito asked Kakashi to take Rin away and keep her safe, which Kakashi did, leaving Obito behind. As the Iwa-nin started surrounding them, Kakashi attacked them for as long as he could, holding them off until Minato eventually tracked them down and finished off the rest. Minato assisted them with destroying the Kannabi Bridge and then returned with them to Konoha to mourn Obito's death. Despite many from the Uchiha clan being against Kakashi wielding a Sharingan as he had no blood-ties to them, [Fugaku Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fugaku_Uchiha "Fugaku Uchiha"), the Uchiha head, chose to honour Obito's dying actions and let Kakashi keep his gift.[^24]

![Kakashi unintentionally kills Rin.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/b9/Kakashi_stabs_Rin.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230919004010)

Kakashi unintentionally kills Rin.

During a later mission, Rin was kidnapped by [Kirigakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kirigakure "Kirigakure"). Kakashi was eventually able to rescue her and started taking her back to Konoha. Along the way, Rin revealed that Kiri had sealed the [Three-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Three-Tails "Three-Tails") into her body with the intention to, once she inevitably lost control of it, have it unleashed on Konoha and destroy the village from within. In order to prevent this from happening, Rin begged Kakashi to kill her, but he refused, unwilling to break his promise to Obito to protect her and hoping to find some other solution. When Kiri-nin caught up with them and made false efforts to retrieve her, Kakashi fought them off with his renamed Chidori, the [Lightning Cutter](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning_Cutter "Lightning Cutter"). During one of these attacks, Rin jumped in front of Kakashi's attack, dying by his hand so that Konoha would be safe.[^25] The trauma of this caused his Sharingan to evolve into a [Mangekyō Sharingan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mangeky%C5%8D_Sharingan "Mangekyō Sharingan") shortly before Kakashi passed out.[^26] He was later found by Konoha reinforcements, but none could explain the slaughter of all the Kiri forces, not knowing of [Tobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Obito_Uchiha "Obito Uchiha") 's actions.[^27]

### Anbu Career

![Kakashi joins the Anbu.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/f/ff/Kakashi_Anbu.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140206124711)

Kakashi joins the Anbu.

Because he'd lost two teammates in such a short timespan, his role in Rin's death, and his failure to honour Obito's last request by protecting her, Kakashi dropped into a depression. In the anime, many believed he purposely killed Rin to prevent her from leaking information, earning him the nickname "Friend-Killer Kakashi". Many, especially those in Anbu believed he would kill a comrade without hesitation if it was for the sake of completing the mission. He would also spend his days avoiding friends and former classmates and at night he would be haunted by dreams of himself killing Rin again. Minato, now the new [Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage "Hokage"), tried to help Kakashi emerge from the darkness he'd fallen into after Obito and Rin's deaths by assigning him to the [Anbu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Anbu "Anbu") in order to have him close in an attempt to alleviate his pain.[^28] Kakashi did well in the Anbu, eventually becoming a captain and the leader of [Team Ro](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Ro "Team Ro"). However, his successes were owed to his cold behaviour and his ruthlessness in combat, signs that he was still upset by Rin's death. Minato therefore tried a different tactic: assigning Kakashi to protect Minato's wife, [Kushina Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kushina_Uzumaki "Kushina Uzumaki"), during her pregnancy. Kakashi carried out his duties faithfully, monitoring Kushina from the shadows whenever she left her home.[^29] During his time off, he would visit Rin's [grave](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Cemetery "Konoha Cemetery") and Obito's [engraving](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Memorial_Stone "Memorial Stone") to tell them his regrets and how life was going without them.[^30]

![Kakashi and his peers are blocked from helping Konoha.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/3/3a/Kurenai_and_the_young_protesters.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20120210133920)

Kakashi and his peers are blocked from helping Konoha.

During the [Nine-Tailed Demon Fox's Attack](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tailed_Demon_Fox%27s_Attack "Nine-Tailed Demon Fox's Attack"), Kakashi and many of Konoha's other young ninja were prevented from helping defend the village, instead being confined within a [barrier](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Barrier "Barrier") to keep them safe.[^31] Minato eventually saved the village from the [Nine-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tails "Nine-Tails"), but at the cost of his and Kushina's lives. After which, [Danzō Shimura](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Danz%C5%8D_Shimura "Danzō Shimura") approached a grieving young Kakashi, noting that it was [Hiruzen Sarutobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hiruzen_Sarutobi "Hiruzen Sarutobi") 's orders for the children, even high-ranking ninja like Kakashi, to not help battle the Nine-Tails, ultimately preventing Kakashi from possibly saving Minato and Kushina. Convincing an emotional Kakashi that the Third was not best for the future of the village, Kakashi agreed to join Danzō's [Root](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Root "Root") division and spy on the Hokage for him. During a mission, Kakashi encountered another Root member codenamed " [Kinoe](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kinoe "Kinoe") " who was able to use [Wood Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Release "Wood Release").[^29] Knowing that Wood Release was unique to the [First Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/First_Hokage "First Hokage"), Kakashi broke in to the [Hokage Residence](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage_Residence "Hokage Residence") to see what he could learn about this anomaly. The Third caught him, but freely gave him the information he wanted: Konoha had tried to recreate the Wood Release powers in the past, but abandoned the project for killing too many test subjects. The Third then mused that the Nine-Tails might have been defeated and Minato saved had the research been successful.

![Kakashi spares Kinoe's life.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/6/64/Kakashi_spares_Kinoe.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140309151059)

Kakashi spares Kinoe's life.

From this meeting with the Third, it became clear to Kakashi that the Third was not the ineffectual, passionless leader Danzō had made him out to be. Moreover, Danzō had not used Kinoe to try and stop the Nine-Tails' attack. Realising he had picked the wrong side, Kakashi told the Third about Danzō's plans to assassinate him and, the following day, personally lured out the assassins, of whom Kinoe was one. Kakashi easily defeated him, but chose to spare his life in the belief that he would become a valuable ally in the future. Kakashi attempted to resign from the Anbu afterwards for working against the Hokage, but the Third insisted his service was invaluable and made Kakashi his right-hand man.[^32]

![Kakashi unable to stop Orochimaru.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/28/Kakashi_being_afraid_of_Orochimaru.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140309151141)

Kakashi unable to stop Orochimaru.

From investigating a series of disappearances, Kakashi provided a lead and it was discovered that [Orochimaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Orochimaru "Orochimaru") was behind the Wood Release research that produced Kinoe. In the anime, Kakashi attacked Orochimaru when the Third Hokage failed to apprehend him, but he was paralysed by Orochimaru's [killing intent](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Killing_intent "Killing intent") and Orochimaru escaped.[^33] Kakashi followed him once he regained his composure, but was captured by the [Iburi clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Iburi_clan "Iburi clan"), who were loyal to Orochimaru. Kinoe, their ally, convinced them to let him interrogate Kakashi. Once alone, Kinoe confided that Danzō was making his own plans against Orochimaru and, with help from [Yukimi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yukimi "Yukimi"), helped Kakashi get free.[^34] Kakashi later planned to use Yukimi to capture Orochimaru, since she was of great importance to him. Later, Kakashi explained that Yukimi's blood could temporarily bestow Orochimaru with the Iburi clan's smoke transformation, allowing him to slip across the border. Overhearing that, Yukimi slipped away and returned to the cave. Orochimaru ultimately killed most of the Iburi clan before escaping yet again, but Kakashi, recognising that Kinoe's relationship with Yukimi is much like his relationship with Rin was, helped keep her safe. Kakashi decided to leave and not report anything about the Iburi or Root's involvement.[^35]

![Kakashi and Kinoe surrounded by Root.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/e/eb/Kakashi_and_Kinoe_vs_Root.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230408180456)

Kakashi and Kinoe surrounded by Root.

Kakashi continued to investigate Orochimaru over the following years, eventually locating one of his secret labs three years after the latter's defection. While there, he was attacked by Kinoe, who Danzō had sent to take his Sharingan. Kakashi tried to reason with him by stating that friendship should take higher priority than the mission. When Kinoe accused Kakashi of violating that creed by killing Rin, enraged by that comment, Kakashi overpowered him and decided to bring him before the Third Hokage.[^36] Before they could leave, one of Orochimaru's [snake experiments](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Power_of_the_White_Snake "Power of the White Snake") escaped its test tube and attacked the duo. As Kinoe was taken captive, Kakashi managed to slay the creature. While saving Kinoe, the creature's corpse began emitting a poisonous vapour which quickly infected Kakashi. Kinoe got Kakashi out safely and, having accepted Kakashi's words, gave Kakashi an antidote and chose to abandon his mission whilst leaving Kakashi a message about it. When Kakashi woke up, he knew Danzō would punish Kinoe for insubordination and asked the Third for help. With the Third's permission, Kakashi forced his way into Root headquarters and stopped Danzō from applying a [cursed seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Cursed_Tongue_Eradication_Seal "Cursed Tongue Eradication Seal") to Kinoe. As Root forces started to surround them, the Third showed up to personally authorise Kakashi's actions, and further negotiated Kinoe's release from Root. Kakashi added Kinoe, now with the new codename "Tenzō", to Team Ro.[^37]

![Kakashi and Itachi mercilessly assassinate the Prajñā Group.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/4/4c/Kakashi_and_Itachi_Anbu.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230408183626)

Kakashi and Itachi mercilessly assassinate the Prajñā Group.

Two years later, [Itachi Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Itachi_Uchiha "Itachi Uchiha") was added to Team Ro. Kakashi and Itachi were at one point assigned to observe a meeting between Konoha and the [Land of Woods](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_Woods "Land of Woods"). When the Land of Woods' [Prajñā Group](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Praj%C3%B1%C4%81_Group "Prajñā Group") tried to betray the Konoha forces, Kakashi and Itachi moved in and defeated them. Although Kakashi was impressed by Itachi's abilities, he warned him against his merciless approach and encouraged him on the importance of friends.[^38] Sometime later, Itachi asked Kakashi if dead friends' requests should be honoured, which Kakashi said they should.[^39] Itachi was eventually promoted out of Team Ro and replaced by [Yūgao Uzuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Y%C5%ABgao_Uzuki "Yūgao Uzuki"). Shortly afterwards, Team Ro was sent to the [Uchiha clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uchiha_clan "Uchiha clan") 's compound with orders to pacify a brewing rebellion, only to find the [entire clan dead](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uchiha_Clan_Downfall "Uchiha Clan Downfall"). When news emerged that Itachi was responsible and that he had killed his best friend, [Shisui Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shisui_Uchiha "Shisui Uchiha"), in pursuit of power, Kakashi lamented his failure to have had a better influence on Itachi.

![Kakashi leaves the Anbu.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/2e/Kakashi_leaves_the_Anbu.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140419114626)

Kakashi leaves the Anbu.

Because of what happened with Itachi, the Third Hokage felt that individuals with kind hearts did not belong in the Anbu. He therefore thanked Kakashi for his years of service and relieved him of his duty, returning him to the standard forces.[^40] Over the years, he was placed in charge of several teams of Academy graduates, but none ever demonstrated the teamwork he considered to be so important. In the anime, he started worrying that his own methods were too harsh, but from seeing how his former underlings that he previously failed prospered due to his teachings, his faith was reaffirmed.[^17]

## Personality

![Kakashi's cold-heartedness.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/6/64/Kakashi%27s_cold_heart.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20250812173949)

Kakashi's cold-heartedness.

Kakashi has been very independent, perceptive, and self-confident since early childhood, and tends to broach difficult conversations with blunt pragmatism. He carries himself with a relaxed, almost bored disposition and is known for being solitary and aloof, with an occasional tendency to ignore his peers. These qualities lead some to perceive him as arrogant or condescending; however, Kakashi is truthfully quite modest about himself, even in the face of his growing reputation as a ninja and leader. He is also habitually tardy, showing up at his leisure with excuses that convince nobody, and later leaving for equally poor reasons. Though Kakashi wants others to believe this is how he always acts, he only does so for matters that aren't of particular importance. In his role as Hokage, Kakashi dutifully worked for the village's progress. He also believes that even in times of peace, no one should become a ninja without appreciating the importance of it.

![Kakashi suffering from post-traumatic stress disorder.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/9/96/Kakashi_PTSD.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230408182808)

Kakashi suffering from post-traumatic stress disorder.

Kakashi's life was shaped by the death of his childhood squadmates. He spends hours of his free time visiting Rin's grave and the Memorial Stone where Obito's name is engraved, telling them about his life, recent events in Konoha, and others they knew. Most of all, he shares his regrets that they cannot be with him and that their deaths were his fault. In the anime, after Rin died, Kakashi struggled to cope with post-traumatic stress disorder, having nightmares about her death and growing depressed whenever others mentioned her. Over time, Kakashi comes to accept that her choice to sacrifice herself and his guilt became more about his broken promise to Obito to keep her safe. This causes Obito's apparent death to weigh more heavily on him, compounded by regrets about his behaviour when they last spoke. Kakashi feels that, had he cared about teamwork and friendship in the same way that Obito did, Obito's "death" might have been avoided. Thinking it would ease his pain, Minato convinced Kakashi to join the Anbu. During his time in the Anbu however, Kakashi gained a reputation for his cold-bloodedness. [Kurenai Yūhi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kurenai_Y%C5%ABhi "Kurenai Yūhi") noted that Kakashi suffered with suicidal thoughts during his Anbu career, with his reckless fighting style and lack of self-care being deemed as Kakashi being in a hurry to die.[^41]

After his father's death, Kakashi initially became stern and cold toward others, chastising any who disobeyed orders and being willing to abandon his comrades to finish missions. However, Obito's apparent death profoundly changed him; he became committed to teamwork and always places teammates' well-being above the mission's. He believes goals can be accomplished more effectively together, asking for help when he needs it, providing help when it is requested of him, and praising allies when they deserve it. An abuse of one's teammates is the only thing that can make Kakashi cast aside his normally stoic demeanour, causing him to attack without mercy or his usual sense of sportsmanship. He expects his prospective students to demonstrate this same devotion to teamwork, which none until Team 7 were able to do; despite the high failure rate, the Third Hokage agrees with Kakashi's choice to require it. Kakashi becomes fond of all Team 7's members, even Sasuke Uchiha, to the point of blaming himself for Sasuke's criminal acts and his team's growing rift over it throughout Part II. He holds out hope that Sasuke will correct his behaviour, and for his patience he is able to see Team 7 reunified when by the end of the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War").

![Kakashi and Guy competing in their usual "contests".](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/82/Kakashi_and_Guy_racing.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230408185345)

Kakashi and Guy competing in their usual "contests".

[Might Guy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Might_Guy "Might Guy") has considered Kakashi his rival ever since they first met in the [Academy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Academy "Academy"). Despite his father's warning that Guy might someday prove stronger than him, Kakashi didn't think much of Guy at the time. Guy took this as a challenge and began striving to earn Kakashi's approval so as to show that his perseverance could be just as good as Kakashi's natural genius. When Guy would suggest contests to test how their abilities compared, Kakashi would respond with indifference, only fuelling Guy's desire to defeat his "cool, hip, and trendy" personality. At the start of Part I, Kakashi's score is 50 wins and 49 losses,[^42] though even when Guy takes the lead the score difference remains the same; considering that their competitions range from eating contests to Rock, Paper, Scissors, the score isn't truly representative of anything. Kakashi eventually comes to consider Guy one of his closest and most reliable friends, and by the end of the Fourth Shinobi World War, he acknowledges that Guy has surpassed him.

![Kakashi's obsession with the Icha Icha series.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/f/fa/Kakashi_Icha_Icha.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230416221328)

Kakashi's obsession with the Icha Icha series.

When he first meets his students on [Team 7](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Kakashi "Team Kakashi"), Kakashi claims to have many hobbies, none of which he chooses to share at the time. However, he is well-known as an avid fan of the *[Icha Icha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Icha_Icha "Icha Icha")* series of novels; he always has one of the books with him and regularly reads it, even while talking to others. [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki") identifies *Icha Icha* as one of Kakashi's few weaknesses, which he is able to take advantage of during a [bell test](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Bell_test "Bell test") by threatening to spoil the ending of the newest book in the series, forcing Kakashi to shut his eyes and ears and thereby leave himself vulnerable. Ultimately, he admits having read the entire series so many times, he has basically memorised it completely, proving such when his knowledge of it helped decipher Jiraiya's code to Naruto. Decades later, he continued to love the series as much as ever. To which, when initially he refused to help with a family feud, he quickly agreed after being promised a rare first edition of an *Icha Icha* volume, with an extended ending. At the same time, Kakashi is uncomfortable sharing the books with others, embarrassed by the presumably adult content.

Following his retirement from active duty, Kakashi has become much more laid-back and unbothered by events and people surrounding him. His speech mannerisms and tone also resemble those of an old man. Although steadfast during any and all important events, regularly aiding in advisory duties, Kakashi actively avoids taking on responsibilities during his free time, such as when he attempted to evade [Boruto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Boruto "Boruto") 's pleas for training. Furthermore, Kakashi also enjoys having others perform the most basic of chores on his behalf, such as roping Boruto into helping him with shopping duties.

## Abilities

![Kakashi intimidating opponents with his jutsu.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/b2/Kakashi%27s_Multiple_Shadow_Clone_Technique.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230224015701)

Kakashi intimidating opponents with his jutsu.

Kakashi is one of the strongest ninja Konoha ever produced, with his advanced skills earning him the title of a genius (天才, *tensei*).[^3] A prodigy of such innate talent and intellect,[^49] he showcased highly advanced skill from a young age and even performed official missions while still in the Academy.[^50] From which, he rapidly rose through the ninja ranks.[^51] His prowess is said to rival an entire country,[^52] something that earned him recognition on sight by various high-level ninja.[^53] The [S-Rank](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/S-Rank "S-Rank") members of Akatsuki acknowledge his might;[^54] he outmaneuvered [Itachi Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Itachi_Uchiha "Itachi Uchiha") (albeit he couldn't use his Mangekyō Sharingan at the time), fought [Kisame Hoshigaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kisame_Hoshigaki "Kisame Hoshigaki") and [Kakuzu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakuzu "Kakuzu") to a standstill, and was deemed a major threat by [Pain](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Pain "Pain").[^55] He even fought on par with the adult [Obito Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Obito_Uchiha "Obito Uchiha"), whose prowess was comparable to [Madara Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Madara_Uchiha "Madara Uchiha").[^56]

Kakashi is very versatile with advanced proficiency in countless areas including mastery of all three main ninja skills; noted as smarter than [Shikamaru Nara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shikamaru_Nara "Shikamaru Nara"), having a better sense of smell than [Kiba Inuzuka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kiba_Inuzuka "Kiba Inuzuka"), more adept with the [Sharingan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sharingan "Sharingan") than [Sasuke Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Uchiha "Sasuke Uchiha"), and more skilled in [taijutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Taijutsu "Taijutsu") than [Rock Lee](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rock_Lee "Rock Lee") —even as each of them are well-known for their high capacities in those respective areas.[^57] Kakashi's [genjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genjutsu "Genjutsu") prowess can use basic techniques to major effect for intimidation and distraction.[^58] Even when normally relying on a single eye, the reduced depth perception and range of vision has no impact on his performance. Kakashi's combat and tactical prowess are so revered, that many foresaw his rise to Hokage status years before it happened.[^59] At the same time, the [Sage of Six Paths](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sage_of_Six_Paths "Sage of Six Paths") believed Kakashi's greatest strength is as a teacher and leader, which proved vital in [Kaguya Ōtsutsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kaguya_%C5%8Ctsutsuki "Kaguya Ōtsutsuki") 's defeat.[^60] Even after losing his Sharingan, Kakashi's combat prowess continued to increase.[^58] Despite being retired, Kakashi was able to pressure [Koji Kashin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Koji_Kashin "Koji Kashin"), one of [Kara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kara "Kara") 's strongest Inners and an enhanced clone of [Jiraiya](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jiraiya "Jiraiya"), into retreating.[^61]

### Chakra and Physical Prowess

![Kakashi engages Kakuzu in hand-to-hand combat.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/8f/Kakashi_vs_Kakuzu.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20210914031021)

Kakashi engages Kakuzu in hand-to-hand combat.

Originally, by his own admission, Kakashi's [chakra](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra "Chakra") reserves were average. He could only use the [Lightning Cutter](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning_Cutter "Lightning Cutter") four times or [Kamui](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kamui "Kamui") three times per day. To compensate, he relied on superb control, requiring no more than the minimum amount to perform his technique, even able to do so with a single hand. However, by Part II, Kakashi's reserves increased to a considerable size, able to perform both techniques several more times and repeatedly in a single battle with only minor exhaustion. In later years, his reserves increased further, able to create and maintain four collective [massive walls](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Earth_Release:_Earth-Style_Wall "Earth Release: Earth-Style Wall") for a few days and casually utilise his [Purple Electricity](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning_Release:_Purple_Electricity "Lightning Release: Purple Electricity") numerous times.

Kakashi's [taijutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Taijutsu "Taijutsu") can take various approaches in close combat. From his time in the [Anbu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Anbu "Anbu"), he can suddenly approach enemies from behind unnoticed, even by other Anbu.[^62] Likewise, with attacks from the front, he can strike with blazing speed and debilitating precision to quickly defeat multiple enemies. Kakashi is able to open at least one of the [Eight Gates](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eight_Gates "Eight Gates").[^63] He has reflexes sharp enough to block attacks he is unable to see,[^64] and has immense speed that allows him to perform [hand seals](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hand_seals "Hand seals") at unreadable rates.[^57]

### Ninjutsu

![Kakashi using Rasengan.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/bb/Kakashi_Using_Rasengan.PNG/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160412234856)

Kakashi using Rasengan.

In battle, Kakashi primarily relies on his great knowledge and skill in [ninjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ninjutsu "Ninjutsu"). As an academy student, he excelled well-past his peers, being able to use [jōnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/J%C5%8Dnin "Jōnin") -level ninjutsu.[^65] Over the years, due in large part to his [Sharingan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sharingan "Sharingan"), he gained a vast arsenal of ninjutsu; reputed to have copied over a thousand techniques, earning his moniker as "Copy Ninja Kakashi" (コピー忍者のカカシ, *Kopī Ninja no Kakashi*).[^66] This, combined with years of experience, allows him to handle almost any combat situation in various ways. He is also well-versed in [fūinjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/F%C5%ABinjutsu "Fūinjutsu"), impressing [Orochimaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Orochimaru "Orochimaru").[^67] Many years after the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War"), Kakashi's skill in [Medical Ninjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Medical_Ninjutsu "Medical Ninjutsu") is able to ease physical pain with a mere touch.[^58]

![Kakashi's ninken.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/86/Ninken_summoned_to_search_Orochi.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140309190444)

Kakashi's ninken.

Kakashi also has his own personal pack of [ninken](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ninken "Ninken"), which he raised since they were puppies; in the anime, he had them since before the [Third Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Shinobi_World_War "Third Shinobi World War").[^68] Able to [summon](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning_Technique "Summoning Technique") one or more of them, Kakashi uses them for various means including fetching, restraining,[^69] and tracking. Outside of battle, he often uses them to deliver messages.

#### Bukijutsu

![Kakashi wielding Kubikiribōchō.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/b4/Kakashi_Kubikirib%C5%8Dch%C5%8D.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230412211329)

Kakashi wielding Kubikiribōchō.

Kakashi is highly skilled in a wide variety of weapons such as [bows and arrows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bow_and_arrow "wikipedia:Bow and arrow"), [kusarigama](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kusarigama "Kusarigama"), [chains](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kusari "Kusari"), and [wire strings](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wire_Strings "Wire Strings"). Since childhood, Kakashi was highly proficient in [kenjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kenjutsu "Kenjutsu"), being able to wield his [father's signature tantō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/White_Light_Chakra_Sabre "White Light Chakra Sabre") effectively enough to be mistaken for Sakumo. From his background in the Anbu, Kakashi's swordsmanship became very adaptable, able to effectively utilise the [Kubikiribōchō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kubikirib%C5%8Dch%C5%8D "Kubikiribōchō"),[^70] a uniquely shaped blade with noticeable size and weight, and he was able to defeat kenjutsu experts such as the Seven Ninja Swordsmen of the Mist with it.[^71] He also has considerable skill in [shurikenjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shurikenjutsu "Shurikenjutsu"), able to lash out at an opponent with a swift flurry of kunai and even effectively throw kunai with a well-placed kick.[^72] Likewise in close-quarters combat, he can skillfully use weapons in both hands simultaneously and even fend off other skilled melee combatants such as Zabuza and Hidan, armed with their respective unique weapons, using a single kunai.

#### Nature Transformation

![Kakashi's signature technique, the Lightning Cutter.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/0b/Lightning_Cutter.PNG/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20251110022322)

Kakashi's signature technique, the Lightning Cutter.

Kakashi can use all five basic [nature transformations](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nature_transformation "Nature transformation"), as well as the [Yin Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yin_Release "Yin Release") and [Yang Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yang_Release "Yang Release").[^5] He can use [Water Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water_Release "Water Release") without a nearby water source,[^73] and use it to produce [massive offensive](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water_Release:_Great_Waterfall_Technique "Water Release: Great Waterfall Technique") or [defensive structures](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water_Release:_Water_Formation_Wall "Water Release: Water Formation Wall"). With [Earth Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Earth_Release "Earth Release"), he can [move underground](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Earth_Release:_Hiding_Like_a_Mole_Technique "Earth Release: Hiding Like a Mole Technique") for sneak attacks or produce [multiple intricate walls](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Earth_Release:_Multiple_Earth-Style_Wall "Earth Release: Multiple Earth-Style Wall") to defend himself or large areas from attack.[^74] Years after the Fourth Shinobi World War, Kakashi learned to convert his Earth Release techniques into [quartz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/quartz "wikipedia:quartz") Improving the quality of his techniques in this way negates its weakness to lightning-based nature attacks by absorbing and dissipating opposing techniques.[^58] He is shown using [Fire Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fire_Release "Fire Release") to launch a [massive fireball](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Fireball_Technique "Great Fireball Technique").[^75]

![Kakashi using Lightning Release: Purple Electricity.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/d/d7/Lightning_Release_Purple_Electricity.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20250224032407)

Kakashi using Lightning Release: Purple Electricity.

Kakashi's natural affinity is [Lightning Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning_Release "Lightning Release"),[^76] which he can [channel](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_Flow "Chakra Flow") to enhance his physical tools offensive might to pierce and pass through large rocks.[^77].[^78] After failing to infuse the Rasengan with his lightning nature, he instead used its basic principles to create his signature technique, [Chidori](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chidori "Chidori").[^79] With it, he collects lightning chakra with one or both hands simultaneously, which can pierce most targets that it is thrust into. Kakashi later refined Chidori to once famously split a bolt of lightning with, earning the name " [Lightning Cutter](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning_Cutter "Lightning Cutter") ".[^80] He can use it to counter other lightning attacks. Other lightning-based techniques of Kakashi’s include creating [hounds](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning_Release:_Lightning_Beast_Tracking_Fang "Lightning Release: Lightning Beast Tracking Fang") to attack with, [clones](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning_Release:_Shadow_Clone_Technique "Lightning Release: Shadow Clone Technique") that paralyse opponents on contact,[^81] and [cords](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning_Transmission "Lightning Transmission") that can even cut through the highly dense [Tailed Beast Chakra Arms](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Chakra_Arms "Tailed Beast Chakra Arms"). By his tenure as the Sixth Hokage, to make up for his inability to no longer safely use the Lightning Cutter without the Sharingan, as it countered the tunnel vision caused when using the technique, Kakashi had created a new signature technique; the [Lightning Release: Purple Electricity](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning_Release:_Purple_Electricity "Lightning Release: Purple Electricity") which allows him to release a surge of purple lightning to produce a variety of effects: attack from afar, augment his physical strikes, and release a burst from all around him.

### Dōjutsu

#### Sharingan

![Kakashi's Sharingan.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/4/42/Kakashi%27s_Sharingan.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240922020327)

Kakashi's Sharingan.

Kakashi received his Sharingan during the [Third Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Shinobi_World_War "Third Shinobi World War") from [Obito Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Obito_Uchiha "Obito Uchiha"), a replacement for the left eye he'd lost shortly beforehand. The Sharingan became his most identifying feature in the years since, widely known as "Kakashi of the Sharingan" (写輪眼のカカシ, *Sharingan no Kakashi*). With this dōjutsu, Kakashi can accurately reproduce any movement he sees (his own physical skill permitting), take in a heightened amount of visual information, and perform the Sharingan's broad category of [genjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genjutsu:_Sharingan "Genjutsu: Sharingan"). Because he isn't an [Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uchiha "Uchiha"), he can't deactivate the Sharingan and it rapidly drains his chakra whenever he uses it, leaving him bedridden after extended use.[^82] As such, he always keeps the eye covered until he needs it. Despite his handicap, he is still noted to have impressive prowess wielding it, as noted by both Itachi and Obito Uchiha.[^83] [^84] Kakashi lost his Sharingan during the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War").[^85] When Obito died with both his Sharingan, his spirit briefly returned from the afterlife and inhabited Kakashi's body. During which, Obito channelled both his eyes' dōjutsu power into Kakashi, granting full usage of their abilities with no strains or setbacks.

#### Mangekyō Sharingan

![Kakashi's Mangekyō Sharingan.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/1/1e/Kakashi%27s_Mangeky%C5%8D_Sharingan.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20141011211403)

Kakashi's Mangekyō Sharingan.

Kakashi gained a [Mangekyō Sharingan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mangeky%C5%8D_Sharingan "Mangekyō Sharingan") when [Rin Nohara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rin_Nohara "Rin Nohara") killed herself on Kakashi's Lightning Cutter,[^26] which he could use after long hours of study and training.[^20] Its design is three triangles wrapping themselves around the pupil, similar to a pinwheel. He was able to maintain it for over a decade, though he was steadily suffering its side-effects with each usage, such as bleeding eyes and deteriorating vision. With his left Mangekyō, Kakashi can use the long-range variant of [Kamui](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kamui "Kamui"): remotely forming a barrier around whatever he looks at to warp the target to [Kamui's dimension](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kamui%27s_dimension "Kamui's dimension"); eventually, he learns how to send himself to the dimension and to a new location from it.[^86] While he initially had trouble aiming Kamui properly,[^87] he improved to the point where he could accurately use Kamui on fast-moving projectiles.[^88] Originally, the technique required some amount of preparation and was a huge drain on his chakra each time it was performed. Later on, Kakashi refined his execution to the point where he could perform it faster than most could even see, and use Kamui repeatedly with much less chakra drain.[^89]

![Kakashi's Complete Body — Susanoo.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/f/f6/Kakashi%27s_Susanoo.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160825181700)

Kakashi's Complete Body — Susanoo.

After Kakashi loses his Sharingan, his body is temporarily inhabited by Obito's spirit, allowing him to use both of Obito's Mangekyō. Due to these eyes possessing [Six Paths Chakra](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Six_Paths_Chakra "Six Paths Chakra"), this allowed Kakashi to utilize the Mangekyō Sharingan and its abilities with much greater power, as well as repeated and extended use with no ill effects to his health, chakra, or eyesight.[^90] In addition to the long-range Kamui he'd originally had, he could use its short-range variant to overlap his body with Kamui's dimension, effectively making him intangible so that any dangers phase through him.[^91] By [combining this ability](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kamui_Lightning_Cutter "Kamui Lightning Cutter") with his Lightning Cutter, Kakashi could bypass all defences and eliminate the possibility of being countered while attacking. Having manifested a dual Mangekyō, Kakashi could use [Susanoo](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Susanoo "Susanoo"), which he quickly honed into its "Complete Body" form. It can be used for flight, defending others with its large size, and even have the intangibility of Kamui extended to it. It can manifest either swords or shuriken to aid him in battle, the latter of which can produce [Kamui Shuriken](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kamui_Shuriken "Kamui Shuriken") in order to warp away whatever they cut. Kakashi lost all these abilities after Obito's spirit returned to the afterlife.

### Intelligence

Kakashi's talent for systematically planning ahead and swiftly changing tactics allows him to adapt to virtually any threat. Part of Kakashi's usual approach to combat is to rely on the [Body Replacement Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Body_Replacement_Technique "Body Replacement Technique") and [clone techniques](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_techniques "Clone techniques"), thereby keeping himself out of harm's way while he observes an opponent and their abilities.[^92] Likewise, he remains alert to any unexplained or unexpected occurrences, coming up with theories for their causes when they arise and exploiting them if they prove true, which they usually do.[^93] Once he identifies an opponent's weakness, Kakashi can quickly formulate a plan to take advantage of it. This allows him to instinctively design the proper countermeasure for the situation, using either his own myriad of techniques or relying on his allies' unique skills to that purpose. Such plans, while often elaborate and unorthodox, are typically highly successful and very low-risk, making Kakashi invaluable as a leader. He has also proven himself a very talented teacher, able to push his students' development by months, if not years, in just a matter of weeks through various innovative methods.

## Part I

### Prologue — Land of Waves

Main article: [Prologue — Land of Waves](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Prologue_%E2%80%94_Land_of_Waves "Prologue — Land of Waves")

![The official photo for Team 7.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/5/50/Team_Kakashi.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20161219035928)

The official photo for Team 7.

Kakashi is assigned as the leader of [Team 7](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Kakashi "Team Kakashi"), comprised of [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki"), [Sasuke Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Uchiha "Sasuke Uchiha"), and [Sakura Haruno](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sakura_Haruno "Sakura Haruno"). The [Third Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Hokage "Third Hokage") explains that he is selected for the team in order to keep an eye on Naruto, the [Nine-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tails "Nine-Tails") ' [jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki "Jinchūriki") and, in the anime, to help Sasuke cope with life after the [Uchiha Clan Downfall](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uchiha_Clan_Downfall "Uchiha Clan Downfall"). During his first meeting with Team 7, Kakashi is unimpressed by them, finding them all to be too self-interested. He nevertheless gives them a [bell test](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Bell_test "Bell test") as he has for all the previous teams he's been assigned to and tells them to take one of the two bells on his person. Naruto launches a first attack before the test officially begins; Kakashi stops him easily, but is nevertheless amused.

Kakashi reads *[Icha Icha Paradise](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Icha_Icha_Paradise "Icha Icha Paradise")* during the test, convinced he won't need his full attention or both of his hands. Naruto and his [shadow clones](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_clone "Shadow clone") fail to take a bell and Sakura, who focuses on looking for Sasuke, is defeated by one of Kakashi's [genjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Illusion:_Hell_Viewing_Technique "Demonic Illusion: Hell Viewing Technique"). Kakashi then goes to find Sasuke, expecting the same outcome as Naruto and Sakura, but finds Sasuke was waiting for him. Kakashi is forced to go on the defensive and, when Sasuke uses the [Great Fireball Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Fireball_Technique "Great Fireball Technique"), he must close his copy of *Icha Icha*, but he still manages to defeat Sasuke. Kakashi gathers them together afterwards and tells them they've failed, explaining that, had they worked together rather than individually, they might have succeeded in acquiring a bell. They convince him to give them another chance after lunch, but Kakashi demands that Naruto not be fed. He secretly watches them as they eat and sees Sasuke and Sakura give Naruto food against his instructions, needing Naruto in top form if they're to get a bell. Kakashi pretends to be angry when he approaches them, but tells them that they pass the test for placing the team's well-being above the mission parameters.

Team 7 completes a number of [D-rank](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/D-rank "D-rank") missions, which Naruto believes they are over-qualified for. He complains to the Third Hokage about this and is able to secure a [C-rank](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/C-rank "C-rank") mission: escorting [Tazuna](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tazuna "Tazuna") to the [Land of Waves](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_Waves "Land of Waves"). Shortly after leaving Konoha, Kakashi notices they're being pursued by the [Demon Brothers](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demon_Brothers "Demon Brothers"). He pretends to be killed by one of the Demon Brothers' attacks so that he can observe them, stepping in to neutralise them once Team 7 can't hold them off anymore. After restraining them, Kakashi confronts Tazuna on why the Demon Brothers were trying to kill him. Tazuna confesses that assassins have been hired to kill him so that he can't build a bridge that will free the Land of Waves from the tyranny of [Gatō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gat%C5%8D "Gatō"). Although this is an [A-rank](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/A-rank "A-rank") assignment that Team 7, being only [genin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genin "Genin"), are unqualified for, they decide to help Tazuna anyway because Tazuna confessed his country is too poor to afford an A-rank assignment.

Kakashi vs. Zabuza.

Recognising how formidable Zabuza is, Kakashi uncovers his [Sharingan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sharingan "Sharingan") and engages him, leaving his students in charge of protecting Tazuna. Kakashi initially does well against Zabuza, defeating Zabuza's [water clones](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water_clone "Water clone") with his own. When Zabuza succeeds in trapping him in a [Water Prison](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water_Prison "Water Prison"), Kakashi instructs Team 7 to leave him behind and escape with Tazuna. While recuperating, Kakashi starts to find Zabuza's death suspicious and worries he may still be alive. In case this is true, he shows Team 7 the [Tree Climbing Practice](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tree_Climbing_Practice "Tree Climbing Practice") in order to improve their [chakra](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra "Chakra") control, which will be useful if Zabuza does indeed return. After a week of rest Kakashi has recovered and all of Team 7 has mastered the exercise; Naruto is tired from the training and is allowed to keep sleeping as they resume their bodyguarding of Tazuna. When they arrive at the bridge Tazuna is working on, they find Zabuza and [Haku](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Haku "Haku") (the hunter-nin from before) waiting for them. Haku traps Sasuke in his [Demonic Mirroring Ice Crystals](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Mirroring_Ice_Crystals "Demonic Mirroring Ice Crystals"), which Zabuza blocks Kakashi from helping him escape from. Once Naruto arrives to lend Sasuke assistance, Kakashi engages Zabuza in combat.

![Kakashi kills Haku.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/c/c5/Kakashi_Impales_Haku.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20221003120202)

Kakashi kills Haku.

Having learned from his previous encounter, Zabuza coats the area in a [thick mist](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hiding_in_Mist_Technique "Hiding in Mist Technique") so that Kakashi can't use his Sharingan. Because of this, Kakashi sticks close to Tazuna, knowing Zabuza will come for him eventually. Zabuza does indeed attack, and Kakashi is badly injured by Zabuza's [Kubikiribōchō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kubikirib%C5%8Dch%C5%8D "Kubikiribōchō") while defending Tazuna. While reeling from his wound, Kakashi senses Naruto using the Nine-Tails' chakra. Fearing that the seal containing the beast is weakening, Kakashi decides to finish the battle quickly: he [summons](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning_Technique "Summoning Technique") his [ninken](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ninken "Ninken") and has them track down his blood which is still on Zabuza's Kubikiribōchō. Just before he hits Zabuza, Haku steps in front of him and takes the attack instead.

As Sasuke is about to clash his Chidori with Naruto's [Rasengan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rasengan "Rasengan"), Kakashi catches them both and flings them apart. Kakashi starts questioning Sasuke about his apparent intentions to kill Naruto, but Sasuke leaves rather than be lectured. He starts by trying to discourage Sasuke from avenging the [Uchiha clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uchiha_clan "Uchiha clan"), insisting that, even if he's successful, it won't bring back those who are already dead. Sasuke is surprised and puts up less resistance, so Kakashi tells him that taking revenge for those that he's lost is not worth also losing those he still has, namely Naruto and Sakura. To that end, he encourages Sasuke to start using Chidori for its intended purpose: protecting friends rather than attacking them. He unties Sasuke and leaves for his mission, intending for what he said to sink in.

![Kakashi retrieves Naruto at the Valley of the End.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/80/Kakashi_finds_Naruto.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20251006044321)

Upset with himself for not spending more time defusing Sasuke's angst, Kakashi goes after him, fearful that he and Naruto may kill each other. He summons his ninken to help him track Sasuke and Naruto, eventually finding the latter unconscious at the [Valley of the End](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Valley_of_the_End "Valley of the End"). Because of the rain, Kakashi is unable to track down Sasuke's scent and with Naruto requiring medical attention, Kakashi prioritizes on bringing Naruto back to Konoha. En route, Kakashi meets with the [Medic Corps](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Medic_Corps "Medic Corps") and receives updates regarding the status of the other genin dispatched on the mission; [Shikamaru Nara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shikamaru_Nara "Shikamaru Nara") and [Kiba Inuzuka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kiba_Inuzuka "Kiba Inuzuka") were injured but overall fine, while [Neji Hyūga](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Neji_Hy%C5%ABga "Neji Hyūga") and [Chōji Akimichi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%8Dji_Akimichi "Chōji Akimichi") were in critical condition. Thankfully, the latter two ultimately survive.

With Sasuke gone to Orochimaru, Naruto decides to start training with Jiraiya while Sakura trains with Tsunade. Having no students to lead anymore, Kakashi resumes his prior mission schedule.

## Part II

### Kazekage Rescue Mission

Main article: [Kazekage Rescue Mission](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kazekage_Rescue_Mission "Kazekage Rescue Mission")

![Kakashi facing Sakura during the bell test.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/bc/Kakashi_vs_Sakura.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230419002747)

Kakashi facing Sakura during the bell test.

Kakashi is the first person to greet Naruto when he returns from his two-and-a-half years of training with Jiraiya. Naruto gives him a copy of *[Icha Icha Tactics](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Icha_Icha_Tactics "Icha Icha Tactics")* as a present, which Kakashi wastes no time starting to read. As Naruto goes off to see Sakura, Jiraiya warns Kakashi that Akatsuki will be making a move in the near future. After Naruto and Sakura have had a chance to catch-up, Kakashi reforms Team 7 and gives them another [bell test](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Bell_test "Bell test"). Unlike last time, taking the bells from him is the real objective. Kakashi also decides to take the test seriously by uncovering his Sharingan and closing his copy of *Icha Icha Tactics*. As with last time, Naruto launches a first attack before the test officially begins, but this time it nearly succeeds. Kakashi is impressed by this and other signs of their improvement, but by nightfall they still haven't taken a bell. Tired of this, Naruto tries spoiling the ending of *Icha Icha Tactics*, forcing Kakashi to cover his ears so as not to hear it and shut his eyes so as not to read Naruto's lips. This allows Naruto and Sakura to each take a bell.

![Kakashi fights "Itachi".](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/8f/Rending_Piercing_Fang.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20180308203641)

Kakashi fights "Itachi".

While Team 7 tries without success to find a mission to go on that Naruto won't complain about, word reaches Konoha that Akatsuki has kidnapped [Gaara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gaara "Gaara"), the Fifth [Kazekage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kazekage "Kazekage") and the [jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki "Jinchūriki") of the [One-Tail](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/One-Tail "One-Tail"). Team 7 is sent to [Sunagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sunagakure "Sunagakure") to lend assistance in rescuing Gaara. When they arrive in Suna, Kakashi is attacked by [Chiyo](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chiyo "Chiyo"), who mistook him for [his father](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sakumo "Sakumo"), but her brother [Ebizō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ebiz%C5%8D "Ebizō") cleared up the confusion before the attack could connect, to Kakashi's great relief at the close call. Team 7 gathers what intel they can on Gaara's kidnappers and leave to go after them; Chiyo volunteers to escort them since they aren't familiar with the country around Suna. While en route to an Akatsuki lair, they are confronted by [Itachi Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Itachi_Uchiha "Itachi Uchiha"). While they fight him, Kakashi notices something is off about Itachi, notably his lack of use of the [Mangekyō Sharingan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mangeky%C5%8D_Sharingan "Mangekyō Sharingan"). The reason for this is discovered after Naruto seemingly kills him with a [Big Ball Rasengan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Big_Ball_Rasengan "Big Ball Rasengan"): they were only fighting a [doppelgänger](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shapeshifting_Technique "Shapeshifting Technique").

![Kakashi using Kamui on Deidara.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/88/Kamui.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150214192357)

Kakashi using Kamui on Deidara.

Team 7 arrives at the Akatsuki lair shortly after [Team Guy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Guy "Team Guy") does, who were sent as backup by [Tsunade](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tsunade "Tsunade"). After Team Guy takes down the barrier over the entrance, Team 7 moves in and finds Gaara's body with his kidnappers: [Deidara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Deidara "Deidara") and [Sasori](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasori "Sasori"). Deidara takes Gaara's body and flies off, which Naruto and Kakashi go after, leaving Sasori to Sakura and Chiyo. Naruto makes repeated failed attempts to rescue Gaara, prompting Kakashi to encourage patience while he prepares a new ability: a Mangekyō Sharingan of his own. Once he's ready, Kakashi uses [Kamui](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kamui "Kamui") on Deidara, but his aim is off and he succeeds only in removing one of Deidara's arms. This creates an opening for Naruto to take Gaara back, which Deidara responds to by creating a [Suicide Bombing Clone](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Suicide_Bombing_Clone "Suicide Bombing Clone") to cover his escape; Kakashi uses Kamui again to warp the explosion away.

After they regroup with Chiyo and Sakura, Chiyo [gives her life](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/One%27s_Own_Life_Reincarnation "One's Own Life Reincarnation") to revive Gaara. Teams 7 and Guy attend her funeral in Sunagakure before returning home. Kakashi is unable to walk due to overusing his Mangekyō Sharingan, forcing [Might Guy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Might_Guy "Might Guy") to carry him; Guy's choice of carrying Kakashi by piggyback deeply disturbs their students.

Kakashi explaining the concept of chakra affinity.

Although Team 7 was able to meet Sasuke, he was too strong for them to capture and bring back to Konoha. Once Kakashi's done recuperating he calls them before him and is introduced to [Sai](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sai "Sai"), who is effectively Sasuke's replacement on the team; they speak only briefly but both of approve of each other. Kakashi believes the best way to match Sasuke would be for Naruto to create a new [jutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jutsu "Jutsu"). Before he can elaborate further, they are interrupted by the arrival of [Asuma Sarutobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Asuma_Sarutobi "Asuma Sarutobi") and the rest of [Team 10](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Asuma "Team Asuma"). To help Naruto create a new jutsu, Kakashi first establishes what his [nature](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nature "Nature") is: [wind](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wind "Wind"). With that settled, Kakashi trains Naruto in how to learn to use the wind nature. Ordinarily, such training would take months or years, but Naruto, by training alongside hundreds of [shadow clones](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_clone "Shadow clone"), can do the same training in a mere fraction of the time. With Kakashi's instructions and the training grounds created by Yamato, Naruto quickly masters how to use the wind nature. The next step is more difficult for him: combining that nature with the [Rasengan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rasengan "Rasengan"). Because Kakashi and [Minato Namikaze](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Minato_Namikaze "Minato Namikaze") both failed to combine their natures with the Rasengan, Kakashi can't offer Naruto any tips and can only watch as he tries to develop something. When they receive news that Asuma died in battle with members of Akatsuki, they take a break to attend Asuma's funeral.

![Kakashi destroys one of Kakuzu's hearts.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/bf/Kakashi_%26_Kakuzu.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150125210543)

Once they locate the Akatsuki members, [Hidan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hidan "Hidan") and [Kakuzu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakuzu "Kakuzu"), Kakashi hides until Kakuzu is driven to use his [Earth Spear](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Earth_Spear "Earth Spear"), at which point Kakashi pierces him through the heart with his Lightning Cutter. Kakuzu, who has more than one heart, fends Kakashi off and uses the elemental masks of his [Earth Grudge Fear](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Earth_Grudge_Fear "Earth Grudge Fear") to put Team 10 on the defensive. Needing to separate Hidan and Kakuzu if they're to have any chance of victory, Shikamaru takes a vial of Kakuzu's blood that Kakashi secretly collected and leads Hidan away. Shikamaru is able to trick Hidan into using the blood to [curse](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Curse_Technique:_Death_Controlling_Possessed_Blood "Curse Technique: Death Controlling Possessed Blood") Kakuzu, destroying a second of his hearts in time to stop him from stealing Kakashi's.

![Kakashi talks to Kakuzu before killing him.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/f/fa/Kakuzu%27s_last_moments.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150222045207)

Kakashi talks to Kakuzu before killing him.

Kakashi summons [Pakkun](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Pakkun "Pakkun") to lead Sakura and Sai to Shikamaru in case he needs help while everyone else prepares to attack Kakuzu. Naruto, having finished his jutsu, volunteers to face Kakuzu alone. His new [Wind Release: Rasenshuriken](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wind_Release:_Rasenshuriken "Wind Release: Rasenshuriken") is temperamental, however, forcing Kakashi and Yamato to rescue him when it fails on its first use. It succeeds the second time, destroying two of Kakuzu's hearts and putting the last one on the brink of failure. Kakashi is deeply impressed by the Rasenshuriken and tells Kakuzu that the previous generation being surpassed by the next was perfectly natural. Kakashi then finished Kazuku off with a Lightning Cutter.

### Pain's Assault

Main article: [Pain's Assault](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Pain%27s_Assault_\(Arc\) "Pain's Assault (Arc)") Back in Konoha, Kakashi is informed that Jiraiya has died while investigating the Akatsuki leader, [Pain](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Pain "Pain"). Before he died, Jiraiya sent a number of things to Konoha that would help them against Pain, including an encrypted message. Shikamaru is able to properly console and get Naruto to help him decode it, who realises that the key to its decryption must be *Icha Icha Tactics*. Kakashi, having the only copy, is asked to read specific passages aloud, which makes him extremely uncomfortable. Nevertheless, the message is deciphered.

Kakashi guesses this tactic and goes searching for one of the latter group. In doing so he finds the [Deva Path](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Deva_Path "Deva Path"), and by confronting it he is able to save [Iruka Umino](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Iruka_Umino "Iruka Umino"). Before he can land the blow, the Asura Path uses itself as a shield, giving the Deva Path time to counter. It defeats all of Kakashi's reinforcements and binds Kakashi in rubble. Anticipating that Kakashi will interfere if allowed to live, the Deva Path propels a nail at his head to kill him and leaves. Kakashi, however, only pretends death, warping away the nail at the last moment with Kamui. When Chōji wakes up, Kakashi sends him to Tsunade to tell her everything they've learned about the Deva Path's abilities. The still-functioning Asura Path fires a missile at Chōji in an attempt to stop him, forcing Kakashi to use Kamui again to warp away the missile. This depletes his chakra reserves and causes his death.

He concludes his story by saying that he understands the choices Sakumo made when he was alive and that he is proud to be his son. Just then, a light envelops Kakashi, a sign of his [revival](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Samsara_of_Heavenly_Life_Technique "Samsara of Heavenly Life Technique"); before Kakashi returns to the living plane, Sakumo thanks him for putting him at peace at long last, allowing him to continue to the afterlife. ### Five Kage Summit

Main article: [Five Kage Summit](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Five_Kage_Summit_\(Arc\) "Five Kage Summit (Arc)") Naruto reflects on Sasuke with Kakashi and Sakura. While he does so, word reaches them that [Danzō Shimura](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Danz%C5%8D_Shimura "Danzō Shimura") has become the new [Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage "Hokage") and that Danzō's first decree has been to allow Sasuke's disposal as a [missing-nin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Missing-nin "Missing-nin"), something Tsunade had never permitted. Naruto insists on trying to reason with Danzō, which Kakashi warns him will not work. When this indeed fails, Naruto decides to talk with the [Fourth Raikage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Raikage "Fourth Raikage") instead, who has made it his goal to personally kill Sasuke. Despite the travel limitations in place due to the approaching [Five Kage Summit](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Five_Kage_Summit "Five Kage Summit"), Kakashi and Yamato agree to accompany him. Kakashi neutralises the [Anbu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Anbu "Anbu") assigned to keep an eye on Naruto and Yamato plants his [Transmission Wood](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Transmission_Wood "Transmission Wood") on [Team Samui](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Samui "Team Samui") in order to follow them to the Raikage.

![Kakashi and Yamato restrain Tobi.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/9/95/Kakashi_and_Yamato_restrain_Madara.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150223084315)

When they find the Raikage in the [Land of Iron](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_Iron "Land of Iron"), Kakashi, the only one amongst them that the Raikage recognises, asks that Naruto be allowed to speak with him. The Raikage permits it, but even with his and Yamato's support, he refuses Naruto's request to forgive Sasuke's recent work with Akatsuki and leaves them. They stay at an inn nearby while deciding what to do, where Naruto is confronted by Tobi. Having expected him, Kakashi and Yamato attack and restrain him, though he points out that he can escape whenever he wants. Rather than do that, he tells them about Sasuke's motivations: to take vengeance on Konoha for forcing Itachi to kill the [Uchiha clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uchiha_clan "Uchiha clan"). They doubt what he says regarding the [Uchiha Clan Downfall](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uchiha_Clan_Downfall "Uchiha Clan Downfall"), but Tobi is unconcerned, leaving once he's finished saying to Naruto what he wanted to. Afterwards, Kakashi encourages that three of them keep what Tobi said between them until they knew more and if what Tobi said was true.

Naruto decides that the only course of action left is to speak with Sasuke himself. Gaara also shares the [Five Kage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Five_Kage "Five Kage") 's requests that Kakashi represent Konoha going forward owing to Danzō's behaviour during the Kage Summit. Naruto is overwhelmed by all the developments going on and passes out.

![Kakashi saves Sakura from Sasuke.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/f/f6/Kakashi_intercepts_Sasuke.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150203065808)

Kakashi tells Sakura that she doesn't need to take responsibility for Sasuke since he, as Sasuke's former teacher, is much more to blame for the team's growing rift and current dire situation. He asks Sasuke once more to let go of his thirst for vengeance, but Sasuke laughs in his face, having been too corrupted by Tobi to be reasoned with any longer. As he prepares for battle, Kakashi remarks that he finally understands what the [Third Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Hokage "Third Hokage") went through with [Orochimaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Orochimaru "Orochimaru"). Sasuke is enraged by this use of an Uchiha's ability and threatens to make Kakashi suffer. While he does so, Sakura approaches from behind to kill him, but ultimately cannot and Kakashi, tired from using Kamui, can't reach her in time to save her from Sasuke's retaliation.

Sakura is saved by Naruto. Kakashi tells Naruto to take Sakura away. Naruto thinks that Kakashi is going to kill Sasuke. Naruto instead restrains Kakashi and fights Sasuke himself. In the anime, he requests that Guy be his second-in-command. The Third Division is mobilised ahead of the other divisions in order to be on hand for any eventuality. With Zabuza immobilised and Haku damaged, both are bound by the [Sealing Team](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sealing_Team "Sealing Team"). ![Kakashi fending off Jinin.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/6/66/The_Helmet_Splitter_Jinin_Akebino%21.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160205045018)

### Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax

Main article: [Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War:_Climax "Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax")

![Kakashi and Guy save Naruto.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/9/9f/Geki_%E2%80%94_Konoha_Kong%C5%8Driki_Senp%C5%AB.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130815124945)

Kakashi and Guy save Naruto.

Kakashi and Guy eventually leave the Third Division to join the original Naruto and [Killer B](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Killer_B "Killer B") in their fight with [Tobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tobi "Tobi"), arriving in time to stop Tobi from capturing Naruto. Seeing that Tobi has six reincarnated [jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki "Jinchūriki"), Kakashi theorises that they are each possess one of the [Six Paths Techniques](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Six_Paths_Technique "Six Paths Technique"), just as the Six Paths of Pain did. From fighting them, however, none use the any of the Six Paths Techniques, leading Kakashi to believe Tobi doesn't have the energy for it. Tobi can, at least, still use their [tailed beasts](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_beasts "Tailed beasts") ' powers, so he forces the [Four-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Four-Tails "Four-Tails") and [Six-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Six-Tails "Six-Tails") to enter [Tailed Beast Modes](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Mode "Tailed Beast Mode"). Unable to do anything against such large opponents, Kakashi and Guy leave them to Naruto and B.

![Kakashi attacks the Demonic Statue.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/b6/Kakashi_vs._Gedo_Mazo.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20131128132625)

Kakashi attacks the Demonic Statue.

After Naruto is able to free the Four-Tails from Tobi's control, Tobi forces the other jinchūriki to enter Tailed Beast Modes as well. As five [Tailed Beast Balls](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Ball "Tailed Beast Ball") bear down on them, Kakashi considers trying to use Kamui. It ends up not being necessary as Naruto, by entering his own Tailed Beast Mode, deflects their attacks and subsequently frees them from Tobi. Tobi is forced to recall the tailed beasts into the [Demonic Statue of the Outer Path](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Statue_of_the_Outer_Path "Demonic Statue of the Outer Path"), which he uses against Naruto, B, Kakashi, and Guy. Fighting continues into the night, with neither side emerging victorious. When a light descends on the reincarnated jinchūriki, a sign that the Impure World Reincarnation has been cancelled, Tobi takes drastic action and prematurely initiates the [Ten-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ten-Tails "Ten-Tails") ' revival. When they realise what Tobi is doing, Kakashi and the others focus on destroying the Demonic Statue before it can complete its metamorphosis into the Ten-Tails.

![Kakashi attacks Tobi.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/a/ab/Kakashi_attacks_Tobi1.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20131212124023)

Kakashi attacks Tobi.

Tobi defends the Demonic Statue from all of their attacks and, as ever, is himself seemingly impervious to damage, with everything passing through him. After an exchange of attacks, of which Kakashi blocks Tobi's with Kamui, Kakashi notices some slight damage to Tobi's mask. Having a theory about this, he has Guy and Naruto help him test it and is ultimately able to confirm: attacks warped away with Kamui at the same moment that Tobi is impervious will damage Tobi, thereby suggesting a link between their abilities. Kakashi asks where Tobi acquired his Sharingan, to which he replies it was on the same mission to the [Kannabi Bridge](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kannabi_Bridge "Kannabi Bridge") that Kakashi got his own. Kakashi is troubled by what this might mean, but doesn't overlook the important fact that they now have a way of fighting Tobi. Kakashi secretly uses Kamui on one of Naruto's shadow clones to send it to [Kamui's dimension](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kamui%27s_dimension "Kamui's dimension") which, when Tobi retreats there to escape the real Naruto's attack, attacks Tobi and destroys his mask. When they see his face, Guy and Kakashi recognise Tobi as [Obito Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Obito_Uchiha "Obito Uchiha").

![Kakashi and Guy are shocked to see Obito alive.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/4/46/Guy_and_Kakashi_see_Obito.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230409020454)

Kakashi and Guy are shocked to see Obito alive.

Kakashi asks Obito why he never returned to Konoha, to which Obito replies that it's because Kakashi allowed [Rin Nohara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rin_Nohara "Rin Nohara") to die. Obito assures Kakashi that he doesn't blame him for the world's flaws before attacking him with [Fire Release: Blast Wave Wild Dance](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fire_Release:_Blast_Wave_Wild_Dance "Fire Release: Blast Wave Wild Dance"). Obito is soon joined by a reincarnated [Madara Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Madara_Uchiha "Madara Uchiha"), who decides to take Naruto's Nine-Tails and Killer B's [Eight-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eight-Tails "Eight-Tails") before the Ten-Tails is revived. Kakashi, meanwhile, tries to question Obito more, who ignores him and instead uses his own Kamui on Kakashi. Kakashi figures out how to use Kamui to return, prompting Obito to try to kill him. Naruto blocks the attack and vows not to let his teammates die.

![Kakashi battling Obito.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/a/ad/Kakashi_VS_Obito.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140515122811)

Kakashi battling Obito.

Naruto's words remind Kakashi of the Obito he used to know, inspiring him to join the offensive again. After the Nine-Tails replenishes his chakra, Kakashi lets Obito send him to Kamui's dimension, where he attacks Obito's body parts whenever they transport there to avoid Naruto's attacks. He then uses Kamui to return, only to witness the Ten-Tails complete its revival. Obito and Madara attach themselves to the Ten-Tails, focusing it destructive power against Kakashi and the others. Just as they are about to be killed, the combined Allied Shinobi Forces arrive to help, deflecting the Ten-Tails' Tailed Beast Ball. Sakura heals Kakashi and they then join the Allies' coordinated effort to restrain the Ten-Tails, but it breaks free by transforming. Many die in the Ten-Tails' counterattack, but another coordinated assault successfully removes the Ten-Tails from Madara and Obito's control.

![Kakashi and Obito pierce each other.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/c/ca/Kakashi_Vs_Obito.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150216001031)

Kakashi and Obito pierce each other.

Now free to act on its own, the Ten-Tails prepares to use [Tenpenchii](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tenpenchii "Tenpenchii"). Kakashi attempts to stop it with Kamui, but Obito intercepts him and takes him to Kamui's dimension. When they get there, Kakashi lunges at Obito with a Lightning Cutter, but stops short of actually killing him with it. When Obito accuses him of having a guilty conscience, Kakashi tries to explain the circumstances of Rin's death, but Obito is already aware that she chose to die. That fact alone convinces Obito that the world as it is isn't worth protecting. Accepting that Obito's mind can't be changed, Kakashi attack in order to protect the world that the Obito he used to know cared about. After a prolonged exchange, Kakashi pierces Obito's heart with a [lightning](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning "Lightning") -infused kunai. Obito escapes and Kakashi, similarly badly damaged, collapses.

Kakashi confronts Rahyō about this and Kahyō, unaware that this was intended, is aghast. Kahyō attacks Kakashi and removes his Chains of Ice so he can fight back, but he stops short of killing her when he realises that's what she wants out of regret for her actions.

The damaged Tobishachimaru starts rocking violently, flinging Rahyō from the ship, and then rises into the air uncontrollably, threatening to suffocate everyone aboard. Kakashi and Kahyō puncture the helium sacs keeping the ship afloat and Kahyō attempts to control its descent with her ice. He is saved by the [Third Tsuchikage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Tsuchikage "Third Tsuchikage"), who delivers him to the ground. Konoha's ninja search the crashed Tobishachimaru, treating the passengers and taking Kahyō into custody. Tsunade threatens to have her executed since she is the highest ranking conspirator to survive the failed jailbreak, but Kakashi requests that he be allowed to pass sentence instead, what would be his first act as Hokage: Kahyō will stay at the Blood Prison for the rest of her life, acting as warden by using her Chains of Ice on the prisoners. Both Tsunade and Kahyō agree to these terms.

As he realised that he was making the same mistake, Hōki also realised from Sukea's hints that there were more bombs still in circulation. Putting aside their differences, Team 25 decided to work together, working to their personal strengths to find the remaining bombs and avert disaster. Later, after the scroll is obtained, Kakashi is assigned to defend it, but he and the other defenders are defeated by the [Nine Masked Beasts](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine_Masked_Beasts "Nine Masked Beasts").

## Creation and Conception

[Masashi Kishimoto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Masashi_Kishimoto "Masashi Kishimoto") originally intended for Kakashi to debut in the second chapter of the *Naruto* manga, appearing prior to the other members of [Team 7](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Kakashi "Team Kakashi"). This Kakashi was designed as a laid back but very skilled ninja that ended his sentences with the polite " *de gozaru* " in the Japanese versions of the series. After speaking with his editor, Kishimoto pushed this debut back, allowing him to better develop Kakashi and the rest of Team 7.[^109] Despite this, Kakashi retains many of his original personality traits, being easygoing, unfazed by the actions of others, and eyes appearing to be half-asleep. Kishimoto feels that this makes Kakashi a compelling leader and helps to keep the diverse members of Team 7 unified.[^110] Because he acts as a link between the other main characters of the series, Kakashi is rarely featured prominently in promotional artwork, instead appearing in the background while his students are the focus of the scene.[^111] [^112] In an interview in [*Shōnen Jump*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weekly_Sh%C5%8Dnen_Jump "wikipedia:Weekly Shōnen Jump") 's, Kishimoto said that if he were to make an extra story from the manga with a different character, Kakashi comes to mind for him.[^113]

When deciding upon Kakashi's name, Kishimoto considered a number of possibilities: Kuwa (クワ, *literally meaning:* Hoe), Kama (カマ, *literally meaning:* Sickle), Botan (ボタン, *literally meaning:* Peony), Enoki (エノキ, *literally meaning:* Nettle tree), and Kakashi (カカシ, *literally meaning:* Scarecrow). He eventually decided upon Kakashi, and remains glad that he did to this day.[^114] In keeping with the meaning of his name, scarecrows are occasionally used to represent Kakashi; Naruto, for example, uses a scarecrow that is dressed like Kakashi to help him train for a combat test between the two.[^115] Likewise, scarecrows are at times added to the background of scenes in which Kakashi appears, as is the case with the cover of [*Naruto* manga volume three](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Volume_3 "Volume 3").[^116] Kishimoto has also noted that Kakashi's design was inspired by Taito Magatsu from the manga series [*Blade of the Immortal*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blade_of_the_Immortal "wikipedia:Blade of the Immortal").[^117]

	- Kakashi's hobby is reading (*Icha Icha* series).
		- Kakashi wishes to fight [Minato Namikaze](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Minato_Namikaze "Minato Namikaze").
		- Kakashi's favourite foods are salt-grilled saury and eggplant miso soup, while his least favourites are tempura or anything sweet.
		- Kakashi has completed 1,141 official missions in total: 197 D-rank, 190 C-rank, 414 B-rank, 298 A-rank, 42 S-rank.
		- Kakashi's favourite word is "Team-work" (チームワーク, *Chīmuwāku*).
