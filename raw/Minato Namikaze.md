**Minato Namikaze** (波風ミナト, *Namikaze Minato*) was the **Fourth [Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage "Hokage")** (四代目火影, *Yondaime Hokage*, *literally meaning:* Fourth Fire Shadow) of [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure"). He was renowned and feared all over the world, across multiple nations as **Konoha's Yellow Flash** (木ノ葉の黄色い閃光, *Konoha no Kiiroi Senkō*, *English TV:* Yellow Flash of the Leaf).[^8] He died during the [Nine-Tailed Demon Fox's Attack](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tailed_Demon_Fox%27s_Attack "Nine-Tailed Demon Fox's Attack"), sacrificing his life to [seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eight_Trigrams_Sealing_Style "Eight Trigrams Sealing Style") a half of the [Nine-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tails "Nine-Tails") into himself and the other half into his newborn son, [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki").

## Background

### Early life

![Minato in Team 6.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/f/f6/Team_jiraiya.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240908011759)

Minato in Team 6.

Minato entered Konoha's [Academy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Academy "Academy") with the dream of earning the villagers' respect and recognition by becoming Hokage.[^9] When [Kushina Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kushina_Uzumaki "Kushina Uzumaki") moved to Konoha, she was placed in the same class as Minato. Minato was immediately drawn to her bright red hair, finding it beautiful. Minato was a natural prodigy as a ninja, the likes of which appears only once in a generation.[^10] When he was added to a [genin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genin "Genin") unit, [Team 6](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_6 "Team 6"), under the leadership of [Jiraiya](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jiraiya "Jiraiya"). The Sannin took a special interest in Minato and took him on as his apprentice, teaching him many of his own signature abilities.[^11] In time, Minato's exceptional skills led Jiraiya to believe he might be the [Child of the Prophecy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Child_of_the_Prophecy "Child of the Prophecy"), someone who would someday save the world.[^12]

Years later, after they graduated and became ninja, Kushina was kidnapped by [Kumogakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kumogakure "Kumogakure") forces. Konoha mobilised its ninja to retrieve her, but only Minato noticed the strands of red hair Kushina left behind to mark their path. Minato, acting alone, rescued her from her captors.[^13] On their way back to Konoha, Minato confessed his admiration for her hair and her strength,[^14] causing Kushina – who had previously believed Minato "unreliable" and even "girly" [^15] – to fall in love with him.

The feat of single-handedly putting a stop to the Nine-Tails' abduction raised Minato's renown greatly, moving him from an ordinary shinobi to one who was well known across many nations for his great accomplishment of foiling Kumo's kidnapping attempt,[^16] both in the [Land of Fire](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_Fire "Land of Fire") and beyond.[^17]

### Konoha's Yellow Flash

Minato and Kushina were a couple from the time that he rescued her, and with his love, Kushina was able to keep the Nine-Tails sealed within her at bay. To aid in this goal, Minato was taught many sealing techniques from both Kushina and Jiraiya. When Konoha became involved in the [Third Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Shinobi_World_War "Third Shinobi World War"), Minato's obligations became split between his efforts to protect Kushina and helping with to win the war. During a confrontation against [Han](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Han "Han") and [Rōshi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/R%C5%8Dshi "Rōshi"), the two Iwa-nin unleashed their [Tailed Beast Modes](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki_Forms "Jinchūriki Forms") to attack Team 6 with their [Tailed Beast Balls](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Ball "Tailed Beast Ball"). After Minato warped himself and his team to safety, Minato took inspiration from the Tailed Beast Ball, researching its nature and began a three-year process of creating the [Rasengan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rasengan "Rasengan").[^18] The day he completed it, Kushina visited him. He explained he developing the Rasengan for her, in the event she had to fight other jinchūriki. The Nine-Tails attempted to break free at that moment, its chakra leaking and injuring him. Minato was able to reinforce the seal, and his words of affirmation towards Kushina encouraged her to resist as well. The Nine-Tails' pierced Minato's gut with its claws during the encounter, but he recovered. Kushina later took him to the top of her residence, and named the Rasengan, as he had pointed out he made it for her. Minato and Kushina would eventually marry each other.

![Team Minato.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/f/fd/Team_Minato.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150224111657)

Team Minato.

After proving himself an accomplished and responsible ninja, Minato was placed in charge of his own team, consisting of [Rin Nohara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rin_Nohara "Rin Nohara"), [Obito Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Obito_Uchiha "Obito Uchiha"), and [Kakashi Hatake](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi_Hatake "Kakashi Hatake"). In the anime, the [Third Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Hokage "Third Hokage") gave Minato the special task of helping Kakashi overcome the bitterness caused by [his father](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sakumo "Sakumo") 's death and remind him of the human element of being a ninja.[^19] Minato gave his team a [bell test](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Bell_test "Bell test") upon its formation in order to teach them the value of teamwork.[^20] Minato often held back less against the prodigious Kakashi than with Rin and Obito so they couldn't obtain the bells without teamwork. Unfortunately, Kakashi realised this, but only used Obito and Rin as a means to an end to obtain the bells. Nonetheless, he passed them because they accomplished the goal of the test by working as a team. Although he did encourage them to improve their teamwork afterwards, a message that Obito and Rin took to heart, but fell on deaf ears with Kakashi.

During a mission into [Kusagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kusagakure "Kusagakure") to destroy the [Kannabi Bridge](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kannabi_Bridge "Kannabi Bridge"), Minato transferred leadership of the mission to Kakashi – who had recently become a [jōnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/J%C5%8Dnin "Jōnin") – while he headed to the front lines. He met up with the [surviving and out-numbered](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hamaki_Mimura "Hamaki Mimura") Konoha ninja and single-handedly wiped out the opposing [Iwagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Iwagakure "Iwagakure") forces. In the anime, his victory in this battle against a thousand ninja convinced the [Third Tsuchikage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Tsuchikage "Third Tsuchikage") to accept a peace treaty.[^21] When he rendezvoused with his team afterwards he found Kakashi and Rin and surrounded by Iwa-nin, Obito having already been lost. He rescued them, helped them complete their original mission once they recovered, and returned to Konoha with them to mourn Obito.[^22] Minato's competing obligations would later repeat the tragedy of Obito's death: while he was away on another mission, Madara orchestrated Rin's kidnapping by [Kirigakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kirigakure "Kirigakure"), who died during Kakashi's attempt to retrieve her, as part of Madara's plot to corrupt Obito.

Minato encountered [A](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/A_\(Fourth_Raikage\) "A (Fourth Raikage)") of Kumogakure a number of times during the war,[^23] developing a rivalry across their many battles.[^16] During their first meeting, Minato and A fought with their speed, with Minato proving the faster. He received orders to retreat before their competition could resolve, but he lingered to compliment A and his brother, [B](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/B "B"), a [jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki "Jinchūriki") like Kushina; he encouraged A to take care of B and remember he was a person. A pursued Minato, so Minato moved against B, threatening to kill him if A didn't let him and his team go.[^24] Minato was confident he and A would someday meet as [Kage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kage "Kage").[^25]

### Fourth Hokage

![Minato becomes Hokage.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/27/Minato_becomes_hokage.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20141026170220)

Minato becomes Hokage.

For his performance during the War, Minato was chosen over [Orochimaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Orochimaru "Orochimaru") to become Fourth Hokage.[^26] He taught the Flying Thunder God Technique to the [Hokage Guard Platoon](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage_Guard_Platoon "Hokage Guard Platoon") to better aid them in their duties of serving the Hokage at any given time.[^27] In the anime, he also tried to help Kakashi, now an [Anbu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Anbu "Anbu"), emerge from the darkness he'd fallen into after Obito and Rin's deaths. During the gradual transfer of responsibilities from the Third Hokage to him, the Third advised Minato to restore Kakashi's value of life by tasking him with protecting Kushina during her pregnancy.[^28]

As a jinchūriki, the strain of childbirth would weaken the seal keeping the Nine-Tails sealed into Kushina. For that reason special precautions had to be taken: the child would secretly be delivered in a remote location outside of the village, with barriers around the area and several Anbu guards as extra protection. Minato was not greatly worried about the birth, and in fact gave Kakashi the day off in gratitude for his good work. He and Kushina had already decided on a name for their son, "Naruto", after the main character of Jiraiya's [first book](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/The_Tale_of_the_Utterly_Gutsy_Shinobi "The Tale of the Utterly Gutsy Shinobi"), hoping that their Naruto would be just as determined as the character (and thus its author). Jiraiya tried to convince them to make a different choice since that would make him Naruto's godfather, but they insisted that this was a good thing.[^29]

Naruto was born on the night of October 10. Only moments after the birth Minato began making preparations to restore the Nine-Tails' weakened seal, but he was interrupted by the arrival of a masked man, otherwise known as [Tobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Obito_Uchiha "Obito Uchiha"). Tobi killed the Anbu guards and midwives and took the newborn Naruto hostage, threatening to kill him if Minato didn't back away from Kushina. Minato immediately took Naruto back from him, yet discovered [explosive tags](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Explosive_tag "Explosive tag") had been planted on Naruto. Minato was forced to teleport away to keep Naruto from harm, allowing Tobi to escape with Kushina. He found a safe place for Naruto and teleported to Kushina in time to save her from being killed by the Nine-Tails, extracted from her body by Tobi. He reunited mother and son before heading out to protect the village.[^30]

![Minato clashing with the masked man.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/e/e5/Minato_vs_Tobi.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20120210084331)

Minato clashing with the masked man.

Minato returned to the village in the early stages of the Nine-Tails' attack on Konoha, arriving in time to [teleport away](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Flying_Thunder_God:_Guiding_Thunder "Flying Thunder God: Guiding Thunder") a [Tailed Beast Ball](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Ball "Tailed Beast Ball") before it destroyed the [Hokage Rock](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage_Rock "Hokage Rock"). He attempted to reach the Third Hokage so that he could inform him of what happened, but was interrupted by Tobi, who tried to [warp him away](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kamui "Kamui"). Minato reacted in time to escape, but Tobi pursued him. Because Tobi was controlling the Nine-Tails' actions, defeating him was of prime importance in order to save Konoha. Minato initially struggled to successfully strike him but, after several failed attacks, he finally hit him with a Rasengan and branded him with a Flying Thunder God seal, allowing him to teleport to Tobi whenever he wanted. He then used a [Contract Seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Contract_Seal "Contract Seal") on Tobi to release the Nine-Tails from his control, forcing him to flee.[^31]

No longer being controlled, the Nine-Tails began retreating from the village, though it continued to attack the Konoha ninja that pursued it. As it prepared another Tailed Beast Ball, Minato once again arrived in time to stop it, this time by [dropping](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning:_Food_Cart_Destroyer_Technique "Summoning: Food Cart Destroyer Technique") [Gamabunta](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gamabunta "Gamabunta") on top of it. He had Gamabunta keep it restrained while he gathered the chakra needed to transport the Nine-Tails to Naruto and Kushina's location, the farthest from the village he could get. Kushina [restrained](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Adamantine_Sealing_Chains "Adamantine Sealing Chains") it and erected a barrier so that it couldn't escape while they decided what to do. To protect the village, Kushina offered to have the Nine-Tails sealed back into her so it would die with her. Minato rejected this, believing the Nine-Tails' power would be needed in the future if and when Tobi struck again. Remembering Jiraiya's words about the "Child of the Prophecy", Minato decided to believe that Naruto was that child and that he would someday save the world. To prepare Naruto for this, Minato would make Naruto the Nine-Tails' new jinchūriki so he could make use of its strength.

![Minato and Kushina protecting Naruto.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/b0/Minato_and_Kushina_protect_Naruto.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240124003940)

Minato and Kushina protecting Naruto.

Because the Nine-Tails' chakra was too immense to seal into an infant, Minato used the [Dead Demon Consuming Seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Dead_Demon_Consuming_Seal "Dead Demon Consuming Seal") to cut its chakra in half, sealing the Yin half in himself. Realising what they were planning, the Nine-Tails tried to kill Naruto, but Minato and Kushina used themselves as shields. With his last ounce of strength, Minato summoned [Gerotora](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gerotora "Gerotora"), gave him the key to the seal he would use on Naruto, and sent him to Jiraiya. Meanwhile, Kushina told Naruto how much his parents loved him, which Minato seconded. He [sealed](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eight_Trigrams_Sealing_Style "Eight Trigrams Sealing Style") the Nine-Tails' Yang half into Naruto, weaving Kushina's chakra into the seal so that she could someday assist Naruto with learning to control the Nine-Tails and its chakra.[^32] He mingled in some of his own chakra, too, programming it to activate if ever the seal was about to break so that he could restore it.[^33] Seeing the Third Hokage nearby, Minato communicated to him a final request for the villagers of Konoha: to think of Naruto as a hero and not the container of the monster that caused so much death and destruction.[^34] With that, his soul was consumed by the [Shinigami](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shinigami "Shinigami"), ending Minato's life.

## Personality

Despite his physical resemblance to Naruto, Minato's personality was considered by both [Jiraiya](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jiraiya "Jiraiya") and [Tsunade](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tsunade "Tsunade") as the opposite of Naruto's; he was a very humble and collected man. Very perceptive and shrewd, he was noted as not to do something without a reason.[^35] He was well-aware of the terror his reputation inspired in rival villages and would take advantage of it at times, but even then he would remain respectful to opponents. A very polite individual, Minato would adress others with the appropriate honourifics, directed even towards dangerous criminals such as [Orochimaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Orochimaru "Orochimaru").

Minato also wouldn't pry into other people's personal affairs unless he felt it was necessary,[^36] and was not the type to hold grudges, only feeling sorry for Obito despite his role in his own and Kushina's deaths, dissuading Kakashi from killing Obito.[^37] If anything, Minato blames himself for his death for several reasons; he couldn't save Obito from the fate that [corrupted](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Curse_of_Hatred "Curse of Hatred") him, Konoha from the difficulties that arose after his death,[^38] and even his own son Naruto from the painful and difficult life he suffered as a jinchūriki orphan.[^39]

Regardless of their differences, Naruto is not without any of his father's personality traits: both have indomitable spirits and fierce ambitions that drive them through all their choices in life. Outside of missions, he would be rather carefree and lighthearted like his son. Both are of unwavering loyalty to Konoha and their loved ones – friends and family – and that devotion inspires all those around them. Also like Naruto, Minato had the tendency to create questionable techniques, although in Minato's case, it was the pointlessly longwinded names he came up with rather than the actually useful technique. This was seen when initially considered "Halo Hair Whole of Jiraiya Inspired by Frozen Dessert Twin Style Sphere" as the name for what would instead be called the Rasengan.[^40]

## Appearance

Minato was a tall, fair-skinned man frequently compared to Naruto in physical appearance; both have blue eyes and spiky, blond hair. Minato also had jaw-length bangs framing either side of his face. Minato was considered to be very handsome as many girls became infatuated with him.[^41] [^42] His normal attire consisted of a standard Konoha uniform with two bands on both of his sleeves, a green [flak jacket](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Flak_jacket "Flak jacket"), blue [forehead protector](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Forehead_protector "Forehead protector"), and blue sandals. After becoming Hokage, he started wearing a short-sleeved long white haori over his normal attire, closed in the front by a thin, orange rope. The haori was decorated by red flame-like motifs on the edges, with the kanji for "Fourth Hokage" (四代目火影, *Yondaime Hokage*) written vertically down the back.

During his time in the Academy, he wore a simple white suit of clothing with green trimmings and a hood. After becoming a full-fledged shinobi, he wore a tracksuit with three stripes on the sleeves, mesh under-armour, a pair of black pants, and sandals.

## Abilities

![Minato defeating Tobi.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/a/ae/Minato_defeats_Tobi.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140220180917)

Minato defeating Tobi.

Minato was one of the most powerful shinobi in history. He was prodigy of such talent that, while still a child, he became well-known and regarded across many nations for foiling Kumogakure's kidnapping of his village's Jinchūriki.[^43] By adulthood he was considered by the likes of Jiraiya and the Fourth Raikage to be unmatched,[^10] [^44] and one of very few people able to fight a [tailed beast](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_beast "Tailed beast") head-on.[^45] [Kurama](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kurama "Kurama") compared Minato's power to [Hashirama Senju](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hashirama_Senju "Hashirama Senju") 's.[^46] During the Third Shinobi World War, flee-on-sight orders were given to enemy shinobi should they encounter him.[^5] In the anime, he single-handedly wiped one thousand [Iwa](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Iwa "Iwa") -nin — a feat that forced [Ōnoki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/%C5%8Cnoki "Ōnoki") to accept a peace treaty.[^21] During the Nine-Tails' attack on Konoha, it was only from Minato's actions that Tobi and the fox were defeated and the village was saved from destruction. Over a decade later, with the looming threat of Orochimaru to the village, Minato was believed the only one who could stop the Sannin at the time.[^47] Minato's fighting style revolved mainly around suddenly appearing and just as quickly taking down enemies, often excluding traditional time-consuming methods like hand seals.

### Ninjutsu

![Minato using the Shadow Clone Technique.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/7/7e/Minato_Shadow_Clone.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230409025028)

Minato using the Shadow Clone Technique.

Minato was taught many skills and techniques by Jiraiya, including to [summon](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning_Technique "Summoning Technique") [toads](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Toads "Toads"), becoming one of few people to earn the full respect and cooperation of Toad Boss [Gamabunta](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gamabunta "Gamabunta").[^48] He had skill with [barrier ninjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Barrier_ninjutsu "Barrier ninjutsu").[^49] Minato was an adept [sensor](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sensor "Sensor"), able to detect and track chakra signatures from great distances.[^50] He could even sense a person’s strength level.[^51] For surveillance, he could detect everyone within an area by placing his finger on the ground.[^52] Minato could also use the [Shadow Clone Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_Clone_Technique "Shadow Clone Technique") for a variety of battling strategies, from gaining strength in numbers to deception. Minato was proficient in the [nature transformations](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nature_transformation "Nature transformation") of [Fire](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fire "Fire"), [Wind](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wind "Wind"), [Lightning](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning "Lightning"), along with [Yin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yin "Yin") and [Yang Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yang_Release "Yang Release").[^4]

#### Rasengan

![Minato forming the Rasengan.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/8e/Minato_Rasengan_and_Kunai.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150214212845)

Minato forming the Rasengan.

After observing the nature of the Tailed Beast Ball,[^53] Minato spent three years developing one of his signature techniques, the Rasengan.[^54] He created it for his then-girlfriend, [Kushina Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kushina_Uzumaki "Kushina Uzumaki"), both for Kushina to use for her own protection and so he himself could battle beside her against enemy [Jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki "Jinchūriki"). Its power proved able to stalemate [Kurama](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kurama "Kurama") 's [Tailed Beast Ball](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Ball "Tailed Beast Ball").[^45] The highest level of shape transformation, it gives him an edge in combat due to requiring no hand seals to create and being self-sustaining. Minato's mastery allowed him to form it with either hand instantly and vary its size from standard to [Big Ball Rasengan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Big_Ball_Rasengan "Big Ball Rasengan") -sized.[^55] He intended to combine it with his element chakra, but failed to before his early passing. When using Tailed Beast Mode, his Rasengan becomes proportionately as large.[^56]

#### Space–Time Ninjutsu

![Minato's Space-Time Barrier.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/4/41/Space-Time_Barrier.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150218014653)

Minato's Space-Time Barrier.

His exploits with the Flying Thunder God technique gained him the moniker "Konoha's Yellow Flash".[^57] The creator of this technique, the [Second Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Hokage "Second Hokage"), openly acknowledged Minato's skills as superior to his in its usage. With the technique, Minato could instantly move to the location of anything marked with his special seal, whether a tool, place, or person. Minato also could bring others with him,[^58] or send them by themselves,[^59] giving him a [defensive option](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Flying_Thunder_God:_Guiding_Thunder "Flying Thunder God: Guiding Thunder") to teleport incoming threats away.[^60] He was also a skilled teacher of [Space–Time Ninjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Space%E2%80%93Time_Ninjutsu "Space–Time Ninjutsu"), having taught the [Hokage Guard Platoon](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage_Guard_Platoon "Hokage Guard Platoon") the [Flying Thunder Formation Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Flying_Thunder_Formation_Technique "Flying Thunder Formation Technique").

#### Shurikenjutsu

![Minato's special kunai.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/1/1a/Minato%27s_Kunai.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20141026182659)

Minato's special kunai.

Minato had his own special [brand of kunai](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Flying_Thunder_God_Kunai "Flying Thunder God Kunai") for combat; triple-prong kunai for better offensive potential. He would throw them at opponents or wield them as melee tools, even in his mouth.[^61] Their main use was for the [Flying Thunder God](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Flying_Thunder_God "Flying Thunder God") seals they were marked with, allowing him to teleport to wherever one of the kunai were.[^62] He carried a large number of kunai in the field that he would scatter across a wide area so that he could move around with more options.[^63] Although his normal tactics with kunai did not require precision, Minato nevertheless had excellent aim and could coordinate his throws' timing and placement to allow him to perform [complex manoeuvres](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Flying_Thunder_God_%E2%80%94_Second_Step "Flying Thunder God — Second Step").[^64]

#### Fūinjutsu

![The key to the Nine-Tails' seal.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/24/Eight_Trigrams_Seal.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150222045043)

The key to the Nine-Tails' seal.

Minato learned several [fūinjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/F%C5%ABinjutsu "Fūinjutsu") from Jiraiya and Kushina.[^65] Kurama was shocked to seeing such a unique sealing prowess when it attempted to escape [Kushina Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kushina_Uzumaki "Kushina Uzumaki").[^46] With the [Eight Trigrams Sealing Style](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eight_Trigrams_Sealing_Style "Eight Trigrams Sealing Style"), he sealed the Nine-Tails' Yang-half into Naruto and the Yin-half into himself, allowing small amounts of the Nine-Tails' chakra to naturally mix with the host's own.[^66] Knowing the seal would slowly weaken over time, Minato created a key that could accelerate or reverse this process. As an extra precaution, he mixed Kushina's and his own chakra into the seal so that, when certain conditions were met, they could help Naruto with his control of the Nine-Tails' power, as well as allow them to see their son after they were dead.[^33]

Minato could also perform the [Dead Demon Consuming Seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Dead_Demon_Consuming_Seal "Dead Demon Consuming Seal"), which he had enough familiarity with to know it couldn't be used to seal all of the Nine-Tails' chakra. With the [Contract Seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Contract_Seal "Contract Seal"), he could remove an opponent's control over any creatures they summoned.[^67] He could also immediately [transfer](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Transfer_Technique "Tailed Beast Transfer Technique") a [tailed beast](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_beast "Tailed beast") from a jinchūriki into a new host.[^68]

### Chakra and Physical Prowess

![Minato saving Kakashi.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/23/Minato_Saving_Kakashi.PNG/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160121001744)

Minato saving Kakashi.

Minato was hailed as the fastest shinobi of his time.[^23] While this recognition was due in part to his space–time ninjutsu, Minato's natural speed and prowess with the [Body Flicker Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Body_Flicker_Technique "Body Flicker Technique") were immense: he could outstrip three other Kage-level ninja [^69] and engage an enemy or retrieve an ally before anyone realised what he was doing.[^70] He could cover a considerable distance in a short amount of time and perform a technique before an opponent could finish theirs.[^71] In the anime, he could appear before [sensors](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sensor "Sensor") could detect his approach.[^72] Minato's reflexes allowed him to react to and avoid techniques with no warning, such as [Kamui](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kamui "Kamui") [^73] and [Lightning Straight](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning_Straight "Lightning Straight").[^74]

Minato had very large chakra reserves, able to summon the massive Gamabunta, as well as [teleport](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Flying_Thunder_God_Technique "Flying Thunder God Technique") multiple massive targets like a tailed beast and their attacks. His chakra control was very refined, evidenced by his skill and versatility in the Rasengan, which demands very precise chakra control to use, as able to weave [hand seals](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hand_seals "Hand seals") with only one hand.[^75] Minato also demonstrated great endurance, able to survive being impaled through the chest by Version 2 Kushina and still fight back Kurama within its seal.[^76] He could also withstand being impaled by Kurama and survive long enough to impart instructions unto others before his eventual death.[^32]

### Jinchūriki Transformations

![Minato's Nine-Tails Chakra Mode.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/9/90/Minato%27s_Chakra_Mode.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20151203005712)

### Senjutsu

![Minato in Sage Mode.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/a/ab/Minato%27s_Sage_Mode.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150604113414)

Minato in Sage Mode.

Minato's chakra strength and reserves are both great enough to learn [senjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Senjutsu "Senjutsu") of [Mount Myōboku](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mount_My%C5%8Dboku "Mount Myōboku"), enhancing his techniques and physical parameters by entering [Sage Mode](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sage_Mode "Sage Mode").[^81] He could perfectly balance the natural energy with his chakra, displaying only the orange pigmentation around his eyes and no other physical changes — the true marking of a Sage. However, by his own admission, Minato's skill in senjutsu was limited as he hasn't used it in actual battle before, making him unable to maintain it for long.[^82]

### Intelligence

Minato was an exceptionally intelligent and keen-minded individual. He graduated the Academy at the age of 10 years old, going on to have the highest scores in the history of the Chūnin Exams written test.[^83] Even at a young age, only he, out of all the ninja sent to rescue Kushina, noticed the trail she left behind to mark her kidnappers' path.[^13] In combat, he could correctly deduce the basic mechanics behind a technique after seeing it only once, and from there plan around its strengths and weaknesses to his or his allies' advantage.[^84] [^85] With his observational skills he could determine an opponent's plans and motivations based on only limited evidence.[^86] After he had finished analysing, Minato would strike quickly and with precision, attacking vulnerabilities at the most opportune moments.

As Hokage, Minato prepared for threats facing Konoha by placing a network of marked locations around the village's outskirts for him to teleport to, including at least two [safe houses](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Minato%27s_Safe_Houses "Minato's Safe Houses") and likewise passed on his space–time ninjutsu onto the [Hokage Guard Platoon](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage_Guard_Platoon "Hokage Guard Platoon").

## Legacy

After Minato died, the [Third Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Hokage "Third Hokage") reported to the villagers Minato's last request that Naruto be regarded as a hero. Very few were able to separate their hatred of the Nine-Tails from Naruto, prompting the Third to forbid anyone from speaking of the Nine-Tails, hoping that the younger generation would not blindly hate Naruto as the rest of the villagers did.[^34] He also concealed Naruto's relation to Minato in order to keep him safe from his father's enemies,[^88] and otherwise did his best to give Naruto a comfortable life. For years Naruto did not know his parents' identities, and neither did most of Konoha.

Even when he was not aware of his relation to Minato, Naruto still greatly admired the Fourth Hokage, considering him a hero who gave his life for the village [^89] and becoming overjoyed whenever their similarities were remarked upon.[^11] [^90] Before Naruto left Konoha to begin his training with Jiraiya, he looked at the Fourth Hokage's face on the [Hokage Rock](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage_Rock "Hokage Rock") and asked him to look after him.[^91] After learning that Minato was his father, Naruto took pride in it and felt it was his responsibility to makes his parents' sacrifices worth it.[^92]

With Minato's death, the Third was forced to resume his position as Hokage, a role he filled until his death, never being able to find a replacement of Minato's calibre. Minato himself became regarded as the village's hero,[^89] and, indeed, one of the greatest ninja Konoha had ever produced,[^41] so much so that villagers would regret his death whenever tragedy struck the village.[^47] Because Minato was never able to tell anyone what happened during the Nine-Tails' attack, villagers came to their own conclusions and the [Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uchiha "Uchiha") were suspected of the act, creating ill-will that would culminate in the [Uchiha Clan Downfall](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uchiha_Clan_Downfall "Uchiha Clan Downfall"). Jiraiya recognised that Minato would not have sealed the Nine-Tails into his own son unless he had a good reason and that Minato intended Naruto to gain control of the Nine-Tails, but he could never conclude why.[^35]

After the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War"), his student, [Kakashi Hatake](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi_Hatake "Kakashi Hatake"), became the Sixth [Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage "Hokage"), and his son, Naruto, became the Seventh Hokage ten years later. Four and six years respectively after the war, Minato had [two](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Boruto "Boruto") [grandchildren](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Himawari "Himawari"), who both inherited his prodigious talent.[^93] [^94] Minato was featured in [Extreme: Shinobi Picture Scrolls](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Extreme:_Shinobi_Picture_Scrolls "Extreme: Shinobi Picture Scrolls") cards, Shikadai telling Kawaki to address Minato with proper honorifics indicating that even new generations hold him in high regard.

- The name "Minato" means "harbour" (港), while his family name "Namikaze" (波風) means "waves and wind".
- Minato ranked ninth in the sixth [character popularity poll](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Character_Popularity_Polls "Naruto Character Popularity Polls") and seventh in the seventh poll.
- Minato's silhouette can be faintly seen in the background of the [*Naruto Shippūden* third opening](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Blue_Bird "Blue Bird") standing behind Naruto and [Sora](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sora "Sora") and under the Nine-Tails, in reference to its attack on Konoha that binds all four characters.
- In *[Hidden Leaf Village Grand Sports Festival!](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hidden_Leaf_Village_Grand_Sports_Festival! "Hidden Leaf Village Grand Sports Festival!")*, Minato suddenly appears in the hallway of the stadium when Naruto is running to the toilets. All of these appearances in this special were only for the comical effect.
- In *[Naruto Shippūden the Movie: The Lost Tower](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Shipp%C5%ABden_the_Movie:_The_Lost_Tower "Naruto Shippūden the Movie: The Lost Tower")*, Minato's chakra appears to be a light green colour, as opposed to the normal blue.
- Studio Pierrot's Settei sheets of Minato show that he was 147 cm when he saved Kushina from Kumogakure.
- According to the databook(s):
	- Minato's hobby was reading; Jiraiya's novels were his favourite.
		- Minato wished to fight Jiraiya.
		- Minato's favourite food was Kushina's homemade cooking.
		- Minato completed 847 official missions in total: 122 D-rank, 147 C-rank, 216 B-rank, 323 A-rank, 39 S-rank.
		- Minato's favourite phrase was "The fire's shadow illuminates the village" (火の影は里を照らす, *Hi no kage wa sato o terasu*).
- On the cover of chapter 178, the colour on Minato's cloak were inverted.
- Minato is ambidextrous. He has been shown wielding his kunai as well as the Rasengan proficiently with each hand. This is also shown when he summons using his left hand,[^95] indicating that he signed the contract with his left hand.
- Prior to his battle with [Pain](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Pain "Pain"), Jiraiya mentioned Minato sealed Kurama within Naruto partially with the intent of having Naruto master a certain technique. Although it was never elaborated what technique it was, it is possible he was referring to the [Tailed Beast Ball](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Ball "Tailed Beast Ball") as only a jinchūriki has access to that technique, not including the Tailed Beasts themselves, and he did base his Rasengan off the Tailed Beast Ball.
- Prior to sealing half of Kurama within an infant Naruto, Minato mentioned to himself that he needed to put up a barrier to both contain and restrain Kurama. It is unknown whether this barrier would have held long enough for Minato to complete the sealing as he was also concerned about his remaining chakra reserves.
- Unlike most people who had a single hair whorl that spiral in a single direction, Minato had two whorls that spiral in different directions.[^96]
