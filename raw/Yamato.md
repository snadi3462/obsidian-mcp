This article is about the Konoha Anbu. For the member of the Ibura clan, see [Tenzō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tenz%C5%8D_\(Iburi_Clan\) "Tenzō (Iburi Clan)").

**Yamato** (ヤマト), also known as " **Tenzō** " (テンゾウ, *Tenzō*),[^4] are both code names for this [Anbu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Anbu "Anbu") -turned- [jōnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/J%C5%8Dnin "Jōnin") of [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure"). As his [Wood Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Release "Wood Release") would allow him to be able to keep the [Nine-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tails "Nine-Tails") sealed within [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki") in check, he was re-assigned from Anbu to the [regular forces](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shinobi_Organisational_System "Shinobi Organisational System"), joining [Team Kakashi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Kakashi "Team Kakashi") as a temporary replacement for their jōnin-sensei, [Kakashi Hatake](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi_Hatake "Kakashi Hatake"). While Kakashi eventually returns to the team, Yamato stays on to provide assistance as needed.

## Background

![Yamato as one of Orochimaru's test subjects.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/0c/Yamato_Test_Subject.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150827123040)

Yamato as one of Orochimaru's test subjects.

He graduated from the [Academy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Academy "Academy") at the age of six, and at that same age, was promoted to the rank of [chūnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin "Chūnin").[^1] As an infant, he was abducted by [Orochimaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Orochimaru "Orochimaru") and became one of sixty children that were his test subjects. They were injected with the [First Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/First_Hokage "First Hokage") 's DNA in the hopes that they could replicate the First's [Wood Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Release "Wood Release"). Before Orochimaru could finalise the results of the experiments, he was caught by Hiruzen for his other experiments and had to flee the village. Because every child who had been subjected to the experiment had died after a certain amount of time, Orochimaru believed all remaining test subjects would perish as well. In actuality, this boy was the lone survivor and found himself endowed with an unquantified Wood Release and likewise the First's abilities to [control](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Control "Tailed Beast Control") [tailed beasts](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_beasts "Tailed beasts").[^5] In the anime, while contained in a test tube, he was fully conscious of his surroundings, watching in horror as other children died from failed experiments. Not wanting to die all by himself, he used his Wood Release to escape. He was soon afterwards found by [Danzō Shimura](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Danz%C5%8D_Shimura "Danzō Shimura"). Having lost all memory of who he was, the boy was taken in by Danzō, where he was recruited into the [Anbu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Anbu "Anbu") 's [Root](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Root "Root") division under the codename **Kinoe** (甲, *Kinoe*).

Having high hopes for Kinoe, Danzō began to personally train him as a Root operative. He also gave Kinoe a scroll containing the secrets and various techniques of the Wood Release.[^6] As [Hiruzen Sarutobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hiruzen_Sarutobi "Hiruzen Sarutobi") began to resume his post as [Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage "Hokage") after [Minato Namikaze](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Minato_Namikaze "Minato Namikaze") 's untimely passing, Danzō sent Root members, including Kinoe, to assassinate the Third. However, Kakashi, a recent recruit to Root, warned the Hokage and went in his place. Kakashi quickly defeated Kinoe, but chose to spare him in the belief that he would become a valuable ally in the future. After the battle, Kinoe began following Kakashi from afar.[^7] Kinoe later informed Danzō of Orochimaru's defection from Konoha and was instructed to meet up with Orochimaru at an underground cave that secretly housed the [Iburi clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Iburi_clan "Iburi clan"). After delivering his message to the clan, who promised to help Orochimaru escape the country, he met a young girl named [Yukimi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yukimi "Yukimi") who insisted that Kinoe was her brother, [Tenzō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tenz%C5%8D_\(Iburi_Clan\) "Tenzō (Iburi Clan)"). While Yukimi began to show Kinoe around, word soon reached them that an unknown individual had entered the cave.[^8]

When the intruder, Kakashi, was subdued by the Iburi clan, Kinoe convinced them to let him interrogate Kakashi. While seemingly alone, Kinoe revealed that his actual mission was the same as Kakashi's, ensuring that Orochimaru did not escape the country. [Gotta](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gotta "Gotta"), the clan's leader, overheard this while spying and quickly ordered for both Konoha-nin to be killed, though Yukimi refused to let Kinoe die, saving them both. Leaving Kakashi behind, Yukimi possessed Kinoe's body and the two travelled to a carnival, where they took part in the festivities. Kakashi later caught up, planning to use Yukimi to capture Orochimaru, since she was of great importance to him. Refusing to let Yukimi be used as bait, Kinoe ran off with her, announcing that his name was "Tenzō".[^9]

Quickly catching up to them, Kakashi explained that Yukimi's blood could temporarily bestow Orochimaru with the Iburi clan's smoke transformation, allowing him to slip across the border. Overhearing that, Yukimi slipped away and returned to the cave. Recognising that Kinoe cared for Yukimi not unlike he had cared for [Rin Nohara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rin_Nohara "Rin Nohara"), Kakashi agreed to work together with Kinoe to save her. Upon returning to the cave, the two discovered that Orochimaru had killed the Iburi clan and captured Yukimi. The spirits of the Iburi guided Kinoe and Kakashi to Yukimi, causing Orochimaru to flee. Yukimi's body then began to destabilise, but the spirits of the Iburi used their power along with Kinoe's Wood Release to re-stabilise her ability. Kakashi decided to leave and not report anything about the Iburi or Root's involvement. Yukimi, no longer needing to fear the wind, parted ways with Kinoe to see the world, while the latter was happy to know that he did have the power to save people.[^10]

![Kinoe starts questioning Root's methods.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/d/d6/Kinoe_fights_with_Danz%C5%8D.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140508172017)

Kinoe starts questioning Root's methods.

Three years later, Danzō, fearing that his transplanted [Sharingan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sharingan "Sharingan") would eventually go blind from its over-usage, assigned Kinoe and [Kinoto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kinoto "Kinoto") to steal the Sharingan from Kakashi as a substitute. Kinoe found Kakashi while on a mission to investigate one of Orochimaru's abandoned labs. While initially working alongside Kakashi, he quickly attacked the older shinobi. As Kakashi tried to reason with him that friends should not kill each other, Kinoe retorted that comment, knowing that Kakashi killed his friend Rin. Enraged that Kinoe believed Kakashi killed her on purpose, Kakashi quickly overpowered him, telling Kinoe that she died because he couldn't protect her, making Kinoe remember the other test subjects he wanted to save. Refusing to kill Kinoe as he still viewed him a friend and ally, Kakashi instead chose to take him to the Third Hokage.[^6] Before he could, one of Orochimaru's [snake experiments](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Power_of_the_White_Snake "Power of the White Snake") escaped its test tube and attacked the duo. As Kinoe was taken captive, Kakashi managed to slay the creature. While saving Kinoe, the creature's corpse began emitting a poisonous vapour which quickly infected Kakashi. Kinoe got Kakashi out safely and, having accepting Kakashi's words, Kinoe gave Kakashi an antidote and chose to abandon his mission, leaving Kakashi a message of it.

!["Tenzō" joins Team Ro.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/bf/A_Shinobi_of_the_Leaf.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140320115519)

"Tenzō" joins Team Ro.

Upon returning to Danzō, the elder was furious at Kinoe's blatant choice to defy his orders. While Kinoe insisted that Root should not target fellow Konoha members as they are allies, Danzō countered by stating that Root exists as a separate section of Konoha. Remembering the feelings of friendship from Yukimi, Kinoe ultimately decided that Root's philosophies were not for him. Disgusted at Kinoe's new view, Danzō knocked him out with his Sharingan, ordering his men to have Kinoe's [cursed seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Cursed_Tongue_Eradication_Seal "Cursed Tongue Eradication Seal") activated. As Kinoe was prepped for the operation, Kakashi arrived and freed him. The two were quickly surrounded by Danzō and several Root members. Before Danzō could act, the Third Hokage appeared, vouching for Kakashi's unsanctioned arrival and convincing Danzō to give Kinoe over to him, though Danzō refused to remove the cursed seal on Kinoe's tongue. Later, Kinoe was re-assigned to [Team Ro](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Ro "Team Ro") under Kakashi's command. Joining his new squad, Kinoe was given a warm welcome to his new allies and insisted by Kakashi that he go by his chosen name of "Tenzō", which he happily agreed to.[^11]

![Tenzō and Kakashi as Anbu members.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/07/Tenz%C5%8D_and_Kakashi_as_Anbu.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140508171820)

Tenzō and Kakashi as Anbu members.

During this time in the Anbu, he [served under the guidance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senpai_and_k%C5%8Dhai "wikipedia:Senpai and kōhai") of Kakashi [^12] and learned much from him, developing great respect for his captain. Two years after joining Team Ro, a new Anbu was added to their team, [Itachi Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Itachi_Uchiha "Itachi Uchiha"), who was the youngest Anbu of all time. While many on the team members questioned the wisdom of allowing someone so young to join Anbu, Tenzō and Kakashi took a great interest in the prodigy's talents.[^13] Two years later, Tenzō was shocked to learn that Itachi was made an Anbu captain while being even younger than Kakashi was. He and Kakashi were then approached by [Yūgao Uzuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Y%C5%ABgao_Uzuki "Yūgao Uzuki"), who would act as Itachi's replacement for Team Ro. Later, Team Ro was mobilised to intercept the planned coup d'état, which was however already stopped by the [slaughter of the clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uchiha_Clan_Downfall "Uchiha Clan Downfall"). As they began gathering the corpses, Danzō told them that the culprit was their former comrade Itachi. Some time after the genocide, Tenzō tried to comfort Kakashi, who was saddened by his inability to help or even understand his former friend.[^14] Later, after Kakashi was honourably discharged from Anbu and reassigned as a jōnin sensei, Tenzō went to meet with his former captain, noting a positive change in Kakashi's demeanour from his new position.[^15] As Tenzō continued his career in Anbu, he would come to have the best performance record of any Anbu serving under the Third Hokage.[^16]

In the anime, when [Team 10](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Asuma "Team Asuma") and Naruto Uzumaki were given a [B-rank](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/B-rank "B-rank"), potentially [A-rank](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/A-rank "A-rank"), mission of rescuing the son of a [daimyō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Daimy%C5%8D "Daimyō"), [Shū](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sh%C5%AB_\(lord\) "Shū (lord)"), who was an ally to the [Land of Fire](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_Fire "Land of Fire"), Tsunade called for Yamato. Because of rumours about there being suspicious activity going on in the [Land of This](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_This "Land of This") where Team 10 was heading, she had Yamato secretly tail the younger Konoha-nin in the event things became [S-rank](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/S-rank "S-rank") danger and also gather any information possible.[^17] Later, after Team 10 completed the initial phase of their plan, capturing Shū, the nation was suddenly attacked by [Akatsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki "Akatsuki"). Yamato took action and destroyed several of [Sasori](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasori "Sasori") 's puppets. Despite his help, the nation ultimately fell to the Akatsuki's attack. With Team 10's mission still going to return Shū home, Yamato tailed them until they crossed the country borders. Afterwards, Yamato returned to Konohagakure to report to Tsunade, who became troubled by the news.[^18]

## Personality

![Yamato using his ghastly face fault to intimidate Naruto.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/5/51/Fear3.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20100422230654)

Yamato using his ghastly face fault to intimidate Naruto.

Yamato is a very discreet, cautious and generally well-prepared person. He projects a calm, stoic demeanour even in very stressful situations. He takes his missions very seriously, even insisting on being referred to by whatever his current codename is. In the anime, having no family nor memories of them, Yamato's greatest desire has become to work hard for the village to make others happy and to be acknowledged by all for his efforts, particularly as leader of Team 7, as revealed by Infinite Tsukuyomi.[^19] Yamato also takes great interest in [architecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Architecture "wikipedia:Architecture") which can be seen when he goes out on missions and creates [a house](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Release:_Four-Pillar_House_Technique "Wood Release: Four-Pillar House Technique") to "camp out" in. This skill to incorporate his hobby with his [kekkei genkai](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kekkei_genkai "Kekkei genkai") was also seen after [Pain's assault](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Pain%27s_assault "Pain's assault") on Konoha where he was key in [recreating](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Release:_Serial_Pillar_Houses_Technique "Wood Release: Serial Pillar Houses Technique") the village. While he has been good-natured so far, Yamato claims to be a different sort of leader than Kakashi, one who is not above "ruling by the use of terror" when his team misbehaves.[^20] Aside from his obviously intimidating skills as a ninja, however, this "terror" has so far primarily consisted of casting a creepy "ghoul-eyes" expression at his charges. [Naruto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto "Naruto"), especially, is easily terrified by it, and stated that it felt like Yamato was controlling him with fear — something Yamato himself has noted that he has no qualms with.[^21] [^22] His intimidation caused the typically informal Naruto to use polite language around him.[^23] He also has less tolerance of Naruto's antics and lack of respect for rules than Kakashi. He is, however, also prone to being a victim of a cruel joke when given misleading information, as witness his visible fear and subsequent annoyance when [Killer B](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Killer_B "Killer B") maliciously joked about the dangers of a [jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki "Jinchūriki") trying to control their [tailed beast](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_beast "Tailed beast").[^24] In the anime, he's shown to use his fear face in a more comical manner when he created a bridge for Naruto and the others and asked if Naruto was happy he came along.

Despite his serious and sometimes overworked demeanour, Yamato is known for being somewhat playful, such as his tendency play pranks on Naruto during their downtime on missions. He usually does this by using his ghoul-eyes' frightening appearance or a wood clone to scare Naruto during ghost stories, showing that he does have a sense of humour underneath all of that responsibility and seriousness. He is also aware of his tendency to be overworked by his peers and gets frustrated by their lack of appreciation of how exhausting his Wood Release techniques are despite their obvious usefulness and versatility.

Yamato is known to respect ranking, as he told Naruto, who was now the Seventh Hokage, to not call him "Captain Yamato" anymore as he doesn't feel comfortable for the Hokage calling him captain.

## Appearance

- ![Yamato's appearance prior to Part I.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/7/73/Young_ANBU_Yamato_OL.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/70?cb=20210423180409)
	Yamato's appearance prior to Part I.

1/3

Yamato has short brown hair and black, almond-shaped eyes. While still a child and test subject of Orochimaru, he had long, unkempt hair going past his shoulders.[^6] When he débuted, Yamato wore the standard attire of the Anbu: a black, sleeveless jumper under a grey flack jacket with strapped-up shinobi sandals, arm-length gloves with guards over them. He carried a [sword](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sword "Sword") strapped to his back, and wore a mask that resembles a cat's face covered in green, and red intricate markings, and the Anbu tattoo on his left shoulder.[^25] When he meets up with Naruto, Sakura, and Sai for the first time, however, he has changed into a variation of the standard attire of a Konoha-nin, with a [flak jacket](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Flak_jacket "Flak jacket").

The differences between his attire and that of the standard one is that his does not have the [Uzushiogakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uzushiogakure "Uzushiogakure") crest on the shoulders of the sleeves, and the neck of his navy-blue shirt is form fitting and goes up to his chin. He also still retains two hip pouches strapped to his lower back instead of just one. In addition to that, he has a [happuri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mengu_\(Japanese_facial_armour\)#Happuri "wikipedia:Mengu (Japanese facial armour)") -style [forehead protector](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Forehead_protector "Forehead protector") that frames his face, similar to that of [Tobirama Senju](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tobirama_Senju "Tobirama Senju").

Years later during the reign of the Seventh Hokage, Yamato's appearance remained largely the same except with minor noticeable changes: he developed small wrinkles under his eyes and at the corners of his mouth, his hair was shorter, spiky and his happuri-style forehead protector is devoid of any emblem and slightly cracked.

## Abilities

![Yamato's kekkei genkai: Wood Release.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/5/5e/Mokuton_no_Tenzo.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20170120065930)

Yamato's kekkei genkai: Wood Release.

In the anime, his talents were shown to be enough to join the Anbu while still a child.[^26] Due to his abilities and potential, Danzō took him on as his personal apprentice and trained Yamato himself.[^6] As a member of the Anbu, Yamato was a highly capable ninja, having one of — if not the finest service record of anyone working under the Third Hokage — leading the [Fifth Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fifth_Hokage "Fifth Hokage") to have the utmost confidence in him.[^16] Upon leaving the organisation, he was promoted to [jōnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/J%C5%8Dnin "Jōnin"), with his skills being both utilised to monitor Orochimaru, as well as being recognised to the point a [X Card](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Extreme:_Shinobi_Picture_Scrolls "Extreme: Shinobi Picture Scrolls") was made of him.[^27]

### Ninjutsu

#### Nature Transformation

![Yamato using the Wood Release: Wood Locking Wall.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/d/d5/Wood_element.PNG/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160407231752)

Yamato using the Wood Release: Wood Locking Wall.

Sharing the same DNA as [Hashirama Senju](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hashirama_Senju "Hashirama Senju"), Yamato possesses the First's signature [Wood Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Release "Wood Release") [kekkei genkai](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kekkei_genkai "Kekkei genkai"). This kekkei genkai allows him to combine [earth-natured](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Earth_Release "Earth Release") chakra in one hand, and [water](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water_Release "Water Release") in the other,[^28] to create vegetation such as trees and even grass which can be grown from any surface.[^29] These trees can be grown not only from the earth and pre-existing flora, but from his own body as well by using his own cells as a source of life. As this ability is not inherently his, however, Yamato has realised that he cannot use the Wood Release techniques to their full potential, which by his own admission results in the scale of his techniques paling in comparison to that of the First Hokage.[^30] Nonetheless, he has enough skill and experience with Wood Release to make it the main element of his [fighting style](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fighting_style "Fighting style"), showing considerable control and gaining fame as a capable user of it. In the anime, he was shown able to effectively use this ability while still a young child.[^26] Much of his techniques come from studying a scroll containing the Wood Release techniques of the First Hokage.[^6]

![Yamato using Wood Release: Great Forest Technique.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/1/1e/Great_Forest_Technique.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150312113626)

Yamato using Wood Release: Great Forest Technique.

Yamato can use Wood Release for both offensive and defensive purposes: he can surround himself with [extremely durable wooden domes](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Release:_Wood_Locking_Wall "Wood Release: Wood Locking Wall"), which he can also use to trap foes within,[^31] and in the anime, create a [wooden shield](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Release:_Threefolded_Wood_Shield "Wood Release: Threefolded Wood Shield") that can rebuild itself, and a [wall of tangled branches](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Release:_World_of_Trees_Wall "Wood Release: World of Trees Wall").[^32] He can also cause [trees](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Release:_Great_Forest_Technique "Wood Release: Great Forest Technique") to grow from his arm, which can be used to trap or impale an enemy, and create a [binding tree](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Release:_Tree_Bind_Flourishing_Burial "Wood Release: Tree Bind Flourishing Burial") around an enemy and subsequently crush them.[^33]

Yamato can also use Wood Release for other purposes, such as to [transform](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Release:_Transformation "Wood Release: Transformation") into someone, with the wood acting as an outer layer, allowing him to leave out the suit when needed. He is able to create a [wood-based clone](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Clone_Technique "Wood Clone Technique") that can be generated from his body or from the ground. As they share the same cells, he is able to track its location, communicate with it over long distances,[^34] and absorb any information it has gathered by laying his hand on it.[^35] Yamato can even turn a wood clone into a [seed](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Transmission_Wood "Transmission Wood") in order to track anyone he plants it on.[^36] He can generate wooden structures almost instantly, which he can use to create a [large cage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Release:_Four-Pillar_Prison_Technique "Wood Release: Four-Pillar Prison Technique") used to hold prisoners and intimidate unruly subordinates,[^37] and a [large house](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Release:_Four-Pillar_House_Technique "Wood Release: Four-Pillar House Technique") that can be erected at a moment's notice instead of setting up a camp.[^38]

Yamato is skilled in the use of Water and Earth Release as well, as seen when he [raised the ground](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Earth_Release:_Earth-Style_Rampart "Earth Release: Earth-Style Rampart") and created a [waterfall](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water_Release:_Waterfall_Basin_Technique "Water Release: Waterfall Basin Technique").[^39] He is also capable of utilising [Yang Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yang_Release "Yang Release").[^2]

#### Tailed Beast Suppression

![Yamato suppressing Naruto's version 2 form.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/5/56/Tailed_Beast_Control.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160129044413)

Yamato suppressing Naruto's version 2 form.

Using the [First Hokage's Necklace](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/First_Hokage%27s_Necklace "First Hokage's Necklace") to amplify his powers, Yamato can [suppress the power of a tailed beast](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage-Style_Sixty-Year-Old_Technique_%E2%80%94_Kakuan_Entering_Society_with_Bliss-Bringing_Hands "Hokage-Style Sixty-Year-Old Technique — Kakuan Entering Society with Bliss-Bringing Hands"). This legendary ability was one of the reasons that Hashirama Senju was made [Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage "Hokage") in the first place, and it was greatly coveted by Orochimaru.[^40] Although the First Hokage could suppress entire tailed beasts, Yamato has admitted to his skill not being as strong as the original's so it is unknown what the limits of his suppression ability are. He was able to suppress Naruto's four-tailed form in mere seconds.[^30] It was for this reason why Yamato was assigned to Team Kakashi in their leader's absence.[^41]

![Yamato being notified that Naruto manifested six tails.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/4/4c/Yamato_hand.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20170728105518)

Yamato being notified that Naruto manifested six tails.

Yamato added a [seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Sealing "Tailed Beast Sealing") to the Hokage's necklace that would activate and attempt to suppress him when he called upon too much of the Nine-Tails' chakra. He used a technique alongside this that would reveal a number on his palm corresponding to the tails Naruto had gained, warning him in the process. With the destruction of the necklace, however, Yamato implied that he could no longer help suppress tailed beasts.[^42] Despite this, during Naruto's [tailed-beast control](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Falls_of_Truth "Falls of Truth") training, when Naruto started losing control, Yamato tried to suppress the beast once again.[^43]

Yamato can also use his Wood Release to suppress the chakra of regular people, as seen when he [suppressed](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Release:_Stocks "Wood Release: Stocks") Kisame's chakra in order to subdue him after his capture.[^44]

### Other Skills

Yamato is highly proficient in multiple areas of combat, including tracking,[^45] trapping, stealth, and diversionary tactics. He has keen instincts in battle, allowing him to quickly react to threats. He can also [merge with the environment](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hiding_in_Surface_Technique "Hiding in Surface Technique") to spy undetected or evade most attacks. During his test of his [new team's](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Kakashi "Team Kakashi") skills, he demonstrated considerable ability in [taijutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Taijutsu "Taijutsu"), as shown when he simultaneously fended off Naruto and Sai.[^46] Thanks to his training as an Anbu, Yamato is proficient in [kenjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kenjutsu "Kenjutsu"), able to clash against his senior Kakashi.[^6] He also has some skill in [fūinjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/F%C5%ABinjutsu "Fūinjutsu"), as he was able to place a [seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Sealing "Tailed Beast Sealing") and rig it to activate under certain conditions. Having been genetically altered to match Hashirama's DNA, Yamato was also granted the legendary shinobi's ability to [heal nearly any wound](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Regeneration_Ability "Regeneration Ability"), without the need for hand seals.[^2]

Yamato is one of the Konoha ninja sent to an isolated [island](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Island_Turtle "Island Turtle") in the [Land of Lightning](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_Lightning "Land of Lightning") as Naruto's bodyguard. When Naruto expresses an interest in finally taming the Nine-Tails, Yamato and [Motoi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Motoi "Motoi") accompany him to the [Falls of Truth](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Falls_of_Truth "Falls of Truth") in order to defeat his [dark side](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Naruto "Dark Naruto"). After a brief encounter with the [Giant Squid](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Giant_Squid "Giant Squid") again, B instructs Naruto on how to enter into his subconscious and face the Nine-Tails. When the Nine-Tails starts gaining the advantage and a chakra cloak develops around Naruto, Yamato tries to suppress it. It eventually stops on its own, surprising him. Naruto soon informs them of his victory, explaining the particulars to B through a fist-bump. Being left out of the loop irritates Yamato, but he is left amazed when Naruto begins demonstrating his new control over the Nine-Tails and the advantages it allows him. Among the advantages is the ability to sense [Kisame](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kisame "Kisame") hidden within B's [Samehada](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Samehada "Samehada"). Naruto pursues Kisame when he tries to flee, but his inexperience with his new strength and speed causes him to lodge himself in the wall. Yamato helps Naruto while B goes after Kisame.

![Yamato restrains Kisame.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/a/a1/Wood_Release_stocks.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20151004044147)

Yamato restrains Kisame.

Yamato splints Naruto's sprained ankle and warns him to exercise restraint until he can gain better control over his powers. They rendezvous with B and find that Kisame has been captured. Yamato fortifies Kisame's wooden restraints while [Aoba Yamashiro](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Aoba_Yamashiro "Aoba Yamashiro") [reads his mind](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Psycho_Mind_Transmission "Psycho Mind Transmission"). Before Aoba can learn anything of real value about Akatsuki, Kisame breaks free and commits suicide. They try to examine the intel Kisame wanted to escape with, but it is booby-trapped and they are trapped in [water prisons](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water_Prison_Technique "Water Prison Technique") while the intel makes its way to Akatsuki.

![Yamato being used to strengthen the White Zetsu Army.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/c/c5/Yamato_fused_with_Hashirama%27s_living_clone.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20120329164226)

Yamato being used to strengthen the White Zetsu Army.

Fearing that Naruto may grow suspicious about their reasons for being on the island given his dominance over the Nine-Tails and the incident with Kisame, Yamato and the others convince him to take a survey of the island's native animal population to keep him busy. Akatsuki, meanwhile, launches an assault on the island, and Yamato looks after Naruto and formulates explanations for any signs of the battle that Naruto notices. When things calm down, Yamato, Aoba, and [Motoi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Motoi "Motoi") go outside to investigate. They come across Kabuto as he is captured by [Kurotsuchi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kurotsuchi "Kurotsuchi"). Yamato creates a wooden beam that Aoba can walk on to approach Kabuto and read his mind like he did with Kisame, but Kabuto sheds his body to free himself. He goes after Yamato and, before being captured, Yamato pushes Motoi out of the way.

Kabuto escapes to the [Mountains' Graveyard](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mountains%27_Graveyard "Mountains' Graveyard") where he and [Obito](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Obito_Uchiha "Obito Uchiha") gather information from Yamato about the [Allied Shinobi Forces](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Allied_Shinobi_Forces "Allied Shinobi Forces"). Kabuto also studies Hashirama's DNA within Yamato to strengthen the [White Zetsu Army](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/White_Zetsu_Army "White Zetsu Army"). Rather than kill Yamato when he has fulfilled his usefulness, they suspend him opposite [Hashirama's clone](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hashirama%27s_Artificial_Body "Hashirama's Artificial Body") for future use.

## Blank Period

Following Orochimaru being pardoned for his crimes, Yamato was assigned alongside a surveillance team to keep an eye on him in case he tried to act against the Five Great Nations. During such, he was ordered to not make direct contact with Orochimaru until such an incident occurred. Over time, Yamato became famous among his team as the only one who could directly approach Orochimaru without fear.[^47]

## New Era

### Sarada Uchiha Arc

Main article: [Naruto Gaiden: The Seventh Hokage and the Scarlet Spring](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Gaiden:_The_Seventh_Hokage_and_the_Scarlet_Spring "Naruto Gaiden: The Seventh Hokage and the Scarlet Spring") Yamato was mentioned by Kakashi during his phone discussion with Naruto, regarding the message sent by one of Sasuke's [hawks](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hawk "Hawk"). Kakashi noted that he will send Yamato to meet Sasuke and Naruto, so that he could help out in the search of the mysterious [boy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shin_Uchiha_\(Clones\) "Shin Uchiha (Clones)") with the Sharingan, who had attempted to kill Sasuke. Later, Yamato joined up with Naruto, Sasuke, and Sasuke's daughter, [Sarada Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sarada_Uchiha "Sarada Uchiha") after Sakura was kidnapped. Deciding to speak with Orochimaru since he seemingly had ties to [Shin Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shin_Uchiha "Shin Uchiha"), they went to one of his bases. Yamato decided to stay outside and wait in case anything happened.

### Graduation Exams Arc

Main article: [Graduation Exams Arc](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Graduation_Exams_Arc "Graduation Exams Arc") As ninja classes began preparing for the graduation exams, Yamato continued his assignment of watching Orochimaru's movements. As such, he followed the Sannin on his journey back to Konohagakure to discussing matters with Naruto about his research on Danzō's old experiments. After Orochimaru delivered promising results to aid Sasuke in his own investigations, Orochimaru went off to meet with the Academy instructors about Mitsuki's performance, which Yamato followed.

### Mitsuki's Disappearance Arc

Main article: [Mitsuki's Disappearance Arc](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mitsuki%27s_Disappearance_Arc "Mitsuki's Disappearance Arc") In the anime, when learning of [Mitsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mitsuki "Mitsuki") 's apparent betrayal and desertion of Konohagakure, Yamato spoke with Orochimaru. While the Sannin insisted he was completely oblivious to his son's actions, Yamato suspected that Orochimaru knew something. Afterwards, Yamato spotted Mitsuki and pursued him, unaware it was actually Sarada in disguise. Yamato's team soon cornered the target and captured the genin in a barrier, but suffered interference from [Karin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Karin "Karin"), who then took the genin's place by disguising herself. After long chase, Karin revealed herself before escaping in a smokescreen. Now more suspicious than ever, Yamato decided to have him team search Orochimaru's base, who had no objections.

## Trivia

- Yamato is an ancient name of Japan, and is also the name of a [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II "wikipedia:World War II") era [Japanese battleship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_battleship_Yamato "wikipedia:Japanese battleship Yamato"). Tenzō (天造) means "heavenly creation" and refers to anything created by nature, rather than by humans.
	- He could also be named after [Yamato Takeru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yamato_Takeru "wikipedia:Yamato Takeru"), who was a Japanese legendary prince of the Yamato dynasty and was believed to have been the first ninja.
- "Kinoe" (甲) is the first sign of the Chinese calendar.
- Studio Pierrots Settei sheets of Yamato show that he was 140 cm as a 10 year old Anbu and 166 cm as a 14 year old Anbu.
- According to the databook(s):
	- Yamato's favourite foods are walnuts, while his least favourites are any food that is oily.
		- His hobby is reading books about architecture.
		- Yamato wishes to fight [Kakashi Hatake](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi_Hatake "Kakashi Hatake").
		- Yamato has completed 996 official missions in total: 80 D-rank, 176 C-rank, 400 B-rank, 305 A-rank, 35 S-rank.
		- Yamato's favourite phrase is "Knocking on a stone bridge before crossing it" (石橋を叩いて渡る, *Ishibashi o tataite wataru*).
- In the anime, it is revealed that Yamato suffers from motion sickness while in enclosed spaces.[^48]
- Yamato is often affected by running gags in the series:
	- The prime running gag is that people overwork him because of his wood abilities. Best seen when he rebuilt some of Konoha and everyone wanted him to do more despite being out of energy after making a street of houses.
		- A running gag in [two](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Omake#Sleep,_Part_1 "Omake") [omakes](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Omake#Sleep,_Part_2 "Omake") is that whenever his teammates stop for the night, they constantly attack Yamato while still asleep. He was almost hit by Naruto's weapons and a [Rasengan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rasengan "Rasengan"), [Kiba](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kiba "Kiba") 's [Fang Passing Fang](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fang_Passing_Fang "Fang Passing Fang") and even [Shino](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shino "Shino") 's [kikaichū](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kikaich%C5%AB "Kikaichū") swarm. Such assaults always force him to use [Wood Release: Wood Locking Wall](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Release:_Wood_Locking_Wall "Wood Release: Wood Locking Wall") to protect himself and getting little to no sleep, making him look terrible in the morning.
		- He and [Orochimaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Orochimaru "Orochimaru") had a running gag during [Naruto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto "Naruto") & [Hinata](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hinata "Hinata") 's wedding with him following Orochimaru, one example is him hiding in a barrel while Orochimaru was on a boat.
- In the *Naruto* character popularity polls, Yamato was ranked as the 14th most popular character in the 7th.
	- In the same poll, his [forehead protector](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Forehead_protector "Forehead protector") ended in 76th place with 6 votes.
- In *[Naruto SD: Rock Lee no Seishun Full-Power Ninden](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_SD:_Rock_Lee_no_Seishun_Full-Power_Ninden "Naruto SD: Rock Lee no Seishun Full-Power Ninden")*, Yamato has a non speaking role in the entire spin-off and only makes cameo appearances.
