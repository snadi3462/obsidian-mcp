This article is about the character Madara Uchiha. For the character who used Madara's name, see [Obito Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Obito_Uchiha "Obito Uchiha"). For other uses, see [Madara Uchiha (disambiguation)](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Madara_Uchiha_\(disambiguation\) "Madara Uchiha (disambiguation)").

**Madara Uchiha** (うちはマダラ, *Uchiha Madara*) was the legendary leader of the [Uchiha Clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uchiha_Clan "Uchiha Clan") and a [reincarnation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Reincarnation "Reincarnation") and descendant of [Indra Ōtsutsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Indra_%C5%8Ctsutsuki "Indra Ōtsutsuki"). He founded [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure") alongside his childhood friend and rival, [Hashirama Senju](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hashirama_Senju "Hashirama Senju"), with the intention of bringing about an era of peace. When the two couldn't agree on how to achieve that peace, they fought for control of the village, a conflict which ended in Madara's death. Madara, however, [rewrote](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Transcription_Seal:_Izanagi "Transcription Seal: Izanagi") his death and went into hiding to work on his own [plans](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eye_of_the_Moon_Plan "Eye of the Moon Plan"). Unable to complete it in his natural life, he entrusted his knowledge and plans to [Obito Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Obito_Uchiha "Obito Uchiha") shortly before his actual death. Years later, Madara was [revived](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Outer_Path_%E2%80%94_Samsara_of_Heavenly_Life_Technique "Outer Path — Samsara of Heavenly Life Technique") during the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War") with his goal involving the [Infinite Tsukuyomi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Infinite_Tsukuyomi "Infinite Tsukuyomi") having reached fruition, only to be upended by [Black Zetsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Zetsu "Black Zetsu") and its own ancient machinations. Finally, realising the error of his ways, he made amends with Hashirama before his final death.

## Background

![Madara as a child.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/3/39/Madara_apperance.PNG/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140703221854)

Madara as a child.

Madara was born during the [Warring States Period](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Warring_States_Period "Warring States Period"), and was the eldest of [Tajima Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tajima_Uchiha "Tajima Uchiha") 's five children.[^6] Madara and his siblings grew up on the battlefield waging constant war with the Uchiha's rivals: the [Senju](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Senju "Senju"). Three of his siblings died young, leaving Madara with only his younger brother, [Izuna](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Izuna "Izuna"). Madara and Izuna became very close through their shared loss and constantly competed with each other to get stronger.[^7] This, combined with his naturally strong chakra, enabled the young Madara to defeat adult Senju in battle and develop a reputation as a genius.[^8]

During his infrequent downtime, Madara met a boy his own age named Hashirama. The two quickly developed a friendly rivalry, be it skipping stones or urinating in rivers. Like Madara, Hashirama was also a shinobi who had lost his brothers on the battlefield. Together they imagined a world where children like themselves wouldn't need to fight.[^9] As a precaution, Madara and Hashirama did not share their family names, but nevertheless discovered each other's identities: Madara was an Uchiha, Hashirama was a Senju; it was their duty to kill each other, even if they were friends. Needing to choose between his family and his dreams of peace, Madara chose to end his friendship with Hashirama so he would have no reservations over killing him in the future, a resolve strong enough to awaken his [Sharingan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sharingan "Sharingan").[^10]

Over the following years Madara and Hashirama continued to meet in combat. Madara could never defeat Hashirama — even after acquiring a [Mangekyō Sharingan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mangeky%C5%8D_Sharingan "Mangekyō Sharingan") — and Hashirama could never bring himself to kill someone he still considered a friend, resulting in a constant stalemate between the two that lasted decades. In time, both Madara and Hashirama became leaders of their respective clans, a position Hashirama tried to use to broker peace between them.[^11] Although some Uchiha found the offer increasingly tempting, Madara refused due to Izuna's death at the hands of Hashirama's own brother, [Tobirama Senju](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tobirama_Senju "Tobirama Senju"). Despite this, some Uchiha defected over to the Senju clan out of self-preservation. Madara then used Izuna's eyes in order to gain "eternal" Mangekyō Sharingan and restore his deteriorating vision. With this new power he waged one final assault against the Senju and was summarily defeated. Rather than killing Madara to bring the era of war to an end, Hashirama offered to kill himself if it would stop the fighting. Madara was moved by Hashirama's gesture and finally assented to peace.[^12]

The Senju, the Uchiha, and all their affiliated clans came together to found a village of peace, where children would never need to die in battle. Madara and Hashirama rekindled their childhood friendship, and Madara named that village [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure"), seeing it through leaves.[^13] But Madara's idea of peace differed from Hashirama's: where Hashirama envisioned cooperation with the other newly-formed villages, Madara desired control so the peace could never be lost, evidenced by his attacking [Iwagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Iwagakure "Iwagakure") 's [Mū](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/M%C5%AB "Mū") and [Ōnoki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/%C5%8Cnoki "Ōnoki") so that they would submit to Konoha's authority.[^14] [^15] When Hashirama was elected as [Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage "Hokage"), Konoha's leader, Madara also became concerned for the Uchiha's future, believing this to be but the first step in the Senju's dominance.[^16]

The [Stone Tablet](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Stone_Tablet "Stone Tablet") [Zetsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Zetsu "Black Zetsu") altered [^17] had been in the Uchiha's possession for generations and was brought with them when they settled in Konoha. Through careful study, Madara was able to decipher enough of it to learn of the history of shinobi: of the endless cycle of failed peace and the destiny of battle between Uchiha and Senju, but also a means of unity for the world.[^18] With this knowledge, Madara decided Konoha was a failed experiment. He tried to convince his own clan and even Hashirama of the same conclusion, but none would hear him.[^19] Madara chose to abandon the village, attacking Konoha on multiple occasions,[^20] later returning with the [Nine-Tailed Demon Fox](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tailed_Demon_Fox "Nine-Tailed Demon Fox") under his control to challenge Hashirama.[^21] They fought to exhaustion, and from the carnage of their battle the [Valley of the End](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Valley_of_the_End "Valley of the End") was formed.[^22] In the end, Madara was killed by Hashirama.[^23]

![An elderly Madara awakens the Rinnegan.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/7/77/Madara_Awakening_Rinnegan.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150730120026)

An elderly Madara awakens the Rinnegan.

News of Madara's death spread fast and his corpse was secretly hidden to keep anyone from finding it and profiting from it. But Madara had planned ahead: he had scheduled an [Izanagi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Transcription_Seal:_Izanagi "Transcription Seal: Izanagi") to activate sometime after his death, changing reality to bring him back to life in exchange for his right eye's vision. Meanwhile, Black Zetsu had spent an unspecific length of time hiding itself inside of Madara's body, allowing it to later deceive Madara into thinking he created it. After being revived, he left a [copy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_Clone_Technique "Shadow Clone Technique") in place of his real body and went into hiding with a special trophy from his fight with Hashirama: a mouthful of Hashirama's flesh that he transplanted into his wounds. It was not until decades later, towards the end of Madara's natural life, that the cells had any effect, awakening the [Rinnegan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rinnegan "Rinnegan") (in the process restoring his right eye). With the Rinnegan, he was able to summon the [Demonic Statue of the Outer Path](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Statue_of_the_Outer_Path "Demonic Statue of the Outer Path"), which he used to cultivate a [mindless living clone of Hashirama](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hashirama%27s_Living_Clone "Hashirama's Living Clone") from which he believed he had produced a [White Zetsu Army](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/White_Zetsu_Army "White Zetsu Army").[^24]

Over the years, Madara had perfected his plans for peace in what he called the Eye of the Moon Plan. But as his years waned, Madara knew he couldn't complete his plans in the time he had left. So he transplanted his Rinnegan into a young [Nagato](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nagato "Nagato") without the boy's knowing,[^24] intending Nagato to someday use the eyes to [restore](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Outer_Path_%E2%80%94_Samsara_of_Heavenly_Life_Technique "Outer Path — Samsara of Heavenly Life Technique") Madara to life. If Nagato was to do this, however, Madara would need an agent to act on his behalf and guide Nagato towards this ultimate goal. Madara waited, connecting himself to Demonic Statue to keep him alive until someone could be found.[^25] Madara spent this time keeping a close eye on Konoha to find a suitable pawn to take his place.

![Madara meeting Obito Uchiha.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/a/a7/Madara_saves_Obito.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20250729153607)

Madara meeting Obito Uchiha.

During the [Third Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Shinobi_World_War "Third Shinobi World War"), Madara found a badly injured [Obito](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Obito_Uchiha "Obito Uchiha"). Madara used Hashirama's cells to replace Obito's damaged extremities and placed a [Forbidden Individual Curse Tag](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Forbidden_Individual_Curse_Tag "Forbidden Individual Curse Tag") in Obito's heart as a fail-safe in case Obito ever turned against him.[^26] Until then, Madara began working towards corrupting Obito. He had [Kirigakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kirigakure "Kirigakure") kidnap the girl Obito loved, [Rin Nohara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rin_Nohara "Rin Nohara"), and seal the [Three-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Three-Tails "Three-Tails") into her. He then manipulated events so that Rin would die at the hand of Obito's friend, [Kakashi Hatake](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi_Hatake "Kakashi Hatake"), while Obito watched.[^27] Driven to despair, Obito offered his services to Madara. Madara divulged to Obito the history of the [Sage of the Six Paths](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sage_of_the_Six_Paths "Sage of the Six Paths") and the [Ten-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ten-Tails "Ten-Tails"), the details of his Eye of the Moon Plan, and various techniques that Obito would need moving forward. As a final act, he left behind [Black Zetsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Zetsu "Black Zetsu") — what he believed to be [a manifestation of his will](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Will_Materialisation "Will Materialisation") — to provide additional guidance to Obito in pursuit of this goal. With that, Madara disconnected from the Demonic Statue and, with his dying breath, entrusted Obito with his name: Madara Uchiha.[^28]

## Personality

![The childhood dynamics between Madara and Hashirama.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/6/6a/Madara%27s_rage.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140703221741)

The childhood dynamics between Madara and Hashirama.

Madara's childhood was a product of the times he lived in: the constant fighting made him a perfectionist that would keep at something until he mastered it, and his triumphs on the battlefield made him very confident in his abilities and talent.[^29] Whenever one of these personality traits was challenged, as they tended to be by Hashirama, Madara's competitive streak would emerge. Madara did not mind Hashirama's influence in this regard; he believed that the only way to survive in the shinobi world was to make allies with one's enemies.[^30] His exposure to Hashirama is credited for Madara not experiencing the Uchiha's " [Curse of Hatred](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Curse_of_Hatred "Curse of Hatred") " or at the very least, not being victimised by it as much as Izuna was and even though he claimed to have abandoned his friendship with Hashirama, Madara still held onto their friendship subconsciously.[^31] Madara's actions and beliefs were singularly focused on protecting Izuna, his only surviving brother.[^32] So long as he had Izuna, Madara was reasonable and kind, willing to make concessions for a perceived greater good.[^33]

![Madara's descent into darkness.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/9/95/Hont%C5%8D_no_Yume.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140724122152)

Madara's descent into darkness.

Madara changed after Izuna's death, becoming bitter towards the Senju, particularly Hashirama since he still had a brother. Embracing his clan's Curse of Hatred, he opted to die for revenge rather than compromise or forgive.[^34] Although he was briefly convinced to set aside his grief and try to replace Izuna with the collective family of Konoha, he could never shake the feeling that he was betraying Izuna's memory. The increasing isolation of himself and other Uchiha from village politics ultimately convinced him of this, causing him to fully break with any other attachments.

During his time as a Konoha shinobi, Madara did what he thought was in the village's best interests. Unlike Hashirama's compassionate methods, Madara took a merciless approach: the shinobi of Iwagakure only had purpose so long as they swore unwavering allegiance to Konoha.[^14] Since their methods were so different, Madara hated to hear Hashirama's name during diplomatic discussions.[^14] [^35] After his defection from Konoha, Madara's priorities became centred around the Eye of the Moon Plan, manipulating countless others in order to satisfy his own goals and putting in place multiple layers of contingencies so that nobody could ever diverge from his own intentions. Despite this, Madara genuinely believed this plan would in fact, benefit the world and simply followed an " [ends justifies the means](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/en:Consequentialism "wikipedia:en:Consequentialism") " principle.

As he valued only power and possessed so much of it, Madara therefore disliked to waste it on unworthy causes or unchallenging opponents, claiming disgust when he was forced to.[^36]

Madara loved a good fight above all else: the sights, the sounds, even the taste of his own blood, the latter implying a certain masochistic nature. He would occasionally describe his battles as "dances";[^37] he felt being [reincarnated](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning:_Impure_World_Reincarnation "Summoning: Impure World Reincarnation") deprived him of the full experience. Yet he was very disciplined in a fight, never allowing his failed plans or attacks to upset him, never letting superior numbers or power intimidate him, and always willing to do whatever must be done to gain victory. Although he sometimes imposed limitations on himself, such as not using certain techniques more than once, he was willing to "lower" himself with unbecoming tactics or excessive displays in order to change tactics and exploit advantages. He was perfectly aware of his talents and did not feign modesty, bluntly stating when he was stronger than his opponents and belittling them when they continued to defy him. Conversely, if he was proven wrong or somebody posed a legitimate challenge to him, he would admit it, and apologise for previous remarks if necessary. In addition, if such an opponent is about to die, he would show them the ultimate respect by taking the time and effort to finish them off himself, even if said person is going to die on their own.[^38] Stemming from this, he feels insulted if he knows an opponent isn't using their full power against him, even if he knows it will result in their death.[^39] He also held the Senju to a higher standard in terms of power due to his encounters with Hashirama, stating a weak Senju disgusts him more than a weak shinobi of any kind.

![Madara's reaction after sensing Hashirama's chakra.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/4/45/Madara_senses_Hashirama.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140807121556)

Madara's reaction after sensing Hashirama's chakra.

One of the exceptions Madara makes was Hashirama. Their years of rivalry left Madara with competing feelings of respect and resentment for Hashirama. Madara considered Hashirama the only opponent worthy of his time and would postpone his own plans if it meant prolonging his time to fight with Hashirama without issue.[^40] Despite this, Madara is quite open-minded about recognising other strong individuals besides Hashirama, as when he declares [Might Guy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Might_Guy "Might Guy") as the strongest taijutsu user he's ever faced.[^41]

Despite his prowess and intellect, Madara is not without moments of recklessness. When he was brought back to life, he showed no hesitation in absorbing Hashirama's senjutsu chakra while admitting he expected to have some difficulty controlling it due to having no prior training.

![In his final moments, Madara was finally able to break free from the Curse of Hatred.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/c/ca/Madara_free.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20251012203219)

In his final moments, Madara was finally able to break free from the Curse of Hatred.

In the later years of his developing the [Eye of the Moon Plan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eye_of_the_Moon_Plan "Eye of the Moon Plan"), Madara became pessimistic about human nature, believing the cycle of battle to be inescapable.[^42] He also came to believe that humanity and the world are incapable of changing from what they were in the past.[^43] He believed the current, "worthless" reality was built too much on the idea of winning and losing. For this reason he was deeply committed to his plan, so much that he would prematurely end a fight he was enjoying or kill any threat, even his [own](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Obito_Uchiha "Obito Uchiha") [clansmen](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Uchiha "Sasuke Uchiha"), for the sake of its success. Despite this, Madara was simply acting on what he believed would benefit humanity as a whole, showing his time in Konoha did indeed influence his actions and he wasn't acting simply out of bloodlust after Izuna's death. His plan's ultimate failure made him see its errors: breaking free of his Curse of Hatred, and admitted the superiority of Hashirama's methods for peace. He and Hashirama were thus able to reconcile their friendship in the moments just before his death.

## Appearance

- ![Madara as a child.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/06/Kid_Madara.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/148?cb=20230320174531)
	Madara as a child.

1/4

Madara was a fair-skinned man with spiky, black hair that had a slight blue tint to it. In his childhood, his hair was chin-length that parted to frame the sides of his face. His casual attire consisted of a blue long sleeve shirt and white pants. While on the battlefield he wore the standard Uchiha outfit: a black shirt with a high-collar left slightly open and the clan's crest on its back, with blue pants and bandages around his shins. Around his waist he had a white sash that held a sack, presumably containing his ninja tools.[^7] Later, his sash was replaced by a belt, adorned with pouches and various weapons. He also wore another sash strapped across his shoulders which he used to hold his swords.[^44]

As an adult, Madara is fairly tall and fit while his hair grew to waist-length with shoulder-length bangs framing the sides of his face, covering most of his right eye. He has several scars throughout his body.[^45] [^46] Although still relatively young, more prominent creases had developed under each of his eyes. At times, he wore crimson armour with numerous metal plates, forming protective guards along his chest, waist, shoulders and thighs. Most of the time, however, he continued wearing the same basic Uchiha outfit: a blue high-collared, long sleeve mantle that splits down the lower half and a simple, light-brown obi and a belt.[^47] During battle, he often carried an orange-brown [gunbai](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gunbai "Gunbai") which had a long black chain running up it. At one point, he wore a Konohagakure [forehead protector](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Forehead_Protector "Forehead Protector").[^48] The anime also shows him wearing a metal belt, as well as a brown leather sash to hold some of his other weapons, and a pair of belt sashes to hold two of his swords.

![Madara in his old age.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/9/94/Madara_Elder.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20251012203503)

Madara in his old age.

In his old age, Madara became extremely worn and emaciated, with his hair turned thin and white, and lost all his teeth. He wore a simple black outfit and used his [kama](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kama "Kama") as a makeshift cane.[^49] [^50] When Madara was [reincarnated](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning:_Impure_World_Reincarnation "Summoning: Impure World Reincarnation") his body's youth was restored, albeit with the generic effect of gaining grey sclerae and minor cracks all over his body. These disappear once he is properly [revived](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Outer_Path_%E2%80%94_Samsara_of_Heavenly_Life_Technique "Outer Path — Samsara of Heavenly Life Technique").

After becoming the [Ten-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ten-Tails "Ten-Tails") ' [jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki "Jinchūriki"), Madara gains the appearance of an [Ōtsutsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/%C5%8Ctsutsuki "Ōtsutsuki"); having taupe hair (white in the anime), pale grey skin (pale mint in the anime), and he became enveloped by a cloak of chakra that manifests into physical clothing: a full-bodied black garment with black pants, gloves, and boots, over which he wore a flowing white robe with six black magatama markings across his chest and the familiar pattern of a black [Rinnegan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rinnegan "Rinnegan") and nine black magatama markings in rows of three on his back. A pale grey horn-like protrusions grew out of his left temple and overlapped his forehead akin to a [protector](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Forehead_Protector "Forehead Protector") with an upward-curve on the right temple, giving him an overall appearance very similar to that of the [Sage of the Six Paths](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sage_of_the_Six_Paths "Sage of the Six Paths"). After absorbing the recreated Ten-Tails produced by Obito, Madara obtained additional black magatama markings around his sleeve cuffs and the rim of his robe. He reverts to his former appearance after the Ten-Tails is removed from his body.[^51]

## Abilities

![Madara's power being compared to that of a god.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/2e/Tengai_Shinsei.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130725115020)

Madara's power being compared to that of a god.

Madara was one of the most powerful shinobi in history, recognised as the strongest [Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uchiha "Uchiha") ever produced even decades after his death,[^52] [^53] and considered potentially the strongest shinobi ever when he was fully revived.[^54] A child prodigy, he killed several adult [Senju](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Senju "Senju") before he had awakened his [Sharingan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sharingan "Sharingan").[^55] He was the only one able to push [Hashirama Senju](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hashirama_Senju "Hashirama Senju"), a " [God of Shinobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/God_of_Shinobi "God of Shinobi") ", to his absolute limits.[^56] [^57] When [Obito](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Obito "Obito") operated under Madara's name, fear of Madara's power forced the [Five Great Shinobi Countries](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Five_Great_Shinobi_Countries "Five Great Shinobi Countries"), and the neutral [Land of Iron](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_Iron "Land of Iron"), to band together, as it was noted Madara's name itself was power.[^58] Madara was able to singlehandedly, respectively, defeat an army of thousands, all five [Kage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kage "Kage") [^59] and all nine [tailed beasts](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_beasts "Tailed beasts").[^60] He was also confident he could overpower Obito - then the Ten-Tails' jinchūriki - something it took the combined [Allied Shinobi Forces](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Allied_Shinobi_Forces "Allied Shinobi Forces") to accomplish.[^61]

### Chakra and Physical Prowess

![Madara absorbing a restrained Hashirama's senjutsu chakra.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/a/aa/Madara_captures_Hashirama.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20141211124430)

Madara absorbing a restrained Hashirama's senjutsu chakra.

Even by Uchiha standards, Madara was born with very powerful chakra,[^44] described as very foul and evil.[^62] [^63] His large chakra reserves allowed him to fight nonstop for 24 hours before collapsing,[^64] and in his old age, summon the [Demonic Statue of the Outer Path](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Statue_of_the_Outer_Path "Demonic Statue of the Outer Path") from its [cage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Six_Paths_%E2%80%94_Chibaku_Tensei "Six Paths — Chibaku Tensei") in the [moon](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Moon "Moon").[^65] His chakra control could perform complicated techniques with a single [hand seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hand_seal "Hand seal"). He could also quickly [absorb](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_Absorption_Techniques "Chakra Absorption Techniques") and synchronise [senjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Senjutsu "Senjutsu") [chakra](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra "Chakra") with his own and enter Sage Mode, despite having no prior training in senjutsu.[^66] Even when distributing his chakra among 25 wood clones, each still had enough power to threaten the Five Kage.

Madara was incredibly skilled in [taijutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Taijutsu "Taijutsu"). Against the [Fourth Division](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Division "Fourth Division"), he calmly defeated thousands of opponents with physical combat, showing exceptional agility, reflexes, and coordination to disarm, dodge, and counter attacks from many directions at once. He could strike with precision before his opponent could react, and he could overpower individuals twice his size.[^67] Madara demonstrated exceptional reactions, strength, and durability during the Fourth Great Ninja War. While still an Edo Tensei, he reacted to and withstood a kick from Tsunade, who was [teleported at lightspeed to the battlefield](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Heavenly_Transfer_Technique "Heavenly Transfer Technique"), landing on his feet unharmed.[^68] He also blocked an attack from the Fourth Raikage, displaying both reaction speed and durability against one of the fastest shinobi alive.[^69]

Madara withstood a massive Rasenshuriken from Sage Mode Naruto, confirming his ability to endure high-level chakra-based attacks.[^70] After being fully revived and regaining his past lifetime’s strength and physicality, as noted by Hashirama, He obliterated the legendary Hashirama's technique, [Sage Art: Gate of the Great God](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sage_Art:_Gate_of_the_Great_God "Sage Art: Gate of the Great God"), and destroyed [Sai’s sealing jutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sealing_Technique:_Tiger_Vision_Staring_Bullet "Sealing Technique: Tiger Vision Staring Bullet").[^71] Madara forced Sage Mode Naruto to block his attack, overpowering him and sending him flying several meters.[^72]

### Ninjutsu

![Madara fighting while blind.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/6/62/Madara_fighting_blind.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20251012204800)

Madara fighting while blind.

Madara was well-versed in a variety of [ninjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ninjutsu "Ninjutsu") styles: he could use [juinjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Juinjutsu "Juinjutsu") powerful enough to [restrict](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Forbidden_Individual_Curse_Tag "Forbidden Individual Curse Tag") the target's actions,[^77] [hide within surfaces](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hiding_in_Surface_Technique "Hiding in Surface Technique") to avoid damage and traverse the battlefield unnoticed,[^78] and through an unorthodox use of the [Shadow Clone Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_Clone_Technique "Shadow Clone Technique"), fake his death by having a clone act as his corpse.[^79] In the anime, Madara showed the ability to levitate.[^80]

Madara was an adept [sensor](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sensor "Sensor"), being able to [detect](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sensing_Technique "Sensing Technique") others' chakra signature countries away,[^35] determine a person's [clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Clan "Clan") and the nature of their [kekkei genkai](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kekkei_genkai "Kekkei genkai"),[^81] and even differentiate species.[^82] When amplified by Sage Mode, he could even fight whilst blind by locating targets and dodging attacks through his sensory perception,[^83] even avoiding a [Flying Thunder God Slash](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Flying_Thunder_God_Slash "Flying Thunder God Slash").[^84] Also with Sage Mode, Madara's pain tolerance was high, as he neither flinched from [Sasuke](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke "Sasuke") stabbing his right arm, the tailed beasts' assault, nor from the loss of his arm.[^85]

#### Nature Transformation

After transplanting Hashirama's DNA, which was later augmented further by Kabuto, Madara could use [Wood Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Release "Wood Release"), a mixture of water and [earth](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Earth "Earth") -natured chakra, with his skill and power comparable to Hashirama himself.[^97] He could create [gigantic flowering trees](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Release:_Advent_of_a_World_of_Flowering_Trees "Wood Release: Advent of a World of Flowering Trees") to render foes unconscious,[^98] [wood clone](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_clone "Wood clone"),[^99] and [wood dragons](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_dragon "Wood dragon") which could bind targets and drain their chakra.[^100] With Hashirama's cells, Madara could also produce roots to connect to and gain [limited control](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Control "Tailed Beast Control") of the Ten-Tails.[^101] After becoming the Ten-Tails' jinchūriki, he could use [Yin Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yin_Release "Yin Release") to create [lightning bolts](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sage_Art:_Yin_Release_Lightning_Dispatch "Sage Art: Yin Release Lightning Dispatch"), and [Storm Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Storm_Release "Storm Release") – a mixture of water and [lightning](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning "Lightning") -natured chakra – to create [energy beams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sage_Art:_Storm_Release_Light_Fang "Sage Art: Storm Release Light Fang") that could cut through the Six Paths Rods.[^102]

Madara's Sharingan.

Madara's mastery over the Sharingan surpassed every other member of the Uchiha clan in his natural lifetime. Only he could tell the difference between a shadow or wood clone, and the original.[^103] With brief eye contact, Madara could place targets under [genjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genjutsu:_Sharingan "Genjutsu: Sharingan") and paralyse them or relay information.[^104] He could even trap the Nine-Tails, and [summon](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning_Technique "Summoning Technique") it as a powerful tool in battle. By [programming](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Transcription_Seal:_Izanagi "Transcription Seal: Izanagi") one of his eyes to activate [Izanagi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Izanagi "Izanagi") after his death, he was able to alter reality and resurrect himself.[^79]

Though brief, Madara's use of the eye was notable on account of being able to use Kamui while the Ten-Tails' jinchūriki, something Obito himself couldn't do.

#### Rinnegan

![Madara's Rinnegan.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/01/Madara%27s_Rinnegan_.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20141026151343)

Madara's Rinnegan.

Years after infusing himself with Hashirama's DNA and unknowingly mixing Indra's and Asura's chakra, Madara's Sharingan evolved into the [Rinnegan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rinnegan "Rinnegan").[^24] As the eyes' original owner, only he could use them to their maximum power.[^117] He was able to switch between both dōjutsu. With the Rinnegan, Madara could use all of the abilities of the [Six Paths Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Six_Paths_Technique "Six Paths Technique"),[^118] such as the [Preta Path](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Preta_Path "Preta Path") to [absorb](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Blocking_Technique_Absorption_Seal "Blocking Technique Absorption Seal") chakra [^119] and the [Deva Path](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Deva_Path "Deva Path") to perform [Chibaku Tensei](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chibaku_Tensei "Chibaku Tensei").[^120] He could also use the [Outer Path](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Outer_Path "Outer Path") to create [chakra chains](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Statue_Chains "Demonic Statue Chains") that could restrain all nine [tailed beasts](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_beasts "Tailed beasts") [^121] and [black receivers](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Black_receiver "Black receiver") for various melee and supplementary purposes. Through the usage of his Rinnegan with that of his [Sharingan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sharingan "Sharingan"), Madara could summon [meteorites](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tengai_Shinsei "Tengai Shinsei") to cause widespread destruction.[^122]

While under the [Impure World Reincarnation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Impure_World_Reincarnation "Impure World Reincarnation"), Madara was unable to use the full extent of his Rinnegan's abilities. After being revived and obtaining one of his actual eyes, he could [summon and control](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning:_Demonic_Statue_of_the_Outer_Path "Summoning: Demonic Statue of the Outer Path") the [Demonic Statue of the Outer Path](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Statue_of_the_Outer_Path "Demonic Statue of the Outer Path"),[^123] [seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Six_Paths_Ten-Tails_Coffin_Seal "Six Paths Ten-Tails Coffin Seal") the [Ten-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ten-Tails "Ten-Tails") inside himself,[^124] and generate a [corporeal invisible shadow](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Limbo:_Border_Jail "Limbo: Border Jail") to aid him in battle. While Madara was in Sage Mode and further boosted by Kurama's chakra, this shadow was powerful enough to knock down the [tailed beasts](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_beasts "Tailed beasts").[^125] With access to both of his Rinnegan, Madara could produce at least four shadows to aid him.[^126]

### Intelligence

![Madara rescinds the Impure World Reincarnation contract.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/6/61/Edo_Tensei_Release.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20131128182143)

Madara rescinds the Impure World Reincarnation contract.

Madara's years of experience on the battlefield honed his strategic ingenuity. He could quickly devise a number of approaches and could switch approaches immediately. The longer he spent against a particular opponent, the less he needed backup tactics, as he could eventually predict what they would do and identify literal or psychological weaknesses to exploit. He could form and verify any theories he comes up with using small details.[^102] In addition to his sheer adaptability, Madara was knowledgeable on a large variety of individuals and abilities, and thus could identify techniques when used and react with the most appropriate counter to them. Likewise, Madara was an excellent teacher, having single-handedly trained the young Obito into a powerful shinobi comparable to himself.

A researcher, he experimented on and cultivated a [clone flower](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hashirama%27s_Living_Clone "Hashirama's Living Clone") through the Demonic Statue of Outer Path, and from it, he created a life-support system for himself.[^138] He had enough medical knowledge to transplant eyes, treat severe injuries, and could replace destroyed body parts with White Zetsu matter.[^139]

Kabuto speaks through Mū, introduces himself, and explains recent developments in the world and Tobi's current actions. After Kabuto points out that he has restored Madara to a state beyond his prime, Madara tests his body by tearing through the [Fourth Division](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Division "Fourth Division"), thinning its numbers. The Fourth Division's initial attempts to stop Madara fail as he protects himself with [Susanoo](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Susanoo "Susanoo"). When the [Fifth Kazekage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fifth_Kazekage "Fifth Kazekage"), [Third Tsuchikage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Tsuchikage "Third Tsuchikage"), and one of [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki") 's [shadow clones](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_clone "Shadow clone") combine efforts, Madara is forced to activate his [Rinnegan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rinnegan "Rinnegan") and [absorb](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Blocking_Technique_Absorption_Seal "Blocking Technique Absorption Seal") the attack.

Madara battles the Fourth Division.

Realising his enemies were too dangerous for him to hold back to basic ninjutsu and taijutsu, Madara pulls down a [meteorite](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tengai_Shinsei "Tengai Shinsei") from the atmosphere. When the Kazekage and Tsuchikage successfully [stop it](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Earth_Release:_Ultralight-Weight_Rock_Technique "Earth Release: Ultralight-Weight Rock Technique"), Madara adds a second meteorite to his attack, both of which fall upon and devastate the Fourth Division. With most of the Fourth Division dead, Madara questions Kabuto about just how far off-plan Tobi has gone. Because Kabuto doesn't actually know much about the plan, Madara is left to check things for himself and tries to summon the [Nine-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tails "Nine-Tails"), only to discover it's sealed in a [jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki "Jinchūriki"), specifically Naruto. Despite knowing the current Naruto was a clone, he decided to delay his plans to test out his new powers, namely, the [Wood Release: Nativity of Trees](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Release:_Nativity_of_Trees "Wood Release: Nativity of Trees") which Naruto's clone barely countered. He then moves in to finish off the remainder of the Fourth Division but is repelled by the arrival of the [Fifth Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fifth_Hokage "Fifth Hokage"), the [Fifth Mizukage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fifth_Mizukage "Fifth Mizukage"), and the [Fourth Raikage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Raikage "Fourth Raikage"). The five [Kage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kage "Kage") combine forces against Madara and propel him from the immediate area.

Madara overwhelms the Kage.

By the time he returns, Naruto's shadow clone is gone and the five Kage remain his only opponents. They battle for some time, with Madara equaling and besting the Kage's efforts. When he is hit with the Tsuchikage's [Dust Release: Detachment of the Primitive World Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Dust_Release:_Detachment_of_the_Primitive_World_Technique "Dust Release: Detachment of the Primitive World Technique"), Madara's armour is torn away, revealing the implant of [Hashirama](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hashirama "Hashirama") 's face on his chest. Madara laments Hashirama's legacy that these Kage have inherited, having been completely unimpressed by their attacks, particularly those of the Hokage, Hashirama's granddaughter. Madara then [creates twenty-five copies of himself](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Multiple_Wood_Release_Clone_Technique "Multiple Wood Release Clone Technique") and has them each activate their Susanoo.

Madara's soul remains intact.

By nightfall, the Kage are still alive and are able to pool their efforts so successfully that he is nearly sealed. Finally impressed by their display, Madara responds by summoning his perfected Susanoo. Before he can use it to kill the Kage, his Susanoo starts to fade and Madara is engulfed in light. Madara realises that Kabuto has elsewhere released him from the Impure World Reincarnation and his soul is returning to the afterlife. Madara responds by performing the hand seals to rescind the Impure World Reincarnation contract that Kabuto has over him, enabling him to return to his immortal body without further interference from a summoner. Now able to do whatever he likes and bored with the Kage, Madara decides to go find Naruto and reclaim the Nine-Tails. The Kage try to block his escape.

After Obito returns Madara's [gunbai](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gunbai "Gunbai"), Madara creates a [wood dragon](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_dragon "Wood dragon") in an attempt to capture Naruto and Killer B and complete the Ten-Tails. Their four opponents put up a resistance but are no match for the Ten-Tails' power. Before the Ten-Tails can finish them off, however, the combined Allied Shinobi Forces arrive to join the fight, with Ino managing to control Obito into redirecting the Ten-Tails' attack.

## Legacy

After his second death, Madara's greatest lasting influence was in Obito's actions, who used Madara's knowledge to create [Akatsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki "Akatsuki"). Several years later, when Obito began operating under Madara's name, the mere possibility that he was truly Madara was enough to unify the ninja of the [Five Great Shinobi Countries](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Five_Great_Shinobi_Countries "Five Great Shinobi Countries") and trigger the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War").[^141]

Madara's actions during life, and even those subsequent actions of Obito, would end up having disastrous effects for the Uchiha clan. Following Madara's betrayal of Konoha, the Uchiha were entirely isolated from any form of control over Konoha's future, chiefly because of the actions of [Tobirama Senju](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tobirama_Senju "Tobirama Senju"). His actions also played a role in Tobirama's growth, who believed that to be Hokage, one must balance between Hashirama's compassion and Madara's ruthlessness. At the time, some Uchiha members saw this as proof of what Madara always feared, but the fears subsided with time. Years later, when Obito causes the [Nine-Tailed Demon Fox's Attack](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tailed_Demon_Fox%27s_Attack "Nine-Tailed Demon Fox's Attack"), Konoha once again becomes suspicious of the Uchiha and the Uchiha once again feel wronged. This time, however, their discontent does not subside. When the Uchiha began planning to overthrow Konoha, the upper echelons of Konoha's government ordered the [Uchiha Clan Downfall](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uchiha_Clan_Downfall "Uchiha Clan Downfall").

One silver lining of Madara's legacy is the name of Konohagakure, which he himself came up with.

## Trivia

| ![Springtime of Youth](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/3/39/Youth_symbol.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/40?cb=20130120020002)  Springtime of Youth | The [*Springtime of Youth* wiki](https://springtimeofyouth.fandom.com/wiki/ "w:c:springtimeofyouth") has an article about this topic:  **[Madara Uchiha](https://springtimeofyouth.fandom.com/wiki/Madara_Uchiha "w:c:springtimeofyouth:Madara Uchiha")** |
| --- | --- |

- Madarao is the name of one of the [Five Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Five_Mountains_of_Northern_Shinshu "wikipedia:Five Mountains of Northern Shinshu") of Northern Shinshu, the other's being mount Iizuna, mount Kurohime, mount Togakushi, and mount Myoko.
	- Madara's name, written in katakana, means "pacific cod".
		- Even though not used for his name, "Madara" (斑) can be translated as "spots" or "speckles" if written in kanji.
- Studio Pierrot's Settei sheets of Madara show that he was 143.5cm when he met Hashirama.
- According to the databook(s):
	- Madara's hobby was [falconry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falconry "wikipedia:Falconry").
		- Madara's favourite food was [inarizushi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sushi#Inarizushi "wikipedia:Sushi"), while his least favourite food was [roe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roe "wikipedia:Roe"), as well as [shirako](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milt#:~:text=In%20Japanese%20cuisine%2C%20the%20testes,pufferfish%20\(fugu\)%20are%20eaten.) (milt) specifically derived from [cod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cod) fish.
		- Madara wished to fight the upper class Konoha ninja.
		- Madara's favourite phrase was "Armour-sleeved one touch" (鎧袖一触, *Gaishū Isshoku*), which refers to defeating someone with a single blow.
- Despite Madara being a user of the Six Paths Senjutsu and using [various](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sage_Art:_Yin_Release_Lightning_Dispatch "Sage Art: Yin Release Lightning Dispatch") senjutsu [techniques](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sage_Art:_Storm_Release_Light_Fang "Sage Art: Storm Release Light Fang"), he is not listed as a user of senjutsu in the fourth databook.[^2]
- Despite Hashirama's blood type being type B, and Madara's blood type being type O, Madara was able to transplant Hashirama's DNA onto himself despite type B not being able to give blood to type O due to incompatibility. This is the same case with Obito, whose blood type is also type O.
