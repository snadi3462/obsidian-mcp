**Team Kurenai**, also known as **Team 8**, was a ninja team led by [Kurenai Yūhi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kurenai_Y%C5%ABhi "Kurenai Yūhi"). Its members are [Kiba Inuzuka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kiba_Inuzuka "Kiba Inuzuka"), his [ninken](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ninken "Ninken") [Akamaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akamaru "Akamaru"), [Hinata Hyūga](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hinata_Hy%C5%ABga "Hinata Hyūga"), and [Shino Aburame](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shino_Aburame "Shino Aburame"). The team specialises in tracking and locating through the use of the [Hyūga clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hy%C5%ABga_clan "Hyūga clan") 's [Byakugan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Byakugan "Byakugan"), the [Aburame clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Aburame_clan "Aburame clan") 's [bugs](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kikaich%C5%AB "Kikaichū"), and the [Inuzuka clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Inuzuka_clan "Inuzuka clan") 's [dogs](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ninken "Ninken") as well as Kiba's own heightened sense of smell.[^2] When Kurenai became pregnant in Part II, [Kakashi Hatake](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi_Hatake "Kakashi Hatake") took over as the squad leader on some of their missions.

Along with the other members of the [Konoha 11](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_11 "Konoha 11"), Team 8 had regrettably decided to personally kill [Sasuke Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Uchiha "Sasuke Uchiha") in order to stop him from sinking any lower as a [missing-nin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Missing-nin "Missing-nin") and risk causing a devastating international war as a result, but were persuaded by [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki") that he alone will take care of it.

Ultimately, however, Naruto was able to defeat Sasuke and turn him back to good. Alongside [Team Kakashi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Kakashi "Team Kakashi"), Team Kurenai was the only team not to lose any members throughout Part II ([Team Asuma](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Asuma "Team Asuma") losing [Asuma Sarutobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Asuma_Sarutobi "Asuma Sarutobi"), [Team Guy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Guy "Team Guy") losing [Neji Hyūga](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Neji_Hy%C5%ABga "Neji Hyūga")), though Kurenai herself was put on maternity leave to take care of her daughter [Mirai Sarutobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mirai_Sarutobi "Mirai Sarutobi"). Presumably, she returned to active duty when her daughter was old enough. Hinata eventually married Naruto and later had two children with him, [Boruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Boruto_Uzumaki "Boruto Uzumaki") and [Himawari Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Himawari_Uzumaki "Himawari Uzumaki"), while Kiba started dating [Tamaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tamaki "Tamaki"), and Shino became a teacher at the Academy.

## Known Missions

[Capture Gosunkugi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gosunkugi_Capture_Mission "Gosunkugi Capture Mission") (Anime only)

- **Rank:** Unknown
- **Status:** Failure

In the anime, when [Gosunkugi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gosunkugi "Gosunkugi"), a notorious thief, has reached Konoha, Tsunade sent Kiba, Hinata, and Naruto to deal with him. However, as it was a bounty hunter by the name of [Sazanami](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sazanami "Sazanami") who eventually caught Gosunkugi, this mission was considered to be a failure on the three ninja's behalf, despite them helping the capture.

[Hidden Treasure Retrieval](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Buried_Gold_Excavation_Mission "Buried Gold Excavation Mission") (Anime only)

- **Rank:** Unknown
- **Status:** Success

In the anime, when Tsunade noticed that Kiba, Hinata, and Naruto had failed several times lately, she gave them one more chance to prove themselves worthy enough to not be sent back to the [Academy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Academy "Academy"): to retrieve a hidden treasure. They encountered the [Kedōin clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ked%C5%8Din_clan "Kedōin clan"), who [disguised themselves](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Face_Copying_Technique "Face Copying Technique") as the three genins in order to infiltrate and destroy Konoha. The three eventually defeated the impostors, but it was revealed that Tsunade hired them to test how they could handle the situation.

[Capture the Bandit](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Friends_You_Can_Count_On "Friends You Can Count On") (Anime only)

- **Rank:** Unknown
- **Status:** Success

In the anime, Team Kurenai was sent on a mission to capture [Suika](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Suika "Suika"), a missing-nin from [Takigakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Takigakure "Takigakure"). Though initially unsuccesful due to the skill of their opponent, they eventually managed to apprehend the criminal.

[Locate Orochimaru's Base](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Three-Tails%27_Appearance "Three-Tails' Appearance") (Anime only)

- **Rank:** Unknown
- **Status:** Partially Successful

In the anime in Part II, Team Kurenai was teamed up with [Kakashi Hatake](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi_Hatake "Kakashi Hatake") to locate one of [Orochimaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Orochimaru "Orochimaru") 's base, only to discover it to be already abandoned.

[Hunt for Itachi and Sasuke](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Itachi_Pursuit_Mission "Itachi Pursuit Mission")

- **Rank:** Unknown
- **Status:** Failure

In Part II, Team Kurenai has been assigned as part of the [Eight Man Squad](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eight_Man_Squad "Eight Man Squad") along with [Team Kakashi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Kakashi "Team Kakashi"), in order to track down Sasuke and [Itachi Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Itachi_Uchiha "Itachi Uchiha"). However, they were stalled by [Tobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tobi "Tobi"), and ultimately failed to bring Sasuke back before he and Itachi's corpse were taken away.
