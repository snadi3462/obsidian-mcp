**Iruka Umino** (うみのイルカ, *Umino Iruka*) is a [chūnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin "Chūnin") of [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure"), who served primarily as an instructor at the [Academy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Academy "Academy") until being promoted to Headmaster years later.

## Background

![Iruka with his mother during the Nine-Tails' attack on Konoha.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/1/14/Kohari_Umino.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160203011340)

Iruka with his mother during the Nine-Tails' attack on Konoha.

Iruka's parents were killed in the [Nine-Tailed Demon Fox's Attack](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tailed_Demon_Fox%27s_Attack "Nine-Tailed Demon Fox's Attack") twelve years before the beginning of the series. Initially, a young Iruka was present on the battlefield, and wanted to stay in order to protect his mother. His father declared that it was the parents' duty to keep their child safe from harm. He was forcibly removed from the battlefield by an unknown shinobi while he called out for his parent to no avail.[^4]

With his family gone, Iruka had no one to give him attention. Iruka thereafter became a prankster, trying to gain attention to stave off his loneliness.[^5] He would spend his time at the monument where his parents' names were engraved, and, in his darkest moments would be cheered up by the [Third Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Hokage "Third Hokage") (who would go on to serve as a mentor for him), including one time about a year after he lost his parents.[^6] [^7] [Mizuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mizuki "Mizuki") was a childhood friend of Iruka's after the Nine-Tails Attack, and usually made him feel better. In the anime, it was noted that due to other people giving Iruka more attention than they do to Mizuki, Mizuki's kindness to Iruka revealed to be an act just to gain the Hokage's approval, all the while making Iruka miserable discreetly.

In the anime, it was revealed that Iruka became a teacher after being injured while on mission lead by Kakashi Hatake. Recognising his heart and compassion might end up injuring himself or others, Iruka decided to retire from field work and become a teacher, as he wanted to pass on the [Will of Fire](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Will_of_Fire "Will of Fire") to the next generation in this manner, inspired by the Third Hokage's wisdom. When Naruto became his student, he found it awkward to deal with the person containing the very monster responsible for his parents' death. He tried to ask the Third Hokage if he could not teach Naruto, though he was eventually disabused by the notion by [Kakashi Hatake](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi_Hatake "Kakashi Hatake"). Later, Naruto went into the woods to steal items from corpses. Iruka chased him down and protected him from [Takigakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Takigakure "Takigakure") spies who were attacking Naruto, eventually backed up by Kakashi. After saving Naruto and bringing back the document attached to the artefact the latter retrieved, he reprimanded Naruto for such a reckless behaviour. This would surprise Naruto as this was the first time anyone in the village showed they actually cared for his well-being. He also thanked the Third for assigning Naruto to him. He thus resolved to be straight forward and deal with Naruto strictly in class from then on, in order to train him to become a fine shinobi.[^8]

## Personality

Iruka is described as being both big-hearted, and soft-hearted. This is most often seen through his teaching methods, often keeping a watchful eye over his students as they progress. This however, does not mean that he is a pushover, as he can be stern when the situation calls for it, most often seen when he shouts at his students in order to get them to obey him. Despite the tragedy of losing his parents at a young age, Iruka remained a determined and strong-willed individual with a kind disposition. This is seen from his lack of hostility towards Naruto Uzumaki who was often the victim of misplaced anger at the [Nine-Tailed Demon Fox](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tailed_Demon_Fox "Nine-Tailed Demon Fox") who was sealed within Naruto. Because of this, Iruka came to be one of the only adults Naruto respected in his youth as well as the only person who could control Naruto's behaviour to some degree. Naruto ignored all chūnin and [jōnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/J%C5%8Dnin "Jōnin") who reprimanded him for painting all over the Hokage's faces and only showed fear when Iruka showed up.[^9]

As a child growing up without his parents, Iruka turned to performing antics in the Academy in order to gain recognition from others who would otherwise pass him over or look at him with pity for being an orphan. This allowed him to also see some of these signs and understand what they meant when displayed by Naruto. Due to Iruka's more nurturing, protective and compassionate nature, Naruto views him as a mentor and Iruka in turn, views Naruto as a younger brother. The two enjoy their favourite ramen dish at [Ramen Ichiraku](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ramen_Ichiraku "Ramen Ichiraku") whenever Naruto returns from a mission, where Iruka gets an opportunity to see how Naruto is developing as a ninja. Iruka's refusal to allow Naruto to graduate at first was not out of spite, but merely out of caring strictness and a desire for Naruto to work harder and reach his own potential. As a result of his genuine compassion, Iruka became the main reason Naruto did not become a sociopath like Gaara did.

He also has a great sense of duty, honour and selflessness as seen when he protected Naruto from [Mizuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mizuki "Mizuki") 's attack, suffering a shuriken wound in his back in order to protect him. He greatly admires both the Third Hokage who was there to help him during his troubled period as a child and believes strongly in the [Will of Fire](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Will_of_Fire "Will of Fire") of the village. As a result, he regards the children of the village as the backbone of Konoha — even being willing to sacrifice his own life to ensure their safety. In the anime, he even saw the renegade Mizuki as an old friend who had inherited the Will of Fire, and tried repeatedly to persuade him to surrender and repent, until Mizuki made it clear that their friendship was a lie and he had no remorse for any of the crimes he committed, forcing Iruka to give up on him.

## Appearance

- ![Iruka as a child.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/01/Iruka_young.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20150703083019)
	Iruka as a child.

1/4

Iruka is a man of average height and build. He has black hair (brown in the anime) that he keeps in a ponytail, dark eyes and a scar that runs across the bridge of his nose — which he has had since his youth. He wears the standard Konoha shinobi outfit complete with [forehead protector](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Forehead_protector "Forehead protector"), sandals, and [flak jacket](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Flak_jacket "Flak jacket"). His sleeves are also rolled up about ¼ way. As a child he wore a simple outfit along with mesh-armour underneath it.

In *[The Last: Naruto the Movie](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/The_Last:_Naruto_the_Movie "The Last: Naruto the Movie")*, two years after the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War"), Iruka's overall appearance stayed the same. The only noticeable difference is he wears an [Uzumaki clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uzumaki_clan "Uzumaki clan") logo armband on his left arm with the new standard flak jacket and high-top sandals. Several years afterwards, Iruka has aged further, with slight creases around his mouth. He also abandons his forehead protector and shinobi attire, and instead dons a grey zipped shirt.[^10]

## Abilities

![Iruka erecting a barrier to restrain Naruto.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/9/91/Isshi_T%C5%8Djin.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20120816112929)

Iruka erecting a barrier to restrain Naruto.

As a former head instructor of the [Academy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Academy "Academy") and a chūnin of Konoha, Iruka is highly proficient in all forms of basic ninja skills, having well-rounded talents and balanced abilities [^11] that have earned him the trust of both his students and his superiors.[^12] He has some skill with [Barrier Ninjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Barrier_Ninjutsu "Barrier Ninjutsu"), using it mostly for binding purposes, such as with the [String Light Formation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/String_Light_Formation "String Light Formation"), and in the anime, the [Sealed Binding Formation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Binding_Formation "Sealed Binding Formation"). He is also able to use [nature transformation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nature_transformation "Nature transformation"), possessing [Fire](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fire "Fire"), [Water](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water "Water"), and [Yin Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yin_Release "Yin Release").[^3] He was noted as having the potential to become a jōnin or [Anbu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Anbu "Anbu") member.[^13]

In the anime, he has shown noticeable skill in [genjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genjutsu "Genjutsu"), as seen when he performed the [Demonic Illusion: Hell Viewing Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Illusion:_Hell_Viewing_Technique "Demonic Illusion: Hell Viewing Technique") to test Sakura,[^14] and saw through Mizuki's illusion.[^15] He also displayed what seems to be an [echolocation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Echo_sounding "wikipedia:Echo sounding") ability, where he could get a sonar image of his surrounding area.[^15] He could also [detect](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sensing_Technique "Sensing Technique") the change in someone's chakra.[^16] He is skillful with [shurikenjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shurikenjutsu "Shurikenjutsu"), being able to calculate the deflection points among a thicket of bamboos to cause a [kunai](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kunai "Kunai") to bounce at his target at unsuspecting angles.[^17]

## Part I

### Prologue — Land of Waves

Main article: [Prologue — Land of Waves](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Prologue_%E2%80%94_Land_of_Waves "Prologue — Land of Waves") When Naruto managed to evade other ninja after desecrating the [Hokage Rock](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage_Rock "Hokage Rock") with graffiti, Iruka captured his student and berated him for not focusing on the upcoming graduating exams. While Naruto was supposed to [transform into Iruka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Transformation_Technique "Transformation Technique"), he instead [turned into a naked girl](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sexy_Technique "Sexy Technique"), with Iruka suffering a massive nosebleed before giving Naruto a reprimanding. Iruka then punished Naruto to clean the Hokage Rock, but then invited him to [Ramen Ichiraku](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ramen_Ichiraku "Ramen Ichiraku"), where he learned of Naruto's desire to be [Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage "Hokage").

![Iruka protects Naruto.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/d/d8/Iruka_protects_naruto.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150504035704)

Iruka protects Naruto.

Next day, when Naruto failed to conjure at least one [clone](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_Technique "Clone Technique"), Iruka failed him, despite [Mizuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mizuki "Mizuki") 's false sympathetic reconsideration. When Mizuki tricked Naruto into stealing the [Scroll of Seals](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Scroll_of_Seals "Scroll of Seals"), Iruka tracked down Naruto before being hit by a barrage of kunai and realising that the boy was set up. Iruka then protected Naruto from Mizuki's [fūma shuriken](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/F%C5%ABma_shuriken "Fūma shuriken") after Naruto learned that he is the Nine-Tails jinchūriki. Nevertheless, Naruto then ran away despite Iruka's pleas and apologies for unintentionally allowing Mizuki to set him up. Intent to protect Naruto regardless of him learning the truth, Iruka insisted that he never hated Naruto and saw him as only a ninja of their village. Witnessing Naruto create [a high amount of](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Multiple_Shadow_Clone_Technique "Multiple Shadow Clone Technique") [advanced clones](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_Clone_Technique "Shadow Clone Technique") after being spurred by his faith in him, Iruka allowed Naruto to graduate from the Academy, giving him his own forehead protector.

### Mizuki Tracking Mission

Main article: [Mizuki Tracking Mission](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mizuki_Tracking_Mission "Mizuki Tracking Mission")

![Iruka battling a transformed Mizuki.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/6/68/Iruka_battling_Mizuki.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160321193807)

Iruka battling a transformed Mizuki.

In the aftermath of the invasion, Iruka was forced to suspend his teaching duties in order to go on missions and fill the void in personnel. In the anime, he was assisted by [Pakkun](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Pakkun "Pakkun") to find Mizuki and go after Naruto and [Tonton](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tonton "Tonton"). When Iruka caught up, they send Tonton back to the village and Pakkun leads Naruto and Iruka to [Shizune](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shizune "Shizune"). Shizune led them in pursuing Mizuki, when they soon fell into a trap. Iruka then revealed that Shizune is not Shizune but Mizuki himself. While Naruto battles with [Fūjin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/F%C5%ABjin "Fūjin") and [Raijin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Raijin "Raijin"), Iruka battles it out with Mizuki. Iruka wanted to bring the "old" Mizuki back, but Mizuki revealed that he was only nice to Iruka because he believed Iruka was the [Third Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Hokage "Third Hokage") 's favourite and secretly used their "friendship" to keep Iruka miserable. Mizuki led Iruka into a building used to test [Academy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Academy "Academy") instructors and battled there. After that, Naruto and Pakkun caught up with Iruka and they went to Mizuki's lover, [Tsubaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tsubaki_\(anime\) "Tsubaki (anime)"). Tsubaki helped them reach Mizuki but it was too late. Mizuki had drank a formula and became stronger and monstrous. They battled again with Mizuki having the upper edge. Iruka came up with a plan to trap Mizuki, giving Naruto a chance to use [Rasengan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rasengan "Rasengan") on [Mizuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mizuki "Mizuki").

## Part II

### Kazekage Rescue Mission

Main article: [Kazekage Rescue Mission](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kazekage_Rescue_Mission "Kazekage Rescue Mission") Iruka met with Naruto when he returned two and a half years later, and cheered him up after he found out that all of his friends had advanced in rank during his absence. ### Pain's Assault

Main article: [Pain's Assault](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Pain%27s_Assault_\(Arc\) "Pain's Assault (Arc)")

![Iruka comforting Naruto over Jiraiya's death.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/d/dd/Irula_consoles_Naruto.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230430042155)

Iruka comforting Naruto over Jiraiya's death.

After Jiraiya was killed, Iruka sought out Naruto and comforted him. Iruka told Naruto that [Jiraiya](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jiraiya "Jiraiya") would always be proud of him. He went on to inform Naruto of how Jiraiya had always bragged about him, and had mentioned multiple times how he thought of Naruto as a grandson. Iruka also reminded him that he was now the one to carry on Jiraiya's legacy. During [Pain](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Pain "Pain") 's attack on Konoha, Iruka was found by one of Pain's bodies while attending to a wounded ninja. He refused to tell Pain where Naruto was, and was almost killed. [Kakashi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi "Kakashi") intervened, however, allowing Iruka a chance to get the ninja to the hospital and to later join a squad of Konoha ninja to help repel the invasion.

Following Pain's attack, Iruka was last seen apparently injured but alive, having been protected by one of [Katsuyu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Katsuyu "Katsuyu") 's [divisions](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Slug_Great_Division "Slug Great Division"). After Pain resurrected everyone he had killed, Iruka was seen standing along with the rest of the villagers. He thought back to the days when Naruto was at the Academy, and compared it to the present while watching Naruto being cheered at by the whole village. After these memories, a proud smile spread across his face.

### Fourth Shinobi World War: Confrontation

Main article: [Fourth Shinobi World War: Confrontation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War:_Confrontation "Fourth Shinobi World War: Confrontation")

![Iruka returning Naruto's forehead protector with a letter inside.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/0e/A_Message_from_the_Heart.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160127043508)

Iruka returning Naruto's forehead protector with a letter inside.

During the Fourth Shinobi World War, Naruto and [Killer B](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Killer_B "Killer B"), as the last two jinchūriki, were confined to an island to keep them out of Akatsuki's clutches, (Naruto being unaware of this) and the war. After Naruto began to get suspicious and tried to leave, a handful of shinobi tried to stop and convince him to go back in. Iruka was among them, and he convinced [Shibi Aburame](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shibi_Aburame "Shibi Aburame") to let him talk to Naruto. Iruka attempted to trick Naruto into believing he had a second mission on the island. However, Naruto decided to go outside anyway and used Sage Mode to barge past the guards, in the process losing his forehead protector. Before he could leave, however, he was bound by the [Shadow Imitation Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_Imitation_Technique "Shadow Imitation Technique") by a [Nara clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nara_clan "Nara clan") member, but not before he sensed the war raging outside.

With Naruto demanding the truth, Iruka revealed that there was a war raging in order to protect him, and tried to persuade him not to intervene and risk capture. When Naruto declared that he would end the war on his own, Iruka said that Naruto was like a little brother to him, and he wouldn't be able to bear it if Naruto was captured. Naruto stated that it was Iruka who first recognised him and gave him his forehead protector, and asked him why he doubted his power now.

![Iruka bumps fists with B.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/7/7f/Bee%27s_Promise.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20120816112737)

Iruka bumps fists with B.

Glancing down at Naruto's fallen forehead protector, Iruka decided to return it to him, only to erect a [barrier](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/String_Light_Formation "String Light Formation") to trap Naruto. Naruto however enters his [Nine-Tails Chakra Mode](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tails_Chakra_Mode "Nine-Tails Chakra Mode") and easily breaks out of the barrier and resisted all attempts to subdue him, before making his way to the battlefield. On his way, Naruto found a note in his forehead protector, from Iruka, telling him to come back alive. When Killer B arrived, Iruka told him to protect Naruto and they bumped fists. The [Eight-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eight-Tails "Eight-Tails") told him that he couldn't keep an eye on Naruto but B said no one could, and said to Iruka that he was in Naruto's heart and everything he taught was still inside, even today.

Main articles: [Konoha Hiden (novel)](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Hiden:_The_Perfect_Day_for_a_Wedding "Konoha Hiden: The Perfect Day for a Wedding") and [Konoha Hiden (anime)](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Hiden:_The_Perfect_Day_for_a_Wedding_\(Arc\) "Konoha Hiden: The Perfect Day for a Wedding (Arc)") Shortly after he received his invitation to the wedding, Iruka was approached at the Academy by Naruto, who asked if Iruka would attend as his father. Iruka happily agreed at the time and, as soon as Naruto left, was overwhelmed with emotion, something that now reoccurs every time he sees Naruto listed on the menu at Ichiraku Ramen. Iruka can't help but feel indebted to Naruto, whose presence first in his classroom and now in his life pulled him out of the self-pity he experienced following his parents' deaths and convinced him to dedicate his life to teaching; were it not for Naruto, Iruka worries he would have turned out like Mizuki.

Iruka buys a wedding gift before stopping by Ichiraku Ramen, where he smiles at the realisation that he won't get to have dinner with Naruto as often because Hinata will now be cooking for him. When he gets home, he looks at the disarray and disrepair he lives in and resolves to find a life partner just like Naruto has.

In the anime, Iruka was also approached with an offer to take the Vice Principal's position at the Academy. He struggled with leaving the classroom for a more administrative role, and with trying to define his relationship to Naruto, having trouble composing a congratulatory message for Konohamaru's video, as a somewhat strict teacher-student relationship didn't quite feel right. It wasn't until both Naruto and Hinata both indicated they saw Iruka as Naruto's father figure that Iruka was finally able to come up with his message for Naruto's wedding day.

## New Era

In the following years, Iruka continued to watch serve as an instructor to the Academy and eventually was promoted to headmaster.[^19] He also continued to proudly watch Naruto grow. On the day that Naruto was to be inaugurated as the Seventh [Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage "Hokage"), Iruka joined the rest of the village to watch the ceremony. As he stood beside Sakura to watch the Naruto give his speech upon becoming Hokage, Iruka noticed that favourite pupil seemed off (who was really a [disguised](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Transformation_Technique "Transformation Technique") Konohamaru filling in for the knocked-out Uzumaki).[^20] While Naruto would diligently perform his duties as Hokage, Iruka noted that Naruto needed to improve his multi-tasking with his family life.

### Academy Entrance Arc

Main article: [Academy Entrance Arc](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Academy_Entrance_Arc "Academy Entrance Arc") In the anime, ten years after the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War") ends, Iruka attends the entrance ceremony for the new students. Ultimately, he and the other instructors were left mortified when [Boruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Boruto_Uzumaki "Boruto Uzumaki") crashed a train into the [Hokage Rock](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage_Rock "Hokage Rock"). When the Academy had a new transfer student, [Mitsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mitsuki "Mitsuki"), and Shino was having problems keeping the class in line, Iruka offered to have [Anko Mitarashi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Anko_Mitarashi "Anko Mitarashi") help out.

After [Sumire Kakei](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sumire_Kakei "Sumire Kakei") turned herself in as the culprit behind an attack on the village, Iruka was given the report. While [Shino Aburame](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shino_Aburame "Shino Aburame") insisted to let her return to the Academy under his supervision, Iruka noted that despite the leniency presented to her, the nature of her returning was still depending on her choice.

### School Trip Arc

Main article: [School Trip Arc](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/School_Trip_Arc "School Trip Arc") In the anime, to improve relationships with other villages and symbolically show the warring era was over, the Seventh Hokage set up an official class trip for the Academy to go to Kirigakure. Upon presenting this idea to Iruka, the principle admired this bold step to easing remaining tension from the previous warring era and approved the idea. Meanwhile, Boruto tries to prank Iruka with a Wind Release jutsu. Naruto appears to reprimand his son but tells him to put more finesse in pulling pranks. As they argue, Iruka remarks at them.

After the class returned from the trip, Iruka received a letter from the [Sixth Mizukage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sixth_Mizukage "Sixth Mizukage") about the events, learning that Boruto, Sarada, and Iwabee aided Chōjūrō in stopping a coup d'état. When confronting the students about it, after Boruto tried to get out of trouble with a longwinded story, Iruka had the three students repaint a classroom as punishment.

### Graduation Exams Arc

Main article: [Graduation Exams Arc](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Graduation_Exams_Arc "Graduation Exams Arc")

![Iruka and Kakashi discussing the Academy students.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/6/65/Iruka_and_Kakashi_talk.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20221020221118)

Iruka and Kakashi discussing the Academy students.

As the ninja classes began preparing for the graduation exams, Iruka requested that Kakashi be a proctor, which the retired Hokage agreed to. On a unique decision, Kakashi decided to get a better understanding of the students by posing as reporter using his Sukea disguise and interviewed them. Afterwards, Iruka requested that Kakashi to try to go easy on the students during the exams. Kakashi however insisted that they can't have students pass with the wrong type of mentality and such would not hold back on them.

Ultimately, Kakashi passed all the students after they showed true team-work and loyalty towards each other. Despite this, Iruka was still very annoyed at how intense Kakashi was on the students, leading Kakashi to apologise.

### Time Slip Arc

Main article: [Time Slip Arc](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Time_Slip_Arc "Time Slip Arc") In the anime, several months later, the Academy began hosting an annual one-day trial session for young children to experience the shinobi life. While Iruka was happy to see that [Himawari Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Himawari_Uzumaki "Himawari Uzumaki") would be attending, it made him nostalgic for his own teacher days, upset that he couldn't directly teach any more as headmaster. Shino offered for Iruka to at least watch from the sidelines, which Iruka agreed to. During the day's final lesson as team mission, he watched as Himawari was scolded by one of her team-mates for not taking this course without being sure it was the career she wanted. After the two team-mates left Himawari to sulk, Iruka approached her. As she admitted to feeling guilty for not taking this seriously like her team-mates, Iruka insisted that there is no rush to find a career path at her age, but rather this trial was merely meant to give children motivation on looking towards the future. Himawari then resolved herself to complete the mission, earning praise from Iruka for her gutsiness akin to her father. Iruka and the teachers then watched with pride as Himawari was able to reconcile with her team-mates and working together, were able to complete their mission.

### Kawaki & Himawari Academy Arc

Main article: [Kawaki & Himawari Academy Arc](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kawaki_%26_Himawari_Academy_Arc "Kawaki & Himawari Academy Arc") In the anime, Iruka hosted the entrance ceremony for a new class of students in the Academy. Later, when Kae had to return to [Land of Bamboo](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_Bamboo "Land of Bamboo") earlier than planned because of her father falling ill, it was decided as a sendoff to restart the festival in her honour. During which, Iruka watched Kae perform a play with her class.

## Trivia

- The name "Iruka" means "dolphin" (海豚), while his family name "Umino" means "of the sea" (海乃). In Japanese, his full name can be interpreted as "dolphin in the sea" (海乃海豚, *Umi no Iruka*).
	- To which, his echolocation ability is perhaps playing on the meaning of his name.[^15]
- Iruka was the second character created by Kishimoto for the *Naruto* series. He was created for the theme of searching for acceptance.[^21]
- Kishimoto stated that Iruka's design was inspired by Manji, the protagonist of the manga series [*Blade of the Immortal*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blade_of_the_Immortal "wikipedia:Blade of the Immortal"). This is most notable in the scar across the bridge of Iruka's nose.[^22]
- Despite having very few appearances later on in the series, Iruka stayed in the Top Ten of the first six [Naruto Character Popularity Polls](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Character_Popularity_Polls "Naruto Character Popularity Polls"); he fell to eleventh place in the seventh poll.
- In the first databook and the first fanbook, Iruka's age was given as 25. His [Ninja Registration Number](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ninja_Registration_Number "Ninja Registration Number") is likewise listed as 011450.[^23]
- According to the databook(s):
	- Iruka's hobby is going to the [hot springs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_spring "wikipedia:Hot spring").
		- Iruka's favourite food is ramen from [Ramen Ichiraku](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ramen_Ichiraku "Ramen Ichiraku"), with his least favourite being mazegohan (混ぜ御飯, *blended rice*); a type of [takikomi gohan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Takikomi_gohan "wikipedia:Takikomi gohan"), but with the ingredients added in various stages while cooking.
		- Iruka wishes to fight [Kurama](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kurama "Kurama") [^23] and his students.[^2]
		- Iruka has completed 573 official missions in total: 288 D-rank, 183 C-rank, 90 B-rank, 12 A-rank, 0 S-rank.
		- Iruka's favourite word is "Reliance" (信頼, *Shinrai*).
