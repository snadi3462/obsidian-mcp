**Might Guy** (マイト・ガイ, *Maito Gai*) is a [jōnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/J%C5%8Dnin "Jōnin") of [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure"). A master of [taijutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Taijutsu "Taijutsu"), Guy leads and passes his wisdom on to the members of [Team Guy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Guy "Team Guy").

## Background

![Guy as a child.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/6/64/Kid_Might_Guy.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150702114648)

Guy as a child.

Guy is the son of [Might Duy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Might_Duy "Might Duy"), who was known throughout [Konoha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha "Konoha") as the " [Eternal Genin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Genin "Eternal Genin") ". Duy was not bothered by this moniker and instead was grateful that other people cared enough to know him at all. Duy encouraged this same kind of optimism in Guy, as well as his belief that one always has youth and that they could both become [taijutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Taijutsu "Taijutsu") masters through diligent training. Guy did his best to embody his father's teachings, but had doubts that Duy's words were devoid of meaning. He nevertheless loved his father and fought others when they called Duy the "Eternal Genin" mockingly; when one of these fights landed him in the [Konoha Hospital](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Hospital "Konoha Hospital"), Duy encouraged Guy not to be upset by this loss and to instead remember that victory is achieved by defending important things.[^6]

Guy applied to enter Konoha's [Ninja Academy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ninja_Academy "Ninja Academy"), but did not pass the entrance exam. [Kakashi Hatake](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi_Hatake "Kakashi Hatake") was not surprised due to Guy's lack of talent in [ninjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ninjutsu "Ninjutsu") and [genjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genjutsu "Genjutsu"), which Guy chose to take as a supportive observation. However, Guy was later offered a position as an alternate in the year's Academy class with Kakashi. Upon graduation, he was added to a [team](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Ch%C5%8Dza "Team Chōza") that, in the anime, was led by [Chōza Akimichi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%8Dza_Akimichi "Chōza Akimichi"). The team at one point entered Konoha's [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams "Chūnin Exams") and passed to the final stage; Guy himself advanced to the exams' last one-on-one match, where he lost to Kakashi.[^7] Later, while on a mission with [Genma Shiranui](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genma_Shiranui "Genma Shiranui") and [Ebisu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ebisu "Ebisu"), Guy and his team were attacked by the [Seven Ninja Swordsmen of the Mist](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Seven_Ninja_Swordsmen_of_the_Mist "Seven Ninja Swordsmen of the Mist"). They were rescued by Duy, who used the [Eight Gates Released Formation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eight_Gates_Released_Formation "Eight Gates Released Formation") to fight off the Seven Swordsmen. Duy died from opening all [Eight Gates](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eight_Gates "Eight Gates"), but it satisfied his one rule for its use: giving his life to protect something precious, namely Guy.[^6]

Guy at some point (still in the academy in the anime [^8]) decided that Kakashi was his lifelong rival,[^9] driven by a desire to prove his perseverance could be just as good as Kakashi's natural genius. He would constantly challenge Kakashi to contests of skill, from eating contests to Rock, Paper, Scissors. Kakashi was indifferent to these contests, which only fuelled Guy's desire to defeat him. In the anime, Kakashi prevailed in the earlier of these competitions, but Guy soon started catching up, eventually achieving only a one point difference in their scores that has remained consistent over the years.[^10] Guy is proud of his score despite the fact that, because of how varied the contests are, it is hardly representative of anything. Through their competition, Kakashi and Guy became good friends; they were hanging out together on the night of the [Nine-Tailed Demon Fox's Attack](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tailed_Demon_Fox%27s_Attack "Nine-Tailed Demon Fox's Attack"), yet were prevented from helping defend the village, instead being confined within a [barrier](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Barrier "Barrier") to keep them safe.[^11]

![Guy, Asuma, and Kurenai plead Hiruzen to remove Kakashi from the Anbu.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/4/4a/Asuma_Guy_and_Kurenai_plead_Hiruzen.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230409011332)

In the anime, Guy was aware of the losses Kakashi experienced during and after the Third Shinobi World War and tried to help him deal with them. Following the death of [Obito Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Obito_Uchiha "Obito Uchiha"), Guy filled in for Obito while [Team Minato](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Minato "Team Minato") was on a mission into [Iwa](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Iwa "Iwa") -territory. When they were surrounded, Guy tried giving his life to allow Kakashi to escape, but they were able to fight off their enemies until reinforcements arrived.[^12] Following the death of [Rin Nohara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rin_Nohara "Rin Nohara"), Kakashi's teacher, the [Fourth Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Hokage "Fourth Hokage"), sent Guy to tail Kakashi on a mission in case memories of Rin started to get to him. This proved wise: when Kakashi began hyperventilating from using the [Chidori](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chidori "Chidori") - the same jutsu that killed Rin - Guy intervened in time to save Kakashi and take him home.[^13] Following the Fourth's death during the [Nine-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tails "Nine-Tails") ' attack, Guy requested to be admitted into the [Anbu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Anbu "Anbu") so that he could help Kakashi through his growing depression. The [Third Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Hokage "Third Hokage") refused his request, which [Danzō Shimura](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Danz%C5%8D_Shimura "Danzō Shimura") seconded, explaining that Guy lacked the necessary darkness for Anbu-service. Guy witnessed this "darkness" when Kakashi provided backup for him during a meeting with the [Land of Woods](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_Woods "Land of Woods"), wherein Kakashi mercilessly killed all the Land of Woods' forces. Guy asked the Third Hokage to remove Kakashi from the Anbu because these kind of acts didn't become him.[^14] When the Third eventually complied, Guy suggested that Kakashi try leading a team of [genin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genin "Genin") to help him regain his lost kindness.[^15]

![Team Guy is formed.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/1/10/Team_Gai.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160303083320)

Team Guy is formed.

Guy at some point heard about a boy in the Academy who was unable to use ninjutsu or genjutsu. Guy approached this boy, [Rock Lee](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rock_Lee "Rock Lee"), and encouraged him to use his youth to keep training. Lee was eventually added to Guy's [Team Guy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Guy "Team Guy"), along with [Neji Hyūga](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Neji_Hy%C5%ABga "Neji Hyūga") and [Tenten](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tenten "Tenten");[^16] in the anime, before accepting them, he required them all to demonstrate a desire to succeed no matter the odds.[^15] Guy took a particular interest in Lee after the team's first meeting, as Lee wished to become a splendid ninja who could only use taijutsu. Guy decided to dedicate his life to helping Lee achieve his dream, teaching him all he knew of taijutsu,[^17] telling him the same keys to self-improvement his father had taught him, and encouraging him to use a rivalry with Neji to push him to new heights.[^18] Lee developed well under his guidance, as did Neji and Tenten, though Guy felt it was best to have them wait a year before entering the Chūnin Exams.[^19]

## Personality

![Guy's "nice guy" pose.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/6/60/Gai_Pose.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150129145419)

Guy's "nice guy" pose.

Guy is perhaps most fully represented by his "nice guy" pose: a thumbs-up, wink, and winning smile - complete with the proverbial *ping*. The pose captures his optimism and confidence, as by striking the pose he is promising that whatever words accompany it are either true now or will become true in time. It represents his energy, as he will, through his understood-only-to-himself-and-select-others "Power of Youth (青春, *seishun*)", enthusiastically seek to live up to his promise no matter the personal investment doing so will require of him. It plumbs his deep emotional well because he only reserves it for the most important promises to the most important people for the most important reasons, and as such tears frequently stream down the face of *somebody* (inevitably Guy) when the pose is struck. He is a very driven and committed man, both to his principles and his training, constantly pushing himself to go beyond his limits. He believes strongly that one must acknowledge their own strengths and weaknesses and work to improve what one can to make up for what one cannot, something he learned from his father.[^20] For all his carefree nature, Guy is a very responsible and selfless man. As one who strives to live every day of his life with no regrets, he commits himself entirely to whatever goal is presented towards him. To which, he is more than willing to make a self-sacrifice with no hesitance if he believes it is for the greater good. To which, ever the optimistic, he is not one to linger in self-pity, as even after his career as a shinobi ended, he instead focused on what he could still take part in and make the most of it. In the anime, it was shown that even after gaining effective use of ninjutsu and genjutsu, he makes it a personal choice to rely solely on taijutsu whenever possible, even preferring unconvincing disguises rather than the [Transformation Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Transformation_Technique "Transformation Technique").[^21] He prefers weapons without blades.[^3]

![Guy disciplining Lee, to be followed by manly tears.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/4/46/Strong_Fist.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150129145542)

Guy disciplining Lee, to be followed by manly tears.

Guy can be serious at times despite his odd mannerisms, usually when engaging an enemy who threatens Konoha or one of his comrades.[^22] But even his serious moments can be undermined by one of his eccentricities: Guy has faced [Kisame Hoshigaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kisame_Hoshigaki "Kisame Hoshigaki") on three separate occasions, yet on each occasion he's been fairly confident that he never met Kisame before - Guy is aware that he has a poor memory for faces.[^23] From observing the vibrancy of youth that his students possess, Guy may, in a moment of thoughtful self-reflection, wonder if his own youth has passed him by, only to immediately dismiss this as impossible because youth can never fade; if his student, Lee, should break one of his tenets of conduct, Guy will discipline him with a physical strike, only to then immediately start crying at the realisation of the harm he's inflicted and the well-intentioned passion that led Lee astray in the first place.[^24]

Guy refers to himself as "Konoha's Sublime [Green/Blue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ao_\(color\) "wikipedia:Ao (color)") Beast of Prey" (木ノ葉の気高き碧い猛獣, *Konoha no Kedakaki Aoi Mōjū*). Like many of Guy's traits, the moniker may be fitting. Lee wholeheartedly approves of Guy, modelling his own appearance and personality after Guy's so that he can become as beautiful a man as Guy is. For these similarities and their common backgrounds, Guy is in turn dedicated to Lee, doing all in his power to help Lee achieve his dream of becoming a master of taijutsu. Their almost father-son bond is so powerful as to sometimes be disturbing to others.[^24] In later years, Guy even developed a similar relationship with Lee's son [Metal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Metal "Metal"), becoming like a surrogate second father/uncle to him.[^25] Neji and Tenten, though they also benefit from Guy's teachings, are only willing to join in on Guy and Lee's antics up to a point, and will either rein them in or leave them to their devices if their patience has been exhausted.

![Guy and Kakashi competing in their usual "contests".](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/82/Kakashi_and_Guy_racing.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230408185345)

Guy and Kakashi competing in their usual "contests".

Kakashi shares Neji and Tenten's reservations with Guy, and is often only an unwilling or indifferent participant in their contests. Rather than discourage Guy, this further motivates him, as he wants to defeat Kakashi's "cool, hip, and trendy" personality. There is no animosity to their rivalry, however, and Kakashi in fact considers Guy to be one of his closest and most reliable friends: they work well together in the field,[^26] can understand what the other is thinking without anything being said,[^27] and are concerned for each other's well-being.[^28] At the start of Part I, Kakashi's score is 50 wins and 49 losses, though even when Guy takes the lead the score difference remains the same; considering that their competitions range from eating contests to Rock, Paper, Scissors, the score isn't truly representative of anything.

Guy is a tall and well-muscled man. He has fair skin, a strong jawline, a somewhat large nose, and black hair. He is most noticeable for his shiny bowl-style haircut and thick eyebrows; [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki") affectionately calls him "Master Intense Brows" (激眉先生, *Gekimayu-sensei*, *English TV:* Bushier Brows Sensei). He wears a green jumpsuit, orange-striped leg warmers, and the standard Konoha [flak jacket](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Flak_jacket "Flak jacket"), which he normally leaves unzipped. His red [forehead protector](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Forehead_protector "Forehead protector") is worn around his waist like a belt. Guy's appearance has not changed substantially during his ninja career, though as a [genin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genin "Genin") his jumpsuit was sleeveless and he wore a scarf around his neck. Also as a child, he had bushy hair reaching his chin,[^29] He also later swaps his flack jacket for the redesigned Konoha model. After the Fourth Great Shinobi War, his right leg is in a cast because of the damage he received during the war.

## Abilities

Despite a poor start in his ninja career, Might Guy's potential growth was openly acknowledged by the famous [Sakumo Hatake](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sakumo_Hatake "Sakumo Hatake").[^30] He ultimately proved himself a late-bloomer, becoming recognised as one of Konoha's strongest ninja ever, considered to have had at one point unrivalled strength in the village.[^31] His extreme power has been acknowledged by the likes of [Itachi Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Itachi_Uchiha "Itachi Uchiha"), warning the entire [Akatsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki "Akatsuki") not to underestimate him, and even Kakashi, who completely trusted Guy to aid him in battle. By the end of the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War"), Kakashi acknowledged that Guy has surpassed him. During which, he fought off the Six-Tails alone and with his fullest capacities unlocked, nearly defeated [Madara Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Madara_Uchiha "Madara Uchiha") as the Ten-Tails' jinchūriki (who compared Guy's performance to the First Hokage).[^32] [^33]

Despite his permanent damage from the Fourth Shinobi World War officially ending his ninja career, after continued rehab,[^25] Guy was able to adjust to this handicap to remain as powerful as ever. Ultimately, Guy became recognised as a legendary shinobi.[^34]

### Taijutsu

![Guy punches an opponent through a wall.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/1/1f/Guy_punches_Oto.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150221070359)

Guy punches an opponent through a wall.

Because Guy had little talent in ninjutsu and genjutsu as a child, he dedicated himself to perfecting his [taijutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Taijutsu "Taijutsu"). By adulthood, Guy is a taijutsu master with advanced skill in several styles and likewise equal knowledge in styles that he does not. He specialises in [Strong Fist](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Strong_Fist "Strong Fist") style to effectively adjust his attack patterns and battle tactics to best handle the situation with various unorthodox yet effective strikes. Ultimately, his fighting skills allow him to rarely rely on weapons and even acknowledged by Madara himself as unsurpassed in physical combat.[^35] After losing usage of his right foot, Guy's style became more stationary, balancing on his left leg and positioning himself so his natural weight works with him. He will use his remaining limbs to battle, even strike with his right knee, and employ flips and handsprings to move about quickly.

Guy conditioned himself to the absolute peak of physical prowess by constantly challenging himself through various arduous training means. Without the aid of chakra enhancement, Guy can perform various seemingly inhuman feats. With a single hand, he can casually plough opponents deep into a rock wall, and with even more effort, launch the foe clear through it and a great distance away.[^36] Likewise, his sheer speed is consistently able to attack an opponent before they are fully aware of his presence, if at all.[^37] His stamina and recovery rate allows him to continue fighting for extremely long periods of time or return to battle-ready in extremely short periods of time.

#### Eight Gates

![Guy opening all eight gates.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/26/Guy%27s_Eight_Inner_Gates.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150709142413)

Guy opening all eight gates.

Taught by his father at a young age, Guy can utilise any and all of the Eight Inner Gates.[^38] Guy's mastery can instantly open up the first seven gates, without the need of opening previous gates. With each gate he opens, different physical and mental attributes are unlocked to their fullest potential, enhancing Guy's physical, mental, and chakra capacities by considerable amounts. Consequently, his body can take on more strain the more gates he opens, potentially causing major damage the more he moves and longer he keeps them open, with using [all eight gates](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eight_Gates_Released_Formation "Eight Gates Released Formation") will near-certainly cause eventual death. For this reason, Guy only opens a single gate as a last resort in order to protect something or someone precious. At the same time, Guy's extreme training allows him to handle opening the gates for longer periods of time, able to endure the sixth gate with only noticeable exhaustion, as well as resist the after-effects of the seventh gate to continue fighting for a short period. By the Fourth Shinobi World War, Guy became able to withstand using the seventh gate repeatedly before succumbing to its effects as well as immediately adapt to the immense pain of the eighth gate.[^39]

![Guy's Evening Elephant, attacking his opponent from several directions.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/e/e8/Sekiz%C5%8D.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150723115343)

Guy's Evening Elephant, attacking his opponent from several directions.

Guy created several different techniques that he can perform once opening certain gates: with the first gate, he can use the pile-driving [Front Lotus](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Front_Lotus "Front Lotus"); with the third gate, he overwhelms opponents with punches through the [Reverse Lotus](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Reverse_Lotus "Reverse Lotus"); with the sixth gate, Guy can use the [Morning Peacock](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Morning_Peacock "Morning Peacock") to unleash a series of rapid punches that ignite through air friction; with the seventh gate, he can use the [Daytime Tiger](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Daytime_Tiger "Daytime Tiger") to launch concentrated air pressure at opponents that violently expands on contact for what he calls a one-hit kill; with the eighth gate, he can use the [Evening Elephant](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Evening_Elephant "Evening Elephant") to punch from a distance powerful shockwaves of air. His ultimate technique with all eight gates is [Night Guy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Night_Guy "Night Guy"), a single kick of such speed that the space around him is distorted. Night Guy, though it almost kills Madara Uchiha as the Ten-Tails' jinchūriki, is responsible for the irreparable damage to Guy's right leg.[^40]

### Ninjutsu

![Guy summoning Ningame.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/25/Guy_summons_Ningame_Midair.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20120218214716)

Guy summoning Ningame.

While rarely performing any ninjutsu or genjutsu, Guy is still capable of them. He can [summon](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning_Technique "Summoning Technique") tortoises, such as [Ningame](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ningame "Ningame").[^41] Additionally, he has a chakra nature of [fire](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fire "Fire") and [lightning](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning "Lightning").[^4] Guy is at least knowledgeable about other ninjutsu, recognising the [Four Red Yang Formation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Four_Red_Yang_Formation "Four Red Yang Formation") and speaking informatively about its power and difficulty.[^42] He is also able to dispel a [high-level genjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Temple_of_Nirvana_Technique "Temple of Nirvana Technique").

### Other Skills

![Guy wielding his nunchaku.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/4/4f/S%C5%8Dsh%C5%ABga.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20131212123731)

Guy wielding his nunchaku.

Guy is a master in various weapon styles, having trained Tenten extensively in using her various [ninja tools](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ninja_tool "Ninja tool").[^43] His preferred weapon in battle is [nunchaku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nunchaku "wikipedia:Nunchaku"),[^44] with which he utilises the [Sōshūga](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/S%C5%8Dsh%C5%ABga "Sōshūga") fighting style, being able to smash through boulders [^45] and trade blows with [Obito](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Obito "Obito") 's [gunbai](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gunbai "Gunbai").[^46] He at times makes herbal remedies to help Lee with his training, though their benefits are unclear.[^47] In his mostly-one-sided rivalry with Kakashi, Guy devises a way to predict an opponent's movements by focusing on their feet, effectively negating the [Sharingan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sharingan "Sharingan") 's eye-contact-based [genjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genjutsu "Genjutsu").[^48]

## Part I

### Chūnin Exams

Main article: [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams_\(Arc\) "Chūnin Exams (Arc)") After holding them back for a year of training, Guy allows his students on [Team Guy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Guy "Team Guy") ([Rock Lee](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rock_Lee "Rock Lee"), [Neji Hyūga](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Neji_Hy%C5%ABga "Neji Hyūga"), and [Tenten](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tenten "Tenten")) to enter the [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams "Chūnin Exams") being held in Konoha. He worries, however, that they may not behave, so he has [Ningame](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ningame "Ningame") keep an eye on them as they prepare to take the exams. Ningame catches Lee trying to use the [Front Lotus](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Front_Lotus "Front Lotus") on [Sasuke Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Uchiha "Sasuke Uchiha"), defying Guy's instructions to only use it while protecting something precious. Guy is summoned to reprimand Lee: he punches Lee as punishment, and then embraces him out of regret for punching him; Sasuke and his fellow [Team 7](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Kakashi "Team Kakashi") members are disturbed by the display. Aware that Team 7 are [Kakashi Hatake](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi_Hatake "Kakashi Hatake") 's students, he asks them how Kakashi is. He apologises to them for Lee's behaviour and tells them all to head to the exams' first stage before he departs.

Team Guy advances to the preliminary stage of one-on-one fights held before the finals, and Guy watches his students' matches and cheers them on. When Tenten is badly abused after her defeat by her opponent, [Temari](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Temari "Temari"), Guy stops Lee from getting into a fight with Temari and her [Suna](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Suna "Suna") -teammates. When Lee is matched against [Gaara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gaara "Gaara"), Guy tells him an important observation he's made: the gourd that Gaara has on his back may be significant. Although Lee takes this information with enthusiasm, it doesn't help him against Gaara's [Shield of Sand](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shield_of_Sand "Shield of Sand") once the fight begins. For this reason, Guy gives Lee permission to remove his ankle weights, thus making him fast enough to bypass Gaara's shield.

![Guy saves Lee from Gaara.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/4/45/Guy_Protecting_Lee.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150723123537)

Guy saves Lee from Gaara.

The increased speed is insufficient against Gaara, for which reason Guy allows Lee to use the Front Lotus. The stress the Front Lotus does to Lee's body provides a brief opening for Gaara to escape unharmed, which he takes. Lee, meanwhile, still experiences the after-effects of using the Front Lotus and has difficulty fighting off Gaara's attacks. Guy signals to Lee to use the [Reverse Lotus](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Reverse_Lotus "Reverse Lotus"). Kakashi expresses disappointment in Guy when he realises what he's allowing Lee to do, but Guy defends his decision, as becoming a splendid ninja is the most important thing in Lee's life, and defeating Gaara will help him achieve that. The Reverse Lotus ends up failing, as Gaara uses the gourd on his back to absorb the attack. Lee is unable to move afterward, allowing Gaara to start crushing his arms and legs. Guy intervenes, forcing Gaara to relent.

Although Lee has lost the match and has fallen unconscious, his body gets up through muscle memory so that he can keep fighting. Guy tearfully embraces him, assuring him he's already a splendid ninja. [Medic-nin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Medic-nin "Medic-nin") come to check on Lee, but from a cursory examination of his injuries, they inform Guy that the damage the Reverse Lotus did to his body is so severe that Lee won't be able to continue life as a ninja. Guy is devastated by the news, blaming himself for not stopping Lee when he had the chance and for teaching him the Reverse Lotus in the first place. A month later, on the day before the Chūnin Exams' finals are to be held, Guy visits Lee in the [Konoha Hospital](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Hospital "Konoha Hospital"). He finds Gaara in Lee's room, trying to finish him off. Guy threatens to admit Gaara to the hospital as an inpatient if he doesn't leave, which Gaara complies with.

Guy takes Lee to see the final matches the next day. Because of Lee's injuries, most of the first round matches have completed by the time they reach the stadium where the finals are being held; Neji lost in his fight with [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki"). While watching the fight between Sasuke and Gaara, both Guy and Lee are surprised by the speed Sasuke has copied from Lee using his [Sharingan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sharingan "Sharingan"), though it makes sense to Guy once he sees Sasuke using Kakashi's [Chidori](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chidori "Chidori").

They attend her funeral in Suna a few days later before heading back to Konoha. Kakashi is too tired to walk due to his use of Kamui, necessitating that Guy carry him. Guy does so by giving Kakashi a piggyback ride and challenges their students to keep up with him; although Lee is fascinated by this training regimen, the others are deeply disturbed.

[Killer B](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Killer_B "Killer B") emerges from the falls and informs them that it is not a puffer fish, but rather [Kisame Hoshigaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kisame_Hoshigaki "Kisame Hoshigaki") of Akatsuki, who is trying to escape with information about B and Naruto. After quickly incapacitating B and Aoba, Kisame attacks Guy and starts swimming to the ocean to send out the intel he's gathered. They're unable to move as quickly as Kisame can swim, so B flings Guy across the island in order to head Kisame off; this isn't enough, so Guy [summons](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning "Summoning") [Ningame](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ningame "Ningame") in mid-flight for him to use as a jumping off point to launch him the rest of the way. When he catches up, Kisame has already passed the intel to a shark, the identity of which he hides with [one thousand more](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water_Release:_A_Thousand_Feeding_Sharks "Water Release: A Thousand Feeding Sharks") so that the shark can escape the island. Guy tries to destroy the sharks with [Morning Peacock](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Morning_Peacock "Morning Peacock"), but there are too many and he's forced to open the seventh gate. Kisame tries to stop Guy with his [Great Shark Bullet Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Shark_Bullet_Technique "Great Shark Bullet Technique"), which Guy counters with [Daytime Tiger](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Daytime_Tiger "Daytime Tiger").

![Kisame, in death, finally gets Guy to remember him.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/1/1f/Kisame_committing_suicide.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20151004044001)

The Daytime Tiger is unimpeded by the Great Shark Bullet and directly connects with Kisame, creating a giant aftershock that not only defeats him but also destroys the remaining sharks. As Guy collects the intel, Kisame states his surprise that Guy could withhold a jutsu as powerful as the Daytime Tiger until their third encounter. Since Guy doesn't remember their previous two encounters, Kisame attempts to keep fighting, prompting Guy to knock him out. He takes Kisame back to the others so that Aoba can [interrogate](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Psycho_Mind_Transmission "Psycho Mind Transmission") him. Rather than be used to betray his allies, Kisame breaks free of his confinements through sheer will, traps himself in a [water prison](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water_prison "Water prison") so that Guy and the others can't stop him, and summons sharks to eat him alive. Guy deeply respects how and why Kisame chose to die, so vows to remember him for the rest of his life.

They afterwards examine the intel that Kisame was trying to send, which turns out to be booby-trapped: they are each caught in a water prison and are trapped alongside a shark. Guy finally succumbs to the rigors of his earlier use of the Eight Gates and can't fight off his shark, requiring that the others rescue him. Another shark, meanwhile, is able to escape with Kisame's intel.

Guy and Kakashi want to help him, but must deal with the others first: while Kakashi deals with the jinchūriki, Guy repels the [Six-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Six-Tails "Six-Tails") ' [Wisdom Wolf Decay](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wisdom_Wolf_Decay "Wisdom Wolf Decay") using Morning Peacock. Naruto, meanwhile, is able to escape the Four-Tails and release it from Tobi's control.

As five [Tailed Beast Balls](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Ball "Tailed Beast Ball") bear down on them, Guy considers opening all Eight Gates. It ends up not being necessary as Naruto, by entering his own Tailed Beast Mode, deflects their attacks and subsequently frees them from Tobi. Tobi is forced to recall the tailed beasts into the [Demonic Statue of the Outer Path](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Statue_of_the_Outer_Path "Demonic Statue of the Outer Path"), which he uses against Naruto, B, Kakashi, and Guy. When they realise what Tobi is doing, Guy and the others focus on destroying the Demonic Statue before it can complete its metamorphosis into the Ten-Tails.

Guy and Kakashi are shocked to see Obito alive.

Tobi defends the Demonic Statue from all of their attacks and, as ever, is himself seemingly impervious to damage, with everything passing through him. After the exchange, Kakashi notices some slight damage to Tobi's mask, which he has a theory about. To test this, he has Guy and Naruto attack Tobi: Guy allows Tobi to absorb the [Nunchaku](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nunchaku "Nunchaku") he's been [using](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/S%C5%8Dsh%C5%ABga "Sōshūga"), creating an opening for Naruto to attack with [Rasengan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rasengan "Rasengan"). Tobi fazes through this, but Kakashi uses his [Kamui](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kamui "Kamui") on the Rasengan, causing Tobi to take damage. This confirms Kakashi's theory that Tobi is also using Kamui and must therefore have the companion [Sharingan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sharingan "Sharingan") to Kakashi's. Tobi confirms this, troubling both Kakashi and Guy because of what this may mean. Nevertheless, this information gives them a way of attacking Tobi, though Kakashi is so distracted in thought that he doesn't join in until Guy brings him back to his senses. Their combined efforts succeed in destroying Tobi's mask. When they see his face, Guy and Kakashi recognise Tobi as [Obito Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Obito_Uchiha "Obito Uchiha").

Naruto notes that the past doesn't change the fact that Obito is now their enemy, which Guy agrees with and uses this to try to help Kakashi recover from the shock of seeing Obito. Obito is soon joined by a reincarnated [Madara Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Madara_Uchiha "Madara Uchiha"), who decides to take Naruto's [Nine-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tails "Nine-Tails") and Killer B's [Eight-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eight-Tails "Eight-Tails") before the Ten-Tails is revived. Guy helps defend them - entrusting Obito to Kakashi - but Madara is too formidable and Guy soon becomes tired. Madara tells them that fighting against him is futile, which Naruto refuses to believe. Guy is moved by Naruto's confidence and attacks Madara with Daytime Tiger, propelling him away and leaving Guy no longer able to move. Naruto and the others team up to try and destroy the Demonic Statue, but they fail and the Ten-Tails is revived.

![Guy celebrates Obito's defeat.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/4/4a/Guy_celebrates.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230409021932)

Guy celebrates Obito's defeat.

Battle with the Ten-Tails interrupts the process before Guy is fully restored, requiring that Kakashi support him. The Ten-Tails attacks again, but they are saved by the arrival of the rest of the Allied Shinobi Forces; [Shizune](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shizune "Shizune") heals Guy's injuries. When the Alliance restrains the Ten-Tails, Guy and Lee open the fifth gate in order to join the attack against it, but the Ten-Tails breaks free at the last moment and repels them. When Neji is killed in the following battle, Lee mourns for him; Guy comforts him by telling him that Neji will live on so long as they continue to fight for the cause that he gave his life for. Naruto gives them [version 1](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Version_1 "Version 1") -like cloaks to protect them and strengthen their attacks, which they and the rest of the Alliance use to remove the Ten-Tails from Obito and Madara's control.

### Kakashi Hiden: Lightning in the Icy Sky

Main article: [Kakashi Hiden: Lightning in the Icy Sky](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi_Hiden:_Lightning_in_the_Icy_Sky "Kakashi Hiden: Lightning in the Icy Sky") A year after the end of the war, Konoha is hired to provide security for the maiden voyage of the [Tobishachimaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tobishachimaru "Tobishachimaru"). When Kakashi catches him, Guy claims to have no interest in flying and that he merely wants to demonstrate that he can continue to be a ninja despite his leg injury. Kakashi doesn't believe him, but lets him stay and finds a place for them to ride out the trip as stowaways. During the voyage, the Tobishachimaru is hijacked by the [Ryūha Armament Alliance](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ry%C5%ABha_Armament_Alliance "Ryūha Armament Alliance"), who demand that Konoha release their leader from the [Blood Prison](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Blood_Prison "Blood Prison"); they will start killing hostages if Konoha takes too long and will blow up the Tobishachimaru if their demands aren't met.

While Kakashi confronts the hijackers to try and rescue the hostages, Guy assists Kakashi's [ninken](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ninken "Ninken") with locating the [explosive tags](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Explosive_tag "Explosive tag") spread around the Tobishachimaru. Once all the explosive tags are dealt with, Guy also confronts the hijackers, drawing [Rahyō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rahy%C5%8D "Rahyō") into battle. Even though his leg is in a cast, Guy remains as fast as ever, allowing him to evade Rahyō's [ice](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ice "Ice") -augmented physical attacks. He is even able to deliver some successful kicks, though has difficulty disguising the pain it causes him. He brings out his [Sōshūga](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/S%C5%8Dsh%C5%ABga "Sōshūga") in order to engage Rahyō, but watching Sōshūga's rapid swinging triggers his motion-sickness. Rather than be debilitated by it, Guy turns it into a fighting style he calls [Seasickness Fist](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Seasickness_Fist "Seasickness Fist"). While Rahyō struggles to hit Guy, Kakashi is meanwhile able to free himself from his restraints and fights [Kahyō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kahy%C5%8D "Kahyō"). Kahyō creates a large hole in the Tobishachimaru that starts sucking out the hostages. Kakashi catches Guy before he's sucked out too, but cannot maintain his hold on the ship's hull. Despite Guy's plea for Kakashi to save himself, he loses his grip and they start falling.

As they fall through the air towards the ground far below, Guy makes eye contact with Kakashi and together they accept the fact that they're about to die. They and the hostages are saved by [Sai](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sai "Sai"), who takes Guy and the hostages to safety while Kakashi returns back to the Tobishachimaru. Guy returns to Konoha and is summoned before [Tsunade](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tsunade "Tsunade"). He tries to come up with a plausible reason for why he was aboard the Tobishachimaru, but she doesn't believe him. She's forced to take a call from the [Third Tsuchikage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Tsuchikage "Third Tsuchikage") before she can punish him, so she tells Guy to sketch the faces of the hijackers. A few months later, after Kakashi has become the Sixth [Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage "Hokage"), Guy is training with Lee when he sees Kakashi reading a letter from Kahyō. Guy pretends disgust by their relationship, but states he won't let it interfere with his duties as Kakashi's adviser.

![Guy, Kakashi, and Mirai head to the Land of Hot Water.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/5/57/Mirai_Joins_Kakashi_and_Guy.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20210420154757)

Guy, Kakashi, and Mirai head to the Land of Hot Water.

On the day of the [Five Kage Summit](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Five_Kage_Summit "Five Kage Summit") in Konoha, Kakashi and Guy met up, before departing from the village with their escort [Mirai Sarutobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mirai_Sarutobi "Mirai Sarutobi"). Later, the group arrived in the [Land of Hot Water](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_Hot_Water "Land of Hot Water"). There, they found annual festival/competition where a town was split between two beliefs on its origins; some believing that it was a cat spirit and others believed it to be a dog spirit. While initially enjoying the festival, they found [Kiba Inuzuka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kiba_Inuzuka "Kiba Inuzuka") and his girlfriend [Tamaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tamaki "Tamaki"), a cat breeder having begun to join in the feud on different sides do to their respective animal expertise. As their couple's argument began to make the town grow into a frenzy, Mirai stopped the chaos by producing a [genjutsu of a Cat-Dog spirit](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genjutsu:_Ghost_Fire "Genjutsu: Ghost Fire"), telling the people to stop the feuding. When Guy believed the genjutsu to be a demon and attacked it, he accidentally knocked down the main wall that split the town, convincing the villagers that it was a sign for them to truly come together.

During a stop an inn, they met a young orphan named [Tatsumi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tatsumi "Tatsumi"), who despite having no money, was determined to visit all the hot springs in the land in memory of her late mother. At Mirai's request, it was agreed that the little girl can join them on their own trip. Later during the trip, Tatsumi and Mirai snuck off in the hopes of a nearby hot spring rumoured to let people talk with loved ones from the past. Kakashi and Guy soon found them, learning that the rumoured hot spring was a ruse conceived by remnants of the near-forgotten [Jashin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jashin "Jashin") cult to restore their former glory and power. Guy, along with Kakashi and Mirai, swiftly defeated the fanatics. Later, it was revealed that Kakashi and Guy's vacation was in fact a cover for their working with the Land of Hot Water to uncover the truth behind the missing girls.

Main articles: [Boruto: Naruto the Movie](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Boruto:_Naruto_the_Movie "Boruto: Naruto the Movie") and [Versus Momoshiki Arc](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Versus_Momoshiki_Arc "Versus Momoshiki Arc") Guy watches the genin battle it out in the final round of the [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams "Chūnin Exams") in Konoha alongside Kakashi and [Iruka Umino](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Iruka_Umino "Iruka Umino").

![Guy spars with Metal Lee.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/c/ce/Might_Guy_vs._Metal_Lee.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20210601183016)

Guy spars with Metal Lee.

In the anime, as [Metal Lee](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Metal_Lee "Metal Lee") continued to struggle with his anxiety of too much attention on him, Guy offered to let the youth listen to his lecture at the Academy. During which, Guy voiced the importance of facing one's problems if they hope to get past them, as he had to deal with his damaged leg. Demonstrating his point, Guy decided to have a sparring match against Metal and Iwabee in a two-on-one handicap. Guy decisively won, albeit it was even easier from Metal not focusing properly from so many people watching. Later, Lee decided to challenge his son to a match with many spectators, telling Metal that he had to win if he wants to learn the Eight Gates. During the match, Guy watch in pride as Metal began understanding the words he and Lee told him as he began using his anxiety to fuel his performance and even momentarily access the first gate to win the challenge. The three then tearfully hugged in joy at the proud moment, much to the spectators' discomfort.

## Trivia

| ![Springtime of Youth](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/3/39/Youth_symbol.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/40?cb=20130120020002)  Springtime of Youth | The [*Springtime of Youth* wiki](https://springtimeofyouth.fandom.com/wiki/ "w:c:springtimeofyouth") has an article about this topic:  **[Might Guy](https://springtimeofyouth.fandom.com/wiki/Might_Guy "w:c:springtimeofyouth:Might Guy")** |
| --- | --- |

- In the *Shonen Jump* magazine, Viz rendered Guy's name as "Mighty Guy" in some of the earlier chapters; this was changed back to "Maito Gai" in most of the graphic novel stories, and later changed to "Might Guy" when the guidebooks were released.
- The name "Might Guy" is a joke aimed at the "hot-headed hero" archetype that appears in many movies and anime.
- Guy's appearance and mannerisms seem to be based off those of [Bruce Lee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruce_Lee "wikipedia:Bruce Lee"). He even wields nunchaku and wears a jumpsuit.
- Studio Pierrot's Settei sheets of Guy show that he was 141 cm in *[Naruto Shippūden the Movie: The Lost Tower](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Shipp%C5%ABden_the_Movie:_The_Lost_Tower "Naruto Shippūden the Movie: The Lost Tower")*, 151 cm during the [Third Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Shinobi_World_War "Third Shinobi World War"), and 170 cm during the [Nine-Tailed Demon Fox's Attack](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tailed_Demon_Fox%27s_Attack "Nine-Tailed Demon Fox's Attack").
- According to the databook(s):
	- Guy's hobbies are repeated side-steps, and hitting [focus mitts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Focus_mitt "wikipedia:Focus mitt").
		- Guy wishes for a rematch with [Kakashi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi "Kakashi").
		- Guy's favourite foods are super-spicy [curry rice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_curry "wikipedia:Japanese curry") and [curry udon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Udon "wikipedia:Udon"), though he finds it impossible to dislike food.
		- Guy's favourite phrase is "Overflowing youth!!" (とび出せ青春!!, *Tobidase seishun!!*).
		- Guy has completed 788 official missions in total: 86 D-rank, 270 C-rank, 210 B-rank, 199 A-rank, 23 S-rank.
- In *[Ultimate Ninja Heroes](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ultimate_Ninja_Heroes "Ultimate Ninja Heroes")*, Guy calls himself the "Beautiful Savage of the Leaf Village".
- In [*Naruto: Shippūden* episode 359](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/The_Night_of_the_Tragedy "The Night of the Tragedy"), Guy states that he is an agnostic of the [Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uchiha "Uchiha") 's patron deity, which is located in the [Naka Shrine](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naka_Shrine "Naka Shrine").
