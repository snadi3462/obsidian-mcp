**Hiruzen Sarutobi** (猿飛ヒルゼン, *Sarutobi Hiruzen*) was the **Third [Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage "Hokage")** (三代目火影, *Sandaime Hokage*, *literally meaning:* Third Fire Shadow) of [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure"). A disciple of the village's two previous Hokage, Hiruzen was a powerful ninja, hailed as a " [God of Shinobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/God_of_Shinobi "God of Shinobi") " and also renowned as **The Professor** (プロフェッサー, *Purofessā*) for extensive and immense mastery of various ninjutsu. Though he was only ever directly the teacher of the [Sannin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sannin "Sannin"), generations of Konoha shinobi benefited from his wisdom during his lifetime.

## Background

Hiruzen was born the son of [Sasuke Sarutobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Sarutobi "Sasuke Sarutobi"). The [Sarutobi clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sarutobi_clan "Sarutobi clan") was one of the first clans to settle in the newly-created [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure"), where Hiruzen struck up a friendship with the [Shimura clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shimura_clan "Shimura clan") 's [Danzō Shimura](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Danz%C5%8D_Shimura "Danzō Shimura"). Hiruzen was part of the first generation of ninja produced by Konoha as he, [Homura Mitokado](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Homura_Mitokado "Homura Mitokado"), and [Koharu Utatane](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Koharu_Utatane "Koharu Utatane") were placed under the tutelage of [Tobirama Senju](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tobirama_Senju "Tobirama Senju"). Hiruzen early on displayed prodigious talent in the ninja arts, earning him additional training from [Hashirama Senju](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hashirama_Senju "Hashirama Senju"), the First [Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage "Hokage").[^11] At the same time, Danzō became jealous of Hiruzen's skills and growing recognition, starting a one-sided rivalry in an effort to surpass him. At one point Hashirama and Tobirama talked to Hiruzen of the importance of believing in and protecting those in the village, telling him he would one day be Hokage.[^12]

![Hiruzen is appointed Hokage.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/24/Hiruzen_New_Hokage.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150214200525)

Hiruzen is appointed Hokage.

During the [First Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/First_Shinobi_World_War "First Shinobi World War"), Tobirama, by that time the Second Hokage, led a [team](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Escort_Unit "Escort Unit") consisting of Hiruzen, Homura, Koharu, Danzō, [Kagami Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kagami_Uchiha "Kagami Uchiha"), and [Torifu Akimichi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Torifu_Akimichi "Torifu Akimichi"). During a mission they started to be pursued by [Kumogakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kumogakure "Kumogakure") 's [Kinkaku Force](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kinkaku_Force "Kinkaku Force"), an enemy that somebody would need to distract and inevitably die at the hands of in order for the rest of the team to escape. Hiruzen volunteered to be the decoy, boasting that, as the strongest of their group, he stood the best chance of surviving the encounter; nevertheless, he asked Danzō to look after the others. Tobirama volunteered himself instead, and before going to his death he appointed Hiruzen as Third Hokage.

In the anime, Hiruzen permitted Danzō to create [Root](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Root "Root") - a more ruthless subdivision of the [Anbu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Anbu "Anbu") under Danzō's exclusive control - shortly after he became Hokage in order to balance the shortcomings of his empathetic rule. For years he had ignored Root's questionable foreign ops, its abduction of children, and the actions it took against Konoha personnel, Hiruzen included.[^13]

At some point, Hiruzen married [Biwako](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Biwako "Biwako") and they had a son, [Asuma](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Asuma "Asuma"), and at least one other child who became an Anbu.[^14] Hiruzen also started leading his own team of new [Academy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Academy "Academy") graduates consisting of [Jiraiya](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jiraiya "Jiraiya"), [Orochimaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Orochimaru "Orochimaru"), and [Tsunade](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tsunade "Tsunade").[^15] Although he was close with all of his students, Hiruzen had the highest hopes for [Orochimaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Orochimaru "Orochimaru"), a genius like himself who, after the deaths of his parents, was only further motivated to have a deep impact on the world.[^16] Hiruzen led Konoha's forces through the [Second](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Shinobi_World_War "Second Shinobi World War") and [Third Shinobi World Wars](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Shinobi_World_War "Third Shinobi World War"); in the anime, his choosing to end the Third Shinobi World War by signing a peace treaty with [Iwagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Iwagakure "Iwagakure") despite the losses Konoha suffered incensed Danzō, prompting Hiruzen to step down as Hokage against the village's protests.[^17]

Hiruzen had long hoped that Orochimaru would succeed him as Hokage but, despite his best efforts, he could never convince him of the [Will of Fire](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Will_of_Fire "Will of Fire"): that Konoha was a family that the Hokage had to risk everything to keep safe.[^18] Unable to choose Orochimaru, Hiruzen considered Jiraiya as his successor, but Jiraiya did not believe he was responsible enough.[^19] Hiruzen ultimately settled on Jiraiya's former student, [Minato Namikaze](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Minato_Namikaze "Minato Namikaze"), who had proven himself time and again during the Third Shinobi World War.[^20] Hiruzen assisted Minato with the transition of power by advising him on how to handle individuals and other village matters. He even retained some of his own [Anbu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Anbu "Anbu"), several of which he assigned as security while [Kushina Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kushina_Uzumaki "Kushina Uzumaki"), the [jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki "Jinchūriki") of the [Nine-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tails "Nine-Tails"), gave birth.

![Hiruzen leads a charge against the Nine-Tails.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/a/a8/Group_Hiruzen_vs_Kurama.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160129150732)

Despite their precautions, the Nine-Tails escaped and [attacked the village](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tailed_Demon_Fox%27s_Attack "Nine-Tailed Demon Fox's Attack"). Hiruzen led the village's defence until Minato arrived to remove the Nine-Tails to a safe distance. Hiruzen followed and, cut off by the [Adamantine Sealing Chains](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Adamantine_Sealing_Chains "Adamantine Sealing Chains"), could only watch as Minato and Kushina gave their lives to [seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Dead_Demon_Consuming_Seal "Dead Demon Consuming Seal") the Nine-Tails into their newborn son, [Naruto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto "Naruto"). Minato did not have time to tell Hiruzen how the Nine-Tails had escaped, instead using the final moments of his life to ask Konoha, via Hiruzen, to think of Naruto as a hero who saved the village and not the container of the monster that killed so many. Hiruzen did so, but the villagers proved unable to follow Minato's wishes, prompting Hiruzen, now the village's Hokage once more, to outlaw any mention of the Nine-Tails, an effort to protect Naruto from their misguided hate.[^21] He also gave Naruto his mother's surname in order to hide his relation to Minato from Minato's enemies.[^22]

![Hiruzen allowing Orochimaru to escape.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/1/17/Orochimaru_leaves.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150223115942)

Hiruzen eventually started investigating a number of disappearances throughout Konoha. In the anime, he followed a lead provided by [Kakashi Hatake](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi_Hatake "Kakashi Hatake"),[^13] which ultimately led him to discover that Orochimaru was behind it all. Hiruzen was dismayed not only by Orochimaru's role or that he was experimenting on those he kidnapped, but also his reasons: to create an [Eternal Youth and Immortality Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Youth_and_Immortality_Technique "Eternal Youth and Immortality Technique"). He attempted to apprehend Orochimaru, but could not bring himself to strike down his favourite student and allowed him to escape.[^23] From examining what Orochimaru left behind, a [young boy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yamato "Yamato") imbued with Hashirama Senju's [Wood Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Release "Wood Release") was discovered, and over the years the boy became one of Hiruzen's top Anbu operatives. (In the anime, the boy was previously discovered by Danzō, and Hiruzen negotiated the boy's release into Hiruzen's care.) [^24]

![Itachi begs Hiruzen to protect Sasuke.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/3/39/Itachi_talks_to_Hiruzen.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150823090159)

Itachi begs Hiruzen to protect Sasuke.

Because Minato never had the opportunity to explain the cause of the Nine-Tails' attack, Konoha's leadership came to suspect a member of the [Uchiha clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uchiha_clan "Uchiha clan") was responsible due to the [Sharingan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sharingan "Sharingan") 's ability to control the Nine-Tails. The Uchiha resented this suspicion and began planning a coup d'état, something reported to Hiruzen and the [Konoha Council](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Council "Konoha Council") by [Itachi Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Itachi_Uchiha "Itachi Uchiha"). Hiruzen attempted to peacefully resolve the conflict but negotiations consistently stalled. Danzō eventually stepped in without Hiruzen's knowledge and ordered Itachi to [slaughter the Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uchiha_Clan_Downfall "Uchiha Clan Downfall").[^25] Hiruzen only learned of the mission afterwards when Itachi reported its successful completion. Before needing to publicly leave the village as a criminal in order to hide Konoha's involvement in the Uchiha's destruction, Itachi asked Hiruzen to look after his younger brother, [Sasuke](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke "Sasuke"), the only Uchiha he could not bring himself to kill. Hiruzen agreed.[^26] In the anime, Hiruzen also promised not to change the [village barrier](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sensing_Barrier "Sensing Barrier") 's formula, enabling Itachi to sneak in from time to time to watch Sasuke from afar. Danzō's role in the Uchiha's assassination was not something Hiruzen could abide, and as such he removed Danzō from the [Konoha Council](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Council "Konoha Council") and ordered Root be disbanded.[^27]

About five years after the Nine Tails Fox's attack on the village, Hiruzen had a disagreement with his son Asuma, leading to the latter to leave to pursue his personal interests. In the anime, Hiruzen came to welcome people in a refugee camp to Konoha. Hiruzen was visited by an orphaned refugee [Mugino](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mugino "Mugino"), delivering a gift for gratitude. After anticipating his actions and noticing the young orphan was branded with a [cursed seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Juinjutsu "Juinjutsu"), Hiruzen allowed Mugino to stab him, catching the Kiri-nin watching over Mugino off guard and swiftly subduing them. Hiruzen comforted Mugino and welcomed him to Konoha with open arms whenever he was ready.[^28]

## Personality

Hiruzen is a firm believer in the [Will of Fire](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Will_of_Fire "Will of Fire"), which holds that all the people of Konoha are a family and that the Hokage is chiefly responsible for that family's well-being. Throughout his tenure, Hiruzen lives up to the Will of Fire by being available for Konoha's villagers and shinobi during their time of need, making decisions to the betterment of as many people as possible, and providing his wisdom and guidance whenever he can. In fact, he was considered to embody the Will of Fire more deeply than anyone else.[^29] His delicate, thoughtful approach to not only issues within the village but also his interactions with foreign powers has given Hiruzen the reputation of being soft, something the [Konoha Council](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Council "Konoha Council") spends much of its time trying to rectify. Nevertheless, Hiruzen's kind and pacifist ways are widely valued by Konoha's residents and even his rival [Kage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kage "Kage"), and for those reasons his eventual death is regretted by many. However, Hiruzen was not entirely for his softness as he allowed Danzō to form an Anbu faction of his own, to handle the dark side of Konoha's actions.

Although he loved all of Konoha, Hiruzen held his [students](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sannin "Sannin") especially dear, believing they were the village's future and, as such, must be cultivated and protected. He favoured Orochimaru, seeing in him a prodigy similar to himself whose contributions to the village could someday be greater than his own. Even when Orochimaru increasingly went astray and then finally defected from Konoha, Hiruzen could never abandon his attachments to Orochimaru,[^30] instead blaming himself for not being a better teacher. When he is [reincarnated](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning:_Impure_World_Reincarnation "Summoning: Impure World Reincarnation"), he reflects on his life and finds it similarly flawed: he feels responsible for Danzō's taking on of the village's darkness, believing Danzō would not have turned out so badly had he been more willing to make the difficult decisions; he believes that some of the village's - and even the world's - problems could have been avoided had he led with a firmer hand.[^31] Hiruzen's own son, [Asuma](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Asuma "Asuma"), was defiant towards Hiruzen's philosophies during his youth, but eventually sees the merits in valuing future generations after Hiruzen's death.

Despite his reputation and selflessness, Hiruzen is not without faults: He is somewhat lecherous. While more subtle about than his student, he and Jiraiya bonded over their shared [desire](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Transparent_Escape_Technique "Transparent Escape Technique") to spy on naked women and talking to young girls was his favourite "hobby".[^1] Likewise, he fell victim to Naruto's [Sexy Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sexy_Technique "Sexy Technique") on more than one occasion and suspects he would be defeated by the [Harem Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Harem_Technique "Harem Technique") as well. He often smokes a pipe during his spare time.

The only consistent physical feature Hiruzen has displayed has been his below-average stature, all his other attributes having changed over the years: during his youth he was tan-skinned and had dark brown, spiked hair, adding a small goatee to his look after becoming Hokage; towards the end of his life his skin is lighter, his hair is grey, his face is gaunter, and he has the wrinkles and liver spots of old age. He initially had a single line running vertically under the outer corners of each eye, which stretched down into his face as he aged until they reached his cheeks.

During the First Shinobi World War, he wore grey [shinobi battle armour](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shinobi_battle_armour "Shinobi battle armour") common to the time period, with heavy protection all over his body. He starts wearing much lighter combat attire at some point, consisting of a black jumpsuit, mesh segments over the lower portions of his limbs, and a green [gauntlet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_armour#Individual_armour_parts "wikipedia:Japanese armour") that covered much of his right arm. He also wore an armoured hood with a bandanna-like [forehead protector](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Forehead_protector "Forehead protector") over this, tied with two long straps.[^32] Across the back is written "Third Hokage" which is replaced with the kanji for "fire" (火, *hi*) during his brief retirement.[^33] When not in combat, he usually wears the official uniform — the customary hat and haori with a red, full-length kimono that is tied using a white sash.

## Abilities

![Hiruzen using the Shadow Clone Technique.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/f/f4/Hiruzen%27s_shadow_clones.PNG/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240908035002)

Hiruzen using the Shadow Clone Technique.

Hiruzen was a natural prodigy, hailed as a " [God of Shinobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/God_of_Shinobi "God of Shinobi") " and was the strongest shinobi produced from his clan.[^34] His talents quickly surpassed that of his teacher, the [Second Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Hokage "Second Hokage"), which made him chosen as the next Hokage at a young age.[^4] He became the most powerful of [Kage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kage "Kage") during his lifetime,[^1] and was once considered the strongest Hokage in history.[^6] While his battle prowess deteriorated greatly due to his old age and ultimately contributed to his death, he was still able to fend off [Orochimaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Orochimaru "Orochimaru") along with the combined might of the [reincarnated](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning:_Impure_World_Reincarnation "Summoning: Impure World Reincarnation") First and Second Hokage (although their power was diminished from the reincarnation). Ultimately, he single-handedly [sealed away](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Dead_Demon_Consuming_Seal "Dead Demon Consuming Seal") the two past Hokage as well as Orochimaru's arms. Orochimaru openly believed that had Hiruzen been even ten years younger, the Third Hokage would've killed his former student.[^35]

### Chakra and Physical Prowess

![Hiruzen in hand-to-hand combat.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/1/1d/Tobirama_attacks_Hiruzen.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240908034934)

Hiruzen in hand-to-hand combat.

In the anime, Hiruzen was said to have immense [chakra](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra "Chakra"),[^36] and even in his old age, his chakra was strong enough to crack stone upon moulding it.[^32] His reserves diminished in later years as even splitting his chakra between any more than two [shadow clones](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_clone "Shadow clone") was a great risk for him.[^37] However, Danzo stated that Hiruzen was unmatched in his usage of jutsu during his time as Hokage, indicating his chakra reserves were still noteworthy.[^38] Despite his old age, his reaction time and sensory perception were still good enough to defend himself, even when [robbed of his sight](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Bringer-of-Darkness_Technique "Bringer-of-Darkness Technique").[^39] Hiruzen could seamlessly lay [explosive tags](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Explosive_tag "Explosive tag") on high-level opponents without them noticing.[^40]

### Ninjutsu

![Hiruzen using the Dead Demon Consuming Seal.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/be/Reaper_death_seal.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240908041630)

Hiruzen using the Dead Demon Consuming Seal.

Hiruzen also was renowned as **The Professor** (プロフェッサー, *Purofessā*), due to his unprecedented versatility in ninjutsu, having mastered all the skillsets and combat forms of the shinobi arts.[^6] Hiruzen was reputed for his advanced knowledge and skill of all techniques to ever exist within Konoha, including the many [Hiden](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hiden "Hiden") techniques,[^40] [^4] [^41] and able to use each of them with the highest level of power and control.[^1] [^42] The sheer number of ninjutsu he mastered far surpassed all other Hokage.[^43] He could instantly deduce the impending technique simply from the [hand seals](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hand_seals "Hand seals").[^4] [^44] Even when he encountered something new, as in the case of [Truth-Seeking Balls](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Truth-Seeking_Ball "Truth-Seeking Ball"), he could discern their attributes and limitations from a brief observation.[^45] Hiruzen was well-noted for having personally created a variety of innovative techniques, preparing himself for any situation and was also capable of using various techniques simultaneously for more versatile and with longer effects.[^46]

His noteworthy skillset included [viewing things from afar](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Telescope_Technique "Telescope Technique"),[^47] creating a [barrier](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Four_Red_Yang_Formation "Four Red Yang Formation") powerful enough to contain the [Ten-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ten-Tails "Ten-Tails") alongside other Kage-level shinobi,[^48] and [sensing](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sense "Sense") the presence of individuals by their chakra.[^49]

#### Nature Transformation

![Hiruzen's clones using all five elements simultaneously.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/f/fe/Five_Release_Great_Combo_Technique.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20250908024043)

Hiruzen's clones using all five elements simultaneously.

Hiruzen was one of the few individuals who could naturally perform all five basic [nature transformations](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nature_transformation "Nature transformation"). Even rarer, he mastered each of them with equal proficiency and raw power. He could also use [Yin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yin "Yin") and [Yang Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yang_Release "Yang Release").[^4] His prowess could intercept an opponent's elemental attack and produce the respective [counterbalancing](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Five_Release_Great_Combo_Technique "Five Release Great Combo Technique") nature.[^50] Hiruzen favoured [Fire Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fire_Release "Fire Release") and was proud of the outstanding power of his [Fire Release: Fire Dragon Flame Bullet](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fire_Release:_Fire_Dragon_Flame_Bullet "Fire Release: Fire Dragon Flame Bullet") technique, which was known as one of the strongest Fire Release techniques in the world.[^51] [^2] In the anime he could also shoot [a barrage of small fire blasts](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fire_Release:_Phoenix_Sage_Fire_Technique "Fire Release: Phoenix Sage Fire Technique").[^36] With [Earth Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Earth_Release "Earth Release"), he could create a [protective wall of mud](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Earth_Release:_Earth-Style_Wall "Earth Release: Earth-Style Wall") that was powerful enough to block the Second Hokage's [Water Release: Water Dragon Bullet Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water_Release:_Water_Dragon_Bullet_Technique "Water Release: Water Dragon Bullet Technique"). In the anime, he could create a [river of mud](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Earth_Release:_Earth_Flow_River "Earth Release: Earth Flow River") to knock an opponent off their feet, and use the same mud to create a [dragon head](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Earth_Release:_Earth_Dragon_Bullet "Earth Release: Earth Dragon Bullet") to shoot a volley of mud balls that can be infused with [flames](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fire_Release:_Fire_Dragon_Bullet "Fire Release: Fire Dragon Bullet") for more damage.[^46] He could likewise produce powerful streams of [Lightning](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning "Lightning"), [Water](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water "Water"), and [Wind Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wind_Release "Wind Release") respectively.

#### Bukijutsu

![Hiruzen utilising his Shuriken Shadow Clone Technique.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/e/e3/Hiruzen_uses_giant_shuriken.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140918125920)

Hiruzen utilising his Shuriken Shadow Clone Technique.

Hiruzen regularly used [shuriken](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shuriken "Shuriken") in combat - both the standard size and the larger [fūma shuriken](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/F%C5%ABma_shuriken "Fūma shuriken").[^52] By using the [Shuriken Shadow Clone Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shuriken_Shadow_Clone_Technique "Shuriken Shadow Clone Technique"), he could create copies of these shuriken, allowing him to bombard a target with thousands of projectiles while simultaneously letting him conserve on his own supply, which won't need to be much.[^32] In the anime, Hiruzen was similarly shown utilising his surroundings like shuriken, such as [roof tiles](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Roof_Tile_Shuriken "Roof Tile Shuriken").[^46]

Hiruzen's personal [summon](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning_Technique "Summoning Technique") and partner [Enma](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Enma "Enma"), could [transform](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Transformation:_Adamantine_Staff "Transformation: Adamantine Staff") into an extendable and/or expandable staff that was as hard as a diamond. The staff was Hiruzen's weapon of choice and wielded it with great proficiency, though its weight became a great burden to him in his later years.[^40]

#### Fūinjutsu

Hiruzen could perform a [suicide-murder technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Dead_Demon_Consuming_Seal "Dead Demon Consuming Seal") that will seal away a target's soul with sacrifice of their own. However, he can bypass this by using a shadow clone to be sacrificed instead.[^53] He was also one of few people who could [remove seals](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Five_Elements_Unseal "Five Elements Unseal") as powerful as the [Five Elements Seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Five_Elements_Seal "Five Elements Seal").[^54]

## Part I

### Prologue — Land of Waves

Main article: [Prologue — Land of Waves](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Prologue_%E2%80%94_Land_of_Waves "Prologue — Land of Waves")

![Hiruzen watching Naruto.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/e/eb/Telescope_Technique.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150116120714)

Hiruzen watching Naruto.

When Naruto fails the [Academy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Academy "Academy") 's graduation exam yet again, Hiruzen takes Naruto's instructor, [Iruka Umino](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Iruka_Umino "Iruka Umino"), aside and advises him to be compassionate towards Naruto because of his lack of parents, something Iruka has had similar experiences with. When Naruto shortly afterwards breaks into the [Hokage Residence](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage_Residence "Hokage Residence") and steals the [Scroll of Seals](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Scroll_of_Seals "Scroll of Seals") (and also defeats Hiruzen with the [Sexy Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sexy_Technique "Sexy Technique")), Hiruzen has no choice but to mobilise a search party to apprehend Naruto. From [observing](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Telescope_Technique "Telescope Technique") events, Hiruzen learns of Naruto's manipulation by [Mizuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mizuki "Mizuki") and watches as Iruka finally shows kindness towards Naruto by protecting him from Mizuki. He calls off the search party when it becomes clear Naruto will return, but Hiruzen worries that Naruto's learning of the [Multiple Shadow Clone Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Multiple_Shadow_Clone_Technique "Multiple Shadow Clone Technique") from the Scroll will risk the Nine-Tails' escape from his body.

From his concern over the Nine-Tails as well as his disappointment that one of Naruto's first accomplishments as a [genin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genin "Genin") is to invent the [Harem Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Harem_Technique "Harem Technique"), Hiruzen assigns [Kakashi Hatake](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi_Hatake "Kakashi Hatake") as Naruto's [team](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Kakashi "Team Kakashi") leader so that Kakashi can guide him appropriately, as well as look over one of Naruto's teammates, [Sasuke Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Uchiha "Sasuke Uchiha"). Despite Kakashi's efforts, Naruto remains obstinate, complaining to Hiruzen about how easy their first [D-rank](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/D-rank "D-rank") [mission](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mission "Mission") was. When Naruto is unwilling to listen to explanations of how missions are assigned according to rank and skill level - both of which Naruto has little of at the moment - Hiruzen agrees to give the team a [C-rank](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/C-rank "C-rank") mission: escorting [Tazuna](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tazuna "Tazuna") to the [Land of Waves](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_Waves "Land of Waves").

### Chūnin Exams

Main article: [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams_\(Arc\) "Chūnin Exams (Arc)")

![Hiruzen calls a meeting.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/6/62/Episode_20.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150220163749)

Hiruzen calls a meeting.

Hiruzen calls together the village's [jōnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/J%C5%8Dnin "Jōnin") team leaders and informs them that Konoha will be hosting the next [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams "Chūnin Exams"), an opportunity for their genin-charges to be promoted to [chūnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin "Chūnin"). He asks that those jōnin assembled who believe their students are ready for the challenge of the exam submit their students' names. He begins with the team leaders of the Academy's most recent graduates and is surprised that all of the [Rookie Nine](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rookie_Nine "Rookie Nine") are deemed suitable by their instructors; he agrees to allow them to enter despite Iruka's protests.

![Hiruzen addressing the genin that passed.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/b1/Chunin_Examination_Arena.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20120422120447)

Hiruzen addressing the genin that passed.

Hiruzen is called to the [Forest of Death](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Forest_of_Death "Forest of Death") during the Exams' second stage by its proctor, [Anko Mitarashi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Anko_Mitarashi "Anko Mitarashi"). She reports Orochimaru's infiltration of the Exams and his warnings to not call off the Exams due to his appearance. Suspecting that Orochimaru is after Sasuke Uchiha, Hiruzen has no choice but to allow things to proceed as planned. He watches the preliminary matches, noting the growth of not only Naruto and Sasuke but all of Konoha's genin. Once the preliminaries conclude, he presides over the assignment of first round match-ups in the finals before they adjourn.

In the month prior to the finals, Hiruzen grows increasingly worried. Not only is he unsure of what Orochimaru and his underlings are planning, but it's also apparent that one of the rival [hidden villages](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hidden_village "Hidden village") is conspiring against Konoha as well, possibly in tandem with Orochimaru. What's more, Hiruzen does not believe that he, nor anyone else in Konoha for that matter, has the skills necessary to meet Orochimaru in battle. To clear his head, he visits his grandson's, [Konohamaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohamaru "Konohamaru") 's, class and is reminded of how important protecting the village and its citizens is to him.

When the finals begin, Hiruzen sits and watches with the [Fourth Kazekage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Kazekage "Fourth Kazekage"), the only other Kage whose village has representatives competing. Hiruzen, as host, remains pleasant towards the Kazekage and tries to assure him that there are no issues when the schedule for the finals increasingly does not go as planned.

### Konoha Crush

Main article: [Konoha Crush](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Crush_\(Arc\) "Konoha Crush (Arc)")

![Hiruzen clashing with Orochimaru.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/28/Hiruzen_tai_Orochimaru.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240908032138)

Hiruzen clashing with Orochimaru.

The last match of the first round of the finals is interrupted by an [invasion of Konoha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Invasion_of_Konoha "Invasion of Konoha"). Hiruzen is taken hostage and separated from his bodyguards by the Kazekage. Hiruzen tries to negotiate with the Kazekage until he realises it is actually Orochimaru in disguise. Orochimaru wants to fight and kill Hiruzen, his former teacher, and has his [followers](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sound_Four "Sound Four") erect a [barrier](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Four_Violet_Flames_Formation "Four Violet Flames Formation") so that the [Anbu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Anbu "Anbu") cannot come to Hiruzen's defence. Hiruzen is glad that nobody will interrupt them and removes his robes, revealing his combat attire underneath. Orochimaru decides he wants Hiruzen to share in the experience of fighting one's predecessor's and as such [reincarnates](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning:_Impure_World_Reincarnation "Summoning: Impure World Reincarnation") the [First](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/First_Hokage "First Hokage") and [Second Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Hokage "Second Hokage").

The Hokage greet Hiruzen in the brief moments before Orochimaru suppresses their personalities. Hiruzen attacks, but he has difficulty overcoming their defences and he must summon Enma to help block the First's [Wood Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Release "Wood Release"). He and Enma try to strike Orochimaru directly, but the Hokage block him. Despite failing to reach Orochimaru, Hiruzen is able to plant [exploding tags](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Exploding_tag "Exploding tag") on the Hokage's leg, but their reincarnated bodies cause them to quickly regenerate. Realising he has few options left, Hiruzen prepares to use the [Dead Demon Consuming Seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Dead_Demon_Consuming_Seal "Dead Demon Consuming Seal"). Orochimaru takes him by surprise before he can do anything by removing his own face, revealing a [new body](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Living_Corpse_Reincarnation "Living Corpse Reincarnation"), the completion of his research and the same fate he has planned for Sasuke.

![Hiruzen struggling to seal Orochimaru's soul.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/8e/Hiruzen_vs_Orochimaru.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150131223950)

Hiruzen struggling to seal Orochimaru's soul.

Hiruzen is disgusted by the levels Orochimaru has sunk to and his own responsibility in allowing Orochimaru to escape years earlier. In order to rectify the mistakes he's made at long last, Hiruzen makes two shadow clones that first catch the two Hokage and then use the Dead Demon Consuming Seal to remove their souls. The Hokage's personalities return to them briefly and they apologise for the hassle they've caused, just as Hiruzen apologises to them for the prison he's sentenced them to: their souls are consumed by the [Shinigami](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shinigami "Shinigami"). Orochimaru is amused by their defeat, unaware of what's happening, and bids Hiruzen to try the same on him. Hiruzen and Enma disarm him of his [Kusanagi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sword_of_Kusanagi:_Longsword_of_the_Sky "Sword of Kusanagi: Longsword of the Sky") and Hiuruzen starts performing the seal on him. When Orochimaru senses his soul's removal, he summons the Kusanagi back to him, causing it to lodge in Hiruzen despite Enma's attempts to intercept it.

![Hiruzen dying with a smile.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/b6/Hiruzen_last_smile.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150214203610)

Hiruzen dying with a smile.

Although stabbing Hiruzen has stopped the removal of his soul, Orochimaru cannot move in order to finish Hiruzen off. Orochimaru encourages him to die, but Hiruzen, doomed to die anyway for invoking the Shinigami, ignores him, instead trying to muster up the strength to finish sealing Orochimaru. After an hour passes with no progress, Hiruzen accepts the fact that he won't be able to complete the seal. He decides to do what little he can to put an end to Orochimaru's plans and protect the village: he has the Shinigami seal Orochimaru's arms, preventing their use. As Orochimaru curses him, Hiruzen dies smiling, hoping that he will see Orochimaru again in the next life.

## Part II

### Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax

Main article: [Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War:_Climax "Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax")

![The four Hokage are reincarnated.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/03/Hokage_reincarnated.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140612141309)

The four Hokage are reincarnated.

[Orochimaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Orochimaru "Orochimaru") releases the souls of the first four Hokage from the stomach of the Shinigami, an act that also restores his arms. At [Sasuke Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Uchiha "Sasuke Uchiha") 's request he then [reincarnates](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning:_Impure_World_Reincarnation "Summoning: Impure World Reincarnation") the Hokage. Hiruzen worries that Orochimaru now plans to use him to destroy Konoha, but is instead questioned by Sasuke about the [Uchiha Clan Downfall](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uchiha_Clan_Downfall "Uchiha Clan Downfall"). Hiruzen confirms the truth of what [Itachi Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Itachi_Uchiha "Itachi Uchiha") did and explains that he only did so on the condition that Hiruzen would keep Sasuke safe in the aftermath. From hearing about what has transpired since his death - Sasuke killing [Danzō Shimura](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Danz%C5%8D_Shimura "Danzō Shimura") and [Madara Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Madara_Uchiha "Madara Uchiha") emerging to start the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War") - Hiruzen feels guilty that maybe things could have been avoided had he taken different actions while he was alive.

![The Hokage arrive on the battlefield.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/2c/The_Hokage_Arrive.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140814200218)

The Hokage arrive on the battlefield.

Hearing from the Hokage and reflecting on his own experiences, Sasuke decides he doesn't want Konoha to be destroyed, as is Madara's current intention. Orochimaru releases the Hokage from his control so that they can help in this goal, and they set out for the site of the [Allied Shinobi Forces](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Allied_Shinobi_Forces "Allied Shinobi Forces") ' battle with the [Ten-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ten-Tails "Ten-Tails"). When they arrive, the Hokage erect a [barrier](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Four_Red_Yang_Formation "Four Red Yang Formation") around the Ten-Tails to confine it. Hiruzen must remain with the barrier to maintain it, so he can only watch as [Obito Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Obito_Uchiha "Obito Uchiha") seals the Ten-Tails into his body, becoming its [jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki "Jinchūriki").

### Birth of the Ten-Tails' Jinchūriki

Main article: [Birth of the Ten-Tails' Jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Birth_of_the_Ten-Tails%27_Jinch%C5%ABriki "Birth of the Ten-Tails' Jinchūriki")

![Hiruzen attacks Obito.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/1/13/Hiruzen_vs_Obito.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140918175808)

Hiruzen attacks Obito.

With his new power, Obito rips down the barrier, finally allowing Hiruzen to participate in the fight. From Obito's display it is obvious to Hiruzen that he is stronger than even the [First Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/First_Hokage "First Hokage"). He therefore personally engages Obito so that nobody needs to put their lives at risk, taking advantage of the fact that he's already dead to analyse Obito's abilities. By observing Obito's [Truth-Seeking Balls](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Truth-Seeking_Ball "Truth-Seeking Ball") he theorises that they are similar to the [Third Tsuchikage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Tsuchikage "Third Tsuchikage") 's [kekkei tōta](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kekkei_t%C5%8Dta "Kekkei tōta") in that they are a combination of multiple [nature transformations](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nature_transformation "Nature transformation"). He also postulates that the Truth-Seeking Balls have limits to the duration of their malleability, but his upper body is destroyed immediately after that.

![Hiruzen saves Naruto from the Ten-Tails.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/d/dd/Hiruzen_frees_Naruto.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20141009135738)

Hiruzen saves Naruto from the Ten-Tails.

It takes Hiruzen a great deal of time for his body to regenerate. When he finally does, he summons [Enma](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Enma "Enma"), has him [transform](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Transformation:_Adamantine_Staff "Transformation: Adamantine Staff"), and then quickly saves [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki") from the Ten-Tails' tree form's chakra-absorbing roots. When Orochimaru later arrives on the battlefield, they join together to help the Allies in the counter-offensive against Obito, first by blocking and cutting back more and more of the tree's roots and branches, then by assisting in pulling the [tailed beasts](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_beasts "Tailed beasts") from Obito's body. Their combined efforts succeed and Obito is defeated.

![Hiruzen battles against Tobi.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/a/ab/Hiruzen_vs_Guruguru.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20141225131658)

Hiruzen battles against Tobi.

The Allied Shinobi Forces are shortly afterwards attacked by [Tobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tobi_\(Zetsu\) "Tobi (Zetsu)"). With everyone too tired from the prolonged fighting to defend themselves, Hiruzen and four of his [shadow clones](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_clone "Shadow clone") arrive to help, [counterbalancing](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nature_Transformation#Counterbalancing "Nature Transformation") Tobi's elemental attacks with their own. Tobi withdraws once the [Infinite Tsukuyomi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Infinite_Tsukuyomi "Infinite Tsukuyomi") is about to be cast, having completed its job of distracting the Allies.

### Kaguya Ōtsutsuki Strikes

Main article: [Kaguya Ōtsutsuki Strikes](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kaguya_%C5%8Ctsutsuki_Strikes "Kaguya Ōtsutsuki Strikes")

![Hiruzen's soul departs to the Pure Land.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/27/Hiruzen%27s_soul_departs.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160908165356)

Hiruzen's soul departs to the Pure Land.

As the world is wrapped up by [God: Nativity of a World of Trees](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/God:_Nativity_of_a_World_of_Trees "God: Nativity of a World of Trees"), Hiruzen, unaffected because he is dead, tries to free Tsunade but fails. He and the other Hokage converge at the location of a powerful chakra signature, but find only Madara Uchiha's lower half, from which emerges the [Sage of the Six Paths](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sage_of_the_Six_Paths "Sage of the Six Paths"). Combining efforts with the spirits of the other dead [Kage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kage "Kage"), they summon [Team 7](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Kakashi "Team Kakashi"), Madara, and the tailed beasts back from [Kaguya's dimension](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kaguya%27s_dimension "Kaguya's dimension"). With the world saved, the Sage returns all the Kage to the [Pure Land](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Pure_Land "Pure Land"). As his soul ascends, Hiruzen entrusts the future to Team 7.

## Legacy

Hiruzen is only one of many Konoha ninja to die in the village's defence during the [Konoha Crush](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Crush "Konoha Crush"). However, a special ceremony to mourn Hiruzen is held, which many of Konoha's ninja attend; [Jiraiya](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jiraiya "Jiraiya") does not, opting to reminisce about Hiruzen privately. [Asuma](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Asuma "Asuma"), while not always agreeing with his father's ideals, has managed to see him in a better light as a Hokage and a great father two years after the funeral, shortly before Asuma's own death. Hiruzen is quickly replaced as Hokage by [Tsunade](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tsunade "Tsunade"), who, during her early days in office, notes how many fine shinobi the village produced under Hiruzen's guidance and makes a point to continue his practices during her own term. As Jiraiya was nearing his end at the hands of [Pain](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Pain "Pain"), one of his regrets was his failure to save his teacher. When Naruto becomes Hokage years later, he lives by the Will of Fire just as Hiruzen did.

## Trivia

| ![Springtime of Youth](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/3/39/Youth_symbol.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/40?cb=20130120020002)  Springtime of Youth | The [*Springtime of Youth* wiki](https://springtimeofyouth.fandom.com/wiki/ "w:c:springtimeofyouth") has an article about this topic:  **[Hiruzen Sarutobi](https://springtimeofyouth.fandom.com/wiki/Hiruzen_Sarutobi "w:c:springtimeofyouth:Hiruzen Sarutobi")** |
| --- | --- |

- Hiruzen's clan name "Sarutobi", inspired by [Sarutobi Sasuke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarutobi_Sasuke "wikipedia:Sarutobi Sasuke"), means "monkey jump" (猿飛), further supported by his Summoning Technique which summons Enma. Tobirama also nicknamed him "Monkey" (サル, *Saru*). Hiruzen's first name could come from [mountain with same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiruzen "wikipedia:Hiruzen") located on the border of Okayama and Tottori prefectures on the main island of Honshu in Japan.
- Many aspects of his character were borrowed from the Chinese literature classic *[Journey to the West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journey_to_the_West "wikipedia:Journey to the West")*. Hiruzen's summon, Enma, bears a very strong resemblance to [Sun Wukong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sun_Wukong "wikipedia:Sun Wukong") (孫悟空, *Son Gokū*), the protagonist of the novel, and Hiruzen's chosen weapon was identical to the Monkey King's magical staff.
- In the *[Live Spectacle Naruto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Live_Spectacle_Naruto "Live Spectacle Naruto")*, the role of Hiruzen was played by Kazuhiro Imagawa.
- Hiruzen's ghost is briefly seen in [*Naruto: Shippūden* episode 129](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Infiltrate!_The_Village_Hidden_in_the_Rain "Infiltrate! The Village Hidden in the Rain").
- During his battle with Orochimaru, Hiruzen's likeness on the [Hokage Rock](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage_Rock "Hokage Rock") cracks across the face. While the crack is fairly prominent due to its size, it doesn't always show up in the anime, due either to a bad angle or a mistake by the animators.
- [Masashi Kishimoto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Masashi_Kishimoto "Masashi Kishimoto") originally planned for Hiruzen to be a dog, but dumped the idea for being "too weird".
- Studio Pierrot's Settei sheets of Hiruzen show that he was 140cm around the time Konoha was founded, 145cm around the time the Shinobi Training Institution was created, and 166cm when Tobirama died.
- According to his [Naruto: Ultimate Ninja Online](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto:_Ultimate_Ninja_Online "Naruto: Ultimate Ninja Online") profile, Hiruzen was the elite in Tobirama's team.[^55]
- According to the databook(s):
	- His favourite foods were [hijiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hijiki "wikipedia:Hijiki") (which he shared with [Danzō Shimura](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Danz%C5%8D_Shimura "Danzō Shimura")) and sardine [fish balls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/fish_ball "wikipedia:fish ball"), while his least favourite food was [narazuke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kasuzuke "wikipedia:Kasuzuke").
		- His desired opponent was a grown-up [Konohamaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohamaru "Konohamaru").
		- His favourite phrase was [Absolute Truth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paramartha "wikipedia:Paramartha") (真諦, *Shintai*).
		- His hobby was chatting with young girls.
