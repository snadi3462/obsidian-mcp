**Kurenai Sarutobi** (猿飛紅, *Sarutobi Kurenai*, [née](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/n%C3%A9e "wikipedia:née") **Yūhi** (夕日)) is a member of [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure") 's [Sarutobi clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sarutobi_clan "Sarutobi clan"). During her time as an active duty [kunoichi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kunoichi "Kunoichi"), she served as the [jōnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/J%C5%8Dnin "Jōnin") -leader of [Team 8](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_8 "Team 8").

## Background

![Kurenai at her entrance ceremony.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/5/5c/Obito%27s_year_group.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150616161326)

Kurenai at her entrance ceremony.

As a child, Kurenai met [Asuma Sarutobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Asuma_Sarutobi "Asuma Sarutobi") during the [Academy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Academy "Academy") entrance ceremony where the two quickly became friends. She graduated at age 9. In the anime, during the [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams "Chūnin Exams"), Kurenai was placed in a team with Asuma and [Raidō Namiashi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Raid%C5%8D_Namiashi "Raidō Namiashi").[^6] She later was cheering for her classmates during the third round of the Chūnin Exams.[^7] Eventually becoming a [chūnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin "Chūnin") at age 13, Kurenai became a [jōnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/J%C5%8Dnin "Jōnin") shortly before Team 8 was formed.[^8]

![Kurenai and her peers are blocked from fighting the Nine-Tails.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/3/3a/Kurenai_and_the_young_protesters.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20120210133920)

Kurenai and her peers are blocked from fighting the Nine-Tails.

During the [Nine-Tailed Demon Fox's Attack](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tailed_Demon_Fox%27s_Attack "Nine-Tailed Demon Fox's Attack"), Kurenai, [Kakashi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi "Kakashi"), Asuma and [Guy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Guy "Guy") were amongst the young ninja that wanted to help in repelling the [beast](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tails "Nine-Tails"), but the group was [barred](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Barrier_Tag "Barrier Tag") from participating by the older shinobi, one of whom was her own [father](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shinku_Y%C5%ABhi "Shinku Yūhi"). Though she protested greatly about this, her father told her that this wasn't war but an internal village issue and as they were [shinobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shinobi "Shinobi"), their lives were brief. In addition, her father told her that due to her being a woman, he wanted her to live long enough to have children that would inherit the [Will of Fire](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Will_of_Fire "Will of Fire").[^9]

![Kurenai, Guy, and Asuma plead Hiruzen to remove Kakashi from the Anbu.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/4/4a/Asuma_Guy_and_Kurenai_plead_Hiruzen.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230409011332)

Kurenai, Guy, and Asuma plead Hiruzen to remove Kakashi from the Anbu.

In the anime, because of Kurenai's natural talent for [genjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genjutsu "Genjutsu"), the [Third Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Hokage "Third Hokage") tasked Kurenai with helping to train [Yakumo Kurama](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yakumo_Kurama "Yakumo Kurama"), another talented genjutsu user who longed to be a ninja.[^10] However, because Yakumo couldn't control [her illusions](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kurama_Clan%27s_Kekkei_Genkai "Kurama Clan's Kekkei Genkai") and thus endangered those around her, Kurenai had to [seal away](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Evil_Sealing_Method "Evil Sealing Method") her ninja abilities, preventing her from ever becoming a shinobi.[^11] About twelve years after the [Nine-Tailed Demon Fox's Attack](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tailed_Demon_Fox%27s_Attack "Nine-Tailed Demon Fox's Attack"), seeing her classmate fall into deeper self-pity from losing [Obito Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Obito_Uchiha "Obito Uchiha") and [Rin Nohara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rin_Nohara "Rin Nohara"), Kurenai, along with Might Guy and Asuma decided to speak with the Third Hokage, voicing their disapproval of Kakashi being in [Anbu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Anbu "Anbu") with such bitterness burdening him, and suggested instead that he be re-assigned as a jōnin sensei in the hopes of restoring Kakashi's kindness.[^12]

At some point in time, Kurenai visited the [Hyūga](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hy%C5%ABga "Hyūga") estate and took on [Hinata](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hinata "Hinata") on as her charge. She asked [Hiashi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hiashi "Hiashi") whether he was certain about his decision to have his daughter join the [regular forces](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shinobi_Organisational_System "Shinobi Organisational System"), although he promptly dismissed her, stating that the Hyūga clan had no place for the weak.[^13]

## Personality

![Kurenai and her daughter Mirai.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/00/Kurenai_and_child.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150501103633)

Kurenai and her daughter Mirai.

Kurenai is a caring and brave woman. She shows great concern towards her students and takes a vested interest in their growth, especially in regards to [Hinata Hyūga](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hinata_Hy%C5%ABga "Hinata Hyūga") due to knowing full well how Hinata was viewed by her father. When Hinata tried to offer Naruto some medicine after his fight with [Kiba](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kiba "Kiba"), Kurenai convinced [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki") to take it because she knew of her student's infatuation with him.[^14] Her personality is unyielding, honest, and simple. She is also portrayed as the "big sister type".[^3]

Throughout the series, it has been heavily hinted that Kurenai was romantically involved with fellow jōnin Asuma. The two appeared together in most of their scenes, and the other characters made it a point to note this fact on several occasions. When [Kakashi Hatake](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi_Hatake "Kakashi Hatake") found the two together at a dumpling shop, he asked if they were on a date, which made Kurenai blush and make up an excuse that she was running an errand only.[^15] During Asuma's battle with [Akatsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki "Akatsuki") members, [Hidan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hidan "Hidan") and [Kakuzu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakuzu "Kakuzu"), Kurenai was often shown wondering about his progress, appearing anxious. When she learned of his death, she fell to her knees in tears and disbelief, and later laid flowers on his [grave](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Cemetery "Konoha Cemetery") during his funeral.[^16] The truth of their relationship was made clear in Part II when Kurenai revealed that she is pregnant with Asuma's daughter, [Mirai Sarutobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mirai_Sarutobi "Mirai Sarutobi").

Since becoming a single mother, Kurenai proved very capable, raising Mirai to be very cheerful and responsible. At the same time, Kurenai could act very strict and severe whenever Mirai acted recklessly or misbehaved, as after getting separated from her team during a cat rescuing mission, Mirai became afraid of her mother's reaction of discovering that she went ahead of her team and gotten herself lost, recoiling at the mere thought of getting scolded by Kurenai.[^17]

## Appearance

- ![Kurenai as a child.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/f/fc/Kurenai_Child-NM.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/82?cb=20260429232600)
	Kurenai as a child.

Kurenai is a fair-skinned woman of slender build. She has long black untamed hair reaching her upper back, and very unique eyes that are red in colour, with an additional ring in them. She wears make-up consisting of red lipstick and purple eye shadow. Her regular outfit consists of a red mesh armour blouse with only the right sleeve visible. Overall, this is very broad material which resembles bandages with a pattern on it similar to those of rose thorns. Her hands and upper thighs are also wrapped in bandages and she wears the Konoha [forehead protector](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Forehead_protector "Forehead protector") and regular shinobi sandals.

In a flashback, it was shown that Kurenai used to wear the standard Konoha shinobi outfit with a [flak jacket](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Flak_jacket "Flak jacket") and a simple sleeveless red outfit underneath it.[^13] During her pregnancy, she wore a long, loose fitting maternal dress with a simple blouse underneath and after giving birth she was seen in a simple kimono. As a [genin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genin "Genin"), she wore a similar outfit to the one she wears currently with the signature thorn pattern on it with a simple sash around her waist.[^7] These thorns were pink in colour when she was a child, and red when she got older.[^18]

Two years after the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War"), she returned to her previous slender frame before the pregnancy. She also wears a black-coloured long-sleeved shirt under a burgundy sleeveless shirt with three red-orange stripes on it. She wears a light shade of grey pants with tan brown sandals. Her hair is also shoulder-length. Thirteen years later, Kurenai develops minor wrinkles at the corner of her mouth, and wears a red kimono with a white belt.

## Abilities

As a [jōnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/J%C5%8Dnin "Jōnin"), Kurenai is a highly skilled kunoichi. Despite being new to the rank at the time of the Chunin Exams,[^19] Kakashi entrusted her and Asuma to face the likes of Akatsuki members, [Itachi Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Itachi_Uchiha "Itachi Uchiha") and [Kisame Hoshigaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kisame_Hoshigaki "Kisame Hoshigaki").

### Genjutsu

![Kurenai using the Demonic Illusion: Tree Binding Death.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/09/Demonic_Illusion.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240820183706)

Kurenai using the Demonic Illusion: Tree Binding Death.

Kurenai is a [genjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genjutsu "Genjutsu") prodigy, who is considered Konoha's number one genjutsu user.[^20] Her skills are said to rival those of even Itachi's,[^2] the fellow genjutsu master himself even praised her skills.[^21] Capable of discerning the truth in an instant to see through an illusion and likewise able to lure enemies to a place far beyond genjutsu,[^3] she is able to simultaneously affect multiple opponents with her genjutsu, and, in the anime, revert the effects of genjutsu onto the caster.[^22] Most of her illusion techniques focus around flora; primarily using [large trees to bind opponents](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Illusion:_Tree_Binding_Death "Demonic Illusion: Tree Binding Death"), as well as [flower petals](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genjutsu:_Flower_Petal_Escape "Genjutsu: Flower Petal Escape") for various purposes.

### Other Skills

Kurenai has shown quick reaction speed, able to defend against Itachi's attack after just recently breaking out of her own reflected genjutsu.[^23] In the anime, she can [travel through surfaces](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hiding_in_Surface_Technique "Hiding in Surface Technique") and [sense](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sense "Sense") others by their chakra.[^24] She can also [seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Evil_Sealing_Method "Evil Sealing Method") away [a person](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yakumo "Yakumo") 's [kekkei genkai](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kekkei_genkai "Kekkei genkai"),[^11] and [enter the subconsciousness](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Psycho_Mind_Transmission "Psycho Mind Transmission") of a person to see their "true nature".[^25]

## Part I

### Chūnin Exams

Main article: [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams_\(Arc\) "Chūnin Exams (Arc)")

![Kurenai and the other jōnin stop Neji from attacking Hinata again.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/21/Shinobi_Stop_Neji.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20221003120551)

Kurenai and the other jōnin stop Neji from attacking Hinata again.

During the Chūnin Exams, Kurenai watched her pupils from the sidelines. After [Shino](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shino "Shino") 's battle, she commented on how it suddenly became cold in the room, in reference to his rather merciless defeat of [Zaku Abumi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Zaku_Abumi "Zaku Abumi"). During Kiba's fight with Naruto, she thought to herself that Naruto couldn't possibly beat Kiba, only to be surprised again and again as Naruto took everything Kiba threw at him and finally defeated her student. She bore Naruto no ill will after this and even acknowledged him as a strong opponent. During Hinata's fight with her cousin, [Neji Hyūga](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Neji_Hy%C5%ABga "Neji Hyūga"), she remembered the terrible relationship Hinata had with [her father](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hiashi_Hy%C5%ABga "Hiashi Hyūga"). She also noted the stark contrast between the current Hinata, who refused to give up, and the former Hinata, who would give up easily. When Neji was sent into a blind rage and tried to kill Hinata, Kurenai helped stop him along with Kakashi, Guy and [Hayate](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hayate "Hayate").

During the one month after the preliminaries, she was seen along with Kiba training Shino in preparation for the final rounds. At the finals she sat next to Asuma and was surprised when Naruto successfully called upon the Nine-Tails' chakra to pull off a victory over Neji. She also doubted that [Shikamaru Nara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shikamaru_Nara "Shikamaru Nara") had a strategy to win over [Temari](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Temari "Temari"), and although the former decided to quit, the skills he had shown during the match made Kurenai think that Shikamaru may have the qualities of a [chūnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin "Chūnin") after all.

### Konoha Crush

Main article: [Konoha Crush](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Crush_\(Arc\) "Konoha Crush (Arc)") After watching the final round matches a month later, Kurenai repelled [the genjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Temple_of_Nirvana_Technique "Temple of Nirvana Technique") that fell over the stadium and assisted with the village's defence against invading [Suna](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Suna "Suna") and [Oto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Oto "Oto") ninja during the [Konoha Crush](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Crush "Konoha Crush"). She later attended the Third Hokage's funeral.

### Search for Tsunade

Main article: [Search for Tsunade](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Search_for_Tsunade "Search for Tsunade")

![Kurenai clashes with Itachi.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/3/35/Kurenai_vs_Itachi.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240820180956)

Kurenai clashes with Itachi.

Soon after Konoha proved successful in defending the village, Kurenai and Asuma discovered that the Akatsuki members, Itachi Uchiha and Kisame Hoshigaki, had infiltrated the village. The two pairs engaged each other in battle. Kurenai was able to trap both men in her [Demonic Illusion: Tree Binding Death](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Illusion:_Tree_Binding_Death "Demonic Illusion: Tree Binding Death") technique. As she was about to land a deadly blow on Itachi from above, he [reverses](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Illusion:_Mirror_Heaven_and_Earth_Change "Demonic Illusion: Mirror Heaven and Earth Change") it onto her. By biting her lip, she is able to use the pain to overcome the genjutsu. She does so in time to dodge Itachi's fatal attack on her outside the illusion, but is kicked into the water with his follow up attack. When Kakashi arrived, he attempted a covert attack on Itachi, with Kurenai being prepared to ambush him from behind. However, she is stopped by Kakashi and forced down to avoid the [explosion](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_Great_Explosion "Clone Great Explosion") of Itachi's clone.

Further into the battle, Itachi activated his [Mangekyō Sharingan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mangeky%C5%8D_Sharingan "Mangekyō Sharingan"), leading Kurenai and Asuma to shut their eyes on Kakashi's directives but the latter is affected by Itachi's [Tsukuyomi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tsukuyomi "Tsukuyomi"). When Itachi instructed Kisame to kill them because they knew too much about their organisation, Guy arrived and [intercepted](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Leaf_Strong_Whirlwind "Leaf Strong Whirlwind") Kisame's attack. Afterwards, she is tasked with taking Kakashi to the [hospital](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Hospital "Konoha Hospital") while their opponents, not wanting to start a war, fled.

Later, in the anime, Kurenai attended Tsunade's inauguration ceremony as the Fifth [Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage "Hokage"), alongside several other villagers.[^26]

### Mizuki Tracking Mission

Main article: [Mizuki Tracking Mission](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mizuki_Tracking_Mission "Mizuki Tracking Mission") In the anime, Kurenai and Asuma were dispatched by the Fifth Hokage to bring the criminal [Mizuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mizuki "Mizuki") in for questioning, regarding his suspected involvement with [Orochimaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Orochimaru "Orochimaru"). The pair arrived at the Leaf Village Maximum Security Prison, only to be greeted by a swarm of released inmates, including the [Legendary Stupid Brothers](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Legendary_Stupid_Brothers "Legendary Stupid Brothers") [Fūjin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/F%C5%ABjin "Fūjin") and [Raijin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Raijin "Raijin"). Outnumbered, the two were beaten into unconsciousness, and were quickly rushed to the Leaf Village hospital for medical treatment.

### Yakumo Kurama Rescue Mission

Main article: [Yakumo Kurama Rescue Mission](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yakumo_Kurama_Rescue_Mission "Yakumo Kurama Rescue Mission") In the anime, Yakumo resurfaced after Kurenai had sealed her [kekkei genkai](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kekkei_genkai "Kekkei genkai") away several years ago which ultimately proved unsuccessful. After Yakumo created the illusion of the [Academy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Academy "Academy") being struck by lightning (something everyone believed to have actually happened), Kurenai took a temporary leave from Team 8 so that she could deal with the problem. Naruto confronted her about her involvement with Yakumo, but she simply used a genjutsu on him to keep him occupied.

![Kurenai seemingly trapped in an enemy's genjutsu.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/89/String_Bean_Illusion1.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150223071126)

Kurenai seemingly trapped in an enemy's genjutsu.

As she approached Yakumo's cabin, Kurenai appeared to be trapped in a rebel's genjutsu. In actuality, she had attacked her attacker in a genjutsu. She discovered that these attackers were in fact [Kurama Clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kurama_Clan "Kurama Clan") members, who were intent on kidnapping Yakumo. From this point, she and the opposing ninja basically fought with nothing but pure genjutsu technique, such as the [Demonic Illusion: Tree Binding Death](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Illusion:_Tree_Binding_Death "Demonic Illusion: Tree Binding Death") and the [String Bean Binding Illusion](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/String_Bean_Binding_Illusion "String Bean Binding Illusion"). Kurenai ended up successful until the final encounter, where she was caught and couldn't escape from his genjutsu, and so she took a [kunai](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kunai "Kunai") and stabbed herself in the shoulder to break out and used [Genjutsu: Flower Petal Escape](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genjutsu:_Flower_Petal_Escape "Genjutsu: Flower Petal Escape") to trap him in her own genjutsu. But he saw the blood from her wound and managed to hit her with a [shuriken](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shuriken "Shuriken").

![Kurenai sneaking into the Kurama mansion.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/7/7a/Hair_Camouflage.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150217174738)

Kurenai sneaking into the Kurama mansion.

Kurenai was saved by the arrival of her team, along with Naruto, [Sakura](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sakura "Sakura"), and Guy. Later, when explaining to Team 8 the reasons for her departure, she claimed that the village "doesn't need any genjutsu masters other than herself". This was her supposed reason for sealing Yakumo's abilities, but Shino recognised that she was lying. Yakumo, on the other hand, believed Kurenai did so in order to eventually wipe out the Kurama clan. She trapped her former sensei, Naruto, Sakura, and Team 8 in a genjutsu-created replica of the village which appeared to have been ravaged by war. [Unkai](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Unkai "Unkai") was also in the illusion. Unkai, concerned by the powers Yakumo had, and her continually degrading mental state as a result of being unable to control them, freed Naruto from the illusion so that he could kill Yakumo. Everyone else was freed as well, except for Kurenai. Yakumo kept her trapped in the illusion in the hopes of getting revenge and answers about her past.

Naruto and the others headed back to the mountain villa and Naruto re-entered Yakumo's genjutsu world to help Kurenai. Yakumo learned that a monster named Ido that lived within her subconscious mind was the one who killed her parents. With Kurenai's help, she overcame the monster in her heart and freed Kurenai from her genjutsu. With her final mission from the Third Hokage completed, Kurenai reassigned herself as the jōnin leader of Team 8.

### In Naruto's Footsteps: The Friends' Paths

Main article: [In Naruto's Footsteps: The Friends' Paths](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/In_Naruto%27s_Footsteps:_The_Friends%27_Paths "In Naruto's Footsteps: The Friends' Paths") About two years after Naruto departed from the village with [Jiraiya](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jiraiya "Jiraiya") for training, Konohagakure decided to host the Chūnin Exams early alongside Sunagakure. After the first part of the exams finished — held in Konoha — Kurenai and Asuma acted as the second exam's supervisors, informing the genin teams who were not part of the first 30 teams to arrive at the checkpoint, that they already lost and could not participate in the second round. The duo was later present to oversee the second exam begin with the other examiners.[^27] Later, Kurenai and the other Konoha proctors received word from [Tsunade](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tsunade "Tsunade") to be on guard for potential assassins towards Gaara as many still rejected him as [Kazekage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kazekage "Kazekage").[^28]

## Part II

### Akatsuki Suppression Mission

Main article: [Akatsuki Suppression Mission](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki_Suppression_Mission "Akatsuki Suppression Mission") At the hospital, before Asuma could speak with Kakashi, Kurenai arrived and the two had to leave, telling Kakashi that they would have to talk later. Later, as Asuma and his team from the [Nijū Shōtai](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nij%C5%AB_Sh%C5%8Dtai "Nijū Shōtai") mobilised, he lingered staring at an apartment window before mobilising. Inside, it's revealed that it was Kurenai's apartment. She senses something, but when she turned to look out the window, Asuma and his team were already gone. Returning to watering the flowers that were given to her by Asuma, one of the petals falls off its stem, and she has a feeling of foreboding.

![Kurenai mourns the loss of her lover.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/80/Asuma%27s_funeral.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150121102736)

Kurenai mourns the loss of her lover.

When Shikamaru returned from the mission, he was the one to break the grim news of Asuma. Kurenai stared on in shock and then collapsed to her knees devastated. During Asuma's funeral, Kurenai laid flowers on his grave. After Team Asuma avenged their sensei's death, Shikamaru found her at Asuma's grave in the [cemetery](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Cemetery "Konoha Cemetery"). Kurenai asked him if he'd be lonely now that his shōgi partner was gone. Shikamaru stated Asuma taught him both important and stupid stuff, and that he had always brushed off responsibility and whined when he was young, because Asuma was there to protect him. Shikamaru noted that Asuma was the coolest adult he knew, and he told Kurenai, when she had her baby, it would be his turn to become a cool adult too, and protect that child.

Kurenai insisted to Shikamaru that he did not need to check on her every time she went to the hospital but Shikamaru said he couldn't help it as Asuma told him to watch over her and the child.

Shikamaru protects Kurenai and her unborn child.

She was then seen going to the evacuation centre falling in line with many other people.

## Trivia

| ![Springtime of Youth](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/3/39/Youth_symbol.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/40?cb=20130120020002)  Springtime of Youth | The [*Springtime of Youth* wiki](https://springtimeofyouth.fandom.com/wiki/ "w:c:springtimeofyouth") has an article about this topic:  **[Kurenai Yūhi](https://springtimeofyouth.fandom.com/wiki/Kurenai_Y%C5%ABhi "w:c:springtimeofyouth:Kurenai Yūhi")** |
| --- | --- |

- The name "Kurenai" means "crimson" (紅), while her family name "Yūhi" means "evening sun" (夕日).
- Kurenai's red lipstick is, in a way, a pun on her own name. The kanji for "Kurenai" can also be read as "beni", which is a word used for red make-up used traditionally in Japan to colour the lips red.
- In her first appearance in the anime during the [Prologue — Land of Waves](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Prologue_%E2%80%94_Land_of_Waves "Prologue — Land of Waves") arc, her dress is red with white sleeves, but every time she is seen after that it's the opposite.
- In later episodes of Part II, she is usually seen with a ring on her left ring finger. It is unclear if this is a wedding ring, an engagement ring, or something else. The ring was first seen while Kurenai was worried about Asuma during his battle with Hidan. After Kurenai is revealed to be pregnant, she is always seen wearing the ring. The ring first appears in the manga after the reveal of her pregnancy.
- During a question and answer session held at Tokyo's Jump Fest '09, [Masashi Kishimoto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Masashi_Kishimoto "Masashi Kishimoto") stated that he was thinking of making Kurenai and Asuma's child a girl, which he follows through with.[^32]
- Kurenai is the only one of the four principal sensei (Asuma, Kakashi, Guy, and herself) who doesn't regularly wear a [flak jacket](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Flak_jacket "Flak jacket"); the only two times she is seen wearing one was in a flashback with [Hiashi Hyūga](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hiashi_Hy%C5%ABga "Hiashi Hyūga") and in a scene with her and Asuma. She can also be seen wearing it in *[Naruto Shippūden: Ultimate Ninja 5](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Shipp%C5%ABden:_Ultimate_Ninja_5 "Naruto Shippūden: Ultimate Ninja 5")* and *[Naruto Shippūden: Ultimate Ninja Heroes 3](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Shipp%C5%ABden:_Ultimate_Ninja_Heroes_3 "Naruto Shippūden: Ultimate Ninja Heroes 3")*.
- Studio Pierrot's Settei sheets of Kurenai show that she was 123 cm when enrolling in the Academy and 144.4 cm around the time of the [Kannabi Bridge](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kannabi_Bridge "Kannabi Bridge") incident and 168 cm during the [Nine-Tailed Demon Fox's Attack](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tailed_Demon_Fox%27s_Attack "Nine-Tailed Demon Fox's Attack").
- According to the databook(s):
	- Her hobby is having evening drinks.
		- She wants to fight Asuma (first databook) and later she doesn't want to fight anyone (third databook).
		- Kurenai's favourite foods are [shōchū](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sh%C5%8Dch%C5%AB "wikipedia:Shōchū") (strong [sake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sake "wikipedia:Sake")), [vodka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vodka "wikipedia:Vodka") and takowasa ([octopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Octopus#As_food "wikipedia:Octopus") and [wasabi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wasabi "wikipedia:Wasabi")), her least favourite food is [cake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cake "wikipedia:Cake").
		- Kurenai's favourite phrase is " *Glory like a violet blooming a single day* " (菫花一日の栄, *Kinka Ichijutsu no Ei*).
		- Kurenai has completed 697 official missions in total: 152 D-rank, 158 C-rank, 235 B-rank, 138 A-rank, 14 S-rank.
- In Viz's translation of [Chapter 35](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Iruka_vs._Kakashi%3F! "Iruka vs. Kakashi?!"), a Yūhi clan is mistakenly mentioned.
- According to *[Naruto Mobile](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Mobile "Naruto Mobile")*, during her childhood, Kurenai's talent for genjutsu was exceptionally outstanding among her peers, drawing the attention of the Third Hokage. She and Asuma grew up together from a young age; with their personalities complementing one another, they shared a bond akin to childhood sweethearts.[^33]
